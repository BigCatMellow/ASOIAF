## Established book-specific causal hinge

The chapter pair **ADWD 29 → ADWD 51** gives the northern political contradiction: Wyman privately wants to restore a Stark heir and promises only *conditional* support to Stannis, yet his men are dispatched against Stannis alongside the very Freys Wyman detests. The text does not give their detailed orders. This is a **working question**, not proof of a secret plan.

The chapter pair **ADWD 44/49 → ADWD 62** establishes the information gap: Jon has tried to warn the king, while Arnolf has already embedded hundreds of soldiers and messengers inside Stannis's starving camp. The kingdom's fate cannot be decided by giving Stannis facts he may not possess.

The chapter pair **ADWD 51 → ADWD 62** establishes a real handoff: Theon/Jeyne flee Winterfell, are received by Mors Umber, then reach Stannis's encampment through Tycho's group. Their knowledge may become consequential **if someone asks and believes them**.
