## Main causal and ethical arc

Theon's public endorsement is the critical device that launders the false Arya into apparent Stark legitimacy. Winterfell's splendor covers ruin, coercion and rival political purposes. The chapter offers suggestive evidence for the Frey-pie hypothesis and the weirwood whisper, but neither is confirmed here.

## Competing interpretations and limits

**Frey pies:** Manderly's prominent pork pies and insistence on the Rat Cook song support revenge suspicion, especially after his earlier anti-Frey positioning. Neither the meat's source nor any named Frey's remains are identified in this chapter; retain an ordinary lavish-feast reading. **Weirwood whisper:** Theon hears his name near the heart tree while ravens watch, consistent with possible Bran intervention. It may be an auditory misperception under trauma; seek corresponding greenseer POV testimony.
