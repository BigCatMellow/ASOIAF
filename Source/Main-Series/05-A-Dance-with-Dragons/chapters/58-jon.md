## Main narrative and rival readings

Jon successfully executes an extraordinarily risky admission day through procedure, hostages, mutual restraint, and Tormund's authority. Yet the same chapter turns the apparent success into an inadequate local solution: Hardhome is collapsing beyond reach. Tormund's horn story and Borroq's warning merit follow-up, not adoption as fact.
