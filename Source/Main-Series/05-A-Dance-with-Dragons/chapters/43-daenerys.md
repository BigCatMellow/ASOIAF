## Main plot, agency and consequences

The long-secret Dornish pact arrives in court one day before the marriage designed to avert Meereen's siege. Daenerys recognizes the historic Viserys-Arianne agreement but does not accept Quentyn as replacement husband. Dany insists her present duty to freedmen and Meereen outweighs her future claim and personal desire; she completes the wedding despite contempt from Daario and a haunting dream.

## Competing interpretations and cross-POV checks

A written agreement between Willem Darry and Oberyn Martell, witnessed by the Sealord, is tangible evidence of a *past* arrangement; it is not an automatically binding Quentyn-Daenerys betrothal. Dany's memory of the red door and the Braavos signature is an important junction, not proof that her childhood memories were false. Quaithe's 'sun's son' could refer to Quentyn, but this does not fix every prophecy symbol or make him a proven traitor. Barristan's court-gossip report about Aerys's conduct toward Joanna supports humiliation, not Tyrion's paternity.
