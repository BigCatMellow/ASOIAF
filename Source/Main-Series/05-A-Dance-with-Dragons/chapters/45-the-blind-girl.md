## Main plot, agency and consequences

Arya improves nonvisual skills through a harsh Faceless Men curriculum while holding onto her Stark identity through Nymeria, memories and her private list of enemies. The order's doctrine denies the right to judge who deserves death, a principle Arya inwardly resists. As Blind Beth she overhears Lyseni sailors describe Hardhome refugees sold into slavery, seemingly viewing the men through a cat. Her restored sight follows a treatment change but causal method remains unspecified.

## Competing interpretations and cross-POV checks

The cat-eye perception combined with Arya's identification of her attacker strongly supports latent skinchanging, though she does not formally explain it to the priests. Her story about the Goodheart and Elephant comes from sailors, not direct boarding of both ships; Goodheart's seizure is strong local evidence, Elephant's location unverified. Arya has access to Hardhome testimony at Braavos while Jon plans a Watch rescue unaware of that testimony.
