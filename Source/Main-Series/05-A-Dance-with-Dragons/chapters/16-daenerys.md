## Plot and theory audit

**Choice that changes the geopolitical story.** Xaro's ships are real enough to pass Admiral Groleo's inspection, but the free-gift framing conceals a strict requirement that Dany abandon Meereen. Her ruling is explicit: she stays because transporting and protecting the thousands she has freed is not solved by thirteen galleys. Her refusal incurs a *formal declaration of war* from Qarth. The war threat is a completed event; a blockade or Qartheen battle is not yet shown.

**The slave economy and emancipated agency.** Xaro contends that refined culture requires enslaved labor, while the newly freed city struggles to feed people, compel irrigation labor and establish non-slave exports. Dany's dismissal of his defense of ownership is sound as an ethical distinction, but she must still face her own coerced labor practice. The house burned its olive groves in war; seven and thirty years quantify the long recovery.

**Competing sources.** The visitor knows of sellsword mobilization, the missing Tolos/Mantarys envoys and warlock Pyat Pree's alleged pursuit. His motives and access to information are unclear. Do not automatically adopt his claim that the Golden Company now belongs to Yunkai when [ADWD 5](05-tyrion.md) has Illyrio describing a Daenerys-linked contract.

**Symbol and continuity.** The old map is material evidence for one pre-Doom geographic representation, not proof its dating is accurate. The bloodied glove announces war. Mezzara's presence shows Dany's hostage policy has already been implemented, while Ghael's broken teeth illustrate how little protection a hostile petitioner can expect despite Dany stopping further violence.
