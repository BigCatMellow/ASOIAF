## Major causal and theoretical junctions

**Blackfyre question:** The Company founded by Bittersteel exists to preserve Blackfyre exiles; Illyrio says its contract is written in blood and then dismisses the distinction between black and red dragons. He mentions only extinction of the **male** line. Together these justify intensified research into a claimant through a female line, not a factual claim that Young Griff is a Blackfyre. The simpler alternative is that centuries of exile have made a Daenerys-assisted return attractive regardless of dynasty.

**Serra:** Illyrio supplies a portrait, a Lysene origin story, a marriage that cost him political status and a macabre claim about preserving her hands. This offers a credible personal stake but does not identify Serra's ancestry or any child. Do not turn a suggestive hair color into genealogy.

**Information limits:** Illyrio assumes Daenerys is already marching west; the reader has seen her working to hold Meereen. His promised convergence at Volantis is a plan built on outdated facts. Tyrion remains unstable and culpable for Shae's killing; a fascination with dragons does not establish Targaryen paternity.
