## Main narrative and rival readings

Victarion pushes a badly depleted expedition toward Meereen because delay threatens Euron's prize and Volantis's arrival. His infected hand makes him vulnerable to Moqorro's counsel. The red priest may have saved him, altered him, exploited him, or some combination; the chapter verifies treatment and Victarion's belief, not a reliable magical explanation.
