## Causal reading

**A hidden prince's education.** Young Griff receives sword practice, multiple languages, mathematics, history, Faith lessons and etiquette. His teacher gives him strategic Volantene history, and Griff has Tyrion produce dragon research. These are concrete investments far beyond ordinary hired escort training. They support a deliberately raised claimant without determining his ancestry or right to rule. Tyrion notices eyes that sometimes look purple, but phenotype alone does not establish birth.

**Dragons and suppressed knowledge.** The alleged locked Citadel copy of The Death of Dragons / Blood and Fire deserves cross-checking with the AFFC Pate/Walgrave stolen-key episode and Marwyn's concerns. The inference that the infiltrator seeks this particular book is plausible but completely unproved. Barth's books were historically burned; even the surviving accounts disagree about mating and dragon physiology. A restricted manuscript does not by itself prove a single Citadel anti-dragon conspiracy.

**Septa Lemore.** Her stretch marks indicate probable previous pregnancy and childbirth, while her openly comfortable bathing habits complicate a narrowly conventional portrait of a septa. Neither observation identifies her as a named lost noblewoman, a Sand Snake or the mother of a claimant. Keep identity alternatives reversible.

**Cyvasse and the river god.** Tyrion appears to win a wager involving Haldon's secrets, but neither a complete play record nor the purported secret is supplied here. The gigantic turtle is physically real; its identity as a Rhoynish deity and Tyrion's interpretation of a kingly birth are beliefs. Read them as motifs until some mechanism emerges.
