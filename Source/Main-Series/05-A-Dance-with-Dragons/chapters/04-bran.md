## Major causal and theoretical junctions

**Coldhands:** Absent visible breath, physical cold, black extremities, refusal of food, and his own stopped-heart explanation make an undead state well supported. The origin of this condition, his personal name and whether his crossing limit is absolute remain open. He is not shown operating like a hostile blue-eyed wight, and the older Benjen identification is not proven here.

**Corpses and alleged sow:** Summer encounters and eats men of the Night's Watch; Bran then sees his hungry party eating meat supplied by Coldhands. The ranger claims it is sow, but the chapter does not identify the source independently. These are two different standards: **Summer's consumption of humans is verified; the companions' possible cannibalism is theory**. Coldhands likely neutralized the pursuers, but who those men were or why they pursued is not confirmed.

**One Eye and Varamyr:** The prologue establishes Varamyr entering One Eye after human death, accompanied by Sly and Stalker. Bran's one-eyed elder/female/young-male pack is a strong physical match. Summer's domination may therefore displace another skinchanger's second life. This is a meaningful cross-POV link, not an explicit identification spoken in the Bran chapter.

**Hodor:** Bran senses Hodor's fear while using his body without consent. The reduction in outward thrashing is not evidence of willingness. Juxtaposed with the prologue's human-possession taboo, this is a concrete ethical hazard of Bran's growing powers.
