## Main causal and ethical arc

The skull is a public diplomatic token, not independent proof that Gregor died. Doran's public cooperation masks a claim of Cersei's plan against Trystane and a three-pronged Dornish intelligence deployment. His concern for vulnerable children is not reducible to indecision.

## Competing interpretations and limits

**Gregor:** large skull, poison and reports of screaming support a death account, but lack independent identity and decapitation witnesses; Cersei's later champions and Qyburn's activities are the obvious disconfirmation checks. **Doran's fleet:** elephants plus ships may indicate Aegon's Golden Company rather than Daenerys's return. Neither Doran nor Arianne has that knowledge here. **Trystane plot:** Doran's sources and Swann's suspicious reaction support the accusation; absent is Balon's own confession or observed attackers.
