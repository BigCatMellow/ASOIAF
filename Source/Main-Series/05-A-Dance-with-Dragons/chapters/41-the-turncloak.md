## Main plot, agency and consequences

Theon has fulfilled his coerced wedding function and returns to social exclusion. Bolton's full castle is warmer and initially better fed than Stannis's army, but its large garrison relies on finite supply. Rowan's questions about his past capture of Winterfell may be disguised reconnaissance; Theon assumes she is another Ramsay trap. Lady Dustin says Jeyne's abuse jeopardizes legitimacy, exposes personal grievances against Ned, and threatens his remains. Neither suspicion of Rowan nor Dustin's ultimate allegiance is resolved.

## Competing interpretations and cross-POV checks

Theon hears sobs by the heart tree, which might relate to Bran's weirwood awareness (ADWD 34) but can be grief, Jeyne, or sensory misperception. The missing crypt swords have a *verified mundane explanation* in ACOK 69: Bran takes Brandon's, Meera takes Rickard's, and Osha takes Ned's. Theon does not know this. Lady Dustin's stated hatred may be sincere yet her understanding of northern pro-Stark sentiment creates incentives other than straightforward Bolton fidelity.
