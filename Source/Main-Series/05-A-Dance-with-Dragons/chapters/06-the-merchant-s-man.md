## Major causal and theoretical junctions

**Mission facts:** Quentyn's covert dynastic assignment comes from Doran, who explicitly knows it risks war. Quentyn is eighteen, and half his original companions are already dead after a corsair ambush. His refusal to return is motivated by genuine duty and grief, but also fear of paternal failure. Daenerys has neither accepted the marriage nor heard this account directly here.

**Transport problem:** With almost a hundred reported refusals, even a giant port does not produce reliable passage into an active war zone. The Adventure skipper's seeming enthusiasm makes murder, robbery or enslavement credible guesses, **not actual events**. Gerris's final plan remains unspecified at this boundary.

**Converging intelligence:** The eastward Golden Company report from this captain supports Illyrio's statement in ADWD 5 that the Company is heading toward Volantis. It does not verify their ultimate destination, allegiance or which claimant they mean to back. Quentyn wrongly anticipates action by the deceased Tywin, an important knowledge gap.
