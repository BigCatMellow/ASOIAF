## Main causal and ethical arc

A voyage sustained by acting, insults and dangerous superstition is wrecked by the storm apparently foretold by Benerro. The prophecy may be accurately fulfilled but is not demonstrated to have caused the storm. Moqorro is missing, not a witnessed corpse. Penny's care and Tyrion's recurrent violence toward his memories complicate his trajectory.

## Competing interpretations and limits

**Storm prophecy:** Benerro's warning fits the ruined voyage, but the episode does not establish supernatural weather manipulation, and the precise meaning was opaque to travelers. **Moqorro:** he is counted lost at sea, not proven dead. Test later reports rather than assigning his fate here. **Penny:** fear-driven kiss and Tyrion's refusal do not establish a settled relationship.
