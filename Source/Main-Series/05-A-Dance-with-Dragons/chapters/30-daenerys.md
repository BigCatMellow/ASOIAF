## Key causal findings

**The peace clock is factual, the mechanism unknown.** Twenty-six days have elapsed without murders while Hizdahr visits eleven great-family pyramids. That makes him an effective elite broker, but Skahaz's stronger claim that he leads the Harpy is unproven. The quality of the Brazen Beasts' torture-supplied names has collapsed, so an apparent confession would be a poor test. Dany's decision to burn the blockade family's list is a deliberate rejection of mass hostage-taking.

**The pale mare is both an omen and a public-health problem.** The rider arrives on an exhausted pale horse and dies with an infected arrow wound and apparent bloody diarrhea. Quaithe warned of a pale mare; now there is a probable bloody flux reaching Meereen from Astapor. Neither the omen nor independent medical causality is equivalent to proof Quaithe controls the event. Later infection among refugees must be logged as observed rather than inferred from one sick man.

**Astapor is no counterfactual cleanly solved by intervention.** Two independent channels—the Windblown view outside and three actual survivors—describe a city destroyed by Yunkish blockade, New Ghiscari assault, factional war and devastating epidemic. Dany regrets not helping, but Barristan correctly notes moving the Unsullied south would have exposed Meereen to insurgency. The fully armored Cleon on a horse was a corpse, while King Cutthroat was later killed by dogs; keep leaders distinct.

**The hard bargain about refugees.** Dany refuses to abandon people seeking safety but accepts Barristan's medical warning and places them outside the city, promising food and separation of sick from well. This is neither an unequivocal welcome nor total refusal of aid. Financial and epidemiological means are thin.

**Military choices create marriage necessity.** Stretched naval supplies, absent Drogon, captive dragons and unreliable sellswords make a field battle risky, while the Harpy makes a static siege perilous. Dany realizes she needs Hizdahr to unite a city divided from within. Her statement is a practical decision, not proof the marriage is wise or doomed.
