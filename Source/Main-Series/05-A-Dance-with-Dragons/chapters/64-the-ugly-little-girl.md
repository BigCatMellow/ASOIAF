## Causal analysis and limits

Arya passes a controlled test by causing one marked man's death without adding unmarked victims. The success is real, but it is deliberately unlike the revenge killings she still imagines. We cannot infer the client, the precise poison/financial instrument, or that her identity has been erased. Her first apprenticeship is the next test. Independent challenge remains open.
