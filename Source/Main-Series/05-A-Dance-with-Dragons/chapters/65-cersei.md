## Causal analysis and limits

The walk is a bargain for release, not a resolution of Cersei's case. Her private response makes it poor evidence of transformed belief: she remains focused on power, Tommen, and revenge. Robert Strong gives her a formidable champion while producing an identity mystery; Qyburn's label and vow are claims, not an answer to that mystery. Independent challenge remains open.
