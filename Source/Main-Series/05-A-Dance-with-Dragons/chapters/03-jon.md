## Interpretation and rival tests

**This occurs before Sam's AFFC voyage.** Sam is here at the Wall with Aemon alive and the infant-swap still in preparation. Reading publication order as continuous chronology creates contradictions about Aemon's death and the baby's position; the scenes must instead be aligned as interleaved. Jon's uncertainties about Bran and Rickon also do not erase the reader's direct earlier knowledge of their survival.

**Jon versus Stannis is a genuine policy collision.** Stannis wants practical reoccupation of nineteen forts and rewards for southern followers. Jon also wants the forts garrisoned, but not granted to foreign lords or detached from Watch command. Jon offers a compromise involving Stannis's troops under black officers without Watch vows; Stannis refuses due rank politics. The episode is not Jon obstructing defense for its own sake. Stannis threatens Jon with beheading and taking the forts; he leaves the room with no signed grant.

**Wolf information.** Ghost's dream contains contact with a black brother, a sister accompanied by a large pack, and a grey brother with northern/beyond-Wall associations. This reinforces the existence of a Stark direwolf information network and parallels [AFFC 44](../../04-A-Feast-for-Crows/chapters/44-jaime.md)'s huge she-wolf report. It does *not* transfer that knowledge automatically into Jon's waking strategic decisions, and cannot certify an exact hidden animal location.

**Mance and the infants.** Stannis wrongly treats wildling authority as a hereditary monarchy and Val as a conventional princess. Jon challenges both, partly to prevent king's-blood sacrifice and the imminent infant substitution. The text here shows plans and deceptive explanations, not the completed exchange later narrated by Sam.

**Melisandre's warning.** She predicts that smiling enemies and dark daggers threaten Jon, while admitting priests can mistake possibility for certainty. This is a high-value signal in light of the later Jon stabbing, but **does not establish that Melisandre identified the attackers, that Stannis ordered them, or that the prophecy compelled events**.
