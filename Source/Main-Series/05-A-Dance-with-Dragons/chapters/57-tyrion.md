## Main narrative and rival readings

Tyrion converts disease and command disorder into his first serious move toward a new patron. He risks Penny's security and leaves vulnerable companions behind, but offers Plumm a package: Jorah's sword, Tyrion's information, and possible Westerosi leverage. Plumm may see assets, infection, or a dangerous Lannister liability; the text has not decided it.
