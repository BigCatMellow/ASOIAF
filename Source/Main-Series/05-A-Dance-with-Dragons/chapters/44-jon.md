## Main plot, agency and consequences

Jon obtains a signed Iron Bank loan and the use of Tycho's three ships to finance food and Hardhome rescue. This is separate from Tycho's intended loan diplomacy with Stannis over Iron Throne debts. Selyse's party threatens the fragile hospitality extended to Wun Wun and wildlings. Jon mistakes a rescued girl for Arya because he remembers Melisandre's grey-girl image, only to discover Alys Karstark, whose report validates a major threat Stannis may not know.

## Competing interpretations and cross-POV checks

Alys's warning is direct *testimony* that Arnolf is Bolton's agent, consistent with Roose's earlier account in ADWD 32, but the treachery is not yet executed. Jon's signed Watch agreement is real; Tycho has not yet agreed to bankroll Stannis or made him king. The prophesied grey girl arrives on a dying horse but turns out to be Alys, undermining Jon's interpretation that she must be Arya. Patchface's dead-dancing image remains ambiguous.
