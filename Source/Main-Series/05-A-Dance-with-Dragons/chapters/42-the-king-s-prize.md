## Main plot, agency and consequences

Asha survives Stannis's Deepwood victory in chains because she surrendered to spare nine ironborn. The march's military promise breaks under terrain, supply and weather; northern clan contingents cope significantly better than knights and heavy horses. The king refuses his queen's men's call to burn unbelievers, yet keeps marching until the host has reached an abandoned settlement with fishable lakes. Stannis's intent to conquer Winterfell remains; no lake-battle outcome occurs here.

## Competing interpretations and cross-POV checks

ADWD 41 independently establishes the blizzard around Bolton-held Winterfell. Arnolf's promised support should not be trusted given the earlier Roose claim and the later direct Alys warning in ADWD 44. The pair of frozen lakes and beacon-bearing watchtower provide *possible* ingredients for Night Lamp speculation, but the episode shows fishing holes as sustenance, not an executed trap. Burning Asha is urged by zealots; Stannis explicitly resists burnings at this checkpoint.
