## Main narrative, decisions and consequences

In sight of the nominally free Meereen, the entire surviving party falls into slave markets. Tyrion buys time by making himself useful and uses a fabricated entertainment role to keep a brutalized Jorah out of a lethal fighting stable. Yezzan owns them, and his bodyguard/overseer Nurse is openly dangerous. Brown Ben Plumm's interest and losses at cyvasse offer a potential contact, not an accomplished defection. Meereen's marriage and peace translate into a commercial reopening of brutal public spectacle.

## Rival readings and epistemic boundaries

**Brown Ben / Tyrion:** the sellsword captain tries to buy Tyrion, then plays him at cyvasse. This is evidence of potential future negotiation, not proof of an alliance or of Plumm's exact bloodline. **Jorah:** the new face brand and submission show punishment and despair, not a dead man or an irrevocable end to his agency. **Poison mushrooms:** Tyrion thinks of using his hidden supply for himself and Penny if necessary. It is not on-page evidence that he poisoned or means to poison Yezzan. **Slave camp economics:** many citizens believe Dany has married Hizdahr, and the wedding is directly confirmed in ADWD 43, but predictions about the end of slavery in Meereen or future pit outcomes remain unsettled.
