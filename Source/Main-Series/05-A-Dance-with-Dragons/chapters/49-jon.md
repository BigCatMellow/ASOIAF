## Main narrative, agency, and consequences

Jon turns Alys's flight into a public alliance: he protects her succession claim, marries her to Sigorn, and tries to make Thenn settlement politically legible to northern lords. That choice also makes his exposure to charges of partiality more acute. He confines Cregan rather than killing him because execution could alienate northerners and look like a land transfer to free folk. Meanwhile, food, weather, and divided command make his large humanitarian commitments fragile. Tormund's arrival and the Hardhome fleet create the next immediate test.
