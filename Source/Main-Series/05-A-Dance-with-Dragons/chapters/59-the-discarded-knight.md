## Main narrative and rival readings

The court scene demonstrates that Hizdahr cannot simply inherit Dany's authority: grief, factional suspicion, and Yunkish force constrain him. Barristan correctly treats the locust poison as evidence of danger but refuses to decide its target from circumstance alone. His warning to Quentyn is protective, not proof that Hizdahr planned an arrest or that Quentyn poisoned anyone.
