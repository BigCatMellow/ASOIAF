## Main causal and ethical arc

Dany's compassion remains real, but cannot reconcile siege reserves, mass disease and an external army. Hizdahr offers an allegedly enforceable peace that would normalize renewed slavery. Her personal reliance on Daario and the unclear three-treasons prophecy complicate that political bargain.
