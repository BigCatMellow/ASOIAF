This is a logistical crisis, not merely a courage test. Fire sacrifice is a verified killing and an unverified religious remedy. Tycho, Theon, and Jeyne supply a new opening, but neither bank policy nor Stannis's use of the fugitives is yet known. Independent challenge remains open.
