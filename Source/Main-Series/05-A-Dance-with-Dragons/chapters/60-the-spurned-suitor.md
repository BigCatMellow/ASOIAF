## Causal analysis and limits

Quentyn converts a failed marriage mission into an attempt to obtain dragon power. Gerris supplies the serious ordinary alternative: return home alive. The Tattered Prince is shown as a hard bargainer, not established ally or secret foe. Quentyn knows Dany is married and missing, but not whether the company can reach dragons, whether Dorne can surrender Pentos, or whether ancestry matters. Independent challenge remains open.
