## Narrative chronology and causality

1. **THE TRIDENT AT LAST:** Arya, Gendry and Hot Pie have finally reached a stretch of **the Trident** and scavenge **carrots and cabbages** from the garden of a dead man beside a burned cottage. The provisions stolen from Harrenhal ran out **six days earlier**. This confirms progress since [ASOS 3 Arya](03-arya.md), where Arya was uncertain which small tributaries she had crossed. **Their present precise distance from Riverrun is still not measured**.
2. **A SINGING CONTACT IS NOT AUTOMATICALLY SAFE:** Arya hears a man singing and playing a **woodharp**, immediately suspects Bolton's soldiers or Brave Companions, and orders Hot Pie to wake the sleeping Gendry and hide their **three horses** behind a partially standing wall. She hides at a **willow** beside the road with a **heavy adult longsword**. Her wish for missing **Needle** is repeated; her current blade is unsuitable for her stature despite being dangerous.
3. **THREE ARMED MEN IDENTIFIED:** The approaching strangers are **Tom Sevenstrings/Tom of Sevenstreams** (singer, axe and throwing knives), **Lem Lemoncloak** (burly man in a worn yellow cloak, sword and dirk), and **Anguy the Archer** (young, freckled red-haired bowman with goose-fletched arrows). Arya recognizes that **three adults, especially the skilled archer, outmatch her despite Gendry's mail**. Their initial laughter and claims of helping her cannot by themselves establish benevolent intent.
4. **THE COST OF A VISIBLE SIGIL:** Arya reflexively insists the children are no one's followers, but Tom instantly identifies **Bolton's flayed-man device on her cupbearer's doublet**. This gives away her recent Harrenhal provenance without exposing her birth identity. **Gendry calls himself the Bull**; Hot Pie volunteers his unusual name. Arya chooses the false name **Squab**, although Hot Pie later almost uses **Arry**.
5. **AN UNUSUAL ALLEGIANCE:** The strangers say they are **King Robert's men**, despite Robert having died long ago. The claim is plausible for **a band originally commissioned under Robert's authority**, but **there is no parchment or living king's current order displayed here**. They refuse to name allegiance to Joffrey, Stannis or Robb. Arya appropriately wonders what kind of king's men have neither horses nor uniforms.
6. **RIVERRUN GOAL LEAKED:** Hot Pie impulsively says they are trying to reach **Riverrun**. Tom calls it a long trip upriver and proposes a meal at **Sharna's nearby inn**, describing an allegedly decent husband and an orphan helper. Arya realizes repeatedly that friendliness may be manipulative, but hunger makes the offer difficult to reject.
7. **ANGUY'S CONTROLLED WARNING SHOT:** Anguy fires an arrow **within an inch of Arya's head into the willow** before she can react, then instantly draws another. He tells her he did **not miss**. Arya now knows from firsthand experience that his bow can defeat her attempted sword intimidation. This is a **direct intimidation demonstration and marksmanship proof**, not a harmless missed shot.
8. **THEY TRAVEL UPSTREAM:** The children follow the band toward the inn, initially trying to maintain a dozen paces' separation. Tom sings, and Hot Pie unexpectedly sings well too. Anguy brings down **a duck at a small brook** with an accurate arrow; Lem retrieves it. These mundane details establish their ability to forage and **lure suspicious people through informal charm**.
9. **THE SAME INN AS JAIME'S CHAPTER:** The inn has **a low riverside structure, whitewashed upper floor, slate roof, arbor, garden, outbuildings and dock**, and its sign shows **a kneeling king**. This matches **the Inn of the Kneeling Man in [ASOS 11 Jaime](11-jaime.md)**. Unlike Jaime and Brienne's previous arrival, the hearth now smokes and the supposed proprietors are active. **Two separated POV threads physically converge on the same site**.
10. **SHARNA, HUSBAND AND THE CROSSBOW BOY:** **Sharna** is a tall, stern woman who directs the household and cooks **rabbit**. **Husband**, a short pockmarked man in an apron, is the same self-described inn occupier Jaime met; a crossbow-carrying **teenage orphan ward** serves food. Sharna takes the vegetables and offers ale, explaining that **the river water is polluted by battlefield corpses**. Their willingness to feed children is real, but their wider group's horse scheme is also real.
11. **GENDRY'S PRACTICAL SUSPICION:** Gendry chooses to **stay with and guard the horses** instead of eating immediately. Arya spots a **boat at the dock** and fantasizes stealing it to go upstream to Riverrun, but Gendry asks about **wrong winds, rowing against river currents and capsizing**. Her idea is **not attempted**, and his doubts arise from basic river mechanics.
12. **CLEAR CROSS-POV CONFIRMATION OF A ROBBERY SETUP:** Husband says he **sold three horses for three gold dragons and received a skiff**, accurately matching **Brienne's exchange in [ASOS 11 Jaime](11-jaime.md)**. He complains Tom, Lem and Anguy were **supposed to recover the horses** from Brienne's group after Husband directed them onto the woodland road. Lem and Tom insist they **did not encounter the travelers**. This **direct dialogue confirms the suspicious innkeeper did intend to send Jaime, Brienne and Cleos toward confederates**, strengthening Brienne's earlier caution from hypothesis to a source-grounded conspiracy about reclaiming the horses. The band did not actually seize those earlier horses.
13. **A LARGE DOCUMENTARY DETAIL:** Husband had said in Jaime's chapter his **wife was absent**; now says Sharna was away near **Lambswold helping Fern give birth**. This is a specific retrospective explanation for the two chapters' differing house occupants, **not proof that Fern's newborn was fathered by Tom or Anguy**, which Husband jokes about without evidence.
14. **HOT PIE'S PRACTICAL ABILITY:** The orphan serves **badly kneaded, scorched bread**, and Hot Pie immediately diagnoses the likely dough problem, boasting he could bake better. His genuine baking knowledge, singing and capacity for social contact are **skills Arya tends to underestimate** because she thinks of him as frightened and clumsy.
15. **PAYMENT AS FORCE:** The Brotherhood says they need the children's three horses to operate faster and offers a **folded parchment IOU for three gold dragons**, with repayment **after the war**. Hot Pie expects metal coins and discovers only written promises. Tom claims this is requisition **for the realm**, not theft. Arya rightly judges they will **take the horses regardless of her consent**. The transaction's claimed future reimbursement is **not cash payment and is not honored in the chapter**.
16. **ARYA COUNTERS WITH A BOAT BARGAIN:** Arya offers to exchange the horses for **the docked skiff plus instruction in sailing it**, rather than accept worthless-looking paper. Their startled laughter demonstrates they know **her practical proposal is unsuitable**, but the scene ends before any contract, transfer or boat lesson. This is negotiation under coercive disparity, not a completed barter.
17. **NEW ARMED GROUP ARRIVES:** Gendry bursts in to report **around a dozen mounted soldiers** coming along the river road. Sharna and Tom are unworried, revealing they expect compatriots rather than enemy troops. Arya, scarred by Lorch/Clegane captors, tries to pull her sword; **Lem grabs her wrist**. She smashes a tankard into his face and **breaks his nose**, briefly escapes, but he holds her again. **Tom blocks Gendry with a dagger**. These men have now used **real physical restraint** against a child, even while Sharna says they are protecting her.
18. **MEN WHO CAN BE NAMED:** The arriving fighters include a towering **Tyroshi with a green-dyed beard**, a **one-eyed fighter**, a **wounded man supported by crossbowmen**, a limping older man, a Braavosi sellsword, and others. This shows a **multiregional armed band**, but not all individual names or their commander are supplied in this chapter.
19. **HARWIN RECOGNIZES ARYA:** Among them Arya spots **Harwin, son of Hullen**, who cared for ponies at Winterfell and knew her family. Her intense plea to him recalls specific Winterfell life and forces him to look beyond **the Bolton doublet and the many assumed names**. He recognizes **Arya Underfoot**, addresses her as **Eddard Stark's daughter** and **kneels**. This is a genuine **firsthand identity validation by a childhood acquaintance**, stronger than Arya's own unsupported claim to noble status.
20. **ENDING POSITION:** The trio is **inside a Brotherhood-associated inn** with the horses effectively subject to their control; Arya has been recognized by a friendly old Stark household man, but **no escort to Riverrun, release or formal resolution of their status occurs before this chapter ends**. The rediscovery of Arya's identity is a psychological breakthrough, not a completed return to her family.

## Competing theories and tests

- **The Brotherhood is both rescue and predation:** It claims Robert's old authority and protects the weak, yet uses intimidation, forced requisition and misleading routing through innkeeper allies. Evaluate this as a **resource-poor armed movement with conflicting practices**, not a flawless moral alternative to Lannister/Bolton cruelty.
- **Perfect cross-POV consistency:** [ASOS 11 Jaime](11-jaime.md) shows the danger of the innkeeper's suggested forest road as **Brienne's inference**; this chapter's conversation establishes **the planned horse recovery**. Use as a model for how Martin confirms a small clue after a viewpoint shift.
- **Harwin's appearance:** A Stark household survivor inside this network creates a possible **information channel** to Robb or Catelyn, but no raven or successful reunion has yet occurred.
- **Singing / social disguise:** Tom's performance disarms Hot Pie and makes contact possible while Anguy's arrow guarantees compliance. The same broad motif of **singer access** appears with Mance and Symon, but **no direct coordination exists between them**.
- **Riverrun:** Arya finds the main river, not a proven route home. The Brotherhood's horses and boat override her logistical autonomy at the close.
