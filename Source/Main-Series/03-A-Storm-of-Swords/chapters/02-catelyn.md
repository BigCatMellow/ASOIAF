## Chronological causal ledger

1. **AFTERMATH OF JAIME'S RELEASE:** Catelyn has deliberately enabled Jaime's departure and expects political punishment. **Ser Desmond Grell**, Edmure's appointed castellan, and **Utherydes Wayn**, Hoster's steward, arrive with grief and reluctance; they initially propose explaining her decision as **a mother's madness caused by news of Bran and Rickon**. This is a proposed social excuse, **not a medically established loss of reason**.
2. **CATELYN REFUSES TO CLAIM MADNESS:** She insists she **knew she was committing treason**, and alone authorized freeing Robb's captive. She invites formal imprisonment in Jaime's empty fetters because allowing her to escape consequence would imply the castle's officers colluded. Her acceptance of responsibility is direct and significant, even as the original false reports of Bran and Rickon's deaths (`ACOK 56/69`) remain uncorrected in her information.
3. **HOUSEHOLD AUTHORITY AND SENTENCE:** Desmond compromises: she is **confined within Lord Hoster's chambers**, allowed to visit the **sept**, denied unrestricted castle movement, and pledges not to escape. Hoster remains **legally Lord of Riverrun** while bedridden, with Edmure commanding military operations in his place. The gentle confinement is real deprivation of liberty, not a dungeon ordeal.
4. **THE EXCHANGE IS CONTESTED IMMEDIATELY:** Utherydes tells Catelyn **Desmond has sent Ser Robin Ryger after Jaime** to recapture or kill him. `ASOS 1 Jaime` corroborates that Ryger pursued with a galley and fired arrows, only to lose the vessel to Brienne's rockfall. This illustrates **collapsing authority inside Riverrun**: Catelyn's order binds one crew, the acting castellan's order sends another.
5. **A SECOND UNDERMINING BY FUTURE ORDERS:** Catelyn watches the **Red Fork and Tumblestone** from her father's chambers, dreading Ryger's return; an approaching boat would mean Jaime captured and her daughters' exchange jeopardized. She cannot directly learn the results of the galley battle, so her repeated silence and watching remain **lack of evidence**, not confirmation he escaped every subsequent danger.
6. **HOSTER'S UNFAMILIAR WORD:** Dying Hoster Tully repeatedly asks forgiveness of **'Tansy'**, refers to **blood** and to future **trueborn sons or babes**, and appears to think he is talking to another woman. Catelyn cannot identify Tansy from her childhood and at first speculates about a servant or unacknowledged lover. These guesses are **her interpretations**, not Hoster's clear statement of identity.
7. **MEDICAL AND HOUSEHOLD NEGATIVE EVIDENCE:** **Maester Vyman** does not know a Tansy at Riverrun, misremembers an unrelated cobbler widow's name, and after consulting **Utherydes Wayn** confirms no woman known by that name served in the household in his time. The absence of a known servant weakens Catelyn's first assumption, but **does not prove 'Tansy' is a woman's given name rather than a euphemism, nickname, herb or code word**.
8. **LYSA'S HISTORY COMES INTO VIEW:** Catelyn remembers Lysa experiencing **five miscarriages after marriage** (three in King's Landing, two at the Eyrie), the sisters' joint wartime marriage, and Lysa's elation when her moon blood ceased shortly afterward. She had assumed the bleeding that followed merely meant **late menses**. Now Hoster's guilt prompts Catelyn to suspect **an earlier pregnancy** and that Hoster imposed a hurtful decision relating to that child. The actual nature, conception and ending of that earlier pregnancy are **not narrated in Catelyn's first-person scene**.
9. **POLITICAL MOTIVE POSSIBLE:** Catelyn recalls **Jon Arryn's age, earlier childless marriages and deaths of male heirs**, and concludes her father may have **forced an alliance marriage on Jon Arryn** on the condition that Lysa marry into House Arryn despite premarital scandal. She interprets Lysa's distress when holding infant Robb and Hoster's dying guilt as support. These are **well-developed deductions by Catelyn**, but neither Lysa nor Jon personally confirms the account in this chapter.
10. **DISTINGUISH ONE DEDUCTION FROM ANOTHER:** Catelyn wonders whether Hoster might have impregnated a woman named Tansy; later she thinks **Tansy may connect instead to Lysa's lost child and imposed marriage**. The latter is substantially better connected to the repeated word **'trueborn'**, Lysa's prior bleeding and Lord Arryn's need for a fertile bride, but **Tansy's referent and exact medical event remain unresolved**. No source-grounded basis for claiming Hoster fathered Lysa's child exists here.
11. **VYMAN CONCEALS ROBB NEWS:** A **raven from the west** arrives; Vyman hesitates to share its contents under Desmond's orders and eventually says **Robb suffered a wound while taking the Crag**, described in Robb's letter as **not serious**. Catelyn remains unconvinced since the wound site, severity and care are not disclosed. His evasiveness is directly observed; **do not assert that Robb is dying or that the true concealed news is definitely his marriage**, neither of which is present in her received details.
12. **CATELYN APPEALS TO LYSA:** Catelyn writes Lysa requesting a visit or letter forgiving Hoster before death. She discusses the reported Stark losses, suggests an escort from the Vale, and reflects that **a reply may arrive too late**. Writing is **completed and entrusted to Vyman**, but **sending, delivery and Lysa's receipt** are not independently witnessed.
13. **EDMURE RETURNS FROM RED FORK:** Edmure comes back with **several hundred riders under Tully banners**. He is physically **pale, exhausted and worried**, although his army turned back Tywin's attempted crossings. He tells Catelyn about **Stannis's defeat at King's Landing** and the alliance of **Highgarden with Joffrey**. He also claims **Dorne has declared for Joffrey**, a **character report that must be tested against actual Martell actions**, not a witnessed Dorne decision in this chapter.
14. **THE CORE AUTHORITY DISPUTE:** Edmure charges Catelyn released a **prisoner Robb entrusted to him**. She says she acted under a **mother's right** to seek daughters, relying more on **Tyrion's public hostage exchange pledge** than Jaime's moral character. Edmure argues Cersei will not release her Stark hostages and Tyrion is reportedly dying of a head wound. His gloomy prediction rests on **rumor**, though `ACOK 67 Tyrion` confirmed a severe, survivable facial injury.
15. **THE THREE RAVENS TO ROOSE:** Edmure reveals he has already dispatched **three ravens to Roose Bolton**, saying **Jaime escaped**, concealing Catelyn's authorization, and offering **1,000 golden dragons for Jaime's capture**. He expects any overland or river route to approach **Harrenhal**, now controlled by Bolton's men. This is a **completed direct action** that sabotages the credibility of a *negotiated exchange*: if the Lannisters learn the prisoner supposedly 'escaped', they have a rationale to refuse Brienne's demand for Sansa and Arya. It also risks delivery to a **Lannister-opposed but brutal garrison**.
16. **THE LAST PERSONAL BREAK:** Catelyn is horrified by Edmure's messages, concludes he has drastically lowered her chance of recovering her daughters, and orders him to leave her with their dying father. Her belief that the exchange is now impossible is an **emotionally persuasive risk assessment**, not a demonstrated outcome of all future negotiations. She remains confined; her daughters are not returned.

## Competing hypotheses and verification agenda

- **Tansy:** Candidate readings include a woman, herb, nickname, abortion instrument or memory fragment. Hoster's combination of blood, forgiveness and future trueborn children plus Lysa's early distress supports a **coerced pregnancy outcome** hypothesis. **Nothing in this chapter unambiguously identifies the child, father, procedure or what 'Tansy' denotes**; preserve ranked alternatives and look for later Lysa/Hoster testimony.
- **The west raven:** Robb's 'small wound' may be paired with unreported strategic or marriage news. Vyman's refusal alone cannot certify content beyond what he states.
- **Dorne's alleged commitment:** Differentiate a negotiated Myrcella betrothal, an announced alliance and an actual **Martell army or political submission**. Edmure's 'all the south' is sweeping wartime rhetoric, not an audited diplomatic map.
- **Hostage logistics:** Arya is not in Cersei's custody, making **two-girl exchange structurally impossible** even before Edmure sends the ravens. Tyrion's probable recovery may save one negotiator while making the original promise no less inaccurate.
- **Bolton:** Edmure's bounty sends actionable intelligence and incentives to a garrison known to be **persecuting former Lannister associates and nominally under Robb**; no future interception is yet shown.
