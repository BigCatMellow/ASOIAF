## Chronological plot / political economy and knowledge

1. **King's Landing's riverfront remains devastated.** Tyrion inspects mud, burned bones and makeshift trade near the city walls with **Bronn**. He knows his Blackwater measures destroyed much of the district and accepts responsibility for rebuilding, though he is angry the poor return so soon. He authorizes removing new huts only if they obstruct fortification security; Bronn proposes mass killing and is rejected.
2. **Kevan's family crisis redirects government work.** A raven reports **Willem Lannister's murder** (seen in [ASOS 20 Catelyn](20-catelyn.md)). His twin **Martyn** remains captive to Robb and Lancel's battle wound festers. Kevan is paralyzed by grief, making Tyrion the reluctant supervisor of repairs and finance.
3. **Littlefinger's Vale mission has progressed.** Tyrion says Petyr **sailed north a fortnight earlier** and imagines him already bedding Lysa. **The sailing is narrative report; the marriage and control of the Vale are speculation**, advancing [ASOS 19 Tyrion](19-tyrion.md) beyond authorization but not verifying success.
4. **War machines become a safety risk.** The **Three Whores** trebuchets stand idle by the Mud Gate while children climb them and throw filth at Tyrion. He briefly asks Bronn to arrange guards, then retracts the concern in anger. This demonstrates his shifting attitude toward civilians rather than an actual security deployment.
5. **The city's food prices expose scarcity despite imports.** Tyrion notes Tyrell food shipments yet gives concrete high prices: **six coppers for a melon**, **one silver stag for a bushel of corn**, **one gold dragon for a side of beef or six thin piglets**. Markets are crowded, but beggars are hungry. These are local observations, not a quantified inflation series.
6. **Sansa's distress continues after nonconsummation.** Tyrion thinks the court knows she remains untouched and suspects **Cersei's bedmaids or Varys's network** of leaking. He and Sansa sleep together clothed now; he longs for willing tenderness but also openly desires Winterfell and sexual access. Her forced marriage has not become a voluntary relationship.
7. **The marriage was leaked before Sansa heard.** In recollection, Tyrion says Shae knew the wedding from a page's report to **Ser Tallad**, allegedly originating with a servant overhearing **Kevan speaking to Tywin**. This chain may be mistaken but illustrates how 'secret' court politics escapes intended audiences.
8. **Shae's apparent indifference troubles Tyrion.** When he personally tells her of the wedding, Shae says Sansa is a little girl and predicts Tyrion will conceive a child with her, then return. Tyrion wants reassurance and mistrusts whether he can be loved. **His reading of Shae's motives is not independently verified.**
9. **Tyrion meets Symon Silver Tongue clandestinely.** Bronn leads him through a maze near Visenya's hill to a low wine cellar. Tyrion brings **thirty gold dragons** and offers funding for a long Free Cities tour as payment for the singer leaving. The premise is relocation rather than murder at the meeting's start.
10. **Symon demonstrates a blackmail song.** He performs a composition about a secret lover and a **chain and keep**, with a refrain about golden hands and warm women's hands. Symon suggests Cersei or Tywin might enjoy hearing it. This is an **intentional extortion threat** aimed at Tyrion's secret arrangement with Shae, not proof the singer knows every practical detail.
11. **The demand is access to the royal wedding.** Symon requests entry to Cersei's **seven-singer** contest for a gilded lute, naming performers including **Galyeon of Cuy, Bethany Fair-fingers, Aemon Costayne, Alaric of Eysen, Hamish the Harper, Collio Quaynis, and Orland of Oldtown**. He hints one existing performer could be incapacitated. The seven/sacred-numerology motif provides an immediate scheduling obstacle.
12. **Tyrion promises an invitation but secretly orders removal.** After meeting the singer, he instructs Bronn to invent an injury to **Hamish the Harper** after three days, lure Symon to be fitted for court clothes and ensure the singer's **body is never found**, possibly taking his tongue. **An order to kill and conceal is verified; execution and disposal are not shown here.**
13. **Bronn names a grim disposal possibility.** He mentions a Flea Bottom seller of 'bowls of brown' containing uncertain meat; Tyrion warns Bronn never to let him eat there. **No direct scene shows Symon being cooked or consumed**, so that theory stays unproven at this checkpoint.
14. **Tywin is preparing a symbolic royal gift.** Master **Tobho Mott** presents a light black-and-red **Valyrian steel longsword** for Joffrey, with gold and rubies; a second, wider and longer blade is intended for **Jaime**. Tyrion receives neither. The blades have separate fullers (Joffrey's two, Jaime's three) and related but nonidentical dimensions.
15. **The metal's origin is not yet declared.** Tyrion marvels at two Valyrian blades and recalls House Lannister lost **Brightroar** to a King Tommen's doomed Valyria expedition; his uncle **Gerion** disappeared pursuing the sword years later. He wonders where Tywin found such metal. **Do not assert Ice has already been named as the source in this chapter.**
16. **Mott describes resistance to recoloring.** He attempted to infuse Lannister crimson and used many spells, but **some folds would not accept the color** and the red repeatedly darkened into black-and-blood ripples. The physical pattern is observed; whether the steel literally remembers, as he says, is an **attributed artisan belief**, not verified sentience.
17. **Tywin prefers heraldic red and gem quality.** He demands rubies over garnets for the scabbard and rejects the need to rework the swords. His purpose is to counter Stannis's reputation for a magical weapon with a spectacular royal display. **No proven magical equivalence** between the blades is demonstrated.
18. **Port repairs have a concrete list.** Tyrion says the Blackwater must be dredged and sunken ships cleared; three-quarters of quays need repair, the fish market is destroyed, and both **River Gate and King's Gate** are damaged. The river's reopening affects trade, logistics and crown solvency.
19. **The crown's apparent income conceals crushing debt.** Tywin cites Littlefinger's accounts showing revenues **ten times Aerys-era levels**. Tyrion says expenses and interest on borrowed money are similarly enormous, including debt to **House Lannister**, and asks whether Tywin will forgive that debt. Tywin refuses. The figures are claims from reviewed accounts, not an independent audit of Littlefinger's books.
20. **Wedding expenditure is a political signal.** Tyrion protests **seventy-seven courses** and about **1,000 guests**, alongside wildfire and chain invoices; Tywin insists extravagance demonstrates royal wealth to the Reach and realm. He threatens to replace Tyrion as master of coin if he cannot pay. Neither financing source nor completed waterfront works is shown here.
21. **Tywin states his reproductive goal.** Hearing that Tyrion has not consummated the Sansa marriage, Tywin demands it to consolidate the Winterfell claim and produce a child. He explicitly says an **unconsummated marriage may be set aside**. This confirms that the previous chapter's nonconsummation is a **legal/political weakness acknowledged by the very strategist who arranged it**.
22. **Tyrion cites formal annulment hurdles.** He claims a **High Septon or Council of Faith** must set such a marriage aside and doubts the present High Septon would oppose the crown. That is Tyrion's practical/legal prediction, not a judicial ruling or permanent protection from later regime change.
23. **The Cersei–Willas fallback has failed.** Tywin reports **Mace Tyrell initially receptive**, then abruptly refusing the offer for Cersei to marry Willas, apparently after **Olenna's intervention**. He orders Tyrion never to tell Cersei a proposal was made. The refusal is a **Tywin report**, Olenna's words come secondhand through Varys, and Cersei has not accepted a different match yet.
24. **Bowen Marsh sends news from Castle Black.** Pycelle presents Marsh's plea: Jeor Mormont sent a message from the haunted forest reporting attack, then **other ravens returned without letters**; Marsh fears Mormont and much of the Watch lost and a wildling assault imminent. Compare [ASOS 18 Samwell](18-samwell.md): **two warning ravens were dispatched** and later birds released empty. The council has a real warning but does **not** have an exact reliable full casualty list or knowledge of Sam's Other kill.
25. **Tywin dismisses the Watch's neutral appeal.** Marsh asks all **five kings** for aid; Tywin refuses the legitimacy of rival kings and denies soldiers while rebels remain. His hostility comes from crown-war priorities rather than an established ability to evaluate the supernatural threat.
26. **Tywin plans to capture the elective Watch office.** Expecting Mormont may be dead, he wants **Janos Slynt** chosen Lord Commander, with threat of withholding reinforcements if the Watch elects another. **Pycelle is ordered to send a suggestive royal letter**, not a literal direct appointment. The Watch's election is not shown and Mormont's death remains unconfirmed to the council.
27. **He rejects more independent northern commanders.** Tyrion suggests the Shadow Tower or Eastwatch leaders; Tywin objects to a **Mallister of Seagard** and an **ironman**, judging them less pliable. Slynt's weakness and willingness to sell his allegiance are advantages in Tywin's design.
28. **The episode ends in pointed irony.** Tyrion recognizes that sending Slynt to the Wall made him a candidate for manipulation and recalls the difference between earlier failures to handle this appointment and his new willingness to have Symon quietly murdered. **The chapter ends with orders**, not proof that either scheme succeeds.

## Serious alternative explanations / research tests

- **Two Valyrian swords:** A repurposed named ancestral sword is plausible given scarcity and the odd recoloring. But the chapter never identifies the parent blade. Locate later confession/description of its provenance before updating the claim from **THEORY** to fact.
- **Littlefinger's financial genius or hidden insolvency?** Tenfold revenues do not mean a solvent treasury when interest and spending soar. Test actual debt instruments and counterparties instead of trusting either Tywin's confidence or Tyrion's frustration alone.
- **Symon's fate:** Tyrion gives a clear murder directive. If later accounts do not verify the killing, mark status **ORDERED / UNKNOWN EXECUTION**, not a personally witnessed death here.
- **The Watch's information:** Marsh has a genuine Mormont warning but lacks battle details. Tywin treats the crisis as political leverage and misunderstands scale of threat, with an alternative that he cannot divert men during an unfinished rebellion. His election interference remains a contingent plan.
- **Tyrion and Sansa:** Tyrion rejects forced sex yet orders killing to protect Shae; sympathetic behavior in one context does not excuse other agency. The marriage can be publicly real yet politically vulnerable if unconsummated.
