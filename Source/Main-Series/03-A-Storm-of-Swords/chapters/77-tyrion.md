# ASOS 77 — Tyrion: Tysha's history corrected, betrayal of Jaime's trust, and the deaths of Shae and Tywin

## Chronological and causal record

1. **Tyrion awaits execution in the black cells.** He has been deprived of ordinary daylight and timekeeping and expects his death; he is unsure whether a public execution or private murder is planned. He fears dying humiliated. These are thoughts, not a witnessed execution order.
2. **Jaime opens the cell well after midnight.** He is visibly thin, one-handed, and reconciles with Tyrion joking briefly about their injuries. Jaime says **Cersei intends to behead Tyrion the next day on the old tourney grounds**. This is Jaime's knowledge of a planned execution, not a sentence spoken aloud in Tyrion's preceding trial POV.
3. **Four guards are unconscious, not killed by the extraction team as described.** Jaime says **Varys administered sweetsleep in their wine**, carefully dosed for unconsciousness; their eventual recovery is **Jaime/Varys testimony and not yet seen**. One turnkey lies on the floor when Tyrion leaves.
4. **Jaime and Varys coordinated a physical evacuation route.** Jaime explains the route through underground passages to the **river**, then a **waiting galley in the bay**, with **Varys's Free Cities agents** intended to provide money and protection. Ship identity, destination and eventual boarding are **not shown before the chapter ends**.
5. **Jaime identifies a long-hidden debt to Tyrion.** He first tries to avoid explaining it; Tyrion insists because he recognizes Jaime's fear and unusual solemnity.
6. **Jaime explicitly confesses that Tysha was not a paid prostitute.** Tysha was actually a **crofter's daughter** who fell in love or married Tyrion as she appeared to; Jaime **never hired her** and had **lied on Tywin's orders**. This conclusively overturns the Lannister family story Tyrion has carried for years, though **Tysha's private motives are still not available through her own POV**.
7. **Tywin rationalized his deception as a caste lesson.** Jaime reports Tywin argued that a lowborn girl wishing to marry a Lannister must want money and therefore was morally equivalent to a prostitute. This is **class-based inference posing as fact**, not proof Tysha agreed to sale of sexual access.
8. **The revelation also corrects a grievous sexual assault.** Tyrion remembers Tywin forcing Tysha into a barracks full of guards and compelling Tyrion not merely to watch but **to participate in her rape**. Jaime says he did not know Tywin would do this. Distinguish Jaime's real false statement from Tywin's later abuse and Tyrion's forced participation.
9. **Tyrion strikes Jaime and severs trust.** His blow knocks the kneeling Jaime down; Jaime accepts the rebuke and unlocks another gate. Tyrion takes the keys and refuses to continue alongside him. Jaime's rescue therefore comes with an emotionally catastrophic confession rather than reconciliation.
10. **Jaime asks whether Tyrion really killed Joffrey.** Tyrion first asserts a theory that **Joffrey stole Robert's Valyrian dagger and hired Bran's catspaw**. This remains **Tyrion's argument; no catspaw confession, payment or order is witnessed**.
11. **Tyrion deliberately lies to inflict pain on Jaime.** He tells him **Cersei has slept with Lancel and Osmund Kettleblack** (and explicitly speculates about Moon Boy) and **claims he killed Joffrey**. His private thought after Jaime leaves is that he wanted to call him back and explain **the confession was untrue**. **Do not cite this as a reliable admission of regicide.** The precise truth of each accusation concerning Cersei is not established solely by Tyrion's taunt.
12. **Varys waits disguised as a septon.** He says Cersei had him under surveillance during the trial, and that Jaime persuaded him to assist. Neither claim proves his ultimate political loyalties; his practical role in the escape is directly witnessed.
13. **Varys claims he cannot locate Sansa or Dontos.** His agents last saw the pair on the **serpentine steps**; he says thereafter his little birds are silent. The reader's Sansa POV has already shown **Littlefinger murdered Dontos offshore and took Sansa to the Fingers**, making this a significant gap in Varys's claimed network or an unverified concealment.
14. **The Red Keep has a fourth level of dungeons.** Varys attributes a four-tier design to **Maegor the Cruel**, distinguishing public/common criminal cells, highborn holding cells, black cells and a deeper torture level. This is **Varys's informed architectural explanation**.
15. **The deepest passages converge beneath the Tower of the Hand.** Tyrion and Varys reach a chamber with **five barred doors, an opening above, dragon-head brazier and red/black mosaic of a three-headed dragon**. Tyrion recognizes the location from Shae's earlier description of secret entrances.
16. **The secret route to the Hand's bedchamber is precisely described.** Varys says **230 ladder rungs upward**, a **left turn**, approximately **60 feet of narrow passage**, and a concealed **third door**. Tyrion chooses to climb it despite Varys's objections. A human of ordinary height would have to crawl where Tyrion can walk upright.
17. **A spy passage passes near guards speaking openly.** Tyrion hears **Lum and Lester**, two of Tywin's men, discussing Tyrion's execution and Shae; their ordinary conversation demonstrates how the architecture can support concealed eavesdropping. It does **not show Varys himself listening at that moment**.
18. **The hidden entrance opens in the bedchamber's fireplace.** Tyrion manipulates an iron hook, passes carefully through warm embers and enters the chamber previously occupied by him and now by Tywin. The door and route are **real architecture**, not a literal sorcerous gateway.
19. **Shae is in Tywin's bed.** She is nude except for **the Hand's linked golden-hands chain around her throat**. She expresses fear that **Cersei forced her earlier testimony** and that **Tywin terrifies her**; these are **her spoken statements**, while the narrator offers no independent Shae viewpoint to confirm the full scope of threats or why she is in the bed.
20. **Tyrion's questions seek genuine intimacy amid betrayal.** He asks whether Shae ever enjoyed his touch. Her reuse of the private 'giant of Lannister' phrase, already turned against Tyrion at trial, enrages him. Do not present this reaction as moral justification for her death.
21. **Tyrion kills Shae by strangling her with the golden chain.** The act is unambiguous **intentional homicide**, performed by Tyrion; her hands struggle against him. **No self-defense necessity is shown**, regardless of Shae's false testimony, coercion or the circumstances of her being with Tywin.
22. **The murder realizes an earlier singer's ominous chain imagery without proving magic.** The **golden-hands chain** is the murder weapon, linking political office, the warning song and betrayed intimacy. This is a strong narrative motif, not evidence the singer foretold the killing supernaturally.
23. **Tyrion takes Tywin's dagger and crossbow.** He arms himself from the bedchamber, loading a quarrel by bracing the crossbow with foot and stirrup. The weapon's slow reload matters, but there is **no firefight with the two hallway guards**.
24. **Tywin is found on the privy.** He tells Tyrion he still intends to send him to the Wall and says **Mace Tyrell's consent prevented an earlier immediate change of punishment**. This is a **claim made during confrontation**, not proof Tywin intended to pardon Tyrion sincerely.
25. **Tyrion demands Tysha's fate.** Tywin claims not to remember where she was sent and repeatedly demeans her. His ignorance is **his assertion**, not evidence she died or of her actual later destination.
26. **The killing is deliberate and preannounced.** Tyrion threatens to shoot if Tywin again uses the degrading label; Tywin does, Tyrion fires and **the bolt strikes low in his torso**, gravely wounding him. His father's refusal to take the threat seriously helps cause the immediate escalation; it does not erase Tyrion's agency.
27. **Tywin dies directly on page.** His last exchange disowns Tyrion verbally, and Tyrion counters that he resembles his father; **Tywin's bowels release as he dies**. **No revival, concealment or uncertainty about Tywin's death is left by this POV**.
28. **End state:** Jaime's rescue and Varys's passage create the means of Tyrion's escape, but **Tyrion has not yet reached the waiting galley on page**. His relationship with Jaime is broken, Shae and Tywin are dead, Tysha's survival/location remain **unknown**, and a new power vacuum is created at the center of the Lannister regime.
