## Chronology and source-evidence audit

1. **Jon's escape has succeeded physically, at substantial cost.** Continuing from [ASOS 41 Jon](41-jon.md), he forces an exhausted horse along the **northbound kingsroad** despite a festering arrow wound in his **right thigh**. Each remount reopens it; fever and exhaustion cause him to ride the wrong direction twice. He has not simply recovered after removing the arrow.
2. **He warns Mole's Town before reaching Castle Black.** At the mostly **underground village**, Jon wakes stable boys, demands a replacement **black gelding, saddle and bridle**, accepts food/wine, and explicitly orders warning and evacuation northward because wildlings have crossed the Wall. **This is a direct successful alarm delivered to civilians**, not evidence all villagers heed it.
3. **Mole's Town's small visible brothel and red lantern remain geographic markers.** They illustrate the settlement's underground nature and potential vulnerability if surprised; no sex-work interaction occurs in this stop.
4. **Jon arrives at Castle Black shortly before dawn.** He rides past empty outer buildings and an apparently unguarded approach, observes abandoned courtyards and old fire damage to the **Lord Commander's Tower**, and sees a tiny chimney plume from the armory. **He has reached the castle** in this chapter, unlike at the end of ASOS 41.
5. **Donal Noye is the first recognized brother.** The one-armed smith receives him with surprise and skepticism, having heard **Jarman Buckwell's scouts saw Jon with Mance's column about a fortnight earlier**. Jon confirms the appearance but says he acted on **Qhorin Halfhand's last command**. Neither a formal court nor full public vindication of alleged desertion is performed.
6. **The Watch has been diverted by multiple feints.** Noye reports **Harma** at Woodswatch-by-the-Pool, **Rattleshirt** at Long Barrow, **the Weeper** near Icemark, climbers near Queensgate, raiders hacking Greyguard's gate and pressure against Eastwatch. Their movements are reports through Watch patrols; Jon correctly **infers a deliberate dispersal strategy** to leave Castle Black unguarded. Mance's private order is not quoted.
7. **Castellanship is functionally vacant.** **Bowen Marsh** has left to manage widespread threats. Officially **Ser Wynton Stout**, an elderly former ranger, is left as castellan but has apparently forgotten the assignment. Noye acts as the practical commander through competence rather than formally assuming the Lord Commandership.
8. **The remaining garrison is tiny.** Noye says only **forty-odd** remain: disabled veterans, the infirm and trainees. Jon says **Styr has 100 Thenns plus about 20 raiders**, wearing bronze and some steel. Those are his immediate tactical numbers, not the size of the whole free-folk army.
9. **Jon distinguishes a place-name-derived rank.** Noye assumes Magnar is a Skagos lord's name; **Aemon** notes that in the older tongue **magnar is a title meaning lord** and Jon identifies Styr as Magnar of **Thenn** in the far Frostfangs. This is important for accurate categorization of free-folk politics.
10. **Ghost did not arrive at Castle Black.** Noye explicitly says there has been **no sign** of the white direwolf, resolving Jon's earlier hope of a successful warning **only up to the time of this conversation**, not proving Ghost dead or gone permanently.
11. **Aemon's medical intervention is invasive and urgent.** The hundred-plus-year-old blind maester and Clydas remove the soiled bandage, see pus and blood, administer **milk of the poppy** and **cauterize Jon's infected wound with a red-hot blade**, physically holding him still with Noye's help. The treatment is witnessed; future freedom from infection is not yet established.
12. **Aemon and Noye already know Jeor was murdered.** **Approximately a dozen survivors**, including **Grenn, Dolorous Edd and Giant**, returned from Craster's Keep and told the story. This confirms the warning has traveled to Castle Black **before Jon's arrival**, contrary to a theory that no intelligence has reached the Watch.
13. **Mormont's death remains a shock to Jon.** He learns Sworn Brothers murdered the Lord Commander; Noye names **Garth of Oldtown, Ollo Lophand, Dirk** among mutineers, though **ASOS 33 Samwell firsthand identifies Ollo as Jeor's stabber and Garth of Greenaway among violent mutineers**, while Garth of Oldtown was himself killed. **CRITICAL:** this discrepancy is a real **reported-name error or confused transmission**, not proof the entire mutiny is fabricated.
14. **The Watch must elect another Lord Commander.** **Bowen Marsh** serves temporarily until a **choosing**. Jon considers **Cotter Pyke** at Eastwatch and **Denys Mallister** at the Shadow Tower, but knows they mutually dislike each other; he understands the Mallister suspicion of ironborn. **No election is held in this chapter**; Tywin's projected Janos Slynt interference in [ASOS 32 Tyrion](32-tyrion.md) is a separate ongoing threat.
15. **Grenn personally confirms Sam killed an Other.** He tells Jon he witnessed Sam use the **dragonglass dagger** and explains the 'Sam the Slayer' epithet. This **corroborates ASOS 18 Samwell's event** through a second named witness, but it does **not** prove Sam's present survival to Grenn.
16. **Grenn says Sam was left with dying Mormont.** The dozen escaped while **Dirk, Ollo and others were killing, looting food and sexually assaulting Craster's wives**. Grenn says they could not move Sam, while Edd feared being killed as witnesses. Jon initially experiences this as betrayal; reader knows from **ASOS 46 Samwell** that Sam, Gilly and infant survived and are still north of the Wall at this point.
17. **Noye takes the threat seriously after the warning.** When Jon wakes again, **the Wall's sentries have been told to watch south**, men posted to **Weatherback Ridge** to watch the kingsroad, and **Aemon has sent ravens to Eastwatch and Shadow Tower**. These are reported completed alerts and changed defensive dispositions, not proof the other castles will arrive in time.
18. **The Horn of Winter search report is imprecise.** Drugged and feverish, Jon says Ygritte described **a hundred graves** opened in the Milkwater valley; earlier ASOS 30 Jon recorded her saying **about fifty graves**. This is an **intra-source numerical discrepancy**, potentially explained by altered recollection or rhetorical approximation. The important shared evidence is **they searched and did not find the horn**, not a stable grave count.
19. **Aemon reports that the realm sent the Watch no aid.** Appeals were sent to competing kings, who are preoccupied with wars and claims. This matches the southern council's political indifference; no reinforcements from Tywin or Stannis have yet arrived in this chapter.
20. **Jon learns Winterfell has fallen and his brothers are supposedly dead.** Aemon says Theon ordered the deaths of **Bran and Rickon**, then burned Winterfell; Grenn adds Bolton's bastard killed ironmen and has Theon under torture. **These are received northern reports, not confirmed events.** The reader has already seen Bran alive in ASOS 40, and earlier Theon/Bran POVs directly undermine the reported attribution for Winterfell's burning.
21. **Jon associates Queenscrown's grey direwolf with possible surviving Bran.** He says the wolf **'knew' him**, wonders whether Bran's consciousness may survive in a wolf like **Orell's eagle**, and rejects the simple reported-death conclusion. **Cross-POV [ASOS 40 Bran](40-bran.md) and ASOS 41 Jon show it was Summer controlled by living Bran**, not Bran's disembodied life after his death.
22. **Dreaming expresses double guilt.** The final dream brings **Winterfell's hot pools, Ned's face in a white weirwood and Ygritte's melting flesh/skeleton in red water**, with Jon insisting he will not father a bastard. This is a frightening **dream**, not proof Ygritte died off-page or Jon fathered a child.
23. **End state:** Jon has warned Castle Black, is undergoing severe treatment, and remains bedbound for now; defenders are alerted but greatly outnumbered. The Watch knows the Old Bear's actual fate, while Jon believes misleading news about Bran/Rickon. Styr's attack has not yet reached the castle.

## Competing hypotheses and disconfirmers

- **Was Jon a deserter?** His stated Qhorin orders, actual refusal of civilian murder, and completed return with warning form a coherent infiltration history. Jarman's eyewitness observation of him beside Mance only establishes outward allegiance, not intent; formal Watch resolution is pending.
- **How reliable are raven/message chains?** Surviving brothers have accurately reported a mutiny but one implicated **Garth is misnamed**. Similar named discrepancies should be tracked when evaluating North–Bolton and Battle of Fist intelligence.
- **Can the Watch hold Castle Black?** Jon's actionable warning and Noye's improvised command improve readiness but do not generate fighters. Test terrain and actual garrison deployment against Styr's approach.
- **Horns:** The Ygritte grave tally drift emphasizes how easily a legendary search can accrete differing numbers; **no horn is identified or used** here.
- **Living heirs and skinchanging:** Jon considers Bran's survival as an animal without knowing he literally saw living Bran's Summer. This reader-ahead evidence intensifies the succession misinformation in ASOS 45 Catelyn.
