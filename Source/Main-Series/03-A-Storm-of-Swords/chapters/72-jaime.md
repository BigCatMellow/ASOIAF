## Sequential source record, political implications and constraints

1. **King Tommen signs actual attainders.** With **Kevan Lannister** guiding his pen, Tommen signs **Edmure Tully and Brynden 'Blackfish' Tully's legal deprivation of Riverrun and revenues**. The next royal instrument grants **Riverrun to Emmon Frey and Genna Lannister**. These are **executed court documents on page**, unlike Tywin's earlier future promise; physical control of Riverrun by Brynden still requires separate military verification.
2. **Bolton's succession arrangements are formally authorized.** Tommen signs **legitimization of Roose Bolton's natural son** (Ramsay) and names **Roose Warden of the North**, together with a grant of **Castamere to Ser Rolph Spicer** and royal pardons for **Gawen Westerling, his wife Sybell and daughter Jeyne**, **Jonos Bracken, Vance, Goodbrook and Mooton**. **Signing legal title, receiving pardon and physically controlling territory are different events**.
3. **Tommen is still a small child.** Jaime sees a boy **not yet nine**, nervous and eager to press seals into hot wax. Kevan oversees each document; actual political initiative plainly resides with older family members. Jaime recognizes Tommen as his biological son but has never been publicly called father.
4. **Jaime attempts practical sword training with the wrong hand.** **Ser Addam Marbrand** beat and disarmed him during private sparring with tourney weapons. Jaime knows his instincts do not transfer to the left, and hides this vulnerability from rival Kingsguard brothers. No improvement to his combat ability is demonstrated yet.
5. **Jaime refuses Tywin's 'gift' in principle.** Kevan asks him to mend the dispute with his father; Jaime sees the recent present as an effort to push him toward Rock and a dynastic future he has rejected. He remains committed to Kingsguard command.
6. **A girl billed as Arya Stark departs with Bolton's party.** Jaime sees **Walton Steelshanks** preparing about the returning escort. The girl wears **grey and white**, a **silver wolf-head clasp** and has brown hair, but looks older than Jaime recalls Arya. She says she met him at Winterfell during Robert's visit and claims to be Ned's daughter. She is **fearful and speaks rehearsed pleasure about marrying Ramsay**.
7. **Jaime knows the supposed Arya is counterfeit.** He privately concludes the true Arya may be dead but acknowledges this girl was supplied to Bolton for her ability to claim Stark lineage; later he **explicitly tells Brienne the girl is not Arya**. Because the reader has followed real Arya with Sandor, the imposture is **decisively verified**. **The counterfeit girl's actual original name is not given in this chapter**.
8. **Bolton's side is aware of the false identity.** Jaime tells Brienne **Roose knows the girl is not Arya but finds her useful for the marriage claim**. This is Jaime's direct description of political complicity, not an independent Roose POV testimony; the false marriage itself has **not yet occurred**.
9. **Grey-clad girl is vulnerable in the escort.** Walton has already received money and divided it among his men; the convoy departs under **Nage's peace banner**. The girl's frightened speech and isolation hint at coercion, while her future actions remain unknown.
10. **Gregor remains alive but mortally sick after Oberyn's spear.** Pycelle reports **necrotic flesh, suppuration, convulsions, darkening veins, failed debridement and bread mold/wine treatment**; astonishingly the **leeches die after feeding on him**. This is strong **clinical circumstantial evidence of a toxic substance on Oberyn's spear**, not a laboratory identification, nor a confirmed death of Gregor.
11. **Tywin wants a politically staged execution.** He refuses to detain remaining Dornishmen and commands Pycelle **to keep Gregor alive** so that Gregor can be formally killed by **the King's Justice** and that vengeance shown to **Doran Martell**, rather than have him die from Oberyn's poison. A letter and body have been sent to Doran according to Tywin. **The execution is desired, not completed here**.
12. **Stannis's fleet movements are a significant intelligence gap.** Varys's fishermen near Dragonstone say **a token garrison remains and Salladhor's Lyseni ships are gone**. Pycelle wrongly concludes Stannis gave up and went to Lys; **Tywin correctly treats Stannis as an ongoing threat**, speculating possible landings at Storm's End or Dorne. The actual destination of the fleet is **UNKNOWN from this report**.
13. **Cersei and Tywin clash over Tommen's custody and remarriage.** Cersei resents exclusion from the council and being sent to Casterly Rock; she fears Tywin will force her to remarry for alliance. Tyrells demand **Margaery be wed to Tommen**, who is much younger. These are ongoing struggles; **neither match nor Cersei's departure is yet enacted in this chapter**.
14. **Jaime acknowledges Tommen's biological paternity privately.** He notes how Cersei repeatedly forbade him to behave like a father in Robert's presence, so he never built ordinary bonds with the children. This shapes his resistance to her appeal: he calls Tommen his 'seed' and says the boy has never called him father.
15. **Jaime revisits the Bran catspaw theory.** He asks Cersei whether she ordered the assassin using the Valyrian dagger. She denies it, while confirming she had wished Bran would die and reporting **Robert drunkenly compared killing suffering animals to allowing crippled children to live**. Jaime reasons **Joffrey, eager to win the approval of the man he believed was his father**, could have acted on Robert's remark. This parallels Tyrion's hypothesis in ASOS 60. **Crucial: neither Joffrey's order nor the footpad's hiring is witnessed; Jaime's inference remains circumstantial**.
16. **Cersei says Robert made that comment with their children present.** This supplies a plausible **information channel to young Joffrey**; Cersei uses the remark as an expression of drunken cruelty rather than confessing complicity. Exact wording and original timing have only **her recollection**.
17. **Jaime and Cersei's values visibly diverge.** Jaime wants their relationship public; Cersei cites probable destruction of **Tommen's claim and Myrcella's marriage**, and invokes their sibling relationship. She presses Jaime to leave the Kingsguard and protect her political position.
18. **Jaime refuses sex in White Sword Tower despite Cersei's pressure.** When Cersei tries to initiate sexual contact there, Jaime repeatedly says **no** and physically stops her. She responds with insults about his missing hand and manhood, then leaves. This is a real boundary and relational rupture, distinct from their earlier sept encounter.
19. **Cersei declares that she has lied to Jaime before.** During their argument she admits both she and Tyrion have told lies; **she does not identify each lie**, so do not silently assume confession of a specific affair. Jaime feels he has lost sister, lover, father, son and perhaps brother even though his house nominally won.
20. **Loras has interviewed Brienne.** Jaime summons him, and he brings her down from the tower. Loras now says **it may have happened as Brienne describes**, including an unnatural shadow, and **he can no longer say he is certain of her guilt**. This is a material change in his assessment from ASOS 67; it does **not establish independent eyewitness confirmation of every magical detail**.
21. **A second shadow-associated death is raised.** Jaime reminds Loras that **Ser Cortnay Penrose**, opposing Stannis at Storm's End, had died unexpectedly after apparently jumping from a tower. This parallels Renly's mysterious death but is **only a reported event in this chapter**; keep separate underlying accounts.
22. **Jaime privately tells Brienne the truth about the fake Arya.** The girl going north is **not Arya Stark**, and Jaime does not wish Brienne to die trying to seize the wrong prisoner from 200 men. The intended Stark–Bolton match depends on a false identity.
23. **Jaime tries to honor Catelyn's oath by safeguarding Sansa.** He believes at least one daughter lives and that **Cersei wants Sansa found and killed**, while the legal defense of Tyrion has failed. He insists Brienne should **find Sansa first and get her somewhere safe**, not assassinate her. The rescue mission is **an actual command and agreement**, not completed rescue.
24. **The gift is one of two Valyrian blades reforged from *Ice*.** Jaime explicitly recounts **Ned Stark's greatsword passing to Ilyn Payne and Tywin melting/reworking it into two new swords**. The red-black patterned sword given to Brienne is named **Oathkeeper**. Thus Ned's steel will be used to protect Ned's daughter if Brienne succeeds. The other Valyrian blade is identifiable from Joffrey's earlier gift as **Widow's Wail**.
25. **Jaime supplies practical equipment and releases Brienne.** The gift includes a jeweled scabbard and a **bay mare** waiting in the stable. He refuses payment or loyalty to House Lannister, and Brienne promises to find Sansa for both Jaime's and Catelyn's sake. **Her route and eventual success are UNKNOWN here**.
26. **Jaime continues to suspect Sansa killed Joffrey.** He tells Brienne he thinks Tyrion is protecting Sansa by remaining silent, but Brienne rejects it. **Neither has direct poisoning evidence**; his effort to protect Sansa is noteworthy **even though his murder theory about her may be mistaken**.
27. **Jaime makes a new White Book entry in his left hand.** He records defeats and captures, his right-hand loss to **Zollo the Fat by Vargo Hoat's order**, and **Brienne's success bringing him back to King's Landing**. He deliberately **gives Brienne credit** and leaves much of his page blank for future deeds. The words are **actually written on page**, not merely planned redemption.
28. **End state:** Crown paperwork confirms the Bolton–Frey settlement; a counterfeit Arya heads north; Gregor is alive and suffering from a likely poisoned wound; Jaime and Cersei are estranged; **Brienne has left to seek Sansa with Oathkeeper**, and Jaime has begun authoring his remaining legacy.
