## Chronological and consequential observations

1. **The morning is immediately before Joffrey's wedding.** Tyrion dresses while Sansa sleeps. **They share a marriage bed without intercourse**; Tyrion recognizes her grief and emotional distance. He has **told Sansa about Robb's death**, and heard her weep through a closed door only after she initially remained outwardly still.
2. **Tyrion withholds graphic news from Sansa.** He knows further reports that **Robb's severed head was replaced with Grey Wind's and crowned**, and **Catelyn's naked body thrown into the Green Fork**. Here these are **secondhand news arriving from the Twins**, not Tyrion's eyewitness verification of corpse handling.
3. **He recognizes the contradiction in his protective wedding cloak.** The Lannister vow to protect Sansa contrasts with his father's strategic involvement in her family's murders. Tyrion considers she may pray for his death at nightly godswood visits; that is **his projection**, not a known wish of Sansa.
4. **Tyrion's married household occupies the Kitchen Keep.** Lord Gyles Rosby was removed from well-appointed rooms for Tyrion and Sansa, including solar, bath, Pod's and maids' rooms and **Bronn's tiny slit-window cell**. Proximity to the main kitchen and **servants' stairs** provides a route for secret meetings.
5. **Brella has a useful past.** **Varys recommended Brella** to serve Sansa, noting she previously ran **Renly's household in King's Landing** and learned not to notice compromising activity. No direct proof is given she currently reports to Varys or either queen; household staffing nevertheless constitutes an intelligence vulnerability.
6. **Tyrion secretly meets Shae through underground passages.** Beneath the Kitchen Keep lies a **vaulted cellar and connecting passage** to a **room of old Targaryen dragon skulls**. Tyrion and Shae have sex there in darkness. This is a **real physical underground castle passage**, not evidence of magical dragon animation.
7. **Shae now serves Sansa as a maid, concealing her previous role.** This brings Tyrion access to her but exposes Shae to detection by Cersei or Tywin. She jokingly proposes **drugging Sansa with dreamwine** so they could be intimate beside her asleep. **No such drugging occurs or is approved in this chapter**, though the casual suggestion illuminates disregard for Sansa's safety.
8. **Tywin previously forbade the relationship.** Tyrion knows revelation of Shae could lead to her **execution**, and describes the mounting risk. The danger is real in his estimation; **no sentence or search for Shae is announced yet**.
9. **A past Varys conversation is recalled in detail.** Varys says the original false employment history was devised for **Tanda and Lollys**, not Cersei. Asked whether he would lie to the queen to protect Shae, Varys says **no**; he must preserve his position by selling intelligence to Cersei as needed. He describes his 'little birds' as his only source of survival. **His account of tactical double-service is firsthand testimony, not an oath of complete honesty**.
10. **Tyrion realizes he has leverage over Varys.** He reminds Varys that the eunuch has lied to Cersei before; both understand revealing it could endanger him. Varys's apparent loyalty to Tyrion is therefore **conditional on continuing usefulness and competing threats**.
11. **Tyrion begins seriously considering removing Shae.** He imagines placing her with **Chataya**, marrying her to **Bronn**, or arranging a match to **Ser Tallad**, whom he notices looking at Shae. He fears Tallad might later learn Shae was a paid companion; these are **unvoiced contingency plans, not actual offers or marriages**.
12. **His interpretation of Sansa's earlier conduct remains ungenerous.** He suspects his wife could reveal his affair, citing her past disclosure of Ned's plans to Cersei. Sansa's own narrated fear and isolation make her motives more complex than his thought that she is 'hardly innocent of betrayal'. This matters to interpretation of her refusal to confide in her Lannister husband.
13. **Tyrion attributes emotional sincerity to Shae but also doubts her.** Shae professes love, kisses his scar and flatters his appearance, while Tyrion reminds himself she works for him as a prostitute. **The scene does not grant a Shae POV**, so her inner motives and actual feelings are not confirmed either way.
14. **The physical dragon skulls are present in the same room during intimacy.** Shae poses in a dragon's jaws; Tyrion cannot reliably distinguish **Balerion from Vhagar** by their skulls in darkness. This is atmosphere and archival remnant, **not a magical sign or a child conceived in this chapter**.
15. **The calendar changes on this wedding morning.** Tyrion notes a **new day, new year and new century**: three hundred years after the conquest. This provides a valuable synchronization anchor for Sansa's wedding-day chronology and the newer regnal dating.
16. **End state:** Tyrion has not confessed Shae's presence to Sansa, ordered Shae away, resolved his vulnerability to Varys/Cersei, or consummated the marriage. The wedding festivities are imminent; the intimate secrecy remains exploitable.

## Cross-POV rival hypotheses

- **Why is Sansa reserved?** Her family's murders and the Lannister coercion of marriage are sufficient causal reasons; Tyrion's shame and resentment do not give him a right to her affection or secrets.
- **Who knows about Shae?** Varys directly knows, Tyrion fears Cersei may learn, and Brella/Sansa might observe unexplained absences. Do not assign knowledge to someone without a witnessed discovery.
- **How trustworthy is Varys?** His skill is balancing the personal survival risks of rival patrons. His willingness to protect one secret today does not demonstrate commitment to Tyrion tomorrow.
- **Is Shae romantically sincere?** Both genuine affection and calculated self-preservation fit the spoken words; future actions and independent evidence are needed.
