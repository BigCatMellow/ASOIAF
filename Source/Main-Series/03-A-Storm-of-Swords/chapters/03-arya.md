## Sequential narrative, agency and consequences

1. **ACTUAL POSTERN ESCAPE CONTINUES:** Arya, Gendry and Hot Pie ride out from **Harrenhal's eastern postern** after the guard killing in `ACOK 64`, traveling **north away from the God's Eye** along a farm track before entering woods and streams. Rain reduces the noise and the traces they leave, but their escape has **not yet put them beyond capture**. Arya is ten and responsible for two companions who are less experienced riders.
2. **WHY PURSUIT IS LIKELY:** Arya has taken **three horses, Bolton's map and dagger, swords from Harrenhal's armory, provisions, and a cupbearer identity** after killing a guard. She expects **Steelshanks Walton or Vargo Hoat's Mummers** to be sent after them. This is a well-founded fear based on their motives and cruelty, but **no chasing human force is actually seen from Arya's waking perspective in this chapter**.
3. **COUNTERTRACKING:** Arya leads the horses **up a stream for about a quarter-mile**, emerges on a rocky bank and avoids the major roads to interrupt a pursuer's scent. She also doubles back at intervals to obscure spoor. These are concrete attempts, not proof that hunting dogs or mounted men have found her trail.
4. **HER LEADERSHIP NOW MIXES FEAR WITH SELF-CONTROL:** Arya tells herself it is useful for **Hot Pie to fear her** after seeing the killing, because he will obey instructions. At the same time she experiences an unfamiliar sense of calm in the night forest, feels stronger with **a stolen sword across her back**, and recalls **Syrio's fear teaching** and Jaqen's **valar morghulis**. This is a psychologically complicated transition from victim to armed organizer, not a simple victory over trauma.
5. **RIVERLANDS CIVILIAN DESTRUCTION:** The three pass a **burned village with twelve corpses hanging from apple trees**. Hot Pie prays to the Mother, while Arya recites her **revenge names**, touches **Jaqen's iron coin** and eats a worm-eaten apple picked from the tree. The actual killers and alleged crimes of the hanged civilians are **not identified**; the tableau echoes `ASOS 1 Jaime`'s murdered women but **does not prove the same band committed both crimes**.
6. **WOLVES IN THE WILD:** The travelers find **three ordinary wolves eating a fawn**, one defending its carcass. **Hot Pie's horse bolts**, and Arya instructs Gendry to withdraw without provoking the animal; later she sees a **larger wolf pack moving across a stream in the valley**, briefly mistaking it for riders. When she howls, the **largest wolf answers**. This is **direct animal response**, not proof she knows which wolf or whether Nymeria was present. **Her dream at the chapter's end** will introduce a much more specific connection.
7. **MAP AND THE TRIDENT PLAN:** Arya tells the boys she intends to reach **the Trident**, then follow it **upstream to Riverrun**. She reads the name **Riverrun** on Bolton's sheepskin map and deliberately hides that **it is the castle of her grandfather and home to Robb's supporters**. She knows Gendry has a secret of his own (Baratheon parentage suspected from earlier reader evidence) but believes **Gendry himself does not understand it**. This is partial social trust, not confession of her identity to Hot Pie.
8. **NAVIGATION IS NOT CERTAINTY:** She points north by **moss on trees**, saying it preferentially grows on one side, but **Gendry later shows moss on three sides of one tree and none on another**. The rule is not a reliable compass under these conditions, and continual detours around hills and gullies create genuine risk they are traveling in circles. The chapter deliberately complicates the competence of a child who **can read a map but cannot determine her current location from landmarks**.
9. **DISTANCE MISJUDGED:** At their first river, Hot Pie triumphantly assumes the **Trident**. Arya recognizes the stream is **only about 30 feet wide** and they have ridden too little distance. She studies potential tributaries—**Little Willow, Big Willow, Rippledown Rill, Darry, Greenapple, Maiden**—but lacks a fixed observation to tell which river they face. The issue is **unresolved location**, not failure to read written labels.
10. **AN IMPORTANT GEOGRAPHICAL RISK:** Gendry notices **one candidate river drains back toward the God's Eye and Harrenhal**, while another reaches the Trident in a different direction. Arya chooses neither upstream nor downstream; she **fords north across the first river**, and later a shallower second crossing, regardless of uncertainty. The crossings are physically completed but **their true named rivers are not determined**.
11. **COST OF STAYING TOGETHER:** Hot Pie complains of saddle sores, injury and exhaustion, while Gendry falls from his horse without injury. Arya initially regards their lack of riding skill as slowing her and recognizes **she could make faster time alone**. She **refuses to abandon them**, explicitly identifying both as **her pack and only living friends**, and remembers they left their safer Harrenhal jobs because she persuaded them to escape. The wolf/pack theme is enacted as a difficult loyalty rather than only a mystical metaphor.
12. **FEAR OF THE BLOODY MUMMERS:** Arya thinks Hoat's men may **amputate limbs or flay the children** on recapture. Her concern is grounded in the Mummers' reputation, but her prior specific claim in `ACOK 64` that Hoat announced **one mandatory amputated left foot for every servant** was a **deliberate fabrication** told to convince Gendry. The present chapter does not convert that invented policy into an actual witnessed order.
13. **NO FIRE / SHARED HARD LESSONS:** When Hot Pie proposes a campfire, **Arya and Gendry forbid it simultaneously** because it could betray their location. Arya is reminded of **Jon Snow** finishing her thoughts at Winterfell. Her family memories remain available alongside increasingly harsh behavior.
14. **ACTUAL PHYSICAL EXHAUSTION:** Arya pushes them past safe riding hours, repeatedly nodding off in the saddle. **Gendry catches her drifting in circles on a grazing horse**, noting Hot Pie was struck by a branch and knocked out of his saddle without waking Arya. She accepts the need to **sleep under a beech**, with Gendry taking first watch. This is explicit recognition that determination cannot eliminate human fatigue.
15. **WOLF DREAM / ARMED HUNTERS:** Arya then dreams **as an enormous wolf**, facing a hunting party of **four Brave Companions/Mummers**—a pale **Lyseni**, a dark **Ibbenese axe fighter**, a scarred **Dothraki named Iggo** with bells, and an **unidentified Dornishman**. The wolf leads or joins a **large pack of gaunt wolves** that slaughter the men and mounts. A horse kicks one wolf's head; Iggo slashes another with an arakh; the POV wolf leaps on him and **tears off his arm**. This is vivid **dream content** and potentially **real skinchanging**, but **there is no immediate human report, corpse or animal POV independently confirming those precise kills** in this chapter.
16. **DREAM CONFIDENCE / FUTURE TEST:** The wolf has heightened **smell, command of pack, sense of prey and strength**, consistent in form with Bran's verified wolf perceptions (`ACOK 69`) and Jon/Ghost's corroborated wounds (`ACOK 53`). Arya's dream targets are men she fears will pursue her; it could be **wish-fulfillment nightmare** or **remote Nymeria pack action**. Follow subsequent waking signs, descriptions of hunters' bodies, missing men and wolf sightings before scoring a theory as proven.
17. **ENDING BOUNDARY:** The three escaped children are **alive, hiding and exhausted** somewhere north of Harrenhal, but **not demonstrably at the Trident or safely on course for Riverrun**. The wolf-pack violence is **a dream with strong series-level reasons for supernatural interest**, yet specific waking events remain uncorroborated.

## Theory tests and continuation signals

- **Nymeria/wolf-POV channel:** Compelling details include predator olfaction, independent pack action and tactical hierarchy. Contrasting hypotheses are **a stress-induced revenge dream**, a **remote literal Nymeria event**, or a mix of dream and extra-sensory information. Do not report the Mummers dead outside Arya's dream without independent source check.
- **Geography as narrative restraint:** Even a capable, literate child cannot turn a hand-drawn map into GPS. Mark potential exit routes and wrong rivers, and later compare physical descriptions with maps rather than assuming every 'north' reference is correct.
- **Gendry/Arya/Hot Pie relationships:** Arya's loyalty is real even as she uses fear to control the group. Gendry's willingness to challenge her on sleeping and navigation is a form of **care and competence**, not mutiny.
- **Mummers:** Identify whether Iggo and the other described riders are actually missing, pursued Arya, or encountered other killers; their deaths would significantly alter the confidence in wolf skinchanging.
