## Chronological close reading

1. **The siege has become attritional.** Mance's woodcutters work day and night. Jon barely sleeps, dreams of fighting when he closes his eyes and remains dependent on his crutch. The Watch lives largely atop the Wall because **the cage is too slow for routine descents**; Aemon and aged brothers are left in Castle Black.
2. **Owen believes Robert may answer the Watch's ravens.** Jon gently allows the hope without endorsing its truth. The other POVs already establish Robert is dead; this is an acute example of **the Watch's informational lag**.
3. **The wildlings have learned from failed mantlets.** They push wheeled wooden archery shelters with slits for five archers, but after Jon ignited some, they **cover them in rawhides**. These are real tactical adaptations, not supernatural immunity to fire.
4. **Red Alyn of the Rosewood is dead.** An arrow wounded him three days earlier, and **his body lies at the Wall's base**. Keep his loss separate from the earlier Eastwatch and Shadow Tower casualties.
5. **The scarecrow sentries have become a morale tool.** Pyp names them after missing Watchmen and organizes wagers on their arrow counts, with **Dolorous Edd, Othell Yarwyck, Tumberjon and Watt of Long Lake** named. Jon lets the practice continue because jokes keep fear manageable.
6. **A Myrish telescope provides limited but useful intelligence.** Aemon's brass 'far eye', once used to watch stars, shows **Mance's large white tent made from snow-bear skins**, pregnant **Dalla**, **Val milking a goat**, **Tormund with two sons**, and **Varamyr Sixskins accompanied by a shadowcat**. These are Jon's actual distant sightings, not evidence he knows Mance's private plans.
7. **Dalla appears near delivery.** Her physical condition is directly noticed; **her child's actual birth has not occurred in this chapter**.
8. **The wildlings build an eight-wheeled siege turtle.** It resembles an upturned longhall or hull with an open rear/front and a stout frame, protected by layers of **sheepskins, pelts and the freshly stripped hide of a dead mammoth**. It promises shelter for men using axes on the **hastily repaired outer gate**.
9. **Watch material reserves are nearly exhausted.** Pitch is gone, oil almost gone, arrows diminishing, no fletchers present; one heavy trebuchet remains broken while enemies have learned the other one's target zone. The siege threatens through **logistical exhaustion**, not solely a single decisive charge.
10. **The Shadow Tower relief is delayed by a real battle.** A raven from **Ser Denys Mallister** reports **Bowen Marsh** pursued raiders past the Shadow Tower into the **Gorge** and fought **the Weeper with about three hundred wildlings** at the **Bridge of Skulls**. Although the Watch won, **over one hundred brothers died**, including **Endrew Tarth and Aladale Wynch**, and Marsh was severely wounded and under **Maester Mullin's care**. This is **raven-carried reporting**, not Jon's own battle view.
11. **Zei's disappearance is not independently explained.** Jon sent her to request aid from Mole's Town. **Mully** later found the settlement deserted, including the brothel, and returned without her. Jon supposes she fled south with the others, but **her fate is UNKNOWN on page**.
12. **Fresh manpower is not readily available.** Owen, Horse, Kegs, Pyp, Satin, Grenn and a few others are worn down by defending repeatedly against Mance's much larger host. No contingent arrives from the Shadow Tower or Eastwatch before the turtle attack.
13. **Fire arrows, scorpions and catapults cannot penetrate the turtle effectively.** Wet skins extinguish flaming heads; bolts lodge without significant effect; ordinary stones bounce off the layered roof. The vehicle continues toward the gate, passing even over a dead mammoth and fallen giants.
14. **Jon devises frozen-gravel ammunition.** Twelve heavy **oak barrels** packed with crushed footpath gravel are soaked with water then left to freeze hard overnight. The solid mass should fall as a boulder rather than disintegrating into scattered pebbles when it strikes the 700-foot Wall.
15. **The device requires ingenuity and teamwork.** Grenn, Kegs, Owen and Jon roll the barrels to the edge, sometimes melting surface ice slightly with a torch to ease movement; a barrel nearly slips away. Grenn's strength is essential.
16. **Four barrels destroy the turtle's front.** From the Wall's edge they drop frozen barrels onto the vehicle, causing it to splinter and its occupants to flee; **eight barrels remain**. **The attack succeeds without proving the entire free-folk host destroyed**.
17. **Jon delegates and finally rests.** Exhausted and hurting, he places **Pyp** in temporary charge atop the Wall, rides down the winch cage, takes **Aemon's dreamwine** and sleeps in the King's Tower. His dream of a long warhorn note is **dream content**, not a verified Horn of Winter blast.
18. **Eastwatch officers arrive and seize Jon.** Waking, he sees several strangers in Watch black, led by **Ser Glendon**; **Ser Alliser Thorne** has returned from Eastwatch, with **Janos Slynt** now occupying Mormont's chair and claiming to command Castle Black **until Bowen Marsh returns**. These are newly arrived officers, **not a completed election of Lord Commander**.
19. **Slynt charges oathbreaking, cowardice and desertion.** Jon explains **Qhorin Halfhand commanded infiltration** and that Jon killed him in the context of preserving the mission. Aemon confirms he and Noye had previously heard and accepted the explanation, reminding Slynt that Jon saved Castle Black.
20. **Jon distinguishes a sexual vow violation from treason.** He directly acknowledges sleeping with **Ygritte** and caring for her, but denies turning against the Watch or taking arms against the realm. Slynt and Thorne refuse this distinction and use her memory to provoke him.
21. **Rattleshirt's evidence is suspect.** Eastwatch men drag in a **battered, chained Rattleshirt stripped of his bone armor**, with broken teeth and bruised face. He accuses Jon of pleading for life and killing Qhorin of his own volition, ignoring the Halfhand's actual commands as witnessed in Jon's prior POV. **His visible injuries make coercion possible; coercion itself is not shown**.
22. **Thorne invents an unsubstantiated wider conspiracy.** He alleges Qhorin's death links to Mormont's murder and speculates **Benjen Stark may be with Mance**, despite no evidence in this scene. **Septon Cellador** also treats Jon's vows before a heart tree as suspicious wildling affinity; **Aemon correctly notes those are the old gods of the North as well**.
23. **Aemon asserts a specific defense of Jon's service.** Jon was Mormont's steward, chosen for promise, trusted by Qhorin, and held the Wall after Noye died. His defense is principled but **Slynt holds the immediate armed authority**.
24. **Slynt invokes old enmity against the Starks.** He calls Ned a traitor and proposes a noose for his bastard. Jon corrects him that Ned was murdered. This political history and Thorne's mocking account reveal a conflict of **personal revenge and military need**, not a neutral discipline proceeding.
25. **Slynt orders Jon to an ice cell.** Thorne grabs Jon; Jon nearly strangles him before Eastwatch men drag him off. This shows **Jon's dangerous loss of control under provocation**, even while the charges are undermined by available source testimony.
26. **End state:** The turtle is crushed, so the gate survives another day. **Jon is arrested and ordered confined by Slynt**, replacing the necessary acting defender with an officer hostile to him. The next command and political struggle remain open.
