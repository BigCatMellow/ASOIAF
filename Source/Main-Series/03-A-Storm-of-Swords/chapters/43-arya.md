## Causal and sequential close reading

1. **The Brotherhood returns to High Heart.** The hill is ringed by cut **weirwood stumps**, windy and overlooking a northern storm. Arya experiences the place as uncanny, but no material ghost is observed. Squire Ned and Arya climb onto the stumps and watch the sunset; **old-gods imagery** remains prominent.
2. **Thoros admits an intermittent rather than reliable gift.** Gendry asks whether he can genuinely read fires. Thoros says **sometimes**, but not at that moment. He openly describes a dissolute early priesthood, early skill at languages and intermittent strange flame visions, and his posting to Aerys's court in hopes that a fire-loving king might convert. **The priest's autobiographical story is testimony**, not direct proof of every claimed vision.
3. **The former tourney flaming swords were a different practice.** Thoros remembers buying swords from **Gendry's master Tobho Mott** and destroying them with fire, including an occasion on which his flaming sword startled **Kevan Lannister's horse** and delighted Robert. This supports earlier accounts of stage/tourney fire performances without explaining **Beric's blood-lit sword** as mere material trickery.
4. **Beric calls fire inherently consuming.** He abruptly interrupts Thoros and says **six returns are too many**, connecting resurrection with loss rather than purely restorative divine favor. This is his present psychological experience, not a demonstrated fixed maximum number of revivals.
5. **The Ghost of High Heart appears again as an anomalous witness.** The white-haired, tiny, cane-bearing woman with gleaming red eyes appears from behind the horses. She recognizes **Beric ('Lord of Corpses'), Thoros and Lem** and bargains for **wine and Tom's song** rather than cash. She is an old woman known to the Brotherhood, not a confirmed literal ghost.
6. **Her first reported death is of the kraken king.** She says **the wet king has died and his ironborn are now turning on each other**. This likely refers to **Balon Greyjoy**, but the evidence in Arya's chapter is a **prophetic claim**, not independent dated news. Cross-reference Stannis burning a leech named Balon in [ASOS 36 Davos](36-davos.md) without presuming either magical causation or chronology.
7. **Hoster Tully's death is corroborated.** She also mentions Hoster, whose funeral was witnessed by Catelyn in [ASOS 35 Catelyn](35-catelyn.md). This is one example of correspondence between an old woman's report and independently narrated death; it does not establish her accuracy in every future vision.
8. **The prophetic 'great dog' and 'goat' are not identified.** She envisions a **fevered goat alone in a hall of kings**, and a great dog descending on him. Arya wonders whether this is **Sandor or Gregor** because both Cleganes bear dogs. Vargo Hoat is a plausible goat referent via Harrenhal's goat emblem; **the precise participants/event are INFERRED** and have not happened on this POV page.
9. **A wolf's grief and a wedding soundscape appear.** She speaks of **a wolf howling unheard in rain**, overwhelming drums, horns, pipes and screams, and **little bells**. These are strong future-event clues, but **which wolves, wedding, victims and place are not named explicitly**. Preserve wording and sequence without treating the Red Wedding as already witnessed here.
10. **Two linked visions involve a maid.** She sees a **maid with purple serpent-like objects in her hair at a feast**, then the same maid **defeating a savage giant in a castle made of snow**. The linkage of two moments is explicit; assigning **Sansa, poison, Littlefinger or a literal giant** is still an interpretation requiring later source confirmation.
11. **Arya alarms the dreamer more than Beric does.** The woman calls Arya **wolf child, blood child**, says she smells of death and grief and commands her to leave. She claims to have gorged on grief at **Summerhall**, a striking old-history clue; the statement **does not prove she caused Summerhall's tragedy**.
12. **The Ghost redirects a concrete travel plan.** She says **Catelyn will be at the Twins for a wedding** and the **Blackfish holds the rivers**; the Brotherhood should search at the Twins rather than count on finding the mother at Riverrun. **The marriage plan** matches [ASOS 35 Catelyn](35-catelyn.md), but the destination and future arrival remain character reports, not completed reunion.
13. **The old gods and flame readings are explicitly juxtaposed.** She says High Heart still belongs to the **old gods**, whose memories persist in roots, oak/acorn and stumps, and advises Thoros he will see little there. This is **her religious/mystic account**; it does not prove rival gods literally block one another's magic.
14. **She requests Jenny's song.** Tom Sevenstrings sings **the song of Jenny**, and the woman rocks and cries. The identity of Jenny, exact relationship with the woman and Summerhall, and how old she is are **not directly established** by this scene, though these connections warrant a dedicated source audit.
15. **Rain and illness become immediate logistical constraints.** The storm turns roads and creeks dangerous; **Ned, Lem and Watty** develop fever/chills and others cough. Notch offers an abandoned village as shelter. These conditions make movement, reconnaissance and preservation of the ransom traveler harder.
16. **Squire Ned identifies himself as Edric Dayne.** He is **the Lord of Starfall**, Beric's squire, aged **twelve**, with pale blond hair and dark blue eyes that can look purplish. His **father was the elder brother of Ser Arthur Dayne**; **Ashara Dayne was his aunt**. **His 'Ned' name is not proof of Eddard's paternity.**
17. **Edric offers a specific alleged answer about Jon Snow's mother.** He says **Wylla**, a long-serving **wet nurse at Starfall**, nursed Edric because his mother had insufficient milk and is **Jon Snow's mother**; thus he calls Jon his **milk brother**, explicitly **not blood brother**. He swears on his House's honor, but he has no narrated firsthand access to Jon's birth or Eddard's secrets.
18. **Arya is unaware of any corroboration.** She says Jon himself did not know his mother's name and thinks of writing him with this revelation. **The accuracy of the Wylla claim is UNVERIFIED** at this checkpoint, regardless of the certainty with which a young Dayne repeats it.
19. **Another potentially incompatible Dayne story is transmitted.** Edric says **his aunt Allyria** believes **Ashara and Eddard Stark fell in love at the Harrenhal tournament**. He also says Ashara later jumped into the sea from the **Palestone Sword**; her death and its motive are reported family history, not depicted firsthand here. The statement does not establish either **Jon's parentage** or that Ashara bore a child.
20. **Harwin supplies a counterreading.** He says the Ned–Ashara romance was an old Winterfell rumor and doubts its truth; even if love occurred, Eddard was **not yet bound to Catelyn**, since Brandon was still betrothed to her. Harwin suggests Ashara's death may relate to her brother **Arthur Dayne's** death, rather than rejected love. **Harwin's explanation is also inference**, not a confirmed suicide motive.
21. **Gendry's father ignorance is deliberate dramatic irony.** Gendry protests that he has never known his father, imagines a drunk and asserts the man may be dead, while Arya defends Eddard's care for Jon. The reader has separate Robert/Gendry clues, but **Gendry has not discovered a verified paternity** in this scene.
22. **The shelter has a history of Stark–Tully-aligned atrocity.** Notch recognizes the old settlement as **Lord Goodbrook's village**, burned on **Hoster Tully's orders** because Goodbrook backed Aerys rather than Robert during the rebellion. Later reconciliation of Goodbrook's son did not restore those who died. This counters a simple 'Lannisters cause all rural ruin' interpretation and adds a civilian-cost parallel to the Brotherhood's original justice mission.
23. **Thoros sees a second purported vision in the stable fire.** He describes **Riverrun as an island amid attacking crimson-and-gold lions**, saying he believes a Lannister siege is coming soon. He expressly admits previous fire-reading mistakes. **No actual siege begins during the chapter**; the vision still requires testing against later movement.
24. **The Brotherhood considers safer verification before returning Arya.** Beric discusses seeking **Sharna, Lord Vance's maester, Lady Smallwood** and scouts to determine whether Riverrun is safe and whether Brynden will acknowledge Arya. They **do not definitively abandon returning her to family**, but the plan changes from immediate direct arrival, worsening her feeling of captivity.
25. **Arya runs from the stable, not toward a known safe destination.** In exhaustion she blames herself for following Gendry and Hot Pie and resents repeated promises. She runs out into storm and ruins despite lack of a route. **Her fears about her friends abandoning her do not establish their actual intent.**
26. **Sandor Clegane physically abducts Arya.** She nearly collides with him around a ruined house; he grips her arm and **drags her toward his waiting horse**. This is on-page coercive capture, not an agreed escort or paid ransom transaction. **Neither the Brotherhood's reaction nor the Hound's destination is shown by chapter end.**

## Theory test matrix

- **R+L=J / alternative maternal identities:** Edric's Wylla statement is a genuine source datum, not an embarrassment to erase. It could reflect accurate family knowledge, a carefully maintained cover story or misunderstanding. The Ashara story is **separate secondhand testimony**. Test the Tower of Joy, Eddard's behavior, the Dayne naming pattern and witnesses before treating any scenario as confirmed.
- **High Heart prophecy:** Distinguish independently corroborated Hoster news from future scenes not yet narrated. Maintain a prophecy ledger with dated reveal order and alternative symbolic readings; Stannis's leech names are a parallel clue, **not proof of causation**.
- **Summerhall:** The Ghost's extreme grief and Jenny song may indicate direct historical connection, but the woman does not explicitly identify herself or the whole tragedy.
- **Rebellion morality:** Notch's account shows Hoster committing war atrocities against a loyalist lord's village. Even if true, it does not justify contemporary Lannister atrocities; it broadens the ledger beyond favored houses.
- **Arya's agency:** Running from delay is understandable, but it creates an opening for violent capture. Sandor's stated motive remains unknown until later first-person dialogue.
