## Narrated sequence and evidence boundaries

1. **RESCUE ROUTE AND RECOVERY:** Davos returns to Dragonstone aboard **Shayala's Dance**, a Lyseni galley commanded by **Khorane Sathmantes** in Salladhor Saan's fleet. They have passed **Driftmark** through the Gullet, approaching the smoldering **Dragonmont**. He remains dangerously ill: repeated coughing with **bloody phlegm**, weakness, poor tolerance of rich food and difficulty standing. Khorane gave him **a cabin, his clothes, boots, food and a long Lyseni dirk**. Those gifts are physically real; Davos's interpretation of the knife as a divine instrument of vengeance is his own.
2. **MELISANDRE BLAMED, AND DAVOS RESOLVES TO KILL:** Davos believes Melisandre 'broke' Stannis and somehow **punished him for fighting at the Blackwater without her**, killing Davos's sons. He expressly plans to **cut out her heart with the gift dirk**. His resolve is a **direct announced intention**, not evidence she materially caused Tyrion's wildfire hulls; previous eyewitness [ACOK 58–59](../../02-A-Clash-of-Kings/chapters/58-davos.md) attributes the fleet destruction to King's Landing's engineered weapon.
3. **BLACKWATER RESCUE REPORT:** Khorane relates that **Tywin and Tyrell forces struck Stannis's flank**, and many bannermen deserted. **A figure dressed as Renly** supposedly rode with the relief; the captain repeats the ghost interpretation rather than identifying a living wearer. Many **Florent-badged men** were evacuated. The report is secondhand to Davos, consistent broadly with earlier post-battle accounts, not a new direct vision of Renly.
4. **ALSTER FLORENT'S NEW OFFICE:** Khorane says **Lord Alester Florent is King's Hand on Dragonstone**, while Queen **Selyse** is prominent among the survivors. When Davos arrives, the harbor is almost empty of royal warships and the remaining identifiable ships have **Lyseni striped hulls**. This is concrete military impoverishment following the defeat, albeit not a census of every vessel.
5. **DAVOS MEETS SAAN ON A MERCHANT COG:** Salladhor Saan is supervising the contents of **Bountiful Harvest**, a Pentoshi merchant cog, with two eunuch clerks (one named **Meizo Mahr**). He counts **spice jars against a bill of lading** and greets Davos emotionally as a presumed-dead friend. This combines **commercially precise bookkeeping and genuine affection** rather than making the pirate a generic rogue.
6. **SAAN'S PRIVATEERING LEGAL COVER:** Saan holds a parchment under **Alester Florent's seal** naming him **Lord of Blackwater Bay** and requiring vessels to pay duties for crossing waters he claims. He uses the claim to seize **ships, cargo, gold and ransoms**, justifying acts that outsiders may call piracy. This is a **nominal wartime grant issued by the Florent regime**; effective lawful recognition across Westeros is unproven. His authority is different from a territorial estate securely held onshore.
7. **A PENTOS CONNECTION:** Saan says the fine chairs aboard Bountiful Harvest were made for **Illyrio Mopatis**, who seldom travels to use them. This is **Saan's assertion about the cog's owner or furnishing provenance** and a potentially interesting shipping-network clue linking Pentos with Dragonstone. It does **not** prove Illyrio directly finances Stannis or deliberately handed the ship to Saan.
8. **NAVAL SURVIVOR ACCOUNT:** Saan lists **Lord Steffon, Ragged Jenna, Swift Sword and Laughing Lord** among royal vessels that were **upstream of the worst wildfire** when the chain rose; some surrendered, others **were scuttled by their own crews** to keep them from Lannisters. He has rumors the **Ragged Jenna and Laughing Lord** later operate as river pirates. These are Saan's accounts and rumors, not Davos's personal view of their completed scuttling or survival.
9. **DAVOS'S SONS—AN IMPORTANT CORRECTION:** Saan cannot offer a survivor report for **Lady Marya or Wraith**, ships of **Allard and Dale**, and calls those sons dead in commiseration. Crucially he does confirm **young Devan was rescued from the shore during Stannis's retreat**, apparently remaining beside his king. Thus **Devan's survival is newly established by strong ally testimony**; the other elder sons' particular deaths still lack individual on-page bodies or eyewitness last moments in this chapter.
10. **AN ALTERNATIVE LIFE:** Saan offers Davos a ship and commercial partnership in **Braavos, Myr and Volantis**, with lucrative smuggling voyages outside a lost war. Davos refuses, declaring continued duty to **Stannis as legal heir**, though the king has neither enough ships nor money. Saan's offer demonstrates a material exit from the destructive cause, not a guaranteed safe withdrawal or a lack of love for Stannis.
11. **STANNIS WITHDRAWN INTO THE STONE DRUM:** Saan reports Stannis **isolates himself in his chambers** and admits **Melisandre alone**, leaving even **Selyse and Shireen** outside. Selyse and Alester conduct court and the queen has given Alester the **royal seal for letters and grants**. This is Saan's well-positioned court report, consistent with exclusion from access; the present Davos chapter **does not include a direct audience with Stannis**.
12. **DRAGONMONT'S HOT INTERIOR:** Saan describes rumors that Melisandre and Stannis visit **hidden stairs and shafts within Dragonmont to watch flames**, in places where only Melisandre can tolerate the heat. The volcano and smoke are directly visible; **interior chambers and their magic or exclusive heat immunity are only hearsay**, and must not be indexed as witnessed tunnels or spells.
13. **EXECUTIONS UNDER THE RED RELIGION:** Saan says the queen's men **burned Lord Sunglass and Hubard Rambton's sons** as supposed servants of darkness during the battle. Davos independently anticipates those names based on their earlier imprisonment. That combination supports the reported execution, but **the direct lighting of the pyres is not witnessed here**. It establishes severe religious coercion among Stannis's surviving faction.
14. **SAAN WARNS OF FUTILITY:** He argues even a successful assassination would lead Selyse's men to **burn Davos**, whereas failure would lead to punishment for attempting it. He suggests a **Faceless Man from Braavos** as a hypothetical alternative. This is not evidence anyone hired a Faceless Man; Saan's words are a desperate attempt to protect his friend.
15. **OPPOSITE INTERPRETATIONS OF A RESCUE WIND:** Davos insists a divine **Mother** spoke to him and sent the ship that would never have navigated the hazardous rocks on purpose ([ASOS 5](05-davos.md)). Saan instead says an **ill wind inadvertently drove Shayala's Dance south**. Both agree the ship actually found him, but **supernatural intention is disputed**. Davos is feverish enough that Saan suspects his judgment.
16. **DESERTED DRAGONSTONE TOWN:** Davos climbs from the harbor to the castle through empty streets, shuttered houses and marks of mourning. **Named former acquaintances** including **Jate Blackberry, Lord Chyttering, Hookface Will and Hal the Hog** are absent or said dead at Blackwater, with Chyttering, Will and Hal explicitly reported killed and Jate's outcome less definite. The civilian and royal loss is visible; this is more than an elite roster.
17. **ACCESS DENIED / FLorent MEN CONTROL THE GATE:** Gate guards do not believe the **onion knight** lives. Davos uses knowledge of old Dragonstone men to prove familiarity, asks for **his son Devan**, and is eventually admitted via the **sally port**, escorted by **Florent soldiers** who take him to **Aegon's Garden**, not Stannis's Stone Drum. Davos's demand for his king is being intercepted.
18. **PATCHFACE'S OMINOUS SONG:** In the garden **Patchface** sings a strange rhyme involving **fool's blood, king's blood, maiden and guests and bridegroom in chains** while playing with **Princess Shireen**. The text directly records his words, but **no particular future wedding, prisoner or sacrifice is identified**. This is worth indexing as an **ambiguous prophetic-sounding fragment**, not an already decoded event.
19. **EDRIC STORM INTRODUCED FIRSTHAND:** A dark-haired, blue-eyed boy knocks Davos down in play, then names himself **Edric Storm, King Robert's son**. Davos recognizes **Baratheon jaw/eyes and Florent ears** and hears his proud childhood memories of Robert practicing arms and giving him a **small warhammer**. Edric's rank and parentage are **his openly presented and physically plausible identity**, not a formal legal succession decree in this scene.
20. **A DIFFERENT VIEW OF STANNIS'S JUSTICE:** Edric asks to see Davos's **shortened fingertips** and says his father Robert would not have cut them after receiving food at Storm's End. Davos explains Stannis **knighted him for rescuing him and punished him for prior smuggling**, reflecting the defining combination of strict law and gratitude. This meeting unexpectedly places a **royal-blooded child and Melisandre's religious faction in the same Dragonstone setting**; no blood sacrifice or plan regarding Edric is explicitly stated yet.
21. **Davos's ASSASSINATION PLAN ALREADY DISCOVERED:** **Ser Axell Florent**, long-standing castellan and Selyse's uncle, approaches with a dozen **fiery-heart guards**, recognizes Davos and orders him **arrested for intending to use the dirk on Melisandre**. The information could have come through Saan, servants, watching guards or other intelligence; **the chapter does not identify the informer or whether Melisandre foresaw it**. The arrest itself is directly witnessed.
22. **ENDING STATE:** Davos reaches Dragonstone but **not Stannis**, never actually sees Devan, **does not strike Melisandre**, and is seized under Florent authority. His physical survival is complete, while his political rescue and family reunion remain unfinished.

## Rival hypotheses and future checks

- **Stannis as the red woman's captive:** Isolation and seal delegation could mean despair, dependence, deliberate religious study or Melisandre's manipulation. **No scene here proves she mind-controls Stannis**, though the political effect of his absence is real.
- **Edric's presence:** Robert's boy is a politically meaningful **Baratheon-blood claimant or hostage**, and Melisandre's magical practices make the juxtaposition ominous; **no person currently announces his sacrifice** in the provided chapter.
- **Patchface:** Record the exact ingredients of the rhyme without filling a specific event in advance. Later weddings, royal executions or hostage uses might constitute plausible matches; many superficially similar outcomes exist.
