## Chronological close reading

1. **Jon is intensely troubled by Stannis's offer.** Unable to sleep, he trains with **Iron Emmett**, one of Eastwatch's most skilled young rangers, instead of beginners. Emmett repeatedly strikes him with practice weapons; Jon wants challenge, not easy victory.
2. **A blow to Jon's helmet triggers a powerful childhood memory.** Jon recalls playing at knights with **Robb**, borrowing the heroic identities of **Aemon the Dragonknight, Florian the Fool, the Young Dragon and Ryam Redwyne**. One childhood contest ended when Robb said Jon **could never be Lord of Winterfell because he was bastard-born**, citing Catelyn. **This is Jon's memory, not proof Catelyn had a legal claim to decide succession**.
3. **Jon violently loses control of the sparring bout.** After that memory, he batters Emmett beyond a yield until **Halder and Horse** pull him away. Emmett is injured or dazed, his shield badly damaged, and he reproaches Jon without demanding punishment. Jon apologizes. This is a **real danger from internal conflict**, not merely symbolic rage.
4. **Catelyn's imagined disapproval remains psychologically forceful.** Jon thinks of her cold gaze, associates it with Stannis's iron hardness, and still experiences the stigma of bastardy despite Stannis's legal solution.
5. **Castle Black's subsurface passages are extensive.** Jon leaves the armory via rear steps into **wormways linking keeps and towers**, then uses the bathhouse to recover. This is concrete infrastructure, not confirmation of magic under Castle Black.
6. **Jon remembers Winterfell's geothermal water system.** Hot spring water ran through walls, and the glass gardens were warm. Rebuilding the castle is plausible in his mind, but the **scale, resources and political authority necessary** remain unknown.
7. **The Winterfell godswood is a decisive obstacle.** Jon recognizes that accepting Melisandre's conditions would require uprooting and burning his family's **weirwood heart tree**. He concludes he has **no moral right** to erase the old gods for his advancement.
8. **Jon accidentally overhears a Slynt coalition meeting in the bathhouse.** **Alliser Thorne, Bowen Marsh and Othell Yarwyck** discuss their intentions without seeing him. This supplies concrete proof of **electoral coordination**, not merely Jon's suspicion.
9. **Marsh says Tywin favors Slynt in writing.** He offers to show Yarwyck a letter describing Slynt as **a faithful friend and servant**. This is **Marsh's report of an actual letter in his possession**, not a fully reproduced document or proof Tywin currently survives to direct the election.
10. **Thorne argues Stannis's preferred Watch leader would effectively become his bannerman.** He predicts **Tywin will ultimately defeat Stannis** and suggests favoring Slynt. This demonstrates a **pro-Lannister faction**, though no direct royal Lannister funding of every ballot is observed.
11. **Jon visits the other side of the Wall.** He passes a newly repaired **outer gate**, charred stairs, the tunnel where Noye and Mag died, and the killing ground with burned wildling debris.
12. **Captured free folk are held in a fenced camp.** The enclosure is built with **ditches, sharpened stakes and palisades**. Nearby are **three large corpse-burning pits**; men and giants alike are burned. Jon's contemplation includes the moral cost of Stannis's victory.
13. **Jon seriously imagines a life at Winterfell with Val.** He wants land, freedom from bastard stigma, marriage and a son whom he might name **Robb**. He imagines fostering both **Dalla's son and Gilly's boy**, caring for Gilly, and Sam visiting. These are **conditional private hopes**, not actual household arrangements, adoptions or pregnancies.
14. **Jon's desire is clear rather than merely hypothetical.** He admits he has always wanted Winterfell as deeply as anything. His earlier insistence that he never wanted it is revealed as mixed with shame and fear of betraying his brothers.
15. **A wolf-like appetite interrupts his thinking.** Jon briefly experiences an urge to hunt and taste prey, then recognizes the sensation is linked to **Ghost approaching**. This is **strong sensory evidence of ongoing wolf-human connection**; it does not establish every limitation of skinchanging.
16. **Ghost returns alive from the haunted forest.** He is larger and leaner, silent, with **white fur and striking red eyes**. Jon physically embraces him and notes the wolf has been absent from dreams since Jon climbed the Wall. **Ghost's fate is resolved as alive and reunited**.
17. **Jon connects Ghost to the old gods and the weirwoods.** The wolf's white coat and red eyes suggest **heart-tree bone and blood** to him; Jon recalls six pups, five dark-furred for the legitimate Stark children and a sixth white for Jon Snow. The parallel is **Jon's symbolic interpretation**, although the coat and eye colors are directly observed.
18. **Ghost's return determines Jon's personal choice.** Jon states to himself that he now has his answer to Stannis. He leads Ghost **around**, not through, Melisandre's nightfire and returns to Castle Black. He does **not stand before Stannis here to deliver a formal rejection**, but the symbolic and practical direction is clear.
19. **Val is visible at her tower window as Jon returns.** Jon privately apologizes that he will not be the man to 'steal' her. There is **no conversation and no romantic agreement with Val**.
20. **The Night's Watch has been physically pressured into voting.** In the hall **king's men block exits and withhold supper** until the brethren choose a Lord Commander. This is external coercion of timing, even while the Watch still casts its own ballots.
21. **Dolorous Edd proposes Jon's name.** Aemon confirms it is procedurally valid for any sworn brother to nominate a sworn brother. **The proposal is not made directly by Sam or Stannis**, although Sam's previous diplomacy laid the ground for the coalition.
22. **Janos Slynt and Thorne object violently.** They label Jon a turncloak, threaten hanging and call him a warg when Ghost appears. Their personal animus persists; it is not turned into a legal disqualification.
23. **Othell Yarwyck withdraws from the race and declines his planned Slynt endorsement.** He says Jon's service, Stark/Benjen connections and experience at the Wall make him a better option than giving Stannis a direct insult. This visible defection breaks Slynt's prospective coalition.
24. **The ballot is interrupted by an unexpected raven.** When Sam and Clydas open the large **kitchen kettle**, **Mormont's old raven flies out**, circles, repeatedly cries **'Snow'**, lands on Jon and asks for **corn**. Alliser notes Sam taught ravens the word 'Snow'; Sam later denies placing the bird inside. **The bird's identity is affirmed by Sam, but neither deliberate magical election intervention nor deliberate Sam staging is proved**.
25. **The final tokens heavily favor Jon.** The novel describes a flood of **arrowhead tokens**, enough to overwhelm the stones, shells and pennies. **It does not print a precise final numeric tally** in this chapter; do not invent one or say a specific percentage beyond a recognized election.
26. **Jon is formally chosen the 998th Lord Commander.** Brothers kneel, congratulate him and accept his authority. **Bowen Marsh** offers to remain Lord Steward; **Cotter Pyke and Denys Mallister** acknowledge the result, with Pyke's crude warning and Mallister's request that Jon justify his trust.
27. **Grenn and Pyp credit Sam's intervention.** They say Sam persuaded the parties; Pyp jokes that he engineered the raven. **Sam explicitly says he did not place the bird in the kettle**, leaving that particular act unexplained.
28. **The new command immediately faces Stannis.** The king's guards let the Watch eat once informed the election is done. Jon leaves the celebration with **Ghost at his heels and Mormont's raven on his shoulder**, accepting only a swallow of wine before preparing to negotiate with the king.
29. **End state:** Jon has chosen **the Watch over legitimization and Winterfell in practice**, although no formal response to Stannis's offer is narrated here. **His election as 998th Lord Commander is completed on-page**, not merely predicted.
