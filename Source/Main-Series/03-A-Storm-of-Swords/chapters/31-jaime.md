## Chronological narrative and causal consequences

1. **The previous chapter's injury is confirmed.** Vargo's Dothraki **Zollo** cut off Jaime's **right hand** with an arakh ([ASOS 21 Jaime](21-jaime.md)). The captors cauterized his stump with a torch. In the days following, Jaime feels agonizing sensations in missing fingers and an infected wound. No 'merely threatened' or reversible injury interpretation survives.
2. **Captivity is engineered as humiliation.** They carry Jaime tied to Brienne on horseback, sometimes face-to-face, and hang his severed hand around his neck. His brow wound from their duel inflames; the hand's stump oozes and produces dangerous fever. His perception of time deteriorates.
3. **Small physical cruelties persist.** Rorge tricks him into drinking horse urine; he vomits, and Brienne is forced to clean him. Shagwell mocks his loss of masculinity and rank. These are observed assaults, not ordinary prisoner treatment.
4. **An attempt to fight left-handed fails.** Jaime seizes Timeon's sword with his left hand, but cannot strike Shagwell, falls and is beaten. Vargo threatens his remaining hand or a foot. This is evidence **right-hand specialization and immediate debility**, not proof Jaime can never learn another fighting method.
5. **Jaime nearly chooses death.** He is no longer eating reliably and says he is dying. Brienne calls the surrender **cowardice** and insists he must live and fight. This threatens the only reputation he has never been accused of losing and revives his determination to survive. Her words precede his decisive choice to eat, rather than a miraculous medical recovery.
6. **He plans a golden replacement before receiving it.** Jaime imagines a forged **golden hand** and using it for vengeance against Vargo. This is a private intention during delirious captivity; the actual making, design and function of any prosthesis lie ahead.
7. **The loss attacks his constructed self.** He equates his former right arm with his worth as a knight and man, explicitly doubting what remains of him. His left was trained as a shield arm. Physical loss and psychological change must be distinguished.
8. **A hidden debt to Tyrion is named.** Jaime thinks Tyrion loves him **for a lie**. This is a meaningful admission of undisclosed earlier deceit concerning Tyrion, without revealing the precise event or confession here. Track alongside Tysha evidence in Tyrion's POV.
9. **His return to Harrenhal revives the year of the false spring.** At about fifteen Jaime received his white Kingsguard cloak during **Lord Whent's great tourney**, but Aerys prevented him from jousting and sent him away. His recollection is firsthand; the reasons for Aerys's decisions need other testimony.
10. **The contrast with the tourney grounds is physical.** As the captors enter ruined Harrenhal, Jaime finds a **privy trench on the spot where he knelt to swear his vows**. The setting links past glamorous fealty to current desecration and broken meaning. It is not proof anyone placed the trench there deliberately.
11. **A planned assault against Brienne is interrupted.** Rorge, Zollo and Shagwell approach Brienne to sexually assault her; Jaime intervenes verbally, shouting about the **supposed sapphires** of Tarth while Rorge kicks his stump. **The sapphire premise is deliberate misinformation** begun in [ASOS 21 Jaime](21-jaime.md).
12. **Vargo orders his men away from Brienne.** Hoat believes she must remain unassaulted because her supposed virginity enhances a huge gemstone ransom. He subsequently arranges guards to prevent his own men reaching her. **This prevents the attempted assault in the immediate episode**, not proof permanent safety.
13. **Brienne thanks Jaime.** She learns why he shouted and thanks him despite their earlier quarrel. Jaime responds by invoking a debt for her actions against Robin Ryger's pursuit and protecting him in the river. The reciprocal obligation is visible; their private feelings should not be prematurely simplified into romance.
14. **The banner arrangement is politically paradoxical.** At Harrenhal Jaime sees **Bolton's flayed man, Frey twin towers and Robb's direwolf banner**. Under the same nominal northern authority, Vargo brutalizes captives while Roose and Freys direct decisions. Heraldry is verifiable; the exact depth of secret coordination with the Lannisters is **not yet**.
15. **The Frey contingent is individually identifiable.** Jaime recognizes **Danwell, Aenys and Hosteen Frey** among Bolton's companions. He tells them Cleos died following an archer attack and was stripped by the Mummers. They show resentment of Robb's broken marriage pact; Brienne knows no details of that dispute.
16. **Frey responses to Jaime diverge.** One favors ransom at Riverrun, another prefers richer Casterly Rock, another killing him for Ned Stark. Ser Aenys asks why Catelyn's daughters should concern them. They are **not a united neutral enforcement body** and Brienne's invocation of her oath has little effect.
17. **Bolton rebukes Hoat but does not renounce the relationship.** Roose reminds Vargo he is not master of Harrenhal until Bolton goes north. Vargo proposes extorting **100,000 gold dragons**, delivering Jaime in pieces if unpaid, then collecting **Karstark's daughter** by handing Jaime to her father's bounty. This is **Vargo's stated plan**, not an accomplished ransom.
18. **Roose has updated intelligence on Robb's justice.** He says **Rickard Karstark is already executed** for treason and murder (corroborated by [ASOS 20 Catelyn](20-catelyn.md)). Hoat's proposed second payment is therefore already invalid. This demonstrates information has reached Bolton, but **how, by whom and in which written form are unshown**.
19. **Roose also knows the Blackwater marriage change.** He says Joffrey will marry a daughter of Highgarden rather than Sansa. Brienne still thinks Sansa is Joffrey's betrothed. The reader knows from [ASOS 28 Sansa](28-sansa.md) she has been married to Tyrion; this episode does not show Roose or Brienne knowing that later fact.
20. **Roose's careful pause about Jaime's kinship matters.** He confirms Cersei is well and calls Joffrey Jaime's **nephew** with a pointed hesitation. Jaime takes this to mean Roose knows the incest allegation. This is an **inference from demeanor and court intelligence**, not an on-page direct confession by Bolton.
21. **Roose formally protects both captives.** He cuts the cord holding Jaime's severed hand and tosses it to Hoat; he orders Brienne's bonds removed and offers her rooms, and sends Jaime for medical attention. Bolton declares displeasure with Hoat's men. He has superior forces in the yard, roughly **five northmen and as many Freys for every Brave Companion**. This makes his protection credible locally, although the breadth and duration of his good faith remain uncertain.
22. **Bolton separates the prisoners.** Brienne is taken to accommodation; Jaime is sent to **Qyburn** under **Walton's** guard. Physical separation limits their ability to corroborate later private conversations. Brienne has not delivered Jaime to King's Landing.
23. **Qyburn diagnoses spreading corruption.** He finds pus and infected flesh at the stump and advises the safest treatment is amputation higher up, perhaps all the way to the upper arm or at least the elbow. Jaime insists on preserving what remains. The risk of death and further loss is clinically presented, not yet resolved.
24. **Treatment is medically specific and constrained by agency.** Qyburn proposes cutting away rotted tissue, **boiling wine**, and a poultice of **nettle, mustard seed and bread mold**; Jaime refuses **milk of the poppy**, fearing he might awaken without more of his arm. Qyburn uses strong wine and stitches a remaining skin flap over the wrist. The full long-term viability of the stump is not established at chapter end.
25. **Qyburn's status is openly irregular.** He says the **Citadel took his chain**, but does not explain why. He tends wounds competently and has medical experience with Vargo's victims; the chain's loss is a specific anomaly, not yet proof of any single prohibited experiment.
26. **Qyburn transmits distant battle intelligence.** He reports that Stannis was trapped between Tywin and wildfire at the Blackwater, and that rumor credits Tyrion with burning the river. Jaime experiences a vision-like memory of green fire previously dreamed. **This is remembered imagery plus report**, not independent testimony to the Blackwater battle.
27. **The eye wound remains serious.** Qyburn cleans the inflamed cut Brienne inflicted, offers fever herbs and proposes leeching next day. Jaime explicitly asks that Brienne's injured thigh be treated. Their relationship now includes deliberate concern for the other's survival.
28. **End state:** Jaime is missing his right hand and feverish but has undergone debridement; Brienne has Bolton's promised protection; Vargo remains dangerous and politically dependent on Bolton. No safe return to King's Landing, formal ransom settlement or healed stump is demonstrated.

## Rival readings / tests

- **Redemptive action vs cruelty:** Jaime intentionally helps Brienne at considerable pain to himself, but previously assaulted and wounded her. Both must remain in the account. His sacrifice also protects a valuable hostage whose safety is politically useful.
- **Bolton's loyalties:** The Stark flag and Bolton's stern correction of Hoat are facts; they do not guarantee adherence to Robb. Compare Vargo's prior service to Tywin and Bolton's future messages and negotiations before calling a specific conspiracy proved.
- **The missing hand as identity rupture:** A total transformation into a virtuous knight is premature; his resolve includes vengeance, Cersei and Tyrion. The manuscript displays **change under pressure**, not a completed arc.
- **Future Qyburn danger:** A removed chain plus apparent surgical skill suggest a story; several reasons for expulsion are possible. Seek later direct explanation.
- **Rebellion memory:** The Harrenhal oath and Aerys's exclusion belong beside [ASOS 24 Bran](24-bran.md)'s mystery-knight account, without assuming Jaime witnessed every tourney event.
