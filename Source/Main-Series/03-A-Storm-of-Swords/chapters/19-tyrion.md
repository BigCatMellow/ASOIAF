## Sequential plot, decisions and consequences

1. **The Small Council has changed.** Lord Tywin sits in the king's chair at the head of the table, with Cersei and Kevan near him; Tyrion has been relegated to Pycelle's former seat at the foot. **Mace Tyrell, Paxter Redwyne, Mathis Rowan, the High Septon, Varys, Littlefinger and the restored Pycelle** attend. Court courtesy covers the reduction of Tyrion's power.
2. **Tyrion's Blackwater service is minimized.** Mace blandly praises his harbor chain, while Tyrion knows popular songs credit Renly's apparition. Kevan alone greets him warmly and says Lancel speaks well of his courage. Lancel's battle wound still alternates between improvement and decline according to Kevan.
3. **A northern defeat is reported, not personally observed.** Varys announces Randyll Tarly has defeated the Glover/Tallhart contingent outside **Duskendale**, with Helman Tallhart reportedly dead, about a thousand other northern casualties, and Robett Glover retreating toward **Gregor Clegane at Harrenhal**. The figures are council intelligence, not a Tyrion battlefield witness; see the prior setup in [ASOS 4 Tyrion](04-tyrion.md).
4. **Robb's undefeated personal record is distinguished.** Littlefinger explicitly observes that the Young Wolf has not suffered a field defeat himself. Tywin believes Robb has withdrawn to Riverrun and will eventually advance north against the ironborn, leaving Riverlands positions vulnerable to **Daven Lannister and Forley Prester**.
5. **Tyrell's advice is not a reliable authority.** Mace urges Robb to take Moat Cailin. Tyrion recalls Randyll Tarly's van did much of the fighting at Ashford; Mace's Storm's End siege achieved little and he yielded to Ned after the Trident. This is Tyrion's evaluation, not neutral proof Mace is always incompetent.
6. **Balon Greyjoy proposes formal division of the realm.** Kevan reports Balon offered alliance if the crown recognizes his title to the **Iron Islands and North**. Tywin observes the ironborn presently control Moat Cailin, Deepwood Motte and much of the Stony Shore, and threaten western shipping.
7. **Tywin deliberately declines to buy what Balon already provides.** Although Redwyne and Tyrell favor the alliance, Tywin says Balon already fights the Starks without payment and hints that waiting may yield a **better option**. **INFERRED:** he has other correspondence in progress. Neither the recipient nor terms of an alternative bargain are disclosed.
8. **The Vale emerges as a bloodless conquest prospect.** Tyrion volunteers to punish Lysa Arryn, resenting his prior captivity in the Eyrie. Mace dismisses the idea. Littlefinger instead offers to **marry Lysa and bring the Vale into the king's peace**, drawing on prior intimacy with her and on his newly awarded title, **Lord of Harrenhal**.
9. **Harrenhal was not a meaningless gift.** Tyrion realizes the title makes Baelish a plausible marriage prospect to an Arryn widow where his formerly low birth would have been a major obstacle. This is a concrete **status-to-access** transaction, although a successful marriage or control of the Vale is not yet depicted.
10. **Tywin and Kevan have prepared the proposal.** Tyrion reads Kevan's synchronized arguments as evidence it was settled before the open council. The council approves the mission in principle, conditional on Lysa's consent and peace. Tywin offers young Robert Arryn restoration as **Warden of the East** in that event; the boy is still lord of the Eyrie.
11. **An exact maritime lead.** Littlefinger proposes boarding the **Braavosi galley Merling King**, presently beyond King's Landing's chain loading by boat, possibly on the morrow before autumn storms. This is **intended passage**, not evidence a departure has happened or that any person aboard has been identified.
12. **Tyrion inherits the treasury.** When he protests that Littlefinger's departure leaves no master of coin, Tywin appoints **Tyrion** to the office. Tyrion suspects a trap because Petyr's ability to generate and manipulate credit is not equivalent to digging Lannister gold. The finances have not yet been audited in this chapter.
13. **Dornish arrival threatens a different alliance.** Pycelle relays a raven reporting **three hundred Dornishmen** traveling to the wedding. Tywin says Prince Doran will claim his council seat and seek justice for Elia and her children's deaths. **CHARACTER CLAIM:** Tywin's expectation of Doran attending; the arrival and composition of the delegation are not yet observed.
14. **A Tyrell–Martell fault line becomes visible.** Mace is offended the Dornish party did not request passage through his lands. Tyrion considers an alternate route via the Boneway, Summerhall and kingsroad. Mace resents the injury **Oberyn Martell inflicted on Willas**. Mathis Rowan looks especially sickened by discussion of Elia's murdered family as the Lannisters avoid admitting Tywin's complicity.
15. **Victory is converted into property and clientage.** The council grants confiscated rebel holdings and wardships to allies, including **Brightwater Keep and Florent income to Garlan Tyrell**. This is a royal award; actual seizure of every estate is not shown. Redwyne requests a **thirty-year relief on taxes** on Arbor vintages and receives it.
16. **War deaths have precise reported counts.** Varys's list shows **forty-seven lesser lords and 619 knights**, besides numerous common soldiers, killed under Stannis's fiery-heart banners; survivors' heirs are treated as traitors for land redistribution. This is court administrative classification, not individual legal hearings.
17. **A broken religious crown must be replaced.** Addam Marbrand finds crystals from the High Septon's stolen crown; gold was apparently melted. Tywin orders a new crown for Joffrey's wedding. This materially links religious pageantry and political authority.
18. **Varys's wider reports are dismissed.** A purported real **kraken** sinks an Ibbenese whaler near the Fingers, eastern Free Cities may fight, and sailors report a **three-headed dragon in Qarth**. These are **intelligence rumors** with mixed accuracy; Tywin refuses to prioritize them. He is much more concerned about missing **Tyrek Lannister**, whose location remains unknown.
19. **The Wall's plea is intentionally subordinated.** Varys says disturbing messages from the Wall describe wildlings and proposes sending returning Gold Cloak deserters to the Watch. Tyrion recalls Mormont's needs and suggests sending most north. Tywin instead orders their **knees broken** as deterrence and treats wildling incursions as a problem for Robb and Balon, perhaps even useful to the crown. The mutilations are **ordered, not witnessed completed**.
20. **Petyr provides critical private intelligence.** After the others leave, Kevan says Littlefinger warned the Lannisters of a **Tyrell scheme to send Sansa to Highgarden and marry Willas**. This confirms the plot's **discovery by the opposing court faction**. How Petyr learned it, and whether he has any additional hidden motive, remain undetermined.
21. **The prospect of the Tyrell match is closed by preemption.** Tywin sees Sansa as a dynastic route into Winterfell and plans to **marry her to Tyrion before Mace can publicly propose Willas**. Her choice is disregarded, and because Mace has not formally raised the issue, Tywin expects diplomatic deniability.
22. **Cersei is assigned as compensation.** Tywin orders Cersei to remarry to counter Stannis's incest accusations. He discusses Balon, Oberyn, a Redwyne twin, Theon, Quentyn and eventually **Willas Tyrell** as prospective husbands. He views a Cersei–Willas match as a way to reinforce the Reach alliance after depriving them of Sansa. **No Cersei betrothal is actually concluded**.
23. **Tyrion's objections include Sansa's age and consent.** He acknowledges Sansa is still a child, recognizes Joffrey's abuse and objects to forced consummation. Tywin takes the onset of menstruation as marriage eligibility and insists on consummation to forestall challenge. Tyrion's protests are genuine; he also considers material advantages and fears Shae's reaction.
24. **Succession is Tywin's stated strategic mechanism.** He expects ironborn occupation to alienate northern subjects and envisages **a future son of Sansa and Tyrion**, grandson of Ned, obtaining Winterfell with local support. **This is Tywin's scenario, not evidence the North will actually welcome the child.** His disdain for Sansa's agency is central to the design.
25. **Tywin rejects alternatives involving captive inheritance.** Returning Sansa to Catelyn or north would permit marriages with Riverlands/North houses; leaving her unwed at court allows the Tyrell move. Kevan notes that a husband can assert Sansa's claim. He considers Lancel and other cousins if Tyrion refuses; Lancel's incapacity makes him an uncertain candidate.
26. **An unusual marriage-history inventory.** Tywin says Hoster refused to wed Lysa to Tyrion, requesting a 'whole man'; Dorne, Yohn Royce, Leyton Hightower and Florent kin similarly resisted proposed matches. The Tysha marriage is recalled privately by Tyrion. Tywin's humiliating litany shapes Tyrion's eventual reluctant consent.
27. **Robb's marriage is an intelligence opportunity.** Tywin discloses Robb married **Jeyne Westerling**, rupturing the Frey arrangement, and predicts he will produce no children from that intended Frey marriage. This fact cross-checks [ASOS 14 Catelyn](14-catelyn.md); **the unspoken implications of Tywin's confidence are not yet a verified conspiracy**.
28. **Sybell Spicer's ancestry is introduced.** Kevan recalls Jeyne's mother **Sybell Spicer**, her trading grandfather, and an eastern-origin grandmother alleged to have been a **maegi/healer** sought for cures and love potions. The report is tinged with class prejudice and does not prove active magic, poison or a bargain.
29. **Tywin shows suspicious composure.** When Tyrion asks why the Westerlings endangered themselves, Tywin indicates they know the warning of **Castamere** and suggests apparent fools may be cleverer than they appear. This elevates the question of Westerling incentives, but **does not establish that Robb's bedding or marriage was engineered**.
30. **End decision.** Tyrion slowly agrees to the imposed Sansa marriage; the wedding itself, its legal execution, consummation and eventual northern claim lie ahead. Cersei has openly resisted her proposed remarriage. Petyr intends to leave for the Vale; he has not yet departed in this scene.

## Serious competing interpretations and downstream tests

- **Westerling involvement:** Tywin's hints and relief over the broken Frey pact invite suspicion of accommodation. An ordinary alternative is that he anticipates exploiting a rash enemy error after learning of it. Look for **contracts, reciprocal benefits, letters, precise timing and Sybell's acts**, rather than converting demeanor into proof.
- **Sansa's wardrobe:** [ASOS 16 Sansa](16-sansa.md) showed Cersei's unexplained urgent tailoring. Here a royal plan requires Sansa to marry **before** Joffrey's wedding; this is powerful cross-POV support for a **marriage outfit** reading. Cersei may not have originated the strategy; Tywin openly directs it.
- **Petyr and Lysa:** Harrenhal is leverage for a Vale proposal. The council and Tyrion may underestimate Lysa's independent will and the Vale lords; a title and past intimacy are not guaranteed control.
- **Kingdom vulnerability:** Tywin views a wildling invasion as leverage against secessionists. Compare Sam's actual Other killing in [ASOS 18](18-samwell.md); the court's policy fails to account for the distinct existential threat.
- **Dorne–Reach friction:** The new coalition contains old dynastic hostilities, but a later rupture is not guaranteed. Preserve Doran arrival uncertainty.

## End position

Sansa faces an unannounced forced Lannister marriage. Tyrion is named master of coin and reluctantly accepts his father's command. Petyr has obtained authorization for a Vale mission. The crown declines Balon's terms and Watch assistance while redistributing southern rebel lands. None of the intended marriages or deployments has yet happened on page.
