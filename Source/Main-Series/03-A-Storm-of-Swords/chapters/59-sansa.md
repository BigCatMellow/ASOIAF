## Chronological record and interpretive guardrails

1. **Sansa dreams of a restored Winterfell before the wedding.** She dreams of playing with **Lady**, her father and brothers in the godswood. On waking she lists **Robb, Bran, Rickon, Arya, Ned, Catelyn and Septa Mordane as dead**, and believes herself alone. **The reader knows Bran and Arya are alive**; Sansa's comprehensive grief is real but her intelligence is mistaken.
2. **Clouds resemble two castles merging into one.** She watches two castle-shaped clouds drift together and lose their distinct form, lit rose, gold and crimson. **This is her observed visual metaphor**, not proven supernatural forecasting or a literal merger of castles.
3. **Shae and Brella are now Sansa's new attendants.** Tyrion replaced servants he believed were Cersei's spies. **Brella sees collapsing ruins in the cloud**, while Shae admires the apparent gold castle. Sansa dislikes talk of ruins and notices Shae can be insolent. She **does not know their concealed employment relationships** from Tyrion's preceding POV.
4. **The event is explicitly dated to a new century.** Tyrion toasts **three hundred years since Aegon's Conquest**. A royal wedding is scheduled midday at the Great Sept; **a feast of seventy-seven courses** follows in the throne room, after a separate breakfast for Lannister/Tyrell guests.
5. **Tyrion looks exhausted and drinks heavily.** Sansa notices soiled clothing and wine at breakfast, unaware of his secret Shae encounter. Tyrion changes clothes with Podrick's help. **No confrontation over the liaison occurs**.
6. **Podrick's arms are identified accurately.** **Purple-and-white chequy with gold coins in the checks**, a distinctive Payne device. He is a nervous youth and cousin to **Ilyn Payne**, but not implicated by kinship in Ned's killing. His polite stammer contrasts with the executioner's terror in Sansa's memory.
7. **Cersei provides a dynastic bride cloak.** It was worn by **Cersei when married to Robert** and previously by **Joanna Lannister when marrying Tywin**. Sansa sees its threadbare age; the reused material links multiple Lannister unions without proving a particular fertility rite or enchantment.
8. **Wedding gifts expose political and military expectations.** **Jalabhar Xho** gives Joffrey a goldenwood bow with scarlet/green fletched arrows; **Lady Tanda** riding boots; **Kevan** a red leather jousting saddle; **Oberyn** a scorpion-shaped gold brooch; **Addam Marbrand** spurs; **Mathis Rowan** a red pavilion. These are **actual gifts on page**; symbolism is an interpretive follow-up, not automatic conspiracy.
9. **Paxter Redwyne announces a massive new galley.** A model represents the **200-oared** warship **King Joffrey's Valor**, being built on the Arbor. Joffrey vows to use it against **Stannis at Dragonstone**. Construction is in progress, not a completed sail or successful campaign.
10. **Tyrion gifts a unique historical manuscript.** **Grand Maester Kaeth's Lives of Four Kings** treats Daeron I, Baelor I, Aegon IV and Daeron II. **Ser Garlan** says only **four illuminated copies in Kaeth's own hand** existed. Joffrey ignores this; the manuscript's destruction is irreversible on page, not recoverable by assuming ordinary reprinting.
11. **Joffrey taunts Tyrion and sexually threatens Sansa.** He mocks their unconsummated union, threatens to enter her bedchamber after impregnating Margaery and humiliates both publicly. Tyrion chooses silence during the initial insult because he fears escalation, not because he consents.
12. **Mace Tyrell gives a distinctive wedding chalice.** It is about **three feet high**, has **two elaborate handles** and **seven faces** decorated with gems representing **lion, rose, stag, trout, falcon, Martell sun and direwolf**. Joffrey says he wants to replace the wolf with a squid. The cup will be important for reconstructing its later use; **gift giver alone does not prove poisoning**.
13. **Tywin presents a new Valyrian steel sword.** The blade has **red/black rippling patterns** and a cherrywood/gold/red leather scabbard with jeweled lions. Joffrey names it **Widow's Wail**, imagines defeating Stannis's magic sword, and recklessly brandishes it around guests. Later Tyrion 60 reveals the other related sword and discloses the fate of **Ned's Ice**; the material source is not spoken outright at this breakfast.
14. **Joffrey destroys the book with Widow's Wail.** He hacks the illuminated volume to pieces after Tyrion's thoughtful gift; Garlan states only three copies now remain. The violent destruction of knowledge is **directly witnessed** and reinforces the crown's disastrous immaturity.
15. **Tyrion suggests a matching Valyrian-steel dagger.** He specifies **dragonbone hilt**, Joffrey substitutes gold with rubies, and Tyrion's expression becomes troubled. **Sansa does not interpret the reference as proof of the Winterfell catspaw**; Tyrion's conclusion is revealed in [ASOS 60 Tyrion](60-tyrion.md) as his own deduction, not a confession by Joffrey.
16. **Oberyn and Ellaria dispute a different royal history.** Oberyn says **Viserys II** may have poisoned his nephew **Baelor the Blessed**, whereas Tyrion thinks Baelor died from fasting and Viserys governed effectively for fifteen years under predecessors. These are **competing historical claims**, not narrated proof of a royal poisoning.
17. **Ellaria challenges a sanctified Baelor miracle.** She says the viper bites in Dorne were **many**, contrary to septon/singer claims Baelor remained unharmed; the venom allegedly contributed to his lasting debility or oddness. Distinguish her countertradition from contemporaneous medical documentation.
18. **Sansa admires Ellaria's social confidence despite stigma.** She knows Ellaria as Oberyn's acknowledged paramour and mother of two daughters; **Shae had spread hearsay about Lysene goddess worship and her former near-prostitution**. **This is third-party gossip**, not proven biography.
19. **Tyrion questions Sansa about Bran's injury.** In the litter to the sept, he asks about Joffrey's past relations with Robb and Bran and recalls Catelyn once accused him. **Sansa reports Bran fell while climbing and later was allegedly murdered by Theon**, repeating false news about Bran's death.
20. **Tyrion states he never harmed Bran.** This is his firsthand denial consistent with his earlier perspective; he does **not** tell Sansa his new catspaw theory during this conversation. Sansa treats it as a possible trap and maintains courtly caution.
21. **Sansa declines graphic details about Robb and Catelyn.** Tyrion offers to discuss their deaths; she says the knowledge would worsen nightmares. Her silence is **self-protection**, not proof of indifference or uncritical Lannister loyalty.
22. **End state:** The Joffrey–Margaery wedding ceremony is still ahead in this chapter. The historical manuscript has been destroyed, Widow's Wail named, and the seven-faced cup introduced. Sansa maintains outward submission while her knowledge of living siblings is wrong.

## Theory tests

- **Who sent the catspaw?** Tyrion's sharp reaction to the dagger hint supports suspicion about Joffrey, but Sansa witnesses **no explicit admission**. A careful theory ledger must keep alternative operators and the actual dagger ownership/custody trace distinct.
- **Royal weapon provenance:** Two new Valyrian swords and Tyrion's later observation of Ice make a recoverable material chain. The precise forge process is not described here.
- **Joffrey's coming fate:** Wedding ritual and lavish drinking vessels provide opportunity, **not evidence that a named donor planned murder**.
- **False Stark death reports:** Sansa's isolation is genuine and dangerous even while Bran and Arya live. Her later choices may be rational under a false surviving-heirs map.
- **Songs versus history:** The Baelor debate and rare book highlight unreliability of official accounts and the value of comparing motivated historians rather than silently choosing a preferred version.
