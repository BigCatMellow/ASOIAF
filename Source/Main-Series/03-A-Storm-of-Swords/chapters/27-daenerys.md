## Chronological spine / consequence ledger

1. **Dany negotiates for the entire trained force and the trainees.** At an Astapori pyramid meeting with eight leading slave brokers she demands **8,000 fully trained Unsullied**, another **six centuries (600)** nearing completion, and even untrained/castrated boys not yet awarded spiked caps. The eight brokers collectively control the specialist Unsullied trade, despite competing in other slaves.
2. **The identity and status markers of the brokers are precise.** A tokar is reserved for Astapor's freeborn men, with **silver fringe on two**, **gold on five**, and **pearls on the eldest Grazdan's** garment. Four brokers share versions of the name Grazdan. These are on-scene observations and reported sumptuary rules, not universal apparel laws for all Free Cities.
3. **Selling half-trained children is initially disputed.** Some masters object on quality and replacement grounds: supposedly ten years of training and no future inventory. Others see immediate profits. Kraznys says 8,000/600 are negotiable but proposes another 2,000 next year; Dany presses for the youngest boys as well, offering full-trained prices or double.
4. **The slavers have accurately appraised her bargaining power.** Their agents inspected the holds ([ASOS 23 Daenerys](23-daenerys.md)); one negotiator says her goods could buy only about **1,000 Unsullied**, and double prices would halve that to **500**. Dany refuses to sell her **mother's recovered crown**, enslave her people or liquidate their personal horses and belongings.
5. **She offers the three ships.** The cog *Balerion* and galleys *Vhagar* and *Meraxes* raise the negotiating bid to a reported **2,000**, insufficient for her stated ambition. Captain Groleo and shipmasters have protested losing those assets; this is a material sacrifice in her apparent scheme, not free wealth.
6. **Dany then offers Drogon.** She knows the brokers' desire for a dragon and proposes transferring one. The eldest Grazdan demands **the largest black dragon**, her remaining trading goods and all three vessels, allowing her to retain her crown and clothes. She accepts; they exchange words of agreement. **The agreement is real**, but a buyer's ability to control the dragon is not tested.
7. **Missandei is transferred as part of the bargain.** Kraznys says Dany will need an interpreter for the Unsullied and adds the captive Naathi girl as a gift. The girl's reactions are carefully muted; silence is **not** evidence she approved being traded.
8. **Arstan openly objects to the dragon transfer.** He kneels and begs Dany not to abandon a dragon for a slave army; Jorah removes him at her order. She later insists he may advise her privately, but not undermine a queen before foreigners. This is a limitation on open counsel; neither advisor knows the complete plan.
9. **Her hidden Valyrian fluency is revealed to Missandei, not to the merchants.** Dany asks the girl her name **in High Valyrian**, startling her. **Missandei** says she has nowhere else to go. Dany immediately declares her free, offers service as a handmaid with compensation unspecified and a genuine right to leave, while warning she can promise freedom but not safety.
10. **The consequences of slavery are personalized.** Asked how the Unsullied would behave after a war, Missandei says they follow owners' commands, including a potential order to kill themselves. Dany probes why the girl objects to such an order; she reveals that **three Unsullied were once her brothers**. This directly challenges the description of the Unsullied as nonpersons.
11. **Dany remembers Eroeh and asks what rulership is for.** During a private night conversation with Jorah she recalls failing to protect an enslaved Lhazareen girl after Drogo's death, and Viserys's cruelty to her. She frames legitimate monarchy as **justice and protection of the vulnerable**, explicitly contrasting Robert's conquest with that duty.
12. **A dragon-Trident dream revises Rhaegar's defeat.** She dreams herself as **Rhaegar riding a dragon** against Robert's force, whose soldiers wear icy armor and melt in dragonfire. This is a verified dream experience, **not** a factual vision that the Trident fought the Others or that Robert's host literally possessed ice magic.
13. **Quaithe's visit is uncertain in physical status.** Dany wakes feeling someone in her dark cabin and hears a woman's voice reiterate Quaithe's injunction to travel by seeming opposites (north/south, west/east, forward/back, light/shadow). After she opens the door, no masked woman remains. Dany calls it a dream; **vision, glamour, magical projection, or ordinary dream are untested alternatives**.
14. **The morning procession is tiny relative to her ambition.** Dany takes dragon-bearing litter, her bloodriders, Jorah, Belwas, the ship crews and **83 remaining Dothraki** (contrasted with Drogo's formerly huge khalasar), together with around a hundred thin horses and vulnerable followers. She places children, the pregnant and frail inside the column. A banner of a three-headed red dragon on black exists only as her thought.
15. **The Worm river and city's crowds are directly described.** Dany passes river islands, expensive statues, watching Astapori citizens and the tall pyramid quarter. The apparent prosperity exists alongside displays of cruelty, without proving every resident owns slaves.
16. **The chosen transfer site changes the tactical situation.** The army cannot fit in the smaller Plaza of Pride and is marshaled in the **Plaza of Punishment**, by the main gate. The punitive platform is intentionally positioned at an entrance for newly arriving slaves to see, according to Missandei.
17. **A fresh victim illustrates the system.** Dany sees a flayed man covered with flies; Missandei says he raised his hand against his owner. The mutilation is directly visible, the reason is an attributed report. This parallels abuses of the Bloody Mummers and bolsters Dany's avowed justice aim.
18. **The force assembled is larger than 8,600.** There are **8,600 capped trained or near-trained soldiers**, with roughly **5,000 uncapped boys behind them**. Dany identifies children among the latter. Counts are approximate as given on page, not certified postbattle strength.
19. **Material payment has detailed accounting.** Goods displayed include tiger skins, silk, spices, onyx, jade monkeys, colored inks, dark amethysts, pearls, pickled foods, ivory eyes, multilingual books and a gong, with additional ship cargo pledged. These unusually specific cargo objects are sale **inventory**, not magical relics by default.
20. **The decisive exchange is witnessed.** Dany hands Kraznys **Drogon's chain**; he gives her a nine-tailed scourge called **the harpy's fingers**, with dragonbone handle and gilded claw tips. Kraznys verbally confirms the purchased force belongs to her. Command-symbol transfer, unlike dragon obedience, is performed.
21. **She exposes her language advantage at the pivotal instant.** Riding along the ranks she announces **in Valyrian** that they are hers, while the brokers are distracted around Drogon. The pearl-fringed Grazdan notices; most do not. This is the fulfillment of the informational advantage built in ASOS 23.
22. **The dragon refuses the new purported owner.** Kraznys pulls his chain, but Drogon will not leave the litter and threatens him. Dany articulates that **a dragon is not a slave**, lashes Kraznys across the face and orders Drogon to breathe fire. He does, killing Kraznys; later the other two dragons are unchained by Irri and Jhiqui.
23. **The brokers' mounted guards are disorganized.** The dragons frighten horses, while Dothraki, Belwas and other retainers attack officials. The pearl-fringed Grazdan attempts to command the Unsullied to defend the Masters but is struck and dies as his chair-carriers flee. **The Unsullied remain still**, choosing to follow the transferred command rather than their former owners during the visible interval.
24. **The emancipation order is explicit but severe.** Dany orders the Unsullied to kill **Good Masters, soldiers, men wearing tokars or holding whips** while **sparing children younger than twelve** and freeing all enslaved people they encounter. She discards the whip and declares freedom; the soldiers echo her battle cry. The **orders and beginning of mass violence** are narrated; exact citywide casualties, legality, post-liberation governance and compliance outside the plaza are **not** established.
25. **Moral contradiction is preserved.** The plan uses a purchase she intends to overturn, violence against an identifiable ruling class, and a broad instruction to kill anyone meeting clothing/weapon criteria. She intends to free slaves and protect children, but this is not proof all slain people deserved death or that emancipation alone stabilizes the city.
26. **End state:** Kraznys and the senior Grazdan are dead on page; dragons and Unsullied fight under Daenerys's orders in Astapor, with liberation begun. **The entire city and military future are not yet settled at chapter end.** No ship departure, Westerosi invasion or long-term civic government is shown.

## Theory / causal stress tests

- **Advance planning or sudden revolt?** Dany's stubborn need for all Unsullied, hidden understanding, statements that she knows what she must do and discreet prebattle trust of the bloodriders support planning. The **precise timing and instructions** to every follower are not disclosed, so do not invent an earlier detailed meeting transcript.
- **How real was the dragon sale?** Agreement and chain exchange happen, but Dany immediately takes violent action. It is a deliberate betrayal of a slaver bargain in service of emancipation, not a harmless fictitious trade with no consequences.
- **Freedom vs obedience:** The soldiers initially stand immobile and then accept orders against former owners; freed people's own choices, wishes and institution-building are unresolved. The strongest test is what Dany does **after** the victory when their autonomy becomes a governance problem.
- **Prophecy:** The ice-armored Trident is dream material and Quaithe's presence unresolved. Neither proves or rules out Dany's eventual opposition to supernatural ice.
- **Collateral harm:** The instruction targets tokars and whips as markers of oppression but may sweep in noncombatants. Assess its implementation, downstream trauma and alternatives, not a blanket 'bloodless liberation' characterization.
