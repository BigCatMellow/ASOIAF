## Causal and chronological reading

1. **The immediate setting is Harrenhal's bathhouse.** Jaime, recovering from the loss of his right hand, enters a shared steamy room where Brienne is bathing. He can barely undress with one hand, demands an attendant's help, dismisses guards and attendants, and bathes in **Brienne's tub** despite her discomfort. His injury does not erase his ability to behave intrusively.
2. **The wound is improving but far from healed.** Qyburn has used leeches on Jaime's inflamed eye; his right arm remains bandaged and must be kept dry. In the tub he is dizzy and overheated, likely from fever, infection, malnutrition and steam; Qyburn later names these factors. He is not mentally or physically robust merely because he can walk.
3. **Jaime's taunts cross an ethical line before he apologizes.** He uses his missing hand to recall killing Aerys, throwing Bran from the tower, and sex with Cersei, then wounds Brienne with an accusation about failing to protect Renly. After seeing her reaction he says it was unworthy, asks forgiveness and offers a truce. **A sincere-sounding apology coexists with deliberate cruelty.**
4. **Brienne demands a basis for trust.** Jaime hears 'Kingslayer' and 'oathbreaker' again and contrasts his disgrace with Robert's celebrated rebellion; she interprets Robert as acting to save the realm, while Jaime imputes pride and desire. These conflicting portraits are **character judgments**, not a single narrator-certified motive.
5. **Jaime recounts court politics after the Battle of the Bells.** According to his own eyewitness/recollection, Aerys exiled **Jon Connington** and pressured **Lewyn Martell** by retaining Elia as leverage over Dorne; **Jon Darry and Barristan Selmy** tried to regroup loyal forces, **Rhaegar** returned from the south, and Aerys summoned **Tywin**, who gave no reply. Cross-check Harwin's battle history in [ASOS 29 Arya](29-arya.md), without treating all details as independently verified.
6. **The king ordered a hidden network of wildfire caches.** Jaime names locations **under Baelor's Sept, Flea Bottom houses, stables, warehouses, all seven city gates and the Red Keep's cellars**. Only a few trusted pyromancers had access; acolytes were excluded. This is detailed **first-person testimony** from a directly placed Kingsguard, not a current excavation of the caches.
7. **Three pyromancers and one failed dissenter are named.** **Rossart, Belis and Garigus** allegedly prepared the caches. Hand **Lord Chelsted** recognized the danger and confronted Aerys, eventually throwing down his Hand's chain. Aerys burned Chelsted alive and elevated Rossart to Hand. Jaime says Rossart also burned **Rickard Stark** in armor. Preserve personnel, location and alleged orders for independent historical tests.
8. **Aerys's aim was indiscriminate destruction of the capital.** Jaime says after the Trident defeat Aerys planned to leave the city in ashes rather than surrender it. Jaime speculates Aerys expected to emerge **reborn as a dragon**, comparing him to **Aerion Brightflame/Brightfire**; that motive is Jaime's inference about Aerys's internal beliefs.
9. **The retreat of noncombatants was selective.** Aerys sent his queen and **Viserys** to Dragonstone, but kept **Elia and Aegon** as hostages to secure Dorne. This is Jaime's account of deliberate royal leverage, not a direct statement from Elia herself.
10. **Tywin's arrival decided the capital's opening.** Jaime says **Pycelle persuaded Aerys to admit Tywin's army**, ignoring Varys's warning, and attributes Tywin's alignment to waiting for the Trident's result. This supports an opportunism reading of Tywin, though Jaime does not narrate Tywin privately giving that reason.
11. **Aerys ordered Jaime to bring him Tywin's head.** Jaime's attempt to ask leave to negotiate received that demand; he concluded the disguised **Rossart** was heading to issue the wildfire signal. He killed Rossart at a postern gate **before killing Aerys**. This is a directly involved confession, but the fatal command was not witnessed by Brienne.
12. **Jaime says he eliminated the remaining pyromancers after the sack.** He claims to have hunted down and killed **Belis and Garigus** in subsequent days. Belis allegedly offered gold, Garigus begged. **Crucial open point:** the account establishes intent and acts against three identified plotters, **not independently that every cache was found/neutralized**.
13. **The real dilemma is competing vows.** Jaime asks why the Kingsguard should obey a king who would burn his own city while being sworn to protect that king and his secrets. He says Ned judged him sitting on the throne without inviting his story. This is his **reason for long silence**, which remains his perspective; it does not prove Ned would have refused to listen under all circumstances.
14. **Telling Brienne costs Jaime.** As he recounts the buried motive, his damaged self-image shifts from proud Kingslayer to a man unsure who he is without the hand that carried both 'glory' and 'shame'. Under fever and steam he collapses; **Brienne catches him and calls for Qyburn**, then helps wash, shave and dress him. He registers unexpected gentleness.
15. **Qyburn treats both captives pragmatically.** He blames fever/toxins, malnutrition and heat, brings clothes and **licorice steeped in vinegar, honey and cloves**, and supplies Brienne an ill-fitting **pink satin gown**. Jaime refrains from at least one set of cruel jokes about her appearance. **Qyburn's professional role persists despite loss of Citadel chain.**
16. **Roose receives Jaime and Brienne privately in Kingspyre.** The great hall has many unlit hearths, a remote door guard, and only **Elmar Frey** as cupbearer at the table. Prunes, hippocras and one-handed eating are repeatedly emphasized; Bolton performs cool control while Jaime is dependent on others for basic tasks.
17. **Roose enumerates rival ransom incentives.** He reports Edmure offered **1,000 gold dragons** for Jaime's recapture, while Jaime expects Cersei to pay at least **10,000**. Karstark's offer involved **Alys Karstark's hand** for Jaime's head. Amounts are interested reports/estimates, not confirmed paid prices.
18. **Harrion Karstark's custody chain receives a precise claim.** Bolton says **Harrion was held at Harrenhal**, then given Karhold men and **sent with Glover**. He wonders whether Harrion survived **Duskendale**. This is evidence of Roose's stated movements and knowledge; it does **not** directly prove Harrion dead, captured or safe after Duskendale. If Harrion fails to return, **Alys** might remain the significant Karstark heir, per Bolton's speculation.
19. **Bolton's marriage and Frey business are explicit.** He says he chose **Fat Walda Frey** because Walder offered silver equal to the bride's weight. He will attend **Edmure–Roslin** wedding at the Twins and intends to leave Harrenhal for Vargo as bargained. Elmar Frey, his cupbearer, was to marry **Arya Stark** before Robb violated the earlier pact.
20. **Roose claims Arya has been found.** Responding to Brienne's fears, Bolton says Arya lives and is to be returned north, though **no girl, authenticating witness or custody chain is presented**. The reader has just seen the real Arya with Beric, so his statement is **not proof Roose currently possesses her**. A future impersonation/deception is a hypothesis requiring its own source confirmation.
21. **Bolton says Sansa married Tyrion; Brienne is shocked.** This is independently verified by [ASOS 28 Sansa](28-sansa.md), but Bolton's assertion that only the gods can separate the pair omits the **unconsummated marriage problem** explicitly raised in [ASOS 32 Tyrion](32-tyrion.md). He is selectively deploying accurate news to defeat Brienne's sworn mission.
22. **The letter from Riverrun calls Jaime's departure an escape, not an exchange.** Roose challenges Brienne's account of Catelyn's authorization by invoking formal responsibility to Robb. **Riverrun's reporting and Brienne's firsthand instructions conflict**, which makes blame politically negotiable.
23. **The 'Harrenhal curse' is explicitly reinterpreted as Tywin's vengeance.** Bolton says Vargo deserted the Lannisters when promised Harrenhal, expecting Stannis to win; after Blackwater he fears Lannister retribution. Bolton compares this to **the destruction of Houses Reyne and Tarbeck**, not confirmed supernatural property of Harrenhal.
24. **Bolton proposes a rational motive for taking Jaime's hand.** Vargo allegedly intended to disarm Jaime, gain proof for extortion and **damage Bolton's value as a rival holder**, because the sellsword formally answers to Roose. Vargo needs a safe northern refuge via **marriage to Alys Karstark**, not merely gold, and may deliver Jaime dead despite ransom. This detailed explanation is **Bolton's interested theory about Vargo's motives**, not confession by Hoat.
25. **Roose forces Jaime to exonerate him.** Jaime understands that release depends on representing Roose to Tywin/Cersei as **rescuer rather than accomplice**. He promises to do so while privately recognizing an unhelpful answer would return him to Hoat. This is **coerced testimonial bargaining**.
26. **A release route is announced but not executed.** Bolton says Qyburn will decide Jaime's fitness, and **Walton Steelshanks** will escort him with chosen men to King's Landing. **Jaime remains at Harrenhal at the chapter end**, not already reunited with family.
27. **Brienne is expressly excluded from Jaime's protection.** Roose refuses to send her along and says Vargo cannot be deprived of both prizes; the threat of sexual violence/hostage extortion returns as his cynical **sapphire** remark. The promise of hospitality proves conditional and strategically self-serving.
28. **End state:** Jaime has given Brienne the substantive wildfire explanation for Aerys's death; Roose intends to ship Jaime to the Lannisters under Walton while leaving Brienne under Hoat's power; political coordination remains inferred beyond these observable bargains.

## Rival readings and source tests

- **Jaime hero or oathbreaker?** His confession makes killing Aerys morally intelligible as preventing mass atrocity, but does not erase his attempt to kill Bran, incest or cruelty. Look for independent wildfire material/caches and chronological confirmation of the pyromancers' deaths.
- **Bolton's real alignment:** Formal northern heraldry and Bolton's spoken support for Robb contrast with his overt bid to be seen favorably by the Lannisters. Demonstrate any advance planning with independent communications rather than retroactively labeling each pragmatic act treason.
- **Harrion and Alys succession:** Roose's testimony is highly significant, but survival and captivity require corroboration; don't promote a presumed death at Duskendale to historical fact.
- **Is Arya really 'found'?** Reader sees Arya elsewhere; possibilities include false intelligence, deliberate deception or another girl designated Arya. Avoid assuming a named substitute absent a witness.
- **Brienne's release:** Roose's planned favor to Jaime requires sacrificing her, demonstrating a precise limit to his earlier benevolence.
