## Chronological source record

1. **Jon has been confined for four days in an ice cell** roughly five feet in each dimension, too cramped to stand or lie flat. Slynt releases him only after **Aemon writes Cotter Pyke in defense of his loyalty**. This is significant institutional opposition to Slynt, not an exoneration by an elected Lord Commander.
2. **Janos Slynt and Alliser Thorne give Jon a coerced assassination assignment.** Mance has requested a parley; they send Jon as ostensible envoy, then reveal he must **kill Mance**. They threaten renewed ice-cell incarceration if he refuses. They exploit both Aemon's defense and Mance's distrust of Jon to arrange an outcome likely fatal to him.
3. **Jon understands killing under truce as dishonorable.** He remembers **Qhorin's earlier principle** that a Watchman's life and honor may be spent for the realm. These recollections explain why he goes; they are **not proof he approves the assassination**.
4. **The gate and battlefield still show severe damage.** Jon sees the **ruined turtle, dead mammoth, slain giant, cracked barrels and pitch patches** during descent and passage across the killing ground. A **raven on a giant's shattered head** calls 'Snow'; the behavior is witnessed, while deliberate messaging or warging is unproven.
5. **Tormund meets Jon in no-man's-land rather than attacking him.** Tormund reports about **200 free folk and a dozen giants** dead in the Watch's defense. This is **his estimate**, not a full casualty census.
6. **Tormund and Jon toast both Donal Noye and Mag the Mighty.** Tormund marvels that a **one-armed blacksmith** slew the giant king; this is a rare direct instance of respect between enemies. The two also toast **Ygritte**, whose fatal archer Jon still does not know.
7. **Tormund confirms the failed Thenn assault was part of Mance's strategy.** **Styr** had promised to open the gate and even bring down the Wall; Tormund disliked the Magnar. Jon understands the previous catastrophic wall/stair collapse as a material defense.
8. **Longspear Ryk and Tormund's daughter Munda reportedly become a couple after wildling bride-stealing.** Tormund mentions sons **Toregg and Torwynd**; he says Munda resisted strongly but ultimately likes Ryk. This is **Tormund's humorous family account**, not Munda's direct testimony about choice or consent.
9. **Mance's camp is populous and poorly fortified.** Jon sees **cookfires, children, goats, sheep, horses, spearwives and painted shield-bearers**, not simply an army of raiders. Its disorder helps explain vulnerability to disciplined cavalry.
10. **Harma Dogshead and Varamyr Sixskins distrust Jon.** Harma calls for his death; Varamyr's **shadowcat and two wolves** threaten him, though Tormund intervenes. Jon is not secure under truce even before the surprise attack.
11. **Varamyr claims a transfer of skinchanging control.** He says **Orell's eagle** was already joined to its former human skinchanger and that Varamyr entered it after Orell died; he adds that **some remnant of Orell persists inside him**, still hating Jon. This is **an experienced skinchanger's account, not an independent experiment proving the full metaphysics**.
12. **Mance knows Castle Black's actual weakness through that eagle.** He details the Watch's low numbers, depleted **pitch, oil, arrows and spears**, broken stair and limited-capacity cage. The claimed aerial reconnaissance is consistent with the earlier eagle episodes but exact dates are not given.
13. **Dalla is in labor or very close to it, with Val assisting.** Mance allows the sisters to remain when he brings Jon into the tent; this means Jon's coerced plan would require killing a man beside his pregnant partner and her sister.
14. **A huge curved rune-banded horn is physically present.** Jon sees a horn approximately **eight feet along its curve, with old gold bands engraved in runes**. Mance identifies it as **the Horn of Winter, once blown by Joramun**. **Identity and magical function are Mance's claims**, not demonstrated by a sounding or any independent test.
15. **Mance says Ygritte's account of an unsuccessful search was deliberately incomplete.** He says he never trusted Jon and concealed possession. This can reconcile the earlier statement that wildlings opened many graves without finding a horn, but the **true findspot and authenticity remain undisclosed**.
16. **Dalla cautions against using magic.** She cites the Horned Lord's warning that sorcery has no safe handle; she also identifies the crucial consequence: if the Wall falls, the Others have no equivalent barrier to stop them. **Her reasoning does not establish that the horn could actually destroy the Wall**.
17. **Mance says his original preference was taking a gate rather than destroying the Wall.** He lists attempted infiltration by **Styr/Jarl**, raids and feints drawing Marsh away and alternatives including rafts across the Bay of Seals, assaults on the Shadow Tower and digging entrances at abandoned castles. These are **plans or threats**, not verified separate attacks in progress.
18. **His stated real objective is survival from the Others.** He says the Others are stronger as days shorten, kill free folk and raise their dead, and that neither **giants, Thenns, Hornfoots, ice-river clans** nor he can defeat them. His account is consistent with prior supernatural encounters, but **he remains an interested negotiator**.
19. **Mance admits he came south to hide behind the Wall, unlike old conquest-seeking kings.** He cites **Raymun Redbeard, Bael, Gendel and Gorne and the Horned Lord** as earlier comparators. The historical characterization is **his speech**, not direct ancient evidence.
20. **Jon identifies the central political problem: can Mance rule the people admitted?** Mance claims free folk choose proven fighters rather than hereditary kings; he says he defeated other contenders, including killing three of five rivals while **Tormund and the Magnar** were the other two. He refuses **kneeling, southern taxes and southern law**. Thus admitting the migrants would not automatically create a governable allied polity.
21. **Mance offers the horn in exchange for passage.** If the Watch opens the gate, Mance says he will surrender it and the Wall will stand. If refused, **Tormund will sound it three days later at dawn**. **This is conditional threat testimony**, not future action shown.
22. **Jon considers smashing the horn but is interrupted.** He recognizes Slynt and Thorne would regard a bloodless meeting as betrayal. No blow is made against Mance or the horn, and **no horn of claimed destructive power is sounded**.
23. **An outrider horn signals troops approaching from the east.** **Varamyr** uses his eagle to report mounted armored men from several directions. At first Mance suspects **Eastwatch under Cotter Pyke**, since black-clad outriders initiate contact; the later larger force is different.
24. **Harma charges with roughly thirty raiders, Tormund forms spears and Mance attempts a wedge.** The camp lacks orderly battle formations. Civilian **women, children and dog carts** become entangled in the sudden fighting, worsening its human toll.
25. **Varamyr and his eagle suffer an extraordinary violent incident.** As he watches through the eagle's eyes, the **eagle erupts in visible flames high overhead**; Varamyr falls screaming and writhing, his wolves fight, and shadowcat bolts. **The cause is not witnessed**; a magical attack is plausible, but naming Melisandre as perpetrator exceeds this chapter's observation.
26. **Three major mounted columns overwhelm the free folk.** Armored knights attack from **east, northeast and north**, pinching camps; men-at-arms and bowmen follow. The mammoths disrupt part of the cavalry, but many unarmored wildlings are routed and tents burn.
27. **Mance's immediate fate is ambiguous.** Jon sees **Mance's mare speared and a tide of cavalry closing over him**, then loses sight. **Harma's severed head is visibly raised on a pole**, Tormund's lines break, and Jon sees civilian flight. Neither Mance's death nor capture is established by this POV alone.
28. **The attacking force is unmistakably Stannis's.** Royal standards show a **gold field and black stag** and **yellow cloth with a flaming heart**; riders shout Stannis's name. **Stannis's intervention is witnessed**, connecting to Davos's transmission of the Watch warning, but this Jon POV does not narrate the sea route.
29. **Jon retreats into Dalla's tent.** The chapter does not confirm the birth, Val's safety, Mance's custody or what happened to the great horn during and after the attack.
