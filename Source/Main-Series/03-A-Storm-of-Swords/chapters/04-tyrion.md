## Chapter chronology and character agency

1. **RECOVERING WITHOUT CONTROL:** Tyrion is still injured and feverish in **Maegor's Holdfast** when **Bronn** arrives. He suspects servants may work for Cersei and she could finish **Ser Mandon Moore's attack**, but **the murderous sponsorship remains his conviction, not a witnessed fact**. Bronn says the summons was **four days ago** rather than Tyrion's fever-distorted estimate of a fortnight; the difference underscores his impaired time sense.
2. **BRONN'S KNIGHTHOOD AND HERALDRY:** Bronn is now **Ser Bronn of the Blackwater**, knighted by the Kingsguard after battle on Tywin's orders. He adopts **a green burning chain on a smoke-gray field** as sigil, directly appropriating Tyrion's river-boom idea into his own identity. Tyrion believes he had promised the promotion first. Bronn wears cleaner, costly clothes, and his political independence from his former employer is increasing.
3. **PUBLIC RETELLING OF SANDOR AND JAIME:** Bronn confirms **Mandon Moore died** and **Sandor Clegane vanished rather than died**, while gold-cloak gossip declares Sandor a coward after three costly sorties. Bronn's report doesn't reproduce all of Sandor's personal terror from `ACOK 59` or his contact with Sansa in `ACOK 62`. Heroic credit is being simplified into public rumor.
4. **JACELYN BYWATER ACTUALLY DEAD:** Bronn reports **Ser Jacelyn Bywater ('Ironhand')** tried to stop gold cloaks deserting after **Cersei recalled Joffrey**. He was struck by **an arrow in the neck**, then **dragged down and killed by his own frightened men**. The arrow's shooter and whether the shot was arranged are **unknown**; the soldiers' killing is reported. This is stronger testimony than Tyrion's prior uncertainty about the commander's fate and corroborates Lancel's analysis of the morale cost in `ACOK 62`.
5. **POSTWAR CONTROL OF THE WATCH:** Tywin has appointed **Ser Addam Marbrand**, a well-regarded westerman, to command the City Watch. Tyrion recognizes that **his appointed commander, authority and network have been displaced**. Timett's Burned Men have left with plunder; **Shagga's Stone Crows remain in the kingswood**; Chella's Black Ears were rebuffed and humiliated at the River Gate. All are Bronn's reports, not his contemporaneous observation of each clan departure.
6. **ALAYAYA'S FATE CONFIRMED AND WORSE:** Bronn says Cersei released **Alayaya**, the hostage mistaken for Shae in `ACOK 54`, **after having her publicly whipped, stripped and expelled naked and bleeding** about eight or nine days previously. The beating is a reported completed act with credible access through Bronn. Tyrion realizes Alayaya was harmed merely for serving as **a protective decoy for Shae**, whom he never disclosed to Cersei.
7. **THE MORAL COST OF TYRION'S THREAT:** Tyrion recalls he threatened to inflict anything done to Alayaya on **eight-year-old Tommen**, recognizes the horror of whipping a child, and feels both vengeful and relieved when told Tommen is **no longer his hostage**. This does not erase the cruelty of the prior threat; **Tommen has not actually been tortured by Tyrion**.
8. **TOMMEN'S CUSTODY CHANGES:** With Bywater dead, **Cersei sent the Kettleblacks to Rosby and recovered Tommen**. Tyrion's double-paid agents **Osney and Osfryd** now enjoy newly awarded knighthoods and apparently prefer the queen's improved patronage. Bronn explains they had been loyal only as long as Tyrion's compensation exceeded hers. This confirms Cersei's completed reversal of Tyrion's Tommen security scheme.
9. **STANNIS'S ACTUAL EXIT:** Bronn says **Salladhor Saan's Lyseni ships**, stationed outside the Blackwater chain (`ACOK 58 Davos`), put to the bay shore and **evacuated many of Stannis's men** once the relief attack succeeded. The flight was chaotic, with men battling to board. This provides an operational explanation for **Stannis's escape** and contradicts unverified post-battle claims he was necessarily killed at King's Landing, though **Bronn is not shown personally boarding with him**.
10. **RENLY'S GHOST STORY STILL A STORY:** Bronn personally saw **banners and rout from the chain winches**, and reports many civilians claim to have watched **Renly in green armor** charge and kill foes. He does not identify the wearer by name and doubts the precision of the conflicting public stories. The apparition's **practical morale effect** on soldiers formerly in Renly's host is plausible; the chapter does not show a ghost or confirm a specific disguised knight.
11. **DUSKENDALE NEW MAJOR CAUSAL LINK:** Bronn reports Robb's northmen are moving **toward Duskendale**. Later Tywin specifically names **Ser Helman Tallhart and Robett Glover**, and says he has sent **Randyll Tarly to confront them and Gregor Clegane up the kingsroad to block their retreat**. He estimates they constitute **one-third of Stark's strength**. `ACOK 64 Arya` gave the *earlier on-page order by Roose Bolton* for Tallhart and Glover to attack those same lands. The triangulation is **strong evidence that Bolton's order exposed a major Northern force to a deliberate pincer**, but **there is no directly observed communication or pact proving Bolton intended Tywin to destroy them**. Test route, messenger origin, troop composition, losses and whether Roose kept his own men out.
12. **THE LOSS OF HIS PRIVATE GUARD:** Tywin's brother **Kevan** paid out Tyrion's hired guards and dismissed them, ending his independent King's Hand security unit. Bronn refuses the proposition that he still commands Hand guards, reminding Tyrion **Tywin is Hand**. Still, Bronn is willing to serve Tyrion if paid. The relationship is openly transactional rather than pure loyalty.
13. **MARGAERY'S PUBLIC GOODWILL CAMPAIGN:** Although **Margaery has not arrived in King's Landing**, Bronn says the Tyrells send **hundreds of wagonloads of Highgarden food** to distribute **in her name**, alongside conspicuous golden rose badges. Smallfolk praise the Reach while Tyrion, who kept the city alive during famine and siege, is resented. This is concrete political use of food supply and reputation; Bronn's colorful accounts of sexual favors for every Tyrell soldier are **generalized gossip**, not reliable individualized facts.
14. **ACTUAL PHYSICAL LIMITATION:** Getting dressed requires **Bronn and Podrick** to help Tyrion; his **arrow wound at the arm-shoulder junction** still produces **pus and blood**. **Maester Frenken**, rather than the earlier Ballabar, has been changing dressings. Tyrion uses **honeyed dreamwine** containing a small amount of poppy and a **walking stick**. Bronn later **carries him up the steps** to Tywin's tower. This is continued major disability, not merely a scar affecting vanity.
15. **GARRISON AND TYREK ACCOUNTING:** Newly appointed Watch captain **Addam Marbrand** reports **approximately 4,400 surviving gold cloaks**, down from deaths and desertion, and says **Cersei refuses further dismissals** despite cost. Tywin remains determined to locate missing **Tyrek Lannister**, son of **Tygett**, who vanished in the earlier capital riot shortly after marrying **infant Lady Ermesande Hayford**. Prior searches by Bywater and Varys failed. **Tyrek is missing, not proven dead**, and the infant-wife marriage creates a hereditary claim worth tracking.
16. **JOFFREY WEDDING DATE:** Tywin says Joffrey and Margaery will wed **on the first day of the new year and the new century**, grounding the intended date of Dontos's escape plan in `ACOK 65 Sansa`. The timeline is Tywin's stated schedule, **not proof the ceremony happens** or that its planned calendar is uncontested.
17. **TYWIN'S ACCOUNT OF ALAYAYA AND THE HEALER:** Tywin says **Maester Ballabar came with Lord Redwyne** and was a capable healer; he and Cersei reportedly visited Tyrion while he was near death. Tyrion dismisses these explanations through deep distrust, but the chapter **does not prove the sedation was intended as murder**. Tywin later speaks as though he **authorized or endorsed Alayaya's whipping** and treats Tyrion's threats against Tommen and Joffrey as a disgrace against his own blood; the exact division of instruction between Tywin and Cersei remains not independently detailed.
18. **HONOR CLAIM AND STRATEGIC CREDIT:** Tyrion asks for recognition of his chain and sacrifice. Tywin says his own attack on Stannis's flank was decisive, emphasizes Tyrell allies, and assigns much of the wildfire production credit to Cersei. He acknowledges **Tyrion's chain was clever and essential**, and his Myrcella–Dorne treaty secured a useful agreement. The scene distinguishes **political credit allocation** from the actual multicomponent battle record (`ACOK 58–61`).
19. **DORNE AND OBERYN-ERA OBLIGATIONS:** Tywin confirms **Myrcella reached Sunspear** and reports that **Arys Oakheart** wrote she likes **Arianne Martell** and Trystane is fond of her. Tywin dislikes giving Doran a Lannister hostage and worries Tyrion has promised **vengeance for Elia** as well as a **council place**. Tyrion insists it was **justice**, not vengeance; Gregor Clegane's fate is implicated. The actual Martell objectives, vengeance target, and what Doran will demand remain prospective.
20. **CASTERLY ROCK CLAIM:** Tyrion finally demands to be recognized as **rightful heir of Casterly Rock**, reasoning that **Jaime forfeited legal claims on joining the celibate, landless Kingsguard**. Tywin refuses absolutely despite not disputing Jaime's Kingsguard restrictions. The legal argument and Tywin's personal opposition are now explicit rather than latent.
21. **TYWIN'S LETHAL PARENTAL CONTEMPT:** Tywin blames Tyrion for **Joanna's death in childbirth**, calls him malformed and unworthy, and says law forces him to recognize the Lannister name because he **cannot prove Tyrion isn't biologically his**. This is **Tywin's hateful rhetoric**, **not evidence Tyrion is the son of another man**. His insult instead exposes an irrational and enduring dynastic grievance.
22. **ULTIMATUM ON SEX AND SHAE:** Tywin calls attention to Tyrion's adolescent marriage to **Tysha**, his camp lover and **Alayaya**, repeats that Tyrion's threat against Tommen brought disgrace, and says any further sex worker found **in Tyrion's bed will be hanged**. Tyrion refuses to name Shae; **Tywin does not identify her in this chapter**, and the direct threat creates major forward danger for her. Tyrion leaves the confrontation with **no acknowledged Rock succession** and no restored Handship.

## Rival theories and specific future tests

- **Bolton's Duskendale exposure:** `ACOK 64` supplies Bolton's outbound order, `ASOS 4` Tywin's interception orders. A planned sacrifice would fit that juxtaposition, but **could still reflect a dangerous strike Roose thought feasible and Tywin opportunistically exploited**. Seek whether Bolton sent Glover/Tallhart disproportionately while protecting his own men, and whether a private Tywin–Bolton channel is later confirmed.
- **Moore's patron:** Tyrion is certain Cersei commissioned his killing, but only Moore's repeated attempt is independently documented. Tywin's dismissal and Cersei's political victory offer incentives yet **not evidence of payment**.
- **Tyrek's disappearance:** The infant bride and Hayford inheritance make this **more than a random lost boy**; possible abduction, riot death or deliberate hidden claimant remain open pending direct trace.
- **Tywin parentage accusation:** His ugly line about lacking proof Tyrion is his can fuel paternity speculation, but there is **no positive witness of another father**. Treat as hostile insult at this point.
- **Food and legitimacy:** The Tyrells gain goodwill by supplying starving smallfolk even though Tyrion created the city's defenses. Their court power is reinforced by a **practical economic service**, not merely a marriage ceremony.
