## Causal sequence, force accounting and political outcomes

1. **The next target is Yunkai, not an already subdued city.** Dany and Jorah reconnoiter approximately **5,000 defenders**: Yunkai's mainly slave infantry in the center, with **Second Sons** and **Stormcrows** on the flanks, about **500 mounted sellswords each**. Jorah thinks victory easy, but Dany fears heavy casualties would leave her unable to take the city.
2. **The emblem itself encodes labor specialization.** Yunkai's harpy grasps **a whip and iron collar** rather than Astapor's chain. Jorah says Yunkai trains **bed slaves**, not soldiers equal to the Unsullied. This explains why the battle's social structure differs from Astapor's.
3. **Dany summons three groups separately.** She calls **Stormcrows at midday**, **Second Sons two hours later**, and Yunkish envoys in the evening. Splitting negotiations prevents coordination, tests factional loyalty and supplies intelligence before battle.
4. **The Unsullied independently elect Grey Worm.** Dany earlier ended the custom of reassigned daily slave names, told soldiers to choose officers and they overwhelmingly chose **Grey Worm** as commander. He retains the name because it belonged to the **day of his emancipation**, not because he approves of slave naming. **Jorah trains him**; he is portrayed as quick to learn and conscientious.
5. **The order is to spare captives who abandon arms.** Dany expressly tells Grey Worm to leave alive enslaved Yunkish combatants who flee or surrender, thinking they may join the free after battle. This is a **specific tactical and ethical restraint**, not proof all units comply in chaos.
6. **Dany has two very different camps.** The defensible Unsullied camp is entrenched, orderly, staked and guarded; immediately beyond it is an unfortified freed-people camp **roughly five times as large**, with old people, women, children, livestock and almost no protective equipment. She gave Astapor to a **healer, scholar and priest-led former-slave council**, but tens of thousands chose to follow her instead.
7. **Liberation has a logistical cost already.** Few former slaves have pack animals; **one in ten** may be physically capable of fighting and none trained, by Dany's assessment. The group eats local food as it moves. Jorah and bloodriders advise abandoning them; Dany refuses because the freedom to travel with her is part of their newly claimed autonomy. **The inability to support them indefinitely remains open.**
8. **Dothraki riders are scarce.** Her combat-capable mounted khalasar is **thirty-odd riders**, with many boys and old men; differentiate this number from the previously reported **eighty-three followers** at Astapor, which is not a count of active cavalry. **Unsullied size is rhetorically described as 10,000** in negotiations, distinct from ASOS 27's more granular 8,000 + 600 capped and approximately 5,000 trainees.
9. **The Stormcrows have three formally equal captains.** **Prendahl na Ghezn** (Ghiscari), **Sallor the Bald** (Qartheen), and **Daario Naharis** (Tyroshi with blue hair/beard and yellow clothes) arrive. Prendahl speaks, rejects Dany's offer of retention of Yunkai wages plus new rewards and threatens her; Dany protects their **safe conduct** despite Belwas's angry reaction.
10. **A different company has a notoriously dangerous captain.** **Mero, the Titan's Bastard**, commands the Second Sons, boasts of sexual violence and personal prowess, but entertains Dany's offer of higher pay and future campaigns. She gives him **a wagon of Astapori wine** to share with his men. Jorah and Arstan report Mero's cruelty and poor reputation; these are warnings, not a scene of atrocities by him in this chapter.
11. **History is used as tactical intimidation.** Dany invokes the **Second Sons' retreat at Qohor** against the celebrated Three Thousand Unsullied, a claim Mero dismisses as before his command. This is a reported precedent relevant to sell-sword loyalty, not direct knowledge of their current intentions.
12. **The Yunkish ambassador offers 50,000 gold marks.** **Grazdan mo Eraz** brings a cedar chest of newly minted currency embossed with a stepped pyramid and a harpy; he demands she withdraw while insisting Astapor's liberation is barbarity. **This is a bribe for departure, not a formal slave-emancipation plan.**
13. **Dany counters with an abolition ultimatum.** In **three days**, Yunkai must give every enslaved person freedom plus arms, food, clothes, wealth and goods of their choosing from their former masters, admit her Unsullied for an inspection, and avoid massacre or plunder in return. This is a **demand**, not proof its full terms will later be carried out.
14. **A safe-conduct inconsistency must not be hidden.** Dany orders **Drogon to breathe fire** near the ambassador, singeing his tokar and endangering him. Grazdan protests that he was promised safe conduct. Unlike her restraint toward the Stormcrows, she **does intimidate an envoy with actual flame**. The dragon does not burn Grazdan to death; Arstan douses the clothing.
15. **The three-day clock is deliberately deceptive.** Immediately after the Yunkish leave, Dany tells Jorah and her bloodriders she will **attack roughly an hour after midnight that very night**, while the sellswords await morning answers and the ambassador thinks there is a three-day deadline. She had not explicitly promised the sellswords peace for the night, but a threatened enemy might nevertheless consider this **bad-faith negotiation**.
16. **The battle plan exploits disunity.** Campfires conceal her movement; **Jhogo** offers to suppress scouts; **Grey Worm's Unsullied** strike flanks while Dany's Dothraki horse go in a center wedge. Unlike the largely spontaneous-seeming Astapor shock, this is an explicit battle design discussed with officers.
17. **Daario brings his own violent proof of defection.** Just before midnight he enters bearing **the severed heads of Prendahl and Sallor**, the other Stormcrow leaders. He claims command and declares love for Dany. The murders are evidenced by heads, while the sincerity of his stated romantic rationale and loyalty cannot be deduced from brutality alone.
18. **Dany overrules Jorah on deploying Daario.** Jorah wants him held under guard until battle is finished; Dany reasons the deaths of his previous co-captains prove commitment, and orders his **500 Stormcrows to hit the Yunkish rear**. It pays off that night, but cannot establish reliability in future crises.
19. **Jorah's objections mix expertise with possessiveness.** Dany openly values his experience but tells him she does **not desire him romantically**, and resents his attempts to exclude every possible rival counselor/man. Jorah submits coldly; Dany privately worries she was too harsh. **His battlefield counsel can be sincere and strategically valid even as jealousy shapes how he expresses it.**
20. **Dany reflects on reproduction and dragons.** She believes Mirri Maz Duur's words mean she cannot bear a living child and sees Drogon, Rhaegal and Viserion as heirs/children. This is **Dany's interpretation of Mirri's prophecy**, not independent biological diagnosis. Her dragons remain too small to bear a rider; future mounted combat is aspiration, not present capability.
21. **Arstan offers a significant Rhaegar account while battle rages off-page.** He describes Rhaegar as exceptionally skilled but melancholy and not fond of tournaments or war. Rhaegar reportedly defeated **Steffon Baratheon, Jason Mallister, Oberyn Martell and Simon Toyne** at an earlier Storm's End tournament, broke twelve lances with **Arthur Dayne**, but lost the final.
22. **Harrenhal and Lyanna are placed under direct questioning.** Arstan says **Rhaegar won Lord Whent's Harrenhal tournament** in the year of false spring; Dany protests that he crowned **Lyanna Stark** despite being married to **Elia Martell**. Arstan will not speculate about his internal reasons; Dany repeats Viserys's story that Rhaegar 'stole' Lyanna. **Neither reported motive nor voluntariness of Lyanna's departure is verified by these speakers.**
23. **Rhaegar's association with Summerhall is unusually intimate.** Arstan says Rhaegar liked to sleep at its ruins alone with a harp, producing songs about loss, love and doomed kings. The music and grief are attributed to an eyewitness-like intimate source; Arstan has not yet explained what happened at Summerhall or why Rhaegar felt a sense of doom.
24. **The night attack's result is reported by Jorah, not depicted.** He returns announcing **Stormcrows changed sides, the Yunkish slave infantry broke and the Second Sons were too drunk to fight effectively**. He estimates **about 200 enemy deaths (mostly Yunkish), at most twelve of Dany's**, and **several thousand captives**. These are commander reports, not a full counted roll or eyewitness battle POV from Dany.
25. **Mero escapes; no capture is established.** Jorah says the Titan's Bastard fled and men are searching; Grazdan already returned to Yunkai to report the ultimatum. Dany orders that enemies who pledge loyalty be spared and considers keeping the Second Sons as a company if enough join.
26. **Yunkai opens its gates on the third day.** The next day the host approaches the walls; on the **morning of the third day** slaves begin filing out. This confirms a **real liberation of many enslaved people**, but the chapter does not show a complete house-by-house Unsullied search, inventories of distributed goods, abolition's legal permanence, or transfer of Yunkai's government.
27. **The crowd names her 'Mhysa'.** A freed man carrying a child cries **Mhysa**, glossed by Missandei as **'Mother' in old Ghiscari**. Multiple languages repeat equivalent words; thousands gather and reach for her. Dany chooses to ride among them despite Jorah's warning. The overwhelming gratitude is witnessed, while the moral and administrative durability of the new society remains **UNKNOWN**.
28. **End state:** Dany has taken a decisive open-field victory, broken Yunkai's defense, acquired Daario's Stormcrows, and seen freed people exit the gates. She has **not** yet ruled Yunkai or resolved the mass-followers' economic needs; Mero remains at large.

## Theory tests

- **Is Dany learning to conquer without ruling?** She correctly assesses supply limits and spares surrendering slaves, but tens of thousands follow from Astapor and no completed Yunkai settlement is administered. Test city-governance follow-through rather than inferring a permanent victory from one crowd scene.
- **Does 'Mother' confer legitimacy?** Popular gratitude is real and freely expressed here; it is **not necessarily consent to indefinite royal sovereignty**, and her own identification with motherhood may complicate how she weighs personal attachment against policy.
- **Daario's loyalty:** The heads prove a defection, not durable fidelity. Jorah's opposition may stem from both professional knowledge and jealousy; neither alone settles the question.
- **Rhaegar and Lyanna:** Arstan's refusal to assign motives and Viserys's interested retelling leave alternatives open. Compare the mystery knight story and other Harrenhal witnesses.
- **The price of surprise:** A deadline and invitation to negotiate create expectations that Dany exploits militarily. Evaluate both low battlefield deaths and possible trust costs.
