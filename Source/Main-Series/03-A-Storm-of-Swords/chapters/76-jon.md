# ASOS 76 — Jon: Stannis offers Winterfell and a conditional settlement for the free folk

**RESEARCH / SEQUENTIAL CLOSE READING — FIRST PASS COMPLETE; INDEPENDENT CHALLENGE OPEN**

Primary text: [ASOS 76 Jon](../text/077-jon.md), fully read 2026-10-17. See [evidence standards](../../../../SOURCES.md).

## Chronological findings

1. **Jon is training Satin with sword and shield**, despite Bowen Marsh's removal of Jon from formal duty. His work fills a real training gap and shows continued service rather than withdrawal from Castle Black.
2. **Melisandre summons Jon** to speak with Stannis in private, atop the Wall. She is escorted by queen's men initially but rides the cage with Jon alone. Jon dresses in Watch black and takes Longclaw.
3. **Melisandre's warmth is directly experienced.** She touches Jon's cheek in the cold cage and her ruby appears to pulse. Jon notices an odor like heated iron, smoke and blood. Her claim that R'hllor's fire lives in her is an interpretation, not a physical diagnosis.
4. **Stannis hears Jon's account of Qhorin Halfhand's death and infiltration, and believes him.** He admits knowing Janos Slynt's character and respecting Eddard Stark's honesty. The king's acceptance is not a formal acquittal by the Night's Watch.
5. **Jon attributes Sam's successful dragonglass weapon to Ghost finding a cache** beneath the Fist, wrapped in a ranger cloak and containing several blades, arrowheads and spearheads. The person who planted the cache is unknown.
6. **Stannis remembers Donal Noye** as the smith who forged Stannis's first sword and Robert's warhammer. Jon corrects any impression that he alone held the gate; Noye died defending the tunnel from Mag the Mighty.
7. **Dalla has died giving birth**; her son survives in Val's care. Jon corrects the king's mistaken identification of Val as Mance's partner. Val has asked to present Mance his child, but the audience is not granted in this scene.
8. **Jon asks about the eagle that burned while Varamyr was skinchanging.** Melisandre makes a figurative theological response but does not give an explicit physical account of causing the flames.
9. **Stannis asks for evaluations of Mance, Rattleshirt and Tormund.** Jon considers Mance honorable in his own culture, Rattleshirt vicious, and Tormund potentially either a valuable friend or dangerous adversary. These are assessments grounded in personal contact.
10. **Stannis credits Davos Seaworth for persuading him to travel north** and defend the realm before pursuing his right to rule it. This independently confirms the political consequence of the Watch's appeal and the counsel Davos began in ASOS 63.
11. **The Other threat becomes Stannis's stated strategic priority.** Melisandre calls its supposed source a god of night and terror. Her religious cosmology and Stannis's political objectives must be separated from observable wights and Others.
12. **Stannis says he needs the North.** He cites Bolton's new royal position and ironborn control of Moat Cailin, Deepwood Motte, Torrhen's Square and the Stony Shore. He lacks the local legitimacy and manpower to restore northern order unaided.
13. **Stannis offers legal legitimacy and Winterfell to Jon.** Jon would receive the name Stark and the title Lord of Winterfell if he pledges fealty. Neither a decree nor an oath has happened at this checkpoint.
14. **Jon's Night's Watch oath is a direct obstacle.** He swore before a heart tree to hold no land and father no children. He recognizes the proposed departure as a real violation, not an infiltration ordered by Qhorin.
15. **Melisandre requires religious realignment**, urging Jon to reject old-god vows, burn weirwoods and accept R'hllor. This element should not be omitted from accounts portraying the offer as a purely secular inheritance.
16. **Jon once dreamed of inheriting Winterfell but considered it disloyal** to Robb, Bran, Rickon, Sansa and Arya. He now believes the inheritance line extinguished, despite other POV evidence that some siblings live. His understanding is incomplete.
17. **Stannis proposes a conditional free-folk settlement.** Those who swear fealty, uphold his laws and adopt his faith may pass the Wall and settle on the Gift, which he recognizes remains controlled by the Watch and its future Lord Commander. The plan has not been carried out.
18. **Captive and uncaptured leaders remain relevant.** Stannis holds Mance, Rattleshirt, other chiefs and a new Magnar of Thenn; Tormund remains at large. Stannis estimates roughly a thousand killed and another thousand captive, while the rest may regroup.
19. **Jon favors cooperation against the Others** despite real fears about the free folk's independence and settlement discipline. His father's earlier idea of resettling the Gift provides a historical analogy, not blanket northern consent.
20. **Stannis proposes a marriage between Jon and Val** to strengthen the new northern order. Val's consent is not given, and her informal description as 'princess' does not establish hereditary royal authority.
21. **Jon neither accepts nor refuses conclusively.** He asks time to consider an offer requiring fealty, a Stark name, Winterfell, Val and abandonment of his Watch vows. Stannis instructs him to keep the conversation secret.
