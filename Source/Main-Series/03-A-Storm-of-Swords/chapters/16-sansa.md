## Sequential close-reading record

1. **THE UNEXPECTED QUEEN'S GOWN:** Cersei's longtime seamstress takes extensive measurements of Sansa and promises **silk, Myrish lace, satin linings, smallclothes, hose and multiple accompanying garments**. She says **Queen Regent Cersei personally commissioned the wardrobe** and insisted all other work be set aside so it be ready **well before Joffrey's forthcoming wedding**. Sansa assumes it is a gracious wedding outfit or influence from Margaery. **Neither assumption is confirmed**, and the unusually early deadline deserves high-priority tracking.
2. **PHYSICAL AND SOCIAL TRANSITION:** Sansa has grown roughly **three inches over the past year**, begun menstruating and outgrown dresses; several gowns were damaged by the smoke when she burned her mattress after her first flowering. Men increasingly stare at her developing body. The seamstress states she should be dressed **as an adult noblewoman rather than a child**. This makes the gown potentially relevant to **marriage and political presentation**, not just a cosmetic gift, but no alternative betrothal is announced here.
3. **TYRELL FEMALE ALLIANCES:** Margaery's company includes **Leonette** teaching Sansa the high harp, **Janna** sharing gossip, **Meredyth/Merry Crane** telling stories, young **Lady Bulwer**, and cousins **Elinor, Alla and Megga Tyrell**. The three cousins draw Sansa into needlework, songs, lemon cakes and evening games. This is genuine observed **companionship and emotional relief** after isolation, not direct proof every woman is consciously involved in a marriage conspiracy.
4. **A SOCIAL COUNTERPOINT:** Elinor, already flowered, is engaged to **Alyn, a squire of Lord Ambrose**, who purportedly killed opposing men at Blackwater while wearing her favor. Megga idealizes kisses and champions. Sansa privately judges them sheltered by contrast with her experience of beatings, Ned's death and the riot, yet **also envies their innocence**. Her emotional development is not identical to having complete political insight.
5. **A CRITICAL SANDOR MEMORY DISCREPANCY:** Sansa remembers the Hound's visit during Blackwater as including **a kiss** alongside his threats and demand she sing. In the direct earlier scene ([ACOK 62 Sansa](../../02-A-Clash-of-Kings/chapters/62-sansa.md)) the remembered kiss is **not corroborated as an enacted event**. Preserve this as **Sansa's current recollection**, a high-value **unreliable-memory or retrospective reinterpretation** clue; do not silently insert a physical kiss into the earlier chapter. Also keep the **stained white cloak in her cedar chest** from [ASOS 6 Sansa](06-sansa.md) as a real object with independently documented continuity.
6. **HAWKING OUTSIDE THE CITY:** Margaery takes Sansa beyond King's Landing to hunt waterfowl. They pass the battered **Mud Gate**, wrecked hulls of Blackwater ships and an extensively burned **kingswood** with ash and ruined trees. A **flat-bottomed ferry** operates across the river. Sansa's **merlin** catches **three ducks**, while Margaery's **peregrine** takes a heron. These are **observed post-battle landscape and practical mobility details**, not merely decorative court scenery.
7. **WILLAS'S FALCONRY SKILL REPORTED:** Margaery says **Willas Tyrell raises exceptional birds and occasionally flies an eagle**. It is **family testimony** strengthening the prospective marriage's characterization of the unseen heir, but he is **not physically present or independently interviewed**.
8. **SANSA WARNS MARGAERY AGAIN:** Margaery warmly calls Sansa her **sister**, prompting Sansa to tell her directly that **Joffrey will hurt her**. This confirms Sansa continues to value another woman's safety even when she benefits politically from Margaery replacing her. Her warning is direct and actionable, not a polite abstraction.
9. **MARGAERY'S LORAS SAFEGUARD:** Margaery says **Mace Tyrell required Joffrey to appoint Loras to the Kingsguard** before accepting the marriage, ensuring she has a powerful protective brother at court. She compares the arrangement to **Aemon the Dragonknight and Queen Naerys**. This states a concrete **negotiated alliance condition**, rather than treating Loras's appointment as a simple personal honor.
10. **DANGERS OF THE PLAN:** Sansa recognizes Joffrey may restrain his cruelty temporarily but will eventually possess **his own guards and command structures**. If Loras challenges a king, it could ignite **Lannister–Tyrell bloodshed within the city**. She recalls **Aegon IV's executions of a Kingsguard and royal mistress** as a cautionary counterexample. Her projected civil war is **inference**, not a conflict already underway.
11. **DONTOS'S COUNTERPLAN REMAINS ACTIVE:** Sansa tells Dontos she intends to go to Highgarden and marry **Willas**, assuming this supersedes his planned wedding-night extraction. Dontos becomes alarmed, grabs her and urges her to **wear the silver hairnet at Joffrey's wedding**, according to his earlier instructions. He now claims arrangements include **a boat to a ship** that will carry her home. This is more specific escape logistics than [ACOK 65 Sansa](../../02-A-Clash-of-Kings/chapters/65-sansa.md), but no boat, crew, ship owner or patron is independently verified.
12. **A DIRECT CLAIM ABOUT WINTERFELL'S INHERITANCE:** Dontos insists Tyrell kindness is instrumental: **they want to wed Sansa's claim as prospective heir to Winterfell**. This brings a previously reasonable inference from [ASOS 6 Sansa](06-sansa.md) into **explicit character testimony**. However, **Dontos is a self-interested alternative escape agent**, not an admissions witness from Olenna or Mace; he supplies no underlying document or confession.
13. **TWO IRONIES IN SANSA'S RESPONSE:** Sansa assumes **Bran and Rickon are dead**, while readers know from [ACOK 69 Bran](../../02-A-Clash-of-Kings/chapters/69-bran.md) they survived. She also assumes **Robb will marry and soon have an heir**, while [ASOS 14 Catelyn](14-catelyn.md) establishes he has **already married Jeyne Westerling**, a political rupture she has not learned. Her claimed succession position is therefore contingent and her deductions are limited by intentionally staggered POV information.
14. **SANSA IMAGINES THE WILLAS MARRIAGE:** Despite not having met him, she hopes Willas will someday love her; she imagines gardens, travel along the Mander, children named **Eddard, Brandon and Rickon**, even a daughter resembling Arya. Her hopes show **longing for a substitute Stark family**, but do not prove Willas's appearance, affection or intentions.
15. **THE HOUND'S KISS AS A SECOND INTERNAL SIGNAL:** Sansa's memory of being kissed by Sandor recurs amid romantic adolescent comparisons about kissing and safety, while she also remembers how he **threatened her life**. Treat an emotionally salient but uncorroborated recollection as possible **reconstruction, wish, fear, or a gap in what was previously narrated**, not unqualified evidence of a consensual or reciprocal encounter.
16. **TRAUMATIC DREAM STILL ACTIVE:** Sansa dreams of a marriage to Joffrey in which the bridegroom transforms into **Ilyn Payne**, waking afraid. This is **survivor trauma and symbolic threat**, not proof that Ilyn is secretly intended as groom or an actual supernatural transformation.
17. **THE FABRIC DEADLINE EXCEEDS SANSA'S EXPLANATION:** Sansa assumes her new clothes are **for Joffrey's wedding at the Sept of Baelor**, and intends to save the outfit for Highgarden. But the seamstress emphasized **finished much sooner than the royal ceremony** at Cersei's own demand. Preserve this **time mismatch**, because the chapter supplies no adequate reason for Cersei's unexpected urgency and generosity.
18. **END POSITION:** Sansa remains **hostage at court**, with a **proposed but uncontracted Willas match**, Dontos's **wedding-night boat-to-ship extraction**, an unexplained **urgent Cersei-commissioned wardrobe**, and the **silver gemstone hairnet** still pivotal. No wedding, departure, poison use, or completed alteration of legal inheritance occurs here.
