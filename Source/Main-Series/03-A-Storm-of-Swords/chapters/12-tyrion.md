## Narrative causal sequence and agency

1. **TYRION ENTERS THE SPIDER'S ROOMS:** While Varys is summoned to **Tywin**, Tyrion enters the eunuch's sparse, windowless **three-chamber residence under the Red Keep's north wall**. He searches unsuccessfully for paper secrets or hidden doors and jokes about the uncomfortable **hard stone bed**. Varys returns, apparently surprised, then bars the door. This **directly establishes Tyrion's failed search**, not that there are no secrets anywhere in the rooms.
2. **POSTWAR PYcelle RESTORATION:** Varys confirms **Pycelle will return as Grand Maester** following Tyrion's removal of the old man in `ACOK 25 Tyrion`. The **Citadel Conclave** maintains that choosing and removing a Grand Maester falls within its authority; it had accepted Pycelle's removal and was selecting a successor. Political bargaining, not Pycelle's innocence, drives the reversal.
3. **A NAMED ALTERNATIVE WITH MAJOR POLITICAL CONSEQUENCES:** Varys says the Conclave was about to select **Maester Gormon Tyrell of Highgarden**, after weighing others, when **Tywin intervened** to restore Pycelle instead. This is a concrete **Lannister countermeasure against accumulation of Tyrell posts** after Margaery's new engagement, Loras's Kingsguard seat and Mace's council role. The reader hears this from **Varys**, not a direct Citadel meeting transcript.
4. **VARYS HAS AN OLDTOWN INFORMATION CHANNEL:** Conclave deliberations are meant to be **secret**, yet Varys knew the shortlisted candidates early enough to alert Tywin. Tyrion concludes the Spider has agents within the **Citadel itself**. That is a strong inference from the insider intelligence, but the **specific informer, instrument, and timing are not revealed**; it is not proof Varys personally controls the Citadel.
5. **BOROS BLOUNT RESTORED:** Varys also says **Boros Blount's Kingsguard cloak is being restored**, after Cersei removed him for failing to defend Tommen from Bronn's Rosby interception. Varys invokes traditional life tenure; Tyrion notes Blount might resent Cersei but may now depend on **Tywin**, not on Tyrion. The **restoration is reported and politically plausible**, not yet a witnessed Kingsguard ceremony.
6. **MOORE INVESTIGATION DEAD-END:** Tyrion's man **Bronn has investigated Ser Mandon Moore**, finding little. Varys says Moore came from **the Vale with Jon Arryn**, received his white cloak from **Robert**, and had few friends even among his sworn brothers. **Barristan Selmy** purportedly believed Moore's only friend was his sword and his life only duty. These are Varys's **historical/character reports**, not a money trail linking Moore to Cersei or another would-be assassin.
7. **CRUEL IRONY OF OFFICIAL MOORE HISTORY:** Varys publicly frames Moore as dying honorably while defending the king's blood; Tyrion privately knows Moore **twice deliberately attacked him** ([ACOK 61](../../02-A-Clash-of-Kings/chapters/61-tyrion.md)). **The gap between official hero narrative and firsthand assault is substantive**. Varys may be withholding more than he discloses, but the scene does not prove he actually commissioned the attack.
8. **SHAE AS AN ACTIVE SURVEILLANCE PROBLEM:** Tyrion asks Varys to arrange a private visit with **Shae**, who remains a maidservant to **Lollys Stokeworth**. He has passed her on a stair and felt jealous when another knight helped her carry a pail. Tyrion says he intends **one final encounter before sending her away** under the threat from **Tywin** ([ASOS 4](04-tyrion.md)). This is his stated plan, not what he ultimately does.
9. **KETTLEBLACK AFFILIATIONS:** Varys confirms the **three Kettleblack brothers** report to **Cersei**, who now grants them knighthoods and patronage. He describes **Osmund's hopes of sexual favors from Cersei** and the queen's suggestive encouragement. Importantly Varys **expressly denies knowing she is actually having sex with Osmund**. A flirtation or manipulation is **not direct proof of an affair**.
10. **MANY INDEPENDENT EYES WATCH TYRION:** Varys names **Janos Slynt's sons**, **Petyr Baelish's brothel contacts**, Kettleblacks and his own role as a spy **for Tywin**. Tyrion understands his public movements and visit to a brothel could expose Shae and herself to the threatened hanging. Varys's list is an insider report, and **not every watcher is shown at work in this chapter**.
11. **THE LIMITED MAEGOR ESCAPE ROUTE:** Varys says **Maegor's Holdfast** contains a secret **escape door to the outside** constructed because Maegor feared enemies trapping him, but it **does not connect to a network reaching Tyrion's room**. This is Varys's explanation; his knowledge of other hidden routes should not be trusted as exhaustive. Tyrion proposes meeting **in Varys's rooms** instead.
12. **A VARYS CROSS-DRESSING DECOY:** On arriving at moonrise, Tyrion is greeted by a **matronly woman with dark curls and round pink face** and realizes it is **Varys in disguise**. Varys disappears without an obvious door while Shae waits, making his physical concealment **direct evidence**, independent of whether the eunuch told the truth about every corridor.
13. **LORAS'S KINGSguard MOTIVE:** En route Tyrion asks seventeen-year-old **Loras Tyrell** why he joined an institution requiring **no marriage, children or inheritance**. Loras says the Tyrell line continues through **his elder brothers** and quotes a mournful comparison of setting sun and inadequate candle. The scene strongly suggests **lasting grief and love for Renly** in context of his emotional reaction in [ASOS 6 Sansa](06-sansa.md), but **Loras does not state his intimate relationship explicitly here**.
14. **BRONN AND VARYS DOMINATE ACCESS TO SHAE:** Shae reports **Varys refused to release her former silks and jewels** while Tyrion lay near death; she still serves Lollys, now heavily pregnant after the riot trauma. Tyrion's restricted ability to provide her own lodging or move discreetly is real. **Shae's internal motives are not directly given**, and she asks for her possessions and higher status.
15. **A DANGEROUS REQUEST FOR THE ROYAL WEDDING:** Shae presses for permission to attend **Joffrey and Margaery's wedding feast** dressed as a noblewoman rather than a maid. She repeats **Symon Silver Tongue's reports** of singers' contests, tricks, dancing bears and an immense live-dove pie. The wedding extravaganza is **Symon's account of planned events**, not yet held or independently verified; Tyrion perceives a chance of discovery by court watchers.
16. **SYMON AS AN INFORMATION LEAK:** Shae says she recommended **Symon** to **Lady Tanda**, who hired him to play for **Lollys**. Thus a performer with prior access to Shae has **entered her employer's household** and now follows royal wedding gossip. Tyrion fears that even an inadvertent report could allow Cersei or Tywin to **identify their relationship**. His suspicion is practical, but the chapter does not show Symon deliberately blackmailing him yet.
17. **INABILITY TO END THE AFFAIR:** Tyrion repeatedly tells Shae she **must leave King's Landing** to survive Tywin's threat; Shae insists he promised to restore her lodgings, treasures and opportunities for pleasure and status. After intimacy he again decides they should separate, but **promises to keep her and keep her safe** rather than sending her away. The contradiction between **recognized danger and behavior** is directly narrated, with his memories of **Tysha** resurfacing.
18. **THE MECHANICAL SECRET PASSAGE:** After Varys disappears, Tyrion searches the walls and cannot locate the exit; Shae reveals **there are secret steps beneath Varys's stone bed** and a place the eunuch presses that lifts the bed. Varys apparently called it **magic**; Tyrion correctly proposes **counterweights**. **A mechanical bed-lift and hidden steps are disclosed by Shae**, not directly activated on the page by Tyrion. This is a high-value **specific architecture clue**, distinct from an invented maze or supernatural teleportation.
19. **TYRION'S PUBLIC CLOTHES ALMOST BETRAY HIM:** Tyrion initially dresses extravagantly and considers putting on his **gold chain of office**, but remembers **Tywin now possesses that chain** and realizes fine court dress at night would raise suspicion. He changes to simple garments. This is direct self-correction showing how social performance can expose covert movements.
20. **BRONN'S CHANGING STATUS AND ALAYAYA:** Tyrion summons Bronn late at night from **Chataya's brothel**; Bronn says he had been with **Alayaya and Marei**. Tyrion feels jealous and guilty about Alayaya's recent whipping, and remembers having sent her a **silver-and-jade necklace and matching bracelets** as apology. The gift is a claimed completed act; Bronn's boasting is his own account.
21. **ORDER TO FIND SYMON, NOT YET TO KILL HIM:** Tyrion considers commanding Bronn to **kill the singer**, but recognizes Symon has only sung songs and talked to Shae. Instead he explicitly orders Bronn to **find Symon Silver Tongue before someone else does**. This is a crucial action threshold: **no murder of Symon is ordered or carried out by the end of this chapter**.
22. **ENDING POSITION:** Tyrion has learned of **Tywin's appointment strategy, Varys's unseen networks, Shae's knowledge of a secret bed, the singer's increasing access, and the risk of court exposure**, but has **not separated from Shae or identified Mandon's sponsor**.

## High-priority cross-book tests

- **Varys and the Citadel:** His intelligence about secret Conclave appointments shows a **broad reach**, but the network's reliability, goals and whether any maester allegiance follows from it need separate proof. Examine Gormon's later place and timing.
- **The under-bed steps:** This is an unusually concrete **security and hidden-architecture clue**. Record its location at Varys's north-wall residence and his ability to vanish, not a generalized claim every Red Keep wall joins the same tunnels.
- **Tyrell institutional balance:** Tywin uses Pycelle to prevent a Gormon Tyrell Grand Maester while the Reach already controls important court seats. This reveals **inter-allied distrust** even while the public royal marriage proceeds.
- **Symon exposure:** Tyrion's attempt to contain the singer may become the cause of more serious violence later, but **at this moment his instruction stops at locating him**.
- **Moore:** Varys offers Vale/Arryn history but no Cersei connection. The missing patron remains open rather than confirmed by Tyrion's suspicion.
- **Loras and grief:** The sun/candle aphorism reinforces his earlier grief after Renly's death, but does not alone establish who shared his bed or why he chose the Kingsguard.
