## Firsthand chronological reading

1. **Arya and Sandor reach a recognizable Trident inn.** She had once slept there with **Sansa and Septa Mordane**; now **a woman's skeletal remains hang from a gibbet** outside. The inn's change from protected royal itinerary to occupied violent crossing marks war's spread.
2. **Sandor goes inside seeking wine and information about the ruby ford.** He ignores Arya's concern that enemy soldiers will recognize him; she again could flee on **Craven with Stranger**, but follows him in. His indifference to his own recognizability is visible.
3. **Three Mountain-affiliated men are present.** **Polliver**, **the Tickler** and a pimply, unnamed **young squire** sit near the locals. Polliver and the Tickler had appeared in Arya's prior captivity; the boy insults Sandor for abandoning the Blackwater.
4. **The Tickler warns the reckless boy by twisting his ear.** Polliver tries to prevent escalation, while Sandor drinks roughly **half a wine flagon very rapidly** on an empty stomach. His intoxication **actually weakens him in the subsequent melee**.
5. **Polliver reports Joffrey's death and blames Tyrion and Sansa.** He repeats the fantastic rumor that Sansa turned into **a winged wolf** and flew from a tower window. Arya immediately recognizes that as absurd, but neither she nor Polliver knows the full Purple Wedding conspiracy.
6. **The inn soldiers describe the Riverlands settlement.** They say **Gregor took Harrenhal** through a cook's revenge-opened postern, after Mummers fled; surviving servants were killed and Hoat was kept as Gregor's captive. **Brynden holds Riverrun under siege**, Walder threatens captive Edmure, Blackwood–Bracken fighting persists, and the Brackens have joined the crown. These are **soldiers' reports, not Arya's direct observation**.
7. **Polliver mentions an alleged Arya headed for Ramsay.** He says Bolton's bastard will wed Sansa's sister. Real Arya is in this very room but **the false identity scheme is not known to her**. Sandor laughs without exposing her.
8. **Sandor asks after ships at Saltpans.** Polliver knows more about traffic to **Maidenpool** than Saltpans. The question reveals Sandor is considering **a sea route to the Vale**, rather than guaranteed arrival at Riverrun.
9. **The Tickler initiates the fight with a thrown knife.** His blade misses Sandor's throat after Sandor moves and grazes his ribs. **Polliver** attacks with longsword, the Tickler with short sword and dagger, and the boy joins.
10. **Arya uses improvised projectiles to assist Sandor.** She hits the boy's face with a heavy cup, fails to strike the Tickler with another, and throws the **pink-stoned dagger recovered from the dying Piper archer** at the Tickler; it hits **hilt-first**. This directly demonstrates that her knife throwing is not reliably lethal.
11. **The young squire seizes Arya.** She pulls **his own dagger** from its sheath and stabs him **in the abdomen**, a fatal wound in Sandor's assessment. The boy later begs for mercy and claims his object was access to the girls in the inn; **his confession is a panicked statement, not verified prior assault**.
12. **Polliver and the Tickler gravely wound Sandor.** A severe cut strikes the **back of his neck**, another **upper thigh**, and Polliver cuts away his **ear**. Sandor's impaired fighting is attributable in part to drinking and real injuries, not suddenly diminished skill without cause.
13. **Sandor kills Polliver by a sudden bench-assisted counterattack.** He drives a bench into Polliver's legs and uses a savage reverse sword cut that cleaves his face/head. Polliver's death is directly witnessed.
14. **Arya kills the Tickler in a prolonged frenzy.** She approaches from behind with the wall-embedded thrown knife and repeatedly stabs him while reenacting his torture interrogations—questions about gold, food, Beric, knights and bowmen. **His death is complete**, and her anger is not merely a detached execution.
15. **Arya retrieves Needle from Polliver's belt.** She recognizes the slim weapon that had been taken from her and fits her hand. **Needle's original name and meaning survive her identity fragmentation**.
16. **Arya gives the squire a deliberate mercy killing.** With Sandor's prompting and his earlier lesson on where the heart lies, she stabs him between the ribs with **Needle**. His previous abdominal wound was almost certainly fatal, but the final strike is specifically meant to end suffering.
17. **Sandor chooses Saltpans over continuing inland.** He tells Arya to loot coins and wine, then sets off, having suffered a damaged ear, neck wound and thigh injury. He needs her physical assistance to mount **Stranger**.
18. **They hide by the Trident and attempt field treatment.** Sandor heats **wine in his dog-head helm**, then orders Arya to pour boiling wine over the cuts. **He screams, faints from the pain**, and she bandages the wounds using strips of the boy's cloak. These acts are real first aid attempts but **not a demonstrated cure**.
19. **Arya reviews her death-list.** She leaves Polliver, the Tickler and Joffrey off as dead; she temporarily omits Sandor, then adds **the Hound** and repeats the Braavosi phrase **Valar morghulis**. Her ambivalence is central: she is angry at him yet treated him and has depended on him.
20. **Sandor weakens sharply the following day.** His ear bleeds through bandages, the **thigh wound smells foul**, his body grows burning hot, and he collapses beneath a tree. He is **conscious intermittently and feverish** but **not shown dying on-page**.
21. **Sandor demands that Arya kill him.** He confesses killing **Mycah** and failing to intervene when Joffrey's men abused **Sansa**; he also claims he contemplated sexual violence toward Sansa. These are **his speech under fever and anguish**, not evidence such contemplated assault happened. He sobs and demands mercy while taunting Arya's courage.
22. **Arya refuses his requested mercy.** She deliberately chooses to leave him **alive but helpless**, saying that he does not deserve it, and rides away on Craven. This is neither proof she killed Sandor nor certainty he died after she left.
23. **Six days later Arya arrives at Saltpans.** The river widens, port damage is visible, and three ships are present: **two shallow-draft river galleys and a larger purple three-masted, two-banked sea trader**. These are observed vessels, not a promise all can sail to the north.
24. **She sells Craven for an unfairly low amount.** The stablewoman recognizes Arya is not gentleborn despite her invented story about inheriting the horse from a knight brother, threatens legal consequences, and takes advantage of her vulnerability. Arya cannot recover Sandor's **two hidden silver stags and three gold dragons**, which remained with him.
25. **Arya tries to pay for passage north to Eastwatch.** The purple ship's captain rejects the small purse, explaining the traders are returning home and that he encountered **approximately twelve pirate ships near Crackclaw Point** heading north. The pirate information is **the captain's report**, not Arya's firsthand sight.
26. **The ship is named the *Titan's Daughter* of Braavos.** An oarsman makes a predatory suggestion about hiring Arya as cabin girl, and the captain rebukes him. She has no guaranteed safe option at the harbor until she produces Jaqen's token.
27. **Jaqen H'ghar's black iron coin changes the transaction.** The captain reacts with startled recognition, then Arya says **Valar morghulis**. He replies **Valar dohaeris**, touches his brow with two fingers and immediately offers **a cabin**. This verifies **a recognized Braavosi exchange or obligation**, but not the exact organizational rules, value or future patron to whom Arya will be sent.
28. **End state:** Arya is bound for **Braavos**, not the Wall. **Polliver, Tickler and squire are dead, Needle recovered, and Sandor's fate is unknown** after being abandoned feverish by the Trident.

## Follow-up questions

- **Sandor's survival:** Later reports of the Hound's death may be mistaken identity or inference; this chapter never shows a body or last breath.
- **Arya's Braavos passage:** A recognized coin/phrase implies some organized prior arrangement with Jaqen, but the **exact duties or future training** should be tested in her subsequent POV.
- **Red Wedding consequences:** The false Arya story is already circulating where real Arya can hear it, demonstrating that lordly political counterfeiting propagates through low-level soldiers.
- **Violence and mercy:** Arya grants mercy to the dying squire while refusing it to Sandor; keep the moral distinction and her trauma rather than flattening her character into a single appetite for killing.
