## Source-grounded sequence and causal interpretation

1. **The setting has changed decisively since ASOS 57: Meereen has fallen.** Daenerys occupies the **Great Pyramid**, looking over the Skahazadhan, sea, scorched farms and the city's smaller pyramids. Her dragons move freely around the site of the **bronze harpy statue she ordered pulled down**. She has taken a throne but not yet established lasting order.
2. **Missandei speaks of Naath's radically nonviolent faith.** According to her, the **Peaceful People** worship a single **Lord of Harmony**, eat fruit rather than animals, and their sacred butterflies cause invading conquerors to sicken. Dany notes these purported protectors **do not prevent slave ships from raiding**. These are **Missandei's cultural accounts and Dany's rejoinder**, not proven magical epidemiology.
3. **Daenerys's three-headed crown comes from Qarth.** The **Tourmaline Brotherhood** gave it; her dress is purple samite with a silver sash and precarious high slippers. She studies herself in a mirror and finds the face of a girl rather than a conqueror, revealing **self-doubt despite political power**.
4. **The siege victory is retrospectively narrated with concrete mechanisms.** Daenerys **sacrificed her three ships**, ordering them driven ashore and their hulls dismantled for **rams, mantlets, ladders, catapults and turtle shelters**. The mainmast of **Meraxes (formerly Joso's Prank)** became the ram nicknamed **Joso's Cock**, crowned with the vessel's jester-shaped iron figurehead. This is real siege engineering, not dragonfire breaching walls.
5. **The attack consumed most of a day and part of the night.** Dany remained half a league back in mail despite wanting to ride in; captains unanimously forbade the attempt. The eastern gate eventually broke, while slave revolts rose within the city, making a **two-front battle**. Her dragons roared and breathed fire when the city's defenders lost heart, but **the text does not assign the gate's physical collapse to dragonfire**.
6. **The true inner-city infiltration used Brown Ben's sewer lead.** **Two hundred men** went along the river at night to burn harbor fire hulks as diversion. Under that cover **Jorah, Barristan, Strong Belwas and approximately twenty mixed Unsullied, sellsword and freedman volunteers** crossed a rusty drain grate and navigated foul, **thigh-deep** water with oilcloth-wrapped torches, eventually freeing fighting-pit slaves from their chains. This confirms the route proposed in ASOS 57 was **actually used**, at substantial risk.
7. **At least one infiltrator was killed by an unknown sewer animal.** A **large pale lizard** seized a volunteer by the leg; Jorah killed it after another disturbance. The species and any supernatural affiliation are **unidentified**; rats alone are not the only sewer hazard.
8. **The internal revolt spreads rapidly.** Within an hour of the freedmen reaching the fighting pits, **about half the fighting slaves rose**. Their action compounds the breach. Dany personally enters only when Unsullied clear final opposition; the plaza and streets are filled with dead, and cheering freed people call her Mother.
9. **Daenerys orders symmetrical vengeance for the 163 children.** She demands **163 leaders or masters**, matching the **163 crucified child victims** on the road, and orders those masters nailed to wooden posts around the plaza. They die in pain. She calls it just but later physically recoils from their suffering and recognizes she has created a comparable horror. This is **directly narrated collective punishment**, not proof each chosen master personally ordered a child's crucifixion.
10. **She rejects the old harpy throne.** It is dismantled for fuel; she holds public audience from **a simple ebony bench** despite complaints it lacks the grandeur of a queen. The choice is a **visible break with slaver iconography**, not evidence local governance institutions have been reformed.
11. **Sack discipline is severe and produces immediate penalties.** She decrees **hanging for murder, removal of hands for looting, and castration for rape**. Eight killers hang on the walls, and **a bushel basket of severed hands and genitalia** has been collected. These punishments show violence used in service of claimed order and are **not equal to a transparent trial process**.
12. **Meereen's corpses and flies create a public-health problem.** Dany orders the decomposing masters and bodies cleared. **Missandei** advises bones be boiled and returned for burial in Ghiscari family crypts. Dany agrees despite recognizing widows will still hate her. This is a **specific reconciliation concession** rather than indiscriminate refusal of funerary rights.
13. **Astapor has suffered regime collapse after Dany departed.** **Ghael**, envoy of **King Cleon the Great**, says Cleon exposed the three-person council (healer, scholar and priest) as pro-slaver conspirators and **executed them with a cleaver**; Missandei confirms Cleon had been **a butcher enslaved by Grazdan mo Ullhor**. Whether the original council actually plotted is **Cleon's interested accusation**, not independently proven.
14. **Cleon proposes war and marriage.** Through Ghael he seeks an alliance against Yunkai and offers Dany marriage while promising to set aside his existing wives, some pregnant. Reports of Yunkai recruiting, building ships and contacting **New Ghis, Volantis and Vaes Dothrak** are the envoy's **alarmist strategic claims**, not independently verified positions.
15. **The merchant captain supplies a second view of Astapor.** The **Qartheen master of the *Indigo Star*** says pyramids are armed camps, food markets empty, dead lie unburied, and Cleon's men are **seizing highborn boys to train new Unsullied** for future sale. This account is **a separate trader's report**, though not Dany's own return visit.
16. **Former elites request voluntary enslavement abroad.** Daario reports some wellborn Meereenese seek to sell themselves to Qartheen traders to escape poverty and chaos, expecting privileged service as tutors, scribes, priests, healers or companions in rich foreign households. This is the **claim about prospective conditions**, not proof their slavery will be safe or reversible.
17. **Dany permits adults to sell themselves, but forbids selling dependents.** She says **men and women may enter the arrangement**, but they may not sell **children or spouses**. **She imposes a one-tenth levy on the sale price** on Astapor's former model, paid in selected valuable commodities, and requires **written accounts maintained by literate freedmen**. This creates an explicit **contradiction between anti-slavery principle and facilitating taxed sales**, without requiring modern reinterpretation to invent it.
18. **Daenerys recognizes that sellsword collectors may steal revenue.** Daario volunteers **Stormcrows** to administer the tenth; she internally expects perhaps half the take could go astray, knows the **Second Sons** are similarly untrustworthy, and considers the Unsullied honest but largely unable to keep books. The issue is **administrative capacity**, not merely insufficient force.
19. **Barristan and Jorah's Meereen service is now explicitly confirmed.** Summoned by **Belwas**, both return after assisting the sewer assault. Dany acknowledges both also repeatedly saved her life and faces the distinction between a **hidden identity** and **betrayed intelligence**.
20. **Barristan confesses he wanted to assess Dany's sanity before pledging.** He says **Aerys II grew more disturbed over time**, recalls **King Jaehaerys II's** saying about Targaryens' greatness and madness being unpredictable, and notes young Viserys resembled Aerys more than Rhaegar. The 'coin toss' is **Barristan's remembered royal aphorism**, not a verified deterministic heredity rule.
21. **Barristan receives a new oath and sword.** He will not take a weapon except from Dany; she gives him **Brown Ben's sword** hilt-first and formally accepts his fealty. **Barristan is reinstated as her knight**, not restored to the former Westerosi Kingsguard by Tommen's government.
22. **Jorah's spying lasted later than Dany hoped.** He concedes taking **Varys's money**, studying ciphers and writing reports, including **one report from Qarth**. Under Barristan's challenge and Dany's questioning, his disclosure of **Dany's pregnancy by Drogo** is implicated in Robert's order that mother and unborn child be killed. He says **Varys warned of assassination attempts** and that he intervened to stop poisoned wine. This account partly vindicates his protective act while establishing grave informational betrayal.
23. **Dany distinguishes Barristan's remorse from Jorah's entitlement.** Barristan acknowledged wrongdoing and offered unconditional service; Jorah initially emphasizes his service, expresses love and insists she 'must' forgive him. Her fury and grief are explicit. A coercive rhetoric explanation is plausible, but she **does not know Jorah's inner motives beyond words and actions**.
24. **Jorah is exiled, not killed.** Dany gives him **until dawn to leave Meereen**, threatens death if he returns after that, and rejects **Daario's offer to bring back his severed head**. She subsequently questions whether banishment was right. **His actual departure and future destination are not witnessed here**.
25. **Dany's loneliness is anchored in home memories.** She handles the Westerosi storybook Jorah gave her as a wedding gift, cannot concentrate, remembers Drogo and her lost family, and searches the waking city for **a house with a red door**, her persistent childhood image. The exact true past location or significance of that door **is not reestablished in this chapter**.
26. **A private encounter with Irri exposes an asymmetry.** Dany takes **Irri** to bed, then recognizes Irri's affection or sexual ministrations may be **duty rather than a freely reciprocal attachment**, while Daenerys imagines Drogo and increasingly **Daario**. This is a relationship-power clue, not proof of Irri's unspoken consent or desire.
27. **The dragons roam increasingly far.** **Drogon** perches where the bronze harpy was, **Rhaegal** rests by the pool, and **Viserion** flies over the river hunting. Dany fears one may someday not return. They are growing but **are not yet carrying human riders**.
28. **The decision is explicitly to stay and govern.** At dawn Dany compares Aegon's post-conquest order with her own recurring sack/abandonment, declares a duty to those freed and refuses the westward march. She will **remain in Meereen, rule and learn to be a queen** rather than allow its people to repeat Astapor's fate. **This is the chapter's completed strategic choice, not proof the policy succeeds later**.

## Stress tests

- **Liberator versus ruler:** Her intentions are consistently compassionate toward freed people, but collective crucifixions, mutilations, unreliable tax collection and re-enslavement licenses reveal real contradictions and governance deficits; do not excuse or vilify by erasing either side.
- **Astapor as experimental warning:** Dany's departure allowed a butcher monarchy to replace her council. The exact council betrayal is alleged by that monarchy; later disconfirmation needs independent witnesses.
- **Barristan and inherited madness:** His firsthand experience with Aerys is significant but the hereditary coin aphorism is impressionistic, not proof Dany's fate is predetermined.
- **Prophetic three heads:** Dany's reliance on two hypothetical trusted partners has not identified dragon riders or future alliances; her immediate observable work is governing one city.
