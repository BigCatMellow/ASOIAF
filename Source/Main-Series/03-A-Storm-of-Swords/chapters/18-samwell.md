## Narrative sequence and causal record

1. **A survivor near physical collapse.** In deepening snow after the Fist, Sam can barely walk, suffering cold, probable exhaustion and severe pain under mail and a laden pack. Boots are caked like ice-clubfeet; repeated falls and frozen tears make this both a logistical and psychological crisis. He lacks reliable sleep and cannot reconstruct elapsed days.
2. **The retreat is shrinking.** Sam recalls **roughly fifty or more** men leaving the Fist in the group now moving together, with several missing or dead since. This is his recollection of a retreating contingent, **not** a verified total of Watch survivors. The rear guard is still under attack.
3. **A fire perimeter protects a diminishing column.** Mormont's torch ring is an organized precaution; Sam fears leaving it. An injured Black Bernarr was placed on a horse days earlier, while mounts are needed to carry food, fire supplies and wounded.
4. **Dangerous cold is rendered as false comfort.** Sam gradually feels almost warm when he sinks into snow and rationalizes sleeping. This is evidence of life-threatening exposure and altered judgment, not magical seduction proven in this scene.
5. **Sam prays across religious boundaries.** Initially invoking the Mother, he thinks the Seven have no power north of the Wall and appeals to old gods or even demons. **This is Sam's theological fear**, not omniscient confirmation about where gods exercise power.
6. **A specific massacre haunts him.** Sam remembers **Maslyn** pleading for mercy before an attacking wight lifts and tears at his throat. Sam distinguishes merciless reanimated corpses from the more terrifying Others, who are not interchangeable creatures.
7. **The raven duty is clear.** Mormont had ordered Sam to send **one report to Castle Black and one to the Shadow Tower**, writing the message himself instead of seeking instructions. He was specifically told **not to fight** at the ringwall. This makes his job communications, not combat.
8. **Two initial warnings were sent.** After hearing the **three Watch horn blasts for Others**, Sam awakens, finds Chett present but unwilling to help, attaches prepared attack notices and releases a bird for **Castle Black** and another for the **Shadow Tower**. Birds escaping their cages do not negate the two specifically dispatched letters.
9. **Chett's role differs from his plan.** Sam sees Chett flee with a dagger when the third horn sounds. [ASOS Prologue](00-prologue.md) established Chett's intended mutiny, but **Sam does not know that plot** and assumes Chett may have separate dog duties.
10. **Sam goes to the wall despite orders.** Fear, loneliness and a desire to die near friends pull him toward Shadow Tower men led by **Blane**. He witnesses arrows failing to halt the advancing dead; archers see figures crawling up through the snow.
11. **Mormont corrects him and orders fire.** At the wall, Mormont hears Sam has sent warnings, commends him, but sends him back to tend birds for any second message. He orders fire arrows. **Sam temporarily fulfills an essential role**, regardless of his self-description as useless.
12. **The message drafts become an accidental battle log.** Sam writes successive incompatible drafts—claims of repulse, ongoing fighting, wounded defense, a bear or giant, withdrawal and finally defeat—as the tactical situation changes. These are **drafts**, not multiple delivered reports.
13. **Wights are not reliably killed by steel.** Sam sees a spear-driven corpse advance along the weapon to kill a Watchman, and later dead men with arrows through throats and faces continue fighting. Fire inflicts real harm, but the Fist cannot hold against numbers and breaches.
14. **A possible bear/giant confusion must be preserved.** During combat, soldiers shout of both a huge figure and a bear. Sam's later view confirms an **animated, decaying bear**; the separate alleged giant at that moment is not independently verified by him.
15. **Second-message failure is concrete.** The call to mount consists of **two short horn blasts and one long**; realizing the Watch must retreat, Sam releases the **remaining ravens without attaching his latest reports**. He notices his omission afterward and chases birds unsuccessfully. This does not cancel the original two warnings already dispatched.
16. **The formation breaks and withdraws.** Ser Ottyn Wythers is kicked by a riderless horse; Thoren Smallwood is **killed by the dead bear** after almost severing its head. Mormont rejects sending more reserves and orders a mounted **wedge down the south slope, then east** because other slopes are too steep.
17. **The dead bear is physically distinctive.** It has exposed rotting flesh, a damaged foreleg and bright blue eyes. Its visible resilience under a sword blow concerns **a reanimated animal**, not proof a living Other shares the same weaknesses.
18. **A mounted escape does not end Sam's trouble.** During the Fist breakout he jumps the ringwall and rides with the spearhead; later an **unidentified black-clad man** forcibly takes his horse after the party reaches trees. Sam never sees his face. Do not identify the thief.
19. **The commander redistributes survival supplies.** Dywen rescued five loaded packhorses, three of which reach the first regrouping. Mormont spreads food, oil and torches across mounts to avoid losing all supplies at once and gives riding capacity to the wounded.
20. **Grenn refuses to abandon Sam.** When Sam collapses and accepts death, Grenn tries hauling him upright, then kicks him in desperation. Another ranger suggests leaving him. The violence is in service of keeping him moving, but Sam experiences betrayal and pain rather than an uncomplicated heroic intervention.
21. **Small Paul carries him.** He takes Sam in his arms and bears him for a substantial stretch. He says Chett promised him Mormont's talking raven and worries that Lark may eat one. [ASOS Prologue](00-prologue.md) offers reader-known context for Chett/Lark; **Sam does not perceive Paul's implication in the failed conspiracy**. Paul's affection for the bird is genuine in his dialogue, not evidence he controls it.
22. **Sam's recollection is limited.** He assumes Jon has died with Qhorin though [ASOS 15 Jon](15-jon.md) establishes Jon is alive among Mance's free folk. His muddled movement memories, uncertain day-count and retrospective reconstruction are not grounds to discard his plainly observed physical details.
23. **The three become isolated.** Small Paul's strength fails after they fall behind the rear guard. Only Grenn's torch remains, and they no longer see the rest of the column. Sam fears they will be found by pursuing dead.
24. **An actual Other appears, distinct from a wight.** A dead horse, apparently recognizable to Paul as **Mawney's**, carries a slim milk-pale mounted Other. The horse has an open belly and frozen entrails. The Other descends silently in rippling, shifting armor; its feet do not visibly disturb the snow's crust.
25. **The Other destroys the torch.** Its faintly blue crystal-like sword moves with extreme speed and shears through Grenn's flaming torch; the flame dies in the snow. This is a witnessed interaction of weapon and fire, **not** proof ordinary fire cannot harm any Other.
26. **Small Paul's death is direct.** Paul charges with his axe; the Other evades and drives its blade through the gaps of his mail and through his body. Paul falls and his weight pulls the blade free of its wielder. Grenn later verifies he has no pulse. His sacrifice preserves Sam and Grenn's chance to act.
27. **Sam's obsidian dagger kills the Other.** He rushes with the **dragonglass blade Jon gave him**, thrusting blindly. The point lodges in its throat. The Other's armor liquefies, pale-blue fluid steams, hands smoke on contact with obsidian and its flesh and then milkglass-like bones dissolve rapidly. Only the dagger remains. This is the crucial **VERIFIED material vulnerability**.
28. **Contact aftermath suggests intense cold.** Grenn immediately drops the recovered obsidian because it is cold, then picks it up successfully. Do **not** assert the dagger was destroyed or used up: it survives the killing and passes to Grenn.
29. **A new day, not proven security.** Grenn identifies pink dawnlight toward the east as a potential route to the other survivors; Sam resumes walking. Rejoining Mormont, delivering detailed intelligence, and safe return to Castle Black are **not** accomplished here.
30. **Counterpoint to Sam's self-reproach.** Sam repeatedly invokes his father's accusation of cowardice despite **sending an advance warning, surviving battle, and discovering the weapon that kills an Other**. Grenn challenges his self-judgment; courage is behavior under terror, not absence of terror.

## Interpretive tests and limits

- **Fire vs dragonglass:** Wights at the Fist suffer from fire, and the Other is destroyed by obsidian. These observations concern **different types of enemy**; avoid presenting fire and dragonglass as interchangeable certainties.
- **Watch communications failure:** Two prewritten warnings definitely go out, later specific battle reports do not. Downstream commanders may have urgent but incomplete information. Compare receipt scenes rather than claiming no warning was sent.
- **Old horn:** Its mere presence in Sam's pack warrants tracking because horn lore is separately important, but an ordinary recovered horn remains a serious alternative to any magical identification.
- **Sam's 'cowardice':** Self-rating is challenged by evidence. His father and Watch bullies can be psychologically decisive without being accurate assessors of courage.
- **Paul/Chett:** Reader has more context than Sam; promises about a raven are not proof Paul knew a supernatural threat was near.

## End state / next dependencies

Small Paul is killed by an Other; Grenn and Sam survive and retain the obsidian dagger. Mormont's column continues somewhere to the east; two early attack messages have been sent but Sam's later battle reports were not. Track whether other witnesses can corroborate the kill and whether the weapon knowledge reaches a command authority.
