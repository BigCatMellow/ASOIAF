## Chronological, political and individual close reading

1. **Jaime learns Joffrey died while he is still traveling with Walton's escort.** At a roadside inn, a **costermonger**, **innkeeper**, **septon** and an **archer wearing Mathis Rowan's colors** report mutually incompatible details. One claims Tyrion slit his throat and drank blood; another says poison; one invents **a supernatural direwolf and brimstone disappearance for Sansa**. This is a clear **rumor contamination exhibit**, not a factual murder reconstruction.
2. **Jaime privately acknowledges paternity but feels almost no parental grief.** He knows Joffrey was **his child with Cersei**, but recalls never holding him and Cersei warning him to conceal obvious affection lest Robert infer the resemblance. Jaime is shocked at his own preference for regaining a hand over regaining the son. His emotional distance is **direct internal confession**, not evidence Joffrey was not his.
3. **The convoy reaches King's Landing, not merely the road outside it.** **Walton Steelshanks, Nage with a seven-point-star rainbow peace banner, Qyburn, Brienne and roughly 200 northmen** come through the **Gate of the Gods**. They use a Lothston bat shield and Jaime's beard/stump make him difficult for civilians to recognize.
4. **Postwar urban markets and taxes are active.** Farmers queue with cider, hay, apples and enormous pumpkins; gold cloaks collect a **fee for selling within the city** ordered by the Hand and master of coin. Commoners believe the Lannisters' victory has reopened profitable trade. This does not show famine has been fully cured.
5. **Lord Piper's changing allegiance is conveyed as report.** At **Brindlewood**, **Ser Bertram Beesbury** had told them Piper chose to end resistance because his **son remained captive at the Twins**. Brienne received news of Robb/Catelyn there and has become withdrawn. The consequence of Frey hostages on Riverlands submission is a specific political mechanism.
6. **Brienne's oath remains unfinished.** She tells Jaime she promised Catelyn to **bring back her daughters or at least Sansa**, not merely deliver Jaime to King's Landing. Jaime offers possible return to Tarth or a court post; Brienne declines serving with murderers or oathbreakers. Neither promises Sansa recovered nor simply absolves Brienne of the duty due Catelyn's death.
7. **The unrecognized rider becomes recognized by Kingsguard.** **Meryn Trant** grants entry when Jaime reveals himself; **Loras Tyrell and Balon Swann** are new Kingsguard members. Jaime invokes his title **Lord Commander** and chastises them for losing two monarchs while he was absent—Robert and Joffrey.
8. **Loras directly accuses Brienne of murdering Renly.** He relies on **Emmon Cuy's dying accusation** and the belief only Brienne and Catelyn were in Renly's tent. Brienne says a **shadow** killed him as candles blew out, and that Catelyn thought it Stannis's shadow. The reader has direct evidence from Catelyn's earlier POV of the bizarre killing; **Loras's charge is emotionally credible but mistaken about actual killer**, while the exact metaphysics are still not fully demonstrated.
9. **Jaime averts a fight by asserting formal command.** He blocks Loras, orders him to sheathe his sword, and **Balon Swann supports the order** while gold cloaks and Dreadfort soldiers begin drawing weapons. He describes Brienne as honorable and capable, but **orders Balon to put her in a guarded tower cell** to satisfy Loras pending resolution. This is **protective custody under constraint**, not outright release or exoneration.
10. **Osmund Kettleblack initially fails to identify Jaime.** Guarding the royal sept, Osmund mocks the man as a 'cripple' or escaped Tyrion before learning he is both Cersei's brother and Lord Commander. The encounter illustrates new politically appointed white cloaks' disconnection from the older order.
11. **Jaime and Cersei reunite beside Joffrey's displayed body.** Joffrey lies in gilded armor beneath the Stranger's image; Cersei mourns before the Mother's altar. Jaime shows her his missing hand and explains **Vargo Hoat** ordered its removal, not Catelyn or the Starks. She seems unaware of Hoat's identity, despite the politically important rescue.
12. **Cersei asserts Tyrion poisoned Joffrey, not that she saw poison administered.** She recalls Tyrion once threatening her happiness, treats Joffrey's last gesture as **pointing to a killer**, and asks Jaime to murder Tyrion in a cell. These are **her accusation and reading of dying movement**, not a confirmed confession or a witnessed toxicological chain.
13. **Jaime refuses to execute his brother without evidence.** He says Tyrion remains his brother and demands to learn what happened, noting physical difficulty after losing his sword hand. This is an explicit limit on Cersei's expectation of automatic solidarity, though Jaime still knows a dangerous personal lie underlies their relationship.
14. **Their intimacy in the sept is troubling and power-laden.** Cersei initially resists sexual contact in a sacred space, speaks of danger and septons, and physically pushes Jaime; Jaime **continues despite her initial refusals**. The narration then records her active participation and hurried encouragement. **Do not erase the initial nonconsensual pressure or simplistically label the whole scene uniformly consensual**; her spoken boundaries deserve explicit record.
15. **Jaime proposes public acknowledgment and marriage.** He wants Cersei to disclose their relationship, marry him, and have another child. Cersei refuses, correctly pointing out **Tommen's legal claim depends on his public Baratheon paternity**. Their goals diverge sharply; this is a proposed dynastic rupture, not an executed confession.
16. **Cersei notices Jaime is changed and rejects continued discussion.** She recoils from his stump, accuses his experiences of altering him, and asks him to leave. She says **Sansa's maids are held in a tower cell** for questioning. This corroborates Tyrion's account of the court search and places **Shae/Brella under possible interrogation**, though this chapter does **not say which maid has confessed**.
17. **Tywin had known of Jaime's release before the reunion.** He says **Varys notified him days after the escape**, and that he dispatched **Gregor Clegane, Samwell Spicer and the Plumm brothers** to search discreetly. **Bolton had expected Jaime earlier**. Thus Tywin's information chain involves multiple named intermediaries, not an independent miraculous discovery.
18. **Tywin confirms Gregor seized Harrenhal and describes Hoat's torture.** The Brave Companions deserted in large numbers; some old **Lady Whent** servants opened a postern; **Vargo Hoat** was found feverish and in pain after Brienne injured his ear. Gregor's men **cut off his hands and feet**; he has **not yet been explicitly reported dead**. Scattered Mummers' fate remains unresolved.
19. **Tywin says postmortem examination argues against ordinary choking.** He ordered **Joffrey's throat opened**; maesters allegedly found **no physical obstruction**. This is **Tywin's report of an autopsy, not a direct medical POV**. It strengthens a poisoning hypothesis but does **not identify a compound, vessel, poisoner or intent**, nor does it make Cersei's choice of suspect reliable.
20. **Investigations and arrests widen.** **Podrick Payne** and **Sansa's maids** are in custody; **gold cloaks** search for Sansa while Varys offers a reward. Tyrion is to be tried for **regicide and kinslaying**. No sentence has yet been rendered.
21. **Jaime declares Kingsguard service is for life.** He tells Tywin there must be precedent for disabled brothers, and that Cersei's removal of **Barristan Selmy** was an institutional error that should not become accepted. Tywin proposes persuading the High Septon to release Jaime by gifts, illustrating how a bad precedent can be used to unmake lifelong service.
22. **Tywin intends dynastic redistribution after Joffrey's death.** He wishes to install **Tommen at Casterly Rock as Jaime's ward/squire**, arrange another husband for **Cersei** (he floats **Oberyn Martell**), and marry **Jaime** (possibly to newly widowed **Margaery Tyrell**, whose family presses to marry **Tommen**). These are **proposals**, not completed betrothals.
23. **Jaime refuses Casterly Rock and dynastic marriage categorically.** He insists **the only role he wants is Lord Commander of the Kingsguard** and rejects every proposed match, including Margaery. This is a publicly consequential identity choice, not a demonstration he has already separated emotionally from Cersei.
24. **Tywin responds by disowning Jaime verbally.** Tywin tells him **he is not his son** and orders him to perform the white-cloak duties if that is all he claims. This is **a witnessed rupture**, not a recorded legal disinheritance instrument or completed transfer of Casterly Rock to Tyrion.
25. **End state:** Jaime is in King's Landing; Brienne is a guarded suspect; Cersei demands Tyrion killed, Tywin demands Jaime abandon his vows, and Jaime refuses both requests. **Joffrey's autopsy is reported, not produced; Tywin/Jaime's legal future and the actual poisoner remain unresolved.**
