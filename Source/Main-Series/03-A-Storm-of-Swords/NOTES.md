## Arya and Bran: third-book source anchors (2026-10-15)

- **ASOS 9 (Bran):** Summer's wolf-body is exhilarating; Jojen warns Bran of losing his human identity in the animal.
- **ASOS 24 (Bran):** The Knight of the Laughing Tree narrative gives a filtered account of past Stark/Harrenhal events, not historical omniscience.
- **ASOS 52, 65 (Arya):** Arya's attempted family reunion fails at the Red Wedding. Her later wolf dream depicts Nymeria drawing a dead woman's body from the river; Arya interprets it as seeing her mother.
- **ASOS Epilogue (Merrett):** Independently depicts a resurrected Catelyn overseeing the Brotherhood's punishment of a Frey. Together these support the **inference**, not direct Arya knowledge, that Nymeria recovered her mother's corpse.
- **ASOS 56 (Bran):** Bran enters Hodor's body while frightened at the Nightfort; Sam opens the Black Gate through Night's Watch vows. Physical ancient magic is shown; its original purpose is not.
- **ASOS 74 (Arya):** Arya leaves the wounded Sandor and arranges passage to Braavos with Jaqen's coin; no Sandor death is shown.

## Robb's will: council attendance vs document custody (focused source check, 2026-10-15)

- **ASOS 35, 45 (Catelyn):** Robb believes Bran and Rickon dead and is concerned Sansa's Lannister marriage undermines the succession. He asks his assembled lords to seal a document witnessing his decision. The legal text and completed seals are **not separately shown**.
- **ASOS 45:** Greatjon, Maege Mormont, Galbart Glover, Edmure Tully, Jason Mallister and an unnamed ship captain are present with Robb and Catelyn. **Presence is not proof each signed**; Ser Raynald escorted Catelyn into the meeting but is not shown remaining for the succession instruction.
- **ASOS 45:** Maege and Galbart are sent separately toward Howland Reed with intentionally misleading military letters. The story does not say they carry Robb's will.

Canonical location/status assessment: [witness ledger](../../../research/continuity/robbs-will-witness-ledger.md). This is an additional selective passage audit, not a completed sequential chapter note.

## ASOS 1–3 sequential close reading (2026-10-16)

- [1 Jaime](chapters/01-jaime.md): firsthand account of Catelyn's sword-enforced vow to recover both Stark girls, Brienne's allegiance to Catelyn and seamanship, Jaime's haircut, murdered riverland women, Robin Ryger pursuit and Brienne's cliff-rock ambush. Jaime helps Brienne back into the skiff despite an escape temptation. Jaime's thoughts about Cersei commissioning the Bran catspaw remain speculation.
- [2 Catelyn](chapters/02-catelyn.md): Catelyn admits her conscious unauthorized exchange, is confined to Hoster's rooms; his dying Tansy/blood/trueborn remarks suggest hidden wrong against Lysa but the identity and medical outcome are still unresolved. Robb reportedly wounded at Crag; Edmure sends **three ravens to Bolton** falsely calling the exchange an escape and offering **1,000 dragons** for capture.
- [3 Arya](chapters/03-arya.md): escape companions move north but navigation and named river identification are uncertain; Arya refuses to abandon Gendry/Hot Pie, recognizes need for rest, and dreams of commanding a wolf pack that kills apparent pursuing Bloody Mummers including belled Iggo. Their real-world deaths **remain unverified** at this chapter endpoint.

- [4 Tyrion](chapters/04-tyrion.md): Alayaya publicly whipped and released, Cersei's agents recover Tommen, Bywater killed during desertions, Marbrand now commands 4,400 gold cloaks; Saan's Lyseni evacuate troops while 'Renly's ghost' remains secondhand. Tywin reveals pincer against Tallhart/Glover at Duskendale, names wedding day, confirms Myrcella in Dorne and rejects Tyrion's rightful-heir claim to Casterly Rock, threatening the next woman found in his bed.

## ASOS 5–7 sequential close reading (2026-10-16)

- [5 Davos](chapters/05-davos.md): survived by swimming beneath the raised Blackwater chain before blackout and waking on a barren bay rock. Starvation, fever, thirst and lost fingerbones lead to near despair, a prayer to the Mother and guilt over Melisandre's earlier religious acts. He signals a Lyseni galley that says it serves Stannis; his sons' individual fates and Stannis's personal location remain unverified.
- [6 Sansa](chapters/06-sansa.md): Margaery and Olenna invite Sansa; Loras acknowledges killing Robar Royce and assesses Garlan as the better sword. Olenna has Butterbumps loudly sing to block spies while Sansa truthfully describes Joffrey's abuse; the Tyrell wedding remains planned, and Olenna proposes Sansa marry **Willas Tyrell**, heir to Highgarden, potentially acquiring a Stark/Northern claim. No marriage or safe departure has occurred.
- [7 Jon](chapters/07-jon.md): Jon enters Mance's huge **civilian-and-military** Milkwater camp under Qhorin's espionage order. Mance is the lute player, not Styr or Tormund, tells of secretly attending Robert's Winterfell feast and his desertion over a wildling healer's red-silk repair to his black cloak; Mance invokes guest right. Jon employs real bastard-status exclusion as a cover story while the **Milkwater diggings' object** remains unanswered.

- [8 Daenerys](chapters/08-daenerys.md): actual travel on Illyrio's three ships; infant dragons fly and can be verbally commanded to breathe fire; Arstan suggests captive Dragonpit dragons grew smaller and describes Rhaegar's unnamed scroll-induced decision to become a warrior. Jorah suspects Illyrio and Arstan, proposes using Illyrio's merchandise to buy Astapor's Unsullied, then kisses Dany uninvited and reads three-headed-dragon prophecy as a marriage to two dragon riders, including himself. A diversion to Astapor is **decided but not yet ordered or executed**.

## ASOS 9–11 sequential close reading (2026-10-16)

- [9 Bran](chapters/09-bran.md): Summer physically takes over an ordinary wolf pack; Bran resists returning to his body and repeatedly forgets Jojen's simple controlled actions. Jojen distinguishes greenseers from green-dreamers and warns of loss of self; Meera secures Bran's decision to risk the journey toward the crow rather than stay hidden at Tumbledown Tower.
- [10 Davos](chapters/10-davos.md): Devan reportedly rescued and still near Stannis; Saan's report of Stannis's isolation, Selyse/Alester's religious court, nominal privateering claims, and burned Sunglass/Rambton kin. Davos meets Robert's acknowledged son Edric Storm, hears Patchface's mysterious chained-wedding song, but Axell Florent arrests him for planning to kill Melisandre before he sees the king.
- [11 Jaime](chapters/11-jaime.md): Brienne turns away from a probable roadside robbery lure and keeps Jaime's wrists chained; Jaime remembers Cersei engineering his Kingsguard appointment to escape Tywin's planned Lysa Tully marriage, thereby losing Casterly Rock and inadvertently separating the twins. He directly recalls killing **pyromancer Rossart** before **Aerys**, whose stated orders threatened mass burning; exact wildfire caches and full conspiracy remain unverified in this chapter.

- [12 Tyrion](chapters/12-tyrion.md): Varys discloses Pycelle's restoration to block the Citadel selecting Gormon Tyrell, implying insider Oldtown intelligence; Boros restored and Mandon's Vale history yielded no murderer sponsor. Kettleblacks now favor Cersei, and Varys's north-wall rooms conceal secret stairs under a rising stone bed. Tyrion again fails to separate from Shae; singer Symon has entered Lollys's household, and Tyrion orders Bronn to **find**, not yet kill, him.

## ASOS 13–15 sequential close reading (2026-10-16)

- [13 Arya](chapters/13-arya.md): reaches Trident, meets Tom Sevenstrings, Lem Lemoncloak and Anguy; the gang's inn is directly confirmed as Jaime/Brienne's Inn of the Kneeling Man. Husband's claim Tom and Lem were meant to recover the horses proves a prior robbery trap; the men now offer Arya an IOU for her horses. Arya breaks Lem's nose resisting restraint; former Winterfell stableman **Harwin** recognizes and kneels to Arya Stark.
- [14 Catelyn](chapters/14-catelyn.md): Robb publicly forgives Catelyn for Jaime release and reveals he married **Jeyne Westerling** after Crag injury and false Bran/Rickon death news, losing the Freys' roughly 4,000 men. Grey Wind distrusts **Rolph Spicer** and is kept away from Robb. Robb/Brynden explain a formerly undisclosed plan to draw Tywin west that Edmure's Red Fork defense disrupted; **Edmure had not been told its strategic purpose**. Catelyn proposes a new personal/marriage concession to recover Frey support.
- [15 Jon](chapters/15-jon.md): direct description of mammoth-mounted giants, Mag the Mighty, Tormund's tall tales, and the diverse civilian free-folk host; the Horn of Joramun remains Jon's speculation. Ygritte identifies the blue-grey eagle as **Orell** after it claws Jon's face. The Fist has been ruined by the walking dead, not a direct wildling assault; Mance elicits Watch numbers and names, orders wight precautions, then assigns Jon and Ygritte to **Styr and Jarl's Wall-crossing party**. No crossing has happened yet.

- [16 Sansa](chapters/16-sansa.md): Cersei has a seamstress urgently prepare a luxurious wardrobe for Sansa before Joffrey's wedding, with motive undisclosed; Margaery presents Loras's Kingsguard posting as her protection against Joffrey. Sansa unusually remembers **Sandor kissing her** in ACOK 62 although the earlier direct scene did not document a kiss (**UnKiss** memory discrepancy). Dontos insists she wear the silver hairnet for the wedding-night boat/ship escape and explicitly alleges Tyrells seek her **Winterfell claim**, though he is an interested rival sponsor.

## ASOS 17–20 sequential close reading (2026-10-16)

- [17 Arya](chapters/17-arya.md): Brotherhood escorts head south despite Arya's Riverrun expectations; Harwin relates the Mummer's Ford ambush and Beric's subsequent commoner-protection mission. Arya flees a highborn-captive transfer but Harwin catches her, explicitly distinguishing loyalty to smallfolk from Robb's crown. Harwin's Lyanna riding comparison is testimony, not a fate claim.
- [18 Samwell](chapters/18-samwell.md): Sam dispatches **two initial warnings** from the Fist, then releases remaining ravens without his subsequent battle reports. Thoren Smallwood dies fighting a dead bear; Small Paul carries Sam and later dies protecting him from an **actual Other**, which Sam destroys with **Jon's dragonglass dagger**. The dagger survives and Grenn carries it; reunion with Mormont is not yet shown.
- [19 Tyrion](chapters/19-tyrion.md): Littlefinger discloses the Tyrell Willas–Sansa proposal and is authorized to court Lysa after Harrenhal makes him a credible suitor; Tyrion inherits master of coin. Tywin plans Tyrion's **pre-Joffrey-wedding** marriage to Sansa and seeks Cersei–Willas as compensation, explaining Sansa's earlier mystery wardrobe. Balon's alliance terms are refused, the Wall's warnings discounted, and Tywin hints at a profitable reading of Robb's Westerling marriage without disclosing its mechanism.
- [20 Catelyn](chapters/20-catelyn.md): Rickard Karstark's men murder the captive Willem Lannister and Tion Frey plus Tully guards; **about 300 Karstark riders had already deserted before** Robb beheads Rickard. Robb rejects Edmure's hostage alternative, considers the captive Harrion's succession problem and warns Bolton in principle (actual dispatch unshown). Jeyne says Sybell supplies a **daily herbal fertility posset**; neither its ingredients nor any contraceptive effect are established.

**Cross-POV priorities:** Compare the **wardrobe deadline** (ASOS 16) with Tywin's marriage deadline (ASOS 19); reconcile **Sybell's rumored maegi ancestry** (ASOS 19) with her posset (ASOS 20) without upgrading suspicion to proof; distinguish **two sent Fist warnings** from later unsent draft messages (ASOS 18); and track Karstark riders and any Bolton warning independently of Robb's execution.

## ASOS 21–24 sequential close reading (2026-10-16)

- [21 Jaime](chapters/21-jaime.md): Cleos Frey dies after a bowman attack and an uncontrolled fall; **the attackers are not identified as Vargo's men**. Jaime attacks Brienne using Cleos's sword, and their fierce duel is interrupted by the **Bolton-aligned Brave Companions**. Jaime deliberately exaggerates the wealth of the Sapphire Isle to improve Brienne's ransom protection. Vargo orders a violent 'message' to Tywin; the anatomical result is **not narrated by the end of this chapter**.
- [22 Arya](chapters/22-arya.md): the Brotherhood's tree villages, scattered cells and food/ransom logistics are described. On **High Heart**, Arya overhears a pale old woman's dreams involving a burning-heart shadow, a faceless figure and drowned crow at a bridge, and a red-teared dead fish-woman; referents remain unknown at this chapter checkpoint. Northerners hunting Jaime have reportedly looted a sept. At Acorn Hall, Lady Smallwood shelters Arya and warns the Bolton insignia on her borrowed clothing could be fatal.
- [23 Daenerys](chapters/23-daenerys.md): Kraznys says **8,000** Unsullied are available and describes violent childhood training and identity suppression. Dany conceals her knowledge of his High Valyrian; a captive Naathi child filters the translation. Arstan objects to slave ownership while Jorah argues for military necessity. Dany **has not bought an army or ordered an attack**. The ship and dragon safety problems intensify.
- [24 Bran](chapters/24-bran.md): a **likely Liddle** gives Bran's party shelter and food but reports the Bastard's flayed men looking for travelers and wolfskins. His account of nearly message-less Watch ravens is compatible with Sam's **two earlier letters and later empty releases**. Meera narrates the Knight of the Laughing Tree story at Harrenhal, including a weirwood-shielded anonymous champion and the dragon prince's search; **the rider's identity remains unconfirmed**.

**High-priority cross-references:** Jaime's captors' purported service to Bolton (ASOS 21) and mountain flayed-man searches (ASOS 24) need authority/chronology testing. The Ghost of High Heart's symbols (ASOS 22) must remain separate from later event confirmations. The Knight of the Laughing Tree (ASOS 24) should be cross-checked with Reed, Harrenhal and Lyanna/Rhaegar testimony without fixing its rider.

## ASOS 25–28 sequential close reading (2026-10-16)

- [25 Davos](chapters/25-davos.md): imprisoned but recovering, Davos meets Melisandre, who **claims** depleted Stannis life-fire limits shadow creation and identifies Stannis with Azor Ahai. Davos does not accept her theology. **Alester Florent** joins his cell after proposing terms to Tywin: Stannis would renounce his crown and bastardy accusations, retain Dragonstone/Storm's End, and marry **Shireen to Tommen**; Davos refuses any peace without Stannis's authorization. Delivery of the letter and Stannis's response are unverified.
- [26 Jon](chapters/26-jon.md): Jon instructs **Ghost to travel east toward Castle Black**, but his compliance/arrival are unknown. In Styr and Jarl's hidden cave north of the Wall, Jon reveals some Watch patrol doctrine while **inflating total strength by three hundred**. Ygritte recounts **Gorne's Way**, an unverified cavern route beneath the Wall, and the relationship moves beyond any simple undercover necessity. The crossing still has **not** happened.
- [27 Daenerys](chapters/27-daenerys.md): Dany negotiates for 8,000 trained Unsullied, 600 further capped soldiers and roughly 5,000 trainees by offering all goods, three ships and **Drogon**. She reveals her Valyrian to Missandei and frees the girl, whose **three brothers** were made Unsullied. After physically exchanging Drogon's chain for the harpy's-fingers whip she has **Drogon burn Kraznys**, commands the Unsullied to attack slavers and free captives, and forbids harm to children under twelve. **Liberation begins** on page; postbattle governance and full compliance are unknown.
- [28 Sansa](chapters/28-sansa.md): Cersei's mysterious gown is confirmed as a **Stark maiden's wedding outfit**. Under royal coercion Sansa publicly **marries Tyrion** before witnesses, with the Tyrells absent from the sept. Tyrion blocks Joffrey's customary public bedding, but privately faces Tywin's command to consummate; recognizing Sansa's fear and age (**almost thirteen**), he **refuses intercourse** and promises to wait for her desire. The marriage is solemnized but **not consummated**, with dynastic implications unadjudicated.

**Cross-POV priorities:** Do not confuse Melisandre's prophecy reading with established cosmic fact, nor Alester's negotiated peace with Stannis's consent. Track whether Ghost reaches the Watch, distinguish Jon's truthful patrol leak from inflated numbers, test legal consequences of the **unconsummated Sansa marriage**, and audit whether Dany's liberation order protects those she means to protect after fighting subsides.

## ASOS 29–32 sequential close reading (2026-10-16)

- [29 Arya](chapters/29-arya.md): Stoney Sept connects the **Battle of the Bells** (Connington, Robert, Ned, Hoster, Denys Arryn, Myles Mooton) to later local devastation. Men accused of atrocities under a 'wolf' designation die in crow cages; Arya **gives three prisoners water** before Anguy **shoots all three**. At Tansy's Peach, **Bella** claims her mother was with Robert during his flight, introducing a possible **Bella–Gendry** blood relationship that no character confirms. Gendry defends Arya by pretending to be her brother. Another rumor misconstrues Catelyn's actual release of Jaime as romantic.
- [30 Jon](chapters/30-jon.md): a leaning tree and neglected Watch maintenance aid an assault on the Wall. **Jarl and three companions die** after collapsing ice sends them down; two Thenns die while climbing the five replacement rope ladders, yielding **six witnessed fatalities** in the crossing. Jon and Ygritte reach the top, not yet south of the Wall. **Ygritte says the search for the Horn of Joramun opened about fifty graves without finding it**. Magical 'Wall defending itself' remains Jon's interpretation, with unstable ice an ordinary rival.
- [31 Jaime](chapters/31-jaime.md): Zollo's **amputation of Jaime's right hand** is confirmed. Brienne challenges him to live; Jaime deliberately uses the **fabricated Tarth sapphire ransom** to stop a planned sexual assault against her. At Harrenhal, **Roose Bolton, Freys and Robb's direwolf** share the outward political space, and Roose already knows **Rickard Karstark has been executed**. Qyburn, whose Citadel chain was removed for reasons not yet given, treats Jaime's infected stump; later survival remains to be checked.
- [32 Tyrion](chapters/32-tyrion.md): Symon Silver Tongue blackmails Tyrion with a song about Shae, after which Tyrion **orders Bronn to kill him and conceal the body**; fulfillment unshown. **Tobho Mott shows two black-and-red Valyrian steel swords** for Joffrey and Jaime without naming the original steel's source. Tywin admits **Sansa's unconsummated marriage is vulnerable to setting-aside**, reports **Olenna derailed Cersei–Willas**, refuses financial relief despite escalating debt, and orders a letter conditioning aid to the Watch on support for **Janos Slynt** as Lord Commander. Marsh's **original warning and later empty ravens** fit Sam's ASOS 18 firsthand sequence.

**Priority cross-checks:** Verify Bella's reported paternity and distinguish Gendry's unawareness; avoid identifying Sam's old horn as Joramun's merely because Ygritte says none was found; track Jaime's injury and Brienne's protection without resolving Bolton loyalty too soon; find later **explicit provenance** for Mott's two swords and **outcome evidence** for Tyrion's Symon order; distinguish ordered political interference in Watch elections from any election result.
