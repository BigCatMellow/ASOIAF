## Robb's will: council attendance vs document custody (focused source check, 2026-10-15)

- **ASOS 35, 45 (Catelyn):** Robb believes Bran and Rickon dead and is concerned Sansa's Lannister marriage undermines the succession. He asks his assembled lords to seal a document witnessing his decision. The legal text and completed seals are **not separately shown**.
- **ASOS 45:** Greatjon, Maege Mormont, Galbart Glover, Edmure Tully, Jason Mallister and an unnamed ship captain are present with Robb and Catelyn. **Presence is not proof each signed**; Ser Raynald escorted Catelyn into the meeting but is not shown remaining for the succession instruction.
- **ASOS 45:** Maege and Galbart are sent separately toward Howland Reed with intentionally misleading military letters. The story does not say they carry Robb's will.

Track whether Ghost reaches the Watch, distinguish Jon's truthful patrol leak from inflated numbers, test legal consequences of the **unconsummated Sansa marriage**, and audit whether Dany's liberation order protects those she means to protect after fighting subsides.

- [33 Samwell](chapters/33-samwell.md): at Craster's Keep the Watch survivors have **two dragonglass daggers, nineteen arrows and an obsidian spearhead**, but Sam rightly distinguishes an **Other** from **wights** when evaluating how well those weapons might work. Sandor contests collective responsibility for the crimes of the Lannister forces, while Arya specifically accuses him of murdering **Mycah**. Beric's bleeding sword ignites; Sandor prevails and seems to kill Beric, then **admits** he did kill Mycah. Arya is prevented from attacking him. **Beric returns visibly alive**, though the actual revival rite is not directly seen.
- [35 Catelyn](chapters/35-catelyn.md): the family commits **Hoster Tully** to the river, Edmure misses three funeral arrows, and Brynden ignites the boat. Catelyn still conceals Hoster's **Tansy** last word. Robb learns of **Sansa's marriage**, yet not that it is **unconsummated**, and is distressed by the disastrous, confusing **Duskendale** operation. **Lothar Frey** delivers a purported Walder-boys letter blaming Theon for Winterfell's destruction and promoting Bolton's bastard as rescuer—**interested testimony at odds with earlier firsthand POVs**. Walder demands Robb's personal apology and **Edmure's immediate marriage to sixteen-year-old Roslin**, accepted under strategic pressure.
- [36 Davos](chapters/36-davos.md): **Axell Florent** threatens Davos to seek appointment as Hand and urges attacking the underdefended civilians of **Claw Isle**, listing a supposed **Valyrian steel axe** and sea-monster horn among Celtigar treasures. Davos argues this would be a crime; **Stannis rejects the raid**, publicly appoints **Davos Lord of the Rainwood, Admiral of the Narrow Sea and Hand**, and explicitly **refuses to sacrifice innocent Edric Storm** to wake a stone dragon. Stannis says he saw a cold forest-and-hill battle in a fire vision and then **burns three leeches associated with Edric's blood**, naming Joffrey, Balon and Robb. The ritual is witnessed; causal consequences are not established here.

**Cross-POV and theory priorities:** Trace Mormont's death against the delayed King's Landing messages and the planned **Janos Slynt** election; distinguish Other-vs-wight weapon testing; treat Sandor's combat acquittal and actual Mycah confession as different evidence classes; audit Frey Winterfell claims versus **ACOK Theon/Bran** before assuming their good faith; test whether Stannis's fire vision genuinely concerns the Fist; and do not infer that named future royal deaths were *caused* by three leeches.

## ASOS 37–40 sequential close reading (2026-10-16)

The named pyromancers **Rossart, Belis and Garigus** and alleged hidden caches (Baelor's Sept, Flea Bottom, all seven gates and Red Keep) are precise leads; the chapter does **not** verify every cache's present state. **Roose Bolton** reports sending **Harrion Karstark with Glover**, claims Arya has been found despite the real Arya being elsewhere, and conditions Jaime's prospective release on clearing Bolton of blame for his mutilation. **Walton Steelshanks** is the announced escort; **Brienne is to remain with Hoat**, despite the earlier promise of protection.

He describes **Willas Tyrell's tourney injury** as accidental and claims they remain cordial; independent Tyrell opinion is unknown.
- [39 Arya](chapters/39-arya.md): The Brotherhood assaults a septry occupied by Mummers, saves **eight remaining brown brothers** and executes **Septon Utt** and other prisoners after short trials. **Thoros says Beric has been revived six times**, describes the flame-breath **last kiss**, and Beric acknowledges **fading memories and diminished appetite/sleep**. - [40 Bran](chapters/40-bran.md): **Old Nan's submerged, zigzagging lake causeway** proves real, giving Bran's party a refuge amid the abandoned Gift. They plan to investigate passage through a deserted Watch castle but **find no entrance in this chapter**. While armed strangers approach and thunder causes Hodor to panic, Bran **involuntarily enters Hodor's mind for half a heartbeat**, making Hodor fall silent; the implications for **human skinchanging, consent and identity** require careful scrutiny. Bran next enters **Summer** to investigate figures at the village inn; their identities are **not revealed before this chapter ends**.

**Cross-POV priorities:** Corroborate Aerys-era wildfire sites and surviving stockpiles; audit **Harrion's** custody and Roose's shifting Lannister incentives; test **Oberyn–Gregor–Tywin** culpability without assuming orders; verify six Beric resurrections against his visible injuries and memory losses; and examine the ethical and cognitive consequences of Bran's **unconsented control of Hodor**.

## ASOS 41–44 sequential close reading (2026-10-16)

- [41 Jon](chapters/41-jon.md): Jon travels **south of the Wall**, having descended at Greyguard, and recalls Ned and Benjen's **unrealized resettlement proposal** for the Gift. At the lake village of **Queenscrown**—the same hidden holdfast and storm depicted in [ASOS 40 Bran](chapters/40-bran.md)—**Jon refuses Styr's order to kill an elderly captive**; **Ygritte** kills the man instead.
