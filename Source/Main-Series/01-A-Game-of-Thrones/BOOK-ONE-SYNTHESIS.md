## I. The eight major causal stories, not just theories

### 1. Stark family separation destroys their common information base

The Stark household begins as a unit but becomes separated across **Winterfell, the Wall, King's Landing, Riverlands, Vale and wilderness**, partly because Ned takes the Handship `AGOT 4`. Bran's disabling injury `AGOT 8/17`, Jon's choice to join Watch `AGOT 10/19/48`, Lady's death and Mycah's murder `AGOT 15–16`, Arya's street escape `AGOT 50/65` and Sansa's captivity `AGOT 51/67` ensure siblings can't compare evidence directly. **This fragmentation is a plot mechanism**, not simply a theme.

**Decisive miss:** Arya `AGOT 32` overhears a credible court-political conversation but cannot communicate the intelligence accurately to Ned. Sansa `AGOT 44–45` unknowingly speaks the central genealogical clue, then `AGOT 51` discloses the planned departure while trying to save her marriage. Robb `AGOT 71` reasons about succession **without learning what Ned confirmed to Cersei**. Each action is understandable from a narrower information set.

### 2. Court power breaks the difference between lawful succession and effective command

Jon Arryn's investigation first appears as a suspected poisoning (`AGOT 4/25`), then an inspection of genealogy and Robert's bastards (`AGOT 27/35/45`). `AGOT 45` **Cersei directly confirms Jaime fathered all three royal children**; this is a strong established fact, **not** an unresolved fan theory. The dagger's catspaw owner is **not** thereby solved: Petyr claims Tyrion took it in a wager (`AGOT 18`), while Tyrion's own analysis (`AGOT 31`) and Petyr's actual betrayed custody `AGOT 49` must be compared.

Robert's dying appointment (`AGOT 47`) places Ned as regent, yet Ned substitutes **“my heir”** for dictated **“my son Joffrey”**. Court destruction of the sealed document plus Janos Slynt's actual betrayal `AGOT 49` demonstrates that **possession of armed command** defeats both genetic truth and fragile parchment. Ned `AGOT 58/65` makes a false confession under threat to Sansa; Joffrey's execution order overrules expected exile.

### 3. War is a material network problem, not simply a clash of heroes

Ned understands the North's vulnerable passages early and orders Moat Cailin, White Harbor and Theon precautions `AGOT 20`. Tywin's Riverlands forces operate through fear, roads and supply (`AGOT 43/56/69`). Robb faces feed limits with a feudal levy `AGOT 53/55`; the **Green Fork** and **Twins** are actual obstacles, not map decorations. Walder Frey's cooperation carries **specific hostages and marriage obligations** `AGOT 59` that remain live after coronation.

The separate campaigns explain the apparent contradiction of the Lannisters beating Stark foot at Green Fork (`AGOT 62`) while losing Jaime's elite mounted wing in the **Whispering Wood** (`AGOT 63`) and the subsequent Riverrun siege (`AGOT 69/71`). A continuation cannot pretend Tywin lacks remaining power or that Robb's victory erased supply constraints.

### 4. The Watch is factually endangered and politically invisible

The prologue directly depicts an Other, distinct from a reanimated human. The Watch's own officers have insufficient men and are missing Benjen/Royce `AGOT 13/19/21`. Two lost rangers become **wights** inside Castle Black; Othor attacks Mormont `AGOT 52`, Jafer kills Rykker and four men (later report in `AGOT 60`). These are no longer mere stories, but **what makes and commands them remains unobserved**.

Osha's `AGOT 53` specific warning that Robb is going **south instead of north** is materially vindicated by Watch events, and Mormont makes the same strategic prioritization `AGOT 70`. This is **not** positive evidence that the ancient Watch or Wall was built facing the opposite direction. The Watch's actual **vow**, tunnel gates, family duty and the older `First Men built it` tradition all require separate analysis.

### 5. Bran's disability and dreams open two different kinds of power

Bran cannot walk after the fall `AGOT 17`; he gains ordinary agency through **Tyrion's saddle and Dancer** `AGOT 24/37`, then a basket carried by Hodor `AGOT 53`. The crow dreams, comatose distant visions, wolf behaviors and crypt dreams with Rickon `AGOT 17/24/66` are a **second** avenue of potential perception, not a magical replacement for medical, social and ethical consequences of paralysis.

Winterfell has **geothermal water and heated buildings** `AGOT 6/53`, a seasonal winter town `AGOT 37`, and extensive crypts with possible older deeper levels `AGOT 66`. This supports **refuge infrastructure and major underground structures**; it does not certify a functioning, inhabited hidden city.

### 6. Arya and Sansa learn very different survival disciplines

Arya's practical and observational training with Syrio `AGOT 22/32/50` lets her identify false-color guards at the still-moored `Wind Witch`, evade capture, hunt pigeons, and survive execution crowds `AGOT 65`. Syrio's death is **not directly witnessed** in `AGOT 50`; his survival and supposed secret Faceless Men affiliation are equally unproved.

Sansa is manipulated into revealing departure plans `AGOT 51`, then writes letters under coercion, one of which reaches Robb `AGOT 53`. The hostage survives Joffrey's cruelty `AGOT 67` through performed courtesy, while briefly considering a lethal shove. Her terror and age must not be flattened into either villainous complicity or mindless passivity.

### 7. Dany's story joins real compassion to structures of violence

Dany grows from a coerced bride `AGOT 3/11` into Drogo's publicly influential khaleesi `AGOT 23/36/46`. The attempt to poison her `AGOT 54` **changes Drogo's decision** to seek Westeros; the invasion is not simply a fulfilled prophecy. Its financing is exposed in `AGOT 61`: Jorah proposes **selling enslaved Lhazareen survivors** to buy ships. Dany stops sexual assaults but protects victims **by claiming them as slaves**, while named Eroeh is later taken and killed `AGOT 68`.

Mirri Maz Duur has a specific, testable **Asshai/Marwyn education story** `AGOT 61`; Drogo's wound is badly infected after removal of her painful bandage `AGOT 64`. She later performs blood magic involving horse death, forbidden tent entry, labor, and Dany's son; Mirri acknowledges reasons to retaliate after destruction of her people `AGOT 68`. *No single-source shortcut should erase competing causes or enslaved persons' lack of consent.*

### 8. Dragons return, but their meaning is not fully settled

The `AGOT 54` experiment of **eggs in coals fails to hatch**. The `AGOT 72` ritual includes dead husband, horse, deliberately burned Mirri, oil, intense pyre, Dany's participation and a red comet. **All three dragons physically hatch**, Dany survives and followers kneel. These are established on-page events. The **necessary and sufficient mechanism**, permanent fire immunity, comet's causal role and any identification with Azor Ahai remain **UNKNOWN / THEORY**.

## III. Critical “who knows?” checks at the AGOT endpoint

- **Reader:** knows Cersei/Jaime parentage and witnessed an Other and wights, **but not** catspaw sponsor, Benjen's fate, R+L=J as fact or hatch mechanism.
- **Ned:** discovers paternity, plans Stannis succession and evacuation; **dies** before sharing a verified family-wide account.
- **Robb/Catelyn:** know Ned executed and Jaime captured; Robb does **not** know paternity at coronation. They cannot assume Arya escaped or Sansa free.
- **Sansa:** knows Ned dead and she is a hostage, not the independent political basis of succession. Arya's whereabouts remain unknown.
- **Arya:** watches failed appeal/public spectacle and is rescued by Yoren; she does not know Sansa's coerced letters or who truly rules the Watch.
- **Jon/Mormont:** know dead men rise and northern range needed; Jon learns father executed but not the specific full southern succession evidence.
- **Bran/Rickon/Luwin/Osha:** know Ned's death by raven and shared troubling dreams; only Osha's warning points to wider old northern dangers, which Luwin disputes.
- **Dany/Jorah:** directly know three hatchlings, Mirri and Drogo's losses. Neither has evidence of detailed post-Ned northern politics.

## IV. Method / challenges carried into ACOK

1. **Maintain close-reading rhythm:** plot/decisions, then precise objects and geography, then reliable witnesses, then counterhypotheses. All `73/73` AGOT narrative units have **first-pass** files; this is **not** a certification of exhaustive line-by-line independent review.
2. **Source-check rejected convenient narratives:** Petyr's knife account, Mirri's infant anatomy, Robert's purported death plot, raven deliveries, and each dream's external confirmation.
3. **Keep justice and actual institutions separate:** crown, Iron Throne, City Watch, Frey bridge, Watch vows, slave ownership, marriage and religious allegiances all make choices hard.
4. **Use later-book evidence to update this synthesis, not overwrite prior uncertainty retroactively.** Store canonical cross-book interpretations in the existing [research theory cases](../../../research/theories/CATALOG.md), character sheets and [source-clue index](../../../research/source-clue-index.md).
5. **Next sequential pass:** [A Clash of Kings notes](../02-A-Clash-of-Kings/NOTES.md), beginning at `ACOK 1 (Arya)`; its prologue has an earlier separate first pass.

The most important guardrail: **a successful theory must explain both the enormous obvious consequences and the ostensibly throwaway physical details without contradicting what a character actually saw or knew.**
