# AGOT 54 (Daenerys) — the poisoned wine and Drogo's new invasion pledge

## Main causal spine (do not substitute symbolism for events)

1. **VERIFIED:** After Viserys's death, Dany asks Drogo to claim her ancestral throne. Drogo refuses to cross the sea: he considers salt water poisonous and ships unacceptable “wooden horses.” He instead imagines conquest eastward after Rhaego's birth. **Jorah's account:** Westeros means little to Drogo, and eastern rewards are more familiar.
2. **VERIFIED:** Dany experiences a genuine identity conflict. She could accept life as khaleesi and eventual `dosh khaleen` elder, but thinks of her lost childhood home and the now-extinct Targaryen line through Viserys. Her remembered **red door** offers emotional grounding but does **not** independently establish a correct geography of her childhood.
3. **VERIFIED:** Merchant Captain **Byan Votyris** arrives at Vaes Dothrak from Pentos via Norvos and Qohor with a caravan of roughly **four hundred horses**. Dany visits the Western Market with four young `khas` guards, attendants and Jorah. Jorah excuses himself to inspect a supposed letter from Illyrio; Dany does not see the contents then.
4. **VERIFIED:** Dany enjoys food and smells of the western Free Cities. A blonde merchant offering imported wines recognizes she is Targaryen and offers an apparently free cask branded with the **Redwyne grape sigil**. She accepts for Drogo.
5. **VERIFIED:** Jorah returns suddenly and orders the merchant to open the cask and **drink first**. The merchant stalls, sweats, grabs the cask and hurls it toward Dany rather than drink. Jorah knocks her away; Doreah prevents her falling directly on her pregnant abdomen. **Jhogo's whip** trips the fleeing man, who is taken by Votyris's guards.
6. **VERIFIED/CHARACTER REPORT:** Jorah says Illyrio's letter warned that Robert offered lordships for the deaths of Dany, Viserys and her unborn son. He did **not** know the cask was poisoned until the man's refusal; the spilled liquid and evasive reaction make poisoning highly convincing, but **its composition is not chemically identified**.
7. **VERIFIED:** Frightened for Rhaego, Dany places the **black-and-scarlet egg**, then the other two, among a brazier's burning coals. She waits until the fire dies. **Nothing visibly hatches or cracks** in this experiment, and she concludes they are still dead stone.
8. **VERIFIED:** Drogo returns from hunting a white `hrakkar` lion, with scratches to his leg. Learning of the attack, he rewards Jorah and Jhogo each with a horse, then **publicly pledges to take his khalasar west, cross the sea by ship and conquer Westeros for Rhaego**. The khalasar rides southwest two days later; the captured wine seller is forced to follow Dany's silver mare in chains.

## Theory checks / rival readings

**Illyrio/Jorah double game:** the courier letter and sudden warning are important, but there are competing readings: Jorah genuinely protects Dany while serving interests connected to pardon, or his role in information transmission is more conflicted. **Eggs only need fire?** This experiment is evidence **against** simple ordinary flame sufficing by itself. **Drogo prophecy made invasion inevitable?** No: he resists the crossing until the attempted assassination changes his choice; character and politics, not automatic magic, connect the scenes.
