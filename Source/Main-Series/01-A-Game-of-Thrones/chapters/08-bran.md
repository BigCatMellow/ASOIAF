## Causal spine

1. **VERIFIED:** On the last day before royal departure, Bran anticipates a future as a knight of the Kingsguard but mourns leaving Winterfell. His fantasy praises exemplary knights and overlooks the gap between ideals and the imperfect Ser Boros, Ser Meryn and Jaime.
2. **VERIFIED:** Unable to say his goodbyes, Bran climbs toward the broken tower. His **still-unnamed direwolf howls repeatedly as he climbs**. Bran treats this as annoyance, not a comprehended warning.
3. **VERIFIED:** Bran's climbing explains the castle's physical irregularities and routes, including a traversable interior stretch of wall between gates and a covered bridge between bell tower and rookery. He accesses disused upper structures by roof, First Keep gargoyles and broken tower.
4. **VERIFIED:** He overhears a man and woman privately arguing about Ned as Hand, Lysa Arryn's knowledge, Robert, Joffrey's succession and their own risk. He sees them together and recognizes Cersei and Jaime.
5. **VERIFIED:** Bran nearly falls; Jaime physically pulls him onto the sill, then intentionally pushes him to protect their secret. Bran falls toward the courtyard. **UNKNOWN within this chapter:** whether he survives or retains the conversation.

## Physical clue inventory: castle and fall

|---|---|
| Castle built on uneven ground | Bran describes ancient walls, sloping floors, hidden passages, disused towers. Strong **architectural** evidence of overlooked spaces; **no underground city is witnessed** |

| Direwolf's successive howls | On-page behavior suggests danger before the fall. Inference of supernatural foresight remains unconfirmed; protective animal alarm is also possible |

## Knowledge asymmetry

**Bran:** knows he witnessed forbidden intimacy and political conversation but is too young to interpret all implications. **Reader:** can recognize a Lannister secret and attempted murder. **Jaime and Cersei:** know Bran has seen them; **do not** know whether he will wake or what he will remember. **Ned/Robert:** not shown learning the secret.
