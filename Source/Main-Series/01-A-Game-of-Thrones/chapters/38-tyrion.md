## Causal sequence

1. **VERIFIED:** Tyrion is imprisoned in an Eyrie **sky cell** with three stone walls and an open drop of roughly six hundred feet. The floor inclines toward the open side; freezing wind, hunger and sleep deprivation are weapons. Jailor **Mord** repeatedly withholds food, beats him and steals his shadowskin cloak.
2. **VERIFIED:** Tyrion recalls his arrival at Lysa's court, where she publicly alleges he murdered **Jon Arryn**, whereas Catelyn's own charge includes the attempted murder of Bran. Robert Arryn wants him thrown through the Moon Door; Catelyn insists her prisoner be kept alive. A sarcastic outburst lands him in the sky cell.
3. **VERIFIED AS TYRION'S PRIVATE THOUGHT:** He wonders whether Jaime or Cersei paid Bran's attacker, but notices how conspicuously clumsy a distinctive stolen knife would be and suspects an additional actor is using him. **This is inference, not confession or proof of the attack's source.**
4. **VERIFIED:** Tyrion bribes Mord with the promise of gold and a signed note to arrange a public 'confession'. His calculated performance lists true embarrassments but **explicitly denies** the killings of Jon Arryn and Bran.
5. **VERIFIED:** Facing Lysa and many Vale nobles, Tyrion demands an open trial under the king's law rather than secret imprisonment; when Lysa accepts and opens the Moon Door, he instead chooses **trial by combat**.
6. **VERIFIED:** He asks to summon Jaime as champion, but Lysa refuses a delay, commands combat the next day, and chooses Ser Vardis Egen despite the knight's visible reluctance to fight a smaller disabled opponent.
7. **VERIFIED:** Tyrion invokes the right to name his own champion; the nobles mock him until **Bronn volunteers**. The chapter ends with that pledge, *not* with a verdict.

## Theory challenge

**Trial by combat proves innocence:** it determines *social/legal result* by violence, not historical truth. **Dagger trail:** Tyrion's internal thoughts strongly disfavor his role in the attack but cannot identify which third party used the blade. **Sky-cell madness and strange blue calling:** psychological and environmental coercion are sufficient; don't assume supernatural calling without other evidence.
