## Main causal sequence

1. **VERIFIED:** Syrio tests Arya by saying one attack direction while striking another; she mistakes his words for his actual movement. He explains his hiring by the Sealord: unlike rival bravos who accepted a fanciful description, he recognized an ordinary fat **male cat** despite the Sealord describing a female exotic animal. The lesson is *observation before interpretation*.
2. **VERIFIED:** **Ser Meryn Trant and five Lannister guardsmen** enter and demand Arya, claiming Ned has sent them. Syrio asks why Ned would send Lannister rather than Stark men, revealing the pretext. Arya recognizes it and refuses.
3. **VERIFIED:** Syrio uses a wooden training sword to disable multiple men by targeting hands, legs, face and exposed joints; Ser Meryn orders him killed. He tells Arya to flee while the knight advances in full armor with steel. The last action **Arya observes** is Trant cutting Syrio's lead-cored stick in two. **UNKNOWN:** Syrio's eventual life/death/escape; the chapter does not narrate his death.
4. **VERIFIED:** Arya escapes via kitchens, pantries and an unusual vaulted cellar, using Syrio's route planning and training. She sees a Stark guard dead outside the Tower of the Hand and hears fighting within.
5. **VERIFIED:** In the stables she finds Hullen dying, three more guards and a groom dead, and **Desmond** lying dead near a red-cloaked Lannister. The household's baggage wagon was attacked while loading for the ship. Hullen attempts to warn her father.
6. **VERIFIED:** Arya recovers **Needle from her fallen clothes chest**. A stableboy identifies her as the wolf girl and says the queen will reward him; he tries to seize her. She thrusts Needle through him, fatally, and experiences terror and horror at the death she has caused. The boy's exact master or reward orders are **not observed**.
7. **VERIFIED:** Arya realizes castle gates will be guarded, remembers the **dragon-skull crypt and underground sewer route from AGOT 32**, and uses the sept's candles to travel back toward that passage. A sensory trigger recalls Robb and Jon scaring the children as a fake walking king in Winterfell crypts. She continues down a dark passage; the chapter ends **before actual emergence outside**.
