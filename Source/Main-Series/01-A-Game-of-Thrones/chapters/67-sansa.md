## Plot and agency

1. **VERIFIED:** Sansa hides inside Maegor's Holdfast, refusing food, gripped by grief and nightmares about Ned's execution. She sometimes wishes to die; she remains a **child hostage** subject to guards, attendants and Joffrey's orders.
2. **VERIFIED:** Joffrey enters with Sandor and demands she bathe, dress and attend court as his betrothed. He explicitly says the marriage will continue. She reminds him she wrote the coerced letters and believed in promised mercy; Joffrey asserts **a “clean death” was his version of mercy**. This contradicts Sansa's reasonable expectation of saving her father.
3. **VERIFIED:** Joffrey delegates physical punishment to **Ser Meryn Trant** on the rationale that a king should not personally strike his prospective wife. Meryn hits Sansa without observable hatred or affection, demonstrating a system of obedient violence.
4. **VERIFIED:** Joffrey's public rulings include taking a thief's hand, ordering land disputants to fight to the death, and threatening a tavern singer for a satirical song about Robert. **Janos Slynt** sits approvingly as newly elevated lord/council figure.
5. **VERIFIED:** Joffrey takes Sansa onto the castle battlements and forces her to look at **Ned's tarred severed head** mounted on an iron spike. **Septa Mordane's head** is also displayed, revealing her fate directly. Two empty spikes are reserved, Joffrey claims, for **Stannis and Renly**.
6. **VERIFIED:** When Sansa suggests Robb may give her Joffrey's head, Joffrey orders **Meryn to strike her twice**; her lip splits and bleeds. He also threatens execution if her future child does not meet his expectations, and pressures a child over future pregnancy.
7. **VERIFIED:** Sansa contemplates **pushing Joffrey from the inner edge of the wall**, a lethal fall of about 70–80 feet. **Sandor Clegane kneels between them and gently wipes her blood**, interrupting the moment. **UNKNOWN:** whether Sandor intentionally prevents murder or merely offers care; Sansa does not act.
