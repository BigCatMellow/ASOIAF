## Causal spine

1. **VERIFIED:** Tyrion finishes a night reading in Winterfell's library while Bran lies unconscious. He notices Sandor offering to silence the howling wolf and Joffrey delighting in the possibility.
2. **VERIFIED:** Tyrion orders Joffrey to make a formal compassionate visit to the Starks. When the prince resists, Tyrion strikes him twice. This protects proper conduct **through humiliation and violence** rather than transforming Joffrey's character.
3. **VERIFIED:** At breakfast with Cersei, Jaime, Tommen and Myrcella, Tyrion reports that Bran might survive. He notices the twins exchange a swift uneasy glance and probes further.
4. **VERIFIED/REPORTED MECHANIC:** Tyrion tells them that Bran's direwolf stays at the window, and a maester observed Bran weaken when the window was shut, strengthen after reopening. Tyrion repeats another person's observation; **cause of physiological improvement is unproved**.
5. **VERIFIED:** Cersei wants the wolves excluded from her southern progress. Jaime speaks as though death would be preferable to Bran's permanent disabilities, while Tyrion defends the possibilities of life. Tyrion chooses to visit the Wall with Benjen and Jon.

## Know / infer / test

**Reader can cross-confirm:** Jaime pushed Bran (`AGOT 8`); their anxiety has an obvious cause. **Tyrion can only infer:** the twins react suspiciously to Bran's chance of recovery. His comments display curiosity and family antagonism but **do not prove** he has worked out or disclosed the sexual secret.

**Wolf-human bond:** striking coincidence between Bran's vital signs and open window; plausible skinchanger precursor; require `AGOT 17 (Bran)` and later magical first-person evidence before assigning a mechanism. Compare `ADWD Prologue` without retroactively declaring the maester certain.
