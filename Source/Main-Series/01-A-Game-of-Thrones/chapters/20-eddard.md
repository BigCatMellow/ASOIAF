## Plot and causal choices

1. **VERIFIED:** Ned arrives exhausted at King's Landing. The small council summons him immediately; Robert is not present. Stannis has already withdrawn to Dragonstone, Barristan is still traveling with Robert.
2. **VERIFIED:** Robert's urgent written order is to organize a tourney celebrating Ned's appointment. Its prizes total **90,000 gold dragons** before feasts and expenses.
3. **CHARACTER ACCOUNT:** As master of coin, Littlefinger says the crown owes **over six million dragons**: around three million to Tywin, plus obligations to the Tyrells, Iron Bank, Tyroshi cartels and the Faith. Ned learns a grave financial constraint, but has **not audited the books**.
4. **VERIFIED:** Ned intends to confront Robert over the extravagance; Renly acknowledges that the council is de facto governing for an inattentive king.
5. **VERIFIED:** After the meeting Littlefinger leads Ned by an obscure exit, down carved niches in the Red Keep bluff and to a city brothel that Petyr owns. Ned initially threatens Littlefinger before Rodrik confirms Catelyn is inside.
6. **VERIFIED:** Catelyn tells Ned of the assassination attempt and repeats Littlefinger's dagger story. **INFERRED BY THEM:** Tyrion probably did not act alone; **UNKNOWN:** whether Littlefinger's claim is accurate.
7. **VERIFIED:** Ned orders Catelyn home and plans discrete northern defenses: Tallhart and Glover are to send one hundred bowmen each to fortify Moat Cailin, Manderly to strengthen White Harbor, and Theon to be watched as potential leverage over Balon's fleet. He seeks proof of Jon Arryn's murder before approaching Robert.
