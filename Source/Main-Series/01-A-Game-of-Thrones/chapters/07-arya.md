## Causal spine

1. **VERIFIED:** Arya fails at fine needlework while Sansa excels. Septa Mordane openly praises Princess Myrcella for similar work and chastises Arya, whose humiliation drives her out of the lesson. Court rank changes what an adult will call successful.
2. **VERIFIED:** Sansa and Jeyne Poole excitedly discuss Joffrey as Sansa's intended match. Arya publicly insists Jon is their brother while Sansa corrects her to *half brother*. Her indignation exposes her own identification with a marginalized sibling.
3. **VERIFIED:** Arya takes Nymeria to watch weapons practice with Jon and Ghost from the covered bridge. Bran defeats Tommen with padded swords; Robb accepts Joffrey's challenge to use real steel, but Rodrik forbids it. Sandor Clegane backs Joffrey's provocation; Joffrey withdraws amid public insults and mockery.
4. **VERIFIED:** Jon explains the contradictory rules: Arya's gender bars her from sword training, while his bastardy bars him from bruising a prince. The exchange shapes Arya's sense of who receives permission to wield authority.
5. **VERIFIED:** The confrontation ends with Theon restraining Robb. Arya returns reluctantly to face her mother and septa; the consequences of this confrontation are not narrated here.

## POV knowledge and important counterreadings

**Arya knows:** Joffrey provokes Robb, while grown men enforce gender, bastardy and dynastic privileges. **She does not know:** the royal children's actual paternity or the Lannisters' private plots. **INFERRED:** Jon's solidarity provides Arya a model for resisting imposed roles. **Counterweight:** he also tells her she is too slight to wield a longsword; solidarity does not mean he has escaped his culture's norms.
