## Major sequence and information control

1. **VERIFIED:** On the **third day** of Sansa's confinement, Ser Boros Blount retrieves her from Maegor's Holdfast. Her captivity began when **Ser Arys Oakheart** escorted her there after she had told Cersei Ned's secret plan to send the daughters away. Sansa directly recalls her own disobedient meeting with the queen in order to preserve her Joffrey betrothal; this is more than guesswork about a possible leak.
2. **VERIFIED:** From her locked room she heard killing in the Red Keep; **Jeyne Poole** was brought in bruised and terrified, reporting the Hound broke down her door and bodies filled the Tower of the Hand stair. Sansa and Jeyne comforted each other but received no accurate information for two days.
3. **VERIFIED:** Cersei summons Sansa in front of **Pycelle, Varys and Petyr Baelish**, with Mandon Moore and Boros in Kingsguard attendance. She directs **Petyr** to take Jeyne away and keep her outside the city; Boros orders the transfer, assuring Jeyne she may see Vayon Poole. Sansa never sees Jeyne again in this chapter. **UNKNOWN:** Jeyne's true destination, father's survival and exact subsequent treatment at the handoff.
4. **VERIFIED:** Cersei shows Sansa a damaged letter in Ned's hand/seal addressed to **Stannis**, describing the king's succession and inviting him to take the crown. **This proves Cersei possesses a Stark-sealed letter**, not that Stannis received it. The queen and council call Ned a traitor, presenting one political narrative.
5. **VERIFIED:** Sansa recalls going to Cersei explicitly to **disclose the girls' planned departure** after Ned refused her goodbye to Joffrey. Cersei says Sansa is innocent but manipulates her desire to marry Joffrey and fear of punishment to demand family compliance.
6. **VERIFIED:** Under direction from the queen and councillors, Sansa writes **four letters** to **Catelyn, Robb, Lysa and Hoster**. They urge allegiance to the new king and state she is treated well and expects marriage. **Varys uses Ned's direwolf seal** in white beeswax to authenticate these politically coerced communications.
7. **VERIFIED:** Jeyne and possessions have vanished from Sansa's room when she returns. **Sansa realizes only at bedtime that she forgot to ask about Arya**, demonstrating a real information gap. **UNKNOWN in this chapter:** whether all four letters are dispatched or received (one arrives in `AGOT 53`).

## Rival readings

**“Sansa betrayed Ned”:** she really did disclose the secret departure plan, despite instructions, motivated by marriage hopes. **Counterweight:** she had no disclosed foreknowledge of the murderous coup or her future imprisonment, was a child and is subjected to coercion thereafter. This action warrants analysis without falsely making her a co-conspirator. **Jeyne's fate:** custody moves from Lannister imprisonment to Petyr's household by Cersei's express decision; no independent proof Jeyne sees her father again.
