## Core narrative

1. **VERIFIED:** Alliser Thorne passes **eight** recruits—Toad, Stone Head, Aurochs, Lover, Pimple, Monkey, Ser Loon and Jon—to Mormont for permanent Night's Watch assignment. **Sam is omitted**, though Jon has promised to protect him.
2. **VERIFIED:** At a celebratory dinner of better food, Jon recognizes Sam is absent. Pyp tries to reassure him, but Jon sees that their reassignment and departures to different castles/orders will leave Sam exposed to new recruits and Thorne's abuse.
3. **VERIFIED:** Jon rides south by moonlight with Ghost and considers continuing away from Castle Black. He has **not sworn vows yet** and could leave freely. He longs for the world and his siblings but anticipates Catelyn's rejection; he *chooses to turn back* before oath, rather than being trapped by it.
4. **VERIFIED:** Jon visits Maester Aemon at night despite **Chett's** resistance. He admits coercing Rast with Ghost and makes the case that leaving Sam in arms training will eventually kill him.
5. **VERIFIED:** Jon uses Maester Luwin's explanation of the multimetal maester chain to argue that a realm—and the Watch—needs different talents. He insists Sam has reading, writing, numeracy, animal-care aptitude and can aid the half-blind steward **Clydas** and blind Aemon with documents/ravens.
6. **VERIFIED:** Chett sharply defends the expertise and burden of the stewards, demonstrating that even those outside combat have pride and real institutional duties. Aemon considers Jon's argument but gives **no final decision in this chapter**.

## Agency and rival readings

**Central decision:** Jon returns from the open road *without the binding vow* because he has found a purpose and a vulnerable person who needs help. This is agency rather than inexorable destiny. **Counterpoint:** social exclusion and fear of Catelyn limit his felt options even before the legal vow.

**Not just prophecy:** Aemon's remark, Jon's recruitment success and Sam's literacy are durable causal seeds for future information flow and leadership. **Possible rival:** Jon is arguing out of friendship rather than calibrated labor needs, but his placement suggestion names concrete jobs and real shortages.
