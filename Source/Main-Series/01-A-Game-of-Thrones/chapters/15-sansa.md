## Essential causal sequence

1. **VERIFIED:** Sansa looks forward to the queen's invitation to ride in the wheelhouse, unlike Arya, who is spending time with the butcher's boy Mycah. The sisters disagree about highborn expectations and how to experience the world.
2. **VERIFIED:** Arya and Mycah plan to look for rubies from Rhaegar's death at the Trident. Arya has made friends outside the nobility and explored plants, animals and ruins during the journey.
3. **VERIFIED:** At the royal convoy Sansa meets Barristan Selmy and Renly Baratheon, but is frightened by silent headsman Ilyn Payne. Joffrey comforts her before the court; Cersei leaves the betrothed pair together.
4. **VERIFIED:** Joffrey, carrying an edged sword called Lion's Tooth, rides with Sansa, leaving behind Sandor and Lady. He requests wine from a holdfast and keeps refilling Sansa's cup. Afterward they encounter Arya and Mycah practicing with harmless wooden sticks.
5. **VERIFIED:** Joffrey puts a live sword against unarmed Mycah's face and cuts him. Arya intervenes with her stick; Joffrey turns on Arya with steel. Nymeria bites his sword arm, then stops at Arya's command. Arya throws Lion's Tooth into the river and flees with the wolf.
6. **VERIFIED:** Sansa, caught between her sister and betrothed, wants the violence to cease but cannot stop it. When she tries to comfort Joffrey afterward, he speaks to her with contempt. No judgment of who is 'to blame' should erase what Sansa actually witnessed.

## Agency and competing explanations

**Sansa knows:** Joffrey threatened Mycah with an edged sword, Arya struck him, and Nymeria intervened when he pursued Arya; she also experienced Joffrey's sudden contempt. **Arya knows:** Mycah had been practicing with her by consent. **Joffrey later has incentive:** conceal humiliation, recast his aggression as self-defense.

**INFERRED:** Sansa's romantic stories make her overvalue public chivalry and discount contradictions. **Counterweight:** her fear of Ilyn, efforts to warn against violence and knowledgeable identification of knights are genuine perceptions; she is a politically inexperienced child, not a fully informed conspirator.
