## Full event, decision and consequence chain

1. **VERIFIED:** Jon's right hand is badly **burned and bandaged** after setting fire to Othor. Mormont has survived, with scorched beard and partially burned tower, but Jon suffers pain, nightmares and uncertainty about family. **Dywen and Hake** return from another failed search for Benjen.
2. **VERIFIED:** Mormont explains **both dead rangers became active wights**. **Othor** was consumed by Jon's fire, leaving cooked bone; **Jafer Flowers** also rose and was destroyed by Watch swords, but first **killed Ser Jaremy Rykker and four other men**. This is *direct later-reported aftermath* of the initial Othor attack, not independent proof of who animated them.
3. **VERIFIED:** Mormont gifts Jon **Longclaw**, a Valyrian-steel bastard sword that belonged to House Mormont for five centuries. When **Jorah** fled justice, he left the family sword behind; **Maege Mormont** returned it to her brother Jeor. The Castle Black fire burned its grip and silver fittings but **not the blade**.
4. **VERIFIED:** Longclaw now has a **wolf-head white stone pommel with garnet eyes**, commissioned for Jon in place of the former bear-head design. The lead weighting and fresh leather/scabbard are specific modifications. Jon accepts with gratitude mixed with shame that Mormont is not the father whose sword he once dreamed of earning.
5. **VERIFIED:** Jon learns **Alliser Thorne** has been sent to Eastwatch to sail to King's Landing with **Jafer's severed hand** as physical proof. **Ser Endrew Tarth** is coming from Shadow Tower to replace him temporarily as master-at-arms. **UNKNOWN:** successful sailing, delivery to court, and what anyone in the capital will believe.
6. **VERIFIED:** Jon's friends have contributed to Longclaw's reworking: **Halder** helped Pate carve the pommel, **Sam** bought garnets in Mole's Town, and **Rudge** worked around Donal Noye's forge. Practical cooperation makes the gift a communal acknowledgment, not just a secret Mormont reward.
7. **VERIFIED:** **Sam reports to Aemon** Jon's anguish over Ned and Robb. Aemon summons Jon to the rookery, where he describes raven biology/communication while Jon feeds meat. He teaches that family love can conflict with the Watch's oath, citing prior Lord Commanders who refused to intervene in dynastic conflicts.
8. **VERIFIED:** Aemon reveals he was born **Aemon Targaryen** and chose vows over family/royal claims, undergoing this test three times. He **does not command Jon to remain or leave**, but insists Jon personally bear the choice and consequences.
