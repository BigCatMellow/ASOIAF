## Causal spine

1. **VERIFIED:** At the Bloody Gate, Ser Donnel Waynwood rescues the party. Six travelers have died on the high road across two clan attacks and fever; Rodrik is badly injured and must be tied onto his horse. Bronn has drawn close to Tyrion while the singer remains a precarious eyewitness.
2. **VERIFIED:** Brynden Tully (the Blackfish), Catelyn's maternal uncle, meets her at the Bloody Gate. He warns that her publicly abducting a Lannister makes Riverrun dangerously exposed to retaliation between Winterfell and the Vale. He says Lysa has ordered knights to remain close instead of fighting mountain clans or attending the tourney.
3. **CHARACTER REPORT:** Many Vale lords resent Jaime's Warden of the East title, suspect Arryn's death and disagree over who should exercise authority while six-year-old Robert Arryn is sick. Brynden thinks Lysa rejects suitors because she wants to rule herself, and warns Catelyn not to expect support.
4. **VERIFIED:** Tyrion is still technically a prisoner but now carries an axe and dirk and has influence over Bronn. Brynden challenges whether someone armed and guarded by loyal companions can be treated like a fully constrained captive.
5. **VERIFIED:** They reach Nestor Royce's Gates of the Moon. Lysa orders Catelyn to ascend the Eyrie immediately at night while other travelers, including Tyrion, are lodged below. Mya Stone, a skilled bastard-born mountaineer, guides Catelyn on fresh mules past fortified waycastles **Stone, Snow, Sky**.
6. **VERIFIED:** At the high saddle Catelyn freezes with vertigo; Mya returns and guides her step by step. Catelyn takes the food-supply basket raised by the Eyrie's winches for the final ascent. High altitude, narrow paths and vertical supply lines are real military/geographic constraints.
7. **VERIFIED:** At the Eyrie she meets **Maester Colemon**, whom Pycelle had replaced while treating Jon Arryn; sickly Robert is closely attached to Lysa. Lysa angrily tells Catelyn the secret accusation was a **warning to keep away**, not a call to war. She interprets Jon's phrase about strong seed as proof of her own son's strength.
8. **VERIFIED:** Six-year-old Robert Arryn urges his mother to make Tyrion 'fly'; Lysa entertains the idea. **UNKNOWN:** actual judicial process, whether Tyrion will be executed, or whether Lysa's initial accusations reflect reliable knowledge.
