## Major events and decisions

1. **VERIFIED:** Tyrion and Bronn bring nearly **three hundred fighters** from the **Black Ears, Stone Crows, Moon Brothers and Burned Men** down toward a red-and-gold Lannister host. Chella has scouted about **twenty thousand**, the count appearing broadly credible when Tyrion sees the actual camp.
2. **VERIFIED:** Clan representatives **Chella daughter of Cheyk, Shagga son of Dolf, Conn, Ulf son of Umar and Timett son of Timett** insist on personal access to Tyrion and consideration in council. The Burned Men's reputation includes ritual self-mutilation; Timett has put out one of his own eyes. Reports that they eat babies are **clan rumor**, not witnessed fact.
3. **VERIFIED:** They pass lowland burned farmsteads and defensive barricades. The crossroads inn—site of Catelyn's earlier arrest—is now **Tywin's headquarters**; nearby village buildings are ruins and a dead man hangs at a gibbet. Tyrion sees material consequences of war.
4. **VERIFIED:** Tywin and **Kevan** greet Tyrion coldly rather than with familial relief. Tywin says the insult to Lannister honor demanded military response regardless of whether Tyrion himself bled. **Kevan's battlefield report:** Jaime has defeated Vance/Piper and the Tullys near Riverrun, **Edmure is captured**, Blackwood and others confined, Lady Whent yielded Harrenhal, and various river houses have suffered raids.
5. **VERIFIED:** Tywin says Ned is held in King's Landing and **Robert is dead; Joffrey reigns**. This is new information to Tyrion, who thinks political control belongs to Cersei. Tywin offers him a minor command against Beric Dondarrion's disruptive bands or Riverlands raiders; Tyrion resists being treated as expendable.
6. **VERIFIED:** Mountain-clan representatives burst into Tywin's meeting, demanding fulfillment of Tyrion's promised **iron, mail and silk**. Tyrion introduces them as free fighters, and Tywin controls Kevan's impulse to take offense. They are impressed by the huge Lannister force but do not abandon their claims.
7. **VERIFIED AS MESSENGER REPORT:** A rider brings news that **Robb Stark's force has come south**. Kevan advises staying inside strong river defenses; Tywin prefers to march and break the boy before dealing with Stannis.
8. **VERIFIED:** Tywin offers the clans what Tyrion promised, plus more, if they fight his enemies. He appeals to their martial pride, and the clans **agree conditionally**, with **Chella explicitly requiring Tyrion to fight beside them until their payment arrives**. This binds Tyrion's safety to continued delivery, rather than giving Tywin free ownership of the force.
