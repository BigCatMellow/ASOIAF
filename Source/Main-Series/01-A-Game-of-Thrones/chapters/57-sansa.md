## Chapter-level causal record

1. **VERIFIED:** At the first formal court of Joffrey's reign, Sansa remains a **restricted hostage** with “freedom of the castle” but no right to leave the Red Keep. Few former friends acknowledge her; Robert's preferred hunting tapestries have been removed. She places enormous hope in Joffrey's smile and a possible mercy petition.
2. **VERIFIED:** Pycelle reads a **summons of alleged traitors** to present and swear to Joffrey, naming **Stannis, Renly, major Tyrells/Royces, Catelyn, Robb, Bran, Rickon and Arya** among others. **Sansa notices Arya named**, even though the court has not found her; official accusation does not establish custody.
3. **VERIFIED:** The council removes Ned and Stannis from their posts and replaces them with loyal appointees. **Janos Slynt** is elevated from common-born City Watch commander to **lord of Harrenhal**, with income and recognition. This directly follows the City Watch coup in `AGOT 49`, demonstrating immediate political payment.
4. **VERIFIED:** Cersei strips **Ser Barristan Selmy** of Kingsguard leadership and service in violation of his understanding that vows endure for life. She offers land, money and servants in the west as compensation. He rejects the offer, discards white cloak, helm, armor and sword in court, and leaves furious.
5. **VERIFIED:** Cersei names **Jaime** new Lord Commander; **Sandor Clegane** receives the vacated white cloak even while saying he **will not swear knightly vows**. The monarchy changes an institution's selection rule openly instead of pretending all traditions remain unchanged.
6. **VERIFIED:** Joffrey orders Barristan seized after his departure, suspecting plotting, but **the chapter does not show Barristan caught**.
7. **VERIFIED:** Sansa publicly kneels on Barristan's discarded cloak and petitions for Ned's life. Cersei, Littlefinger and Pycelle challenge her, and Sansa offers the narcotic effects of milk of the poppy as an explanation for his claim that Joffrey is illegitimate. She does not renounce her father as malicious; she tries to save him using claims the court might accept.
8. **VERIFIED:** Cersei says confession would prove repentance; **Joffrey promises mercy conditionally**, if Ned confesses and recognizes his kingship. **No confession, judgment or pardon is actually performed in this chapter.**

## Competing interpretations

**Barristan “betrayed” Joffrey:** he rejected dismissal from an office he considered a lifetime oath; no direct evidence of a coup is narrated here. **Cersei/Joffrey fully control justice:** court can reward Slynt and humiliate Selmy, but cannot compel absent Robb, Stannis, Renly, Arya or Barristan merely by proclaiming their names. **Sansa colluding in father's fate:** she is actively seeking mercy under captivity and threat, but her limited information and continued trust in Joffrey create vulnerability.
