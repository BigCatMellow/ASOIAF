## Causal structure

1. **VERIFIED:** Robb, Catelyn and leading bannermen **enter liberated Riverrun by water** along the Tumblestone and through its **Water Gate**. **Edmure** embraces Catelyn and reports Hoster is near death. Robb's river assault has succeeded, but Tywin remains dangerous.
2. **VERIFIED:** **Hoster Tully** is severely emaciated and in constant belly pain (he calls it “crabs”). He asks for **Lysa** and **Brynden**; the latter returned from the Eyrie while Lysa remains isolated. Hoster revisits his attempt to force Brynden to marry **Bethany Redwyne**, who has since married **Lord Rowan**, and complains about other offers, including Frey/Bracken matches.
3. **VERIFIED:** Hoster withheld the severity of his illness so Lannisters could not exploit it; his correspondence was restricted by fear that **riders are questioned and ravens shot down**. This is a practical reason for incomplete family information, not proof his children ignored him deliberately.
4. **VERIFIED:** In Riverrun's godswood Robb prays before a **weirwood**; Catelyn remembers childhood with Lysa and Petyr in this same place, linking older personal political debts to the new alliance.
5. **VERIFIED/NEWS RECEIVED:** **Renly has declared himself king** with Reach/Stormlands support. Roose's broken host has regrouped near the causeway and Tywin's field army heads toward **Harrenhal**. Riverrun lords debate marching on Tywin, raiding the West, negotiating or joining a claimant.
6. **VERIFIED:** Robb reasons that **Stannis outranks Renly** under ordinary inheritance but also voices a critical **knowledge gap**: he treats Joffrey as Robert's legitimate eldest son because **he does not know Ned's discovery of incest**, although the reader does. He cannot choose a universally accepted king simply by deductive law.
7. **VERIFIED:** Catelyn advocates a **negotiated peace** to retrieve the remaining living Starks despite Ned's execution. Brynden asks what terms can be enforced; Karstark and Riverlords cite murdered kin and devastated communities. Negotiation is an actual proposed choice, rejected in volatile council.
8. **VERIFIED:** **Greatjon Umber** proposes independence rather than either Baratheon: the northern lords followed Starks before Aegon's conquest. He kneels to Robb, followed by **Rickard Karstark, Maege Mormont, Blackwood, Bracken, Mallister** and other northern/river lords, acclaiming **Robb as King in the North**. The collective action changes political obligations and defines a new strategic objective.
