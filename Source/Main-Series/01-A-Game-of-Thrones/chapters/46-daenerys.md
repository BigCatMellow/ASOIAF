## Major narrative and irrevocable decisions

1. **VERIFIED:** In Vaes Dothrak, pregnant Daenerys successfully eats an entire raw stallion's heart in a sacred ceremony after two months of preparation. Dothraki participants interpret completing it without retching as a favorable sign for a strong male child. **This is a ritual belief**, not reliable proof of the child's eventual fate.
2. **VERIFIED AS PROPHECY SPOKEN BY DOSH KHALEEN:** A one-eyed elder announces that Dany's son will become the **stallion who mounts the world**, a khal of khals commanding an innumerable host. Dany names the unborn child **Rhaego** for her dead brother Rhaegar; chants and acclaim follow. *A proclamation is verified; its future fulfillment is not.*
3. **VERIFIED:** Dany cleanses herself in the **Womb of the World**, a lake whose first horse/first man story is Dothraki religious tradition. The feast features **Drogo, Jommo and Ogo**, with their bloodriders and women separated by rank. Dany searches for absent Viserys.
4. **JORAH'S ACCOUNT:** Viserys has been seeking wine at the Western Market and trying to recruit sell-sword caravan guards for an army without money. Jorah warns that Viserys had planned to **take and sell Dany's three dragon eggs**, which have extremely high material value. Dany is willing to give him the eggs, illustrating continued familial attachment despite growing separation.
5. **VERIFIED:** An intoxicated Viserys enters the sacred hall **wearing and then drawing a steel sword**, breaking its no-blades rule. Drogo publicly mocks him using Common Tongue nicknames; Jorah tries to prevent escalation.
6. **VERIFIED:** Viserys advances upon Dany and presses the blade against her pregnant belly, demanding his crown and threatening Dany and her unborn child. Dany offers eggs and urges him to sheathe the weapon. Viserys refuses.
7. **VERIFIED:** Drogo's bloodriders restrain and disarm Viserys. Drogo melts the gold medallions of his belt in an iron stew pot and pours the molten metal over Viserys's head, killing him. The violence is engineered to shed **no blood** in the sacred city.
8. **VERIFIED AS DAENERYS'S THOUGHT:** Dany concludes Viserys was not a true dragon because fire killed him. **Her inference is not objective proof that Targaryens or dragons cannot be harmed by heat or molten gold.**

## Theory tests

**Rhaego as prophetic ruler:** the `dosh khaleen` directly predict worldwide mounted conquest, but a future claimant or fulfillment remains unknown. **Dragonfire resistance:** Dany's own conclusion is a social/mystical interpretation; the scene establishes one Targaryen dies from molten metal. **Dothraki law protects lives:** Viserys's death is powerful counterexample: prohibition on blades/blood does not bar deadly loopholes or enslaved people's exploitation.

**Narrative hinge:** The man who claimed the sole dynastic right to lead the invasion is dead; Daenerys's child and her rising standing among Dothraki now alter possible leadership routes. **Do not** collapse her future decisions into an automatic destiny or treat prophecy as established causality.
