# AGOT 14 (Catelyn) — attempted murder, a distinctive dagger, and a new mission

## Complete major sequence

1. **VERIFIED:** Eight days after Ned and the girls depart, Catelyn remains beside unconscious Bran. Luwin asks for decisions on spending, replenished stores and vacant household posts: steward Poole, captain Jory and stable master Hullen have all gone south. Catelyn is unable to engage.
2. **VERIFIED:** Robb, only fourteen, accepts the needed appointments but also tells Catelyn that three-year-old Rickon is distressed and needs her. Robb admits the strain of acting as head of the household. Their family crisis becomes an **administrative and emotional crisis**.
3. **VERIFIED:** The wolves howl; the castle dogs begin barking. Robb sees the library tower ablaze and leaves with the guards. An unknown man then enters Bran's room carrying a dagger, says no one was supposed to be there, and attempts to kill the boy. Catelyn fights the assassin and is seriously cut across both hands.
4. **VERIFIED:** Bran's previously unnamed direwolf enters and kills the attacker, saving Catelyn and Bran. The wolf settles alongside Bran on the bed. After medical treatment Catelyn sleeps for four days.
5. **VERIFIED:** On awakening, Catelyn resumes governing. Robb has installed Hallis Mollen as captain. The attacker has ninety silver stags hidden in the stable and a costly **Valyrian-steel dagger with dragonbone hilt**, recovered by Rodrik. Someone must have furnished the means, but no sponsor is established.
6. **INFERRED by Catelyn:** The library fire deliberately diverted guards and family from the sickroom; the man targeted Bran because he might awaken with dangerous information. Catelyn tells Robb, Rodrik, Luwin and Theon of Lysa's allegation against the Lannisters and her suspicion Jaime shoved Bran.
7. **VERIFIED:** Luwin warns there is conjecture rather than proof; Rodrik says the unusual dagger may be traceable. Catelyn resolves to travel secretly with Rodrik by the White Knife and White Harbor rather than lead a visible entourage down the kingsroad. She leaves Robb as acting master of Winterfell.

## Facts separated from hypotheses

**VERIFIED:** someone with a special dagger tried to kill Bran and was stopped by the direwolf; library fire occurred simultaneously; money and a hiding place were found. **CHARACTER CLAIM:** Catelyn recounts Lysa's accusation of Lannister guilt; no Lannister orders appear on-page. **INFERRED:** the fire was planned as a lure; Jaime may have pushed Bran, an inference that the reader can separately compare with `AGOT 8`. **UNKNOWN:** who paid the killer, who supplied the blade, who set the fire, whether any surviving witness observed the planning.

## Theory challenge / next evidence test

- **Dagger proves a Lannister hired the killer?** No. Valuable weapon ownership can change, and tracing it requires an actual witness or proven custody chain. Before closing the question, examine later testimony, incentive and contradictions independently.
- **Direwolf saved Bran via greenseer direction?** Not demonstrated. Ordinary protective animal action is sufficient, although earlier sustained vigilance and subsequent magic make tracking worthwhile.
- **Winterfell was left undefended by inept Starks?** Household departures and Catelyn's breakdown create vulnerabilities, but the assassin required a deliberate distraction and still failed.
- **Major causal choice:** Catelyn abandons her vigil to protect her other children and seek justice; this does not mean her inferences are reliable.
