## Plot and consequences

1. **VERIFIED:** At the familiar **crossroads inn**, Tywin's officers hear an exhausted messenger: Jaime's prisoner status and the defeat of his field forces have been followed by the **breaking of the siege of Riverrun**. **Messenger account:** Blackfish's advance removed sentries and opened barricades; Stark/Tully columns overran multiple siege camps; **Lord Brax** drowned trying to cross by raft in heavy armor; siege engines burn and **Edmure is freed**.
2. **VERIFIED:** Officers debate ransom, prisoner exchange, immediate counterattack, truce and whether Joffrey's custody of Stark girls could purchase Jaime's freedom. Tyrion notes the arithmetic and uncertainty rather than pretending hostage bargaining guarantees release.
3. **VERIFIED:** Tywin keeps **Tyrion and Kevan** for private council and acknowledges that Ned, while alive, might have supported negotiation. **Now Ned has been executed**, removing that diplomatic leverage. The meeting thus confirms the King’s Landing death news has reached the field command.
4. **VERIFIED AS TYWIN'S NEW INTELLIGENCE:** **Renly has claimed a crown with Reach/Stormlands backing** and Cersei wants Tywin's army brought to defend the capital. **Stannis** is presently inactive in Tywin's view, which he considers more worrying than Renly's publicity.
5. **VERIFIED:** Tywin orders the army to **Harrenhal**, aims to keep Bolton's battered force in view, and **explicitly unleashes Ser Gregor, Vargo Hoat and others to ravage Riverlands** before him. This is a primary **direct order for future atrocities**, not merely Ned's earlier suspicion of Lannister complicity.
6. **VERIFIED:** In private Tywin gives Tyrion authority to go to King's Landing and **rule in his stead as acting Hand**, over Cersei/Joffrey's wishes, to restrain the child king and council. His denunciation of **Petyr, Pycelle and Varys** includes a practical recognition the court cannot be trusted.
7. **VERIFIED:** Tywin **forbids Tyrion bringing Shae** to court. Tyrion inwardly resents the order, suspects his father has already given Jaime up as lost and recognizes the chance for new power; the chapter ends with **Tyrion telling Shae he intends to take her to King's Landing anyway**.
