## Primary causal sequence

1. **VERIFIED:** Catelyn and Ser Rodrik arrive by the Tyroshi galley *Storm Dancer* after riding to White Harbor and enduring contrary winds and a storm near Dragonstone. Her damaged hands have not healed normally: the last two left-hand fingers remain stiff and hard to move.
2. **VERIFIED:** They plan to locate Ser Aron Santagar, Robert's master-at-arms, for identification of the murder weapon while avoiding the court. Rodrik, seasick and newly shaven, offers to visit the castle alone; they lodge at a quiet inn in Eel Alley.
3. **VERIFIED:** Men of the City Watch fetch Catelyn using a grey-wax mockingbird seal. **CATELYN'S INFERENCE:** Captain Moreo may have sold news of her arrival; she does not witness him do so.
4. **VERIFIED:** Littlefinger meets Catelyn privately and confirms his prior youth at Riverrun: he challenged Brandon Stark over her proposed marriage and was badly wounded. Varys arrives with detailed knowledge of Rodrik's trip to Santagar, the blade inquiry and Catelyn's lodging.
5. **VERIFIED:** Catelyn produces the Valyrian-steel dagger. Littlefinger **CLAIMS** it was once his until he lost a wager backing Jaime against Loras Tyrell at Prince Joffrey's name-day tourney; he names Tyrion Lannister as the winner. Varys watches her reaction. **UNKNOWN:** the wager's truth, current chain of ownership, who ordered Bran's murder.
