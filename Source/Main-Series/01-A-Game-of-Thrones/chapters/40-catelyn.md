Catelyn had heard **Casterly Rock** was arranged **after** Jon's death. Colemon starts to correct her before a bell interrupts them. This is a highly specific unresolved discrepancy, not proof of who killed Arryn.
5. **VERIFIED:** In the Eyrie's rooftop garden (intended as godswood but lacking a rooted weirwood), Bronn avoids repeated heavy attacks, uses reach and speed, and wears down armored Ser Vardis. Vardis is compelled to wield **Jon Arryn's ornate ceremonial sword**, chosen by Lysa instead of his familiar blade.
6. **VERIFIED:** Bronn damages Vardis's armor joints, the knight's borrowed blade snaps on a marble statue, and Bronn topples the statue over him and delivers the killing thrust. The knight dies; **Tyrion wins the combat and legal release**, not factual verification of every charge.
7. **VERIFIED:** Lysa obeys the judgment reluctantly, orders Tyrion and Bronn escorted to the Bloody Gate with supplies and arms; sending them down a clan-infested high road effectively subjects them to another mortal risk. Catelyn recognizes this.
