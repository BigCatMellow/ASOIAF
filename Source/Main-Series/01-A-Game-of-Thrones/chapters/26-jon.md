## Narrative with agency

1. **VERIFIED:** Samwell Tarly arrives with expensive unsuitable armor and is brutalized during Thorne's sword drill after yielding. Jon intervenes. Pyp and Grenn join Jon when Alliser turns the exercise into a three-on-three contest; they win.
2. **VERIFIED:** Sam admits fear rather than pretending skill. Jon seeks him out later; Ghost calms the crying Sam and permits their first serious conversation.
3. **SAM'S FIRST-PERSON TESTIMONY:** Randyll Tarly repeatedly subjected Sam to humiliating martial conditioning. At Sam's fifteenth name day, Randyll allegedly threatened to arrange his murder in a hunting 'accident' unless Sam surrendered inheritance and joined the Watch, benefiting younger brother Dickon. The coercion is Sam's direct account, not a witnessed scene through Randyll's POV.
4. **VERIFIED:** Jon confides his recurring dream of an empty Winterfell culminating at the crypt staircase, where he fears being denied a Stark place. Sam describes Horn Hill with dread, and Jon discusses other Watch recruits' contested pasts.
5. **VERIFIED:** Jon organizes recruits to stop harming Sam. When Rast refuses, Jon, Grenn, Pyp and Ghost intimidate him in his cell. From then on practice blows against Sam become harmless. Sam eventually joins the group's meals and conversation; Jon recognizes them as brothers.

## Interpretive challenge

**Jon's humane leadership:** real benefit to Sam and group. **Counterweight:** he uses coercion and an animal threat against Rast while defying his superior; solidarity and vigilantism coexist. **Sam's 'cowardice':** fear is not inability to learn or lack of moral worth, and his truth-telling offers a useful alternative to martial reputation.
