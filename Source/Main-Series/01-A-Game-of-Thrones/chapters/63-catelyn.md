## Main sequence

1. **VERIFIED:** Catelyn waits with **Hal Mollen** and her personal guard on a wooded ridge beside a stream. Robb commands roughly **six thousand mounted men** after the army split at the Twins. His battle guard includes **Torrhen and Eddard Karstark**, Patrek Mallister, Smalljon Umber, Daryn Hornwood and others.
2. **VERIFIED AS PLAN/OBSERVED EFFECT:** A small raiding force under Tully banners draws **Jaime and about three quarters of the Lannister horse** out from the Riverrun siege. The attacking cavalry enters a narrow wooded valley while Stark scouts signal by northern snow shrike calls.
3. **VERIFIED:** Maege Mormont's horn gives the eastern signal, answered by Greatjon, Freys, Mallisters and Karstarks. Concealed archers fire from trees as Stark horse attacks from several ridges. Grey Wind accompanies Robb into combat.
4. **CATELYN'S EPISTEMIC LIMIT:** From high in the trees she **hears** much of the battle rather than witnessing every clash. Her impression of silvery lights resolves into **ordinary moonlit lances**, an explicit counterexample to upgrading evocative imagery into magical fire.
5. **VERIFIED:** Robb returns alive at dawn on a replacement horse, his shield damaged and his glove stained with others' blood. **Jaime Lannister is brought before Catelyn alive**, disarmed and wounded, then held in irons rather than executed.
6. **VERIFIED/AFTER-BATTLE REPORT:** Jaime charged toward Robb and killed **Torrhen and Eddard Karstark** and **Daryn Hornwood** trying to reach him; his sword was left lodged in Eddard's neck. This creates a specific, escalating **Karstark grievance against Jaime**.
7. **VERIFIED:** Catelyn rejects Theon's call for immediate execution and agrees Jaime is more valuable alive for exchanging **Ned, Sansa, Arya and Edmure**. Theon estimates a ten-to-one Lannister loss and names near a hundred captured knights plus lords and Tywin relatives; the ratios are **Theon's battlefield claim**, not audited final counts.
8. **VERIFIED:** Catelyn insists the battle is **not** the war; Tywin's independent army remains free and the Riverrun siege still needs lifting.
