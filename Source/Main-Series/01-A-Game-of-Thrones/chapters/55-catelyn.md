## Causal spine

1. **VERIFIED:** Returning north by sea from Gulltown, Catelyn lands at **White Harbor** with Ser Brynden and meets Wyman Manderly's household; his sons **Wylis and Wendel** have joined Robb. She rides south with Wendel to **Moat Cailin**; she sends **Ser Rodrik Cassel** to Winterfell as castellan rather than bring him with the army.
2. **VERIFIED:** Moat Cailin is ruined, but its **Gatehouse, Drunkard's, and Children's Towers** command the single traversable causeway through quicksand and bog. **Children's Tower folklore** credits children of the forest with a hammer-of-waters ritual; this is *legend*, not an observed event. The defensive value is verified as **geometry**, not an omnipotent spell.
3. **VERIFIED:** Catelyn meets Robb before lords, carefully avoids undermining his public authority, and privately reprimands his decision to command a host at fifteen while recognizing that sending him home now would destroy his credibility. Robb acknowledges youthful fear.
4. **VERIFIED:** Robb's assembled host is now roughly **18,000**, larger than Luwin's earlier in-Winterfell count in `AGOT 53`. Catelyn reads **Sansa's coerced letter** addressed to Robb and identifies threats between its lines; the missing mention of Arya deepens uncertainty. A separate letter to Catelyn reportedly lies at Winterfell, **not delivered to her here**.
5. **REPORTED TO ROBB:** Jaime has beaten Vance and Piper at the Golden Tooth and pushed Edmure's scattered men toward Riverrun; Tywin brings another host through the southern Riverlands. Beric's royal-banner force reportedly suffered an ambush at the **Mummer's Ford**, with Ser Raymun Darry killed and Beric's fate uncertain. The reports are battlefield intelligence, **not independently witnessed in this Catelyn scene**.
6. **VERIFIED:** Robb rejects permanent defense at Moat Cailin because provisions are running low and an assembled feudal host cannot stay mobilized indefinitely. His **proposed two-prong offensive** sends foot south down the kingsroad toward Tywin while horse cross the **Green Fork at the Twins** to relieve Riverrun; the river then separates Tywin and Jaime.
7. **VERIFIED:** Catelyn insists Robb state his own strategic choices rather than hide behind bannermen. He initially proposes the Greatjon to command foot; she distinguishes **fearless courage** from careful battlefield judgment. Robb appoints quiet, frightening **Roose Bolton** instead, while keeping personal command of the mounted force.
8. **VERIFIED:** Catelyn declines to return to Winterfell despite the dangers, determined to reach ill father Hoster and besieged brother Edmure at Riverrun. The plan depends on a river crossing **controlled by Walder Frey**, whose past late arrivals and self-interest she recalls.
