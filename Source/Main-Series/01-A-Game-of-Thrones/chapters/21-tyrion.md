## Essential action and consequence

1. **VERIFIED:** Tyrion dines with Jeor Mormont and senior Night's Watch officers before returning south. His public exchange with Alliser Thorne humiliates the trainer, reinforcing an antagonism already present in `AGOT 19`.
2. **CHARACTER ACCOUNT:** Ser Jaremy Rykker and Alliser Thorne were forced onto the Wall after siding against Robert in the fall of King's Landing; they attribute the ultimatum to Tywin. It is significant motive context, not firsthand verification of every political bargain.
3. **VERIFIED:** Maester Aemon, blind and old, treats Tyrion with unusual respect, calling his mental stature greater than appearances suggest.
4. **VERIFIED/CHARACTER PLEA:** Privately, Mormont asks Tyrion to carry an appeal to Robert, Tywin and Jaime. He reports fewer than a thousand men spread among Castle Black, Shadow Tower and Eastwatch; only about one third are fighting men. He describes missing rangers and too few literate leaders.
5. **CHARACTER REPORT:** Mormont says Benjen was sent to find Waymar Royce, that Ned sent Gared's head back to the Watch, and that Royce and Benjen have not returned. He reports warnings from Eastwatch fisherfolk about white walkers on the shore, unusual wildlife, southern movement of mountain people, and Citadel/Aemon calculations indicating summer's end.
6. **VERIFIED:** Tyrion does not fully credit these apocalyptic reports but promises to convey the Watch's urgent material needs. On a final midnight walk atop the Wall, he meets Jon; Jon is teaching Grenn and Pyp, and asks Tyrion to help Bran on his way south. Tyrion agrees.

## Information and theory challenge

**Reader:** saw an Other in the prologue. **Mormont:** assembles disappearances, lore and secondhand warnings; he has no full eyewitness report from Will. **Tyrion:** knows the practical staffing crisis but doubts the rumored threat. **Jon:** does not have confirmation of Benjen's death.

**Interpretation:** The Watch's problem is a failed institution with a potentially real external enemy, not simply stupidity for looking north. **Counterweight:** Mormont's estimates and old winter sayings can be inaccurate even when his warning deserves attention. **Next tests:** northern settlement change, missing-ranger custody, and practical supply/personnel decisions.
