## Main action and change of state

1. **DREAM, NOT WAKING EVIDENCE:** In prolonged fever dreams, Dany flees pursuing cold toward her childhood red door, sees ancestral Targaryens, dead Viserys and Rhaegar, and imagines her son as a terrifyingly altered warrior. She experiences internal fire and wings; no witnessed waking flight or verified prior-life vision follows.
2. **VERIFIED:** Dany awakens extremely ill, disoriented and weak; **her unborn son Rhaego has died**. **MIRRI'S DESCRIPTION OF DELIVERY:** he emerged scaled, tailed, winged, decayed and filled with graveworms, allegedly dead for years. Dany and Jorah did **not** independently examine the child in this narrated POV. The death is established; the **specific anatomy is character testimony**.
3. **VERIFIED:** Jorah admits he **carried Dany into the forbidden tent** to obtain help during labor. She accuses him of bringing the unborn child within range of deadly powers. Mirri argues Dany knew death was the price, and calls the belief that the sacrificial horse alone paid a **self-protective misunderstanding**.
4. **VERIFIED:** The enormous khalasar has **disintegrated**: **Pono** and **Jhaqo** each proclaim themselves khal and take substantial followers, animals and captives, while only a few loyal fighters, servants, infirm and enslaved remain with Dany.
5. **REPORTED BY ATTENDANTS:** **Eroeh**, a girl Dany rescued from sexual assault in `AGOT 61`, was seized by **Mago** and Jhaqo's riders, raped and killed. Her story is explicit counterevidence to any claim Dany already abolished slave exploitation simply by declaring temporary personal protection.
6. **VERIFIED:** Drogo is physically alive but **unresponsive**: follows the sun with vacant eyes, eats or walks only when guided, cannot ride or speak. Mirri says Dany bought **life**, not restoration. Her answer to when he will be whole includes the sun rising west, dried seas, wind-scattered mountains and Dany conceiving and bearing a living child; **a despairing poetic condition**, not verified prophecy of future events.
7. **VERIFIED:** Mirri directly confronts Dany with the Lhazareen town's destroyed temple, murdered neighbors, enslavement and assaults. She says the allegedly saved life meant little after everything else was taken, and that preventing the prophesied conqueror from growing is a benefit. **This supplies an explicit motive for deliberate retaliation**, without giving a full independently observable laboratory account of the ritual.
8. **VERIFIED:** Dany tries to reach Drogo emotionally and physically, discovers no remaining personality, and **suffocates him with a feather-filled cushion**. Her decision is direct; the corpse's later pyre is not until `AGOT 72`.
