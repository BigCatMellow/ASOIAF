2. **VERIFIED:** Winter town houses are mostly empty after long summer but fill with displaced farmers during winter. Robb rides with Bran, Theon, Joseth, Luwin and four guards. Grey Wind and Summer range ahead.

5. **VERIFIED:** Two wear remnants of Night's Watch black; one is **Stiv**, another **Wallen**. **OSHA'S ACCOUNT:** she and the other women did not take Watch oaths. She suggests selling Bran as a hostage to Mance Rayder; Stiv replies that the *white walkers* will not care about a hostage.
6. **VERIFIED:** Stiv cuts Bran's leg while slashing saddle straps; Bran bleeds but experiences no pain. Robb returns with both wolves; violence kills four attackers, while Osha and Stiv survive temporarily. Stiv takes Bran at knifepoint.
7. **VERIFIED:** Theon makes a lethal longbow shot through Stiv, freeing Bran. Robb rebukes him for endangering Bran with a risky shot. Theon admits he and guards had stopped after he saw a turkey, helping explain their delay.
8. **VERIFIED:** Osha surrenders. Luwin advises interrogation, and Robb brings her alive to Winterfell; two actual Watch deserters' heads are ordered returned to the Wall. The chapter closes with *potential knowledge from a wildling witness* rather than a confirmed explanation of the Others.
