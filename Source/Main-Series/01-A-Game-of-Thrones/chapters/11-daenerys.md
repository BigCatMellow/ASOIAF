## Narrative and consequences

1. **VERIFIED:** Before the wedding Illyrio and Jorah tell Viserys that Drogo's promised aid is neither immediate nor his to command; the khal must return to Vaes Dothrak and consult omens and the dosh khaleen. Viserys demands a timetable but lacks leverage.
2. **VERIFIED:** Daenerys dreams of Viserys's abuse ending in an emerging dragon and fire. She is frightened; the dream is not an independently witnessed magical event.
3. **VERIFIED:** The Dothraki wedding includes a vast mounted retinue, forced sexual acts, lethal fighting and people enslaved by the khalasar. Daenerys is thirteen, scared and unable even to speak freely with her husband because they lack a language in common.
4. **VERIFIED:** Wedding gifts make the political bargain tangible. Viserys supplies three attendants chosen with Illyrio; Jorah gives books of Westerosi histories and songs; Illyrio gives **three reputedly petrified dragon eggs**; Drogo gives a silver-grey filly.
5. **VERIFIED:** Riding the filly produces an unexpected moment of confidence and skill for Daenerys, publicly recognized by Drogo. Viserys immediately threatens her to secure his political investment. The secluded wedding night contains an apparent question-and-answer form of assent, within a coerced marriage in which the child has had no meaningful freedom to refuse.

## Competing meanings

**Political:** Viserys considers Daenerys a purchased route to the Iron Throne; Drogo's own timetable makes that plan unreliable. **Personal:** Daenerys's first taste of freedom comes from an animal gift inside the system exploiting her. **Rebuttal to triumphal reading:** the riding episode and a restrained wedding-night encounter do not erase coercion, age, slavery or violence.

**Theory stress:** Illyrio's decision to surrender immensely valuable dragon eggs is suspicious *in hindsight*. Ordinary explanation is ostentatious wealth, bribery and a belief in dead eggs. Test his later ventures before concluding he anticipated their hatching or arranged a unique blood ritual.
