## Major plot and character change

1. **VERIFIED:** Sam joyfully reports his transfer from arms training to work for **Maester Aemon** with books and ravens. The proposal Jon made in `AGOT 41` has produced a **real job assignment**, not a merely symbolic favor.
2. **VERIFIED:** Mormont tells trainees the Night's Watch is their new House, erasing old debts/allegiances after a voluntary last chance to leave **before sunset vows**. Jon and Sam request to swear before a heart tree north of the Wall, although Sam was raised in the Faith of the Seven.
3. **VERIFIED:** Assignments: **Halder and Albett → builders; Grenn, Pyp, Matthar, Todder → rangers; Sam, Dareon, Jon → stewards.** Dareon is intended for Eastwatch's trading/supply work. Chett is displaced from Aemon's post to kennels.
4. **VERIFIED:** Jon reacts angrily to being made the Lord Commander's *personal steward*, believing Alliser Thorne engineered humiliation. Sam explains that attendance on command brings access to decisions and instruction, not only menial tasks. **SAM'S INTERPRETATION:** Mormont is grooming Jon for command; **not a declaration by Mormont**.
5. **VERIFIED:** Jon admits he misjudged Sam, accepts the appointment and joins him for sunset vows. Crossing north requires a twisting **ice tunnel sealed by three locked iron-barred gates**. The Watch uses physical barrier security, not an entirely solid wall.
6. **VERIFIED:** In a grove about **half a league north** stand **nine weirwoods**, faces turned inward. Both men swear Night's Watch vows before them. This is the legally and spiritually consequential moment that **changes Jon from an unsworn youth into a sworn brother**.
7. **VERIFIED:** Dywen smells something wrong in the night. Ghost reappears holding a **severed human hand**, recognized by alarmed witnesses. **UNKNOWN:** identity and original condition of the owner until the follow-up investigation.
