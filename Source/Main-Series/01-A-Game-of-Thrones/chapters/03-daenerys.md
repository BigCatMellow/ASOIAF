## Essential narrative

- **VERIFIED:** Thirteen-year-old Daenerys is prepared by Viserys and Illyrio for a meeting with Khal Drogo. Viserys uses threats and physical cruelty, treating her prospective marriage as the price of his army.
- **VERIFIED:** Daenerys remembers years of displacement, especially a large house in Braavos with a red door and a lemon tree near her window. She yearns for safety and childhood while Viserys calls Westeros their only home.
- **CHARACTER MEMORY / CLAIM:** The siblings' flight from Dragonstone, Ser Willem Darry's protection, their mother's death, Rhaegar and Elia's fate, and pursuit by hired killers are mostly inherited stories or partial childhood recollections in this POV, not direct witnessed proof of every detail.
- **VERIFIED:** Illyrio trades in valuable goods and maintains enslaved servants despite Pentos's formal anti-slavery stance. His contacts extend beyond Westeros, and he carefully flatters Viserys.
- **CHARACTER CLAIM:** Illyrio says there are clandestine supporters and dragon banners awaiting Viserys in Westeros. Daenerys perceives his evasive sweetness; support is **UNVERIFIED** here.
- **VERIFIED:** At Drogo's manse, Daenerys sees Ser Jorah Mormont, recognizes the bleak personal stakes, and says she does not want to marry the khal. Viserys coerces her to present an agreeable face.

## Competing readings

- **Lemon tree / red door:** literal Braavos memory; displaced or composited childhood memory; deliberate protective deception. Keep all until independent geographic and witness evidence is assembled.
- **Viserys and support:** he could be deceived by Illyrio, or Illyrio could have real but limited agents; his flattering account is not itself reliable intelligence.
- **Daenerys's 'destiny':** bloodline rhetoric comes from an abuser and a political broker, which matters when evaluating later messianic certainty.
