## Major events and decisions

1. **VERIFIED:** Morning after Robert's final testament, Ned eats with Arya and Sansa. Arya has a last Syrio lesson, their belongings are packed, and Ned expects them aboard a ship **that evening**. Sansa asks to say goodbye to Joffrey; Ned forbids it.
2. **VERIFIED:** Pycelle arrives about an hour after breakfast to announce **Robert's death**. Ned summons the council to the more secure Tower of the Hand rather than the customary chamber.
3. **VARYS'S REPORT:** Renly has fled before dawn with Loras and about fifty men, probably south toward Highgarden or Storm's End. **Observed in this chapter:** Renly does not attend council; the intended destination is *not* confirmed.
4. **VERIFIED:** Barristan inspects **Robert's intact seal** and reads the will making Ned Protector until “the heir” reaches majority. Ned withholds his belief that Stannis, not Joffrey, is the adult legitimate heir; he hopes to defer public confrontation until his girls and Stannis are safe.
5. **VERIFIED:** Littlefinger announces he completed Ned's task with the gold cloaks. Ned follows the king's steward, guarded by **eight Stark men**, to the throne room, leaning on Petyr because of his broken leg.
6. **VERIFIED:** Joffrey declares himself king with Cersei and the other children beneath the throne, five Kingsguard, Sandor, twenty Lannister men and apparently loyal City Watch lining the room. Cersei rips Robert's testament into pieces. Ned says Stannis is true heir and commands Janos Slynt to arrest the queen's family **without harming them**.
7. **VERIFIED:** City Watch spearmen turn on Stark guards instead. A gold cloak stabs **Tomard** from behind; **Slynt himself cuts Varly's throat**; Sandor kills **Cayn**, disarming him first. Petyr draws the **Valyrian dagger from Ned's sheath** and holds it under his chin, explicitly reminding Ned he had warned him not to trust him.
8. **UNKNOWN in this POV's final moment:** Ned's ensuing custody, casualties in other towers, actual fate of his daughters and content/delivery of his Stannis letter.

## Interpretation and alternatives

**What defeats Ned?** Not honor *alone*: concentrated command of the City Watch, unwitnessed bribery, injury, loss of Renly, missing Stannis and leak of his intentions. **Legality versus power:** Joffrey's court asserts a crown while Robert's sealed regency order is physically destroyed. **Petyr's professed love of Catelyn:** cannot establish loyalty; his direct betrayal is unambiguous, though the entire prior dagger plot remains a separate question.
