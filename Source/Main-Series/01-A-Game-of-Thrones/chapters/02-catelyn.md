## Essential narrative

- **VERIFIED:** Catelyn finds Ned beneath Winterfell's heart tree cleaning Ice after the execution. Their different faith traditions are embodied in the godswood versus the small sept Ned built for her.
- **VERIFIED:** Ned calls the deserter the fourth of the year and recalls overwhelming fear. Benjen has reported a Watch population below a thousand, depleted through desertion and missing rangers.
- **CHARACTER CLAIM:** Ned expects wildlings to explain the losses and calls the Others extinct or imaginary, invoking Luwin's disbelief. The reader knows from the prologue that this assessment is wrong.
- **VERIFIED:** Catelyn tells Ned of Jon Arryn's sudden death and Robert's journey north. The king's letter reports Jon's quick illness and Pycelle's attendance; **those are reports**, not a firsthand account of the cause of death.
- **VERIFIED:** The decision to prepare for Robert, Cersei and the Lannisters follows. Ned is delighted to see his old friend; Catelyn privately fears the portent of the stag-antler death.
