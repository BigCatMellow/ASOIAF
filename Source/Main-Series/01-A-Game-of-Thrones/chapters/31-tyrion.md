# AGOT 31 (Tyrion) — false route, mountain clans, and the wager's weak point

- Record role: `RESEARCH / SEQUENTIAL CLOSE READING`
- Status: `FIRST PASS — INDEPENDENT REVIEW OPEN`
- Source: `AGOT 31 (Tyrion)`, project EPUB checked 2026-10-15. [Evidence policy](../../../../SOURCES.md).

## Core sequence

1. **VERIFIED:** From Tyrion's POV, Catelyn's arrest at the crossroads is revealed as tactically clever but only **about a dozen** of nearly fifty in the inn actually join the seizure. Yoren stands aside due to Watch neutrality. Tyrion offers to accompany her to avoid suicidal fighting.
2. **VERIFIED:** Catelyn loudly announces Winterfell as the destination, binds and hoods Tyrion, then takes the **eastern high road** to the Vale instead. Tyrion realizes news can spread by many witnesses (including Freys, Yoren, singer, sellers of information); she has deliberately misdirected pursuit.
3. **VERIFIED:** Their forced march costs three horses, including Tyrion's own gift from Jaime. The mountains, low supplies, dangerous tracks and hostile clans limit escape and movement. Bronn and Chiggen, mercenary captors, begin to assess risk differently.
4. **VERIFIED:** Tyrion denies owning the Valyrian-steel dagger and explains that equipping an assassin with a conspicuous personal weapon would be foolish. He challenges the sincerity of Littlefinger's account and reports Littlefinger has falsely boasted at court of a sexual relationship with Catelyn. **Tyrion's rebuttal is substantial counterevidence**, though it does not independently settle the dagger's true owner.
5. **VERIFIED:** Mounted mountain clans attack. Tyrion persuades Catelyn to arm her prisoners, fights awkwardly with a borrowed axe, kills attackers and moves to rescue Catelyn even while thinking he should abandon her. Catelyn also kills an attacker to finish the rescue.
6. **VERIFIED:** Jyck and two Bracken men, Kurleket and Mohor, are killed; Rodrik is badly wounded. The dead enemy group are poorly equipped and underfed. Catelyn wants cairns but the living must move before fresh attacks. She allows Tyrion to keep an axe and dagger without granting him trust.
7. **VERIFIED:** On the last line Tyrion states the wager account's central flaw: he **never bets against his own family**. This is his own direct assertion, not a recorded ledger of all wagers.

## Theory / counterevidence

**Littlefinger dagger account:** the announced loss when Jaime was beaten by Loras is plausibly contradicted by Tyrion's assertion that he never wagers against Jaime. One named contradiction is not full proof of Petyr's own ownership: audit exact original wager claim, other witnesses and eventual custody. **Tyrion a calculating villain:** he does cause Marillion severe injury; he also risks his life to protect his captor. Both are real.
