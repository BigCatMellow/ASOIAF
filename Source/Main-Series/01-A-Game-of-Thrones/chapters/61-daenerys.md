## Causal spine

1. **VERIFIED:** Drogo defeats **Khal Ogo** and then his successor-son **Fogo** at a Lhazareen town Ogo was already attacking. The Dothraki raid kills sheep, burns the settlement and enslaves survivors. **Jorah estimates** as many as ten thousand captives and proposes selling them at **Meereen** to fund ships for Drogo's proposed invasion.
2. **VERIFIED:** Dany sees a young boy tortured and killed, and witnesses Dothraki soldiers raping Lhazareen girls. She **orders** her khas to stop assaults, despite objections that rape is a warrior's spoil; her men kill two resisting assailants. Dany claims the victims **as her own slaves**, a protection within coercive ownership, **not general emancipation**.
3. **VERIFIED:** Jorah compares her conduct to **Rhaegar**, not Viserys. Drogo backs her right to the captive women but does not reject Dothraki conquest customs. Qotho and Mago are angered; Dany's protection depends on Drogo's authority.
4. **VERIFIED:** Drogo has a **barbed arrow through his arm and deep arakh slash across chest, with nipple torn away**. His bloodriders say he turned away traditional healers in favor of letting wounded warriors be treated first; Dany insists on immediate care.
5. **VERIFIED:** Among claimed women, **Mirri Maz Duur**, godswife of the ruined temple, offers treatment. **MIRRI'S BIOGRAPHICAL ACCOUNT:** training from mother, mages in **Asshai**, a Jogos Nhai moonsinger, a Dothraki woman and **Marwyn**, a metal-chain-wearing maester who taught anatomy and the Common Tongue. Jorah recognizes the maester's chain description. These details support a highly specific **Oldtown–Asshai–Lhazar** intellectual route, but no stated Marwyn plot.
6. **VERIFIED:** Mirri removes the arrow, uses boiling wine, leaf poultice, green paste, silver needle, silk and ointment to close wounds. She requires the **lambskin bandage for ten days**, warns of fever and pain, and cautions against wine/milk of poppy. Drogo rejects the restrictions. Dany asks Mirri to attend **Rhaego's birth**.
