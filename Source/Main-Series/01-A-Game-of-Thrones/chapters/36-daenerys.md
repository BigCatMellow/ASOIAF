## Main events and causal consequences

1. **VERIFIED:** Drogo's khalasar enters Vaes Dothrak through a monumental bronze horse gate, along a godsway lined with statues and deities looted from ruined cities. The apparently enormous sacred city is nearly empty outside markets and attendants of the `dosh khaleen`. Dany distinguishes it from the imagined palatial luxury Illyrio promised.
2. **VERIFIED:** Viserys remains bitter at being made to walk. The Dothraki secretly call him the Sorefoot King and Cart King; Dany interceded for his horse to be restored, partly to prevent his humiliation. He still thinks Drogo owes him an immediately deliverable army.
3. **CHARACTER EXPLANATION (Jorah):** The Dothraki understand Dany as a gift to Drogo, requiring a reciprocal gift when the khal chooses—not a binding purchase for troops. Viserys has been warned of this different political custom repeatedly and refuses to accept it.
4. **CHARACTER MILITARY ANALYSIS (Jorah):** Drogo has about forty thousand mounted warriors with mobile archery superiority; knights and foot soldiers would suffer in open battle. Yet a Dothraki host lacks siege expertise and might fail against even weak Westerosi castles. Robert's preference for open fighting could negate that defensive advantage. These are Jorah's judgments, not a tested invasion.
5. **VERIFIED:** Weapons are surrendered at Vaes Dothrak. The rule prohibits blades and shedding a *free man's* blood, while rival khalasars suspend feuds there. The city has markets drawing people from Yi Ti, Asshai and the Shadow Lands. Buildings were constructed in varied foreign styles by enslaved craftspeople.
6. **VERIFIED:** Drogo ascends the Mother of Mountains with his bloodriders for a night sacrifice; women may not ascend. Dany arranges food and new garments for Viserys, hoping he will adapt. He hits Doreah, sneers at the gifts, seizes Dany's arm and threatens her. Dany strikes him with the offered bronze medallion belt and orders him away.
7. **VERIFIED:** Distressed by the failed reconciliation, Dany holds the **green egg with bronze flecks** against her pregnant body and interprets her child's movement as akin to a brother answering a dragon. This is **Dany's subjective analogy**, not a witnessed mind-to-egg exchange.

## Information and rival readings

**Dany knows** Viserys no longer commands her new household and that Drogo's timetable is different from a bargain. **She does not know** whether any invasion will happen. **INFERRED:** compassion for Viserys coexists with emerging separation from him.

**Theory tests:** A prophesied gathering of khalasars could signify Dany-led unity, ordinary religious aspiration, or an event never realized. The egg/child juxtaposition may foreshadow hatching, but pregnancy and touch can explain movement. Never equate Dothraki gift exchange with genuine consent to Dany's marriage; she remains coerced and her attendants enslaved.
