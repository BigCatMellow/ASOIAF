## Major events and decisions

1. **VERIFIED:** Bran has lain unconscious for nearly a fortnight. Catelyn lives in his room, feeding him a sustaining mixture and scarcely sleeping. Jon has stayed away out of fear of seeing him.
2. **VERIFIED:** Catelyn first denies Jon entry; he defies her and says goodbye to Bran, horrified at his frailty. She discloses she had prayed Bran would not go south, then turns her grief against Jon and wishes he had suffered instead.
3. **VERIFIED:** Jon does not tell Robb what Catelyn said, describing her as kind. The two brothers embrace before a separation whose duration they cannot know. Robb is already managing the departure bustle while Ned is away.
4. **VERIFIED:** Jon gives Arya a slim, specially forged sword made by Mikken. He explains that the blade suits speed and thrusting rather than heavy blows, and urges constant practice, running and riding. He and Arya share the joke of naming it **Needle**.
5. **VERIFIED:** Arya keeps the weapon secret from Septa Mordane and Sansa. Jon rides north afterward, carrying a memory of her joy.

## Interpretation and rebuttal

**INFERRED:** Jon's care for the ostracized helps shape Arya's agency; his compassionate choice contrasts with his wounded response to Catelyn. **Counterweight:** giving a sharp sword to a child without family permission is reckless as well as generous.

**Catelyn's cruelty:** directly witnessed and severe, but it occurs during sustained crisis and exhaustion; do not convert one scene into proof she never loved anyone or that grief excuses it. **UNKNOWN:** whether Robb ever learns the words.
