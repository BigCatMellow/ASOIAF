## Plot and decision chain

1. **VERIFIED:** Robert rides privately with Ned away from the royal party. He shares a report from Varys that Daenerys has married Drogo; Jorah is identified by Robert as an informant interested in a royal pardon. **CHARACTER CLAIM:** Robert's estimate of Drogo's strength and exact network in Essos.
2. **VERIFIED:** Robert advocates killing Daenerys before she can have a child or bring a host west. Ned objects to killing children and remembers confronting Robert after Tywin's men murdered Elia Martell's children. The two men differ on whether perceived future danger excuses present atrocity.
3. **VERIFIED:** Robert rejects Ned's proposal to make sickly Robert Arryn Warden of the East. Robert admits he has already promised the office to Jaime Lannister; Ned raises concentration of military power and divided loyalties.
4. **VERIFIED:** Ned narrates what he personally saw after Tywin's capture of King's Landing: Lannister banners over the city, dead Aerys, Jaime in Kingsguard white and golden armor sitting on the Iron Throne. Robert dismisses Ned's interpretation as too rigid.
5. **VERIFIED:** Ned follows the king despite a renewed desire to return to Catelyn and Bran. Duty wins this immediate choice, but the personal and institutional conflict remains unresolved.

## Theory challenge: the same events, different explanations

**R+L=J:** Ned's evasiveness and Lyanna memory strengthen a pattern when joined to other passages. **Counterargument:** he also directly supplies the name Wylla, and secret grief might involve multiple obligations. Name biological origin, deception, and legal status as different questions.

**Jaime's kingslaying:** Ned sees an oathbreaker enjoying a stolen throne; Robert sees a necessary killing. Neither narrates Jaime's underlying decision from his own POV. Check `ASOS` Jaime recollections rather than adopting a simple moral verdict.

**Dany invasion:** Robert is not irrational merely because he sees long-term danger; Ned is not naive merely because he refuses preventive murder. The immediate ethical action remains a real contest.
