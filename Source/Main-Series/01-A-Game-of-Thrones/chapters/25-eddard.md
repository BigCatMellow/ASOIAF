## Primary plot

1. **VERIFIED:** Ned questions Grand Maester Pycelle about Jon Arryn's death. **PYCELLE'S TESTIMONY:** Jon sought a genealogy book, became violently ill the next morning, and received competing treatment from Maester Colemon before Pycelle took charge and dismissed Colemon.
2. **CHARACTER CLAIM:** Pycelle says Jon repeatedly called 'Robert' during fever and whispered that the seed was strong before losing speech; Pycelle gave him milk of the poppy. Whether 'Robert' meant the son or king is not established.
3. **VERIFIED:** Ned asks whether poison might explain the illness. Pycelle dismisses it without offering an independent postmortem, stresses Lysa's instability, and redirects suspicion toward Varys by invoking stereotypes about poison.
4. **VERIFIED/CHARACTER REPORT:** Pycelle identifies Jon's borrowed book as Grand Maester Malleon's lineage history; he agrees to send it. He says Cersei and the children were traveling to Casterly Rock with Tywin when Jon died, following Joffrey's name-day tourney.
5. **VERIFIED:** Ned meets Arya practicing balance for Syrio. Their exchange reveals Ned assumes Bran's disability excludes him from his prior knightly dreams, yet imagines varied positions of power for Bran; he assigns Arya the future of a lord's wife despite her explicit rejection.
6. **VERIFIED:** Littlefinger identifies four remaining members of Arryn's household who could be questioned, including the recently knighted squire **Ser Hugh of the Vale**. He points out an alleged Varys informer and queen's watcher surveying the Hand's rooms and warns Ned not to investigate openly.

## Test alternatives and future implications

**Poison:** sudden illness and treatment disagreement justify investigation, not proof of a culprit. **Genealogy:** a parentage problem looks increasingly plausible but nothing in this scene directly identifies every hidden parent. **Spycraft:** Petyr can be helpful while controlling witness selection and information flow; competence does not certify trustworthiness. **Gender/identity:** Arya/Bran conversation foreshadows conflicting social possibilities without a destiny decree.
