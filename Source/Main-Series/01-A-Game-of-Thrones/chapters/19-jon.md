## Major sequence and decisions

1. **VERIFIED:** Jon repeatedly defeats fellow recruits, including Grenn, in the training yard. Ser Alliser humiliates recruits and calls Jon 'Lord Snow'. Jon enjoys victories while despising his peers' ineptitude.
2. **VERIFIED:** Benjen left on a ranging to search for Waymar Royce; Jon was refused permission to accompany him. Benjen has missed his promised return by more than two weeks after Jon's name day. Jon fears the possibility of his uncle's death.
3. **VERIFIED:** Grenn and others corner Jon in the armory; a taunt about Jon's mother provokes him to attack Todder/Toad. Donal Noye stops the fight and reveals that the others lack Jon's privileged lifelong instruction under Ser Rodrik.
4. **VERIFIED:** Noye forces Jon to reassess his victories as humiliation inflicted on inexperienced men, not proof of effortless superiority. Jon changes behavior by the chapter's end, offering Grenn help in defending against Robb's maneuver.
5. **VERIFIED:** Tyrion meets Jon outside; they discuss Castle Black's decay, isolation and Benjen's absence. Mormont's summons turns out to be Robb's raven: Bran has awakened but remains paralyzed. Jon's relief leads to his gesture of reconciliation.
6. **VERIFIED:** Jon answers a Thorne insult with a joke that draws laughter from the hall and enrages the trainer. This small triumph does **not** resolve their institutional antagonism.
