## What happens and why

1. **VERIFIED:** Catelyn and Ser Rodrik travel north in the rain from King's Landing after Ned's warning. She considers diverting to Riverrun or the Eyrie but chooses Winterfell and her sons as her immediate duty.
2. **VERIFIED:** They lodge at Masha Heddle's busy inn at the Trident crossroads, a site Catelyn knows from childhood. Her disguise as Rodrik's daughter works on the road: Lord Jason Mallister and son Patrek pass without recognizing her.
3. **VERIFIED:** The inn contains sworn men of multiple Riverlands houses—Whent, Bracken and a large Frey party—whose duties to her father could become leverage. Catelyn studies their numbers and identities while remaining initially anonymous.
4. **VERIFIED:** Tyrion, traveling south from the Wall with Yoren and two servants, arrives and pays for lodging. The singer Marillion calls attention to him, and Tyrion recognizes Catelyn in the common room.
5. **VERIFIED:** Catelyn makes an immediate, high-risk decision to invoke the guests' feudal oaths house by house and publicly accuses Tyrion of conspiring to murder Bran. She commands his seizure in Robert's name. Armed men draw swords; the chapter ends **before** any subsequent journey or trial.
6. **EVIDENCE LIMIT:** Catelyn's accusation rests on the actual attempt against Bran **plus Littlefinger's uncorroborated statement** about the dagger's prior ownership. The reader does not observe Tyrion ordering an attack or receiving the knife.

## Moral, political, and theory tests

**Consequential contradiction:** Catelyn intends to prevent war, yet arrests the son of Tywin Lannister in a room full of witnesses, under a public allegation that could provoke exactly such a war. The action is understandable under urgent exposure and grief; its legality, wisdom and evidentiary strength are distinct questions.

**Rival interpretations:** Catelyn may be acting from reasonable fear when discovered, or may be gambling too heavily on Tully obligations. The observed danger is real; Tyrion's personal culpability remains **UNKNOWN**. **Next test:** track which bannermen actually escort the prisoner and where Catelyn goes, rather than projecting an intended Winterfell journey onto the outcome.
