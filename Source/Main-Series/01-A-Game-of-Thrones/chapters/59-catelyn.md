## Primary events and strategic significance

1. **VERIFIED:** Robb's host marches down the causeway past Moat Cailin. The **Blackfish** has already taken **100 picked scouts and 100 swift horses** ahead to screen the march. He and Theon relay that Tywin's army lies farther south and **Walder Frey is holding approximately four thousand men** at the fortified Twins rather than joining Riverrun.
2. **VERIFIED:** Catelyn orders the Blackfish's archers to shoot down outgoing **ravens** that might warn Tywin of the force's maneuver. Theon says he already did so, confirming a mundane way of disrupting intelligence rather than requiring supernatural secrecy.
3. **VERIFIED AS CAPTURED LANNISTER OUTRIDER'S REPORT:** **Jaime has defeated Edmure outside Riverrun**; Edmure is wounded/captured, with Blackwood and others besieged inside. This makes the crossing urgent.
4. **VERIFIED:** The high-running **Green Fork cannot be forded** at this point. Robb lacks time and timber for a heavy-armored raft crossing. The Twins comprise paired fortified castles connected by a single wide stone bridge and **water tower**, with moats, drawbridges, closed gates and archers. A siege without a western-bank force and while Tywin advances is impracticable.
5. **VERIFIED:** Four Frey sons bring an offer to treat with Robb **inside** the castle. His bannermen refuse to risk his capture. Catelyn volunteers instead and rides under a **hostage exchange**, with younger **Ser Perwyn Frey** remaining among the Starks to secure her return.
6. **VERIFIED:** Catelyn confronts **ninety-year-old Walder Frey**, who frames his own delays as lawful mustering and resents Tully/Arryn family slights. He acknowledges owing fealty to Tullys **and** the Crown, allowing him to bargain rather than automatically serve either side.
7. **INDEPENDENT SECOND TESTIMONY:** Walder explicitly says **Jon Arryn intended to foster Robert Arryn with Stannis**, not Tywin. This independently supports **Maester Colemon's Dragonstone account (AGOT 40)** against Catelyn's earlier understanding of a later Casterly Rock plan. **Unknown:** who changed the foster arrangement or whether any instruction was executed.
8. **VERIFIED:** After private negotiations, Walder agrees to open the crossing and add his swords except **four hundred retained to guard the Twins**. In exchange, **two Frey grandsons** (aged eight and seven) will be fostered at Winterfell; **Olyvar Frey** will serve as Robb's squire; **Arya**, if safely restored, is promised to **Elmar Frey**; and **Robb himself consents to marry one of Walder's daughters after the fighting**.
9. **VERIFIED:** The Starks cross at evening. **Nine tenths of mounted men** traverse with Robb, Catelyn and Brynden; the majority of infantry remains **east of the river under Roose Bolton**, ordered south to draw Tywin's attention. The original two-part strategy from AGOT 55 is now physically underway.
