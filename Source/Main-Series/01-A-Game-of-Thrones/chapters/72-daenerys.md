## Final novel sequence

1. **VERIFIED:** In a parched landscape Dany orders Drogo's **funeral pyre** built in three layers with his slain stallion's carcass underneath; logs, oil, brush and costly possessions surround his body. She binds **Mirri Maz Duur** nearby and arranges to burn her alive as part of an intended ritual. Mirri insists that killing a horse alone cannot fuel magic; she is whipped at Dany's command when she challenges her.
2. **VERIFIED:** Jorah kneels and explicitly transfers his allegiance from dead **Viserys** to **Queen Daenerys**, though he fears her death in the fire. She reassures him she does not intend suicide.
3. **VERIFIED:** Dany declares the remaining enslaved people **free and able to leave**, with an offer of a place if they choose to stay. She offers **Jhogo, Aggo and Rakharo** the status of bloodrider/`khas` leaders, rewarding Aggo with her **dragonbone bow** and Rakharo with a rich arakh. The Dothraki men initially object that a **woman cannot be khal**; their ritual assent is not yet secure.
4. **VERIFIED:** Dany washes, oils and braids Drogo's corpse, restores his bells and places each of the **three stone eggs** at known places: **black/scarlet by heart**, **green/bronze by head**, **cream/gold between his legs**.
5. **VERIFIED:** Dany has Mirri **bound to the pyre** and personally pours oil over her. She says Mirri's **life**, not her screams, is what she wants because only death can pay for life. This is a deliberate lethal sacrifice, **not** nonviolent liberation or a victim's consenting contribution.
6. **VERIFIED:** A **red comet** appears low in the east. Dany interprets it as a dragon herald; this is an **interpretation of a real celestial object**, not a proven supernatural message with a specific sender.
7. **VERIFIED:** Dany lights the pyre. Mirri is consumed. Dany approaches the flames, hears **three distinct explosive cracks** during the collapse and enters the fire. This differs significantly from the earlier failed experiment in `AGOT 54` where eggs rested among coals without hatching.
8. **VERIFIED:** After the fire dies, Dany is found **alive amid ashes with three newly hatched dragons**: **cream/gold suckling at left breast, green/bronze at right, black/scarlet around her shoulders**. The black one releases smoke and the three call aloud; these are **direct waking physical hatchlings**, not a dream or symbolic prophecy.
9. **VERIFIED:** Jorah and then Jhogo, Aggo, Rakharo, the handmaids and other surviving Dothraki kneel to Dany. She now possesses unprecedented living creatures and visible followers, but not Drogo's former forty-thousand riders or the ready ships needed to invade Westeros.

## Theory adjudication

**Dragon birth needs death/fire:** strong scene association between a human sacrifice, dead husband/horse, intense heat, three eggs and emerging dragons. **UNKNOWN:** necessary/sufficient roles of blood magic, comet, Dany's ancestry, latent eggs, and intended spell. Avoid pretending the pyre is a controlled scientific experiment. **General fire immunity:** direct unusual survival here only; the experiment does not establish inherited permanent invulnerability. **Azor Ahai / Lightbringer / Nissa Nissa:** thematic fit with death-for-life and fire, not textual identification of Dany as one literal prophesied figure or a dragon as the exact legendary sword.
