## Major scene sequence

1. **VERIFIED:** Sansa attends the Hand's tourney with Jeyne and Septa Mordane. The knights' splendor confirms her romantic expectations, but the day's jousts also produce serious injuries and an on-page death.
2. **VERIFIED:** Ser Gregor Clegane's lance strikes and kills an unnamed young Vale knight whose gorget was imperfectly secured; the blood-soaked blue cloak with moons leaves a vivid impression on Sansa. Jeyne is distraught; Sansa reflects that an unknown dead man may receive no song or lasting remembrance.
3. **VERIFIED:** Several competitors advance, including Jaime, Sandor, Gregor and Loras Tyrell. Loras presents Sansa a **red** rose while giving other women **white** ones. This is deliberate in the scene, but why he chooses red is not independently established.
4. **VERIFIED:** Petyr Baelish approaches Sansa, remarks on her Tully likeness and memories of Catelyn, touches her hair and abruptly leaves. Sansa has not met him before and is uncomfortable.
5. **VERIFIED:** At the feast Joffrey is attentively courteous to Sansa after previously mistreating her. Wine is served freely; drunken Robert publicly argues with Cersei over his desire to fight in the tourney and humiliates Jaime. Joffrey sends Sandor to escort Sansa home while the septa sleeps.
6. **SANDOR'S TESTIMONY:** In a darkened field, Sandor asserts Gregor deliberately targeted the young knight's exposed throat and tells Sansa that Gregor caused his facial burns as a child by pushing him into hot coals over a wooden toy. Their father allegedly concealed the assault as an accident and Gregor was later knighted by Rhaegar. Sandor threatens to kill Sansa if she repeats his confession.
7. **VERIFIED:** Sansa responds with pity and privately rejects Gregor's claim to true knighthood. Sandor delivers her safely to the castle despite coercive threats; kindness and intimidation coexist.
