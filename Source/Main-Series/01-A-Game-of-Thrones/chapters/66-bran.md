## Major narrative and causal changes

1. **VERIFIED:** Bran observes emergency training of Winterfell's young and inexperienced defenders while **Luwin measures a bright comet**. He wishes he could still wield weapons as a knight; Luwin distinguishes physical sword combat from romantic tales of **Symeon Star-Eyes**.
2. **DREAM:** The **three-eyed crow** leads Bran down the crypts, where he dreams Ned is present and speaks to him about **Jon**. Bran cannot recall the message's details. **VERIFIED:** Hodor refuses to carry Bran into the crypt, and Luwin warns against beating him; Osha instead carries Bran and Summer.
3. **VERIFIED:** The crypt is **longer than Winterfell** according to Bran, with deeper vaults reportedly existing below; stone king statues and real iron swords line chambers. Bran recalls House Stark histories, including **Brandon the Shipwright** who sailed west and never returned, and stories of past northern wars. **This is direct evidence for large crypt architecture, not a live secret city.**
4. **VERIFIED:** Rickon is physically hiding in **Ned's still-empty reserved crypt space**, with freed **Shaggydog** who violently attacks Luwin. Summer intervenes; Rickon says he **also dreamed of Ned**, and that their father is coming home. Two children share a potentially striking dream motif; no external magical sender is directly identified.
5. **VERIFIED:** Osha carries Bran and Rickon back to Luwin's tower. Luwin treats his torn arm with **firemilk**. Rickon is four; his temper and wolf's attacks are household dangers, not proof a dead king was present.
6. **VERIFIED:** Bran finds sharp **dragonglass/obsidian arrowheads** in Luwin's study; Luwin says children of forest formerly made weapons from it, while **Osha claims they still do** north of the Wall. Neither demonstrates in this chapter that obsidian can kill an Other.
7. **LUWIN'S HISTORICAL ACCOUNT:** The **children of the forest** inhabited woods and had **greenseers** carving faces in weirwoods; arrival of **First Men**, tree-felling and wars followed. The **Pact** between First Men and children was made on the **Isle of Faces**, granting woods to children and forbidding cutting heart trees, with **green men** maintaining the site. **Crucial distinction:** no Other negotiated or signed this *particular* pact in Luwin's story.
8. **LUWIN'S HISTORICAL CLAIM vs OSHA'S:** Luwin places the children's disappearance in the past; Osha says they and giants survive north of the Wall. Luwin describes the Andals' invasion and weirwood destruction as later; these claims remain attributed oral/historical knowledge.
9. **VERIFIED:** Summer and Shaggydog **howl simultaneously**, then a raven arrives. Luwin reads it and asks for a **stonecarver who knows Ned's likeness**. This, with preceding direct execution evidence, establishes that the household has received news of Ned's death even though Bran has not heard the contents directly.

## Theory challenges

**Underground Winterfell:** unusually extensive *known crypts* are source-supported, but no populated underground city is shown. **Wrong-way Watch/Others treaty:** the identified Pact is between **First Men and children**, not proof of a negotiated Other boundary. **Greenseer dreams:** cross-child imagery is suggestive, but a read-through of independent later POVs is required before assigning sender, purpose or exact prophecy.
