## Full causal spine

1. **VERIFIED:** Ned is held beneath the Red Keep, alone in a windowless, filthy dungeon, with an untreated hot, throbbing shattered leg, little food/water and no reliable timekeeping. He fears for Arya and Sansa and regrets trusting **Littlefinger, Janos Slynt**, and others. His intermittent memories and visions are affected by **fever, darkness, physical deprivation and despair**.
2. **DREAM/MEMORY:** Ned recalls the **year of false spring at Harrenhal**. Rhaegar defeats **Barristan** at the joust and gives the queen-of-love-and-beauty crown of **blue winter roses to Lyanna**, passing his wife Elia. The memory bleeds into Lyanna's final deathbed request and bloodied petals. This is an important *remembered event* but the petals cutting Ned's hand belong to dream imagery.
3. **VERIFIED:** The prisoner's principal guard gives only occasional water, ignores his pleas for news and assaults him. A different turnkey brings **wine**; Ned recognizes the disguise as **Varys**, who does not free him.
4. **VARYS'S REPORT:** **Arya escaped Meryn Trant** and remains missing even to Lannisters; Sansa is held and intended for Joffrey's marriage. Tyrion has escaped the Eyrie, and Varys speculates he may die in the Mountains. **Ned has no independent way to check these accounts** from his cell.
5. **VARYS'S ADMISSION/CLAIM:** He could arrange escape but will not because exposure would cost him his own position. He says he acts for *peace and the realm*, professes to have protected Robert for fifteen years, and acknowledges that the queen wishes Ned to confess, repudiate Stannis and Renly and command Robb to stand down.
6. **REPORTED BY VARYS:** He questioned Lancel, who said **Cersei furnished the wineskins for Robert's hunt** as Robert's favorite vintage. This narrows an earlier access question (AGOT 47), but Varys remains a reporting intermediary and does not produce Lancel before Ned.
7. **VERIFIED:** Ned initially refuses to trade his honor for his own survival. Varys shifts from persuasion about political peace to an explicit threat that **Sansa will suffer** if Ned will not confess. Ned pleads for his child, demonstrating the precise vulnerability Varys is exploiting.
8. **VERIFIED AS VARYS'S STORY:** He recalls **Rhaenys**, daughter of Rhaegar, having a small black kitten named **Balerion** before being murdered. The kitten's later fate is **UNKNOWN**, and the anecdote reminds Ned that royal children are not protected by their innocence.

## Theory and alternatives

**R+L=J:** Harrenhal roses and the deathbed return are important cumulative clues. **Counterweight:** the source never narrates Jon's birth, marriage or child's transfer at the tower. **Robert's death:** the Varys–Lancel claim strengthens a Cersei wine-access hypothesis, but does **not** turn the boar wound into a proven deliberate hunting assassination on this chapter alone. **Varys “serves the realm”:** his survival calculus and Sansa coercion show his methods compromise that assertion; motive remains **CHARACTER CLAIM**.
