## Core sequence

1. **VERIFIED:** Janos Slynt reports rising disorder around Robert's tourney. Ned authorizes fifty more City Watch men and temporarily assigns twenty of his own fifty guards under Alyn; this improves security but reduces the Hand's immediate household strength.
2. **VERIFIED:** Ned studies Malleon's genealogies, concentrating on Lannister lines without yet identifying the decisive pattern.
3. **CHARACTER REPORTS from servants via Jory:** Ser Hugh refuses informal questioning; a kitchen girl, potboy and former stablehand offer fragmented, partly conflicting glimpses of Jon Arryn. The stablehand says Arryn rode with Stannis to a brothel; potboy gossip describes a brilliant falcon suit of armor and visits with Stannis.
4. **VERIFIED:** Ned remembers Renly showing him a portrait of Margaery Tyrell and asking whether she resembles Lyanna. Ned does not see the resemblance. Renly's objective remains **INFERRED**, not confessed.
5. **VERIFIED:** Ned visits master armorer Tobho Mott on Street of Steel. Mott says Stannis and Jon Arryn visited **not to commission armor** but to see a boy in the forge.
6. **VERIFIED:** Ned meets the apprentice Gendry: strong, black-haired, blue-eyed, working on a personal bull-headed helmet. Gendry reports that Arryn asked about his mother, an alehouse worker with yellow hair; Stannis mostly looked at him. Ned recognizes a resemblance and concludes the boy is likely Robert's bastard.
7. **CHARACTER CLAIM:** Mott says an unnamed, well-dressed man with a brown-red beard and purple velvet cloak paid double Gendry's apprenticeship fee, partly for silence. He will not identify the parentage. **UNKNOWN:** payer, actual employer's motive and full paternal proof.
