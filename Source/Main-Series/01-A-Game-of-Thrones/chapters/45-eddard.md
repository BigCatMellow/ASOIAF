## Plot: investigation reaches an answer

1. **VERIFIED:** Pycelle mentions a raven from Tywin to Cersei objecting to Ned's men being sent against Gregor. Ned suspects the maester is passing a warning deliberately on the queen's behalf. This is Ned's inference, not a witnessed message from Cersei to Pycelle.
2. **VERIFIED:** Littlefinger brings word that men are gathering at Casterly Rock; Robert pursues a large boar after wolves have destroyed the white hart. Joffrey and some hunters returned earlier than the king. Petyr notices Ned reading Grand Maester Malleon's genealogies, but Ned avoids explaining their decisive meaning.
3. **VERIFIED:** Ned realizes **Sansa's observation** that Joffrey does not resemble Robert unlocked a pattern: documented historical Lannister–Baratheon marriages produce black-haired children, and every known acknowledged bastard of Robert has black hair. Ned connects this with Jon Arryn's reported deathbed words and Bran's possible witness of something dangerous.
4. **VERIFIED:** Ned considers the prospective killing of Cersei's children by Robert and recalls the bodies of Rhaegar's children. His plan prioritizes preserving innocent lives while fulfilling his duty to tell Robert the truth. He arranges a passage on the **Braavosi `Wind Witch` in three days** for Sansa and Arya, with household guards.
5. **VERIFIED:** With a shattered leg and reduced escort after Beric/Alyn depart, Ned orders Fat Tom (Tomard) to double the Tower of the Hand guard and bring a sealed note to **Cersei** asking her to meet him alone in the Red Keep godswood beneath its oak.
6. **DIRECT ON-PAGE ADMISSION:** Cersei confirms Jaime is her brother *and lover*, says Bran saw them together, and affirms that **Joffrey, Myrcella and Tommen are all Jaime's children**. This resolves the specific incest/false-paternity question far beyond Ned's earlier resemblance inference.
7. **CERSEI'S ACCOUNT:** Robert once impregnated her; with Jaime's help she found a woman to end the pregnancy. She attributes her hatred in part to Robert speaking Lyanna's name during their wedding-night intercourse. These are Cersei's first-person claims, not another POV's independently observed events.
8. **VERIFIED:** Cersei tempts Ned to political alliance and suggests his own publicly acknowledged bastard makes him a hypocrite. She raises **Ashara Dayne** and asks whether Ned killed her brother or stole a child; these are **rhetorical allegations/questions**, not a reliable confession about Jon Snow's origin.
9. **VERIFIED:** Ned warns Cersei he will tell Robert when he returns and advises her to escape immediately with the three children to the Free Cities or farther. Cersei explicitly rejects his assumptions about honor and survival, leaving him under the oak. **NOT VERIFIED:** whether she will flee, what countermeasures she initiates, or whether Robert will return to hear Ned.

## Theory adjudication and hard boundaries

**Incest/royal succession:** strongly established *in this primary text* by Cersei's direct statement, Bran's on-page witness and historical genealogy. Avoid reducing it to mere fan theory. **Bran catspaw:** motive for silencing Bran exists, but **who hired the later assassin remains unresolved**. **R+L=J:** Ned repeatedly links protecting endangered children with Lyanna's unspoken promise, but he does not identify the subject of that promise; Cersei's Ashara allegations cannot prove an alternative origin.

**Critical causal hinge:** Ned consciously chooses transparency toward Cersei over detaining her or quietly advising Robert. Whether morally defensible, this creates a window for the queen's independent decisions before his own daughters leave.
