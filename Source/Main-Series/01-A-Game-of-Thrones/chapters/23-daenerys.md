## Story arc and critical turning points

1. **VERIFIED:** Drogo's khalasar reaches the grasslands called the Dothraki sea after an arduous eastward ride from Pentos. Daenerys recalls severe saddle injuries and marital coercion that nearly led her to consider suicide. Her increased skill and comfort are gradual and bodily as well as emotional.
2. **VERIFIED AS DREAM:** Dany sees a blood-dark dragon and experiences transformative fire in a recurring dream. The next day she feels less pain; she briefly senses unusual warmth from her black-and-red wedding egg. **UNKNOWN:** whether dragon magic, physiology or psychological change explains the improvement.
3. **VERIFIED:** On a ridge Dany orders Jorah and her attendants to stay back while she rides alone. Viserys treats this as an unacceptable command to him and assaults her. Daenerys physically pushes him away.
4. **VERIFIED:** Jhogo lashes a whip around Viserys's neck. Dany prevents greater injury but orders him deprived of his horse and made to walk. Jorah publicly supports her command, a visible change in their political relationship.
5. **VERIFIED:** Jorah tells her that ordinary people care more about safety and seasons than disputed dynastic titles; **HIS GENERALIZATION**, not independent polling. Dany recognizes Viserys is incapable of leading an army to reclaim Westeros.
6. **VERIFIED:** Doreah conveys a trader's folklore about a moon hatching dragons; Irri/Jhiqui give another solar-lunar religious explanation. Dany experiences her eggs as warm, even hot, and notices a transient red shimmer.
7. **VERIFIED:** Dany asserts greater personal control during a subsequent encounter with Drogo; by the far side of the grass sea Jhiqui identifies a pregnancy and Dany says she already knows. It is Dany's **fourteenth name day**.

## Theory tests and opposing explanations

**Egg activation:** emerging dragon magic is plausible when checked against later hatchings; warmth may also result from sun exposure as Dany herself suggests. **Dany's fire immunity:** a dream without pain and hot baths are not evidence of general immunity to burning. **Daenerys as liberator:** her empathy and expanding authority coexist with enslavement of the attendants and the khalasar's violence; future choices must account for both.

**Immediate character change:** she rejects Viserys as viable king while still initially defending his legal claim. Keep the legal, emotional and practical distinctions separate.
