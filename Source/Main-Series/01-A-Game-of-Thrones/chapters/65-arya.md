He rejects them and orders **Ser Ilyn Payne** to remove Ned's head. **Ned's greatsword Ice** is raised above him. 6. **VERIFIED:** Arya attempts to rush to her father with **Needle drawn**. An unknown hand grabs her; Needle is **knocked from her grasp** in the crowd. She is devastated and frightened, not suddenly transformed into a fearless assassin.
