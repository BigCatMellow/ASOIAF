# AGOT 52 (Jon) — impossible corpses, wartime news, and a firsthand wight attack

- Record role: `RESEARCH / SEQUENTIAL CLOSE READING`
- Status: `FIRST PASS — CROSS-BOOK CHALLENGE OPEN`
- Source: `AGOT 52 (Jon)`, EPUB read 2026-10-15. [Evidence rules](../../../../SOURCES.md).

## Major causal sequence

1. **VERIFIED:** Ghost's severed-hand discovery leads Watch searchers to two bodies identified by Rykker as **Othor and Jafer Flowers**, members of Benjen's six-person ranging. Their fates and Benjen's whereabouts remain otherwise unknown. Horses and kennel dogs resist the bodies while Ghost approaches them.
2. **VERIFIED:** Rykker initially proposes recent wildling ambush; Sam argues observed **dried blood at the ripped wrist**, no pooling at scene, no decomposition, no scavengers and altered blue eyes contradict a recent killing. **Dywen's alternative:** bodies may have been moved as a warning. Mormont orders the corpses returned intact for Maester Aemon's examination **rather than burning them**.
3. **VERIFIED:** Mormont commands a search for all missing rangers within **ten leagues**; patrol shortages and his own limits matter. The warm, wet 'spirit summer' is believed by older men to precede cold. The bodies have strange black hands even when the remaining flesh is pale.
4. **VERIFIED:** At the Wall, Jon learns of **Robert's death** through Pyp and then receives a letter via Mormont, read by Pycelle's report, saying **Ned is imprisoned for treason**. It gives no direct word about Sansa or Arya. Mormont proposes asking court acquaintances to permit Ned to join the Watch, an intended request **not yet shown as dispatched**.
5. **VERIFIED:** Jon reacts to Thorne's mocking and tries to stab him; Sam, Pyp, Grenn and Todder restrain him. The officers disarm Jon and confine him with a guard, but allow Ghost into the cell.
6. **VERIFIED:** That night Ghost alerts Jon to danger. The guard outside has been killed with his head twisted around; Jon hears someone in Mormont's chambers and takes the guard's sword.
7. **VERIFIED:** A **blue-eyed Othor corpse** attacks the Lord Commander, despite being previously identified as dead. Jon cuts off its arm and repeatedly wounds its face, but its severed fingers move independently, no blood issues, and it continues fighting. Ghost grapples and saves Jon from being strangled.
8. **VERIFIED:** Mormont appears holding an oil lamp; the raven calls for burning. Jon ignites curtains and throws burning cloth over the attacker; the chapter ends **with the wight set alight**, not with a complete later examination/confirmation of all magical mechanics.

## Implications and rival readings

**Others vs wights:** a directly reanimated **dead human** attacks and must be distinguished from the **Other** seen in AGOT Prologue. The mechanics of raising him and whether an Other directly commanded him are not observed. **Watch orientation theory:** failure to burn suspected corpses nearly kills Mormont; it does not support flipping the Wall's ancient original purpose. **Mormont and Jon:** Jon's family anger tests whether oath to realm can coexist with kinship; he has *already sworn* in AGOT 48.
