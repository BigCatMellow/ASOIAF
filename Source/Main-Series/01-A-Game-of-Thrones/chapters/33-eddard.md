## Core sequence, chronology, consequences

1. **VERIFIED:** Robert's council learns from a **Varys report attributed to Jorah Mormont** that fourteen-year-old Daenerys is pregnant. Robert orders her, the unborn child and Viserys killed. Ned argues the sea, the uncertainty of the child's future and the injustice of killing a girl.
2. **VERIFIED:** Renly supports an assassination that he believes should have happened years earlier. Varys and Pycelle advocate preventive killing to avoid a possible future war. **Ser Barristan alone publicly sides with Ned.** Littlefinger treats the order as an expedient expenditure.
3. **CHARACTER CLAIM:** Varys says Jorah hopes for royal pardon but the no-weapons rule at Vaes Dothrak complicates assassination; he suggests poison. Pycelle reacts visibly to Varys's mention of **tears of Lys**. Whether his reaction betrays previous knowledge is **INFERRED**, not proved.
4. **VERIFIED:** Ned refuses to attach the Hand's authority to killing a child, resigns by setting his badge on the table, and leaves Robert threatening to execute him if he returns.
5. **VERIFIED:** Ned starts secret arrangements to send Sansa and Arya north immediately, before the household can pack everything. He weighs traveling via **Dragonstone** to question Stannis, whom he believes shared Arryn's investigation, but Stannis has not answered an earlier letter.
6. **VERIFIED:** Littlefinger visits, says he argued against paying for the costly Faceless Men and secured a less certain reward-based attempt to kill Daenerys. **HIS ACCOUNT:** that this served her interests better. He offers Ned a chance to meet one of Robert's bastards in a brothel that evening.

## Analytical stress tests

**Could killing Daenerys prevent wider war?** A consequentialist claim voiced by council members; **counterweight:** their predicted invasion depends on pregnancy outcome, Khal Drogo choosing to cross, ships, sieges and Westerosi alliances. **Ned is 'naive':** not necessarily; he identifies uncertainty and a deliberate ethical limit, then acts on it at high personal cost. **Pycelle connected to Arryn poison:** his alertness at the phrase is a useful lead but cannot independently establish murder.
