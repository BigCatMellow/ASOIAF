## Major sequence and stakes

1. **VERIFIED:** Three Night's Watch rangers track eight wildlings far north of the Wall. Will has seen them motionless by a makeshift shelter; Waymar Royce asks whether they could really have frozen when the Wall was weeping recently. Gared and Will want to turn back.
2. **VERIFIED:** Returning to the campsite, Will finds the apparent corpses gone while a valuable axe remains. Their disappearance is real within Will's immediate observations; **what happened to each body is UNKNOWN**.
3. **VERIFIED:** Several silent pale figures conceal themselves in the woods. One duels Waymar; the others watch until his ordinary steel blade shatters, then attack together. Their armor changes appearance against its surroundings; their weapons do not behave like forged human steel.
4. **VERIFIED:** Will hears one of the Others speaking unintelligibly, in a mocking manner as he perceives it. He observes intelligent coordination, but cannot establish their language, political organization, motives or origin.
5. **VERIFIED:** Will later finds Waymar's ruined body and tries to bring his broken sword as evidence; Waymar rises with an altered eye and strangles him. Reanimation is directly depicted, though the specific means and controller are not.

## Knowledge ledger

- **Gared:** Was ordered to remain with horses; the chapter does **not** show what he personally saw after separation.
- **Night's Watch/Winterfell:** No verified receipt of Will's account.

## Theory intersections and rebuttals

- **Others as intelligent adversaries:** supported by observed coordination; *not* automatically peaceful or bound by treaty. See [Others/Wall case](../../../../research/theories/cases/others-wall-pact.md).
- **The Others singled out Waymar because he looked like a Stark/Jon:** a possible fan interpretation, but the chapter supplies neither an explicit recognition nor prior identification.
- **The apparent dead wildlings were wights:** possible, **unverified**; they are absent when Will returns, and their transition is never shown.
