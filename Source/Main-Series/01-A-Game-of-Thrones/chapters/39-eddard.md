## Major events

1. **VERIFIED AS DREAM/REMEMBERED HISTORY:** Sedated Ned dreams of the **Tower of Joy**, a fight by a round tower in Dorne after Aerys's death. Seven ride with Ned against three Kingsguard: **Arthur Dayne (Dawn), Oswell Whent (black bat) and Lord Commander Gerold Hightower**. His companions are **Martyn Cassel, Theo Wull, Ethan Glover, Mark Ryswell, Howland Reed and Lord Dustin**.
2. **DREAM DIALOGUE:** The three Kingsguard say they were not at the Trident, fall of King's Landing, or surrender of Storm's End, and claim they remained where their oath placed them. **UNKNOWN:** the full historical spoken wording and which royal person/oath they guarded; this is Ned's old dream, not a preserved contemporary transcript.
3. **VERIFIED IN NED'S AWAKENED RECOLLECTION:** After the fight Ned pulled the tower down, used its stones to build **eight cairns**, and only **he and Howland Reed** rode away alive. Lyanna's bloody deathbed and a promise recur, but what she asked remains unrevealed.
4. **VERIFIED:** Ned wakes after **six days and seven nights** with shattered leg splinted, medicated by Pycelle. Alyn reports Jaime left the city, Jory/Wyl/Heward's remains are sent to Winterfell, and Sansa/Arya visited Ned, Arya silent with anger.
5. **VERIFIED:** Robert and Cersei visit; Ned says he ordered Catelyn to seize Tyrion. Robert tries to end the violence by ordering Tyrion released and a reconciliation with Jaime. Cersei repeats an inaccurate story that Ned's men attacked Jaime while Ned was drunk returning from a brothel. Robert has spoken with Littlefinger but does not investigate all evidence.
6. **VERIFIED:** Ned reveals the brothel trip was to see Robert's infant daughter **Barra**, whose mother is very young. Robert's reaction demonstrates indifference; his broken relationship with Cersei erupts when she questions his courage.
7. **VERIFIED:** Robert **strikes Cersei**, acknowledges the action is shameful, then displaces responsibility onto her. He says he won combat against Rhaegar yet lost Lyanna; he flees urgent discussion for a hunt.
8. **VERIFIED:** Robert restores Ned as Hand, forbids his departure to Winterfell, and threatens to appoint Jaime instead if Ned resigns again. Ned has not secured Robert's reversal of the Daenerys assassination order.

## Questions and competing readings

**R+L=J:** placement of Lyanna's secret and defense by royal Kingsguard can strengthen the theory; however, no biological father, birth, or child's custody is directly narrated. Alternative explanation: a deathbed political/family secret unrelated to the exact legal status of Jon. **Honor of the three Kingsguard:** their dream insistence on vows is relevant but not a verified declaration they guarded Rhaegar's newborn. **Robert:** shows self-awareness yet continues violence, avoidance and abdication of judgment; do not reduce the marriage to Cersei alone causing his conduct.

**Next tests:** Ned's later memory of promises, Dorne's chronology, Howland Reed references, Pycelle's treatment, and whether the Daenerys order actually reaches Essos.
