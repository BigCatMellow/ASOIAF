## Main narrative

1. **VERIFIED:** At Tywin's officers' dinner the camp prepares to engage a Stark host moving south from the Twins. **Kevan proposes Tyrion and four under-disciplined mountain clans in the vanguard under Gregor Clegane**. Tyrion thinks Tywin may hope to dispose of him, but that intent is **his suspicion**.
2. **VERIFIED:** Each clan keeps separate campfires; internal fighting over a sausage has led to actual killings. Tywin uses their disorder to humiliate Tyrion. **Bronn obtains Shae** from another soldier by threat; Tyrion begins paying for companionship and sex, while privately aware that her professed delight is probably performed.
3. **VERIFIED:** Tyrion receives the shy young squire **Podrick Payne**, distant cousin of Ilyn Payne. Shae and Pod help with mismatched borrowed armor when the army is awakened; Tywin's men report the advancing northern foot host less than a mile away.
4. **VERIFIED:** The Lannister right is ~**4,000 heavy cavalry**; center under Kevan, reserve under Tywin ~**5,000**, with Tyrion and clan fighters under Gregor on the **left by the river**, given insufficient protection. Tywin anticipates an undisciplined left **breaking**, tempting the enemy to overextend into a prepared counterstroke.
5. **VERIFIED:** In violent close combat Tyrion wounds/kills foes but is repeatedly imperiled; his **helmet spike becomes embedded in an attacking horse's belly**, killing or toppling it and trapping its rider. He suffers a serious elbow/arm injury; Bronn finds him afterward. Many clansmen die.
6. **VERIFIED:** Tywin admits he expected his expendable-looking left to collapse and deliberately **did not share the feigned-rout plan with Tyrion**. The attackers do not bite as decisively as he hoped. **Ser Addam Marbrand reports** that **Robb himself is not with the northern foot**: he crossed at the Twins with much of his cavalry for Riverrun. Tywin has won a tactical engagement but has been operationally deceived.

## Theory tests

**Tywin wishes Tyrion killed:** placement is knowingly dangerous, but battle plan gives a genuine military reason and there is no confession of filicide. **Tyrion's luck:** helmet design, terrain and confused combat explain survival without divine favor. **Robb ‘loses’ at Green Fork:** Bolton's foot experiences defeat while Robb wins a better position elsewhere; operational intent differs from tactical outcome.
