## Main causal sequence

1. **VERIFIED:** Drogo grows feverish and weak while traveling south; large **bloodflies** settle on him. He **falls from his horse**, a culturally catastrophic sign because a khal's authority depends on riding. Dany instructs the bloodriders to present a planned halt as his order, concealing the cause.
2. **VERIFIED:** Jorah inspects Drogo's wound: dried mud, black leaves, foul smell and **pus/black corruption** cover a festering slash. **DANY'S REPORT:** Drogo removed Mirri's burning lambskin poultice and accepted a soothing replacement from Dothraki herbwomen. This is important counterevidence to any **single-cause** assertion that Mirri definitely poisoned him; the original protocol was not followed.
3. **JORAH'S MILITARY WARNING:** The `kos` **Jhaqo and Pono** may replace Drogo, divide the khalasar and kill unborn Rhaego as a prophetic rival. Dany may be forced to Vaes Dothrak to join widowed `dosh khaleen`. He proposes flight through **Asshai** to Pentos.
4. **VERIFIED:** Mirri says the infected wound is beyond ordinary healing and offers blood magic learned from a **Shadow Lands bloodmage**. She states **death pays for life**, expressly says the price will not be **Dany's** death, but does **not** promise that Drogo will regain awareness or name the specific other life to be taken.
5. **VERIFIED:** Mirri orders Drogo's great **red stallion** slain; its blood floods the bath where Drogo lies. The **carcass** is dragged away to burn. Dany believes the horse is the price, but Mirri never explicitly equates it with the final life cost.
6. **VERIFIED:** Mirri burns aromatic red powder and chants behind a translucent blood-streaked tent; Dany sees what appear to be **additional shadows dancing**. **Source distinction:** Dany perceives shadow forms, not independently identified dead people or demons.
7. **VERIFIED:** Qotho, Haggo and Cohollo try to interrupt the forbidden ritual, and the conflict turns violent. **Qotho kills Quaro**; **Jorah kills Qotho** but loses part of his ear and suffers wounds. Bloodrider Cohollo's earlier kindness no longer guarantees loyalty when Drogo's authority collapses.
8. **VERIFIED:** Dany goes into sudden labor in the struggle. Jorah finds no conventional birthing women and **carries Dany into Mirri's tent**, despite the explicit injunction that no living man enter. Dany is physically unable to stop him. **UNKNOWN:** whether labor began because of magic, violence, normal gestation or some combination.

## Theory adjudication

**Rhaego's death “caused solely by Mirri”:** she warned of costly blood magic and concealed specificity, but Dany chose it and Jorah violated its entrance rule; mechanics remain unobserved. **Drogo infection “proves Mirri murdered him”:** discarded poultice and other healers intervene. **Later dragon hatching:** horse blood ritual, apparent dancing shadows, pregnancy and a prior failed egg-and-coals experiment form a plausible chain, but the chapter does not itself hatch a dragon.
