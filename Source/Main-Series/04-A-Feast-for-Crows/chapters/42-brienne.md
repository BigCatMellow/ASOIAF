## Key connected cases

**Rorge, Biter, Gendry and the children:** [AFFC 37](37-brienne.md) ended with Biter's attack, whose result is now explicitly provided **by Jeyne Heddle's testimony**: Gendry stabbed him through the back of the neck with a spear. Brienne lives, her arm and ribs badly damaged and face mutilated. Gendry returns to the forge; Jeyne gives care. Meribald was released, but **Dog's exact whereabouts and safety are not fully accounted for** by Gendry's phrase 'you killed the dog', apparently referring to the Hound impersonator. Rescue occurs through a hostile Brotherhood intervention, not a guaranteed deliverance to safety.

**The helm has three identities:** Sandor (former owner; Elder Brother AFFC31 claims he died), Rorge (murderer and Saltpans raider; Brienne kills him), then **Lem Lemoncloak** (seizes helmet from the corpse). This chapter directly confirms Rorge and Lem; the earlier cairn theft remains *testimony*, not a witnessed act. Thoros objects because adopting a symbol of monstrous acts perpetuates the same fear and ugliness. Lem claims it helps terrify enemies; his own wife/daughter loss gives human meaning to his rage without excusing threats against Podrick.

**Broken Brotherhood's leader and mechanics:** Thoros is alive, caring and deeply pessimistic. He states **Beric gave Catelyn his last kiss of life after she lay dead three days**, an explanation corroborating the reader's independent **ASOS epilogue recognition of Stoneheart** but not giving a complete medical mechanism. A child-facing orphan network, women, and children remain in the Brotherhood's orbit. The same organization that once protected commoners now rejects guest right, uses guilt by employer or kin, and contemplates hanging a child squire. Its moral degeneration is narrated from *within*.

**The material evidence against Brienne is real but its intended meaning is wrong:** Jaime's **Oathkeeper** with lion hilt, Tommen's sealed letter and Podrick's service to Tyrion all exist. Stoneheart interprets these as treason, dismissing the original Catelyn oath and Jaime's later protective acts. Her mutilated voice and questions of past fidelity mark a revenant with memories and vendetta, not the same political mercy or judgment that Brienne knew in life. She gives Brienne a binary trap—**kill Jaime or be hanged**. Brienne explicitly rejects the choice; she is taken with Hyle and Podrick to nooses, and the hanging begins. Final word is **unidentified**. A plea, acceptance or different word are competing readings: **no actual selection can be documented**.

**The sword-rimmed crown:** Stoneheart fingers a bronze circlet rimmed with iron swords matching one worn by Ryman Frey's companion in [AFFC 38 Jaime](38-jaime.md). This is a very strong **object recurrence**; the source has **no intermediate theft/transfer scene**. Track Ryman and the crown independently and don't assume a confirmed named executioner merely from the artifact.
