## Major sequence

- **VERIFIED:** Citadel novices and acolytes at the Quill and Tankard debate reports of living dragons across the sea. Independent sailors' stories agree in broad outline but differ in detail; nobody at the table offers direct confirmed sighting.
- **VERIFIED:** Alleras is an unusually accomplished new acolyte and precise archer, with a Summer Isles bow and ties to his mother's culture. His account of three dragon heads has interpretive force but cannot independently determine a future triad.
- **CHARACTER CLAIM:** Lazy Leo says Marwyn's glass candle burns. Others deride Marwyn's unconventional studies. The rumor must be checked against `AFFC 45 (Samwell)`, not promoted solely from Leo's statement.
- **VERIFIED:** Pate, frustrated by failure at the Citadel and eager to leave with Rosey, steals a key belonging to Archmaester Walgrave in exchange for an old gold dragon coin.
- **VERIFIED:** The stranger calling himself an alchemist is shown with an ordinary young face, hooked nose and faint cheek scar, then the exchange is followed by Pate's apparent fatal collapse. **UNKNOWN** at this point: the alchemist's true identity and use of the key.
