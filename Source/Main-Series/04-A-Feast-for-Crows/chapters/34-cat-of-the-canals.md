## Causal plot spine and character consequences

**Cat of the Canals is a working identity, not an erased Arya.** She performs daily physical labor for Brusco alongside his daughters, navigates the commercial channels and uses the trade tongue, concealment and personal contacts to gather three reportable facts on each dark moon. The House employs ordinary access, memory, performance and social intelligence. Yet she still has vivid wolf-scent dreams, a recurrent traumatic dream of failing to save her mother, bodily gestures the waif spots, memories of Lysa as an aunt and the Red Keep's cats, and an urge to punish a Night's Watch deserter in Arya Stark's name. **The training is already technically successful in parts and fundamentally unsuccessful in extinguishing her loyalties.**

**Dareon's death is a completed personal choice, not a Faceless Men assignment.** AFFC 26 Samwell established his direct abandonment of Sam, Gilly and sick Aemon, his plan to desert and Sam's unsuccessful assault. Here Dareon flaunts his purchased clothing at the Happy Port; Arya accompanies him into a dark alley and later gives Brusco boots. Reporting to the kindly man, she says the black singer was throat-cut and his body left in a canal, the boots taken, and identifies **Arya of House Stark** as the killer. The strike is not described at the moment it happens and there is no corpse inspection, but her admissions plus boots support a strongly grounded homicide. The House did not give an order to kill him and the kindly man makes the divergence of loyalties explicit.

**The poison lesson creates several narrow cross-book clues.** The waif says **sweetsleep** can calm, sedate or kill depending on dosage and is sweet enough to hide in prepared foods; **tears of Lys** supposedly mimics fatal bowel illness; **basilisk-blood paste** supposedly induces madness in warm-blooded creatures. These are claims by a knowledgeable speaker, not demonstrations performed on a patient in this chapter. The details connect to Petyr's use of sweetsleep proposals for Robert Arryn (AFFC 23), the Tears-of-Lys theory and Lysa's confession concerning Jon Arryn (ASOS 80), and the temple's earlier unmeasured black-pool additives (AFFC 22). **Do not conflate these separate substances or invent which one is later in Arya's milk.**

**Blindness is confirmed; its cause is open.** Immediately after Arya admits killing Dareon, the kindly man orders warm milk. It tastes faintly bitter and burnt; she dreams of a lone wolf moving through *Braavos* rather than roaming with her pack; she wakes unable to see. The timing strongly invites deliberate punishment or a training regimen, particularly after an earlier blind acolyte showed remarkable use of hearing/smell in AFFC 22. However **no substance is named, no one acknowledges drugging the milk, and no duration or future restoration is established**. The solitary rooftop wolf may be identity displacement or another skinchanging-related clue, but the text doesn't adjudicate either.
