## Major plot mechanics and real choices

**Military stalemate is not a mere numerical contest.** Daven holds three encampments with uneven loyalties, siege engines and a boom across the Red Fork. Brynden has expelled nonessential people and exhausted local provisions, and Daven *estimates* two years of stores remain. The besiegers obtain fish from the river but face horse fodder scarcity, Frey hoarding, defections and lynched foragers. A rapid storm assault may be possible but costs casualties, damages the estate Emmon claims and likely violates Jaime's prior promise to Catelyn. Jaime decides to request a peace meeting with the Blackfish at dawn **before convening a siege council**. He has not negotiated or won terms by the chapter's end.

**Claimed legal authority is not possession.** Emmon carries a **royally signed and sealed grant of Riverrun**, but Brynden and his men continue to hold the gates. Jaime explicitly says the document grants Riverrun and its lands and incomes, **not command over the Trident**, which remains nominally subject to **Petyr Baelish, Lord Paramount based at Harrenhal**. Don't confuse Riverrun's disputed holder, the crown's riverlands overlord or the military besiegers. Emmon's wish to protect 'his' walls conflicts with Daven's ability to take them. The separate **Faith Militant decree** is not demonstrated simply because a different Tommen-sealed parchment is present here.

**The Frey marriage and hostage puzzle is explosive.** Roslin's pregnancy could produce a rival **male Tully heir**, while Frey custody of Edmure and their repeated gallows demonstration make his survival fragile. House Westerling has **Gawen's wife and children, including Robb's widow Jeyne**, inside Riverrun and fears retaliation against them if Edmure is hanged. Genna suggests a *future daughter* of Roslin might marry her son Ty, but this is conditional on sex at birth and Walder Frey's approval. Brynden's own surviving claim and a proposed claim through **young Robert Arryn** further complicate succession; no future contender has actually laid such a claim here. Meanwhile Lancel's intended renunciation unsettles the Darry arrangements, where **Jeyne, Cleos's widow and Lady Mariya's younger full sister**, has a genealogical grievance.

**The besieging alliance's legitimacy and loyalty are weak.** Ryman humiliates Edmure and frustrates Brynden, but an army that violated guest right cannot easily promise safe terms. Daven resents Frey interference and wants several dead. Recalcitrant riverlords accept the king's peace yet don't necessarily send troops; some banners are missing and forager murders may involve insurgents, defectors, northern stragglers or lordly agents. Jaime suspects Beric's men partly from hanging patterns, yet the reader of the ASOS epilogue and AFFC 30 knows **Catelyn/Stoneheart leads a changed Brotherhood-related hanging faction**. There is no direct witness connecting the particular **crabapple corpses** to Stoneheart, Beric, Tytos Blackwood or any named lord.

**Jaime's conscience is mixed rather than redeemed.** He favors negotiation and claims peaceful settlement would keep his vow to Catelyn, but still threatens to kill Brynden if he refuses and puts Tommen's oath first. He treats Pia more protectively than formerly while nevertheless **lies to Genna and Emmon about Cleos's death**, sparing their feelings by inventing an honorable burial. His dark Cersei/Moon Boy dream reveals violence and jealousy that are not acts. Nightly Ilyn Payne training leaves visible wounds and may eventually restore competence, but he is still repeatedly disarmed.

**Genna's final contrast is characterization, not a paternity revelation.** She loves Tywin for challenging her childhood betrothal, yet sees his harsh legacy clearly: Kevan served him, Tygett competed, Gerion joked, and Genna herself had little choice. Her judgment that **Tyrion is Tywin's true son** means resemblance of character, political instincts and personal flaws; Jaime is also Tywin's biological son on the established family record. She remembers Tywin himself withdrawing from her for months after that judgment. Her warning raises a major question about what sort of Lannister leadership can follow Tywin.

## Cross-book audits and candidate theory questions

- **AFFC 27/30 Jaime:** He was sent to bring Riverrun under crown control and had invoked his Catelyn oath. A specific peace banner, named negotiator **Jon Bettley** and dawn meeting are now ordered. AFFC 30's conditional Pia/Peck arrangement has progressed to their intimate relationship as Jaime reports hearing them in his tent. Lancel's intention to join the restored Faith reaches Daven and Genna, and the Frey marriage's practical collapse is visible to Jaime; this does not prove completed church vows/annulment.
- **ASOS Epilogue / AFFC 30:** The Frey Red Wedding conspiracy and the Stoneheart-led hangings supply **reader-only intelligence** about an anti-Frey execution campaign. Jaime and Daven know Beric and Thoros rumors, but cannot identify the specific killers of foragers, whose **crabapple markers** add one distinctive detail not seen in the ASOS epilogue. The Dead Lady's command does not imply every noose is hers.
- **AFFC 28/30/32:** Genna's alarm about Cersei rearming the Faith echoes Pycelle's explicit objection and Jaime's surprise at Lancel's recruitment. Reconstruct the early Targaryen Faith conflict from separate original sources before promoting her remembered bounties to precisely audited history. The Tyrell/Euron naval emergency competes with the siege for attention, but these specific characters do not discuss it in AFFC 33.
- **Lannister family dynamics:** Genna offers independent family testimony that Tywin once defended her against an unequal match arranged by Tytos and Walder. It complicates simplistic claims that Tywin was never protective, without excusing subsequent abuses. Daven's reluctance to usurp Kevan as Warden and Jaime's respect for Kevan expose Cersei's isolation.
- **Baelish and the Riverlands:** Tommen's royal grant to Emmon reveals Baelish is nominal **Lord Paramount of the Trident**; it does not show Baelish administrating, collecting incomes or delivering military support. Distinguish his titular authority from castle possession and the Vale schemes witnessed in AFFC Sansa chapters.
- **Riverrun fealty and the Blackwood–Bracken war:** The absence of Mallister, Ryger and Paege from the besiegers is as significant as the known Blackwood/Bracken conflict. Verify each family's specific opportunities before treating absenteeism as membership in a single rebel conspiracy.
- **Jaime's missing sword hand:** The visceral Payne bout tests possible practical recovery against unearned optimism. Do not infer a magical second hand or guaranteed duel triumph from dreams where the gold limb works.
- **The successor-to-Tywin hypothesis:** Genna's statement predicts possible rivalry between Jaime's military honor and Tyrion's strategic/sarcastic Tywin-like traits, but is not evidence of adoption, bastard status, supernatural lineage or Tyrion's location. Preserve ordinary personality resemblance as the default.

1. Track the **dawn meeting with Brynden**, actual offered terms, safe-conduct and whether Jaime obeys his Catelyn oath in effect rather than by technicality.
2. Verify who commands and moves the Frey camps, bridge/ford lines and river boom; determine whether a small group can cross despite the siege before assuming no escape is possible.
3. Track **Roslin's pregnancy**, Edmure's survival and status, the new child's sex and inheritance claim, and any attempts to wed Genna's descendants.
4. Trace **Gawen Westerling, Jeyne and kin** after siege negotiations and whether they can be used as hostages or safely departed.
5. Investigate the **crabapple hanging signature** separately from red-priest fire reports and Stoneheart's Oldstones nooses.
6. Check absent Mallister, Bracken, Ryger and Paege forces one by one; determine whether omission is disobedience, competing wars, communications failure or secret coordination.
7. Trace **Genna's Darry genealogy** and the impact of Lancel's attempted renunciation on Jeyne Darry's children, Martyn Lannister and the Freys.
8. Test Genna's Faith Militant chronology and debt warning against historical chapters rather than a present legal document.
9. Track **Kevan's westward journey** and what he knows about Cersei/Jaime; Genna's suspicion is not proof of any exact secret.
10. Preserve **Genna's Tyrion-is-Tywin's-son statement as a character-identity judgment**, not unsupported speculation about Jaime's biological parents.
