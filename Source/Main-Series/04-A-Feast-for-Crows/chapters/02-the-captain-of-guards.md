# AFFC 2 — The Captain of Guards (Areo Hotah): Oberyn's death, Sand Snake plans and Doran's bloodless crackdown

- Record role: `RESEARCH / SEQUENTIAL CLOSE READING`
- Status: `FIRST PASS COMPLETE — INDEPENDENT CHALLENGE OPEN`
- Primary source: complete [AFFC 2 — The Captain of Guards](../text/003-the-captain-of-guards.md), read 2026-10-18; [evidence rules](../../../../SOURCES.md).
- POV: **Areo Hotah**, whose loyalty to Doran is direct but whose access to **Doran's thoughts and covert policy** is limited.

## Scene and decision chronology

1. **The chapter opens at the Water Gardens with overripe blood oranges.** They burst on pale pink marble by **Doran Martell's wheeled chair**, made by **Maester Caleotte** from ebony, iron and goose-down cushioning. The recurring rotting fruit is observed physical detail and an apt image of unused time, but not a magical omen.
2. **Doran is physically incapacitated by advanced gout.** His left knee is swollen like an apple and right like a melon; toes dark reddish and extremely tender. He is **52 years old**, and the painful condition prevents reliable walking or climbing. Hotah cannot always distinguish physical pain from grief.
3. **Doran delays reading the sealed raven of Oberyn's death.** He holds it through daylight and into night while watching children play; a candle is brought at **moonrise** before he opens it. The delay is **direct observation**, not proof he knew the precise contents beforehand.
4. **Obara Sand arrives furious and demands war.** Hotah recognizes her rapid step and confronts her with his **six-foot mountain-ash longaxe**. She wears **whip, round shield, worn leather** and left her spear outside. She is **about thirty and the oldest Sand Snake**.
5. **Oberyn's death has widespread political repercussions.** Obara describes thousands heading toward the Boneway to accompany **Ellaria Sand** bringing his remains, packed septs, red temple fires and public mourning from Sunspear to the sands. These numbers and details are **her excited report**; later street unrest supports the general mood.
6. **Doran differentiates judicial combat from murder.** He insists Oberyn died during a legally sanctioned trial by battle; Obara rejects that moral classification. This is a conflict over **legal form versus vengeance**, not denial that Gregor directly killed him.
7. **Obara proposes two invasion routes.** She wants the **Prince's Pass force** while **Nymeria** uses the **Boneway host**, then to take marcher castles and **sack Oldtown** without holding it. Doran points out the inability to hold the city; Obara openly prioritizes blood over durable gain.
8. **Doran has written to Tywin regarding Gregor's head.** His expectation that Tywin will provide that retribution reflects a **time-lagged diplomatic belief**: from ASOS 77 the reader knows **Tywin is dead**, a fact Doran has not yet learned in this chapter. Do not imply a fresh Tywin response after his death.
9. **Obara recalls Oberyn claiming her from her mother.** Her mother, described as an **Oldtown prostitute**, disputed paternity. Oberyn offered the child a choice between a spear and remaining with her mother, assaulted the mother and took Obara after she picked the spear. Obara recounts her mother later dying through drink. This is **her traumatic family testimony**, not consent freely given by a child with equal power.
10. **Doran uses the playing children as a moral counterweight.** He insists Obara look at boys and girls of similar ages as those who would suffer from war. It is a repeated concrete choice to contemplate civilians, not evidence he is indifferent to Elia and Oberyn.
11. **The family age gap is exact.** Doran recalls infant brothers **Mors and Olyvar** died, that he was **nine when Elia was born prematurely**, and **Oberyn arrived the next year**; Doran had become an adult by the time his siblings played in the pools. His grief is visible despite restraint.
12. **Areo Hotah's own history has parallels to chosen servitude.** He was sold as a poor youngest son of a Norvosi family to the **bearded priests of Norvos**, branded with an **axe emblem on his chest**, and swore **Serve, Obey, Protect** at sixteen when he 'wed his axe'. This is firsthand memory of institutionalized violence and duty.
13. **Norvosi details are distinctive and retrievable.** Hotah remembers **Noom, Narrah and Nyel** (three bells), **wintercake with ginger, pine nuts and cherry**, **nahsa** fermented goat milk with honey, and the **Sinner's Steps**. His old culture remains vivid despite decades guarding Martells.
14. **Doran decides to leave the secluded Water Gardens for Sunspear.** He believes public authority needs to be seen despite his weakness; **Caleotte and Hotah** warn the palace and shadow city are full of watchers, steps and political risk.
15. **The current Vale/royal hostage connection is Myrcella.** **Princess Myrcella** resides in Dorne under the protection of **Ser Arys Oakheart**, whose letters may inform Cersei. Hotah fears a future clash with Arys. **No actual duel occurs** in this chapter.
16. **Hotah brings 20 of the 30 guards** from the Gardens, with **five ahead, five behind and five on either side of Doran's litter**. The prince eats and says farewell to favorite children including **a Dalt boy, Blackmont children and an orphan whose father traded on the Greenblood**, delaying travel to midday.
17. **The three-league coastal route contrasts Dorne's two political worlds.** The secluded Water Gardens' pale marble, pools, oranges and shared children contrast with **Sunspear's dusty, overcrowded shadow city and mudbrick rings**. The **Threefold Gate** aligns the three winding walls for direct palace passage.
18. **Nymeria Sand intercepts the litter en route.** **About 25**, born to a noblewoman of **old Volantis**, she wears **lilac robes, a long black braid bound with red-gold**, and brings **a dozen spearmen**. Her elegance and hidden knives contrast with Obara's openly carried weapon.
19. **Nymeria proposes assassination of four political targets.** She wants **Jaime and Cersei for Elia's two children, Tywin for Elia, and Tommen for Oberyn**, invoking Stannis's accusation of royal incest. Doran explicitly reminds her **Tommen is personally innocent**. The plan is **proposed, not executed**.
20. **Nymeria has heard Oberyn repeatedly struck Gregor and expects poison to kill him.** The report is via **Ser Daemon's raven**, and she thinks Tywin will only deliver what Oberyn has already accomplished. This strengthens a poison hypothesis before Tyene's stronger confirmation later in the chapter.
21. **Doran explains Oberyn's authorized mission.** He asked his brother to **assess Joffrey and his council, find friends, and learn about Elia's fate without unduly provoking Tywin**. This is Doran's retrospective claim about instructions, not proof Oberyn promised compliance.
22. **Nymeria rejects waiting seventeen years longer.** Her statement records the Martells' long grievance; she is not shown issuing orders to her intended assassination partner **Tyene** yet.
23. **The royal arrival is met by actual hostile crowds.** Commoners chant for arms and revenge, toss **oranges, lemons and limes**, and crowd the palace approaches. A guard is struck in the eye; Hotah intercepts a boy carrying a spoiled pomegranate. This directly confirms real anger, though not every Dornish person's preference.
24. **Arianne Martell receives her father at Sunspear.** She wears **copper sun ornaments and colored silk**, accompanied by **Ricasso, Ser Manfrey Martell, young Maester Myles** and the court. **Myrcella and Arys** are present. She tells Doran **Tyene awaits in the throne room**.
25. **The Tower of the Sun holds two symbolic thrones.** One is decorated with the **Martell spear**, the other **Rhoynish sun**, emphasizing Dorne's dual heritage. Hotah must physically carry Doran up the steps.
26. **Tyene presents innocence while presenting highly dangerous proposals.** The daughter of a former **septa**, Tyene is blond, blue-eyed, wears **pale blue and Myrish lace**, holds **golden embroidery needles** and presents a portrait of Oberyn. Hotah sees 'viper eyes' despite her different coloring; this is **his analogy**, not biological verification of venom.
27. **Tyene confirms inside-family knowledge of Gregor's poisoned wound.** She states explicitly that she knows the poison Oberyn used and that **it is exceptionally slow and agonizing**. This is **knowledge claimed by a poison-capable daughter**, much stronger than Tyrion's earlier visual guess or Pycelle's unexplained dead leeches, but the exact poison **is not named or chemically tested**.
28. **Her plan is to provoke a Dornish defensive war.** Tyene wants **Myrcella crowned as elder sibling**, thereby provoking Lannister/Tyrell retaliation into Dornish mountain passes and desert; she would then fight on favorable home terrain. This is materially different from Obara's march north and Nymeria's targeted murders.
29. **Doran corrects the succession argument's jurisdiction.** He says equal elder-child succession is **Dornish law**, not automatically the law of the **Iron Throne**. Tyene notes Myrcella is physically in Dorne and proposes **white-gold crowns and marriage to Trystane**. **Neither marriage nor coronation has occurred**.
30. **A dangerous gesture is intercepted.** When Tyene raises her hand, Hotah orders her away from the dais; after Doran blesses her, **Caleotte examines and smells the prince's hand for scratches or substance**. He finds **no scratches**. This is strong on-page indication guards and maester fear **contact poisoning**, but **no poison attack is proven to have happened**.
31. **Doran asks for milk of the poppy** after the encounter and then quietly assesses whether his guards will obey him personally. Hotah promises obedience and to bring disloyal heads, but Doran explicitly requests **no heads and no blood**.
32. **Doran orders all eight Sand Snakes into custody.** **Tyene, Nymeria and Obara** are to be secured first; the younger girls by **Ellaria** are also to be kept safely. **Sarella, nearly twenty, is away**, and Doran orders her left to her 'game' unless she returns. **This is a custody order, not confirmation that all were successfully arrested**.
33. **Doran anticipates anger, not public gratitude.** He knows the political price of detaining Oberyn's daughters while mourning is still underway, but chooses preventing unauthorized war over immediate popularity.
34. **End state:** Dorne's grieving court is politically split between revenge, assassination, a Myrcella succession provocation and Doran's enforced restraint. The immediate actual policy is **quiet arrest of the Sand Snakes**, not alliance-breaking invasion or Myrcella's crowning.
