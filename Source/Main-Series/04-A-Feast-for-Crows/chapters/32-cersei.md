## Cross-book source junctions

- **AFFC 29 The Reaver:** Victarion confirms Euron's invasion tactics and exactly which four new Shield lords were appointed. The Serry heir was pushed from a captured vessel alive but not found afterward. Willas's western approach is well supported; the thousand-ship count remains an estimate. Cersei's Stannis/Euron conspiracy is particularly poorly supported beside Euron's explicit competing designs on Daenerys and the Reach.
- **AFFC 28 Cersei and AFFC 30 Jaime:** The royal navy was imagined as completed naval counterweight, but now only a single new dromond is reportedly ready. In religion, Cersei's verbal debt-remission bargain has progressed toward Pycelle's draft papers; armed Poor Fellows already guard Lancel's sept and he reports a High Septon letter claiming Tommen consent. Distinguish each stage from an examined executed decree.
- **AFFC 17/24 Cersei and AFFC 32:** The Osney plan to compromise Margaery is still future; Cersei calculates removing Loras makes coercing Margaery easier. Qyburn previously received authority to study dying Gregor and reported a skull readied for Dorne. The impossible-weight armor and new unkillable champion merit **Gregor/Robert Strong case investigation**, not an identity verdict.
- **AFFC 24 Cersei → AFFC 32:** Falyse and Balman previously received Cersei's solicitation to kill Bronn through an apparent hunting accident. This chapter provides the disastrous **reported** result: an honorable-challenge fantasy, horse-stabbing tactic, Balman's death and Bronn's takeover. Cersei knows her premise that Tyrion hides with Bronn was weak, yet uses her failed allies as scapegoats.
- **AFFC 12/24 Cersei → AFFC 32 (Senelle and the puppeteers):** Taena previously supplied an allegation of Senelle spying. Cersei sent Senelle to Qyburn; later Qyburn received puppeteers punished for a satirical lion-and-dragon performance. The present statement that the puppeteers are 'used up' and Cersei's delivery of Falyse **confirm a pattern of supplying vulnerable detainees**, without revealing medical or experimental outcomes.
- **AFFC 31 Brienne → AFFC 32 Cersei:** The Quiet Isle's care for assaulted Saltpans civilians provides a near-immediate contrast to the queen handing an assaulted, distressed ally to Qyburn. This is a textual and moral juxtaposition, not proof those institutions have direct contact.
- **Jaime/Cersei intimate contrast:** AFFC 30 Jaime learns details of her affair with Lancel and struggles with old abuse memories; AFFC 32 Cersei revisits Robert's assaults and commits a new intentional boundary violation with Taena. Both POVs are psychologically revealing without making either narrator a complete judge of the other.

## Competing interpretations and research tests

1. **Dragonstone:** Track Loras's actual departure, castle defenders, duration, battlefield outcome and all independently attested casualties. The queen's desire to sacrifice him is not itself a completed casualty.
2. **Euron's strategy:** Compare his reported ship count with the Iron Fleet, Shield lordships, river geography and onward plans. His western route is independently corroborated; his agreement with Stannis is not.
3. **Qyburn's unnamed knight:** Verify physical identity, size and armor use, status of Gregor's body after the purported head delivery, and who sees the champion. Oppose both automatic resurrection assumptions and overly confident dismissal of the clues.
4. **Faith Militant:** Find the royal seal, financial ledger entry, operational hierarchy and actual enforcement powers. Draft papers and an alleged letter of consent support movement but not every legal step.
5. **Stokeworth:** Verify Bronn's effective control, Lollys's inherited title, Balman's final outcome, Tanda's health and whether Falyse can emerge from custody. The queen's refusal to fight around the capital is a real practical constraint.
6. **Qyburn's human subjects:** Trace Senelle, the satirical puppeteers and Falyse separately; do not infer precise surgical procedures or deaths from ominous euphemisms alone.
7. **Taena's position:** Seek independent witnesses to Senelle's alleged betrayal, Taena's information channels, her former lover's identity and Russell's possible move to court. State sexual consent and loyalty with source limitations.
8. **Violence and self-justification:** Compare Robert's deliberate abuses and Cersei's exploitation of Taena without making one cancel out the other. Taena's own unobserved thoughts constrain conclusions.
9. **Redwyne/dromond logistics:** Watch for `Sweet Cersei` actually leaving with Loras, refitting and crew costs, and the moment siege ships can be released.
10. **Reach casualty identification:** Do not treat deaths of Hewett, Chester or Talbert, or Grimm's captivity, as independently body-confirmed simply because the queen is told.
