# AFFC 29 — The Reaver: the Shields fall, Euron divides his captains, and Victarion accepts the dragon mission

- Record role: `RESEARCH / SEQUENTIAL CLOSE READING`.

- Primary source: complete [AFFC 29 The Reaver](../text/030-the-reaver.md), read 2026-10-18; [evidence standards](../../../../SOURCES.md).
- Scope: Entire Victarion POV, from the naval clash off the Mander through the negotiation in Euron's captured bedchamber. Categorize observed military action as VERIFIED; self-serving accounts, religious and sorcerous explanations, remembered history and offers as CHARACTER CLAIM; nonliteral predictions as THEORY/UNKNOWN. Do not conflate victim's silence with consent or assume Euron's word is trustworthy.

## Chronological close reading: battle, occupation, banquet and private commission

1. **The *Iron Victory* rams a Shield Islands longship displaying House Serry's white roses**—a red escutcheon bearing a white rose and a mast banner with a gold rose on green. The ironborn break oars and board at close range. This is an actual battle, not simply Euron's untested promise of southern plunder.
2. **Victarion boards ahead of his men in heavy armor and the kraken helm.** He views the defenders' lack of plate and reluctance to risk drowning with contempt. Most have no armor; a few wear sewn scales. The equipment disparity matters more than his contemptuous judgment of their courage.
3. Victarion kills several defenders using axe, shield and strength. **Wulfe One-Ear, Ragnor Pyke and Nute the Barber** subsequently reach the enemy deck and fight alongside him. His first-person exhilaration must be recorded alongside the deaths.
4. **The enemy captain identifies himself as Ser Talbert Serry**, son and heir of the lord of Southshield. Victarion seeks to fight a worthy highborn opponent rather than concentrating on captures.
5. **Talbert moves quickly and lands blows on Victarion's helm and thigh**, but the ironborn's armor protects him. Talbert's defense and swordsmanship are credible; Victarion is not fighting an incompetent or unarmed adversary.
6. **Talbert's shield breaks and Victarion's axe lodges in the rail.** Victarion catches the knight's blade with his armored hand and throws the sword into the sea, suffering a cut to his **palm** despite his gauntlet.
7. **Victarion shoves Talbert overboard** after telling him to retrieve the sword. Nobody later confirms retrieving the knight. A valuable ransoming opportunity is squandered; Talbert's **death by drowning is likely but not directly confirmed**.
8. **Some defenders surrender and others resist until killed**. Victarion praises the latter while framing quarter-seeking as contemptible. The scene verifies mass casualties and captures, not a universal ethic shared by the dead men.
9. **Heavy plate largely protects Victarion from ordinary attacks**, but joints and hands are acknowledged weak points. His injured palm is a concrete wound even though he initially minimizes it. The later mission will not start with an entirely uninjured captain.
10. **The fighting ends with Iron Fleet victory, burning and sinking ships, floating dead and survivors**, some southern longships retreating toward the Mander. Victarion deliberately lets the fleeing ships carry news of the defeat.
11. Victarion wonders whether **Talbert Serry** might still be alive for ransom; nobody saw his fate after the fall. His religious wish that the knight feast in the Drowned God's halls is not evidence of the actual afterlife or a confirmed corpse.
12. **Victarion rewards Ragnor Pyke with the captured longship**, appoints crew and commands records of captured banners, noble captives and vessels. The flags are intended for a future hall display; their later installation is not depicted.
13. **Victarion orders the dying thrown overboard**, granting a quick throat-cut to those who plead for mercy. This is a directly voiced lethal order, separate from claims that he honors brave foes.
14. **Victarion privately recognizes his victory will increase Euron's authority.** The Crow's Eye stayed behind in Oakenshield's castle while Victarion fought at sea; the result is not a simple Euron battlefield feat.
15. **Euron has already distributed conspicuous wealth**—silver, gold, exotic goods, fine textiles, Valyrian-steel daggers, purported unicorn horns and objects described as Valyrian antiquities. Victarion knows the political potency of these gifts; individual origin stories and enchanted properties are unverified.
16. Victarion explicitly contrasts **Euron's newly granted territorial conquest with gifts of imported plunder**: holding the Shields is more effective at winning captains than a distant promise. Victarion envies Euron's ability to appropriate credit.
17. **Victarion considers obedience almost instinctive**, recalling how he followed Balon and accepted Balon's sons as future heirs. He now resents Euron even though the kingsmoot made the latter king. This deference is an exploitable psychological and institutional mechanism.
18. Victarion descends into his cabin and has sex with **the tongueless dusky woman** Euron gave him. Her mutilation, enforced transfer and lack of speech make **consent and wishes unknowable**, not affirmative. Blood appearing on her body comes from Victarion's hand wound, not a claimed injury to her.
19. **The woman washes his wounded palm with boiled vinegar** and later binds it with linen; his pain worsens under treatment. The medical description is practical and leaves prognosis unresolved.
20. Victarion explains the **Mander's strategic geography**: broad and shallow, with snags and sandbars, navigable upstream to Bitterbridge by shallow-draft ironborn longships while most seagoing craft seldom pass Highgarden.
21. **The four Shield Islands historically controlled the river mouth**, defended by watchtowers, signal beacons, shore-based fleets and mustering farmers and fishers. Victarion frames their creation as a response to ancient ironborn raiding, not a random ceremonial name.
22. **Euron's operation used a decoy**, sending **Torwold Browntooth and the Red Oarsman** up the Mander with **twelve fast longships** to draw Shield defenders away from their own harbors.
23. **The main force attacked in the evening**, using the westward sun behind it and favorable winds to obscure its approach. Greyshield, Greenshield and Southshield fell before sunrise; Oakenshield lasted about half another day.
24. **Retreating Shield longships found Victarion's Iron Fleet blocking the river mouth**. This completes a military pincer through a prearranged diversion, not proof that winds or divination caused the victory.
25. **Sailors whisper that Euron's three alleged enslaved wizards and blood sacrifice appeased the Storm God**, giving him unusual following winds and confidence sailing out of sight of land. These are rumors attributed to crew; ordinary seamanship and a calculated attack time are strong alternatives.
26. Victarion calls Euron's plan successful but insists that **steel and men, not wizards, won the battle**. His claim does not settle whether any magic assisted; neither the wizards' alleged spell nor lack of one is directly demonstrated.
27. **Victarion privately speculates about arranging Euron's death through a subordinate** to avoid religious condemnation as a kinslayer. He considers Nute's thrown axe and trusted men including Wulfe and Andrik. No assassination order is issued.
28. **Aeron's conversation after the kingsmoot is remembered in detail**: he says crowning Euron was a mistake and offers to remove the driftwood crown and put it on Victarion. This is a proposed coup, not a successful deposition.
29. Victarion replies that the kingsmoot chose Euron and Aeron himself crowned him. Aeron counters that **wizards may have enchanted the gathering** so it could not hear the Drowned God's voice. The enchantment remains **Aeron's explanation**, not verified magical voter manipulation.
30. **Victarion and Aeron both recall fear of Euron's horn**. Its physically disturbing kingsmoot blast was observed earlier in AFFC 19; the claim that it can command dragons is still not tested.
31. **Aeron declares he will raise the common islanders, fishers and farmers against the captains' choice**, visiting Great Wyk, Harlaw, Orkmont and Pyke. He distinguishes the kingsmoot's electorate from the broader population, widening the dispute over legitimacy.
32. **Aeron disappears from Old Wyk by the following morning**, even his drowned men lacking an explanation. Euron reportedly laughs when told. The passage leaves **Aeron's location, capture and survival UNKNOWN** and does not prove Euron ordered any abduction.
33. **Baelor Blacktyde tried to leave the kingsmoot without acknowledging Euron**; the Iron Fleet blocked departure, *Nightflyer* was seized and Blacktyde was taken in chains.
34. **Victarion reports Euron's men cut Blacktyde into seven pieces** in a ritualized mockery of his Seven worship. This is retrospectively reported by Victarion; it contrasts with Euron's outward claims to religious tolerance and Blacktyde's earlier antiwar position.
35. **Euron gave Victarion the mutilated woman as a reward for his cooperation** and threatened to kill her if he refused. Victarion recognizes the coercive gift but still treats her as his possession.
36. **The dusky woman reminds Victarion of his murdered wife**; he earlier disclosed that Euron violated her and **Victarion himself beat her to death**. The wife's killing remains **Victarion's act**, despite his attempts to transfer responsibility to Euron.
37. After the battle Victarion **leaves a five-ship rear guard**—*Iron Victory*, *Grief*, *Lord Dagon*, *Iron Wind* and *Maiden's Bane*—to retrieve survivors and watch losses while most ships go to Oakenshield.
38. **The ironborn count six ships lost and thirty-eight captured**, with *Hardhand* seen sinking after its rammed target dragged it down. These are reported battlefield counts, not a confirmed tally of the complete Shield Islands maritime forces or total deaths.
39. **Victarion believes Asha escaped with her crew the night Euron was crowned**, and hopes she will marry a northern lord safely away from Euron. This is his patriarchal projection, not an observed account of where she now is.
40. **At Lord Hewett's Town, ships, stolen cargo and provisions accumulate** in a harbor larger than Lordsport. Three large cogs and a dozen smaller ships load booty and food; hundreds of longships occupy the piers and shoreline.
41. **The town has been violently sacked**. Doors and shutters are broken, bodies fill streets, and only the sept is burned. Survivors try to bury their dead; Victarion judges land burial irreligious, showing his detached view of civilian suffering.
42. **The *Silence* appears with its mouthless female figurehead**, whose pearl eyes Victarion finds haunting. He associates it with Euron's mutilation of women's mouths; the figurehead's gaze is his subjective impression, not demonstrated sentience.
43. **Women and children—specifically widows and orphans—are bound by rope and readied for sale abroad as slaves.** Victarion protests because this violates his understanding of ironborn custom, not because he rejects captive forced labor.
44. **Victarion distinguishes captives taken as thralls from bought or sold chattel**, claiming thralls' children are free if given to the Drowned God. Nute argues the practical difference does not matter to the conquered. This is a legal/cultural clash **inside an already coercive system**.
45. **A guard cites Euron's decree authorizing sales**. Victarion's objection receives no remedy; the immediate shipment remains visibly underway. The fleet's strategy is turning conquered civilians into marketable voyage finance.
46. **Rodrik the Reader warns that taking the Shields gains only exposed islands and the enmity of House Tyrell**, not an easily defensible economic prize. He predicts a concentrated mainland response and rejects plunder as adequate strategy.
47. **Dunstan Drumm notes Randyll Tarly's Valyrian-steel sword *Heartsbane***. Victarion imagines taking it; Drumm's ancestral *Red Rain* is already confirmed in AFFC 19, allowing a contrast between a real blade and weapon-boasting.
48. **Rodrik points out that Euron ordered Lord Hewett's ravens permitted to fly**, and infers Euron **wants** the Reach to marshal in response. The existence of the instruction is **Rodrik's report**; Euron's deeper objective is **Rodrik's inference**, not Euron's on-page confession.
49. **Euron's followers display exotic stolen valuables and enslaved hostages at the feast**, while Victarion and Nute receive martial glory but comparatively little booty. Unequal loot distribution generates resentment among the very captains doing the fighting.
50. **Victarion sits with Ralf the Limper rather than Euron**, and the Limper suggests a shield island lordship would reward Victarion. He is susceptible to a place in a new political hierarchy despite contempt for Euron's promises.
51. **Hotho Harlaw tells Victarion that Ser Harras Harlaw alone took Grimston by fighting successive challengers**; two reportedly yielded, seven fell, and a septon persuaded Lord Grimm to surrender. The duel sequence is Hotho's story; the subsequent gift of Greyshield establishes Harras as a major beneficiary.
52. **Harras bears the Valyrian-steel *Nightfall*.** Victarion previously fought him in childhood and remembers his friendship with **Rodrik Greyjoy**, Balon's eldest son, who died at Seagard. Personal relationships and inherited swords matter in separating ironborn factions.
53. **Hotho expects that making Harras lord of Greyshield will clear Hotho's own path to inheriting Rodrik the Reader's Harlaw lordship**. This is **Hotho's interested succession calculation**, not proof the Reader has selected him or that Greyshield cancels every hereditary claim.
54. **Lady Hewett and seven daughters or daughters-in-law are forced to serve the banquet**, while Lord Hewett is bound to his own chair and gagged. The survivors cannot freely refuse their humiliations.
55. **Falia Flowers**, Hewett's bastard daughter, sits on Euron's lap and appears to welcome his attention. Euron promises her access to the highborn women's clothing, then **orders the serving women stripped naked**. Their forced obedience and the youngest girl's tears establish abusive domination; Falia's present cooperation does not demonstrate secure future status or freely chosen equality.
56. **Victarion recalls Euron humiliating him and his wife**, but contrasts killing an enemy with dishonoring a defeated house. The moral distinction is undermined by Victarion's own killing and abuse of people; the passage illuminates his selective code.
57. **Euron publicly proclaims the Four Shields only the first morsel of Westeros** and distributes the islands as lordships, converting conquest into patronage and dividing the strength of rival captains.
58. **New lords are named:** **Harras Harlaw—Greyshield; Andrik the Unsmiling—Southshield; Maron Volmark—Greenshield; Nute the Barber—Oakenshield.** These are witnessed appointments, distinct from the future ability of each appointee to hold the castle.
59. **Victarion recognizes the partisan logic of those choices**: Harras was Rodrik the Reader's chosen heir, Andrik the power behind Dunstan Drumm, Maron a youth with maternal Black Harren descent, and Nute one of Victarion's trusted champions.
60. **Victarion orders Nute to refuse but cannot prevent acceptance.** The Barber is thrilled at gaining land and rank. Victarion sees the appointment as Euron stealing his own man, not simply rewarding service.
61. **Euron orders the fleet to load grain, beef, water, sheep, goats and other provisions** for a coming eastward expedition; healthy wounded can row, more injured men remain to hold islands. The logistics are visible orders rather than a proven completed provisioning program.
62. **Rodrik challenges the plan to take a mass fleet to Slaver's Bay**. He cites long voyage times, the Redwyne Straits, dangerous Dornish coast, Stepstones pirates, poor ports, hostile Free Cities, food and water, and storms breaking up a fleet.
63. **Rodrik's illustrative 'thousand ships, three hundred arrive' is a warning projection**, not a verified forecast of actual voyage losses. The tactical difficulty is genuine; the exact losses hypothetical.
64. **Euron claims to have sailed the Smoking Sea and seen Valyria**, despite its lethal reputation. Rodrik's softly spoken **'Have you?'** directly challenges Euron's credibility; Euron reacts sharply. Neither a confirmed Valyrian landfall nor a verified lie is established.
65. **Euron says selling slaves in Lys and Volantis plus the Shields' loot will fund provisions**. This is an explicit economic link between the earlier captive shipment and the dragon expedition.
66. **Rodrik asks why pursue dragons no captain has personally seen**, instead of plundering the Reach. Many men openly prefer **the Arbor, Oldtown and Highgarden**, with Redwyne's fleet currently away. The assembly is not blindly united behind Euron's longer-term ambition.
67. **The captains' counterproposal embarrasses Euron**, who leaves the hall with Falia rather than immediately winning assent to the full expedition. Victarion briefly believes his brother's political control may be weaker than expected.
68. **Feasting includes an overt sexual assault on a captive Hewett daughter by Left-Hand Lucas Codd**. This is forced abuse during occupation, not legitimate spoils, and should be counted in any analysis of the human cost of Euron's strategy.
69. **A ten-year-old son of Euron with woolly hair summons Victarion**. The boy's existence is independently visible, but the claim of parentage comes from his role as Euron's messenger and Victarion's recognition; Euron later treats such children as unfit for succession.
70. **The wounded and intoxicated Victarion enters Euron's seized bedchamber**; Falia is asleep there. Victarion recalls murdering his wife and insists to himself that Euron made it unavoidable. The chapter does not absolve him.
71. **Euron describes childhood dreams of flying**, questioning whether one knows the limits of human ability without leaping from a high tower. This is suggestive imagery, not proof he literally flew, will fly, or was guided by an identified sorcerer.
72. **Euron wants 'the world,' not merely Shield Islands or Reach grapes.** The boast frames why he will not satisfy the captains' preference for nearer targets.
73. **Euron says he once owned a dragon egg and killed a Myrish magician** who promised to hatch it within a year. He then says he **threw the egg into the sea** when troubled. The egg's existence, fate and what he did with it are entirely **Euron's account**—a strong high-value question, not a proven loss.
74. **Euron reports Cragorn, blower of the kingsmoot horn, died**, and that an examining maester found **his lungs charred black**. This is **new post-horn medical testimony relayed through Euron**; it corroborates danger to the blower but not the horn's dragon-binding function, stated provenance, or mechanism.
75. **Euron proposes that only Victarion's Iron Fleet can survive a round trip to Slaver's Bay**, conceding Rodrik's mass-fleet logistical objection in substance. Victarion considers the force his own; an impending clash over command and ownership is latent.
76. **Euron offers Victarion thick blue-black shade-of-the-evening**, identifying it as Qartheen warlock wine. Victarion tastes a little and spits it out; Euron drinks. Color, oiliness and foul smell are visibly experienced; Euron's intended magical mind-opening is a claim.
77. **Euron says he captured a Qartheen galleass carrying four warlocks**, along with silk, cloves and nutmeg. He claims to have killed one for threatening him and forced the others to eat the body. This is **his grotesque story**, not an eyewitness retrospective passage. The source directly connects Euron's spoils to a Qarth-to-west warlock route without naming the prisoners.
78. **The shade-of-the-evening description matches ACOK 48's drugged-vision practice among Pyat Pree's circle**, while ACOK 63 reports surviving warlocks mobilizing against Daenerys. The captured four may belong to that wider network; no text here identifies them as Pyat personally or proves the route's exact dates.
79. **Euron instructs Victarion to retrieve a Targaryen woman with silver-gold hair and amethyst eyes** from Slaver's Bay as his wife and prospective mother of heirs. Reader knowledge identifies the intended target as **Daenerys**, but Victarion does not know her or her dragons firsthand.
80. **Euron calls his existing sons unfit and promises Victarion the Seastone Chair once he takes the Iron Throne**, saying Victarion's eventual legitimate sons would inherit. This future reward depends on several uncertain conquests and appointments and may be a deliberately manipulative inducement.
81. **Victarion explicitly accepts the mission but privately intends to keep Daenerys for himself**, rationalizing this as revenge for Euron's earlier violation of his wife. He has committed to departure, not completed contact with Daenerys or acquired any dragon.
82. **End state:** the Shield Islands are occupied, Euron has distributed four contentious titles and authorized slavery, Rodrik has challenged the long eastern campaign, Aeron is missing after an announced agitation plan, and Victarion's wounded hand, bitter obedience, distrust and concealed betrayal will accompany the Iron Fleet toward Slaver's Bay.

## Cross-book junctions and competing hypotheses

- **AFFC 1, 11, 18, 19 — election and dissent:** Euron's day-after-Balon arrival remains a conspicuous but unproven death-timing mystery; Aeron's kingsmoot generated an elected king he now rejects. In this chapter the newly elected regime violently suppresses Blacktyde and seems to threaten Aeron's nascent popular movement. No scene proves Euron personally captured the priest.
- **AFFC 11 Rodrik and Asha versus AFFC 29 Rodrik and Euron:** Rodrik's mainland-oriented, durable-alliance advice and Asha's negotiated peace policy are vindicated in part by his shipping and garrison objection. Conquering Shields creates further opponents rather than a stable bargain; Euron is nevertheless far more tactically capable than a mere mad plunderer.
- **AFFC 18, 19, 29 — the ironborn horn:** At the kingsmoot the horn's noise, apparent runes and injuries were witnessed; the new **Cragorn charred lungs** detail is a distinct, secondhand medical report, and still no dragon obeys it in the narrative. Retain alternative mechanisms and the unverified 'Valyria' origin.
- **ACOK 48 Daenerys and ACOK 63 Daenerys ↔ AFFC 29 Euron:** Shade-of-the-evening was a real psychoactive substance Daenerys drank in a hostile Qartheen magical setting; surviving warlocks were said to seek retaliation after Drogon's burning of the Undying. Euron now has a matching drink and claims four warlock prisoners; **do not infer Pyat Pree was on that galleass** without an identifier.
- **ASOS 8, 27 Daenerys ↔ AFFC 29:** Dragon numbers, Dany's female identity, world region and contested freedom are reader-level facts; Euron's claim to acquire and marry her is not Dany's consent. The source does not establish Euron can use the horn to control her children.
- **AFFC 17, 24, 28 Cersei ↔ AFFC 29:** The regency has financed new royal dromonds by suspending Faith and Braavosi debts, and relies on Redwyne-linked naval arrangements. The ironborn's seizure of the Shields at the **Mander mouth** creates a southern strategic threat that Cersei has underestimated.
- **AFFC 19 Asha campaign ↔ AFFC 29 Harras/Harlaw:** Ser Harras, Asha's champion, is installed at Greyshield, apparently changing the Reader's succession; **Hotho's new succession expectation is self-serving**, not a verified inheritance decree. Euron's gifts target rivals' military support rather than merely cronies.
- **Ironborn honor as contested ideology:** Victarion respects a knight's fighting ability but orders dying prisoners killed, objects to chattel sale while accepting thralls and salt wives, and condemns Euron's dishonor while excusing his own wife's murder. These inconsistencies are directly relevant to how easily Euron manipulates him.
- **Euron's egg and the Balon death question — THEORY ONLY:** A dragon egg has immense value, and Euron offers the convenient story that one was discarded. Investigate whether a specific transfer or payment is ever **demonstrated**. It would be premature to claim he paid a Faceless Man with it or arranged Balon's fall based solely on these statements.
- **Euron's flight language — THEORY ONLY:** Repeated questions about flying or leaping are memorable and fit the series' visionary imagery, but the present scene shows neither flight nor a confirmed prophetic dream. Preserve mundane rhetoric, intoxication and ambition as rivals.
- **Wider social mirror:** The sparrows in AFFC 28 request physical defense against atrocities, including those by Lannister lions and the 'Hound'; AFFC 29 directly shows organized ironborn invasion, captive women and children and a burned sept. Institutional ideologies make claims about safety and righteousness but frequently produce victims.

## High-priority disconfirmation tests

1. **Dragon horn:** Find the precise original blower, maester, corpse report and any subsequent attempt to sound or bind dragons. An incendiary internal injury does **not** establish rider control; treat Cragorn's death as reported here.
2. **Dragon egg provenance:** Search for independent identification of Euron's egg, origin, sale, exchange or disposal. A story about throwing it into the sea cannot support a specific magical-assassination transaction without a source.
3. **Aeron's disappearance:** Keep last sighting at Old Wyk separate from his stated itinerary to the islands; test future location and captor against direct witness evidence.
4. **Warlock prisoners:** Find identities of the four Qartheen captives, vessel and dates; distinguish Qarth survivors, Pyat Pree's associates and independently unrelated travelers.
5. **Valyria:** Test Euron's claim against named crew, authentic relic provenance, route and Rodrik's skepticism. Possession of strange objects does not verify a landfall.
6. **Shield lords and backlash:** Track Harras's hold over Greyshield, Hotho's actual succession, Andrik and Drumm, Volmark descent and Nute's estrangement from Victarion. Identify the first direct response from House Tyrell and any use of deliberately released Hewett ravens.
7. **Slaver's Bay expedition:** Track exact ship selection, departure, water and provisions, storms and actual ability to cross; the full fleet's voyage and the Iron Fleet's voyage are not interchangeable.
8. **Victarion/Daenerys:** Distinguish Euron's desired marriage, Victarion's secret intended seizure and Daenerys's own choices. No party can assume Dany will submit or that a Targaryen marriage confers dragon control.
9. **Injured hand:** Follow wound infection or healing with physical evidence, rather than a symbolic reading of blood or assumed magical disease.
10. **Missing enemies and victims:** Talbert's body/fate, the Hewett women's later safety, Falia's free choice and the women and children shipped toward slave markets are unconfirmed; avoid treating occupied populations as faceless political tokens.

**End-state receipt:** Victarion survives a naval victory off the Shield Islands, helps consolidate occupation, witnesses Euron's strategic political appointments and receives a conditional commission to seize the dragon queen. He agrees openly and plans betrayal privately. The entire chapter was read, and AFFC 1/11/18/19 and ACOK 48/63 plus relevant ASOS Daenerys evidence were cross-checked; future magic, actual marriage, dragon control and ironborn monarchy are unresolved.
