## Four priority interpretations

**Sweetsleep cumulative risk:** Robert already received a pinch three days before and Colemon explicitly warns the drug **does not leave the flesh**. Alayne proposes another before descending and still another at the feast; the text shows her *ordering* two, not personally watching both administered. Colemon asks about nosebleeds and suggests a six-month moratorium. [AFFC 34](34-cat-of-the-canals.md) supplies waif's escalating dose warning; [AFFC 36](36-cersei.md) records Pycelle's use in Rosby. These are independent cases, not proof a poisoning plot is underway. Petyr's cold certainty Robert will die raises motive as an **open hypothesis**; no murder is demonstrated.

**Alayne's competence and identity cost:** She conducts physically dangerous evacuation, persuades Robert without overt brutality, and masters household officials, while concealing name, age, lineage and memories. Sansa recalls a supposed Blackwater *kiss* with Sandor, a classic **un-kiss** discrepancy requiring independent ACOK scene audit; don't silently add a physical kiss to prior source. Her involuntary “Jon Snow?” response exposes risk to Myranda Royce, whose merry chatter may probe rather than simply sympathize. Myranda's own knowledge remains unshown.

**Reconciling distant war reports:** Myranda reports **Riverrun fallen but Dragonstone still held**, whereas AFFC 38 ends with Jaime threatening Edmure *before* a surrender is shown, and AFFC 36 had Aurane announcing Dragonstone taken. This is **not** enough to declare either informant false: trans-regional ravens, chronology interleaving, and interested battle accounts all require more dates and independent witnesses. Mark the military states **REPORTED WITH POV DISAGREEMENT**.

**Harry's inheritance chain:** Petyr traces Jasper Arryn → Jon (whose only surviving son is Robert), Ronnel (whose son Elbert was murdered), and Alys Arryn → Waynwood daughters → youngest daughter → **Harrold Hardyng**. The older alternative Arryn-Waynwood descendants died or left no inheriting offspring. **Harry is heir apparent to the Vale if Robert dies**, not Lady Anya's heir and not presently Lord of the Eyrie. Petyr has acquired Waynwood debt and paid a large dower, and proposes an eventual **Sansa reveal at the wedding** to muster knights for Winterfell. Requires Robert death, Sansa widowhood, Harry's agreement, ceremony and active knightly support; none established by his speech. The plan creates incentive for Robert's death but no direct plot confession.
