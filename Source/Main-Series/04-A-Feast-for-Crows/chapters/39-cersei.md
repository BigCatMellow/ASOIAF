## Source-driven interpretations and alternatives

**Moon tea is a clue, not proof of the desired case.** Pycelle says Margaery sought moon tea; her use, its recipient, exact indication, and any actual intercourse are not independently observed. Moreover Pycelle gives the information under threat of return to the black cells. Cersei treats this as a license to charge Margaery and every cousin, then **deliberately manufactures an incompatible, politically tailored confession**. The Redwyne twins and Loras are dropped from Wat's tortured list because the queen needs Redwyne ships and a positive narrative about Loras. The Bard's torture, including mutilation, converts social proximity into a fabricated legal record.

**The appointment and succession puzzle:** Rosby's death is directly reported by Pycelle and Qyburn but his cough is a plausible ordinary cause. Cersei invents a will leaving Rosby to Tommen, discounting the ward (known from AFFC 24), and considers a new grant to Aurane. Orton receives appointment as Hand in conversation while Harys is to be displaced to treasury; formal documents and subsequent acceptance are not depicted. Cersei relies on Taena despite coercive dynamics and unknown private loyalties.

**The self-destroying conspiracy:** The Blue Bard's false evidence is prompted with torture and wall exile; Taena is to recruit Alla; Osney is explicitly told to lie to the High Septon on Maiden's Day, describing sexual involvement with Margaery, Megga and Elinor. Yet Osney points out a core falsehood and seeks payment through an encounter with Cersei. This generates real potential witnesses/evidence of **Cersei's** impropriety as she invents Margaery's. The chapter stops before his statement to the High Septon or any completed bedroom encounter. Never upgrade the selected confession to proof of Margaery's misconduct.
