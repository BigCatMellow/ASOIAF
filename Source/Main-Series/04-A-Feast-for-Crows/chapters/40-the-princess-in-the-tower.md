## Major case studies

**Arianne's confinement and confession:** Food, clothes, bathing, views and cyvasse conceal a coercive regime of absolute silence that leaves the 23-year-old physically debilitated. She persuades Cedra to carry a letter, but the girl's disappearance turns out **not** to indicate her execution; Doran sends her to Water Gardens. Arianne's uncertainty about the informer is never fully resolved in this chapter. Her recruiting servants against their duties mirrors the larger question of which subjects Doran can truly trust.

**Myrcella and a false report:** Doran states she **survived Darkstar**, with severed right ear and cheek-to-bone wound, medically managed by Caleotte. This is different from a coronation failure alone: it risks crown retaliation and the collapse of their protective ward relationship. Doran delays Ser Balon Swann through Wyl/Yronwood/Jordayne/Toland hospitality, but the envoy is still approaching. Arianne proposes claiming Arys died defending Myrcella. Whether Myrcella would endorse that narrative is open; Doran pointedly questions whether one further murder would be needed. No such killing is authorized in this scene.

**The population correction:** Doran says Dorne is actually **least populous** and believes Daeron the Young Dragon historically inflated its troop strength for self-serving reasons. This directly challenges overconfident readers who assume the prince could safely declare independent war. His peace policy masks a sustained intent to destroy Tywin and vindicate Elia, but his long secrecy has bred domestic mistrust.

**Dynastic revelation:** Doran admits a previous intention for Quentyn to inherit if Arianne's secret marriage succeeded. Arianne's childhood contract was with a man dead by molten gold, **reader-level clearly Viserys Targaryen** from AGOT. Doran acknowledges deliberately outrageous suitors and a missed chance to send her to Tyrosh. This is **reported secret pact**, not a signed contract presented to Arianne. He promises Dorne still belongs to her; Quentyn has gone abroad with **Cletus Yronwood, Maester Kedry and three knights** for a hazardous mission intended to bring back the family's dream of vengeance. The final **onyx dragon** and “fire and blood” link the enterprise to House Targaryen; *whether Daenerys receives or accepts Quentyn has not happened on this page*.

**Cross-book:** Compare the attempted Myrcella queen-making in AFFC 21 *The Queenmaker*, the direct Arys death in AFFC 13 *The Soiled Knight*, Viserys's fatal molten-gold 'crowning' in AGOT, and Aemon's Daenerys-centered prophetic argument in [AFFC 35 Samwell](35-samwell.md). A convergence of dragon restoration strategies is not proof they have coordinated.
