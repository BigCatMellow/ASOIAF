# AFFC 21 — The Queenmaker: Myrcella's substitution, Dornish inheritance and the intercepted coronation

- Status: FIRST PASS COMPLETE; independent challenge and later account reconciliation open.
- Source: full [AFFC 21 The Queenmaker](../text/022-the-queenmaker.md), reviewed 2026-10-18.

## Sequential close reading

1. Shandystone is an abandoned dry-well holdfast; its former water source failed a century earlier. The setting makes water control a practical form of Dornish political power.
2. Arianne arrives with Andrey 'Drey' Dalt and Sylva Santagar, while Garin and Gerold 'Darkstar' Dayne are already at the rendezvous. Arys is expected to arrive separately with Myrcella.
3. Oberyn once brought Arianne, Tyene and Sarella here. Tyene learned venom extraction, Sarella examined ruins and asked historical questions, while Arianne remembers imaginative fantasies and listening to Oberyn's stories. The childhood differences help distinguish their later methods.
4. Garin is Arianne's milk brother, and his mother once nursed her; their childhood intimacy explains trust and makes the later question of informants especially painful.
5. Darkstar interrupts the Garin the Great story to observe that the legendary Rhoynar leader's immense army was ultimately destroyed by Valyria. He challenges triumphal history by counting costs.
6. Arianne finds Darkstar attractive but recognizes his cruelty and fears that his hostility toward Arys could lead to bloodshed. She believes his sword and High Hermitage castle could be strategically useful.
7. Darkstar declines the group's summerwine in favor of unsweetened lemonwater. This is directly reported behavior, not grounds to infer poisoned drinks.
8. Garin repeats sailors' eastern news: Astapor slave revolt, dragons in Qarth, plague in Yi Ti, raiding in the Basilisk Isles, and religious upheaval in Qohor. Each item is port gossip, not verified local observation.
9. The Golden Company breaking its Myr contract recurs as a report. Arianne interprets it as evidence Quentyn has secured the company; she has not established that causal link.
10. Arianne associates Nymeria with a bright white star and imagines the milky band behind it as ten thousand ships, a specifically Dornish navigational/mnemonic image.
11. Arianne recalls Doran sending young Quentyn to Yronwood as settlement of a blood debt demanded by Ormond Yronwood; Mellario vehemently opposed fostering her son away. Doran's answer frames children as political currency.
12. Garin's mother saw Quentyn passing through Planky Town disguised as a merchant, accompanied by a man with Cletus Yronwood's lazy eye and a linguistically skilled maester. These are reports relayed to Arianne rather than direct sightings by her.
13. A contact broke into Quentyn's strongbox and found scrolls sealed with Dorne's sun and spear. Their contents remained unread, but the official seals materially weaken Arianne's hope that Quentyn travels entirely on his own initiative.
14. Darkstar believes crowning Myrcella will not make her queen in practice or provoke the desired war. He advocates direct violent provocation instead, foreshadowing the risk posed by his inclusion.
15. Arianne explicitly rejects harming Myrcella and considers the child under her protection. Her stated objective is to claim her own inheritance, obtain justice, and free Sand Snakes; her chosen method nevertheless endangers Myrcella.
16. Darkstar mentions a historical Dayne–Oakheart feud, adding a personal rivalry to the operation. The longevity claimed for that feud is his statement, not independently audited history.
17. Arys arrives with Myrcella hooded on a grey palfrey. The child does not know the political scheme and fears Tommen has been harmed when Arianne's supporters address her as queen.
18. Arianne uses Dornish elder-child succession logic to argue that Myrcella outranks Tommen for the Iron Throne. This is her partisan legal argument, not settled succession law in the other kingdoms.
19. Myrcella is introduced to Drey, Sylva, Garin and Darkstar. She insists on a formal address for a knight she has not learned to trust, demonstrating intelligence and caution.
20. Darkstar identifies himself as a Dayne of High Hermitage rather than successor to Arthur Dayne as Sword of the Morning; he disparages the legendary knight's reputation.
21. Arys privately reports Tywin's death, Tyrion's escape, Cersei's regency and the bounty on Tyrion. Arianne had been operating with significant information lag about the capital.
22. Arianne thinks a woman regent will normalize female sovereignty and aid Myrcella's claim. This ignores differences between regency for Tommen and a disputed older sister claiming in her own right.
23. Arys and Arianne confirm they secured Myrcella's removal by feigning a case of redspots. Prince Trystane was kept away; Maester Caleotte accepted the reported symptoms without an examination, partly because adult infection could be dangerous.
24. Rosamund Lannister of Lannisport, a distant Lannister relation, serves as Myrcella's double. Myrcella herself curled Rosamund's hair and painted false spots, making her an active participant in the substitution despite not knowing the conspiracy's political purpose.
25. Rolder wears Arys's armor and keeps his visor down, although he is three inches shorter; Dake more closely matches his height but has the wrong build. The disguise depends on avoiding close examination for a few days.
26. Myrcella previously traded appearances with Rosamund aboard the Seaswift en route to Braavos, including brown hair dye, as a genuine protective precaution against Stannis. The same technique is repurposed for a wholly different operation.
27. Arys asks where Arianne intends to go next; she withholds the plan even from her Kingsguard ally. This is a specific compartmentalization that reduces the participants' capacity to respond once the operation fails.
28. The party rides south and west at night. Arianne counts seven riders and treats that number as auspicious, although the earlier unplanned delays mean they cannot complete the exposed desert crossing before sunrise.
29. Arianne imagines removing Doran to retirement at the Water Gardens and placing Quentyn there too; she assumes most Dornish houses will back her and threatens the Yronwoods if they defect. These are her anticipated political outcomes, not established endorsements.
30. On the journey Arianne and Garin teach Myrcella the history of the orphans of the Greenblood, Rhoynar exiles who rebuilt boats from Nymeria's burned fleet and continued venerating Mother Rhoyne rather than fully adopting Dornish customs.
31. Garin refers to the Old Man of the River and a struggle with the Crab King. This is Rhoynar religious lore, not independent proof of living deities.
32. Myrcella demonstrates skill at cyvasse: she recognizes Trystane's repetitive defenses and uses a dragon piece to defeat his elephants. The game detail is a concrete sign of her tactical learning, not a reliable prophecy of future dragons.
33. Myrcella comments that Rosamund finds cyvasse's rules too difficult and that casual observers can mistake one girl for the other in exchanged clothes; she clearly understands the mechanics of their masquerade.
34. The heat and wind threaten Arys especially because he refuses Arianne's advice to veil his face and wears heavy clothing. The group accelerates, exposing the tradeoff between protecting Myrcella and exhausting the horses.
35. Sandbeggar thorn trees, olives, devilgrass and lemon orchards signal proximity to irrigation and the Greenblood. The chapter gives a physically plausible transit from ruins to river.
36. Arianne's planned route is by orphan poleboat up the Greenblood and Vaith, then with aid from Sandstone and the Hellholt to crown Myrcella. She expects ties from Oberyn's fostering and Ellaria Sand's Uller family to secure support; these are assumptions.
37. The poleboat is hidden beneath a willow, but no orphan crew is visible. Garin steps forward and finds Areo Hotah instead, exposing an interception prepared in advance.
38. Hotah has crossbowmen and spearmen concealed in and above the boat; by Doran's order they must preserve Arianne and Myrcella but may kill the other conspirators if resistance occurs.
39. Drey immediately relinquishes his sword, Darkstar urges Arys to yield, and Arianne recognizes the plan is lost. Arys nevertheless charges the much larger force after a last look at Arianne.
40. Arys wounds two men but is struck by projectiles, loses his horse and is killed by Areo Hotah. His white-cloak duty, love and desperate charge are observed; the exact mixture of motive is not stated in an objective narrative.
41. Myrcella ends the fight on the ground with blood streaming through her hands over her face. The scene does not explicitly show who struck her or with what; assigning responsibility here would exceed the POV evidence.
42. Hotah orders pursuit of a fleeing male conspirator, but Arianne is disoriented and the narration does not name the escapee. Darkstar's later role must not be inserted into this immediate scene as directly observed.
43. Arianne is detained, her boot knife—gift from Nymeria Sand—confiscated, and she asks how Doran learned of the plan. Hotah replies that someone informed him; the identity and channel remain unknown.
44. End state: Arys is dead, Myrcella injured, Arianne and several conspirators in custody, at least one man pursued, and the Myrcella coronation never takes place.

## Series-wide tests

- **AFFC 13 Arys:** Reconcile his earlier choice to aid Arianne with his last charge; never conflate Arianne's protective intentions with Myrcella's actual safety.
- **Doran and Quentyn:** Political fostering for a blood debt, sealed documents, mother Mellario's anger, and a secret overseas mission all point to layered obligations; read the documents' actual contents only when a later scene supplies them.
- **Cersei 17:** Queenmaking takes place while King’s Landing is under Cersei's regency; Arianne mistakenly treats that as a precedent for Myrcella's legal succession.
- **The Sands' agency:** The orphans' religious traditions, geography and boats are substantive political resources, not decorative folklore.
- **Causality of the violence:** Doran laid an ambush, Arys elected to charge despite an opportunity to surrender, and Myrcella was wounded amid that violence; distinguishing decisions from still-unknown agents is essential.
- **Information leak:** An advance interception at the specific meeting boat implies Doran learned more than the general existence of a plot. This narrows hypotheses but does not identify a spy.
