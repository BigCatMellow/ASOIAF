# AFFC 18 — The Iron Captain: Victarion, the kingsmoot and Asha's rejected settlement

- Status: FIRST-PASS COMPLETE; independent cross-POV challenge open.
- Primary source: complete [AFFC 18 The Iron Captain](../text/019-the-iron-captain.md), read 2026-10-18.

## Complete scene and causal sequence

1. The *Iron Victory* approaches Nagga's Cradle on Old Wyk, sacred place of the Grey King's hall and Balon's earlier kingship declaration. Victarion experiences the setting as holy; no new magic is independently demonstrated.
2. Victarion remembers his captains urging his claim at Moat Cailin because Balon's sons were believed dead and Asha was a woman. He initially refused to abandon his brother's order to defend the Moat and feared fratricidal conflict with Euron.
3. Aeron's call for a kingsmoot provides Victarion a religious and political justification to seek the Seastone Chair without fighting Euron directly. Victarion left **Ralf Kenning** commanding Moat Cailin; this is a consequential military withdrawal.
4. Nine-tenths of the Iron Fleet reportedly reaches Old Wyk, with only one ship lost in the passage. Victarion enumerates notable ships and identifies them by sight, demonstrating intimate fleet knowledge.
5. Victarion spots *Silence*, Euron's distinct dark-red galley with black sails and a **mouthless female figurehead**. The ship's crew includes men from distant lands who do not speak in the encounter; Victarion's insulting descriptions are his prejudiced judgments.
6. Victarion orders *Grief* and *Iron Vengeance* to position between *Silence* and the open sea and the rest of the fleet to close the bay. He deliberately attempts to prevent Euron or his associates leaving without permission.
7. He keeps guarded **chests** aboard his ship, thinking much depends on them. Their contents are not disclosed; likely kingsmoot resources, but the chapter does not reveal their actual inventory.
8. Victarion wears full protective mail because poisoned projectiles at Moat Cailin caused severe illness and deaths. His account grounds the bogmen's tactical effectiveness without proving every detail of the purported poison.
9. Aeron blesses Victarion with seawater and supports a god-sanctioned selection at the kingsmoot. He remains committed to preventing Euron's rule.
10. The assembled captains represent numerous houses and social strata, including men descended from thralls and salt wives. The political assembly is more diverse in birth than Victarion's assumptions about gender and legitimacy.
11. At a feast, numerous captains voice support for Victarion; **Hotho Harlaw** offers his twelve-year-old daughter as a dynastic match. Victarion says he will consider the offer only once crowned.
12. Victarion recalls his first wife died giving birth to a stillborn daughter, the second died of disease, and he himself killed the third. His past is inseparable from the present struggle with Euron.
13. **Baelor Blacktyde**, a former Oldtown hostage who adopted the Faith of the Seven, condemns Balon's wars and asks Victarion to end the campaign even through submission if necessary. Victarion refuses. This is a concrete antiwar constituency inside the Iron Islands, not unanimous desire for conquest.
14. Feast details including finger-dance injuries and war songs show the social setting in which rival claims are tested, with mockery and spectacle central to political allegiance.
15. Asha attends Victarion's feast and refers to it as a queensmoot. She has grounded *Black Wind* away from the main beach and traveled overland, showing independent preparations.
16. Asha points out Moat Cailin remains exposed to the Boltons, directly challenging Victarion's assessment that only weakened 'bog devils' remain.
17. Asha reports additional claimants: Drumm and Maron Volmark; the kingsmoot cannot be reduced to only the Greyjoy siblings.
18. Euron arrives with a conspicuous retinue and asserts kingship before any vote. His unsettling account of gods worshipped from Ib to Asshai is a claim of extensive travel and widespread violence, not proof that he has literally tested divine power.
19. Aeron publicly denounces Euron's godlessness and leaves after Euron's men recognize him as king. The scene exposes a divide between religious authority and visible factional strength.
20. Asha notices Euron returned within a day of Balon's death and openly questions the timing. Euron invokes the Storm God and offers the purported alibi that *Silence* was at sea, then invites her to question his non-speaking crew. No independent verification is possible here.
21. Euron and his followers attempt to humiliate Asha; she answers through ridicule and her axe. Victarion intervenes to prevent bloodshed at his feast, despite his own contempt for her ambition.
22. After Euron's departure, some captains leave Victarion's tent. This is an observable sign of political fluidity rather than proof they are committed Euron voters.
23. Privately Asha reports her canvassing has produced insufficient support: she counts her own followers, many Harlaws and Tris Botley, but not enough to win outright.
24. **Asha offers to crown Victarion herself in exchange for shared rule**, specifically proposing to serve as an equivalent of the King's Hand. Victarion misunderstands and rejects the precedent, partly because he fears ridicule for relying on a woman.
25. **Asha's peace proposal is explicit:** return Deepwood Motte, Torrhen's Square and Moat Cailin; in return Lady Glover reportedly says northern leaders would cede Sea Dragon Point and the Stony Shore, an area much larger than the islands. Hostages and mutual defense would secure the bargain.
26. The terms are not a signed northern agreement. Asha attributes the offer to Lady Glover, whose capacity to bind other northern houses is not confirmed here. Victarion's reply that the Stony Shore is already theirs confuses occupation with defensible settlement.
27. Asha argues the North is too large and populous for the Iron Islands to retain by naval raids. Her proposal is an alternative strategic model, not a retreat without gain.
28. Victarion imagines marriage into **Hotho Harlaw's** branch would secure House Harlaw. Asha corrects him: **Rodrik the Reader**, not Hotho, currently rules. Victarion assumes Hotho's eventual succession will make the alliance sufficient.
29. Asha warns that Euron's travel stories and reputation dominate the talk around the fires. Victarion insists on credit for physically burning Tywin's flagship at Lannisport; Asha distinguishes executing the raid from Euron's originating strategy.
30. **Asha finally asks about the real reason for Euron's exile. Victarion discloses that Euron violated his salt wife, Victarion killed her, and Balon banished Euron rather than permit fratricide.** This establishes the family's violent past from Victarion's own account.
31. Victarion rationalizes killing his wife as unavoidable dishonor and suppresses Euron's mocking remarks. The perpetrator of the woman's death is Victarion; Euron's prior wrongdoing does not transfer culpability for her killing.
32. Asha expresses sympathy for both Victarion and, more pointedly, the woman. When Victarion refuses her alliance, she resolves to press her own claim.
33. End state: Victarion, Asha, Euron and additional claimants converge on a contested kingsmoot; Asha's negotiated peace coalition fails; Euron's accusation trail and the guarded chests remain unresolved.
