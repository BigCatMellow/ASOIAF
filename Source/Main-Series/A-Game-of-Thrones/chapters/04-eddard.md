## Essential narrative

- **VERIFIED:** Robert's court arrives at Winterfell; Ned is struck by his old friend's physical decline since the Greyjoy rebellion. Robert immediately asks to visit Lyanna's tomb, despite Cersei's objection.
- **VERIFIED:** Ned and Robert stand by Rickard, Brandon and Lyanna's statues. Ned remembers making an urgent promise to Lyanna while she was dying in a blood- and rose-associated room; its subject is withheld.
- **CHARACTER CLAIM:** Robert's hatred of Rhaegar shapes how he speaks of the past. His assumptions about Lyanna's wishes and Rhaegar's motives are not directly verified here; Ned's memories are also incomplete.
- **VERIFIED:** Robert describes Jon Arryn's rapid fatal illness and Lysa's refusal to leave their son with Tywin Lannister. Robert wants the ward under Tywin's control and resents Lysa's departure.
- **VERIFIED:** Robert offers Ned the Handship and proposes Joffrey's betrothal to Sansa. Ned dreads moving south and asks for time to consult Catelyn.
