## Essential narrative

- **VERIFIED:** In the ninth year of summer, seven-year-old Bran attends an execution with Ned, Robb, Jon and Theon. The condemned Watch deserter has lost both ears and a finger to frostbite; these details strongly connect him to Gared in `AGOT Prologue`.
- **VERIFIED:** Ned personally executes the prisoner using Ice and later teaches Bran that a ruler who orders death must face the condemned directly. Bran sees blood; Theon kicks the severed head, Robb calls the man's behavior brave, Jon recognizes terror.
- **CHARACTER CLAIM / LIMIT:** Ned says the deserter was half-mad and dangerously frightened. Bran does **not** recall the exchange before sentence. **UNKNOWN:** whether Gared tried to explain the Others and precisely what Ned heard.
- **VERIFIED:** The party finds a dead female direwolf, a broken stag antler lodged in her throat, and five living pups. Jon uses the count of Ned's five trueborn children to win permission for them to raise the pups, visibly excluding himself.
- **VERIFIED:** A sixth, separately placed white pup with open red eyes is detected by Jon. He claims it as his own despite Theon's prediction of its death.

## Knowledge asymmetry

Reader witnessed the Others and Will's killing. Ned is shown only a frightened deserter, not the supernatural event. Bran interprets the man through Old Nan's tales and cannot accurately assess the report. In later theory work, do not give early political actors the reader's prologue knowledge.

## Alternative interpretations / theory routes

1. **Six wolves as Stark destinies:** striking authorial structure. It does not require a literal one-to-one prophecy about death, crown or magic.
2. **White pup as Jon's distinct bloodline:** could fit disputed parentage; it also directly represents Jon's status as a socially excluded child. Compare [R+L=J](../../../../research/theories/cases/r-l-j.md).
3. **Stag-antler omen:** symbolic foreshadowing is plausible; agency, inevitability and specific event mapping are not proven.
4. **Gared as ignored witness:** evidence of an information failure, not proof Ned heard an explicit warning and chose to conceal it.

**Next junction:** `AGOT 2 (Catelyn)` confirms Ned's recollection of the frightening deserter; later Jon/Ghost, Bran/Summer and weirwood chapters can test the animal bond.
