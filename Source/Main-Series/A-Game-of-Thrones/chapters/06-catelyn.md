## Essential plot and decisions

1. **VERIFIED:** Ned initially plans to refuse Robert's Handship. Catelyn fears royal offense and sees the proposed Joffrey–Sansa marriage as political opportunity. Ned resists imagining power meant for his dead elder brother Brandon.
2. **VERIFIED:** Luwin receives an anonymously deposited Myrish lens in a box with a false bottom; the concealed letter, sealed with Arryn arms, is addressed to Catelyn and written in a childhood private language shared with Lysa. **UNKNOWN:** who physically delivered the box.
3. **CHARACTER CLAIM:** Lysa's secret message says Jon Arryn was murdered by the Lannisters, especially Cersei. Her detailed accusation is a **claim by a potential participant with interests**, not proof of who killed him.
4. **VERIFIED:** Catelyn burns the letter immediately after reading it. She urges Ned to accept the Handship to investigate and protect Robert; Luwin argues the office gives him means. Ned finally agrees.
5. **VERIFIED:** Ned instructs Catelyn to remain in Winterfell and train Robb in governance; Rickon remains, while Sansa, Arya and Bran are intended to go south. Bran's ability to connect Robb and Joffrey is presented as strategic.
6. **VERIFIED:** Catelyn demands that Jon not remain with her. Ned says he cannot bring a publicly acknowledged bastard to Robert's court. Luwin reveals Benjen has reported Jon's interest in joining the Watch. Ned accepts that prospect reluctantly, planning to tell Jon later.

## Knowledge and reliability map

- **Catelyn:** knows an apparently Lysa-authored accusation; accepts it partly because of risky concealment. She does not know the alleged killing's cause from direct evidence.
- **Ned:** learns a claim secondhand and initially prefers protecting Winterfell by avoiding court; changes course because his duties and suspicions interact.
- **Luwin:** handles the material object and advocates accepting office; neither his receipt nor his interpretation verifies murder.
- **Jon:** is not party to the negotiation shaping his future; his wish to belong at the Watch has been reported by Benjen.
- **Reader:** has not witnessed Jon Arryn's killing and should not adopt Catelyn's certainty as narrative omniscience.

## Theory stress tests

- **Winterfell underground survival system:** geothermal heat and glass gardens genuinely support long winters. A connected network of inhabited tunnels or ancient city needs physical/timeline corroboration, not architectural symbolism alone.
- **Ashara as Jon's mother:** a real rumor and emotional reaction exist, but the chapter supplies neither direct proof nor a full timeline. Contrast [R+L=J](../../../../research/theories/cases/r-l-j.md).
- **Lysa's letter as reliable intelligence:** encoded transmission and motive to warn cannot certify the killer; audit subsequent Lysa/Littlefinger accounts and who profits from Ned's journey.
- **Maesters engineered Stark departure:** Luwin has political influence, but his stated pragmatic reasons are an adequate alternative to a central conspiracy.
