## Plot and agency

1. **VERIFIED:** On Joffrey's **thirteenth name day** Sansa sees the red comet and asks **Arys Oakheart** its meaning. Arys says it celebrates Joffrey; Sansa has heard **Dragon's Tail**. She notices that the lion's red and Baratheon stag's gold undermine confident interpretation. **No observed causal link** connects it to Joffrey's power.
2. **VERIFIED:** Sansa wears a purple silk gown, moonstone net gifted by Joffrey and sleeves covering **bruises from a beating he ordered after Robb's coronation**. Arys is a preferable escort but **he has also struck Sansa under order** after protesting. The Hound has never been personally ordered to beat her in Sansa's account.
3. **VERIFIED:** The small and poorly attended jousts take place **inside the closed Red Keep**, reflecting war, scarcity and fewer noble competitors. Tyrion is not yet at court when the day's contest starts. Sansa notices **Hobber and Horas Redwyne** are held as unwilling royal guests, like herself.
4. **VERIFIED:** **Meryn Trant unhorses Hobber**; **Balon Swann unhorses Morros Slynt**, Janos's son, who is dragged but survives; **Horas Redwyne** defeats an older knight. Sansa privately wants punishment for Morros because his father displayed Ned's head; her wish is **not** a demonstrated supernatural intervention.
5. **VERIFIED:** Drunk **Dontos Hollard** arrives improperly dressed, cannot mount and forfeits. Joffrey orders him **drowned in a wine cask**. Sansa impulsively objects and invents a warning that killing on the king's name day brings bad luck. Sandor **backs the warning as a proverb**. Sansa then frames Dontos as better suited to service as court fool; **Joffrey makes him the new fool**, saving his life.
6. **VERIFIED:** After Joffrey ends the joust, **Tommen** rides against an antlered **quintain**, falls when it swings back, then courageously remounts while Myrcella helps him. Sansa suggests Joffrey praise Tommen rather than mock him. This produces a sharp contrast between brothers.
7. **VERIFIED:** A Lannister column unexpectedly enters the closed gate: **Tyrion, Bronn, Timett and mountain-clan fighters**. Tyrion's **right arm is in a sling**, Bronn escorts him and Timett's missing eye identifies him. Tommen and Myrcella greet their uncle with genuine affection; Joffrey demands Robb's head as a gift.
8. **VERIFIED:** Tyrion offers Sansa condolences, recognizes her compelled profession of loyalty, and promises not to hurt her. Sansa **does not trust him**, remembering that Cersei likewise spoke gently before Ned's death. This skepticism is informed, not blind hatred.
