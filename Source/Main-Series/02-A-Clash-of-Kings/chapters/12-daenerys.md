## Main narrative and consequential choices

1. **VERIFIED / INTERPRETATION:** Daenerys calls the comet **shierak qiya** a herald of her dragons and follows it east. She privately recognizes other routes are unsafe: the north belongs to hostile khalasars, the **Lhazareen** can resent Drogo's raiding, and **Pono** controls the Slaver's Bay road with thousands of warriors. Jorah warns that living hatchlings will attract captors. A path presented as prophecy is also the sole practical option.
2. **VERIFIED:** Dany's remnants cross the **Red Waste** by night, suffering thirst, hunger and dwindling mounts. At first she counts only **four warriors** among women, children and frail men. An old man and then a baby girl die; Dany arranges burial rites despite the loss of precious horses. Dany, fourteen, suppresses outward doubt. Poorly supplied people and animals were left after other Dothraki abandoned Drogo.
3. **VERIFIED:** As food and water vanish, **Doreah** develops fever, blisters and hair loss. Dany refuses to abandon her and sits beside her, sharing water until she dies. Dany herself is emaciated and her breast milk dries up. She attributes the lives of the dragons to those lost in Drogo's funeral pyre—her *interpretation* of the sacrifice.
4. **VERIFIED:** Hatchling dragons reject raw horseflesh but eagerly eat meat **charred** after Dany recalls Viserys's observation that dragons eat cooked food. Their skin feels hot and their wing membranes translucent. Dany names black-red **Drogon**, green **Rhaegal** and cream-gold **Viserion** after Drogo, Rhaegar and Viserys respectively; the descriptions are concrete identifiers, not fate predictions.
5. **VERIFIED:** After a foul scalding sulphurous pool, Dany estimates **a third of her people dead**. Scouts find an apparently white-walled city, which she wisely approaches only after reconnaissance. The city is deserted: weathered blank white facades, scorched ruins, broken gate, an empty statue plinth and bleached human bones. Dothraki raiders may have carried off a statue, but original inhabitants, timing and cause of abandonment remain **UNKNOWN**.
6. **VERIFIED:** In the deserted city, hidden gardens yield figs, peaches, grapes and a clean well; the khalasar rests and recovers. Irri and Jhiqui fear ghosts, but Dany prioritizes edible fruit and water. She later removes paving stones to create grazing space and repairs gates: specific early **settlement management**, not purely prophetic travel.
7. **JORAH'S CHARACTER ACCOUNT:** Over a peach, Jorah relates his first marriage to a **Glover** who died after three miscarriages; his role at the storming of Pyke; a surprising Lannisport tourney triumph; and marriage to **Lynesse Hightower**, daughter of **Leyton**. Her dissatisfaction at austere **Bear Island** and his costly efforts to please her led to debt (including Braavosi borrowing), tournament losses and unnamed shameful acts for gold. He fled Ned Stark's judgment to **Lys**, where Lynesse reportedly entered merchant **Tregar Ormollen's** household. His omissions, depiction of her motives and claimed current status are **interested testimony**, not neutral fact.
8. **VERIFIED / DANY'S INTERPRETATION:** Jorah admits Lynesse resembled Daenerys. Dany newly sees his desire as sexual/romantic and cannot imagine returning it; she thinks of Drogo and plans someday to restore Jorah's honor and land. She believes **Mirri's words** mean she can never bear a living child; that future biological outcome is **not independently verified**. She dreams she and Drogo ride dragons.
9. **VERIFIED:** The refuge is called **Vaes Tolorro** ('city of bones'). Dany sends three separate nine-horse expeditions: **Rakharo south, Aggo southwest, Jhogo southeast**, to chart options instead of wandering blind. Rakharo reports a dragon skeleton so large his horse went through its jaws and a barren coast; Aggo reports two ruined cities, an abandoned scroll cache, skulls on iron spikes and a fire-opal bracelet. The huge skeleton and artifacts are scouts' **reports**, not direct observations by Dany.
10. **VERIFIED:** Jhogo returns from **Qarth** with three people on humped mounts: **Pyat Pree** (claims warlock status), **Xaro Xhoan Daxos** (Thirteen merchant), **Quaithe of the Shadow** (masked visitor). Each speaks in a different language and explicitly comes to see the dragons. Their arrival is verified; shared motive or trustworthiness beyond this declared goal is not.
