## Main cause and effect

1. **VERIFIED:** Yoren's recruits avoid the kingsroad after the encounter with gold cloaks and edge toward the **western shore of the Gods Eye**. Concealment exposes practical costs: narrow winding tracks, stuck wagon wheels, lost time and exhausted food. Arya's habitual stealth leaves her fearful of pursuit but reluctant to flee alone.
2. **GENDRY'S ACCOUNT / CHILDREN'S ASSUMPTIONS:** The queen's pursuit confuses **Gendry**, who knows only that **Tobho Mott** abruptly sent him to the Watch. Lommy speculates that he is **Ned Stark's bastard**; Arya dismisses that because she believes Jon is Ned's only bastard. Neither belief reveals Gendry's true ancestry by itself. His carefully polished bull-horn helmet remains unused for now.
3. **VERIFIED:** With stores consumed, former poachers **Koss and Kurz** hunt, while boys gather food. Arya catches a rabbit, shares her prized portion with Gendry, and the whole party including chained **Jaqen, Rorge and Biter** is fed. Jaqen thanks her while Rorge taunts her: these are distinct present behaviors, not secure predictions of future loyalties.
4. **VERIFIED:** Farmhands at **Briarwhite** demand money for corn and threaten the Watch recruiters. Yoren contrasts this with remembered hospitality freely offered to black brothers, showing the Watch's declining practical prestige amid war.
5. **KOSS'S REPORT / VERIFIED CHOICE:** Scouts find **twenty to thirty wounded armed men** with little transport, carrying a **yellow/black spotted treecat** badge on muddy brown. Neither Yoren nor the chapter identifies their allegiance. Rather than gamble on an encounter, he diverts around them, costing at least **two days**. Armed local farmers later monitor fields with bows and axes.
6. **VERIFIED:** A distant fire is followed by direct discovery of a razed village: slaughtered animals, burned houses, impaled charred bodies, ravens and a breached palisade. It is tangible evidence of the war's civilian costs alongside `ACOK 7 (Catelyn)`, but **no verified perpetrator** is supplied.
7. **VERIFIED:** Yoren and companions rescue a **girl around two years old**, nearly unable to speak, and a severely traumatized woman missing her **right forearm**. The woman repeats a single plea. **Rorge and Biter laugh** until rebuked. Hot Pie admits fear and reveals he lied when claiming to have killed another child, complicating his initial intimidating performance.
8. **VERIFIED:** The wounded woman dies by evening; **Gendry and Cutjack** bury her under a willow. Arya hears the leaves as if repeating her plea: this is her **perception**, not verified supernatural speech. The rescued child's later fate remains **UNKNOWN here**. Yoren prohibits a campfire as food runs short; even the water tastes strange, prompting an unverified claim of rotting corpses upstream.
9. **VERIFIED:** Arya slips past sentries to relieve herself alone and encounters a pack with **six pairs of shining wolf eyes**. One wolf comes closer and bares its teeth but withdraws; the pack disappears. Arya recalls expelling **Nymeria** from her side to save the direwolf from Cersei and wishes Nymeria had protected Ned. The wolves are not identified as Nymeria's group in this scene; compare the **rumor** of a huge she-wolf near the Gods Eye in `ACOK 5`.
10. **VERIFIED:** Yoren warns of wolf-like **human** raiders who ruined the village. He recalls losing only three recruits over nearly thirty years of past trips and wonders if taking a ship instead of his customary route would have been wiser. Compare the earlier death of **Praed** for precise scope and timing of his recollected tally rather than announcing a contradiction without proof.
11. **VERIFIED / AMBIGUOUS SOUND:** Exhausted Arya admits she wants home, despite cultivated ferocity. Near the end she hears howling and a more distant noise that **might be screams**. No source for those sounds is confirmed.

## Agency / competing explanations / next tests

**Arya** can take her horse and leave but reasonably sees that she would lose sentries and mutual protection. Her stealth offers control yet her isolated night excursion almost exposes her to wolves.

**Yoren** chooses avoidance and sheltering survivors, but the consequences of the longer route call his habitual decisions into question. **Countercase:** a ship may never have been a secure available option.

**War crime attribution:** Brynden's reports in the adjacent Catelyn chapter name raiders, but no one identifies this particular village's killers. The ordinary explanation is human wartime brutality; attaching a specific banner requires corroboration.

**Nymeria theory:** Earlier oversized she-wolf rumor, local geography and Arya's memories make a connection worth testing against later animal POVs. There are other wolves, and the one that approaches remains unnamed. Compare `ASOS 65 (Arya)`.
