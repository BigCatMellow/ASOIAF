## Narrative, decisions and consequences

1. **VERIFIED / APPROACHING SIEGE:** Sansa sees **smoke and night fires south of the Blackwater**, while Tyrion has **burned the civilian waterfront structures on the city side**. This confirms the **order in `ACOK 49 Tyrion` has materially been carried out**. The smoke obscures the sky, enters the Red Keep and compounds her terror. Stannis's **vanguard** has appeared across the river **two nights earlier**, described to Sansa as about **5,000 men** under **Ser Guyard Morrigen**, with **Fossoway, Estermont, Florent, Morrigen and burning-heart** banners. The number is reported, not independently counted by her.
2. **RIVER OPERATIONS:** Royal ferries are withdrawn, civilian galleys fled or impressed into wartime service, and **Joffrey's war galleys** patrol the Blackwater's deep channel, exchanging arrows with Stannis's archers across the river. **Dontos** tells Sansa a crossing requires Stannis's fleet to sail **around Massey's Hook, through the Gullet and across Blackwater Bay**. These facts ground why Stannis's army is delayed despite its apparent superiority; a successful crossing has **not happened** here.
3. **DONTOS'S MIXED-QUALITY INTELLIGENCE:** In the Red Keep **godswood**, drunk **Ser Dontos Hollard** meets Sansa and says Tyrion's **Mountain clans** raid the advancing army's scouts and supplies, while **both armies set fires** in the kingswood. He says that because he is regarded as a fool, courtiers speak before him and **Varys purchases gossip**; he specifically claims **Moon Boy** has long acted as Varys's informer. The existence of eavesdropping opportunities is plausible; **Moon Boy's actual employment is Dontos's uncorroborated claim**.
4. **FALSE CERTAINTY / PROPAGANDA:** Dontos repeats the claim that Stannis will **burn the Great Sept of Baelor**, describing it as a vow. `ACOK 49 Tyrion` directly showed Tyrion **instructing his installed High Septon to spread this alleged threat while admitting he did not know it was true**. Record the chain as **Tyrion's uncertain prediction → religious propaganda → public rumor**. Stannis's burning of the **Storm's End godswood** is separately reported; he is **not shown vowing to burn the Sept**.
5. **DONTOS'S ESCAPE CHANNEL:** Dontos says he has spoken with **an unnamed man he calls a trusted friend**, who will **hire a fast ship** when the moment is right. Sansa presses to leave **before the battle**, observing the castle is less closely watched, but Dontos says **the guarded city gates and sealed river** still prevent effective escape. **The backer is not named, the ship not yet hired as an observed fact, and no route is established**. This is an important continuing hidden-sponsor question rather than proof that Dontos can save her.
6. **PROTECTED LOCATION / UNPROTECTED PERSON:** With gold cloaks reassigned to the walls, **Maegor's Holdfast drawbridge is temporarily unguarded** and Sansa can roam inside the Red Keep, but **cannot leave**. She climbs to a roof to escape the suffocating bedchamber, sees the fires, **defensive hoardings**, and the three towering siege engines at the **Mud Gate**. The city's defensive measures are physically visible yet offer her no psychological reassurance.
7. **SANDOR'S PHYSICAL RESCUE AND THREAT:** **Sandor Clegane** grabs Sansa when she is startled atop the battlements, preventing a potential fall. He recalls saving her during the **Myrcella procession riot** (`ACOK 41`), and Sansa directly remembers his **severing a rioter's hand or arm to free her**. He is a genuine rescuer, but simultaneously taunts her over **Bran's paralysis** and Ned's execution. He **draws his sword and presses its edge against Sansa's neck**, then confesses pleasure in killing people including the weak. This is direct intimidation; **do not sanitize it because he has protected her before**.
8. **EXPLICIT HOUND WORLDVIEW:** Sandor rejects **knightly ideals, gods, mercy and protective chivalry**, saying strong arms and steel decide who lives. His conduct presents a contradiction: he protects Sansa in danger yet scares her personally. He recoils at the widespread fire across the river, describing fire as the weapon of cowards, consistent with his earlier burn trauma; the chapter **does not yet show how he responds in the coming battle**.
9. **SANSA'S TRAUMA:** After the confrontation Sansa dreams a **distorted recurrence of the mob attack**, calling unsuccessfully for relatives, Dontos and songbook heroes, before dreaming she is assaulted and disemboweled. This is a **trauma nightmare**, not evidence a future knife attack is prophetically foreseen. Her daily fear, crying and desire to escape must be interpreted against real hostage coercion, not as irrational childishness.
10. **DIRECT BIOLOGICAL EVENT / REPRODUCTIVE COERCION:** Sansa wakes to **her first menstrual bleeding**. She recognizes its court meaning: she can now be considered **capable of marriage consummation and bearing Joffrey's children**. In panic she tries to erase evidence, **cuts and burns her sheets and then attempts to burn the featherbed** with oil from a lamp, risking serious fire in the Red Keep. Servants extinguish the flames, pull her away and discover the bleeding despite her efforts. The event is **not an initiated wedding, pregnancy or consummation**.
11. **CERSEI'S AUDIENCE:** Queen **Cersei** summons her, identifies the bleeding as ordinary first flowering and discusses the dangers of **childbirth** and her own prolonged labor with **Joffrey**. Cersei claims **Robert repeatedly chose hunting instead of attending births**, while **Jaime stayed near or forced access to her birthing chambers**. These are **her first-person reminiscences**, not a verified medical report.
12. **CERSEI'S EXPLANATION FOR JOFFREY'S ABUSE:** Cersei says Joffrey **never forgot Arya humiliating him at the Trident** (`AGOT` Mycah/Nymeria episode), and now **punishes Sansa as a substitute**. She tells Sansa she could **thank her sister if Arya were not dead**. The reader has observed **Arya alive at Harrenhal** in `ACOK 47`; Cersei's statement is **mistaken belief, cruel rhetoric, or a politically useful assertion**, **not a confirmed death or disclosure of Arya's whereabouts**.
13. **CERSEI'S FAMILY OBSERVATIONS:** She recalls that **Joffrey cried when Robert held him**, unlike Robert's acknowledged bastards, an irony beside the **Jaime/Cersei paternity admission** elsewhere (`ACOK 55`). She criticizes **Robert's need to be loved** and Tyrion for a similar need. Cersei warns Sansa that **love is dangerous even when sweet**, expressing a philosophy formed by her life and power—**not an objective rule the narrative requires readers to accept**.
14. **ENDING:** Sansa's biological development has become known to servants and queen, amplifying fear of **Joffrey's threatened marriage**. **No completed marriage or escape** occurs. The battle's approach and the possibility of Dontos's hidden patron sharpen, while Sandor and Cersei represent distinct forms of conditional 'protection' that preserve threats.

## Hypotheses and later tests
