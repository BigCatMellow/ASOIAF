## Full chapter: key narrative and decisions

1. **VERIFIED / FAILED DIPLOMACY:** Daenerys leaves an audience with the **Pureborn**, Qarth's hereditary rulers, after they politely but decisively **refuse her request for a fleet or soldiers**. Access to the **Hall of a Thousand Thrones** required ceremonial offers at the **Temple of Memory**, a bribe to **the Keeper of the Long List**, gifts to the **Opener of the Door**, and payments or gifts to three influential petitioners, **Mathos Mallarawan, Wendello Qar Deeth and Egon Emeros the Exquisite**. This confirms she has spent wealth obtaining a hearing rather than secured an army. The Pureborn command the **Civic Guard and strait galleys**, but control does not imply willingness to give them to a foreign claimant.
2. **XARO'S CHARACTER GUIDANCE:** **Xaro Xhoan Daxos** advises that Qartheen courtesy, weeping and gifts may matter more than Dany's arguments. He warns that requesting return of bribes could lead to **Sorrowful Men** assassinating her. The Sorrowful Men are described as an ancient guild apologizing to victims; **Xaro's threat and description are interested testimony**, not an attempted killing witnessed in this scene.
3. **VERIFIED / QARTH POWER STRUCTURE:** The **Pureborn** are distinct from the rival merchant groups **the Thirteen** (Xaro's faction), the **Ancient Guild of Spicers**, and the **Tourmaline Brotherhood**. **Warlocks** are feared and compete for Dany's attention. Dany learns that possession of a power base or wealth does not guarantee shared goals among these groups. Their claims that other factions are pirates or frauds are political characterizations, not neutral audit.
4. **VERIFIED / DRAGON ECONOMY:** As dragons become famous, merchants and visitors give Dany goods from **Myr, Yi Ti, Asshai** and other distant trade routes, including **amber, dragonglass, spices and money**, a pair of **Jogos Nhai striped zorses**, and a purportedly magical preserved corpse. **Xaro organizes tribute and attention**; Dany sells nearly everything to recoup the expensive bribes, keeping a **three-headed dragon crown** given by the **Tourmaline Brotherhood**. The crown has **gold coils, silver wings and heads of jade, ivory and onyx**; it is political iconography and a symbol of refusal to repeat Viserys selling his mother's crown, not a proven prophetic relic.
5. **VERIFIED / DANY'S SELF-ASSESSMENT:** Dany calls herself a **splendid beggar**, recognizing that luxury, celebrity and a symbolic crown have not given her the real transports or political support needed to return to Westeros. She briefly considers returning to **Vaes Tolorro** and making the abandoned city bloom but refuses to abandon her claimed throne. She understands Viserys's humiliation more sympathetically without declaring his cruelty justified.
6. **XARO'S REFUSAL / MARRIAGE OFFER:** Xaro protests he lacks **warships** and that trade vessels are insecure or dispersed. Dany asks for ships from the **Thirteen**, estimating **130 vessels if each lent ten**. Xaro points out that ships without **crews**, loyalty and pay do not carry an invasion force, and asks Dany repeatedly to **marry him and remain in Qarth**. His lavish gifts include **one thousand miniature jeweled knights**—an 'army' only in a decorative sense. Dany recognizes that his words offer affection and treasure, not her requested military support.
7. **OBSERVED / IMPLIED PRIVATE MOTIVE:** Dany notices **Xaro gives little attention to her exposed breast**, in contrast with Jorah, and has **beautiful male attendants**. She reasons his expressions of desire are unconvincing. This alone is not enough to determine every aspect of Xaro's sexuality or whether the marriage offer is false; the later property custom provides a **more concrete strategic incentive**.
8. **VERIFIED / BAZAAR FIREFEAT:** At a crowded market, Dany watches a **firemage** create an unsupported, roughly **forty-foot flaming ladder**, climb it and vanish when its burning rungs disappear. **Jhogo** calls it a trick; the crowd provides cover for **organized pickpockets**, which Dany recognizes from her life in the Free Cities. Whether the climbing was accomplished by magic or a concealed trick cannot be independently determined just from what she sees, although the visual performance is unambiguous.
9. **QUAITHE'S CONSEQUENTIAL CLAIM:** **Quaithe**, in her red mask, appears unexpectedly, says the firemage's ability was far weaker **six months earlier**, when he could only do small fire displays with powders and wildfire, and asserts **his powers are now increasing because Dany has revived dragons**. The comparative prior performance and causal link are Quaithe's statements, **not direct tests** by Dany. The development is a major candidate for magic returning across the world, but the degree and mechanism remain unverified.
10. **QUAITHE'S WARNING:** She tells Dany to **leave Qarth soon or be unable to leave at all**, then gives a riddle involving traveling **south to go north**, **east to reach west**, **backward to go forward** and **beneath shadow to find light**. When Dany guesses **Asshai**, Quaithe says the destination can give her **truth**, not ships, gold or troops. This is a prophecy-like directional statement with no objective resolution here, and **Asshai is Dany's interpretation**, not expressly named as the sole destination by Quaithe.
11. **VERIFIED / DRAGONS' ACTUAL MATURITY:** Back at Xaro's manse, Dany chars a **snake** for the baby dragons. They have grown to roughly **twice their size at Vaes Tolorro**, squabble for food and breathe smoke. Dany realizes they will take **years to be war-capable** and must be **trained**, a skill she does not yet know. This is a stronger military counterweight than vague declarations of 'dragon blood': she recognizes both future force and present limits.
12. **JORAH'S REVEAL OF MARRIAGE LAW:** **Jorah** says Qartheen marriage custom allows each spouse **one irresistible wedding-gift request** from the other's possessions, whatever is named. Dany previously heard that spouses retain separate property after marriage and thus presumed the dragons safe. Jorah infers **Xaro wants one dragon** as his claim. This is a strong structural explanation for the offer, but the custom and Xaro's hidden intention are relayed by **Jorah**, not put to Xaro and tested in the chapter. It is **not established that Dany has already married or forfeited a dragon**.
13. **VERIFIED / TRUST IN ILLYRIO TESTED:** Jorah warns that **Illyrio Mopatis** might sell Dany again if lucrative; Dany insists he sheltered her and gave her eggs. Jorah reminds her that **Illyrio sold her to Khal Drogo** through a politically arranged marriage, which she recognizes as true and resents hearing. Jorah doubts an invasion by **foreign sellswords** could win Westerosi acceptance absent prior lordly alliances: **a claimant unknown to Westerosi lords** may be seen as a foreign aggressor. This is coherent political counsel despite his protective possessiveness.
14. **VERIFIED / CLIFFHANGER:** Dany says remaining in Qarth is unsafe and unrewarding but cannot confidently choose between west/east, Illyrio, Xaro or Quaithe. She nonetheless decides **Jorah must visit Pyat Pree the next day**, signaling a move toward the warlocks' promised answers. **No Undying audience takes place in this chapter**.

## Competing readings and future research

- **Dragons and world magic:** The ladder show is observed, Quaithe's prior comparison is testimony, and causal connection to dragon hatchings is a theory with real narrative support. Do not infer every supernatural occurrence elsewhere has this sole cause.
- **Marriage-trap theory:** Xaro's proposal could be driven by wealth, influence, sex or access to a dragon; Jorah's account of the customary gift is a sharper and testable motive than assumptions about Xaro's attractions.
- **Foreign restoration:** Dany's personal belief in hereditary right and public perception of invasion by strangers are not equivalent; Jorah has a practical objection she must answer.
