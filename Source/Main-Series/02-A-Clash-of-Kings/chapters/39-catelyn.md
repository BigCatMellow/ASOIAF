## Main plot and causal chain

1. **VERIFIED:** Catelyn and her party are intercepted **two days from Riverrun** by a scout wearing **House Frey's twin-tower badge**. **Brynden Blackfish** has accompanied Robb west; **Martyn Rivers**, one of Walder Frey's bastard sons, now leads his scouts, who bring Catelyn back across ravaged riverlands. Catelyn avoided **Bitterbridge** after Renly's death because the loyalty of his surviving widow and troops was uncertain. Tywin has left **Harrenhal marching west**, according to scout information; his actual movement is supported independently by `ACOK 38 (Arya)`, where Arya witnesses his army departing.
2. **SCOUT ACCOUNT WITH SEPARATE CONFIRMATION:** Rivers reports **Robb's Oxcross victory**, Stafford's death, the retreat of shattered western forces toward Lannisport, and subsequent western raids: **Karstark/Glover** along the coast, **Maege Mormont** taking thousands of cattle toward Riverrun, and **Greatjon Umber** seizing mines at **Castamere, Nunn's Deep and the Pendric Hills**. These operational specifics are Martyn's reports, not things Catelyn herself sees. Singer **Rymund the Rhymer** has already made a victory song called **Wolf in the Night**, showing how military reports become celebratory story.
3. **CRITICAL CROSS-POV SOLUTION:** When **Perwyn Frey** asks how Robb bypassed **Golden Tooth**, Rivers says Robb **did not capture the fortress** but followed a **goat path located by Grey Wind** through a stony defile and ridge beneath Lannister watchtowers, permitting single-file passage unnoticed. This **resolves the military route mystery Tyrion recorded in `ACOK 32 (Sansa)`** at the level of a scout's confident report. It does **not** establish whether Grey Wind located the track by scent, ordinary animal perception or conscious supernatural coordination.
4. **RUMOR CONTROL:** Rivers repeats a claim Robb cut out **Stafford's heart** and fed it to Grey Wind, but Catelyn calls it untrue. This and tales of giants/warg armies at Oxcross should remain **unverified slander and sensational retelling**, distinct from the fact that a direwolf aided a surprise attack.
5. **VERIFIED / NEW BRIENNE ALLEGIANCE:** Brienne asks to leave Catelyn's company and return alone to **Storm's End to kill Stannis** in fulfillment of her oath after Renly's death. Catelyn argues that dying in futile revenge serves no living person, while confessing her own desire to strangle **Cersei** for Ned's death. Brienne disputes kings' divine legitimacy, and Catelyn acknowledges even Robb's claim may need reassessment if protecting children requires peace. Brienne offers her sword to **Catelyn herself** rather than to Robb, because Catelyn believed her innocence and protected her against Renly's guards.
6. **VERIFIED / RECIPROCAL OATH:** Brienne kneels with **Renly's sword** and vows to keep Catelyn's counsel, shield her back and give her life if necessary, by **old gods and new**. Catelyn vows **hospitality, food, a place at her hearth and no dishonorable commands**. Catelyn additionally promises **not to restrain** Brienne from seeking Stannis when the time comes, which creates a possible future conflict between immediate protective service and vengeance. **This is an accomplished sworn relationship**, not merely admiration.
7. **VERIFIED / FORD PREPARATIONS:** Crossing the **Red Fork**, Catelyn sees Mallister pikemen guarding a ford deliberately seeded with **iron spikes and caltrops below water**. Such measures, and the return of banners **Blackwood, Bracken, Piper, Darry, Paege**, reveal **Edmure is gathering forces for a stand**, rather than simply sheltering in Riverrun while Tywin marches west. Refugees and livestock are being admitted into the castle; Catelyn worries they will consume stores during siege, whereas Edmure calls them **his people**. Both practical food risks and legitimate civilian-protection motives are directly present.
8. **CRITICAL PAYOFF TO `ACOK 25 TYRION`:** Catelyn sees several **red-cloaked Lannister men hanged** on Riverrun's walls. Edmure explains they came with **Cleos Frey's return embassy** and were initially allowed to stay as disarmed envoys. On the **fourth night** the disguised **four-person extraction group** Tyrion had concealed among Vylarr's escort attempted to free **Jaime**. One strong man killed two guards barehanded; another picked Jaime's lock, while an actor imitated **Edmure's voice** to have the River Gate opened. Edmure, absent at a personal engagement, returned by boat near dawn; a watchman recognized him and challenged the false orders. The operation briefly freed Jaime but **did not get him out**.
9. **VERIFIED AS EDMURE'S ACCOUNT / LOSSES:** Jaime acquired a sword and **killed Poul Pemford and the squire Myles**, gravely wounded **Delp**, and was recaptured. Edmure had **the infiltrators and some guards who joined them executed**, remaining Lannister red cloaks imprisoned; **Jaime is chained hand-and-foot to a wall in a deeper cell**. Cleos professes ignorance of the rescue plan and is confined in Jaime's former tower room. Edmure doubts him, but Cleos's knowledge is **unproven**, unlike the extraction attempt itself.
10. **IMPORTANT STORM'S END NEWS:** **Cortnay Penrose** has sent **three ravens** from Storm's End seeking a king to break Stannis's siege. Stannis demands delivery of **Edric Storm**, Robert's acknowledged **bastard son**, before letting the garrison leave unharmed. Penrose refuses. This reveals that **the boy is a central obstacle**, not just Renly's corpse or Penrose's stubborn pride. Brienne directly identifies Edric. Neither his custody after a transfer nor the final fate of Storm's End is depicted here.
11. **VERIFIED / STRATEGIC CONFLICT:** Edmure tells Catelyn he plans to field **8,000 infantry and 3,000 cavalry**, attempting to block Tywin's **Red Fork crossings**, while **Roose Bolton**, said to hold the ruby ford with **10,000**, is ordered to seize **Harrenhal**, joined by **Helman Tallhart and forces Robb left at the Twins**. His purpose is to trap Tywin between Riverrun, Harrenhal and Robb's return. Catelyn objects that moving the Twins garrison may loosen the hold on **Walder Frey's loyalty**; Edmure says Frey assistance, Robb's marriage bargain, Roose Bolton's new Frey marriage and the two wards at Winterfell are sufficient bonds. These are **Edmure's orders and expectations**, not a successful entrapment already performed.
12. **VERIFIED / MARRIAGE AND COMMUNICATION:** Catelyn learns **Stevron Frey died** and **Roose Bolton reportedly married a Frey daughter**, strengthening the significance of shifting Frey succession and growing Bolton-Frey kinship. She says wards may become hostages if needed. Robb wanted her to inspect Walder's daughters at **the Twins** as prospective brides; she **refuses to leave her dying father**. Since Robb is in the field, ordinary ravens cannot locate him; multiple Stark and Tully military decisions therefore unfold with **delayed or inadequate command coordination**.
13. **VERIFIED / HOSTER'S CONFUSED REGRET:** Bedridden **Hoster Tully**, feverish and heavily medicated, initially recognizes Catelyn but then **speaks as though she were Lysa** before her marriage to **Jon Arryn**, demanding she accept the older highborn husband and not name a **young stripling**, and begs forgiveness for a past act he insists was 'best.' His language suggests **an old dispute involving Lysa's preference and Hoster's compulsion**, but Catelyn does **not identify the boy**, nor does Hoster clearly describe what he did. Do **not** assert the specific pregnancy or medicinal intervention without later confirmation.
14. **VERIFIED / LORD'S DEATH NOT YET:** **Maester Vyman** administers **milk of the poppy**, reports he cannot postpone Hoster's death much longer and recommends summoning **Brynden** and **Lysa**. Catelyn believes Lysa will refuse to come. Hoster remains **alive** at this chapter's close, despite Catelyn anticipating imminent loss.
15. **VERIFIED / NED'S REMAINS:** **Silent sisters** accompanying Cleos have brought **Ned Stark's bones** to Riverrun, wrapped as a Stark lord, his severed head attached to the skeleton with **silver wire**. The sword laid with his remains **is not Ice**, which the Lannisters retained (see `ACOK 25`). Catelyn orders **Hal Mollen** and the sisters to carry the remains to **Winterfell's crypts** and sits alone with them. **Arrival and burial at Winterfell are not yet shown**; this is an explicit transport assignment with future tests.

## Theory challenges, consequences and next questions

- **Information warfare:** Tyrion's covert extraction was not hypothetical. Edmure's story corroborates detailed roles from Tyrion's plan and shows **failure after briefly freeing Jaime**. Future effects include mistrust of peace delegations and increased danger to Stark hostages.
- **Grey Wind/warg debate:** Animal route-finding is concrete. The claim that Robb literally used magical mind-control is not established by Rivers's report.
- **Edmure's independent plan:** The river defense is defensible against an invading lord, but **Robb's wider intentions are unknown to Edmure/Catelyn**. Do not assume preventing Tywin crossing must be strategically good for the whole coalition; test later outcomes against original orders and resulting Blackwater timetable.
- **Lysa/Hoster:** The confession fragment is a high-priority unresolved family secret; its evidence requires **Lysa's own testimony or later narrative corroboration**, not a guess.
