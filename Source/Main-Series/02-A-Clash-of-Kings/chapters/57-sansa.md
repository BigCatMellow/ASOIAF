## Chronology and causal events

1. **DIRECT START OF BLACKWATER BATTLE:** The Red Keep's **sept** has been singing since the first report of **enemy sails**. Sansa watches **Joffrey and Tyrion** depart fully armored for the city's defenses, with **Meryn Trant, Mandon Moore, Osmund Kettleblack** and guards. **Joffrey** rides an ostentatiously gilded horse, while **Tyrion** is plainer but carries practical weapons. Sansa notices the contrast between royal display and combat equipment, privately regarding Joffrey as hollow rather than heroic.
2. **SANSA'S REPEATED PUBLIC COERCION:** **Joffrey** summons her for his departure, newly armed with a sword named **Hearteater**, replacing the **Lion's Tooth** Arya took from him at the Trident. He **demands Sansa kiss the sword**, claiming she will later taste **Stannis's blood** on it. Sansa outwardly complies while inwardly hopes the weapon will be lost and Joffrey fail. This is **coerced symbolism of service**, not proof she endorses Joffrey's kingly claim.
3. **COMMAND ASSIGNMENTS AND UNCERTAINTY:** Tyrion says the battle may change Sansa's position and wishes he had **sent her with Tommen to Rosby**; he expects her relatively safe in **Maegor's Holdfast** but leaves his caveat unfinished. Joffrey says he will **command the Three Whores siege engines**, and Tyrion expects Stannis may not reach his bank. These are **reported assignments and hopes before combat**, not eyewitness evidence Joffrey will lead an actual successful charge.
4. **SEPT AND RELIGIOUS COMPLEXITY:** Sansa joins a remarkably diverse congregation of **servants, guards, women, children, knights and laborers** in the Red Keep sept. She lights candles at **all seven faces**, including the Stranger, and recalls Septa Mordane's teaching that **Mother and Warrior are aspects of one god**, while wondering whose prayers for protection and mercy take priority. The ritual shows **genuine spiritual and communal purpose**, not simply Cersei's arranged political propaganda.
5. **WHAT SANSA PRAYS FOR:** She sings for the living and dead on both sides: **Bran, Rickon, Robb, Arya, Jon Snow, Catelyn, Hoster, Edmure, her companions and Ned**, as well as former court figures, friends and **the Hound and Tyrion**, for their safety or relief from anger. This breadth sharply contrasts with Joffrey's bloodthirst. She believes Bran and Rickon have been lost, but this chapter does **not provide independent knowledge of their fate** after `ACOK 56` reveals the false heads.
6. **HER LIMIT ON PUBLIC OBEDIENCE:** When the officiating septon asks divine protection and victory **specifically for Joffrey as rightful king**, Sansa **leaves the crowded service** and inwardly hopes his sword and defenses fail. She can wish mercy for ordinary soldiers and even for Tyrion/Sandor without endorsing the royal faction that holds her captive.
7. **BATTLE SOUNDS DIRECTLY HEARD:** Outside the sept Sansa hears **horns, catapults and scorpions firing, impacts, flames and dying men** from the Blackwater. The actual engagement has **begun**. She does not observe the detailed ship maneuvers, chain/wildfire tactic or the final outcome; this is the experience of a protected but exposed hostage in a city at war.
8. **LOLLYS'S ONGOING TRAUMA / PREGNANCY CORROBORATED:** At Maegor's Holdfast, **Lady Tanda**, daughter **Falyse**, daughter **Lollys**, and **Lollys's new maid Shae** struggle to cross the drawbridge. Lollys repeatedly resists and clings to Shae, consistent with the earlier riot's sexual violence (`ACOK 41`). **Sansa's interior narration says Lollys's pregnancy is common gossip**, adding corroboration to **Shae's report in `ACOK 54 Tyrion`**, but not a physician's independently documented diagnosis. Falyse has arrived from **Stokeworth with a small soldier contingent**, partially addressing earlier requested reinforcements.
9. **SHAE'S PHYSICAL LOCATION CONFIRMED:** Sansa directly sees and hears **Shae** as Lollys's **short-haired dark-haired bedmaid**, and Falyse calls her by name. This gives independent cross-POV confirmation of the **placement Varys arranged in `ACOK 44` and Tyrion discussed in `ACOK 49/54`**. It is **not evidence Sansa knows Shae is Tyrion's secret lover** or that Cersei knows her identity; `ACOK 54` showed **Alayaya** captured in error.
10. **THE QUEEN'S BALLROOM:** The queen has assembled almost all the capital's **highborn women**, some aged men and boys in an enclosed **ballroom** with **silver-backed torches, food, music and heavily draped windows**. Sansa occupies the place of honor **beside Cersei as Joffrey's betrothed**. The luxurious atmosphere is a form of protection and denial: battle noise is muffled but cannot be eliminated. **No actual assault reaches the ballroom in this chapter**.
11. **MAJOR SYMBOLIC OBJECT / THREAT:** **Ser Ilyn Payne**, King's Justice, waits in the rear with **Ice**, Ned Stark's immense **Valyrian-steel sword**, which Catelyn's earlier chapter `ACOK 39` had confirmed **was not returned with Ned's remains**. Its presence here is direct physical corroboration of continued Lannister control over Ice, and a personal threat to Sansa whose father was executed by the same headsman. **Osfryd Kettleblack** says Cersei expects to need him before the night ends.
12. **CERSEI'S MORALE/CUSTODY EXPLANATION:** When Sansa asks why Ilyn is there, **Cersei** says he will **punish treason and defend the ladies if necessary**, noting he had once been a knight. Cersei openly doubts her **red-cloaked sellsword guards** would stay loyal if the Red Keep falls; she predicts they might discard cloaks, loot and flee. This is a character's informed prediction, **not proof she has privately ordered Ilyn to execute Sansa or every woman present**. The ominous combination of **the king's executioner, an earlier hostage, and a failing-city contingency** warrants testing in the next Sansa scene without claiming a secret command already shown.
13. **CULTURAL AND HUMAN COST:** Cersei mocks Sansa's hope that 'true knights' would protect women and children, reminding her cities fall into **indiscriminate violence**. Yet Sansa's hope is already troubled by the presence of Ilyn with Ice and soldiers whose loyalties are purchasable. Cersei's protective role is deeply **conditional**, while Sansa increasingly understands the gulf between songs and institutional violence.
14. **ENDING BOUNDARY:** The battle is underway, the women are assembled, and **Ser Ilyn stands armed**. **Neither the Blackwater's outcome nor Ilyn's intended specific victim is known** at the end of this chapter.
