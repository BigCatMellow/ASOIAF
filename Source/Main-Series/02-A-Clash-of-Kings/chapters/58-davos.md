## Causal narrative and observed action

1. **FLEET AND FAMILY:** **Davos Seaworth** approaches King's Landing aboard **Black Betha**, with sons commanding adjacent ships **Wraith** and **Lady Marya** (Dale and Allard are named in the battle); **Matthos** is aboard his flagship relaying orders, while younger sons including **Maric** are part of the fleet's family losses at risk. Davos's personal seamanship and care for his sons are important contrasts with the lordly fleet command. **No son's final fate is established by this chapter.**
2. **CHAIN OF COMMAND:** Stannis stays with his **mounted army on the south bank** and gives the fleet to his wife's brother **Ser Imry Florent**, now a lordly newcomer to the king's side. **Fury**, Stannis's powerful **300-oar flagship**, carries scorpions and catapults but is **overloaded with armored fighting men**. Imry's high social status and certainty are not substitute evidence of naval competence.
3. **FAILED SCOUTING ADVICE:** At a council three days earlier Davos advised sending **fast scouts upriver** to test the harbor and boom works. Imry dismissed the caution because he commanded roughly **four times the number of royal ships**, organizing **ten lines of twenty ships**. The leading lines would crush the king's fleet, following lines land troops, rear vessels ferry the army; **Salladhor Saan's Lyseni**, whom Imry mistrusted, were left in the bay to cover the rear. These are **the actual battle dispositions** disclosed in retrospect, not a successful plan.
4. **FORCED TIMETABLE:** The fleet had lost **two cogs** at Shipbreaker Bay and **a Myrish galley** in the Straits of Tarth; storms scattered vessels at the Gullet, delaying the fleet until all but **twelve** regrouped behind Massey's Hook. Meanwhile **Stannis's approximately 20,000 mounted men** reached the south bank first and waited for ships. These factors help explain Imry's haste without proving it was strategically necessary.
5. **EARLY WARNINGS:** Fishermen captured off **Merling Rock** said Tyrion was building a boom across the river mouth but disagreed whether it had been finished. Davos suspects **the current** favors the defenders and the narrow Blackwater will negate a larger fleet's envelopment advantage. He notes the fleet cannot extend more than about **twenty ships abreast** without collisions and fouled oars.
6. **UNUSED RELIGIOUS SYMBOLISM:** Davos privately wishes Stannis had used **Robert's traditional crowned stag** instead of the **flaming-heart emblem**, believing the latter alienates King's Landing. This is an informed sailor's **political opinion**, not evidence the city would actually welcome Stannis if he changed flags.
7. **THE GATEWAY DELIBERATELY OPEN:** Davos identifies the **new small towers** on opposite sides of the Blackwater mouth from his memory of smuggling routes. They have high doors and a moatlike defensive cut. As he passes he sees **enormous chain links partly submerged** and recognizes the **river-mouth boom remains open intentionally**. His thought that the royal defenders mean to lure ships inside is an **on-page tactical inference**; later events corroborate it.
8. **CROSS-POV MISSING SHIPS EXPLAINED:** Davos notices **King Robert's Hammer**, **Lionstar** and **Lady Lyanna** are missing from Joffrey's fleet. `ACOK 41 Tyrion` had already placed these warships on **Myrcella's convoy toward Braavos**, explaining their absence without positing a hidden second royal fleet. It makes the smaller royal navy part of a broader diplomatic resource tradeoff.
9. **SHIPS AS PHYSICAL TARGETS:** Davos sees wildfire's precursor **burning pitch**, city catapults and scorpions striking Stannis's starboard wing as the lines pass under the Red Keep. Royal galleys **back water to draw attackers upriver**, and Davos recognizes the danger of clogging the channel with so many ships and oars. The north bank is defended by **Sandor Clegane**, who rides onto the troop-laden **Prayer** while breaking one of the landing attempts. Tyrion's waterfront clearances have left a **burned strip** before the Mud Gate and denied easy access to intact docks.
10. **DAVOS FIGHTS, NOT JUST OBSERVES:** Black Betha and Lady Marya **ram Lady's Shame** simultaneously while it is locked with **Queen Alysanne** and **Lady of Silk**; they rupture and sink the Lannister ship. Davos later meets **White Hart** in a glancing collision, leads a **boarding party**, suffers an axe blow glanced off his helm, kills an attacker at close quarters, and takes the royal galley. His direct actions demonstrate courage under risk; his earlier warnings cannot fairly be dismissed as cowardice.
11. **WILDFIRE FIRST APPEARS LOCALLY:** Davos spots a green explosion on **Queen Alysanne**, followed by burning men jumping into water; he knows from experience that **wildfire resists smothering and burns flesh in contact**. Imry had told his captains to expect a little of it but assumed the alchemists **could not produce very much**. `ACOK 49 Tyrion` revealed **nearly 13,000 jars**, so the attackers' intelligence about supply was profoundly outdated.
12. **EARLY TACTICAL SUCCESS WITH STRATEGIC COST:** Before the trap closes, **Stannis's ships sink, board, disable or force ashore many royal vessels**, and some Stannis transports start moving soldiers across the river. Davos estimates Imry may win the channel, **but at ruinous cost**. Explicitly distinguish **partial immediate ship-to-ship successes** from the final outcome once the trap is triggered.
13. **TRAP MECHANISM DISCLOSED:** Davos sees a **low, decrepit floating hulk** taking on water and **leaking green substance**. The slow **Swordfish**, whose giant iron ram he had repeatedly criticized, **charges and breaks the hulk open**. Numerous **wildfire jars** pour out and ignite in a spreading explosion. Davos attempts to order **Black Betha astern**, but is thrown into the river by the blast.
14. **MASS BURNING BEYOND TARGET SELECTION:** Davos sees a vast **green fire** racing among packed galleys, burning both sides: **Black Betha, White Hart, Fury, Piety, Cat, Courageous, Sceptre, Red Raven, Harridan, Faithful, Kingslander, Godsgrace, Pride of Driftmark** and others in differing states of fire. The fire is not a precise weapon: currents, boarding collisions, rigging and burning pitch spread it unpredictably. Each sighting is **damage/burning**, **not verified total sinking or named captain death**.
15. **SECOND HULK AND CHAIN RISE:** While Davos clings to debris another **wildfire hulk detonates**. He sees the great **chain raised across the river mouth**, now **a few feet above the water**, with ships crashing against it and burning downstream. **Salladhor Saan's striped ships are visible beyond the trap**, still outside the river. Davos fears he cannot get past the burning chain. This is the **direct witnessed payoff** to the tower works and boom planned in `ACOK 20/41/49`.
16. **ENDING BOUNDARY:** **Davos survives the explosion long enough to surface and cling to wreckage**, but the chapter ends **with him in the river, trapped by the chain and mass inferno**. It does **not** show his eventual rescue, whether Stannis wins or loses the whole siege, a confirmed list of dead Seaworth sons, or the final fate of Imry Florent.

## Competing theories and later tests

- **Engineering plus enemy arrogance:** The chain alone does not destroy ships; the success depends on **attracting a mass of vessels into confined water, igniting wildfire hulks and then raising the boom**. Imry's failure to scout is necessary context, but **weather, time and royal deception** also matter.
- **Religious and logistical command:** Davos's concern that the fiery-heart flag alienates the city is a coherent political claim, not a quantified explanation for defenders' loyalty. Imry's exclusion of Saan and disregard of Davos underline hierarchical failures.
- **Magical conflation warning:** The supernatural **shadow at Storm's End** is directly witnessed in `ACOK 42`; **wildfire** is a material alchemical weapon. The green blaze's appearance does not alone establish a living dragon attack. The Hallyne/Quaithe correlation about stronger spells is a separate theory.
- **Seaworth family accounting:** List ships and sons separately. A flaming ship or Davos's panicked thought of sons is not a witnessed death. Reconcile future Davos POV and any return from Blackwater.
