## Plot, choices, consequences

1. **VERIFIED:** Yoren cuts Arya's hair and presents her as **Arry**, an orphan boy traveling with some **thirty** recruits for the Night's Watch. Her disguise works because the Lannister gate guards search for a noble girl, while Yoren is personally acquainted with a guard. She leaves without looking back.
2. **VERIFIED:** Five wagons carry goods for the Wall, including **books, paper, ink, ravens, medicine, cloth, hides, pig iron and foodstuffs**. Yoren obtained men from royal dungeons and destitute boys from the streets. He warns Arya that some will sell her to the queen, or assault her, if they learn her identity.
3. **VERIFIED:** Arya keeps **Needle**, Jon's castle-forged gift, and a **wooden practice sword**. Lommy Greenhands and Hot Pie taunt her and try to take Needle. The boy nicknamed **the Bull** objects to bullying her. Arya uses her practice sword and Syrio's footwork to brutally beat Hot Pie after he threatens her, including striking even when he is incapacitated.
4. **VERIFIED:** Yoren drags Arya aside and **whips the back of her bare legs three times**, drawing blood and leaving her unable to ride for days. He explicitly tells her that Hot Pie and Lommy did not kill Ned. His real concern includes her identity, group cohesion and the damage uncontrolled anger could cause.
5. **YOREN'S DIRECT ACCOUNT:** He had already purchased and packed wagons when **an unidentified man** arrived with **a boy, purse of money and a message** instructing him to wait because **Ned was expected to take the black**. Yoren was therefore present at Baelor's Sept for a **planned exile**, not because he foresaw an execution. **UNKNOWN:** identity of messenger and accompanying boy, who exactly authorized the change, and whether Yoren would have succeeded transporting Ned.
6. **VERIFIED:** Arya knows Joffrey ordered Ned's death and longs for revenge, yet retains concern for Sansa still in King's Landing. At night she sees the **red comet as Ice carrying her father's blood**, but it is **her interpretation**, not an observed cosmic instruction. She yearns most to be reunited with Jon.
