## Main narrative chronology and characters' knowledge

1. **AFTER THE UNDYING:** Dany rejects an ornate **ivory Qartheen gown** and chooses **Dothraki riding clothes**, dagger, mare and newly braided **bell** to visit Qarth's harbor. **Jhiqui** regards the destruction of the House of the Undying as a victory warranting a bell; Dany privately credits **Drogon** for defeating the Undying (`ACOK 48`). **Rakharo** stays guarding her khalasar and dragons; **Jhogo, Aggo and Jorah** accompany her. This is identity management as well as practical travel.
2. **LOST QARTHEN SPONSORSHIP:** Xaro warns that **Pyat Pree is gathering surviving warlocks against Dany** following their palace's destruction. The **Tourmaline Brotherhood calls for expulsion**, the **Ancient Guild of Spicers demands her death**, and Xaro claims he can barely restrain **the Thirteen**. These are political developments through Dany/Xaro's information channels, **not witnessed executions or organized assaults by every group**.
3. **MANY REPORTS OF STRONGER MAGIC, NOT DIRECTLY VERIFIED:** Xaro says **glass candles are burning in the house of Urrathon Night-Walker**, something thought impossible for a century; **ghost grass** grows in a garden, **phantom tortoises** carry messages, **rats chew their tails**, a merchant's wife suffers intense skin torment after slighting a warlock, and **Blind Sybassion allegedly sees again**. This is a **cluster of rumors collected by a trader** with reasons to frighten Dany away, not an independent demonstration of each marvel. The glass candles are a concrete, testable recurring object to track with `ACOK 40` Quaithe and `ACOK 49` Hallyne.
4. **QARTH NO LONGER SAFE:** Dany recognizes that comfortable lodging and gifts had not provided real ships or armies; after Pyat's defeat her presence has become commercially and magically dangerous to her hosts. **Jorah recommends farther east**, her bloodriders prefer the **Dothraki sea**, and Dany considers **Vaes Tolorro** until the dragons grow. All are **considered options**, none undertaken before the harbor visit.
5. **EXPLICIT DRAGON TRANSACTION:** Xaro, after repeatedly proposing marriage (`ACOK 40`), offers **ten, then twenty, then thirty ships** for **one dragon**, expressly claiming it is only one of three and Dany can breed more. This strongly corroborates **Jorah's earlier theory** that Xaro wanted a dragon more than Dany's hand, while the present **sale offer is directly spoken and rejected**. **No dragon changes owner here.**
6. **FLEET COUNTS FROM AN INTERESTED PARTY:** Xaro says he owns **83 trading ships**, the **Thirteen collectively perhaps 1,000**, **Spicers 1,200–1,300** and **Tourmaline Brotherhood around 800**. Dany argues one of the only **three dragons alive** is worth **a third of all the world's ships**. These are **Xaro's approximate commercial claims and Dany's rhetorical price**, not verified fleet censuses or a realistic proposed transaction.
7. **BREAK WITH XARO:** When Dany refuses, Xaro ends support, demands **she leave his mansion**, stops feeding her people and asks return of gifts. His outward weeping and lavish declarations of love cannot override his actual conduct. The prince and merchant's incentives remain economic, political and personally ambiguous; **the direct stated object is a dragon**.
8. **PROPHECY REMAINS OPEN:** Dany revisits the Undying's **three fires, mounts, treasons and three-headed dragon**. She tentatively identifies **Mirri Maz Duur** as a treason for blood, but doubts **Xaro and Pyat** satisfy the gold and love categories. Her attempted slotting is **speculation by the subject of the prophecy**, not an authoritative resolution. **Jorah** counters that the Targaryen three-headed emblem historically invokes **Aegon, Visenya and Rhaenys**, supplying a symbolic reading beyond future riders.
9. **RHAEGAR'S VISION CROSS-CHECK:** Dany describes seeing **Rhaegar with a silver-stringed harp** by a woman nursing infant **Aegon** and hearing an invocation of **the prince that was promised** and the **song of ice and fire**. **Jorah** independently recognizes the harp as fitting **Prince Rhaegar** and identifies **his son Aegon by Elia of Dorne** as the murdered royal infant. This strengthens the probable **Rhaegar/Elia/Aegon identification** of the scene seen in `ACOK 48`, without proving the Undying vision's prophecy was true or that the apparent death record is complete.
10. **'MUMMER'S DRAGON' LANGUAGE:** Jorah asks about a vision of a **cloth dragon carried on poles**; Dany explains that **mummers use such props in plays for heroes to fight**. This is a **source-native definition**, tightening the leading **performance/false legitimacy interpretation** of the earlier dragon-on-poles vision, but still **does not name a specific claimant or puppet master**.
11. **HARBOR ECONOMY AND SLAVERY:** The Qartheen waterfront openly markets **slaves**, including a newly arrived **Spicer galley Sunblaze from Astapor**. Dany passes quays of the powerful guilds and proceeds to the cheaper, distant berths where Free Cities and Westerosi ships dock. There she requests passage for **roughly a hundred Dothraki, their horses, her people, herself, Jorah and three dragons**. Captains reject her on grounds of **fire risk, Dothraki prejudice, route mismatch, insufficient capacity or exorbitant fare**. This directly grounds the logistics of invasion and exile beyond magical grandeur.
12. **TWO MEN FOLLOW HER:** Jorah detects **an elderly white-haired staff-bearer** and a very large brown-skinned **bald man** following Dany through the docks. They appear Westerosi/exotic respectively, and Dany fears bounty hunters for Robert's prize or agents of **the warlocks**. Jorah uses a **polished brass platter** as a mirror to check their movements while a merchant absurdly haggles from **thirty honors down toward two**. Their presence is verified; **hostile intent is not**.
13. **DIRECT ASSASSINATION ATTEMPT:** A **Qartheen stranger** interrupts with a beautifully carved jewel box, apologizing as Dany opens it. An apparent **green scarab** within unfolds into a **venomous manticore**, poised to sting her. The elderly pursuer **strikes the box/creature away with his hardwood staff**, hurting Dany's hand but preventing the sting. The assassin **escapes into the crowd** before identification or interrogation; Dany calls him a **Sorrowful Man**, consistent with earlier warnings about that apologetic guild (`ACOK 40`), but **specific commissioning party and name remain unknown**.
14. **IDENTITIES CLAIMED, NOT FULLY PROVEN:** The elderly man calls himself **Arstan**, called **Whitebeard** by his companion. The giant names himself **Strong Belwas**, a scarred **eunuch fighter from Meereen's pits** who says each opponent is allowed to cut him once before he wins; this is **his own boast**, not an audited duel record. Belwas initially insults Dany's Dothraki bloodriders; **Aggo** nearly challenges him, and Dany commands him to respect her people. This establishes her **active authority over new retainers** at the moment they meet.
15. **ARSTAN'S WESTEROSI CLUES:** Arstan switches fluently into the **Common Tongue**, says he was born on the **Dornish Marches**, squired for a knight in **House Swann's household**, and later saw **Jorah fight at Lannisport and on Pyke**. Jorah senses a **familiar face** but cannot identify him from crowds at those events. Arstan's quiet dignity and knowledge imply more experience than a conventional old servant, but **his full identity, allegiance and credibility are not established by his chosen name**.
16. **ILLYRIO AS REPORTED PATRON:** Both say **Magister Illyrio Mopatis of Pentos** sent them to bring Dany and her dragons back. They report Illyrio cannot comfortably travel himself; Arstan cites **Robert's death and multiple competing kings** as the cause for urgency. **Dany knows Illyrio arranged her marriage to Drogo and gave her dragon eggs** (`ACOK 40`), so his favorable acts and capacity to exploit her must remain side by side. **The exact political price Illyrio expects is not disclosed**.
17. **ACTUAL SHIPS AND NEW NAMES:** Belwas and Arstan identify **three hired ships** ready for her: the cog **Saduleon** plus the galleys **Summer Sun** and **Joso's Prank** at the far quays. Dany agrees to prepare her followers to depart, but orders the ships **renamed Vhagar, Meraxes and Balerion**, the three conqueror dragon names, in large golden letters. This is **a concrete planned embarkation with available hulls**, not proof she has sailed, reached Pentos or obtained an invading army.
18. **ENDING POSITION:** Dany survived a credible assassination attempt because a new stranger intervened; she has acquired **possible means of travel** from her old sponsor while warlock retaliation and Qartheen guild hostility remain live. The offered ships and the new helpers' true motivations must be followed in next Dany POVs.

## Rival hypotheses and follow-up tests

- **Magic's return:** The **glass candles** report adds a concrete object to Quaithe's rising-firemage claim (`ACOK 40`) and Hallyne's improved alchemy spells (`ACOK 49`). But unlike Hallyne's own account of his craft, **the candle report is passed through Xaro**, mixed with unverifiable port rumors. Keep evidence tiers distinct.
- **Arstan Whitebeard:** An elderly Westerosi staff-fighter, familiar to Jorah and able to place events at Lannisport/Pyke, merits a **hidden-identity hypothesis**, but no positive identification is made here. Future statements and recognitions should test the claimed Marches and Swann background.
- **Illyrio:** His agents both rescue and seek to transport the dragon queen. His previous patronage may be altruistic, instrumental or mixed; **he receives no dragon or explicit fealty in the chapter**.
- **Sorrowful Men and Qarth:** The apology before the attack and the familiar guild lore are significant. The employer could be a merchant faction, Pyat's surviving warlocks or another rival; the chapter does not identify a sponsor.
- **Three heads / false dragon:** Rhaegar's apparent son Aegon, Dany's stage-prop clarification and three dragon names all invite later contender theories, but prophecy identities remain open and separate from on-page ship renaming.
