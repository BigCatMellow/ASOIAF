## Full narrative and verified developments

1. **VERIFIED:** Tyrion enters **Cersei's chambers** while **Lancel Lannister** and performers entertain her. Cersei has ordered **Vylarr** to imprison about six street preachers for denouncing Lannister conduct, overriding Bywater and Tyrion; **Lancel** favors cutting out tongues. The great defensive **chain** ordered previously is still **under construction**, with hammers continually working, not yet complete or deployed.
2. **VERIFIED:** Tyrion reveals **Stannis is besieging Storm's End** and **Renly is moving to confront him**, corroborating the messenger's report in `ACOK 22 (Catelyn)`. Cersei and Tyrion unexpectedly laugh and embrace at their enemies' internal conflict. Cersei says **Robert deliberately slighted Stannis** by giving Storm's End to Renly; this is **Cersei's account** of motive, not a private admission by Robert.
3. **VERIFIED:** Tyrion privately sprinkles **fine powder in Cersei's wine** while her back is turned. Next morning she is unwell and cannot leave her chambers/privy; Tyrion internally treats the condition as the expected result. This establishes a **surreptitious drugging** and apparent temporary illness. His earlier pocketed bottle in `ACOK 17` is the plausible source, but **the powder's exact name/composition is not given in this scene**, and her medical condition is not independently diagnosed. His action is an abuse of power even if intended as temporary political disruption.
4. **VERIFIED:** Sitting publicly on the **Iron Throne** in Cersei's absence, Tyrion rejects Robb's independence terms. He demands Robb lay down arms, swear fealty, give Jaime command over Stark forces against the Baratheons, free Jaime and supply **a child hostage from every bannerman**. He announces that another Lannister host is training at Casterly Rock and **claims Doran has agreed to a Myrcella–Trystane betrothal**; the announcement is direct dialogue, but Doran's signed reply is not separately depicted. Tyrion offers **Harrion Karstark and Wylis Manderly** for **Willem Lannister**, and **Lord Cerwyn and Donnel Locke** for **Tion Frey**, prioritizing Lannister value. He will send **Ned's bones**, but withholds **Ice** until peace, and will keep **Sansa and purportedly Arya** as hostages pending Jaime's safe release. Tyrion knows Arya is missing, whereas the public statement conceals this.
5. **VERIFIED:** Tyrion orders **Vylarr** to escort Cleos's party back with **all 100 red-cloaked Lannister guards**, against Pycelle's protest that Tywin intended them to protect Cersei. This materially strips the queen of her separate armed force while leaving Tyrion's personal men and Bywater's Watch. **Cleos departs that afternoon** with his escort and original northern personnel: the first trip's return **is now underway**, not yet arrived at Riverrun.
6. **VERIFIED / SEPARATE SECRET OBJECTIVE:** Tyrion tells **Varys** that **four criminals** recruited by Bronn—a thief, poisoner, actor and killer—will be disguised as guards among Vylarr's hundred, enter **Riverrun** with the escort to Ned's bones, and try to **free Jaime**. Varys must sell the loss of guards to Cersei as part of that rescue plan. Their infiltration and outcome are **not shown in this chapter**, though the cover party's departure is.
7. **VERIFIED:** **Alliser Thorne** interrupts court to report two dead rangers **reanimated at Castle Black**, one killing **Jaremy Rykker** and the other attacking **Mormont**. His preserved **hand of Jared** has **rotted to bones while Tyrion kept him waiting**, so the physical specimen no longer demonstrates the phenomenon. Tyrion privately recalls a real unease at the Wall and respects Jeor, but fears being made a laughingstock; he **openly mocks Thorne** with an offer of spades instead of investigating. He gives the Watch prison recruits, which also reduces mouths in the starving city. This is a **policy failure in assessment of evidence**, not a confirmation the undead did not rise.
8. **VERIFIED:** **Littlefinger** confronts Tyrion over the false **Myrcella-for-the-Vale** disclosure, saying he dislikes being deceived. Tyrion deflects with a claim that the Dornish offer later became real; Petyr is not appeased. **Varys** admires the combined effects of replacing Tywin's queen guards and managing the Watch petition, demonstrating his clear awareness of Tyrion's political aims.
9. **VERIFIED / DIRECT EXPOSURE OF INFORMER:** At midnight Tyrion brings **Shagga and Timett** to **Pycelle's rooms**, breaks the door and confronts him. Tyrion explicitly articulates the three-way trap: **Pycelle alone held the sealed truth of Myrcella–Dorne**, Littlefinger was told Myrcella–Vale, Varys Tommen–Dorne. Tyrion states Pycelle sent **only one letter** and gave the other to **Cersei**. Pycelle first denies this and blames Varys, but then admits he has served the Lannisters for years; the explicit allocation of secret information and **Pycelle's concessions under threat** substantiate the leaker's identity. The exact physical handover of the letter is *reported by Tyrion*, not pictured in a flashback.
10. **PYCELLE'S CHARACTER TESTIMONY UNDER COERCION:** Threatened with mutilation, Pycelle says **he persuaded Aerys to open King's Landing's gates** to Tywin during the Sack; that he tended **Robert's wounds** but denies deliberately killing him; and that he **dismissed Maester Colemon** when Colemon was purging **Jon Arryn**, although he says he did not administer poison. Pycelle attributes poisoning to Arryn's squire **Hugh** and claims Cersei silently wished Arryn dead. **These are not all independently verified confessions of murder**. Tyrion speculates Pycelle could have finished Robert or poisoned Arryn, but Pycelle explicitly denies those acts.
11. **VERIFIED:** Tyrion has Shagga **shave Pycelle's beard with an axe**, terrorizing and physically cutting him; the frightened maester supplies more testimony. Tyrion orders him **imprisoned in a black cell**, then removes more medicine jars from Pycelle's quarters. His distrust of all three advisers remains despite catching one; he cannot conclude Varys or Littlefinger loyal simply because their stories did not reach Cersei in this test.
