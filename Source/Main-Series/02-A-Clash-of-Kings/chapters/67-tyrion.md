## Narrative chronology and major consequences

1. **TRAUMATIC RECOVERY / INITIAL DREAM:** Tyrion lies feverish, intermittently unconscious amid memories of Blackwater injuries and **filth, burning and dying men**. In a nightmare he walks a gray field of corpses, recognizes that **his commands killed multitudes on both sides**, and discovers his **mouth vanished beneath unbroken skin**. The bodily mutilation and speechlessness echo his actual facial injuries, but **the dream is not a prophetic physical event**.
2. **THE WOUNDED HAND IS ALIVE:** Tyrion awakes in a bed with drawn curtains, confused about where he is and whether he still has a mouth. **Podrick Payne** appears with a candle and summons a **maester**. The healer administers **milk of the poppy through a copper funnel** attached to a plasterlike mask of his bandaged face, putting Tyrion back to sleep. These are **direct medical events**, not evidence Cersei personally poisoned him.
3. **DREAMED PRAISE / REAL NEGLECT:** In a victory dream Tyrion is acclaimed as hero, **Jaime knights him**, **Tywin approves**, and **Shae embraces him**. He wakes alone in a cold, unfamiliar room. The contrast between imagined recognition and actual loss of status structures the chapter. It is a **dream of approval**, not proof anyone has granted him knighthood.
4. **TRAUMATIC MEMORIES OF THE ATTACK:** Tyrion vividly recalls **Ser Mandon Moore offering a hand across the broken ship bridge before striking his face**, and recognizes his survival depended on **Podrick's intervention** from `ACOK 61`. That he is now alive independently checks the reports of his death in `ACOK 62 Sansa` as premature or false. He has not yet identified an outside conspirator.
5. **HIDDEN ROOM / LOSS OF POWER:** Tyrion tears down his bed curtains to discover he is **not in his own Tower of the Hand quarters**. He fears the relocation means he has been hidden and abandoned. At this point it is **his anxiety**, not proven attempted medical murder. His voice is faint and his strength minimal; he cannot call reliable guards.
6. **THE TYShA MEMORY:** Fever dreams return to his marriage with **Tysha**, living in a poor cottage by the sea. He remembers detailed tenderness, intimacy and her calling him handsome and loved. Then he rejects those recollections as a purchased **'lie'** he was previously told by Jaime and Tywin (`AGOT Tyrion`). His conviction that Tysha deceived him is **the current character's belief**, not an independently established truth about her intentions. The dreams reveal an enduring wound from family coercion, not only longing for Shae.
7. **REFUSING SEDATION:** The maester attempts another dose of milk of the poppy; Tyrion **grabs the man's chain around his neck and chokes him** until the healer promises to listen. Tyrion demands his face bandages removed **despite the man's warning**, and rejects further poppy. This is active agency and fear of helplessness, but also direct violence against a caretaker who has not been shown to intend harm.
8. **UNBANDAGED INJURIES:** The maester, **Ballabar**, describes a **broken rib**, an infected **arrow wound near the shoulder**, treated with wine and **maggots**, and a severe facial slash. When Tyrion inspects himself in a mirror, he sees a diagonal scar from **left eye to right jaw**, **about three-quarters of his nose missing** and damage to his lip, all crudely stitched. These are **firsthand observations and a medical account**: permanent disfigurement is evident, but exact future healing is not shown.
9. **SUSPECTING CERSEI, NOT PROVING PATRONAGE:** Tyrion believes **Cersei paid Mandon Moore to murder him** in the battle. He notes he knew Trant, Blount and Osmund were susceptible to Cersei, but had trusted Moore more. **No payment, instruction, intercepted letter or confession is presented**. Index as **Tyrion's suspect hypothesis**, even though Moore's attack itself was directly witnessed.
10. **LOCATION CONFIRMED:** Ballabar reports Tyrion is housed in **Maegor's Holdfast in a chamber over the Queen's Ballroom**, and asserts **Cersei wished to keep him nearby to oversee his recovery**. Tyrion distrusts the explanation. He orders return to his old chambers, but Ballabar says **Tywin has taken over the Tower of the Hand**.
11. **THE POLITICAL REVERSAL IS REAL:** Ballabar says **Tywin and the Tyrells relieved King's Landing**, with **Loras and Littlefinger** involved, and Tywin resumed the Handship the night of battle. The received report broadly corroborates `ACOK 62/65`. Tyrion has been **stripped of the office he held and the rooms he worked from**, despite engineering the chain and fighting the sorties. The supposed sight of 'Renly's ghost' is framed as **popular superstition**, not verified resurrection.
12. **LOYALTY OF PODRICK AND BRONN:** Tyrion demands **Podrick Payne**, wants **Maester Frenken** instead of Ballabar to prepare **dreamwine**, and sends for **Bronn**. Pod reports Bronn has **been knighted** since the battle, a concrete promotion but not evidence his personal loyalty to Tyrion has ended. Tyrion is rebuilding a private circle, because his formal office is gone.
13. **MANDON'S DEATH AND POD'S ROLE CONFIRMED:** Tyrion asks Pod about Moore; the boy **flinches and stammers that he never meant to kill**, then says Moore **drowned**. This strengthens the interpretation that **Pod directly caused Moore's fatal fall while rescuing Tyrion**, beyond the earlier last-second unidentified push, although no blow-by-blow reconstruction is volunteered. Tyrion instructs Pod to **remain silent about both the attack and their actions**. The secrecy may complicate later investigation.
14. **ENDING STATE:** Tyrion is **alive, disfigured, weakened, displaced from power**, determined not to remain under Cersei's medical supervision, and still convinced but **unable to prove who sent Mandon**. He wishes he might dream again of Tysha. His physical injuries and political vulnerability are real, but neither intentional medical neglect nor Cersei's role in Moore's attempt is proven.

## Competing interpretations and downstream tests

- **Moore conspiracy:** Direct attempt and death are established, **Cersei as paymaster is not**. Compare assignments, Moore's previous service to **Jon Arryn**, Cersei's interests, and possible rival schemes; do not fill sponsor slot from Tyrion's suspicion alone.
- **Tysha:** The repeated tenderness is emotionally concrete. Tyrion's assertion that it was all paid performance depends on what he was told during adolescent family coercion. Later firsthand testimony may radically alter that assessment.
- **Postwar reward:** Tywin gets the public glory; Tyrion inherits scar, dream wounds and no Handship. It would be too simple to say no one saved him—**Pod demonstrably did**, while court power consolidated elsewhere.
