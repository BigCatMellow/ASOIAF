## Sequence, agency and decisive revelations

1. **THEON'S LAST GARRISON:** **Maester Luwin** warns Theon that **Ser Rodrik Cassell's northerners are about to surround Winterfell**, and that even abundant provisions cannot save a castle held by too few men against simultaneous escalade. Theon orders more ravens to **Balon** and **Victarion** despite having had no reply. Luwin explicitly says his oath to **the realm and Winterfell** obliges him to counsel Theon while Theon holds the castle, even though he neither loves nor trusts him. This is genuine duty to an occupier, distinct from moral endorsement of his conduct.
2. **A VOLUNTARY FIGHTING LINE:** Theon draws a line in the yard and invites the men who wish to **fight and die with him** to cross. **Wex**, his mute boy squire, goes first; **Black Lorren** next, followed by others. **Seventeen ironborn remain**, while **Urzen, Stygg, and all ten men Asha lent** leave. This is exact on-page garrison strength at the time, not a guessed siege strength of fifty.
3. **EARLY STRATEGIC REALISM:** Theon knows the outer and inner walls and sufficient food cannot compensate for **17 defenders** against approximately **2,000 besiegers** with engines. **Black Lorren** wants to fight to the death on the drawbridge and kill or expel castle people; Theon rejects the proposal to massacre residents but authorizes another coercive hostage tactic. His decision to resist remains his own despite viable alternatives.
4. **RODRIK'S RELIEF COALITION:** Through Luwin's **Myrish lens tube**, Theon identifies banners of **Cerwyn, Tallhart, Manderly, Flint, Karstark, and Hornwood**. He sees **no Glovers, Boltons or Umbers**. **Cley Cerwyn** approaches with a peace banner to request a parley. These are **actual observed contingent symbols**, not proof of each absent house's permanent allegiance.
5. **PARLEY TERMS AND MORAL DISAGREEMENT:** **Rodrik Cassel** accuses Theon of betraying the Starks who raised him and of murdering Ned's boys, whose displayed heads remain **the miller's children** per `ACOK 56`. Rodrik proposes that Theon **surrender Winterfell**, with soldiers uninvolved in child murder permitted to leave, while Theon would be held for **Robb's justice**. Theon invokes his decade as a hostage after Balon's rebellion and demands northmen swear to **Balon and Prince Theon** instead. The terms make their political claims clear; **Theon does not confess his substitution**.
6. **BETH AS LEVERAGE:** Theon raises a fist, triggering **Black Lorren to display Rodrik's daughter Beth Cassel bound with a noose**, between the two false Stark heads above the castle. Theon threatens **to hang her at sunset** unless the relief force leaves and to kill **another hostage every dawn and dusk** afterward. Rodrik asks Theon take him instead; Theon refuses because **Rodrik's own surrender would not disarm allied bannermen**. The threat is **real coercion against a child**, while **Beth has not yet been hanged or injured in this scene**.
7. **THEON'S HOSTAGE COMPARISON:** Theon says the 'noose' of his own childhood captivity was psychological rather than hemp, and discovers the depth of his resentment while speaking. This provides a causal insight into his identity conflict, but **his trauma cannot establish moral equivalence between an exchanged noble hostage and a threatened execution of Beth**. Rodrik offers mercy conditioned on justice, not automatic pardon.
8. **THE DEAD-END CALCULATION:** Once inside, Theon privately recognizes that if he **hangs Beth, the northmen will storm Winterfell at once**, while failure to hang her exposes his threat as empty. Even Black Lorren understands doom. Theon repeatedly thinks of friendship with Robb and past shooting of the wildling that threatened Bran, showing the clearest contrast yet between his former home and his current atrocities.
9. **LUWIN'S WATCH SOLUTION:** Luwin suggests **yielding and taking the black** with Rodrik's support, since **House Stark has long protected the Watch**. Theon allows himself to imagine **Eastwatch, ships, rangership, perhaps Lord Commander**, and freedom from ironborn inheritance struggles. These are personal imagined possibilities, not a **formal promise from Rodrik or a completed pardon**. It is a concrete **untried survival alternative** before the interruption.
10. **DREADFORT RELIEF IS AN AMBUSH:** **Kromm** reports hundreds of **flayed-man-banner riders** arrived as if to aid Rodrik's siege, then suddenly **attacked Rodrik's assembled host**. Theon watches from the battlements as a smaller but coordinated, well-led Dreadfort force **destroys the surprised northern coalition** in streets and market square. This is a real **Bolton force attacking Stark loyalists**, not merely a perceived alliance conflict. Theon initially interprets it as rescue because **Reek** has gone to recruit men.
11. **THREE NORTHERN LEADERS' BODIES:** A commander in a **blood-red flayed-face helm** presents **the corpses of Ser Rodrik Cassel, Leobald Tallhart and Cley Cerwyn** at Winterfell's gate. The deaths are **physically exhibited and recognized**, unlike the fake boys' heads. The newcomer states he **shook Rodrik's hand, cut off half his arm, and then showed his face** as part of the treachery. The **same banner as Bolton's Northern army** should not be treated as proof Roose ordered the ambush here: no communication from Roose is seen in this chapter.
12. **REEK'S TRUE IDENTITY REVEALED DIRECTLY:** The red-helmed leader removes his helmet. Theon recognizes the 'Reek' he freed and employed, but the man explicitly explains that **the actual servant Reek was killed in the earlier pursuit** while the fugitive **Ramsay Snow** wore his clothes, covered himself in grime and filth, and took his identity. He says he switched **his own cloak, swordbelt, boots, ring and fast horse** with Reek so his pursuers would kill the wrong man. `ACOK 46/50/56`'s suspicious servant knowledge, writing, flaying and armed recruitment are decisively accounted for. **This is a direct confession plus identifying face and operational capacity**, much stronger than a rumor, though minute details of the initial swap are still his narrative.
13. **RAMSAY CLAIMS BOLTON, RECOUNTS HORnWOOD ABUSE:** The newcomer gives his name as **Ramsay**, recalling his wife **Lady Hornwood's finger-eating fate**, and identifies himself as **Snow by law but Bolton by choice**. His version of the earlier sexual assault and body swap is self-incriminating; don't adopt his language as an exculpation. The 'dead Ramsay' reports Roose himself repeated (`ACOK 45`) were wrong concerning the man now speaking.
14. **SIX HUNDRED ARMED RIDERS:** Ramsay says he brought **three times the 200 men** promised to Theon, recruited from his **father's Dreadfort garrison**. This claim is compatible with the devastating cavalry attack Theon watched, though **Theon does not count all six hundred individually**. Ramsay demands the **woman** promised as a reward for recruitment (formerly **Palla**) but changes his demand to **Kyra**, Theon's companion, revealing he does not intend an obedient servant's bargain.
15. **DOUBLE BETRAYAL / THEON INJURED:** When Theon protests, Ramsay **backhands him with a steel gauntlet**, shattering his cheekbone and briefly blacking him out. He awakens in the yard as **Ramsay's men kill Theon's remaining ironborn**, including visibly **Red Rolfe and Kenned**, and attack **Black Lorren and Ulf**. Theon **cannot successfully command the gates closed**. This confirms Ramsay's relief was **never safe allegiance to Theon**.
16. **LUWIN SPEARED AND WINTERFELL FIRED:** Theon watches **Luwin** attempt to help, but a **Dreadfort rider stabs him from behind with a spear and rides over him**, leaving severe injuries; **his ultimate death is not directly witnessed in this scene**. Ramsay orders **the Frey boys saved** while everything else is **burned**, and raiders torch the **stables** and begin destruction of Winterfell. The specific order to spare the Freys signals **selective political protection**, but the strategic motive and their later custody remain unspecified.
17. **THEON'S LAST SIGHT / SURVIVAL OPEN:** His last viewpoint image is his horse **Smiler** charging out of the burning stables with a mane ablaze. **Theon's later survival, captivity, torture, and eventual fate are not established by this chapter's ending.** The visual destruction of Winterfell, murder of Rodrik's relief army, and Ramsay's identity are established.
18. **MACRO CAUSALITY:** Theon successfully seized a capital with tiny forces, failed to secure either northern loyalty or ironborn reinforcement, and **let the worst possible rescuer into his gates**. The attack on Winterfell is not simply an Ironborn sack: **Ramsay's Dreadfort contingent actively kills Stark loyalists and then destroys the castle while deliberately protecting Frey hostages**.

## Contested theory tests and follow-up

- **Reek/Ramsay:** This is a resolved identity puzzle: Ramsay confesses the swap, reveals the face, commands Dreadfort riders and speaks as Ramsay. Earlier `ACOK 45` claims Ramsay was dead and `ACOK 46`'s servant 'Heke' story must now be marked **mistaken or false**, not left as equal alternatives.
- **Who ordered the betrayal?** Ramsay clearly kills Stark-loyalist leaders and sacks Winterfell, but Roose's **direct authorizing letter or private instruction is not shown in this source**. Compare his earlier disavowal of Ramsay, postwar dispatches and later testimony before attributing command.
- **The Frey rescue:** Ramsay **selectively protects the Frey youths**, suggesting tactical value and potential collusion, but **their ultimate fate is not witnessed**.
- **Theon's possible Watch escape:** Luwin offers a recognized oath-based solution; the speed of Ramsay's arrival prevents testing Rodrik's approval. It is plausible an alternative existed, but not proved feasible in every detail.
