## Causal narrative and decisions

1. **NEW OCCUPIERS, CONTINUED ATROCITIES:** Harrenhal's new Bolton garrison has **tarred severed heads** above its gates. Under Roose, **Maester Tothmure** is executed for sending ravens to the Lannisters on the night Harrenhal fell; **Lucan the armorer**, **Goodwife Harra** and Tywin's steward are similarly killed for serving the old occupier. **Pia and other women** suspected of intimacy with Lannister soldiers are shorn, exposed in stocks and sexually abused by men including **three Frey soldiers**. This is not a Stark 'liberation' in any morally comprehensive sense: those serving the defeated regime become targets, irrespective of coercion under earlier occupation.
2. **ARYA'S AMBIVALENCE AND GENDRY'S CHALLENGE:** **Amabel** threatens that Arya will suffer the same punishment after the Lannisters regain the castle; Arya threatens her back but deliberately **does not report Amabel to Bolton** although that could mean death. **Gendry** reminds Arya she helped engineer the 'weasel soup' liberation which put Roose and **Vargo Hoat's Brave Companions** in charge. His complaint is grounded in their observed brutality and **Lucan's execution**, not blanket sympathy for Amory Lorch.
3. **VARGO'S SELF-PERPETUATING PURGES:** Bolton has authorized Brave Companions to hunt 'Lannister traitors' in surrounding villages. Hoat's bands revisit communities that **previously gave aid or supplies to Tywin under coercion** and rob or kill them for the same acts. Rorge, Biter, Shagwell and the rumored child-abusing **Septon Utt** remain part of a dangerous new security apparatus. The chapter demonstrates **opportunistic turncoat violence**, rather than dependable Northern moral reform.
4. **Jaqen's TOKEN RETAINED:** Arya still carries the **small, rusted iron coin** Jaqen gave her in `ACOK 47`, with worn face and writing she cannot read. She throws it away in anger, then **returns to find and keep it**. The object is physically available later during her escape; its value is **the Braavosi access pledge**, not an ordinary precious-metal price.
5. **ELMAR FREY AND UNRECOGNIZED BETROTHAL:** Roose's squire **Elmar Frey**, a trueborn son of **Lord Walder**, boasts he will **marry a princess**. Arya mocks him, not recognizing the intended spouse; the earlier Frey–Stark marriage agreement (`AGOT` and Robb's march terms) makes **Arya the strong intended referent**, but the children are **not told the full contract in this scene**. Later a raven from the Twins leaves Elmar sobbing that **his father has broken the promised match**. This is direct evidence that **a Frey political marriage has become void**, but the message's detailed cause is not quoted in this POV. Do not make Elmar's canceled engagement personal rejection by Arya.
6. **BOLTON'S BODY AND OFFICE:** Roose receives **large pale leeches** on his bare torso and limbs, ministered by **Qyburn**, an unchained ex-maester associated by rumor with necromancy. Bolton's low, soft voice forces generals to strain to hear him. His quietness and delicate medical routine exist beside decisive willingness to kill; **leeches are direct physiological practice**, not proof of supernatural immortality.
7. **FREY DEFEATISM POST-BLACKWATER:** **Aenys, Hosteen and Jared Frey**, accompanied by others, urge leaving Harrenhal: it is vast, impossible to hold against Tywin without a major garrison, and **nearby crops and stores have been ruined**. They say **Tywin and Highgarden have defeated Stannis**, Robb has lost Winterfell and (as they believe) his brothers, and Robb should **give up his crown and make peace with the Lannisters**. Their arguments are explicit military/political positions, not decisions by Robb or automatic proof Bolton privately supports surrender.
8. **ARYA LEARNS THE FALSE STARK DEATH NEWS:** In the room she hears that **Bran and Rickon were killed at Winterfell** and struggles to hide her grief. `ACOK 56 Theon` establishes the heads shown publicly are **the miller's boys**, not the true Stark princes. Her grief is fully real, but the Freys' conclusion is **false as an identification** and cannot be elevated to POV-confirmed death.
9. **A DISQUIETING BOLTON ORDER:** After dismissing the Freys, Roose instructs Qyburn to write **Ser Helman Tallhart**: execute the **Darry garrison captives**, burn the captured Darry castle, then join **Robett Glover** and **attack the rich Duskendale region**. Tallhart's recent conquest of Darry is reported to Roose. The offensive orders appear to expose two northern contingents to danger far from Robb's main field force; **intentional betrayal or a secret arrangement is a testable theory, not an admitted motive in this scene**. Roose invokes **the king's authority** in directing the atrocities; the text does not show Robb specifically authorized the execution and burning.
10. **ARYA AND HER UNDISCLOSED NAME:** Roose never learns **Nan is Arya Stark**. Arya wants to reveal herself to her fellow northerners but has missed the opportunity to approach the Tallharts or Glovers before they marched. Bolton intends to leave Harrenhal for the north and tells Arya he will **give the castle to Vargo Hoat**, while **she is to stay with Hoat**; he threatens to cut her tongue out for asking to accompany him. The imminent return to Brave Companion control drives her decision to leave.
11. **DESTROYED INFORMATION OBJECT:** Roose reads **a thick leather-bound book** beside the fire and **deliberately burns it**, pages and cover together, while Arya brings supper. The act is personally witnessed; **contents, title, reason and significance remain completely unknown**. It should enter a low-confidence object ledger rather than a fabricated theory of hidden histories.
12. **MAP AND FREEDOM ROUTE:** Arya finds in Bolton's solar a **sheepskin map labeled the lands of the Trident**, showing **Riverrun**, Harrenhal and the rivers and crossings. She notes Riverrun seems attainable. That evening she **steals the map and Bolton's dagger**. The map is a concrete navigational tool; her ability to use it perfectly is not tested.
13. **WOLVES, PRAYER AND IDENTITY:** Roose returns from hunting **nine wolves (seven adult, two pups)** and orders their skins made into a coverlet and gloves, recalling 'winter is coming' without concern that his banner flies beside Stark's. Arya trains with a **broken broomstick**, rehearses her revenge names and says **valar morghulis** in the godswood. Hearing an outside **wolf howl**, she experiences **Ned's voice or memory** urging that the pack survive and calling her Arya of Winterfell. The chapter narrates **subjective voice and animal sound**, but does not prove a physically present ghost or identify a magical sender.
14. **CHOOSING REAL STEEL:** Arya breaks the broomstick and decides wooden practice will no longer suffice. She tells **Gendry** Roose is leaving them to Hoat, falsely asserting that **Hoat personally threatened to amputate every servant's left foot** to force Gendry to act. This is **Arya's conscious lie**, distinct from Roose's confirmed plan to leave her with the Mummers.
15. **THREE-PERSON ESCAPE PLAN:** She assigns Gendry to obtain **swords** and wake **Hot Pie for bread**, while she acquires **three fast horses**. Using Bolton's **flayed-man livery and a commanding voice**, she convinces a stableboy the lord wants horses without requiring verification. She expects the stableboy could be punished after her departure; this is one more cost of escape not under her control.
16. **THE COIN BECOMES AN INSTRUMENT:** By the lightly used **eastern postern near the ruined Tower of Ghosts**, Arya approaches a **Dreadfort guard** whom she cannot easily bluff because he knows his lord. She claims to bear a reward of **silver for watchmen**, displaying **Jaqen's iron coin** in darkness, and deliberately lets it fall. The guard kneels to retrieve it; Arya uses the opening to **cut his exposed throat with Bolton's stolen dagger**, preventing an alarm. She then **retrieves the iron coin**. This is a direct intentional killing of an armed man who is not attacking her at that moment, undertaken to free herself and the boys; it is neither a Jaqen killing nor a deathless trick.
17. **EXIT ACHIEVED:** Arya lifts the postern bar, **howls** to summon her companions (rejecting Hot Pie's owl call) and **rides out with Gendry and Hot Pie** as heavy rain falls. Gendry has swords, mail and his hammer; Hot Pie has bread and cheese. They have left the walls of Harrenhal, **not yet reached Riverrun or any protected Northern position**.

## Rival explanations and future tests

- **Roose's Duskendale strategy:** A seemingly needless march toward powerful Lannister territory with a depleted Northern host could deliberately diminish Robb's available forces. It may instead be ordinary foraging and retaliation; later intelligence about instructions, timing, troops and Tywin's movements should resolve it.
- **Frey marriage:** The broken contract's diplomatic consequences are larger than Elmar's tears; compare earlier Catelyn-Robb-Frey terms and later marriages to see whether *this precise lost match* explains emerging Frey bitterness.
- **Arya and the pack:** The auditory wolf pack and imagined Ned voice strengthen her Stark self-recognition, but no exact wolf identity is established. Track Nymeria's later POV evidence before treating this as direct psychic contact.
- **Braavosi coin:** It remains available following the guard's death; Arya does **not** spend it as ordinary currency or lose access to the phrase.
