## Plot and consequential decisions

1. **VERIFIED:** Theon inspects the new **longship** built for him by shipwright **Sigrin** at **Lordsport** before Aeron's planned blessing. The vessel, which Theon names **Sea Bitch**, has a black hull roughly **100 feet** long, a single mast, **50 oars**, room for some **100 men**, and a pronounced iron ram. It is smaller than Balon's **Great Kraken** or Victarion's **Iron Victory**. Theon admits privately that despite years around ships he has **never captained one** and has to learn the men available to crew her.
2. **VERIFIED / IDENTITY DECEPTION:** A self-assured sailorwoman calling herself **Esgred**, supposedly **Ambrode's daughter, Sigrin's wife and pregnant**, meets Theon at the shipyard. She flirts, challenges his confidence and counsels him that experienced **crews must row together**, rather than simply choosing intimidating individuals. She knows many named ironborn and shipwright details, but Theon accepts her asserted identity without testing it, partly because he values her attention.
3. **VERIFIED:** Lordsport is packed with captains; **Lord Goodbrother's near forty longships** recently arrived from Great Wyk. **Balon has barred visiting merchantmen from departing**, including the **Myraham**, so they cannot warn the mainland before his attack. The ship captain's daughter Theon previously pursued now seeks recognition as a **salt wife**, according to Theon, but the girl's wishes are not independently heard here. Theon seeks a **loyal war crew** distinct from his father's and uncles' allegiances.
4. **VERIFIED:** Theon's boy squire **Wex**, a clever **mute** bastard tied to the Botleys, is found winning at dice. Theon obtained his dark and temperamental riding stallion **Smiler** from Botley, and taking Wex as squire was part of the price; Theon values the horse because his decade at Winterfell gave him skills most ironborn lack. He puts Esgred before him on the saddle for the ride to Pyke.
5. **VERIFIED:** Esgred questions Theon about **Asha**, **Aeron**, **Victarion** and absent **Euron**. He dismisses his sister as physically unappealing and unmarriageable without his intervention; he regards his uncles as too fanatical, loyal or absent to challenge him. He admits internally that uncles can dispossess nephews among the ironborn, but asserts his own superiority. Esgred raises the danger that **a stranger returning after years** might struggle for the **Seastone Chair**—a warning Theon only belatedly realizes applies to him.
6. **CHARACTER CLAIM / FORESHADOWING:** Theon recalls **Euron** has been absent nearly two years and that his ship **Silence**, black-sailed with dark red hull, is notoriously associated with distant seaports. His presence, life and future designs are **not verified** by these tales. Theon also admits he has not visited his mother **Alannys** on Harlaw and justifies this by his father's needs; Esgred reasonably questions whether his visit could bring her peace. Theon tells Esgred unusually much about **Winterfell, Robb and Lannister warfare**, believing he is impressing a prospective lover.
7. **VERIFIED / REVEAL:** At Pyke, dogs, a stableman and other household members instantly recognize **Esgred as Lady Asha Greyjoy**, Theon's elder sister, newly returned from Great Wyk. Asha tells him she wanted to see what sort of man he had become; he had judged her based on a distorted childhood picture and did not recognize her. He strikes **Wex** for finding the humiliation amusing and not warning him. Asha is **not actually Sigrin's wife or pregnant**; she later explains that the purported husband was the axe she handles and the baby a concealed dagger. The false persona is a deliberate **test/manipulation**, not a verified sexual relationship.
8. **VERIFIED:** At Balon's feast for approximately **400 lords and captains**, Asha sits in the place of honor immediately to her father's **right**, while Theon is seated beside her. Her ship **Black Wind** is already established under her command. Theon is mocked for relying on greenland notions of hereditary seniority, ornate clothing, and ignorance of local loyalties. Asha catches a hurled axe during the **finger dance**, showing practiced skill, then uses the axe and dagger to expose her deception publicly. **Balon and Victarion laugh**: Theon's formal status is not his family's effective recognition.
9. **VERIFIED / STRATEGIC ORDERS, NOT ACCOMPLISHED CONQUEST:** In his solar **Balon finally reveals his planned northern invasion**:
   - **Theon** is assigned **eight longships** to **raid the Stony Shore**, burn fishing settlements and sink local ships, hopefully drawing northern defenders out. **Aeron Damphair and Dagmer Cleftjaw** will accompany him. Theon judges his independent command nominal.
   - **Asha** gets **thirty carefully chosen longships** to sail around **Sea Dragon Point**, land in tidal flats north of **Deepwood Motte** and quickly seize the Glover stronghold. Glover brothers are away in Robb's war, leaving the site vulnerable. This is the more prestigious fortress objective Theon wanted.
   - **Victarion** commands the **main thrust** through **Saltspear and Fever River**, moving inland toward **Moat Cailin** (less than **twenty miles from the river headwaters** by Theon's account in the scene) to seal the **Neck** and prevent Robb's forces returning from the south. Ironborn control of the western seas enables this coastal approach; actual success remains **UNKNOWN** at the chapter end.
10. **BALON'S REASONING / POSSIBLE FLAW:** He expects northern castles weakly held by old men, boys and those left behind after Robb marched south, and proposes to reduce these positions while making inhabitants **thralls and salt wives**. He explicitly thinks **Winterfell could resist for a year** while other sites fell. The operational strategy is concrete, not spontaneous raiding, but **depends on disorganizing northern response**, the Neck being held and local strongholds actually surrendering; it underestimates possible adaptation by isolated defenders.
11. **VERIFIED:** Theon is angered by subordinate raiding work, by the placement of Asha above him, and by fear he is no longer at home in the Iron Islands. He follows the Drowned God ceremony words under social pressure. After the war council he slips on the storm-tossed bridge, and **Asha helps him back across**. Their closing exchange shows both hostility and an existing familial bond; it is not a reconciliation of inheritance.

## THINK-style alternative explanations and next steps

- **Theon:** His exclusion and Asha's embarrassing deception are real grievances, but not proof he deserves command. He admits a lack of seamanship and local networks while assuming birthright controls succession. Alternative: his father's distribution recognizes experience, not merely punishment for being a former hostage.
- **Asha:** Her fabricated persona is invasive and at moments sexually manipulative, but also exposes Theon's presumptions about women and local competence. Her retrieval of him from the bridge complicates a purely hostile reading.
- **Balon:** A coherent and dangerous invasion plan, not the caricature of uncontrolled reaving. It assumes the northerners cannot quickly coordinate, Robb's army stays south, and Moat Cailin can be captured before reinforcements. Test against available northern garrisons from `ACOK 16 (Bran)`.
- **Succession:** Theon claims a right under greenland custom; the actual ironborn succession process, potential **Asha/Victarion/Euron** support and Balon's personal preference are **not settled**.
