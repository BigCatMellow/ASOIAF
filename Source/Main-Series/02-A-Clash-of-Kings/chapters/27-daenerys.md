## Main narrative and causal consequences

1. **VERIFIED:** Qarth ceremonially welcomes Dany and her small khalasar with camel riders, gongs, banners, child flower-scatterers and vast architecture. **Pyat Pree** asserts his city is the greatest in the world; his extravagant history of Saathos blinding himself is **interested local lore**, not a verified event. Dany nevertheless confirms tangible splendor. The city's **three great walls** are increasingly tall—red sandstone **30 feet** with animals, grey granite **40** with images of warfare and killing, black marble **50** with erotic reliefs; copper, iron and gold-garnished gates open for her. This progression contrasts public wealth, violent origins and display, not itself an established prophecy.
2. **VERIFIED:** Tall, elaborately clothed Qartheen watch Dany pass. She is self-conscious in **lionskin** with baby **Drogon** on a shoulder, and questions whether her Dothraki see the city merely as future plunder. She is aware that Qartheen might view her entourage as savages. The jeweled **serpent arch**, dragon/griffin/manticore fountains and crowded bazaar with exotic goods establish an extremely wealthy trading metropolis but no assurance of an available invasion force.
3. **VERIFIED / COMPETING OFFERS:** Three visitors from `ACOK 12` each frame access to Daenerys differently. **Pyat Pree**, the blue-lipped warlock, directs her toward the **House of the Undying**, promising truth, wisdom and obscure power. Merchant prince **Xaro Xhoan Daxos** invokes the **Thirteen** and offers wealth, home comforts and a black-jade/fire-opal crown. **Quaithe**, masked shadowbinder, offers warnings rather than gifts. Dany explicitly asks for **ships and swords**, not a ceremonial crown. No one in this chapter gives her such forces.
4. **XARO'S INTERESTED CLAIM:** Xaro disparages warlocks as former masters whose power has dwindled, says their blue lips come from **shade-of-the-evening**, and alleges their promises turn to dust. Jorah warns Dany to distrust **both Xaro and Pyat**. They may know real things or be competing manipulators; neither man's assessment is neutral proof about the others' magic.
5. **VERIFIED:** Xaro lodges **all of Dany's people and horses** within a huge lavish palace and gives her an entire wing, pool, gardens and luxuries. Dany notes all the leading Qartheen actually desire to **see her dragons**. Pyat offers an ointment he claims can reveal spirits; efficacy is **unverified**. Quaithe privately warns that everyone will covet dragons as **embodied power**, without identifying a single conspirator. Dany orders **her own bloodriders to guard the dragons and entrances**, rather than cede security to her hosts.
6. **VERIFIED / ACTIVE RECONNAISSANCE:** Dany sends **Rakharo** to survey Qarth outside the ceremonial route, including sending women to places men cannot enter. She sends **Jorah** to the harbor to examine ships, origins and crews, over his protest that she needs his direct protection. His languages and Westerosi knowledge make him useful. She trusts the hosts' generosity **conditionally** while looking for independent confirmation of what help exists.
7. **DANY'S SELF-REFLECTION:** Bathing in Xaro's pool, Dany wants to **make a prosperous beautiful kingdom** rather than merely plunder cities as Drogo's horsemen did. Her conquest still requires force; she explicitly knows Dothraki excel at sacking but not ruling, and the small khalasar and hatchling dragons do not equal an army. She doubts Viserys's belief that Westeros will spontaneously welcome a Targaryen restoration. These doubts are strategic growth, but her image of the smallfolk happily greeting Aerys is **inherited from Viserys**, not independently checked.
8. **VERIFIED / DISTANT TRADER'S TESTIMONY:** Jorah returns with **Quhuru Mo**, captain of the **Cinnamon Wind** from **Tall Trees Town** in the Summer Isles. He uses **Valyrian** to speak with Dany and says his ship called at **Oldtown less than six months earlier** and other ports in Dorne and Lys. The widely repeated news is **Robert Baratheon is dead**, reportedly killed in a boar hunt; rival rumors blame the queen, brother or Ned Stark. Because multiple ports agree on his death, Dany embraces that part; exact circumstances from these reports are **not conclusively verified by the captain**. To the reader AGOT corroborates the death.
9. **IMPORTANT INFORMATION LAG:** Quhuru says **Joffrey now sits the throne, with Lannisters ruling**, and reports **Ned Stark has been seized for treason** and Robert's brothers will claim the crown. Ned's **execution has already occurred in earlier AGOT/ACOK POVs** but has **not reached this captain's narrative of events**. Dany mistakes Ned's motive as treason toward her father; Jorah challenges the allegation against Ned's honor. Neither has the full truth of the post-Robert succession crisis.
10. **VERIFIED:** The **Cinnamon Wind will sail east around the Jade Sea** and cannot transport Dany west for **a year or more**. Quhuru values seeing living dragons as sufficient reward and is poised to spread word of them. Dany invites him to King’s Landing when she rules, while Jorah cautions that advertising her return plans creates risk. His warning is prudent even after Robert's death.
11. **DANY'S INFERENCE / OPEN QUESTION:** Dany compares Westeros after Robert to Drogo's khalasar after his death: a formerly united host now splitting among would-be successors. She treats the chaos as her opening; Jorah says **armies, gold, allies and ships** remain necessary and doubts rapid conquest. Dany insists she has survived much at **fifteen name days**, is unlike Viserys, and invokes her dragon blood. Jorah reminds her that **Rhaegar was killed by Robert** and dragons are not invulnerable. No return voyage or strategic alliance is yet concluded.

## Rival readings and tests

- **The three Qartheen agents** may each hope to exploit rare living dragons. Treat their different promises and warnings as interested acts; no evidence yet identifies Quaithe as a true ally or identifies Xaro as an enemy.
- **Conquest vs rule** may be Dany's central tension: noble stated ends and a restoration claim confront coercion, wartime harm and an unreliable image of her father's reign. The captain's news gives her an opportunity but not a coalition.
- **Information lag** should be indexed carefully: Dany knows Robert dead, not the full Ned execution or subsequent military developments.
