## Full narrative and consequential choices

1. **VERIFIED / LANCEL INFORMANT PAYOFF:** **Lancel** secretly meets Tyrion at a sept and reports **Cersei means to send Tommen to Rosby**, in **Gyles Rosby's** custody, disguised as a dark-haired **hedge knight's page**. Cersei fears both the **mob** and **Tyrion**, by Lancel's account. This extends his success as a coerced mole from `ACOK 29/41`. **Lancel** asks Tyrion for his own command in the coming battle as reward, but Tyrion makes no firm promise.
2. **VERIFIED / HIDDEN COUNTERORDER:** Tyrion writes **a deliberately misleading sealed note** ostensibly to send **Jacelyn Bywater with fifty men to scout the roseroad**, then orally instructs **Bronn** to tell Bywater to **ignore its words and set an ambush on the road to Rosby**, intercepting Gyles and Tommen's party. This is a concrete **layered secrecy tactic**: written document for an interceptor, real instructions relayed through an intermediary. The ambush is **ordered**, not yet performed or independently witnessed.
3. **VERIFIED / TOMMEN TO REMAIN AT ROSBY:** Tyrion expressly **does not intend to return Tommen to King's Landing**. He wants Bywater to take the prince into **Rosby Castle**, expel the garrison and retain him under loyal City Watch custody. He fears sickly Gyles and ordinary escorts could quickly yield the boy to **Stannis**; a living Tommen offers the Lannisters a further claimant **if Joffrey is captured or killed**. This is a preemptive custody seizure against Cersei, not a child-abduction plot proven successful.
4. **VERIFIED:** Tyrion considers granting Bywater a lordship for service; **Bronn jokes that he would take the prize for a title**, but Tyrion distrusts sellsword loyalty with a boy who could be sold to the enemy. Tyrion orders that no violence **occur in Tommen's sight**, reflecting a protective distinction between the brothers. He leaves the **post-riot curfew** in force; reports say it has reduced bodies found in streets, but people blame Tyrion for harsh enforcement.
5. **VERIFIED / SHAE LOCATION EXPOSED:** Against habitual secrecy Tyrion rides alone to the guarded **manse near the Iron Gate** and discovers the singer **Symon Silver Tongue** visiting **Shae**. Symon recognizes Tyrion as Hand and now knows both her address and relationship. Tyrion threatens him into silence, and Shae argues the singer merely entertains her while Tyrion is absent. **Symon is left alive and physically present in the city here**; a threat does not equal execution.
6. **VERIFIED / VARYS'S DECEPTION FAILS WITH SHAE:** **Varys arrives in an elaborate disguise as a foul-smelling begging brother**, complete with altered movement and fake lesions. Shae **recognizes him immediately**, claiming that experience in sexual commerce taught her to distinguish character from clothing; Tyrion does not recognize him at first. This is **demonstrated ability to penetrate Varys's disguise**, but not proof of Shae's sincerity or foreknowledge of every disguise.
7. **VARYS'S REPORT / ACOK 42 CONSEQUENCE:** Varys reports **Cortnay Penrose is dead and Storm's End has yielded to Stannis**. He later says Penrose **apparently fell or threw himself from a tower**, although chamber guards **saw no intruder enter or leave**. This confirms a **completed death and fortress transfer through an intelligence source**, with exact precipitating action and witness chain unshown. `ACOK 42 Davos` supplies extraordinary eyewitness evidence of Melisandre's **Stannis-shaped shadow traversing the portcullis** on an operation intended to remove Penrose. Reading both chapters supports a magical assassination explanation strongly, while not showing an eyewitness of the final fatal push.
8. **VERIFIED / TIMETABLE COLLAPSES:** Tyrion had counted on **Penrose holding Storm's End for months** while his chain defenses developed and Tywin pursued Robb. The report ruins that time buffer. He smashes a wine cup in distress, and warns Shae that **Stannis can now turn toward King's Landing**. This is a credible imminent strategic threat rather than confirmation of a completed invasion.
9. **VERIFIED / SHAE AND TYRION POWER STRUGGLE:** Tyrion proposes taking Shae into the **Red Keep as a kitchen scullion**, to keep her out of danger, but she objects to degrading labor and asks instead to be acknowledged publicly as **his lady** or placed in his tower. She recalls coercive exploitation by her father when made both servant and sexual object. Tyrion fears **Tywin's explicit ban on bringing her to court** and Cersei's retaliation.
10. **VERIFIED / PHYSICAL VIOLENCE:** After Shae mocks Tyrion's fear of Tywin's authority, Tyrion **slaps her**. His subsequent remorse does not erase the act. He recounts **his marriage at thirteen to Tysha**, the claim from Jaime/Tywin that she was a prostitute, and **Tywin's ordered assault by guards**. Tyrion describes the marriage as fraudulent because that is **what he was told and presently believes**, not an independent verification of Tysha's underlying consent or identity. This moment is a vulnerability disclosure after violence, **not proof of Shae's free agreement or devotion**.
11. **VERIFIED / MORE SECURE COVER ARRANGEMENT:** Back on horseback Varys warns that a kitchen worker will face intrusive questions, harassment and surveillance. He proposes instead that Shae replace a thieving maid as **bedmaid to Lollys Stokeworth**, whose trauma following the riot has left her confined to her rooms; **Lady Tanda** would dismiss the current maid after theft is revealed. Tyrion recognizes this is a better hidden location with nicer clothes. It is a **plan**, not depicted as a completed employment change.
12. **VERIFIED / SECRET PASSAGE:** Varys tells Tyrion there is a **concealed route into his chambers in the Tower of the Hand**, potentially allowing Shae to visit without being seen entering through normal corridors. Varys refuses to disclose exactly where the door is. This is a **crucial palace information asymmetry**: Varys controls a covert approach to the seat of government that even the acting Hand cannot locate.
13. **VARYS'S ORIGIN TESTIMONY:** After Tyrion asks how Penrose died, Varys reveals an account of being **sold as a young mummer boy in Myr** to a sorcerer who **drugged, immobilized and mutilated him**. The sorcerer burned his severed parts in a brazier, and Varys heard a **voice responding from the blue flames** to the ritual chanting. The boy was abandoned; Varys survived through begging, theft and eventually exploiting private letters. He says the remembered **voice**, not the injury itself, haunts him and motivates hatred of magic and its practitioners. This is intensely specific **first-person testimony**, not an event externally observed in Tyrion's POV. Varys himself allows that drugging, pain and memory could affect its reliability.
14. **VERIFIED / DIFFERENT STANDARDS OF EVIDENCE:** Tyrion prefers belief in **steel, coins and political cunning**, suggesting a hired assassin might explain Penrose. Varys asks whether old powers exist and notes that a man openly challenging Stannis to a duel was not obviously contemplating suicide. The reader's direct `ACOK 42` shadow witness makes Varys's magical suspicion better grounded than Tyrion recognizes, **without proving Varys's remembered flame voice is the same phenomenon**.
15. **VARYS'S LATEST POLITICAL REPORT:** **Dorne will not descend to wage war** simply because Stannis gained Storm's End (consistent with `ACOK 41` limited feint). **Tywin's crossing of the Red Fork** is not yet known to Varys. At Bitterbridge, **Randyll Tarly** reportedly seized Renly's stores, killing **Florent men** in particular; **Lord Caswell** withdrew into his castle. **Littlefinger's mission** has sent no word, so the projected Tyrell alliance is still **unconfirmed**. The city and court remain exposed.
16. **VERIFIED / CONCLUDING IRONY:** Tyrion recognizes that, despite the populace's hatred and his own physical vulnerability, he may be the one person holding together King's Landing's defenses against Stannis. This is **his private assessment of importance**, not an objective ranking of every participant's efforts.

## Alternative hypotheses and follow-up tests

- **Penrose's death:** The multiple POV chain is unusually strong: **Stannis plans clandestine removal**, Davos witnesses a **Stannis-shaped shadow slip into Storm's End**, Varys shortly reports **Penrose dead by tower fall** and the castle surrendered. However, the **actual final encounter with Penrose is not depicted**; exact mechanics and intent should stay distinct from the strong causal inference.
- **Tommen custody:** Tyrion is secretly manipulating the plan Cersei designed to protect her son. The outcome, Tommen's whereabouts and effect on family unity must be checked later; do not report Rosby as under Bywater yet.
- **Varys and magic:** His personal history gives a real motive for hostility to Stannis if magic is used, but neither the identified voice nor the faith it belonged to has been corroborated. Keep 'heard answering voice' as **Varys's testimony**.
- **Shae:** Tyrion's love narrative coexists with **physical force**, employment dependence, control of her safety and fear of his own family. The scene cannot be used as proof she has fully endorsed the kitchen plan or given up her wish for status.
