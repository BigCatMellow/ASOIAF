## Main narrative, actions and outcomes

1. **VERIFIED:** The Night's Watch reaches **Whitetree**, four deserted stone homes under an extraordinary weirwood with an approximately **eight-foot-wide trunk** and a mouth-like hollow large enough for a sheep. **Human skulls and ash**, not animal remains, rest within; the hollow has burned and dried sap. Jon senses power. **Smallwood** wants to fell the tree, while Mormont and Jon recall a belief that men cannot lie in front of a heart tree. These supernatural properties are **beliefs**, not demonstrated by this scene.
2. **VERIFIED / JON'S INFERENCE:** Mormont says wildlings **burn their dead** and wishes he had learned why. Jon, remembering the wight with blue eyes, is certain cremation guards against the dead rising. It is a strong hypothesis, **not direct testimony** from whoever burned the Whitetree remains.
3. **VERIFIED:** Mormont orders searches and hounds; Jon with **Eddison Tollett (Dolorous Edd)** finds only stale straw and dung. Whitetree is the **fourth abandoned village** encountered, with no signs of attack in any and no residents or livestock. The location of the inhabitants and cause/timing of evacuation are **UNKNOWN**. **Chett** tends the hounds and resents Jon; **Thoren Smallwood** wears deceased **Jaremy Rykker's** reclaimed armor and clasp.
4. **VERIFIED:** Rangers disagree: **Mallador Locke** considers fewer raiders good news; others note wildlings lived here a year before. **Jarman Buckwell** commands scouts. **Bedwyck**, nicknamed **Giant** despite being small, climbs the weirwood and spots possible lake water north and low flint hills west. Mormont chooses to march on toward the lake instead of camping under the tree.
5. **VERIFIED:** Mormont writes **Maester Aemon** about the fourth empty village. Jon brings parchment to **Sam**, who ties it to a raven and **releases it**. No delivery or reading by Aemon is shown. There are **200 men and roughly 300 mounts** in the column: scouts, Smallwood's vanguard, Mormont's main party, Locke's baggage and **Ottyn Wythers's** rear guard. Qhorin's expected additional men, discussed in `ACOK 6 (Jon)`, have **not yet joined**.
6. **VERIFIED:** Fear rises among veteran rangers as game too seems scarce and settlements are empty; **Ghost** hunts away from the column without success. Jon thinks the army's noise can explain wildlife moving away; **Dywen** suggests something else has frightened game. Both causal accounts remain open.
7. **VERIFIED:** Jon flexes burned **sword-hand fingers**, conscious of the danger of stiffness, and thinks anxiously about Arya, with no knowledge of her actual route. Sam handles his riding horse and two packhorses with **wire/wicker raven cages**, and has trained some birds to say 'snow.' Unusually, he becomes less frightened as he lives through the journey; other ostensibly braver men grow worried. His maps and bird handling have tangible value.
8. **MORMONT'S HYPOTHESIS:** Jon wonders if his missing uncle **Benjen** found the empty settlements. Mormont suggests an adversary may have silenced Benjen for discovering the cause. No evidence in this chapter establishes Benjen's fate. Jon ends believing an enemy may find the Watch before the Watch finds it.

## Agency and serious rival readings

**Mormont** has little actionable proof beyond the settlement pattern; he chooses reconnaissance and communication instead of retreat, exposing men to danger but perhaps to essential information. **Jon** reasons from the actual wight but projects one cause onto unseen wildling behavior. **Sam** may be adjusting psychologically, while veterans may be correctly alarmed by genuinely anomalous signs; confidence and accuracy are not the same.
