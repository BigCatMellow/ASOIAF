## Whole-chapter chronology and causality

1. **VERIFIED / EDMURE TAKES THE FIELD:** **Edmure Tully** leads Riverrun's capable fighters to defend **Red Fork river crossings** against Tywin. He says he hopes to make their dying **father Hoster proud**. **Catelyn** reminds him Hoster always loved him and sees that the remaining garrison is largely **old, injured, sick, untrained youths and peasants**, with **Ser Desmond Grell** in command, while frightened refugees and children crowd the castle. Edmure's plan has a real defense strength but leaves **Riverrun itself exposed to a sudden attack**.
2. **CATELYN'S INTERIOR HISTORY / CHARACTER:** Catelyn recalls the duty expected of her after two older brothers died young, and after her mother died—becoming Riverrun's acting lady even before Edmure was born. She accepted both arranged Stark marriages (**first Brandon, after his death Ned**), and did not visit wounded young **Petyr Baelish** after his failed duel with Brandon, before Hoster sent him away. Her story is direct internal recollection; it does not independently tell Petyr's feelings or justify later actions.
3. **VERIFIED / GENDER AND DUTY:** In her childhood sept Catelyn prays for Edmure and Robb, longs for advice about **Renly's unnatural death**, and recalls earlier religious and familial authorities now dead or absent. **Brienne** says battle gives soldiers the power of acting while waiting feels helpless; Catelyn counters that **childbirth kills women without generating knightly songs** and that raising children is a continuing struggle. Brienne says she **never knew her own mother**. Their reciprocal sworn protective relationship from `ACOK 39` is being lived out as service rather than imagined.
4. **DIRECT LETTER / STORM'S END RESOLUTION:** **Maester Vyman** brings a letter from **Lord Meadows**, identifying himself as the new castellan of **Storm's End**. Meadows says **Cortnay Penrose is dead**, the castle **opened to Stannis**, and the surviving garrison **swore its swords** without suffering harm. This is a **contemporaneous written notice independent of Varys's report in `ACOK 44 Tyrion`**, corroborating the forecast from `ACOK 42 Davos`. The letter **does not disclose the cause of Penrose's death, the involvement of shadow magic, or the custody of Edric Storm**.
5. **VERIFIED / MESSAGING DELAYS:** Catelyn asks for an urgent message sent to **Robb**; Vyman says the last report placed him marching from **Ashemark toward the Crag** (Westerling seat), and that a raven to Ashemark **might** be relayed by a rider. No instantaneous messenger can find a mobile army. This creates a continuing **control and information gap** while Tywin and Edmure maneuver.
6. **CATELYN'S INFERENCE / EDRIC:** **Brienne** describes **Edric Storm** as a child of perhaps seven or eight with **black hair and bright blue eyes**, frequently mistaken for **Renly's son** because Renly resembled Robert. Catelyn deduces Stannis may wish to show the boy as **evidence of Robert's ordinary dark-haired children**, contrasted with golden-haired **Joffrey**. This is a credible **character hypothesis about propaganda and inheritance**, **not a statement of Stannis's true private reason**. `ACOK 42 Davos` suggests Melisandre's flame visions are central to Stannis's insistence, which Catelyn does not know.
7. **CATELYN'S IMPORTANT MATERNAL UNCERTAINTY:** Catelyn thinks **Arya alone among her legitimate children strongly resembles Ned** and recalls that the illegitimate **Jon Snow** does. She speculates about **Jon's unknown mother**, including rumors of **Ashara Dayne of Starfall**. She knows Ned guarded this history. **The rumor is not direct proof of Jon's mother, parentage or birth circumstances**, and Catelyn explicitly admits she does not know.
8. **BOLTON LETTER / SERIOUS POLITICAL WARNING:** Catelyn recalls a **cold letter** from **Roose Bolton** received by Edmure within the preceding few days. Roose says he **crossed the Trident** and is marching on **Harrenhal** as commanded, vows to take it even at the cost of every living soul, and asks that the king weigh his service against crimes by his **bastard Ramsay**, whom **Ser Rodrik Cassel reportedly killed**. Roose calls Ramsay vicious and claims he is glad to lose him because his new **Frey wife** promises legitimate sons. This is a **documentary character statement** with potential political self-protection; it does **not** independently verify the killed man's identity or ensure Roose has no continuing Bolton interests.
9. **VERIFIED / FIRST FORD TEST:** A scout reports an **armored Lannister advance** beneath a **purple unicorn** banner, likely **Brax**, at the ford visible from Riverrun. Catelyn and Brienne watch **Lord Jason Mallister's men repel about fifty riders** with cover and a strongly defended stream crossing. Casualties enter the river. **Ser Desmond** says Edmure's plan gives the defending higher western bank, woods and archers advantages; **some Lannisters may yet force another ford**, so this single repulse is not definitive.
10. **VERIFIED / NIGHT ATTACK:** Lannister soldiers attempt the same crossing after midnight, stepping on hidden **caltrops** and losing footing in pools, while Mallister archers use **fire arrows** and inflict serious casualties. Catelyn watches from the castle roof as the men withdraw. The chapter directly demonstrates **Edmure's river fortifications work tactically**, but the failure of a probing attack still does not certify Tywin's grand intent.
11. **REPORT / PARALLEL FAILED THRUSTS:** The next day a Mallister messenger reports **Ser Flement Brax** tried another ford six leagues south, with soldiers advancing on foot behind shortened lances. Mallister arrows and **scorpions** break formation; further attacks near **Karyl Vance's upstream defenses** also reportedly fail. These are credible military reports from participating men, not Catelyn's own view of each site.
12. **VERIFIED / CLEOS INTERROGATED:** Catelyn orders **Cleos Frey** given wine before she questions him about **Tyrion's returned peace terms** and earlier attempt to free Jaime. Cleos insists he was ignorant of the disguised attackers (`ACOK 39`); she has no independent proof he knowingly conspired. He confirms Tyrion publicly promised to exchange **Sansa and Arya** for **Jaime**, before a crowded court. Catelyn knows Robb will not accept Jaime's freedom in return for the daughters despite maternal desperation. **The offer's actual feasibility is undermined by the reader's knowledge that Arya is missing from the royal court**.
13. **CATELYN'S SECRET-STATUS CLUE:** When pressed, Cleos admits he **saw Sansa at court**, appearing beautiful but visibly wan, and **did not see Arya**. This is narrower than the public assertion that both girls are available. Catelyn entertains multiple explanations: concealment for unruly behavior, imprisonment, or murder. **The reader knows Arya is alive at Harrenhal**; Catelyn still lacks this. **Do not record Catelyn as learning her exact location**.
14. **CATELYN'S BELIEF LIMIT / DAGGER:** Catelyn almost considers trusting Tyrion in light of his earlier rescue of her from hill clans and absence from Ned's death, but then touches her scarred hands and remembers that the assassin sent against Bran carried a dagger she believes was **Tyrion's**. She rejects trust. This is **her conviction** inherited from earlier manipulative testimony, not independently proven ownership or sponsorship; later research must challenge this accusation rather than treat it as a verified fact.
15. **VERIFIED / MUSIC AND BRIENNE:** At supper **Rymund the Rhymer** leads an exultant **Oxcross victory song**, transforming Grey Wind and Stark wolves into popular heroic imagery. **Brienne** says she once knew songs at **Evenfall Hall** but never sang for Renly, fearing ridicule by his fool; she remembers a foreign beautiful female singer with plum-colored eyes and an extraordinarily small waist. Her descriptions record formative embarrassment and gender pressures. The singer's nationality/identity is **unknown**, and no magical role is indicated.
16. **LATER VICTORY MESSAGE / STONE MILL:** **Three days after** Brienne predicts Tywin will probe weak points and concentrate force, and **five days after the actual clash**, **Edmure's messenger brings a victory letter**. Tywin attacked **around a dozen fords**, all reportedly held. **Lord Lefford drowned**, **Strongboar Crakehall captured**, **Addam Marbrand repulsed three times**. **Gregor Clegane** pressed hardest at **Stone Mill**, with a few men initially reaching the western bank, but Edmure used reserves to drive them back; the Mountain reportedly lost his horse and withdrew badly wounded. This is **Edmure's detailed battle report**, not Catelyn witnessing every ford or certifying later casualty conditions.
17. **VERIFIED / THE STRATEGIC TWIST:** Edmure's dispatch says **Tywin is now marching southeast**, either retreating or feinting. Riverrun celebrates the victory, and Catelyn permits it, recognizing the smallfolk's relief and her brother's competence. Privately she studies a **map** and estimates Tywin may have reached the **headwaters of the Blackwater Rush**. Because **Stannis has just gained Storm's End and threatens King's Landing**, a southeastward Lannister march may place Tywin nearer the capital: **a strategic danger that Catelyn intuits but cannot establish as his intention**. `ACOK 42–44` confirms the Stannis timetable tightening; `ACOK 45` does not yet show an army arriving at King's Landing or a decisive Blackwater encounter.
18. **ENDING CONFLICT:** The chapter ends with Catelyn **anxious despite multiple Stark/Tully victories**, no longer entirely dismissive of Edmure's skills, but unconvinced the direction of Tywin's march is favorable. This is a rare example of tactical success creating a new strategic problem for a coalition that lacks timely communications.

## Competing readings and next tests

- **Edmure's defense:** He appears militarily competent in the local task he defines: no Lannister crossing and protection of his people. However **Robb's broad strategic purpose** and the connection between Tywin's movement and imminent **Stannis assault** are not shared among all commanders. Later arguments that Edmure simply 'failed' or 'saved the capital' must reconstruct **his actual orders, intelligence available and resulting timetable**.
- **Shadow aftermath:** Lord Meadows's written message confirms Penrose dead and Storm's End surrendered, independent of Varys's channel. It does not show the impact on the child or witness shadow killing.
- **Ramsay/identity:** Roose's own written dismissal of his reported dead bastard could reflect pragmatism or a tactical distancing; do not resolve the identity of the killed party or of Reek yet.
- **Jon's parentage:** Ashara is named as *rumor*, not verification. Catelyn's thoughts provide a reference point for how little even a Stark family insider knows.
- **Arya/Sansa hostages:** Cleos verifies Sansa, not Arya, exposing fragility in Tyrion's grand prisoner exchange proposal.
- **Next:** `ACOK 46 (Bran)` to check Winterfell's response to Rodrik and Ironborn; later Tywin, Tyrion, Stannis/Blackwater chapters to explain southeastward movement and whether Edmure's tactical successes help or harm their shared cause.
