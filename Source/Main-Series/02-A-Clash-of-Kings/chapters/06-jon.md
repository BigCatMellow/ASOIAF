## Main cause and effect

1. **VERIFIED:** Jon searches for **Sam** in Castle Black's vast underground library, where Sam has spent the night compiling maps for Mormont's **great ranging**. Maps describe old wildling villages; an ancient ranger **Redwyn** records travel from **Shadow Tower to Lorn Point on Frozen Shore**, with purported giant combat and trade with **children of forest**.
2. **VERIFIED:** Sam values historical inventories and decaying texts, explaining a six-hundred-year-old fish/salt ledger can reveal *number of Watchmen and patterns of consumption*. He reports documents on children’s language, carved weirwood faces, Valyrian scrolls and maester seasonal counts **not even held by Citadel**, though their actual claims and dates remain to be checked.
3. **VERIFIED:** They use **wormwalks**, subterranean tunnels connecting keeps/towers of Castle Black that become essential when winter snow reaches **40–50 feet**. This is real shelter and logistics infrastructure distinct from the Wall's actual original architectural purpose.
4. **VERIFIED:** Mormont will lead **~200 experienced Castle Black men, three quarters rangers**, and **Qhorin Halfhand will bring another ~100 from Shadow Tower**. Two raven cages accompany the ranging because blind, aged **Aemon stays behind** and Sam serves as raven handler. These are **planned strength and logistics**, not a confirmed complete final muster on the move.
5. **VERIFIED:** **Endrew Tarth** trains a new group of recruits brought by wandering crow **Conwy** from Gulltown environs; **Donal Noye** gives an informed characterization of brothers Robert, Stannis and Renly: **Robert as steel that rusted after crowning; Stannis as strong but brittle iron; Renly as attractive copper of little practical value**. Noye actually knew the Baratheon family, but this is **judgment/analogy**, not objective prophecy of all their later choices.
6. **VERIFIED:** **Thoren Smallwood** asserts he should now be First Ranger, citing lost Benjen and dead Jaremy Rykker; **Mormont refuses**, maintaining **Benjen retains the office until proof of death** and that only the Lord Commander appoints a successor. This preserves a precise institutional status rather than assuming Benjen is confirmed dead.
7. **VERIFIED:** The Watch seeks aid from **Joffrey, Renly** and whoever else can send practical goods. Thorne is en route via **Eastwatch with Jafer's preserved hand**, still moving in its jar, to present physical evidence of wights. Mormont worries that Joffrey will not heed the warning; no successful receipt is reported.
8. **MORMONT'S HISTORICAL ACCOUNT:** Aemon descends from **Maekar I** and was offered the crown when succession became disputed after deaths of Daeron, Aerion, and Maekar. He refused, even after religious offer to release vows, so younger brother **Aegon V** ruled. Aemon chose the Wall to avoid his presence becoming a claimants' rallying point. **Directly narrated:** Mormont relates this to Jon's own **crowned brother Robb** and challenges him to acknowledge resentment alongside sworn duty.
9. **VERIFIED:** Jon admits the unequal futures trouble him but vows to keep the oath. Mormont's raven repeats “king,” an ordinary talking behavior **not proven** an external magical proclamation.
