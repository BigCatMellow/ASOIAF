## Main events and causal chain

1. **VERIFIED / GUILD INVENTORY CLAIM:** Tyrion inspects the **Alchemists' Guild**'s cold underground wildfire stores with **Hallyne**. The guild counts **7,840 jars**, including **about 4,000 Aerys-era jars**, and hopes to produce **10,000**. The sticky volatile liquid thins with heat, can burn through material and is not extinguished by water. Old jars are more sensitive to heat and sparks and can set off a chain reaction; the numbers are Hallyne's report, not a jar-by-jar independent audit.
2. **HALLYNE'S ACCOUNT:** Some antique stock remains in a flooded lower vault. **Two hundred jars** were reportedly found **beneath Baelor's Great Sept** the year before and removed carefully. The reason Aerys-era wildfire was stored there remains **UNKNOWN**. Hallyne describes sand-filled rooms above production cells with allegedly enchanted floors that release sand in an accident. Tyrion suspects engineering rather than a literal spell, but neither explanation is confirmed by a fire test.
3. **VERIFIED:** Tyrion takes **thousands of empty clay pots** for **training**. He later directs **Jacelyn Bywater** to have city gate crews practice loading and launching with **green paint**, then lit **lamp oil**, and only after proficiency with true wildfire. Men who spill should be removed from the team. This is a staged operational safety plan, not an already successful battlefield tactic. Tyrion also refuses a pyromancer demonstration for Joffrey because he fears the boy's cruelty.
4. **VERIFIED / BRONN'S REPORT:** **Bywater's urgent summons** draws Tyrion to the Gate of the Gods before he answers Cersei. **Cleos Frey has reached King's Landing** with **Robb Stark's peace terms and map** from `ACOK 7 (Catelyn)`. This confirms delivery of the first embassy leg, but not Cleos's eventual promised return to captivity. Cleos reports two armed attacks on the escort despite the peace banner, with **three dead and six injured**, and describes devastation by bands of uncertain allegiance and by both opposing armies.
5. **VERIFIED:** Tyrion refuses a simple exchange of Stark daughters for **Tion Frey and Willem Lannister**, treats Cleos courteously, and **bars the escort from entering the city** lest they see the hunger and shaky defenses. He wants to propose terms that will keep Robb hopeful while **Stafford's levies** complete their training and Tywin can resume campaigning. The narrative explicitly reveals a **delaying intention**, not actual Stark assent.
6. **VERIFIED / PUBLIC SPEECH:** A city prophet beneath the red comet denounces incest and tells a story of women producing monstrous children with fools. This *echoes the knowingly fabricated Patchface/Shireen parentage story* Littlefinger planned in `ACOK 15`; a version of the motif is now **publicly circulating**, though no directly established distribution trail runs from Littlefinger to this individual preacher. Tyrion also recalls that Joffrey recently had guards shoot **four hungry demonstrators** and cruelly told the rest they could eat the dead: a reported previous event, not witnessed in the chapter's current action.
7. **VERIFIED:** Cersei has heard that **Myrcella** will be sent to **Dorne**, and furiously confronts Tyrion. He specifies an intended betrothal to **Trystane Martell**, with Myrcella **nine** and Trystane **eleven**, wedding when she reaches fourteen. She would be an honored ward at **Sunspear**, with **Arys Oakheart** as sworn guard. The larger offer includes **Elia's killer**, a council seat and castles in the Dornish Marches. These are proposals rather than delivered hostages or concluded marriage.
8. **INFERRED / LEAK EVIDENCE:** Cersei's knowledge of **Myrcella-for-Dorne** does *not* match the **Myrcella-for-the-Vale** story Tyrion told Littlefinger or **Tommen-for-Dorne** story told Varys in `ACOK 17`. It closely fits the actual Dornish correspondence placed in **Pycelle's** hands. Tyrion closes the chapter confident he knows the informer. The person who informed Cersei is **not named on-page here**; exact mechanism needs subsequent direct corroboration.
9. **VERIFIED:** Cersei slaps Tyrion during their dispute but then **cries**, terrified of losing Myrcella and fearful of enemies taking King's Landing. Tyrion is briefly sympathetic, but she rejects his touch. He offers reassurance: Tywin's position at **Harrenhal** can keep northern infantry and cavalry apart, prevent Robb marching south, and allow a strike into Renly's rear if necessary. Tyrion admits he inferred this from **a map**, not any private statement from Tywin.
10. **VERIFIED:** Tyrion produces Robb's actual letter to Cersei as an additional bargaining instrument. The chapter closes with his expectation of gaining her assent to the Dornish offer and clarifying the leak. It establishes **neither Doran's final agreement nor the final Lannister reply to Robb**, nor use of the chain or wildfire in battle.

## Theory challenge and continuation tests

**Leak identity:** Three distinctive disclosures support a controlled information trap, and Cersei's detailed Dornish news points toward Pycelle's access. **Countercase:** Cersei might have learned via interception or another aide privy to the letters. Do not turn probability into a witnessed confession.

**War strategy:** Tyrion plans to stall Robb, and has learned enough about wildfire to mitigate risks; either plan could fail. **Cersei** is frightened for her child and may be correct that Dorne's grievances create hostage danger. **Theon/Balon and Renly/Stannis** are still independent decision-makers whose moves Tyrion does not reliably know.
