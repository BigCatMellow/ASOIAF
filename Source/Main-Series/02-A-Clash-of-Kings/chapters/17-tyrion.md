## Main narrative and causal sequence

1. **VERIFIED:** Before dawn Tyrion asks **Grand Maester Pycelle** to send two sealed copies of a secret letter to **Doran Martell**, without informing Cersei. Pycelle reluctantly agrees. While alone among the maester's neatly arranged medicinal stores, Tyrion **steals a small labeled bottle**; this scene does not state what it holds. He notes that the birds depart and privately counts **one**.
2. **VERIFIED:** Bronn, newly captain of Tyrion's guard, appraises the fighting men and notices a tactical habit in **Tallad**, a knight with three eyes on his shield. Bronn reports petitions from **Lady Tanda** (hoping to marry daughter **Lollys** to Tyrion), a **Braavosi creditor** pursuing royal debt, a river lord devastated by Lannister troops and food merchants who want protection.
3. **BRONN'S REPORT / VERIFIED RESPONSE:** A baker was reportedly burned in his own oven by an angry mob; merchants allege gold cloaks were involved. Tyrion fears hungry residents may aid besiegers, cannot supply bread, and offers the ruined river lord a comfortable room and boots to encourage loyalty. He also learns **Alliser Thorne has arrived from the Wall with a decaying hand in a jar**. Because he dislikes Thorne, Tyrion orders him confined rather than hearing him. Evidence has reached the court, but **has not been examined** here.
4. **VERIFIED / CHARACTER ASSESSMENTS:** Cersei tours the city's siege machinery and fears **Renly's reportedly vast army** may arrive quickly; she asks Tywin to come from Harrenhal and demands Jaime's release. Tyrion judges Renly's slow pageantry a strategy of waiting for others to bleed and regards **Stannis**, with professional sailors and a hidden island muster, as more dangerous. Tyrion's informers sent to Dragonstone have not returned.
5. **VERIFIED:** Littlefinger watches Joffrey shoot hares, with crossbow bolts also endangering attendants, and offers Tyrion a **Valyrian steel and dragonbone dagger**. Tyrion recognizes the implied provocation about the dagger previously attributed to him after Bran's fall. The exchange reinforces a contested provenance and Littlefinger's confidence, not independent proof of a new conspiracy.
6. **TYRION'S INVESTIGATION:** Littlefinger's rise from a minor Jon Arryn customs position rests on collecting revenue, juggling crown debt, reinvesting in grain and trade, and planting loyal **key keepers, tax assessors, mint officers, harbormasters, factors, pursers and other administrators**. Tyrion estimates most such appointments belong to him. He sees a patronage network harder to dismantle than a small lord's armed retainers; exact fraud or allegiance of each appointee is **not separately proved**.
7. **VERIFIED / UNSIGNED TREATY:** Tyrion asks Littlefinger to court **Lysa Arryn** for Joffrey, with clan raids reduced, Lysa's boy restored as Warden of the East, promised justice for Jon Arryn and **Myrcella pledged to marry Robert Arryn**. Lysa's troops would oppose **Renly or Stannis**, not the Tullys. Tyrion offers **Harrenhal and overlordship of the Trident** as Littlefinger's reward. Littlefinger claims intimacy with both Tully sisters, which Tyrion doubts, and requests a fortnight before sailing; neither promise nor title is accomplished. Tyrion counts **two**.
8. **VERIFIED:** When Varys arrives, Tyrion describes a distinct Dorne proposal involving **Elia Martell's killer**, lands, a small-council seat and a royal hostage. Varys guesses **Tommen**, and Tyrion confirms that Myrcella cannot be offered to Dorne and the Vale simultaneously. Varys says Cersei would fiercely resist losing both children. Tyrion warns that whoever tells her these plans will be his enemy and privately counts **three**.
9. **INFERRED:** The sequence **one, two, three** and incompatible marriage disclosures strongly signal a controlled **loyalty/leak test**. Pycelle has access to the sealed Dornish letters, Littlefinger hears **Myrcella–Vale**, and Varys hears **Tommen–Dorne**. The contents of Pycelle's letters are not recited here; compare the version Cersei later knows in `ACOK 20 (Tyrion)`.

## Motives, competing readings, and next tests

**Tyrion** wants viable anti-Baratheon alliances and to find who leaks council secrets, but has to use Littlefinger's indispensable financial machinery. He neglects the Watch's warning while preparing against human armies. **Pycelle, Varys and Littlefinger** each possess different information, but access is not proof of betrayal. **Lysa and Doran** have not accepted any proposed bargain; Tyrion's disclosure may be as much bait as genuine diplomacy.
