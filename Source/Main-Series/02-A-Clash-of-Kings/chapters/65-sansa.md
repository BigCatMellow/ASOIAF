## Chronological court, power and character ledger

1. **PUBLIC VICTORY AS CEREMONY:** The Iron Throne's court assembles in sumptuous finery after Blackwater. **Tywin Lannister** enters on a lavishly armored warhorse and is publicly hailed **Savior of the City**; the horse deposits manure beneath the throne, puncturing the staged grandeur. **Joffrey formally invests his grandfather as Hand of the King** until his coming of age. Tyrion's role as wartime acting Hand ends, though the chapter has heard only that he is **gravely wounded**, not a funeral or complete medical outcome.
2. **TYRELLS REWARDED:** **Mace Tyrell** arrives with sons **Loras and Garlan**. Joffrey grants golden rose-and-lion chains; Loras asks for and **receives a Kingsguard seat**, Mace requests and **receives a place on the royal council**. These are actual public grants, not merely a promise that the alliance might form later.
3. **POLITICAL THEATER OF THE NEW BETROTHAL:** **Garlan** petitions for Joffrey to wed his sister **Margaery Tyrell**, widow of dead **Renly**, claiming her marriage to Renly was **unconsummated** and she has admired Joffrey from afar. Joffrey pretends reluctant fidelity to his vow to **Sansa Stark**. **Cersei and the High Septon**, with the small council, declare the contract invalid because the Starks have allegedly committed treason, freeing him to accept Margaery. The cheers follow the staged sequence. The court's moral assessment of Ned and Robb is **Lannister propaganda, not a neutral judgment of treason**.
4. **SANSA'S CONSTRAINED JOY:** Sansa is terrified Joffrey might still refuse the annulment as he once promised mercy then ordered Ned's death, but **the formal public severance occurs** and she internally celebrates her escape from **compulsory royal marriage, bedding and pregnancy**. She must publicly appear miserable so Cersei will not interpret her relief as insolence. This is **real relief from a specific marriage**, not release from imprisonment.
5. **HOSTAGE STATUS RETAINED:** Cersei bluntly tells Sansa she will **remain a ward at court**, with disposition to be determined, and will not be allowed to go to Winterfell. Sansa understands she is still valuable to the victors. **Dontos later warns Joffrey may abuse her without a marital bond**, an informed and alarming **character forecast**, not a narrated new assault after the betrothal break.
6. **SPECIFIC BLACKWATER DISTRIBUTION:** The court publicly credits **Paxter Redwyne** (and wounded twin son), **Mathis Rowan, Randyll Tarly, Kevan Lannister, Addam Marbrand**, the lords **Lydden, Crakehall and Brax**, and others. Rewarded lesser fighters include **Philip Foote** (said to have slain **Bryce Caron**), **Lothor Brune** (heroic action against Fossoways), **Willit** (saved **Harys Swyft**) and youthful **Josmyn Peckledon** (reported multiple captures). These are **royal heralds' military claims** backed by rewards, not Sansa's live battle eyewitness accounts.
7. **ACTUAL POSTWAR GRANTS:** **Philip Foote** receives **House Caron's lands and a lordship**, **Lothor Brune** gets knighthood and promised riverlands lands after war, **Willit** receives equipment and advancement opportunities for his sons, and **Josmyn** gets armor, a sword, horse and promise of future knighthood. These are politically consequential attempts to **redistribute the property of defeated houses**, though many territories may remain contested.
8. **ALCHEMY AND DARry INHERITANCE:** **Hallyne** is raised to **lordly style** for wildfire, but receives no land or castle, leaving the title weak in practice. **Lancel Lannister** is awarded **Castle Darry and its rights** following purported extinction of legitimate heirs. He is too badly wounded to attend; rumors say his injury may cost him his arm or life. This estate transfer directly competes with the northern Tallhart takeover and Bolton's order to torch Darry in `ACOK 64 Arya`; **neither legal control nor effective physical occupation is settled by the proclamation**.
9. **LITTLEFINGER'S ENORMOUS REWARD:** **Petyr Baelish** is made **Lord of Harrenhal and Lord Paramount of the Trident**, with hereditary claims that all riverlords must acknowledge his authority. Sansa realizes **Harrenhal is currently not in Lannister hands** and the traditional riverlords swear to House Tully and Robb; the titles are **real legal grants from the royal faction but largely aspirational without conquest**. Yet this is a dramatic transformation of **Petyr's dynastic rank**, with implications for marriage eligibility, succession and alliances not necessarily visible to Sansa.
10. **SIX HUNDRED NEW KNIGHTS / ROYAL BODYGUARDS:** More than **600 new knights** are dubbed in the Great Hall after a sept vigil. Only **Balon Swann, Meryn Trant and Osmund Kettleblack** are available to do the dubbing: **Mandon Moore died**, **Sandor disappeared**, **Arys Oakheart is escorting Myrcella in Dorne**, and **Jaime remains captive**. This corroborates the general loss of Moore following the deliberate attack on Tyrion in `ACOK 61`, without naming the conspirator behind the attack.
11. **DEFEATED LORDS AND CONDITIONAL PARDON:** Numerous formerly Stannis-aligned lords appear as captives: **Celtigar, Bonifer Hasty, Estermont, Varner, Mullendore, Red Ronnet Connington, Aurane Waters** and others. Those **swearing to Joffrey** are restored to lands or rewarded; defiant captives are threatened and executed. Some 'changed allegiance during battle', indicating the depth of the **Renly-armor and Lannister–Tyrell relief's psychological effect** in `ACOK 62`.
12. **RELIGIOUS COUNTERCLAIM REMAINS ALIVE:** A defiant knight denounces Joffrey's **incestuous parentage** and promises **Stannis's return**. The **factual incest** has already been confessed by Jaime in `ACOK 55`, whereas the religious prediction of Stannis's victory remains a partisan claim. **Tywin and Kevan** suppress dissent through **Meryn Trant and the gold cloaks**, not open legal contest.
13. **THE IRON THRONE CUTS JOFFREY:** Joffrey rises in fury and accidentally **slices his arm on one of the throne's blades**, crying for his mother and interrupting the session. A fanatic prisoner claims this means the throne rejected Joffrey. The **physical cut is verified**, but the thesis that the Iron Throne judges legitimacy is **his supernatural interpretation, not independently demonstrated**. **Tywin** calmly moves proceedings forward from a lower throne step, an effective illustration of who exercises state authority.
14. **DONTOS SETS AN ESCAPE DATE:** Sansa meets Dontos in the godswood and expects less need to flee after the annulment. Dontos correctly stresses she is **still a politically valuable hostage** and claims arrangements are made to leave **on the night of Joffrey's wedding**, after the feast, exploiting drunk guests and concentrated royal attention. **Margaery is still in Highgarden and the wedding is projected at least a moon's turn away**. No ship, sponsor or escape route is directly witnessed.
15. **THE HAIRNET APPEARS:** Dontos hands Sansa **a delicate silver hairnet** dotted with **small black stones** he calls **rare black amethysts from Asshai**, which appear deep purple in daylight. He says it is **'magic', 'justice', vengeance for Ned, and home**. This is an extraordinarily high-priority physical object and ominous **speaker claim**. **The stones' material identity, poison content or method of use are not verified here**. Track provenance, handling, ceremony attendance, dress and who instructs Sansa to wear it. Avoid substituting later spoiler knowledge for present evidence.
16. **ENDING STATE:** Joffrey is betrothed to Margaery, Tywin has resumed the Handship, Littlefinger has immense new claims, and Sansa is still retained at court. Dontos's hairnet may be central to his hidden sponsor's plans; **no wedding, assassination, poison use or completed escape occurs here**.

## Competing readings and required later tests

- **Petyr's reward:** A mere castle in hostile territory sounds empty to Sansa, but elevation to **Lord Paramount** can independently alter Petyr's status for political marriage. Test if the **title itself**, rather than immediate holding of Harrenhal, enables his strategy.
- **The hairnet:** Its dark stones and Dontos's language are suspicious, but there is **no observed drug, ingestion or transfer to another person**. Rank as **high-priority suspicious object; function unresolved**.
- **Tyrell consolidation:** The marriage is a negotiated alliance, not pure romance. Sansa recognizes Margaery faces a dangerous husband; the recipient's own private consent and aims are **not directly represented**.
- **Throne rejection:** Cuts may symbolize unfitness in the narrative but are **not empirical evidence of a magical judicial throne**. Compare historical throne accidents without confusing symbol with cause.
