## Main narrative, operational choices and physical evidence

1. **VERIFIED:** The Great Ranging reaches a solitary flat-topped hill called the **Fist of the First Men**, beside the **Milkwater**, between extensive forests and the more distant **Frostfangs**. The crest contains an ancient **chest-high stone ringwall**, attributed by ranger tradition to First Men of the Dawn Age. This is physical evidence of long-standing habitation/fortification, but its precise builder, age and earlier uses are **not independently dated** here.
2. **VERIFIED / COMMAND DECISION:** **Jeor Mormont** and **Thoren Smallwood** choose the Fist for their camp and to await **Qhorin Halfhand**. The hill affords views and defensible slopes, with gaps in the wall to stake and ditch. **Jon notices the nearest brook is at the hill's foot, outside the defenses**, requiring arduous and potentially exposed water retrieval. Smallwood mocks the concern, but Mormont orders a reserve carried uphill and later **cisterns**, explicitly accepting this weakness for the strong high ground.
3. **VERIFIED:** Two hundred men and their horses create a sprawling camp of tents, shelters, tethering lines, fires and brush clearance. Mormont plans pits, spikes, caltrops, repaired wall breaches and camouflaged lookouts **in the trees and along the river**. The planned defenders will be **about 300 only when Halfhand arrives**, against an unverified thousands-strong wildling mass. **Supply and warning time**, not simply stone strength, constrain the plan.
4. **GHOST'S DIRECT BEHAVIOR:** **Ghost repeatedly balks at entering the ringfort**, even after following Jon up the hill, and is agitated rather than simply hungry. Jon first lets him hunt. His behavior is unusual but **the wolf does not explain a cause**, and a claim that he senses an imminent specific supernatural attack is **a hypothesis** rather than dialogue.
5. **VERIFIED:** Jon and **Sam** talk under stars and the red-tailed **Mormont's Torch** comet; Sam is gaining confidence and is responsible for the **ravens**. Mormont studies the rough map Sam drew from **Craster's account** (`ACOK 23`). Officers propose following the Milkwater to Frostfangs or taking passes called **Giant's Stair** or **Skirling Pass**, while **Ottyn Wythers** fears storms. Mormont decides that Mance's host must emerge to find food and the Milkwater offers a bottleneck. This is a **reasonable tactical inference**, not Mance's declared plan.
6. **VERIFIED / SEARCH LOGIC:** Smallwood wants deeper reconnaissance; Mormont keeps rangers **near the fort and on this side of the river** until Qhorin arrives, minimizing further losses after missing patrols. Jon worries about **Benjen**, but Mormont explains that 200 men searching an immense landscape are less likely to discover one man than an unharmed Benjen is to find their obvious fires and trail. Mormont privately acknowledges the dreadful possibility Benjen might return **as a wight**, like Othor or Jafer Flowers. Neither Benjen's death nor his reanimation is established.
7. **VERIFIED:** Mormont's raven repeatedly calls words such as **'old', 'corn', and 'dead'** near discussion of the Fist, food and Benjen. These echo dramatic thematic cues. It is a **talking trained raven**, so do not label each utterance verified prophecy or supernatural foreknowledge without a more discriminating test.
8. **CHARACTER SENSORY REPORT / CONTEXT:** Veteran **Dywen** says the woods **smell cold**; Jon recalls an odor like death from the earlier rising corpses and loses appetite. Other watchmen mock the assertion. The perception and Jon's memory are real; the **identity of any approaching presence is not seen** at this moment.
9. **VERIFIED:** After nightfall, **Ghost** reappears from the trees and seems urgently to lead Jon out of the camp, stopping and looking back. Jon tells a sentry he is fetching water for Mormont; the guard waves him through **without checking for a bucket**, a concrete failure of perimeter discipline. Jon descends steep, dangerous ground with a **torch**, which can also advertise his position in darkness.
10. **VERIFIED / PHYSICAL DISCOVERY:** Ghost leads Jon through brush **around roughly a quarter of the Fist** and digs at a shallow **recently disturbed mound**. Jon expects a corpse but finds a cloth-wrapped bundle **about two feet across**, buried **roughly two feet deep**, containing **a dozen obsidian/dragonglass knives**, leaf-shaped spearheads and many arrowheads. The material is black, lightweight and sharply edged; Jon identifies it as **obsidian**, not ordinary metal.
11. **VERIFIED:** Beneath the dragonglass lies an **old bronze-banded aurochs warhorn** that still has arrowheads inside it. The wrapping fabric is **thick, good-quality double-woven black wool**, damp but not rotted; Jon unfolds it and recognizes **the cloak of a sworn brother of the Night's Watch**. Its condition points to **relatively recent burial** rather than an undisturbed cache from the Dawn Age. No maker's marks, owner, depositor, magical properties or intended recipient are established in the passage.
12. **UNKNOWN:** Whether the cache was planted **deliberately for Jon/Ghost to find**, left by a ranger, buried to conceal weapons, or guarded by unseen parties is not answered. The fact Ghost was able to locate it is suggestive but not independent proof of a supernatural guide. **No test of dragonglass against Others or wights is performed**; the horn is not sounded.
