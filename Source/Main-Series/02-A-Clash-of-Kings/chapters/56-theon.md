## Sequential narrative and the central factual correction

1. **THEON'S RECURRING GUILT DREAMS:** Theon awakes from a nightmare in which **wolf-sized monsters bearing children's faces** pursue him among laughing, face-bearing trees. He remembers **dead young boys dressed at the mill** and insists, even in dreams, he had no choice. In another nightmare, the **miller's wife**, with whom he earlier had sexual encounters, kills him after he recalls that **Gelmarr struck her with an axe** as she begged Theon for mercy. These are dream impressions and Theon's memory, not proof supernatural wolves are hunting him.
2. **OCCUPATION AFTERMATH:** Theon guards himself even walking inside Winterfell. Shortly after the mill raid, **Gelmarr** reportedly fell and broke his back, **Aggar** was found with throat cut, and **Gynir Rednose** was drowned in a well despite trying to guard himself with armor and a noisy dog. Theon privately observes that the **Ironborn men involved could not keep secrets and 'had to die'**, strongly indicating **a purposeful cover-up** of the mill killings. The chapter does **not directly show who physically killed each man**, though **Reek is implicated by Theon's subsequent thought that he should have had Reek killed 'after he did the others'**.
3. **FARLEN'S WRONGFUL EXECUTION:** Rather than reveal the deception, Theon **condemns kennelmaster Farlen** as a convenient suspect in the ironborn deaths. Farlen invokes **Ned Stark's practice of personally carrying out executions**, forcing Theon to swing the axe. Theon botches it, striking **between the shoulders** and requiring **several further blows to decapitate Farlen**, becoming sick at his own violence. Theon does not establish Farlen's guilt; the narrator supplies direct internal support that **Farlen has been used as a scapegoat**.
4. **DEFINITIVE FALSE PRINCES REVEAL:** Theon has publicly mounted two **heads dipped in tar** above the gates, and recalls displaying two **headless children's bodies**, dressed in clothing to imitate **Bran and Rickon**, then burning them. **At the chapter's final lines he explicitly knows the victims were the miller's boys**, similar enough in **age, size and coloring** to the Stark heirs, while **Reek flayed their faces** to prevent recognition. Therefore the public reports of the Stark princes' murder in `ACOK 54 Tyrion` and `ACOK 55 Catelyn` are **materially false as to the identities of the displayed dead**. This does **not yet independently locate Bran or Rickon after fleeing Winterfell**, but it decisively establishes these specific corpses **are not them**.
5. **BROOCH OBJECT EXPLAINED / DECEPTION:** In `ACOK 50`, Reek showed a **silver-and-jet wolf-head clasp** resembling Bran's. Theon now recalls **recovering the melted silver and cracked jet from the burned false bodies**, retaining it as a relic. This confirms the **brooch was used to authenticate the substitution**, not that the true Bran was killed. How Reek first obtained it is **still not witnessed**.
6. **VIOLENCE AGAINST WOMEN AND CHILDREN:** The **miller's wife was killed by Gelmarr** under the occupation's mill operation; she previously had sexual encounters with Theon. The younger boys are deliberately murdered for a politically expedient substitution. The fact Theon tells himself his guards 'gave him no choice' is **not evidence of necessity**. Theon later also treats **Kyra** roughly, leaving bruises and terror after a violent sexual encounter, an additional direct example of his power used abusively.
7. **REINFORCEMENTS FAIL:** **Asha Greyjoy** arrives with about **twenty** ironborn total, but insists **half will return with her**, leaving Theon **roughly ten** additional men. Her soldiers consume a breakfast in the Great Hall while she sits in **Robb's former high seat**, mocking Theon's misshapen **iron crown with black diamonds and gold**. Theon expected a true garrison; her visit is counsel and limited escort, **not a decisive rescue force**.
8. **DAGMER'S DEFEAT NOW REPORTED:** Asha tells Theon that **Rodrik Cassel defeated Dagmer Cleftjaw's shield wall at Torrhen's Square** using superior local knowledge and mounted northern forces. **Dagmer survives and retreats toward the Stony Shore**, while **Leobald Tallhart** joins Rodrik; **Manderly** and **Umber** reinforcements are said to be moving toward Winterfell. These are informed **reports and predictions** in Theon's council, not live battlefield scenes.
9. **ASHA'S STRATEGIC COUNTERARGUMENT:** Asha credits the clever original seizure but says the conquest became a liability because Winterfell lies **far inland**, beyond practical Ironborn resupply and reinforcement. She contrasts **Deepwood Motte**, near the coast and sea routes, and says Theon should have **taken Stark heirs by ship as hostages and razed/abandoned Winterfell**, or now **burn it and retreat with her**. These are **her alternative plans**, not actions actually taken; her advocacy of razing the castle is itself severe and not a moral endorsement of her entire position.
10. **THEON'S REVENGE RATIONALIZATION:** Theon tells Asha the killings of supposed Stark princes avenged their dead brothers **Rodrik and Maron Greyjoy**, and would please their father **Balon**. Asha questions this and reminds Theon that vengeance can provoke **Rodrik Cassel** similarly. Because the dead children are **not actually Bran or Rickon**, Theon's explanation is also a **cover story built around a false equivalence**. Asha believes the displayed heads represent the Stark boys at this time, though she judges the act appalling.
11. **THEON REFUSES EXIT:** Despite recognizing the logic and fearing future relief forces, Theon refuses to abandon his claim to the **'Prince of Winterfell'** title. Asha leaves after warning he may hold Winterfell only **for the rest of his life**, which he recognizes could be very brief. No outside relief army arrives in this chapter.
12. **REEK'S INTELLIGENCE AND LEVERAGE:** Theon is frightened that **Reek knows the secret of the mill** and thinks about killing him but doubts he can prevent a written account from surviving; **Reek can read and write**, surprisingly for the apparent servant. Reek offers to **recruit 100–200 northerners** for Theon, asking for a **horse and Stark silver**. Theon approves and promises a reward if recruits materialize. The chapter does not demonstrate a recruitment network or actual reinforcements yet.
13. **PALLA USED AS BARGAINING PROPERTY:** Reek demands **Palla**, the kennel worker previously assaulted by occupying men, as a personal reward. Theon **promises to hand her over if Reek brings 200 men**, despite claiming to protect her from his own followers. **This is a concrete coercive promise endangering Palla**, not proof the bargain is fulfilled.
14. **LYANNA VISION AS A DREAM:** Theon later dreams Ned's first feast for Robert turned into a hall of corpses including **Robert, Ned, old Stark servants, Mikken, Chayle, Benfred, Farlen, miller's wife** and others. Among unfamiliar dead drawn from crypt statues are **Lyanna Stark in a pale blue rose crown, her white dress bloodied**, and **Brandon and Rickard Stark**. Finally **Robb and Grey Wind** enter **covered in many wounds**. This is explicitly **Theon's guilt-haunted dream**, not a validated prophecy of Robb's fate. The **blue roses associated with Lyanna** provide a distinct textual cross-link to **Ygritte's winter rose in `ACOK 51 Jon`** and **Dany's blue flower in a wall of ice in `ACOK 48 Daenerys`**, but **do not independently prove any parentage theory**.
15. **CLOSING SELF-RECOGNITION:** Theon walks the walls at sunrise, sees **the weirwood and godswood** and acknowledges Winterfell is culturally and spiritually **Stark**, not his. He regrets not leaving with Asha but returns his gaze to the **false heads**, explicitly identifying the real victims as **the miller's children**. His remorse and fear coexist with deliberate continuing cover-up; neither reverses what he did.

## Follow-up and rival explanations

- **Stark death reports:** This chapter **falsifies the identification of the displayed heads**. Update all earlier status theories about the boys to **'presumed dead by multiple characters, publicly staged as dead; true whereabouts unresolved'**. It would be premature to state the actual boys' precise shelter based solely on `ACOK 51` crypt folklore.
- **Reek:** His manipulation, flaying skill, literacy, recruitment proposal and knowledge of the mill make the claimed servant identity suspect. Still **do not assert a replacement identity without on-page verification**.
- **Miller's boys' parentage:** Theon had slept with their mother in earlier years, making paternal speculation possible. **No evidence of their biological father or conception timing** is given here; reject certainty.
- **Lyanna symbol:** Three independent-ish contexts now place blue roses near Starks, the Wall or Lyanna, but two are supernatural/dream imagery and one is wildling oral history. The association is **supportive of later theory research**, not independently identified familial genealogy.
