## Main plot, direct actions and emerging military plan

1. **VERIFIED:** Theon's **eight-ship Stony Shore raiding force** has attacked a fishing settlement and subsequently ambushed **Benfred Tallhart's** little band, the **Wild Hares** first mobilized in `ACOK 16 (Bran)`. Benfred and his men approach with rabbit pelts as decorations, singing and without scouts. Theon's archers fire from concealment behind gorse, and his footmen overwhelm the party. The attack succeeds not due to an equal test of formations but because of surprise, disorganization and the Tallharts' lack of recon.
2. **VERIFIED / PERSONAL CONFLICT:** Benfred, captured and bleeding, calls Theon a **turncloak** and invokes Robb's vengeance. Theon had known him years before when visiting **Torrhen's Square** with Ned Stark. **Aeron Damphair** demands death as religious punishment after Benfred spits and insults the Drowned God. Theon declines to administer execution but allows Aeron's men to take him to the surf and **drown him according to the 'old way'**. His **body is observed being dragged from the water**. Theon retains nominal command, yet his priest uncle controls a critical public display of authority.
3. **VERIFIED:** Ironborn loot the corpses, even severing a dead man's finger for a ring. **Todric**, an ironborn fighter, drunkenly quarrels with a Botley man over spoil. Theon intends to **shoot the hand holding Todric's drinking horn** to discipline him but **Todric stumbles into the path**, taking the arrow in the belly. Theon then commands Botley to silence the wounded man; the Botleys **cut his throat** and strip him. Theon publicly presents the event as a deliberate sentence against drunkenness, although **his internal intent was to shoot the hand, not kill by belly shot**. This distinction demonstrates how he performs fearsome command by reinterpreting an error.
4. **VERIFIED / ATROCITIES:** A fishing hamlet has been **burned**. Theon's raiders killed most men, let a few flee to carry terror toward **Torrhen's Square**, abducted women as **salt wives**, and raped or killed other captives unless their skills made them useful as **thralls**. Theon planned and led the assault and knows its consequences; his inward disgust and rhetorical sense of having 'no choice' **do not reduce responsibility for the actions he commanded**.
5. **VERIFIED:** Theon contrasts looting and butchery with the pride he once felt in **Robb's Whispering Wood victory**. He remembers Ned's judgment, then defensively rejects him as dead and not his father. The chapter directly records a developing **identity conflict**: honor learned at Winterfell versus harsh Ironborn status demands and anger at Asha's perceived preferential assignment.
6. **VERIFIED / DAGMER RELATIONSHIP:** **Dagmer Cleftjaw** remained with the ships aboard **Foamdrinker**, so no one could credit the ambush to him. Yet he greets Theon warmly, remembers training him to ride, shoot and fight, and smiles as a paternal mentor. Theon reflects Dagmer praised him more often than either Balon or Ned. Dagmer is **not actually a blood uncle in close kinship**, but Theon has always called him that.
7. **CHARACTER ACCOUNT / SUPPORT NETWORK:** Dagmer explains why **Balon** relies on **Asha**: after his sons died or were fostered away, she stayed, learned and did not disappoint. He does not accuse Theon of cowardice, only of wanting premature proof of manhood. This is a plausible alternative to Theon's accusation of simple spite. Dagmer names **Andrik the Unsmiling**, **Black Lorren** and **Qarl the Maid** among the islands' other reputed dangerous fighters; reputations are his accounts, not battle outcomes in the chapter.
8. **VERIFIED / DEPARTURE FROM BALON'S ORDERS:** Theon proposes **distributing the eight ships**: give **Aeron six** to continue distracting Stony Shore raids while keeping **Sea Bitch and Foamdrinker** for an **unauthorized inland operation** with Dagmer. He argues that success will force Balon to recognize him as rightful heir. Dagmer questions both the authority and the practicality.
9. **VERIFIED / FEINT STRUCTURE:** Theon asks Dagmer to take the larger part of their **remaining mobile force** against **Torrhen's Square**. He wants the approach **conspicuous**, with songs and siege equipment visibly assembled outside stone walls **thirty feet high**, even though Dagmer's men cannot actually storm or starve out the place. Theon's crucial instruction is **not to prevent a raven from flying**. He expects castellan **Leobald Tallhart** to ask for help, prompting **Ser Rodrik Cassel** to leave Winterfell with its remaining fighting men out of duty.
10. **VERIFIED / FINAL REVEAL BOUNDARY:** When Dagmer objects that he cannot realistically **capture Torrhen's Square** or defeat reinforcements, Theon smiles and reveals his goal **is not Torrhen's Square**. The natural strategic inference is an attack on **Winterfell**, now weakened by Rodrik's departure, especially given Theon's familiarity with its defenses. However the chapter **stops without Theon explicitly naming the actual target or demonstrating that the raven reaches Winterfell**, so record Winterfell as **strongly inferred plan**, not executed seizure.
11. **VERIFIED / OPERATIONAL RISKS:** Theon has only eight ships total, incomplete numbers of seized horses and questionable shore troops; the deliberate diversion creates vulnerability if Rodrik acts differently, garrison scouts detect the true attack, or Dagmer refuses to play the part. The plan uses knowledge of Tallhart holdings and Rodrik's dutiful nature, learned during Theon's Stark upbringing. Its success remains unknown at end.

## Rival readings, ethics and continuation research

- **Theon's 'no choice':** He chooses methods of intimidation and slaughter in hopes of belonging. Asha's recognition by Balon and his own insecurity explain his motives but do not absolve civilians' suffering.
- **Authority:** Aeron can claim men's religious allegiance, while Dagmer provides affection, legitimacy and possible consent to divergence. Theon must cultivate Dagmer because **neither inherited title nor eight ships alone guarantee obedience**.
- **The plan:** Using a **raven as bait** and making Torrhen's Square a loud, impossible siege is a materially specific deception. The reader can infer Winterfell as an intended target, but defer the actual attack and outcome. Compare `ACOK 35 (Bran)` for the existing threat warnings and Rodrik's dispersal of defenders.
- **Prophecy:** Jojen's **salt sea around Winterfell** looks especially relevant once the ironborn turn the coast attack into an inland decoy. This is corroborating political intelligence, **not literal seawater flooding the castle** at this stage.
