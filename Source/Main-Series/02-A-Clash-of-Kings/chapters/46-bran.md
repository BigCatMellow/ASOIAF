## Narrative sequence and consequential decisions

1. **DIRECT WOLF EXPERIENCE:** The chapter opens in **Summer's sensory perspective**, with **Shaggydog** alongside him in Winterfell's godswood. Both hear unfamiliar **steel scraping stone**, detect strange man-smell, and attempt to raise an alarm. Summer howls and rushes to a **locked, chained gate**, finds it secured, and hears a thoughtlike direction toward a **slanting sentinel tree** that reaches toward rooftops. The wolf-brain momentarily remembers how Bran used to **climb that tree**; Summer attempts the ascent, slips on wet bark and **falls**. Bran awakes sharing the impression of physical pain. This is direct narrative confirmation of **Bran's consciousness perceiving through Summer**, further corroborating Jojen's explanation, not proof Bran can fully control every animal action.
2. **DREAM–WAKING MATCH:** Bran hears the same dogs barking upon waking and understands the danger behind Jojen's **'sea flooding Winterfell' green dream** (`ACOK 35`). The external attackers have not conjured literal salt water; they are **Ironborn**. Crucially, Bran **tries urgently to summon Hodor, Osha and the Reed children but cannot get immediate help**. The chapter illustrates accurate warning with too little time and an inaccessible witness; foresight does not ensure prevention.
3. **DEFENSIVE VACUUM REALIZED:** **Eight days earlier Ser Rodrik Cassel** departed Winterfell with **about 600 men** toward **Torrhen's Square**, where **Dagmer Cleftjaw** had staged the attack as Theon's lure (`ACOK 37`). **Cley Cerwyn** was to join him with **300 more**, while **Luwin** had called up more northern levies. Bran's door guard has been removed; Winterfell retains only a **token garrison**. This decisively confirms that Theon's plan to make Rodrik leave actually succeeded.
4. **DIRECT CONQUEST:** An armed stranger **Werlag** enters Bran's bedchamber, followed by **Theon Greyjoy**, who announces he has taken Winterfell and styled himself **Prince Theon**. He says **four men scaled the walls with grappling claws and ropes**, opened a **postern**, and let the rest into the castle. He demands that **Bran publicly yield** and order the inhabitants to obey Greyjoy rule. Bran initially refuses because Theon is supposed to be **Ned's ward**; Theon explicitly turns that personal relationship around, saying Bran and Rickon are now his wards. Theon promises to spare residents if Bran cooperates; this is **coercive hostage governance**, not freely granted lordship.
5. **CORROBORATION / RAVENS:** **Luwin** arrives wounded and confirms attackers **swam the moat** and surprised **Alebelly** on the gate, **killing him**; guard **Hayhead** was wounded. Luwin managed to send **two ravens**, but only the **White Harbor** bird escaped while the **other was shot down**. News has a plausible path to a major northern ally, but **receipt, response and timing** are unverified.
6. **VERIFIED / CHOICE TO SAVE CIVILIANS:** Luwin tells Bran that Ned could not overcome all Theon's upbringing and that a lord's duty is to **protect his smallfolk**. Bran reluctantly dresses in princely clothes and prepares to submit, explicitly weighing his threatened people against symbolic resistance. **Rickon** demands his mother and Shaggydog. **Meera, Jojen, the two Walder Freys, Osha, Mikken, Septon Chayle and numerous servants** are herded to the Great Hall. **Jojen** has a sorrowful, recognizing look; no claim that he can halt the attack is supported.
7. **DIRECT OCCUPATION / FORCE NUMBERS:** Theon occupies **Robb's carved high seat** beneath the golden kraken. Bran counts **roughly twenty ironmen in the hall** and thinks perhaps **thirty total** including gates and armory. The exact initial assault strength is not independently mustered, but the on-page picture is a **small, mobile strike party overcoming a largely empty stronghold**. Theon claims **Balon has crowned himself king of the Iron Islands and the North** by right of conquest; these are **Ironborn sovereignty claims**, not accepted northern consent.
8. **DIRECT ABUSES / CONFIRMED DREAM DEATHS:** **Palla** is brought in visibly injured, her clothing torn, walking painfully with **Farlen**; the condition shows serious assault or abuse, but **the specific act perpetrated is not narrated firsthand**, so do not assert a named sexual attacker from this scene alone. **Septon Chayle** is struck while trying to help her. **Mikken** denounces the invasion despite Bran's plea for quiet. A bald ironman identified as **Stygg** beats Mikken, then **drives a spear through his neck**, killing him in front of the hostages. When Hodor cries out, **two ironmen brutally beat him**. Bran remembers Jojen's 'sea' dream with **Mikken drowned in blood**: this is a direct on-page fulfillment of one named fatality and **Alebelly has already died**; the fate of **Septon Chayle** remains open here.
9. **BRAN'S PUBLIC SUBMISSION:** Bran announces that he has yielded Winterfell to **Prince Theon** and calls for obedience to protect the inhabitants. Mikken continues to resist, dying. Bran's statement is produced under overwhelming force and threats, and therefore **cannot be conflated with the Stark dynasty voluntarily recognizing Balon's sovereignty**.
10. **REPORTED NORTHERN OBJECTIVES:** Theon asserts that **Torrhen's Square and Deepwood Motte will soon be Greyjoy-held**, and that his uncle will push up **the Saltspear toward Moat Cailin**. These statements combine a documented overarching invasion plan (`ACOK 24`) with **forecasts**, not eyewitness verification of each objective's success. Theon's seizure of Winterfell itself is independently witnessed by Bran.
11. **REEK'S IDENTITY CLAIM / PARTIAL DREAM PAYOFF:** The prisoner **Reek** is freed from a tower cell and gives his name as **Heke**, allegedly a former servant to Ramsay Bolton killed in the Hornwood affair. Theon mocks his smell, then accepts his pledge after Reek insists he has knowledge of northern lords and might serve him. Bran sees this as **Jojen's dangerous Reek dream coming true** at the level of Reek **changing alignment and obtaining a sword**. **The true identity of the captive, Ramsay's confirmed fate, and any attack on Bran and Rickon remain unknown** in this POV.
12. **OSHA'S OATH / INTERESTED PERFORMANCE:** **Osha** steps forward, describes herself as an unwilling Stark kitchen slave and offers fighting service. After humiliating and disarming **Stygg**, she impresses Theon and **swears to him**. Bran privately experiences this as betrayal, but only her **overt pledge** is established, not her private allegiance or intentions. She is a capable survivor with experience presenting a plausible motive.
13. **ENDING:** Theon releases the frightened servants to their tasks, with threats. **Hodor carries Bran away**, visibly swollen and bloodied from beating. **Winterfell has fallen but its population and Stark boys are alive at the chapter's end**; the predicted flayed-faces deaths from Jojen's later dream (`ACOK 35`) are **not** confirmed here.

## Interpretive tests and future source checks

- **Green dreams:** The **'sea'** is clearly compatible with Ironborn movement, with **Alebelly and Mikken now confirmed dead**, substantially strengthening Jojen's symbolic predictive credibility. This does not automatically validate the entire **Reek/crypt/flayed-faces** vision; it must be tested separately.
- **Theon and agency:** His operation succeeds through a calculated distraction and intimate knowledge of Winterfell's inhabitants. The command decisions, coercion and killings under his rule are not excused by his past hostage position.
- **Wolf memory and guidance:** The sentinel thought and recovered childhood climbing technique are stronger than a normal dream resemblance, yet whether Bran consciously directs Summer or whether a further influence operates remains a genuine mechanistic question.
- **Osha/Reek:** Both pledge to a successful invader. They have different incentives and uncertain inner loyalties; neither is independently proven a trusted Ironborn partisan by words alone.
