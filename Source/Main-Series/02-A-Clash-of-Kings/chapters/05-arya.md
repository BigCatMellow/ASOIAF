## Plot and choices

1. **VERIFIED:** Yoren's convoy travels **northward against massive southbound refugee traffic**; fields are burned, holdfasts closed, roads unsafe. A merchant offers to buy the wagons at a quarter their worth, explicitly exploiting the war. Arya notices children's graves multiplying.
2. **VERIFIED:** **Praed**, who had been coughing, dies at camp. His weapons and armor are redistributed: the **Bull receives Praed's longsword**; Tarber drops acorns into the grave, hoping a tree will grow above him. This is an actual small death/ritual, not an irrelevant detail.
3. **VERIFIED:** At an inn Yoren buys pork pies and bathhouse access. The innkeeper says his brother **Lync** took the black after stealing pepper; his fate there is not verified. Travelers spread reports about Robb riding a huge wolf and a **massive wolf pack near the Gods Eye led by a dangerous she-wolf**, possibly linked to Nymeria.
4. **RUMOR:** One speaker claims the leading she-wolf carried off a baby, killed dogs hunting her and terrorized a town. Arya, who associates the area with Nymeria's release near the Trident, instinctively disputes the child-eating story. **NO narrator confirms the she-wolf is Nymeria in this chapter**; it is a high-value identity/movement hypothesis with embellished hostile folklore.
5. **VERIFIED:** Arya meets the three fettered prisoners: **Jaqen H'ghar**, who **says** he is from **Lorath** and speaks in unusual third-person constructions; **Rorge**, noseless and threatening; **Biter**, tongueless and violent, teeth filed to points. Jaqen tries to win her attention with a plea for beer/friendship. Rorge and Biter intimidate; Arya strikes Biter with her wooden sword when his chains stop him short.
6. **VERIFIED:** **Six gold cloaks** ride into the inn, an officer displaying a **queen's written/sealed warrant** for a particular boy. Arya initially believes they seek **her** and hides with the Bull; she threatens to fight when Yoren and the recruits take arms.
7. **VERIFIED:** Yoren asserts men under his Night's Watch charge cannot be retrieved for old crimes and faces down the officer by blade and by the recruits' numerical advantage. Hot Pie, Tarber, Cutjack, Kurz, Koss, Reysen and even Dobber visibly stand their ground. The gold cloaks withdraw after Yoren disarms their officer and warns of more men within.
8. **CRUCIAL DIRECT REVEAL:** The officer says “the one we want” is **the Bull**, not Arya. The boy directly identifies himself as **Gendry**. Yoren gives **Arya and Gendry his two fastest horses** and instructs both to flee toward the Wall if gold cloaks return; he doesn't know why the queen wants Gendry. **UNKNOWN:** exact instruction source to the officers (despite queen's seal) and whether they know Gendry's parentage.
