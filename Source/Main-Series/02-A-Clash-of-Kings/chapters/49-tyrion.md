## Narrative and operational record

1. **DIRECT MILITARY DEPLOYMENT:** Tyrion sends **Shagga's Stone Crows** across the Blackwater by ferry to harass Stannis's advance in the **kingswood**. **Timett's Burned Men** entered two days earlier; **Black Ears and Moon Brothers** followed yesterday. The raiders are ordered to **ambush scouts and supply trains, strike at night, kill stragglers and avoid pitched battle**, using guides familiar with the woods. This demonstrates coherent delay and morale-disruption planning, not an imagined destruction of Stannis's main army. Their results are **not reported here**.
2. **VERIFIED / FORCES AND RELIABILITY:** Bronn now controls **nearly 800 hired swords**, promised knighthoods and lands after victory. Bronn says inducements may make them **kill** but do not make them **die** for Tyrion. **Bywater** estimates a **6,000-man City Watch**, only **about one quarter fully dependable**; hundreds are new recruits who joined for food, beer and safety and may rout together if one breaks. Tyrion has **no more than 300 knights, squires and professional men-at-arms**. These figures are his briefing-level assessments, not a census guaranteeing particular individual actions during the siege.
3. **VERIFIED / STARVATION:** With land supply routes cut, fish cost **roughly ten times prewar prices**, desperate buyers crowd the river market, and **children with swollen bellies fight over pieces of a rotten fish thrown at Tyrion**. This is a direct, unusually precise observation of **food insecurity and anger**, continuing `ACOK 41` riot. Tyrion experiences curses and calls for retaliation yet cannot rapidly solve the blockade.
4. **VERIFIED / WATERFRONT CLEARANCE:** At the **Mud Gate** Tyrion notes dense wooden shacks, stalls, inns, warehouses and houses attached to the defensive wall. Such structures could assist **enemy assault without ladders**. He orders **Bronn with one hundred men to burn/remove everything between waterfront and city walls**, giving residents time to remove property, forbidding rape and instructing that no civilians be killed unnecessarily. The command is real; **completion, losses of property and compliance with those restrictions are not witnessed**. Defensive utility coexists with brutal displacement of vulnerable residents.
5. **VERIFIED / SIEGE MACHINES:** Three enormous oak-and-iron **trebuchets** stand just inside the **Mud Gate**, nicknamed the **Three Whores** by the City Watch. Carpentry crews install hoardings and enhance wall defenses. Tyrion also seized **merchant ships** for wartime naval needs, bringing angry foreign captains to petition him. He offers compensation **after the war**, contingent on the Lannisters' survival. These are tangible material preparations and liabilities.
6. **IMPORTANT CROSS-POV AWARENESS:** **Varys** already told Tyrion that **Theon Greyjoy seized Winterfell** while **Stark loyalists have occupied Harrenhal**. `ACOK 46 Bran` and `ACOK 47 Arya` independently verify both events. Tyrion should welcome the damage to Robb but feels displaced memories of Winterfell and its **godswood**; he believes Greyjoy occupation of the stones can never confer a Stark-like identity upon that sacred place. This is his emotional interpretation, not a magical ownership rule.
7. **VERIFIED / NEW KINGSGUARD:** At a ceremony conducted by a newly installed **High Septon**, Joffrey appoints **Balon Swann** in place of riot-killed **Preston Greenfield**, and **Osmund Kettleblack** in place of **Boros Blount**. The Swanns hedge their family's civil-war prospects by placing one son with different claimants. Tyrion admires Balon's genuine martial reputation but privately knows **Osmund has been supplying Cersei's information through Bronn**, expanding Tyrion's espionage close to Joffrey. Osmund's appointment is real; permanent loyalty remains untested.
8. **TOMMEN'S CUSTODY RESULT CONFIRMED:** **Boros Blount** was escorting Tommen and sick **Gyles Rosby** when **Jacelyn Bywater** intercepted them as Tyrion secretly ordered in `ACOK 44`. Boros **surrendered without meaningful resistance**, so Cersei stripped him of his Kingsguard cloak; he is held **in Rosby's dungeon**. **Tommen is at Rosby**, defended by Bywater's men, enjoying a **pet fawn** and missing Myrcella more than Joffrey. Bywater visits Tyrion and mentions contingency orders to move the boy if King’s Landing falls, but **refuses to repeat the secret instructions**, exactly as Tyrion demanded. The Rosby counterplan is **successfully executed**, not merely proposed.
9. **SHAE POSITION CONFIRMED:** Tyrion recalls that **Shae has entered Lady Tanda Stokeworth's household as Lollys's bedmaid**, as Varys proposed in `ACOK 44`. Tyrion has required her to leave most expensive jewelry and conspicuous clothes behind to maintain her cover. **Varys reports she is well**, but Tyrion has not yet seen her in the new role. The work placement is confirmed as accomplished, while protection from Cersei or discovery is **not guaranteed**.
10. **VERIFIED / PRIESTLY PROPAGANDA:** The new High Septon is **Tyrion's chosen candidate** and knows who arranged his elevation. Tyrion instructs him to tell worshipers **Stannis means to burn the Great Sept of Baelor**, reasoning that Stannis destroyed the **Storm's End godswood** as part of his new faith. Asked whether the Sept claim is true, Tyrion replies **it may be**; he intentionally blurs unproven prediction into **religious mobilization propaganda**. The statement must **not** be indexed as an established Stannis battle plan.
11. **BALON'S DIPLOMATIC OFFER:** A letter from **Balon Greyjoy**, signing himself **King of the Isles and the North**, invites **Joffrey** to negotiate territorial boundaries and possible alliance. Tyrion sees an Ironborn fleet could help **only if it could reach Blackwater in time**, which geographic separation makes unrealistic, and he hesitates at conceding **half the realm**. This is an **actual letter**, not a concluded alliance or surrender of Lannister claims to the North.
12. **CRITICAL WILDFIRE TALLY:** **Hallyne the Pyromancer** says the Guild now possesses **almost 13,000 jars of wildfire**, far beyond earlier projections (`ACOK 20 Tyrion` reported 7,840 then). **Over 300 extra Rossart-era jars** were found in a hidden storage space **under the Dragonpit** when a customer fell through a rotten floor and reportedly tried drinking from a container. This is an extraordinarily dangerous rediscovered cache, part of a recurring pattern of **Aerys-era wildfire hidden around the capital**. Exact bookkeeper totals are **Hallyne's reported inventory**, not independently counted by Tyrion.
13. **EXTRAORDINARY CROSS-POV MAGIC CLUE:** Pressed on how the total rose so rapidly, Hallyne initially cites training and harder labor, then admits that **ancient spells used in producing wildfire are working substantially better** than in previous years. He asks Tyrion whether **dragons have returned**, recalling elder **Pollitor's** explanation that magic began failing when the last dragons died. **Tyrion denies any knowledge of living dragons**. This is a nearly independent **King's Landing corroboration** of **Quaithe's explicit claim in `ACOK 40 Daenerys`** that magic is reviving after Daenerys hatched dragons and of the startling Undying encounter in `ACOK 48`. Importantly, it remains **a correlation supported by two practitioners/reports**, not a controlled proof that dragons cause every magical effect or spell. Hallyne's confessions may also be motivated by fear of fraud accusations.
14. **ANTLER MEN CONSPIRACY:** **Varys** sends Tyrion a **list of prosperous merchants, craftsmen and traders** calling themselves the **Antler Men** who believe Stannis will win. They have reportedly **armed several hundred followers**, intending to **seize the Old Gate during the battle** and admit Stannis's troops. The named conspirator **Salloreon**, a skilled armorer making Tyrion a demon-horned helm, is among them. Tyrion writes **an arrest order**. These are **Varys's intelligence allegations and Tyrion's response**, not an on-page attempted gate seizure or already completed roundup.
15. **ENDING STATE:** The capital prepares for a siege through divided forces—defenses, harassers, false religious stories, coerced civilian clearance, secret dynastic fallback and stockpiled wildfire—while internal allegiance remains fragile. The chapter has not yet depicted the Blackwater battle itself.

## Competing interpretations and future research

- **Dragon–magic correlation:** Hallyne's surprise and his question about dragons **independently echo Quaithe**, who has no established communication with him. That materially strengthens the proposition that the world is changing, while magical revival could have a third cause—or alchemists could be wrong about causal direction.
- **Wildfire stockpiles:** The repeated discoveries of old Rossart/Aerys stores should be tracked **by location, jar count, physical safety and provenance**. Avoid confusing new manufacture with caches; the hidden Dragonpit jars are a subset of total stock.
- **Tommen/Joffrey:** Tyrion's Rosby operation succeeded and preserves a surviving royal claimant if Joffrey falls. Boros's cowardice has altered the Kingsguard; Osmund's double-paid loyalties create a further internal weak point.
- **Political defense:** Inflaming fear of Stannis's religion is deliberate propaganda, even if the theological conflict is real. The Antler Men show how an ostensibly secure gate could be compromised from within by economic elites.
- **Next:** Blackwater Tyrion/Sansa/Davos for wildfire and chain deployment, fate of Antler Men and defenders; later evidence on dragons/magic worldwide; Tyrell and Greyjoy negotiations.
