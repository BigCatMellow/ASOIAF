## Main plot and causal hinge

1. **VERIFIED:** **Mandon Moore** tries to block Tyrion from the small council on Cersei's orders. Bronn and Timett provide implicit protection but do not fight. **Tywin's sealed letter** names Tyrion to act as Hand until Tywin arrives; Cersei reads the document but challenges his legitimacy. Tyrion takes the Hand's chair.
2. **VERIFIED:** The council comprises **Cersei, Varys, Littlefinger, Pycelle and Janos Slynt**; all have different incentives and reaction to his arrival. Tyrion chooses the **Tower of the Hand** despite Petyr's dark comparison to prior murdered/exiled Hands.
3. **VERIFIED:** Cersei confirms **Joffrey personally overrode the planned pardon of Ned**, and that she needed Slynt's gold cloaks, arranged by **Littlefinger**. **SHE CLAIMS** Ned was plotting jointly with Renly; the accurate family-information chain is more complicated.
4. **DIRECT CERSEI CONFIRMATION:** Sansa voluntarily told her the **Stark evacuation plans**, corroborating Sansa's remembered act `AGOT 51`. Cersei confirms **Arya escaped Meryn Trant** and that the regime falsely claims she is in custody; her whereabouts **UNKNOWN** to Cersei.
5. **CERSEI'S ACCOUNT OF ROBERT:** Lancel brought Robert **fortified wine three times normal strength** during the boar hunt, at their direction. Cersei says Robert might have refused but emptied two skins. This is a very significant **first-person admission of intentionally facilitating his drunkenness**, adding substantially to Varys's earlier secondhand account (`AGOT 58`), without proving guaranteed death from a boar.
6. **VERIFIED:** Cersei says **Varys proposed Barristan's dismissal**, and gold cloaks have failed to arrest the knight after he killed two of them at the Mud Gate (**CHARACTER REPORT**). Tyrion realizes Selmy's military reputation may now benefit an opponent.
7. **VERIFIED:** Tyrion gives a deliberately **false promise** to share all intentions with Cersei. She slaps him repeatedly when he describes her incest with Jaime; he presses further to expose real operational failures and demands Sansa be protected as leverage for Jaime.
8. **VERIFIED:** Tyrion tours the capital and sees **severe food shortage, prices roughly triple a year earlier, refugees and corpses**. Guard Captain **Vylarr** reports Slynt tripled the City Watch, a thousand workers strengthen defenses, siege equipment is under construction, and the Alchemists' Guild promises **ten thousand jars of wildfire**. **Petry's entry tax** on refugee arrivals helps finance defenses; its material effect is exploitation of people seeking sanctuary.
9. **VERIFIED:** Tyrion orders Ned's and other tarred **heads removed from city walls** despite Joffrey's desire to display them. He lodges **Shae in the Broken Anvil inn outside the Gate of the Gods**, leaving Black Ears as guard, contrary to Tywin's command that he not bring her to court.
10. **VERIFIED:** **Varys is already at the inn when Tyrion returns**. His seemingly pleasant chatter about the gate's watching eyes and lawless men implies he knows Shae's whereabouts and can reach her, a **plausible threat inferred by Tyrion**. Varys then gives the parable of **king, priest and rich man each commanding a sellsword to kill the other two**. Tyrion says the outcome depends on the sellsword; this is a **power analysis, not verified metaphysical law**. Tyrion ends thinking he will use Handship to do justice.
