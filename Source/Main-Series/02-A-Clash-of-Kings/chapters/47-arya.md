## Main sequence and decisive reversals

1. **VERIFIED:** Arya, still disguised as **Weasel**, slips barefoot from the Kingspyre servants' cellar while **Pinkeye (Mebble)** sleeps drunkenly, visiting **Hot Pie** in Harrenhal's night kitchen. She retains Syrio Forel's training in stealth and listening. Hot Pie dislikes leaving because he receives food and relative safety, a contrast with Arya's urgency to return to Winterfell. The **two horn blasts** she hears signal something approaching or a gate being opened; this is not a Watch horn code and has no direct connection with Jon's cracked horn.
2. **DIRECT ARRIVAL / HOSTILE SHOW:** **Vargo Hoat's Brave Companions** return at night with plunder, **striped zorses, a caged black bear**, and **more than 100 wounded-looking northern prisoners** marching bound in a chain. Arya recognizes sigils including **Karstark, Frey, Cerwyn and a mailed silver fist**; the leading figures are **Robett Glover** and **Ser Aenys Frey**. Hoat publicly tells **Ser Amory Lorch**, Tywin's remaining castellan, that his sellswords defeated **Roose Bolton's van**. At this point it is **Hoat's claim**, not a separately observed battle.
3. **VERIFIED / IMPENDING BETRAYAL SIGNS:** Lorch and Hoat openly dislike each other. Lorch refuses to honor Hoat's promise of gentle treatment, instead crowding the men into the **great cell beneath the Widow's Tower**. They are allegedly wounded but will later move with unusual readiness when freed. A hundred-man Lannister garrison inhabits a castle with perhaps **a thousand entrances/doors**: after Tywin's departure `ACOK 38`, control over movement is thin, creating a vulnerability to conspirators already inside.
4. **VERIFIED / ARYA AS DECISION-MAKER:** Arya asks **Gendry** to break the prison door with his smithing hammer and help northern captives seize the castle. He refuses because even two guards are formidable and he fears **Hoat's punishment of amputating hands and feet**. He also explains why a smith might prefer safe employment under any occupying lord, citing **Ben Blackthumb**, who served several dynasties at Harrenhal. Arya offers him a place at Winterfell; he challenges her assumption that a lord's daughter can decide what a working smith's life should become. **His refusal is self-protective and class-conscious**, not proved treachery.
5. **VERIFIED / PSYCHOLOGICAL SHIFT:** Arya privately values the third promised killing partly because **once the final name is spent, she may again be powerless**. Chiswyck and Weese killed or dead shortly after her names (`ACOK 30/38`) provided a sense of protection even though she did not control the methods. She rehearses **Syrio's water-dancing** with a crude **broken broomstick sword**, practicing stealth and balance among the **godswood branches**, not a genuine steel Needle recovery.
6. **VERIFIED / PRAYER AND APPEARANCE:** Alone at Harrenhal's **weirwood**, Arya prays for captives to be freed, her safe return home and courage, then angrily rebukes the gods for failing Ned. **Jaqen H'ghar** appears silently and again claims a debt of **one life**. His timing encourages Arya to wonder if gods sent him, but the chapter **does not demonstrate divine causation**.
7. **VERIFIED / THE CRUCIAL CONTRACT:** Jaqen reiterates that **she may name any living person** and he will eventually kill them, even a king, though travel could take days or a year; he swears by **old gods, seven gods and gods of sea, air and fire**. When he refuses her request to free the prisoners because it requires more than one killing, she names **Jaqen H'ghar himself** as her **third target**. He suddenly produces a knife and visibly panics, then asks her to change the name in exchange for help. **Arya conditions her withdrawal on a concrete rescue**, creating a stronger bargain than her earlier literal death requests. His willingness to comply demonstrates **some constraint of oath or identity**, but whether it is magical compulsion or strict personal honor is not yet proved.
8. **DIRECT EXECUTION PLAN:** Jaqen insists she stay in the **kitchens** and arrange **hot broth** for the company, while he coordinates with **Rorge and Biter**. She does not invite those dangerous prisoners herself and is surprised they appear. Jaqen, Rorge, Biter and Arya carry **scalding kettles** to the **Widow's Tower** under the claim that even captives must eat. Two door guards admit them; the occupied castle's routine feeding operation has become the weapon.
9. **DIRECT MASS KILLING:** **Eight dungeon guards** collect around the soup. **Rorge, Jaqen and Biter fling the boiling broth over them**, then slaughter the scalded men—Rorge cuts throats, Biter breaks necks and Jaqen kills a swordsman through the heart. The attackers take the key and release **Robett Glover and the other northerners**. Arya is an **intentional participant and accomplice to a violent prisoner release**, although she is not shown personally killing those guards. Jaqen wipes blood onto her clothes, asserting the violence was done on her behalf.
10. **REEVALUATION OF 'CAPTIVES':** Glover is surprised that **Hoat's allies** saved them. He says these men **were not with Hoat when he came to Bolton's encampment**, strongly suggesting prior planning with **Roose Bolton**. After release, the 'wounded' captives move quickly, disarm or kill guards, and fighting immediately erupts across Harrenhal. **Rorge** identifies himself and Biter as members of the **Brave Companions now**; their power position has shifted, not their moral character.
11. **VERIFIED / NAME DISCHARGE:** Jaqen states the death-debt was already more than paid by slain dungeon guards and asks Arya to **unsay his name**. She withdraws the curse but asks for one remaining death, which he **refuses**. This is an **explicit settlement of the three-deaths bargain by multiple rescue killings**, not proof he has an unlimited mandate to kill for her.
12. **DIRECT FACE CHANGE:** Jaqen then says his old identity must die and deliberately **runs a hand down his face**, changing his **nose, scar, facial structure, hair and gold-toothed appearance** in front of Arya. This is a **direct narrated extraordinary transformation**, stronger than generic stories of his stealth or the unexplained Weese-dog killing. Whether the mechanism involves glamor, physical facechanging, specialized training or something else is **not identified**. It is **not** shown that Rorge and Biter share the ability.
13. **VERIFIED / BRAAVOSI KEY:** The transformed man invites Arya to learn abroad. She refuses because **Winterfell remains her goal**. He gives her a small **iron coin** and instructs her to show it to a **Braavosi** while saying **`valar morghulis`**, if she ever seeks him or his people. Arya does not know the phrase's meaning and cannot use the coin simply to purchase a horse. This is a **specific future access token and verbal credential**, not mundane cash.
14. **VERIFIED / HARRRENHAL'S FLIP:** By dawn the **Brave Companions have killed Lorch's men in beds and at drunken tables**, according to Pinkeye. The new lord **Roose Bolton** arrives, receives the castle from **Vargo Hoat**, and raises **Stark direwolf** and **Bolton flayed man** banners after removing the **Lannister lion and Lorch manticore**. This materially confirms **Hoat's declared anti-Bolton victory was deception**, and the prison convoy provided men and cover to overthrow the Lannister garrison. **Arya does not witness the entire prior negotiations between Hoat and Bolton**, so the precise initial bargain remains unobserved.
15. **DIRECT NEW LORD / IDENTITY:** **Shagwell** exposes Arya as the servant who prepared the broth. **Roose Bolton** asks her age, interests and actual name. She gives the plausible false name **'Nymeria', shortened to 'Nan'**, replacing 'Weasel' and keeping her **Stark identity concealed even from a Northern lord**. Roose installs her as his **cupbearer**, close to decisions and leaders while exposing her to a potentially dangerous commander. She is now in a radically more influential information position but has **no safe proof Bolton is a trusted personal ally**.
16. **DIRECT BRUTAL ENDING:** **Amory Lorch** is paraded naked and begging while Rorge and Shagwell force him into the **caged bear pit**. The chapter ends with Arya pouring wine, not spilling it. **No last breath or confirmed corpse of Lorch is shown in this specific scene**; future records may establish the outcome. An *unchanged* northern banner is **not** evidence that Bolton's personal goals align fully with Robb's.

## Alternative readings and next tests

- **Was Jaqen's oath magical?** His panic over naming himself is real; one interpretation is supernatural obligation, another is professional code or personal fear. The rescue is **real and violent** regardless.
- **Hoat's allegiance:** The apparently anti-Bolton raid is revealed to be the pathway to Bolton victory. The earlier 'prisoners' should be flagged as **probable staged captures**, not confidently assigned their physical condition solely from appearances.
- **Arya's access:** Her role as **Roose Bolton's cupbearer** may yield high-quality overheard intelligence, but has no guarantee of protection. The chapter shows her concealing identity from even a potential northern ally.
- **Jaqen and the future:** The face change and coin are explicit stronger data than speculative membership theories. A Braavosi destination is implied; no named organization is provided here.
- **Next:** subsequent Arya chapters for Roose's plans and Lorch's fate, future Braavos scenes for coin/protocol, and direct tests of face manipulation and assassination methods.
