## Narrative: observed events, decisions and outcomes

1. **VERIFIED:** **Two separately transcribed copies** of Stannis's proclamation have reached the Lannister small council—one through **Maester Frenken at Castle Stokeworth**, the other through **Lord Gyles**. **Pycelle** presents the information; Cersei panics at the incest allegations and wants all copies burned, while Tyrion rightly considers mass recall impossible. This is the first direct **successful reception** shown after Stannis's plan for 117 ravens in `ACOK 10 (Davos)`. Littlefinger assumes other lords were reached; this remains his **INFERENCE** rather than individually verified delivery.
2. **VERIFIED / TYRION'S PRIVATE KNOWLEDGE:** Tyrion privately knows Jaime and Cersei's incest allegation to be true, yet advises dismissing it as an unproved pretext for rebellion. He notices the ending **'Done in the Light of the Lord'**, correctly identifying Stannis's adopted red-priest formula as a vulnerability with adherents of the Seven. **Pycelle** notes similar phrasing among Free Cities' red worshippers. Tyrion understands that Stannis's *claim to kingship* and the conflict with Renly are more consequential politically than the accusation alone.
3. **VERIFIED:** Cersei proposes **cutting out the tongues** of anyone spreading the allegation; Tyrion counters that such punishment confirms fear, not falsehood. **Littlefinger** proposes retaliating with a *deliberately invented rumor* that **Shireen** is the child of **Patchface**, not Stannis. He and Cersei consider Axell Florent as an alternate father before favoring the grotesque fool. The scheme exploits Shireen's old **greyscale scars** and Patchface's actual attachment to her, distorting recognizable facts. Tyrion approves dissemination through **Littlefinger's brothels** and Varys's tavern network, but this is planning, **not proof the lie has spread**.
4. **VERIFIED:** Tyrion grows wary of Littlefinger's ability to fabricate plausible scandals. Varys is absent from council on Tyrion's secret business. Tyrion claims he is making Joffrey a **chain**, a joke that disguises a genuine major military order.
5. **VERIFIED:** In formal audience, Tyrion calls **Ironbelly** and the capital's smiths to replicate enormous interlocked **steel links**, demanding **1,000 further links** and that all metalworkers suspend production of swords, mail and armor until the chain is built. Cersei had ordered arms for new gold cloaks under threat to crush smiths' hands. Tyrion promises no crushed hands, but separately threatens proud master armorer **Salloreon** with wearing chains if he does not comply. Iron is expensive; Tyrion relies on **Littlefinger** for funds and even suggests melting horseshoes. **Purpose is concealed in this chapter**; do not prematurely write that the chain has been deployed or worked.
6. **VERIFIED:** Tyrion has already made several efforts to relieve the city's food shortage: **carpenters building fishing boats instead of catapults**, opening kingswood hunting, and sending gold cloaks to forage south and west. These materially compete with military production and have not eliminated hunger or angry citizens. He travels through streets under guard and notices how difficult identifying spies is by appearance.
7. **VERIFIED:** Tyrion visits **Chataya's brothel** under a sexual pretext, using Chataya's daughter **Alayaya** to guide him to a private room. She knows his true purpose: a wardrobe panel leads to a concealed ladder and underground passage. Tyrion goes down to meet **Varys**, who is disguised as an armed rough-spoken scarred man rather than his usual perfumed courtier.
8. **VARYS'S CLAIMS / VERIFIED TUNNEL:** Varys says the passage was constructed for **an unnamed former King's Hand**, and leads underground to a stable several blocks away. This is shown to be a functioning route, but its original patron is **not identified**. Varys says some of Cersei's informants report to him too, and that Chataya resents Cersei's circle for **Allar Deem's** actions. None of these assurances proves exclusive loyalty. In the stable, Varys outfits Tyrion in a cloak that encourages passersby to mistake him for a child on an ordinary horse; Tyrion intends to visit **Shae** without being followed.
9. **VERIFIED / VARYS'S INFORMATION:** Varys denies himself being the person who alerted Stannis to Jaime/Cersei's children, while refusing assurance that he'd confess if he had. He reports approximately **eight known children of Robert** by women with differing hair colors, all **black-haired**, unlike Cersei's three blond children. This is a strong described hereditary pattern, though **eight** is Varys's approximate count and private knowledge; it is not an independently verified exhaustive registry.
10. **VERIFIED / UNRESOLVED SUSPECT:** Tyrion suspects **Littlefinger** may have leaked the incest claim, but Varys names no source. The chapter ends with reciprocal statements that Tyrion and Varys might be one another's closest friend or worst enemy; this reflects actual insecurity even after a successful clandestine route is established.

## Character choices and rival readings

**Tyrion:** Rejects Cersei's open intimidation while approving calculated slander and coercing smiths; political competence and moral consistency are not equivalent. His selective disclosure about the chain underscores operational secrecy, even from his own sister. **Cersei:** Her rage follows a true accusation, but public proof and scandal management are separate. **Littlefinger:** Demonstrates control of a plausible lie, yet *capacity to invent Shireen rumor* does not prove he leaked the original incest information. **Varys:** Executes a concrete covert escort and gives intelligence, but his double agents and refusal of a verifiable denial leave him structurally unreliable.
