## Main narrative and consequences

1. **VERIFIED:** A soldier's bloated **body in the reeds** has a green cloak, four coppers and a **lock of blond hair tied with red ribbon**. His identity and cause of death are unknown. Soft mud and deep water prevent wagons fording the river; downstream the **bridge is burned**. Yoren maps the **Gods Eye** in mud and abandons the planned western path.
2. **VERIFIED / YOREN'S BELIEF:** His new plan is to find boats at a settlement upriver, travel across the lake to Harrentown and perhaps gain protection at **Harrenhal** through **Lady Whent**. He does not appear to know **Tywin holds Harrenhal** (see `ACOK 7 Catelyn`). This is a *consequential cross-POV information lag*, not evidence Yoren knowingly walked toward the Lannister army.
3. **VERIFIED:** The group passes evacuated cottages and reaches a deserted town by the lake, with sept, towerhouse, inn and docks but **no usable boats**. **Woth**, a former galley oarsman, searches with Arya, Gendry, Hot Pie and Lommy, finding a rotten rowboat, abandoned equipment and a **mother cat with newborn kittens**. Missing inhabitants' destination and motive for flight remain unidentified. Yoren considers Gendry's proposal to build a near-shore raft but chooses fortification overnight.
4. **VERIFIED:** The holdfast has **rough ten-foot unmortared walls**, an iron-barred main gate, north **postern**, wood catwalk and barn with a **concealed tunnel opening at the lake**. Gerren discovers it; Yoren covers its trap with a wagon. He establishes watches and posts **Kurz, Tarber and Cutjack** with a warning horn in the towerhouse. The group feeds the surviving **traumatized toddler** rescued in `ACOK 9`; Gendry remains preoccupied with his polished **bull helm**.
5. **VERIFIED / ARYA'S UNCERTAIN PERCEPTION:** Arya wakes to what she believes was **wolf howling** and warns of danger. Others say she dreamed it; then **Kurz's real horn** warns of approaching armed horsemen. The independent alarm establishes danger but not the first sound's source: external wolf, dream, magic or acute anxiety all remain possible.
6. **VERIFIED:** Soldiers with a **gold lion on red banner** torch the town. **Ser Amory Lorch**, visibly marked by a **manticore shield**, identifies himself as Tywin's bannerman acting for Joffrey. The men deride Yoren's black Night's Watch cloak, pretending it might mark **Beric Dondarrion** (whose black shield bears purple lightning). Yoren claims Watch neutrality and refuses to open. Lorch treats refusal as rebellion, orders assault and states he will kill the boys and old men alike. **Woth** is struck through the throat at the outset.
7. **VERIFIED:** The stone wall is scalable without ladders. Arya cuts an assailant's fingers with **Needle**, uses Syrio's sword lessons, and helps **Gendry and Hot Pie** fight climbers. Yoren kills a spiked-helm knight by tangling the helmet in his black cloak before stabbing him. **Koss** uses bow shots to repel attackers at the gate; **Dobber**, **Qyle** and other defenders fall. Arya sees Koss killed after he tries to yield. The invaders penetrate the walls/postern amid fire and slaughter.
8. **VERIFIED:** Recognizing the defense is lost, **Yoren** orders Arya to get the boys through the barn escape tunnel; he disappears back into battle. **His death is not depicted in this chapter.** **Lommy** suffers a calf wound; **Gerren** is too injured to move. The barn is now burning and the three chained men **Jaqen, Rorge and Biter** remain trapped in a wagon.
9. **VERIFIED:** Arya will not leave the toddler to die, pulling her toward the barn; **Gendry comes back and carries the girl** toward the tunnel. Arya retrieves a woodcutting axe despite the burning holdfast, injures a man who seizes her, then deliberately **returns inside the fiery barn**. She throws the axe to **Rorge**, who smashes open the **wagon's wooden bottom**, creating an escape opportunity for the chained men. The text does not explicitly confirm each man's escape or that all manacles were unlocked.
10. **VERIFIED:** Arya falls into the tunnel and crawls away from fire as smoke rushes in behind her, crying without a clear single object of grief. The chapter ends there: **no roll call** of Yoren, Lommy, Gerren, the chained men, or the other fleeing children has yet been verified.

## Character decisions, competing hypotheses and next checks

**Yoren** invokes actual Night's Watch neutrality; Lorch has military strength and disregards it. Yoren's choice to seek a holdfast can be questioned, but he faced broken bridges, no boats, limited food and a hostile army—there was no obviously safe alternative. **Arya** chooses to save a child and to risk a second entry into an inferno for shackled prisoners; it is not evidence she knows or approves their later conduct. **The wolf-warning theory** can be tested against later verified wolf-POV experiences; a dream after recent wolf encounters is an ordinary rival.
