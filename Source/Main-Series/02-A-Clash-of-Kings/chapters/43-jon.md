# ACOK 43 — Jon: Qhorin's arrival, a cracked horn given to Sam, and the Wall-breaking search

## Primary causal sequence

1. **VERIFIED / HORN SIGNAL:** The Night's Watch hears **one horn blast** in the night at the **Fist of the First Men**, signaling **brothers returning**; brothers tense at the possibility of more blasts but hear none. **Qhorin Halfhand** arrives from the **Shadow Tower**, a long-anticipated reinforcement. This is a **sentry signal**, not the recovered ancient horn from Jon's cache.
2. **VERIFIED / CAMP STRATEGY BEFORE ARRIVAL:** In recent councils **Thoren Smallwood** proposed rushing Mance's force up the Milkwater, thinking numerous civilians would make it easy prey; **Ottyn Wythers** argued retreat to Castle Black; **Mallador Locke** wanted to seek Qhorin along his likely route. **Jarman Buckwell** warned dangerous fighting chiefs such as Rattleshirt, Harma Dogshead and Alfyn Crowkiller are not passive sheep. **Mormont postponed the decision while awaiting Qhorin**. Disagreement is real; the relative strength of Mance's army remains uncertain.
3. **VERIFIED / QHORIN CASUALTIES:** Jon sees half of Qhorin's arriving men riding doubled because some are wounded. Qhorin says his party intercepted **Alfyn Crowkiller's returning scouting band**, slew Alfyn and most followers, but some enemies escaped. He lost **four sworn brothers killed and twelve wounded**, despite inflicting many enemy casualties. Qhorin emphasizes that some escaped wildlings could reach their own host and report the encounter; this is a *possibility*, not a confirmed delivered warning.
4. **VERIFIED / QHORIN'S CONNECTION TO STARKS:** Qhorin is a veteran ranger known by the **missing fingers of his sword hand** and extreme fighting reputation, acknowledges that he knew **Ned Stark and Rickard Stark**, and notices Jon's familial resemblance. He says he is no lord. Jon's **Ghost** has gone hunting rather than attending the arrival. Qhorin's choosing Jon later may reflect more than lineage, but his precise reasoning is not yet disclosed.
5. **VERIFIED / DISSENT LEFT UNREPORTED:** Jon overhears **Chett**, **Lark the Sisterman** and other rangers complain the ranging is doomed. After Chett says the commander may not let them retreat, he hints **the men may not give Mormont a choice**. Jon declines to bring the words to Mormont, rationalizing them as desperate frustration and not wishing to inform on brothers. This is a **specific operational intelligence failure**; it is not proof a coordinated mutiny has already been executed.
6. **VERIFIED / OBSIDIAN CACHE DISTRIBUTION:** Jon has made **an obsidian knife with a wooden handle for himself**, another for **Grenn**, another for **Mormont**. He judges dragonglass sharper than steel but much **more brittle**, while Edd mocks its utility. Mormont accepts the knife but is still seen carrying steel. Jon distributed the other cached points and implements to friends; their **effect against Others is not tested**.
7. **VERIFIED / ANCIENT HORN OWNERSHIP:** The recovered **bronze-banded warhorn** from `ACOK 34` is **cracked and has a chipped rim**. Jon tries to blow it after cleaning it, but **gets no sound**; he therefore gives it to **Sam Tarly** as a memento, alongside a spearhead and **twelve arrowheads**. **Sam's custody is confirmed**; no horn sound and no supernatural outcome occur. A damaged horn is not yet proven to have ever belonged to a legendary figure or to be completely beyond repair.
8. **VERIFIED / CACHE PROVENANCE STILL OPEN:** Mormont says the unrotted **black Watch cloak** in the Fist cache **might have belonged to Benjen or his rangers**, but neither Jon nor Mormont has proof. Why it and obsidian were buried recently is not answered, even after Qhorin's arrival.
9. **QHORIN'S CAPTIVE TESTIMONY:** The single wildling captive who lived long enough for questions spoke of **Rattleshirt, the Weeping Man, numerous chiefs, wargs and mammoths**, and more power than expected. **Ebben** thinks the captive exaggerated to postpone death; Qhorin explicitly refuses to certify the answers as certain. The interrogation was **too harsh and the captive died with information still missing**, again showing torture as a poor intelligence guarantee (compare Gregor's Tickler in `ACOK 26 Arya`).
10. **VERIFIED / WARNING TO ALL KINGS:** **Mormont** says all competing kings—legitimate or not—must be warned of the wildling threat. **Qhorin** says **Winterfell and the Starks** are the Watch's best realistic hope. This does not mean a message has already reached Robb or Winterfell; these are orders and opinions.
11. **VERIFIED / WALL LOGISTICS:** The Watch once garrisoned **seventeen castles** along its hundred-league Wall and now holds **only three**. Mormont contemplates opening **Greyguard and Long Barrow**, while Qhorin suggests the less ruined **Stonedoor**, perhaps **Icemark or Deep Lake**, contingent on men being available. Mormont notes that a host of women, children, animals and warriors could not easily scale a **700-foot wall**; their attempts to make ladders or ramps would take weeks and expose them to harassment. **The Wall's defenses are not self-sufficient** without garrison and watches.
12. **QHORIN'S CORE NEW HYPOTHESIS:** Qhorin argues that **Mance is not primarily seeking a passage over the Wall but a way to break it**. The Frostfangs' remoteness and hunger make them a strange place for mere mustering; Qhorin therefore thinks **Mance is searching for some exceptional power or magical object**. His captive **could not name the object/power**. This is a consequential **strategic inference**, not direct proof Mance has already obtained a specific legendary horn, spell or artifact. **Do not equate Jon's small cracked horn with the target of Qhorin's search without independent evidence.**
13. **VERIFIED / FIFTEEN-MAN SCOUT PLAN:** Qhorin proposes scouting into the mountains with **fifteen men in three five-man parties** to investigate **the Milkwater, Skirling Pass and Giant's Stair**, with **Jarman Buckwell, Thoren Smallwood and Qhorin** commanding the groups. Mormont reluctantly approves, knowing his men may not return and their sacrifice might buy the Wall time to muster, reinforce empty castles, and prepare defenses. This is a **planned disposition**; reconnaissance results are unknown in this chapter.
14. **VERIFIED / JON'S CHOICE:** **Qhorin selects Jon Snow** for his party despite Jon's role as Mormont's **steward** and relative inexperience as ranger, citing the importance of **First Men/old gods and Starks beyond the Wall**. **Mormont explicitly asks Jon's preference**, and Jon immediately chooses to go. Qhorin tells him the scouting party **leaves at noon**, so his participation is authorized but the departure/expedition's outcome are not shown on page.

## Theory ranking and follow-up

- **The horn problem:** The proximity of a **nonfunctional bronze-banded horn buried with dragonglass** and Qhorin's talk of **an unknown power to break the Wall** is a legitimate **hypothesis-generating juxtaposition**. However, the text never explicitly identifies either as the legendary Horn of Winter in this chapter; more evidence is required. **Most concrete finding: Jon put the horn in Sam's hands**.
- **Mance's motive:** Qhorin's inference toward Wall-breaking preparations does not exclude fear of Others, refugee migration or multiple objectives. The captive's unknown power and the Frostfangs' geography strengthen the question, not answer it.
- **Chett:** Hinted insubordination may escalate from frustration to dangerous collusion. Record when, whether and with whom it crosses into an actual plan.
- **Leadership:** Mormont accepts a three-party reconnaissance despite prospect of deaths and relies on a small northern family network; Qhorin risks Jon deliberately and respects the old gods.
