## Established book-specific causal hinge

The chapter pair **ADWD 29 → ADWD 51** gives the northern political contradiction: Wyman privately wants to restore a Stark heir and promises only *conditional* support to Stannis, yet his men are dispatched against Stannis alongside the very Freys Wyman detests. The text does not give their detailed orders. This is a **working question**, not proof of a secret plan.

The chapter pair **ADWD 44/49 → ADWD 62** establishes the information gap: Jon has tried to warn the king, while Arnolf has already embedded hundreds of soldiers and messengers inside Stannis's starving camp. The kingdom's fate cannot be decided by giving Stannis facts he may not possess.

The chapter pair **ADWD 51 → ADWD 62** establishes a real handoff: Theon/Jeyne flee Winterfell, are received by Mors Umber, then reach Stannis's encampment through Tycho's group. Their knowledge may become consequential **if someone asks and believes them**.

## Meereen source checks (additional focused pass)

- **ADWD 2 (Daenerys):** Harpy murders and damage attributed to Drogon reach Daenerys's court, showing the burdens of governing and dragons she cannot fully control.
- **ADWD 43 and 50 (Daenerys):** Her marriage and negotiated peace involve severe personal and political concessions. Outside the city walls, Yunkai is expressly allowed to continue slave trading; her disgust does not erase the concession.
- **ADWD 52 and 71 (Daenerys):** Drogon carries her from the fighting pits; on the Dothraki sea she cannot compel him to return to Meereen. Khal Jhaqo arrives at the end of her last chapter.
- **ADWD 67 (The Kingbreaker):** Barristan's removal of Hizdahr is based on a suspicion concerning poison and the Harpy; the actual culprit is **not proven** in the chapter.
- **ADWD 70 (The Queen's Hand):** Barristan prepares war with Yunkai; hostages and plague victims become instruments of conflict, and the city faces a threatened siege.
- **ADWD 66 (Tyrion):** Tyrion is **still with the Second Sons on the Yunkish side**, considering how to induce Brown Ben Plumm to defect. He has not met Daenerys.

Canonical cross-book development, available choices, and serious counterevidence: [Daenerys/Barristan/Tyrion](../../../research/characters/daenerys-barristan-tyrion.md).

## Source-checked decision cliffs — Aegon, Ronnet, Jaime (additional pass)

- **ADWD 22 and 24:** Tyrion undermines Aegon's assumption of a compliant Daenerys; Aegon actively proposes sailing west, overruling the company's earlier eastward focus. This decision does not establish future success.
- **ADWD 61:** Griffin's Roost is captured before the prince's banner is raised publicly. Connington's scattered force, incomplete horse/elephant muster, and Strickland's objections constrain his proposed move on Storm's End. **Aegon says he will lead it; Connington's reply and result are not narrated.**
- **ADWD 61 ↔ Epilogue:** Connington holds Red Ronnet's relatives while Ronnet publicly supports the royal court and is treated with suspicion. Some of Ronnet's soldiers were reassigned to him by Jaime, supplying a **real cross-plot connection**.
- **ADWD 48:** Jaime sees Brienne at Pennytree; she asks him to travel alone toward the girl she says is Sansa. **The chapter does not show him departing with her.** No Jaime/Stoneheart encounter has yet occurred on-page.

## Dornish information-gap audit (2026-10-15)

- **ADWD 38 (Hotah):** Doran and Arianne participate in a state cover story that falsely attributes the killing of Arys Oakheart to Darkstar. Hotah's AFFC 21 narration directly establishes the contrary.
- **ADWD 38:** Doran sends Obara with Balon toward Darkstar, Nymeria toward the King's Landing court, and Tyene toward the new Faith; **orders are not proof of completion**.
- **ADWD 38:** Doran hears an uncertain report of a fleet with elephants at Lys and speculates that Daenerys could be on it. He has not learned Quentyn's fate on-page.
- **ADWD 61 (Connington):** Connington orders Haldon to prepare a letter to Doran about the alleged survival of Elia's son, but **Doran's receipt of the letter has not been narrated**.

## Arya and Bran: fifth-book source anchors (2026-10-15)

- **ADWD 4, 13 (Bran):** Coldhands guides the party through growing supernatural danger toward Bloodraven's cave. Bran asks whether his legs can be healed and is told no.
- **ADWD 34 (Bran):** Practices raven and weirwood sight; repeatedly, secretly occupies Hodor as Hodor withdraws in fear. He sees Eddard apparently react to a whisper during a past-tree vision, while Bloodraven insists the past is immutable. An ancient human sacrifice is directly observed, with motive unknown.
- **ADWD 45 (The Blind Girl / Arya):** Braavosi cat-sight is narrated as an additional form of skinchanging; Arya obtains word that enslaved wildlings from Hardhome were seized aboard Lyseni ships. No warning to Jon has been shown.
- **ADWD 64 (The Ugly Little Girl / Arya):** The House gives Arya a target who issues maritime loss binders; she plans and carries out the assigned death, receives her original face back, and is instructed to apprentice to Izembaro. The client's identity and the target's moral guilt are unproven.
