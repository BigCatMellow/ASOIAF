## Major sequence

- **VERIFIED:** A brilliant red comet hangs over Dragonstone. A Citadel white raven brings its official seasonal announcement. Cressen treats omens skeptically while privately feeling their force; Shireen hears incompatible interpretations of the comet.
- **VERIFIED:** Cressen remembers Steffon Baratheon's fatal return voyage, the failed search for a bride for Rhaegar, and Patchface washing ashore alive after apparently spending two days at sea. Patchface's transformation is observed retrospectively; the explanation is **UNKNOWN**.
- **VERIFIED:** Davos returns from attempts to secure support for Stannis. He and Cressen understand that Stannis lacks troops. Stannis refuses to concede Renly's claim or ally with those he sees as usurpers.
- **CHARACTER CLAIM:** Selyse attributes a coming military victory and Renly's predicted death to Melisandre's visions. Her belief does not establish the precise means of death or the legitimacy of any prophecy.
- **VERIFIED:** Cressen attempts to poison Melisandre with the strangler by sharing a cup. She knowingly drinks first; Cressen drinks the rest and dies. Her survival is depicted; the exact mechanism is not independently demonstrated in this chapter.

## What the reader knows that actors do not

Cressen acts from long parental attachment and distrust of Melisandre, but his subjective certainty that killing her will save Stannis is not validated. Stannis is hard and defensive yet rebukes the court's ridicule of his aged maester. The chapter resists flattening either into one trait.
