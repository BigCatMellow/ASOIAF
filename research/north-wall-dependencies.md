## Constraints for a future cross-plot scene

1. **Geography:** Stannis/Theon/Asha and Jon/Melisandre/Selyse are in separate locations. Travel through deep snow is difficult and slow. Only a credible messenger, raven, or travel interval may connect them.
2. **Information asymmetry:** Jon knows Alys Karstark warns against Arnolf's betrayal (ADWD 44). **Do not assume Stannis has received or believed all warnings** without tracing the delivery. Stannis's advisors are divided (ADWD 62).
3. **Identity claims:** Jeyne is *not* Arya. Ramsay's demands for his bride and 'Reek' in ADWD 69 may reflect some real knowledge but do not certify other claims.
4. **Church vs. army:** Queen's men and northern followers disagree about sacrifices and gods in ADWD 62. Stannis cannot simply command a homogeneous army.
5. **Adjacent crises:** Hardhome's rescue report (ADWD 58) and Jon's response to Ramsay are contemporaneous pressures for the Watch, not interchangeable goals.
6. **Unseen events:** The actual Battle of Ice and any attack on Winterfell remain outside the five-book narrated resolution.

## THINK challenge: the Night Lamp

**First principles:** Asha sees fishing holes, blizzard, a watchtower beacon, starvation, and conflicting officers. Those facts can support several maneuvers, not just one elegant scheme.

**Counterexample:** An army too weak or disorganized to coordinate the trap could suffer from the same conditions. The presence of ice holes does not by itself establish intentional military preparation.

**Next source test:** Read all Asha Stannis-camp chapters plus nearby Theon escape, and identify who has actual access to the lake terrain, knowledge of enemy route, available beacon controls and reliable command. Discard the Night Lamp branch if those details do not support it.
