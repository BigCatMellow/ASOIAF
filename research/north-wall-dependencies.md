## Three strands, their known order

### Wall / Tycho / Karstark strand

1. **ADWD 44 (Jon):** Selyse and Tycho Nestoris arrive at Castle Black. Alys Karstark separately arrives seeking asylum, names Arnolf Karstark's plan to betray Stannis, and describes the attempted marriage to Cregan.
2. **ADWD 49 (Jon):** Jon presides over Alys's marriage to Sigorn of Thenn and has Cregan detained. His thoughts confirm that **Clydas dispatched a raven warning of Arnolf's treachery to Deepwood Motte**. Jon explicitly *does not know* whether it reached Stannis. Tycho is already on his way toward the king.
3. **ADWD 62 (Asha):** Arnolf has reached Stannis with his retainers and a cage of ravens. Tycho arrives at Stannis's winter camp with a group escorting Theon and Jeyne.
4. **ADWD 69 (Jon):** Jon later receives the Pink Letter, reports its purported news at Castle Black and is stabbed. Neither his knowledge nor the letter establishes Stannis's military fate.

**Crucial limitation:** Stannis left Deepwood Motte before the lake camp. A raven dispatched to Deepwood may have reached the garrison but not the king promptly, or could have been forwarded, delayed, or lost. **No five-book POV directly depicts Stannis receiving this warning.** We cannot promote Alys's knowledge to Stannis's knowledge.

### Winterfell / Bolton sortie / escape strand

1. **ADWD 37 (Prince of Winterfell):** Ramsay marries Jeyne Poole under the false identity of Arya Stark, with Theon forced to validate her supposed identity.
2. **ADWD 41, 46 (Turncloak / Ghost):** Severe blizzard, tension among Bolton, Frey and Manderly contingents, unidentified killings, and Abel's scheme to remove Jeyne; uncertainty about the saboteurs persists.
3. **ADWD 51 (Theon):** Little Walder Frey is found dead; Hosteen Frey accuses Manderly and wounds him in a hall fight. Roose, holding a fresh raven report of Stannis's starving position, **orders Hosteen's force out the main gate and White Harbor's force out the east gate**. Theon, Jeyne and the remaining rescuers attempt escape; Theon jumps off the wall with Jeyne.
4. **ADWD 62 (The Sacrifice):** Tycho and the men accompanying him bring Theon and Jeyne to Stannis's camp. Asha sees Theon. **There is no narrative scene of the Frey–Stannis engagement.**

**Crucial limitation:** Orders to muster at two gates are not proof of when either force reached the lakes or that those factions stayed united.

### Stannis / crofter's village strand

1. **ADWD 26 (The Wayward Bride):** Stannis defeats/captures Asha at Deepwood Motte.
2. **ADWD 62 (The Sacrifice):** Snow immobilizes his force at the crofter's village between two lakes. The soldiers have drilled numerous ice-fishing holes, and the watchtower has an ever-burning beacon. Famished commanders debate marching, staying or retreating. Stannis permits the burning of four convicted cannibals; queen's men want Asha burned next. Arnolf Karstark publicly urges immediate assault, and Theon/Jeyne arrive at chapter end.
3. **ADWD 69 (Jon):** A **letter** claims Stannis's defeat. This is a report, not a scene from the camp.

**Crucial limitation:** The physical world (snowfall, starving horses, ice holes) is established; Stannis's exact intended use of terrain is not.

## Open dependencies before choosing the Battle of Ice result

- **Courier chain:** Who, if anyone, forwarded Clydas's raven from Deepwood to the king; did Tycho learn Alys's warning before leaving?
- **Tactical chain:** Did Stannis direct fishing activity or control the watchtower beacon? Who can see the enemy's approach through the blizzard?
- **Political chain:** Can Manderly's force defect mid-engagement without a promised Stark heir in hand? Is the likely Frey–Manderly collision exploitable or simply chaotic?
- **Captive chain:** Does Stannis accept Theon's testimony, and will Asha protect him at the cost of her remaining leverage?
- **Pink Letter chain:** Who knew Mance's identity, the six spearwives, the escape, and the Wildling/queen's people at Castle Black, and *when*?

## Branching point, not a fixed outcome

**Frey/Manderly forces have been dispatched; Stannis's hungry host has damaged lake ice and a beacon; Theon and Jeyne have arrived as potential witnesses.** 

- A calculated terrain victory is viable ([Night Lamp](theories/night-lamp.md)).
- A compromised trap, confused engagement, or divided-army battle is also viable.
- A false account of what happened could plausibly spread even if Stannis survives, but no author or motive is established.
