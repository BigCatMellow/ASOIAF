## Research question

**How does George R. R. Martin construct meaning, tension, character, emotion and coherence in *A Song of Ice and Fire*?**

The existing research usually asks *what happened, what is true, and what may happen next?* This parallel project asks *why is the story told this way, how does it affect the reader, and what craft choices make those effects possible?*

The goal is not praise by default. Identify successful and unsuccessful techniques, alternatives, interpretive uncertainty and the possible costs of Martin's choices.

## What belongs here

1. **Architecture and pacing:** book-level structure, chapter placement, transitions, convergences, temporal overlap and the distribution of attention.
2. **Point of view:** limited knowledge, unreliable judgments, dramatic irony, emotional alignment and narrator-specific perception.
3. **Character construction:** motives, contradictions, changing self-conceptions, agency and the causal pressures that expose character.
4. **Themes and ethics:** duty, justice, identity, violence, class, leadership, memory, family and power—especially meaningful disagreements between characters.
5. **Prose and symbolism:** diction, recurring images, dreams, songs, physical detail, dialogue and how meaning shifts through repetition.
6. **Worldbuilding as narrative machinery:** religion, hierarchy, resources, distance, institutions and material conditions that create choices.
7. **Emotional design:** anticipation, dread, surprise, grief, relief, empathy and the effects of delayed information.
8. **Creative process and limitations:** compare observable textual methods with separately sourced author statements, without assuming either proves intention.

These are **lenses**, not eight new compulsory processes or eight duplicate chapter indexes. A single study may cross several lenses.

## Acceptance criteria and present state

The **pilot** is complete when four bounded studies address (A) the opening, (B) competing perspectives on the Mycah incident, (C) Jaime/Brienne and identity, and (D) Brienne's *Feast* journey; each cites the novel, explains the mechanism and its reader effect, tests at least one rival reading, and receives an independent challenge before being called reviewed.

The **larger research program** is complete only after representative five-book crosschecks across the eight lenses, an integrated set of supported findings and limitations, locator/link verification, and independent assessment of major structural claims. The program is **not** complete now.

**Current receipt (2026-10-22):** folder and method established; Studies 01 and 02 drafted and checked against selected original chapters. No independent review of the new study yet. No claims about unpublished outcomes.
