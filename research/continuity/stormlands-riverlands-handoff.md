## Purpose

Prevent a future *Winds* chapter from assuming that different characters share the reader's knowledge. The Stormlands invasion and Jaime's Riverlands journey are **already linked through a named family and armed men**, but they have not merged into a single openly coordinated storyline.

Even though these storylines are physically separated, the court's report of invasion does not settle the exact status of Aegon's attack on Storm's End.

## The Red Ronnet family link

- **ADWD 61 (Connington):** Jon Connington captures Griffin's Roost and holds Ser Ronnet Connington's younger kin, including a younger brother, a sister, and a natural son. They may be bargaining counters. Jon has not sent an on-page demand to Ronnet.
- **ADWD Epilogue (Kevan):** Ser Ronnet at King's Landing publicly wishes to fight his uncle; Kevan suspects he might not be reliable because of the family tie. Ronnet arrived with around twenty men, many previously serving Gregor Clegane, whom Jaime assigned to him. Some of those men have committed serious crimes since their transfer.
- **ADWD 48 (Jaime):** Jaime is away in the Riverlands and is *not shown* learning that Jon Connington holds Ronnet's kin. He cannot exploit this fact in his next choice unless he receives news through a credible intermediary.

**Narrative possibility, not prediction:** Ronnet may press to prove his loyalty by attacking his own relatives; a court faction could use him as evidence for or against an invasion report. Connington might threaten innocents in order to influence him. None has yet happened.

## Verified issue and a fix to existing shorthand

The earlier character sheet said Jaime “departs with Brienne.” **That was false.** ADWD 48 ends with her invitation, so the next story beat must include a decision or reaction from Jaime (or a viewpoint observing it) before any travel or captivity can follow.

The “Hound” warning at the end of ADWD 48 is ambiguous because **Lem wore Sandor Clegane's former helm** in AFFC 42. A character invoking that name may be referring to Lem, not Sandor; do not force the Quiet Isle gravedigger into this scene.

## What must be sourced before merging the threads

1. **Communication:** Does Ronnet learn who is being held at Griffin's Roost? Is there a raven or messenger? Has Jaime learned the southern landing yet?
2. **Movement:** Who commands Jaime's Riverlands force if he rides alone? How far away can Stoneheart credibly summon them? The text gives Brienne's claimed *one-day* ride, not a verified destination.
3. **Consent/control:** Do Podrick and Hyle live after the hanging? Is Brienne released independently or under guard? Are Thoros/Lem still together?
4. **Storm's End:** Is Aegon permitted to command? What forces are actually assembled for the attack? The five-book narrative doesn't show the outcome.

**Disposition:** `USE AS CONSTRAINTS, NOT PLOT`. Next: stage adjacent decisions without instantly reconciling the simultaneous strands.
