## Separate, converging lines

1. **Iron Islands — kingsmoot (AFFC 19, Aeron):** Euron shows off a huge Valyrian horn, claims it will bind dragons, and wins support despite rival claims by Asha and Victarion. **The horn's advertised ability is Euron's claim**, not verified use on a dragon.
2. **Iron Islands / Shields (AFFC 29, Victarion):** The Ironborn have struck at Reach shipping and seized shield-island positions. Euron recounts the death of Cragorn after sounding the horn: a maester reportedly found his lungs charred. Euron orders Victarion to sail east to bring Daenerys. **The death is reported, not shown as an autopsy POV**.
3. **Oldtown approaches (AFFC 45, Sam):** Sam arrives after the Arbor raids and sees damaged shores and corpse-filled waters. Hightower officers report building ships to face the Ironborn, while Lord Leyton remains secluded.
4. **Oldtown / Citadel (AFFC Prologue, 45):** Pate's key falls to an alchemist; a possibly different Pate appears when Sam reaches the Citadel. Alleras and Marwyn redirect Sam; a glass candle is visibly burning. No direct scene identifies Euron's agents there.
5. **Victarion's eastward voyage (ADWD 56):** His ambition to acquire Daenerys and dragons has become **different from simply serving Euron**; Moqorro comes aboard and offers an alternative supernatural interpreter. The Ironborn threats to Meereen and Oldtown are connected by royal policy but not synchronously controllable by Euron.

## Three future branches, not selected

**A. Conventional coastal battle:** Oldtown's fate is driven by fleets, limited supplies and competing Reach obligations. Sam must decide whether to stay and learn or help evacuate people.

**B. A concealed-magical threat becomes real:** Only if the next text earns Euron's ritual mechanism, costs and targets. The conspicuous horn and Valyrian artifacts are suggestive, not a completed spell.

**C. Internal Citadel breach worsens a military crisis:** Pate's key and a possible infiltrator could compromise knowledge/security; do **not** assign the agent to Euron without proving communication or motive.
