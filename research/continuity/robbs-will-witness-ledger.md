## Strict distinction

**ASOS 45, Catelyn:** Robb discusses naming a successor, then tells assembled lords to place their seals on a document as witnesses. **This is an on-page order, not a narrated view of the completed seals** or a reproduction of the legal text. The strong inference from Catelyn's preceding exchange is that Jon is Robb's intended beneficiary. The precise wording, number of original copies, exceptions and later physical custodian remain **UNKNOWN**.

In the **same chapter**, Robb sends Maege Mormont and Galbart Glover toward Howland Reed with two ships and intentionally false written military instructions. These messages are **not explicitly said to be the succession instrument**.

Presence ≠ proof of signature, later possession, or future political allegiance.

## Two independent threats to the document's effect

1. **False premise about surviving brothers:** Robb believes Bran/Rickon dead and Sansa bound to the Lannisters (ASOS 35/45). The reader knows this is incomplete: Bran is in Bloodraven's cave (ADWD 34), and Wyman independently wants Rickon (ADWD 29).
2. **Institutional recognition:** Even an authentic witnessed decree cannot force Jon to accept Northern kingship, release Watch vows, or compel houses loyal to a living child or Sansa to submit. Robb's authority to affect Night's Watch vows requires its own legal/political argument.

## Information transfer paths and failure cases

- **Galbart/Maege → Howland:** Military rendezvous orders are described. If a **separate** succession copy exists, it must be discovered rather than silently inferred.
- **Greatjon → Umber supporters:** He is held hostage, constraining otherwise anti-Frey kin (ADWD 41).
- **Edmure → Riverlands:** Escorted prisoner and potential witness, but communication and extraction are unshown.
- **Jason → Seagard:** His position is politically constrained; the appendix is supplemental orientation, not proof of his access to paper.
- **Stoneheart → Brotherhood:** She knows what Catelyn heard, *if* memories and motivations are sufficiently intact. No first-hand will testimony occurs in AFFC.

## THINK challenge and nearby experiments

**Test B:** Manderly presents a living Rickon to Northern houses before any will copy emerges. Would original witnesses change their position in light of what Robb did not know?
