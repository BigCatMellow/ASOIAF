## Causal and partial chronological chains

**Dornish internal line (AFFC):** Doran refuses premature war under family pressure (AFFC 2) → Arianne conspires with Arys and companions to crown Myrcella and forces a confrontation (AFFC 13, 21) → Hotah kills Arys, Myrcella is wounded, and Arianne is confined (AFFC 21) → Doran discloses the Viserys marriage pact and Quentyn's mission and offers Arianne a future task (AFFC 40).

**Dornish outward line (ADWD 38):** Balon Swann arrives with the skull said to belong to Gregor → Doran/Hotah/Arianne rehearse the Darkstar cover story → Doran warns of a reported Trystane-killing plot, dispatches Obara with Balon, Nymeria toward the Red Keep and Tyene toward the new Faith → **Doran speculates**, from a fleet-with-elephants report at Lys, that Daenerys may be sailing west. His ignorance of the actual occupant is an essential information gap.

**Aegon separate line (ADWD 61):** Connington recaptures Griffin's Roost → instructs Haldon to prepare a letter telling Doran that Elia's son has returned → Aegon insists on personally leading the proposed Storm's End attack. **Receipt of the letter has not been shown.** No five-book Arianne POV shows her meeting Aegon or seeing a confirmed message about him.

**Vale line (ASOS/AFFC):** Sansa witnesses Petyr kill Lysa (ASOS 80) → repeats a false cover story and observes the grant securing Nestor Royce (AFFC 10) → notices Lyn Corbray's hostile appearance is staged (AFFC 23) → persuades Sweetrobin to leave the Eyrie and hears Colemon's warning; inadvertently speaks Jon Snow's name to Myranda (AFFC 41) → hears Petyr's conditional Harry–Sansa plan (AFFC 41). **Harry does not marry her. Sweetrobin does not die.**

AFFC/ADWD chapters overlap in calendar time. Relative order in the separate strands follows narrated cause and effect only; exact dates of mail travel, Vale intelligence or the elephant fleet are **UNKNOWN**.

## Nontrivial cross-story constraints

1. **No automatic Martell mobilization:** Doran's secret Targaryen affinity is conditional and complicated by Myrcella, Trystane and his own daughter. Aegon's lineage claim alone cannot instantly mobilize Dorne.
2. **No spontaneous Vale army:** Harry, Sansa, Nestor, Myranda, Royce, and Petyr hold different forms of power; their agendas cannot be combined by a single off-screen agreement.
3. **Identity is information, not possession:** Sansa and Arianne both face inheritance arrangements designed by older male relatives. A claim or marriage proposal becomes real power only if affected people and supporting houses accept it.
4. **Medical and political ethics:** Sweetrobin's well-being cannot be treated solely as a convenience in Sansa's story. Cersei's purported danger to Trystane likewise exposes the limits of playing with dynastic children.
