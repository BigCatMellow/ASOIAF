## Crucial hidden contact: Arya, Nymeria and Catelyn's corpse

**ASOS 65 (Arya):** Arya's wolf dream follows a scent to a drowned, pale woman's body; through the wolf she drags it onto the riverbank, then runs from approaching riders. On waking she tells Sandor she saw her mother in a dream. **ASOS Epilogue** independently shows Catelyn alive in an altered undead state with the Brotherhood. Together they support a **strong inference** that Arya-as-Nymeria recovered the body that the Brotherhood later revived.

**What remains unknown:** Arya knows only her dream of Catelyn's body, not the revival. Stoneheart does not know Arya's present Braavosi identity or that her direwolf was inhabited by Arya. A future reunion must reckon with mutual ignorance, trauma and distance.

## A second hidden connection: Braavos and Hardhome

**ADWD 45 (The Blind Girl):** Arya hears that the ship *Goodheart* carried enslaved wildlings from Hardhome, a northern crisis also occupying Jon at the Wall (ADWD 58, 69). The ship was seized by Braavosi authorities because slave trading was prohibited.

**Narrative significance:** This is an **existing information route** through sailors and international trade. It could bring important news south/west without Bran magically broadcasting it. **Not established:** Arya warned Jon, can identify a messenger to Castle Black, or knows the current state of Hardhome after the sailors departed.

## Powers do not erase distance or consent

- Arya's Nymeria dreams and cat-sight (ASOS 65, AFFC 34, ADWD 45) are directly shown, but **no conversation with Bran** is shown. Animal senses are not instant in-world raven letters.
- Bran can see **weirwood-recorded past** and use known hosts (ADWD 34); there is no reliable proof of free access to every presently living Stark's thoughts and location.
- Bran's use of Hodor is deeply coercive even when it helps save lives (ASOS 56; ADWD 34). A proposed Stark reunion must not bypass what his friends have suffered because of him.
- The Black Gate responds to a Watchman's vows (ASOS 56); whether Bran can return south, and through which route, remains unresolved.

## Succession fork, not a tidy birth-order answer

**Robb's decision (ASOS 45)** was made believing Bran and Rickon dead and Sansa effectively a Lannister-controlled heir. **Wyman (ADWD 29)** separately plans to restore Rickon. **Petyr (AFFC 41)** wants Sansa's northern claim. **Jon (ADWD 69)** is politically/mortally uncertain. The older siblings' actual identity, survival and wishes cannot be compressed into “the Starks reunite and select one ruler.”

## Continuity tests

- Is a claim **narrated**, **witnessed**, **reported** or only a **character's plan**? Track each separately.
- Is there a credible **raven, traveler, trade route or animal perspective** to transfer new facts?
- Does each character know other Starks are alive? Don't give all survivors the reader's shared information.
- Avoid automatic 'pack telepathy' and instantaneous coordination via Bran.
- **AFFC/ADWD overlap:** don't infer identical calendar dates from consecutive book chapters.
