## Experiment 1 — Doran's next instruction to Arianne

**Established entrance:** ADWD 38 closes after Doran sends three Sand Snakes on dangerous tasks, reports an unidentified elephant fleet at Lys, and tells Arianne he has another task for her. Connington elsewhere orders a **letter** about Aegon prepared (ADWD 61). Neither the letter's arrival nor Aegon's identity is known to Doran on-page.

**Character impulses:** Arianne demands to be taken seriously; Doran wants controlled intelligence and a valuable dynastic alliance; both have seen the Myrcella plan fail.

| Next choice (conditional on actual news) | Immediate cost | New consequential question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arianne demands independent verification before departing | Delays response while rival claimants build momentum | Can Doran allow her to evaluate reports without treating her as disloyal? |
| She agrees to investigate a claimant while retaining independent judgment | Makes her vulnerable to court flattery or manipulation | Does she report what she sees honestly if it undermines Doran's favored choice? |
| She rejects another opaque mission until Doran shares what he knows | Forces a trust confrontation at the worst political moment | Will Doran lose more by excluding his heir or by sharing secrets that could leak? |

**Counterexample to any predetermined marriage:** Arianne might discover Aegon is untrustworthy, unappealing, unverified, already promised, or militarily unable to help Dorne. She remains heir in her own right.

## Experiment 2 — Sansa and the consequences of caring for Sweetrobin

**Established entrance:** AFFC 41 ends with Alayne at the Gates and Littlefinger presenting Harry as the future Lord of the Vale *when Robert dies*. Earlier that day Sansa helped Robert descend the Eyrie and heard Colemon object that sweetsleep accumulates.

**Character impulses:** Safety through concealment, love of family, genuine pity and responsibility toward Robert, suspicion of Petyr's manipulations, desire to get home.

| Immediate choice | Immediate cost | New story question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sansa returns to Colemon and demands a clearer medical explanation | May expose conflict with Petyr's handling of Robert or delay public duties | What does she choose if safe care interferes with the Harry succession scheme? |
| She tests Myranda with guarded conversation rather than inventing a complete new identity | Gives possible access to Nestor's household knowledge | Can she afford another confidante; what does Myranda actually know? |
| She plays along with the Harry idea while withholding private assent | Increases Petyr's power over her public behavior and marriage prospects | Where is the point at which temporary pretense becomes complicity? |

**Counterexample to instant political competence:** Identifying Lyn as an actor does not give Sansa his loyalty or legal authority over Vale accounts, forces or marriage policy.

## Shared question

Both heirs live inside carefully controlled public fictions: **Arianne's false account of Arys's death and Alayne's false parentage / Lysa cover story**. Their first meaningful independence may not involve taking a crown, but deciding which truth must be told, to whom, and what harm follows.
