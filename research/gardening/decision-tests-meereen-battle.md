# Gardening test — Meereen’s first battle decisions

- Record role: `THINK / PLAN EXPERIMENT`.
- Status: `REVERSIBLE / NO STORY BRANCH ADOPTED`.
- Evidence: [Meereen endpoint handoff](../reconciliation/meereen-endpoint-handoff.md) and [battle-start order](../reconciliation/meereen-battle-start-order.md).

## Starting condition

Barristan commands a city without Daenerys, after arresting Hizdahr on unresolved suspicion. Yunkish forces and hostages constrain him; plague affects everyone. Tyrion, Jorah, and Penny are inside the Second Sons, where a possible switch remains unperformed. The Windblown retain separate interests. Victarion is approaching by sea, while Daenerys is absent in the grass sea with Drogon.

## Experiment A — Barristan chooses a first objective

| Decision | Immediate advantage | Cost / next question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sortie to break siege pressure | Uses initiative and may protect civilians from prolonged blockade | Who holds pyramid, hostages, and Hizdahr; can a divided force return in order? |
| Prioritize hostage negotiation | May save Daario, Jhogo, Hero, and others | Can Yunkai’s demands be trusted after Groleo; does delay worsen disease and morale? |
| Consolidate city defense | Limits exposure of an improvised command | Gives opponents time and can look like paralysis after the coup. |

**Counterexample:** Barristan’s honor does not require the same tactical answer in every branch. His refusal to kill children constrains methods, not necessarily whether he fights.

## Experiment B — Tyrion makes a company-level choice, not a private wish

| Decision | Immediate advantage | Required follow-through |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Press Brown Ben to defect before contact | Exploits Yunkish weakness and Tyrion’s promissory leverage | A company order, signal, risk to Penny, and a credible route to friendly lines. |
| Wait for battle confusion | Preserves cover and lets rivals reveal strength | Delay can trap the Second Sons under Yunkish command or make them look treacherous to both sides. |
| Seek a message to Barristan | Could turn an opportunistic switch into negotiated aid | Requires a messenger who survives/can be trusted and exposes Tyrion’s identity. |

**Counterexample:** Tyrion’s intelligence and signed notes do not make him commander of the company or Dany’s adviser.

## Experiment C — Victarion meets facts, not prophecies

| First observable event | Choice | Consequence to test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| He sights combatant sails | Attack, shadow, or seek a captive for information | Which flag/force does he identify, and can his fleet distinguish enemy from a future ally? |
| A horn proposal arises | Delegate a blower, refuse, or delay | Who claims mastership; what evidence supports Moqorro’s reading; what happens if it fails? |
| He learns Dany is absent | Continue toward Meereen, search for Dany, or exploit the battle | His marriage fantasy conflicts with his logistical position and incomplete information. |

**Counterexample:** A prior fatal hornblower and several accurate Moqorro predictions do not prove Dragonbinder’s target, master, or result.

## Stop rules

1. Dany cannot judge Barristan’s coup until a plausible communication/reunion occurs.
2. A dragon is not a factional weapon until a specific observed behavior establishes one.
3. A company’s history of turncloaking is not a narrated switch in this battle.
4. Preserve hostages, disease, civilians, and wounded people as causal constraints—not disposable spectacle.
5. Any battle outcome must include a first surviving messenger and a partial, fallible report.

The experiment tests choices under uncertainty; it does not select a victor, dragon rider, or political settlement. Independent challenge remains open.
