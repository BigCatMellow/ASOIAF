## Exact opening constraint

**VERIFIED:** Daenerys is separated from Meereen, hungry, feverish/physically weakened, barefoot and minimally equipped. Drogon repeatedly returns to his own grassland lair and does not reliably follow her attempted directions. She chooses to follow water toward the Skahazadhan, is found by a Jhaqo scout, then uses Drogon's fear-inducing presence to approach the scout's khalasar. The chapter closes when roughly fifty mounted Dothraki emerge; no conversation, capture, allegiance, or settlement is depicted (ADWD 71).

This makes her next situation a contact problem, not a completed return, a completed Dothraki restoration, or proof that she has abandoned Meereen.

## Tests for common premature readings

1. **“Drogon solves logistics.”** Rejected by his repeated independent movements and Daenerys's inability to turn him reliably. A later flight may be possible, but must show how she gains enough cooperation/control for that particular flight.
2. **“Jhaqo's arrival means she commands his khalasar.”** The chapter shows riders, fear, and her former status; it does not show their response to her claim. Fear can enable flight, capture, bargaining, or violence.
3. **“Her internal fire-and-blood resolution means a specified atrocity.”** The passage shows revulsion at compromises and a renewed self-conception during sickness and isolation. It does not name a target, policy, army, or result.
4. **“Meereen is now irrelevant.”** She repeatedly thinks of its people, Daario, Hizdahr, and her responsibilities. Conversely, those memories do not make a rapid rescue or return physically available.
5. **“A show endpoint supplies the missing bridge.”** It does not. Any analogous later event needs a book-native recipient of news, coercive or consensual Dothraki politics, a dragon mechanism, and consequences for the people already under Dany's rule.

## Discriminating later observations

- Jhaqo's first demand and the riders' reaction to Drogon would distinguish coercive custody, tactical deference, or a fragile alliance.
- Whether Daenerys gains food, medical care, clothing, a horse, or interpreters determines which routes are physically available before strategy does.
- The first credible carrier from Meereen, if any, determines whether she returns to a siege, a changed government, hostage deaths, or an incomplete report rather than to reader-known facts.
- Her response to a real report of Barristan's removal of Hizdahr would test the difference between anger at unwanted guardianship and judgment based on evidence.

## Provisional gardening result

The strongest book-grounded forecast is modest: Daenerys must first negotiate survival, status, and mobility in a Dothraki encounter while Meereen's actors make independent, consequential choices. The order in which she gains agency, transport, and credible news remains an open branch point. Independent challenge remains open.
