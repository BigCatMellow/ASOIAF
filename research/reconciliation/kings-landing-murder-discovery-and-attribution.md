# King’s Landing — Kevan/Pycelle murder discovery and attribution

- Record role: `RESEARCH / RECONCILIATION`.
- Status: `WORKING — READER SEES KILLERS; COURT RECEIPT UNPUBLISHED`.
- Primary text: ADWD Epilogue, “Kevan”; related [post-Kevan decision gates](kings-landing-post-kevan-decision-gates.md).

## What exists at the endpoint

**VERIFIED FOR THE READER:** A boy relays a purported summons from Pycelle; a serving girl admits Kevan to Pycelle’s chambers. Pycelle is already dead from a head wound. Varys shoots Kevan with a crossbow; child agents then close around the mortally wounded Kevan with daggers. Varys explicitly tells Kevan he intends suspicion to divide Cersei, Tyrell, and perhaps Dorne while Aegon advances.

**NOT SHOWN:** Any body discovery, the first guard entering, preservation or disturbance of the room, an autopsy, a public accusation, a council meeting after the deaths, or the exact condition in which either body is found. Varys’s explanation is reader/Kevan-only and does not become a court fact merely because it is true.

## Evidence available versus explanation withheld

| Possible observable item | What a later discoverer might infer | What it cannot by itself prove |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pycelle’s body and fatal head wound | He was murdered inside his chambers; a violent intruder or insider gained access. | Varys's identity, motive, or a Tyrell/Cersei/Dornish culprit. |
| Kevan’s crossbow wound | A weapon associated in memory with Tywin’s death has been used against Kevan. | Tyrion's presence, Cersei's responsibility, or a deliberate imitation by any one faction. |
| Cold/dim chamber, open window, white raven | Timing and disrupted routine may be noticed; the white raven independently marks winter. | A route of entry/exit, a magical cause, or a perpetrator. |
| Serving child/novice access | A child or servant could have delivered Kevan into danger; household access is compromised. | That any particular child can still be found, questioned, or linked to Varys. |
| Absent guards | Kevan did not post the requested guard in Pycelle's chambers before arriving. | That the guard command itself conspired, or that any named faction controlled the room. |
| Existing court conflicts | Cersei, Tyrells, Faith, and Dornish interests offer plausible targets of suspicion. | Actual guilt; motive is not forensic proof. |

## First-response decisions that cannot be skipped

1. **Discovery:** A named person or routine must find the room and decide whom to summon. The more time passes, the more scene control and rumor diverge.
2. **Security:** Guards must decide whether the threat is still in the Red Keep, protect Tommen/Cersei/Margaery, and control servants, rookery access, and council movement.
3. **Attribution:** Each interested party weighs physical evidence against pre-existing fear. An accusation can be politically useful while factually wrong.
4. **Authority:** Kevan's death removes the person who had constrained Cersei, balanced the council, and planned the trials. His vacancy is a governance problem before it is a succession answer.
5. **Communication:** Only after these choices can any court response be compared with fresh, still uncertain reports from the Stormlands.

## Consequence for trajectory work

The first capital crisis is not “Aegon versus a single Lannister regime.” It is an evidentiary crisis in which the people responsible for protecting Tommen can make contradictory defensive moves from incomplete facts. A later Aegon report may be discounted, exaggerated, or weaponized according to the murder framing already chosen. The adaptation’s later Sept sequence cannot fill any of these missing steps.

Independent challenge remains open.
