# Davos — Rickon retrieval and custody gates

- Record role: `RESEARCH / RECONCILIATION`.
- Source scope: ACOK 56 and 69; ADWD 19 and 29; no *The Winds of Winter* sample material used.
- Status: `WORKING — LEAD RECEIVED, RETRIEVAL UNNARRATED`.

## Narrow question

This audit separates (1) the verified prior separation of Osha, Rickon, and Shaggydog, (2) Wex Pyke's later limited report, (3) Wyman Manderly's conditional political bargain with Davos, and (4) the unshown work required to find, identify, transport, and protect a child. It does not treat a Skagos lead as a completed recovery or a recovered child as a consenting political instrument.

## Evidence chain before the ADWD lead

1. **VERIFIED:** The heads Theon publicly passed off as Bran and Rickon were the miller's sons, not the Stark boys (ACOK 56). This disproves the staged identifications but does not itself locate either heir.
2. **VERIFIED:** Bran's ACOK 69 POV identifies the six human fugitives after Winterfell's sack as Bran, Rickon, Jojen, Meera, Hodor, and Osha; Summer and Shaggydog range separately as wolves.
3. **VERIFIED:** In that same chapter, Luwin urges the heirs to separate. Osha chooses the East Gate and says she may follow the kingsroad for a time; Rickon follows her and Shaggydog follows Rickon.
4. **UNKNOWN:** ACOK 69 does not name the final destination of Osha's group. Its route language is an immediate plan, not an arrival scene.

## The conditional bargain is not an alliance already delivered

**VERIFIED:** Wyman says he can deliver Stannis the allegiance of lands east of the White Knife *if* Davos meets his price. He immediately specifies that the price is Ned Stark's son and direwolf, and says he will take Stannis as king if Davos smuggles back his liege lord (ADWD 29).

**VERIFIED:** Wyman's motive is political as well as personal: he says a living son and direwolf would counter Bolton possession of the purported Stark daughter, with the wolf serving as proof against a denial by the Dreadfort.

**INFERRED:** The requested wolf functions as an authentication tool in Wyman's proposed public claim, not as conclusive proof by itself. It would still require witnesses who recognize the child, an account of custody, and political audiences willing to credit the evidence.

**UNKNOWN:** Whether Wyman's pledge would be honored if only Rickon, only Shaggydog, neither, or a contested claimant returned; how Stannis would respond; and whether White Harbor's forces know the precise private terms.

## Competing interpretations of the mission

### A. Wyman's offer is a sincere, practicable Stark-restoration strategy

**Support:** He risks Davos's life to expose a living Stark possibility, has already concealed a false execution, and states the condition plainly after Wylis's return removes the immediate hostage constraint.

**Counterweight:** His capacity claims, secret warships, and projected allegiance are his own statements. The mission remains dangerous and uncompleted, and a practical strategy can still fail or encounter new political conflicts.

**Future test:** A completed retrieval plus an actual Manderly order, message, or public alignment would distinguish a stated plan from an unrealized aspiration.

### B. The lead is strong but not precise enough to guarantee a recovery

**Support:** Wex's account independently fits the ACOK separation and supplies a geographic pointer that Davos understands as ominous.

**Counterweight:** Wex observed the party long before ADWD and communicates through a narrow system; the text does not narrate the exact destination, a duration of stay, or a current messenger from Osha.

**Future test:** A direct encounter, local testimony with a traceable chain, or a current sign uniquely tied to the party would improve location confidence.

### C. Rickon's recovery would settle the northern succession

**Support:** Wyman explicitly frames the boy and wolf as a counter to the Bolton marriage claim.

**Counterweight:** Rickon is a young traumatized child, not a demonstrated governing agent. Stannis's plans, surviving Stark claims, Wyman's terms, White Harbor's loyalties, and the question of false Arya remain separate conflicts.

**Future test:** Identify who first has custody, who recognizes the child, what claim is asserted, and which armed/political actors accept it.

## Prohibited shortcuts

1. Do not say Rickon is already on Skagos, has been found, or is traveling with Davos; the five published novels do not narrate those events.
2. Do not turn Wex's report into a precise live map, a full spoken testimony, or an infallible account.
3. Do not call Wyman a sworn Stannis partisan before the stated price is met and the allegiance is actually communicated or performed.
4. Do not treat Osha as absent from Rickon's survival story, or Rickon as an object whose transfer needs no safety, assent, or guardianship consideration.
5. Do not let a successful retrieval instantly solve the Bolton marriage fraud, Stannis's Winterfell policy, or all Stark succession questions.
