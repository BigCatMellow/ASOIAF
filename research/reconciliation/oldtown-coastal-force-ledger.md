## Safe operational inferences

- Oldtown is threatened, not already fallen or surrounded beyond access: merchant and patrol ships still move, but under danger and inspection.
- The Ironborn can raid and stage forward, but their own losses, dispersed possessions, and longer supply problem remain material constraints.
- Harbor defenses make a simple “sail in and take city” assumption unjustified; they do not prove the city is secure.
- Any battle plan must state which reports are seen, which are captain testimony, and how either force learns changes elsewhere.

3. Has a reported Hightower or sellsail preparation become completed fact?
4. Is the threat a raid, reconnaissance, blockade attempt, harbor breach, or land incursion?
