## Narrow question

This record tests what must occur before Tyrion Lannister, inside Brown Ben Plumm's Second Sons, can become an actor in Ser Barristan Selmy's Meereen command. It does **not** select a battle result, assume Daenerys meets Tyrion, or treat a potential company switch as completed.

## Verified sequence

1. **VERIFIED:** Brown Ben previously tried to acquire Tyrion and Penny, then played Tyrion at cyvasse (ADWD 47). That establishes recognition and curiosity, not loyalty.
2. **VERIFIED:** During Yezzan's illness, Tyrion deliberately steers Penny and Jorah to the Second Sons rather than back to their enslaver (ADWD 57). Guards initially treat all three as runaways and take them to Plumm; entry is a custody transfer, not a rescue by allies.
3. **VERIFIED:** Plumm hears Tyrion's self-interested case, including the risks of disease, Cersei's distant bounty, Jorah's fighting value, and Tyrion's claimed Westerosi resources (ADWD 57). The chapter ends before an acceptance is narrated.
4. **VERIFIED:** By ADWD 66 Tyrion has signed the company book and personal promissory notes, including especially large notes to Plumm, Kasporio, and Inkpots. Plumm directs that Tyrion and Penny be hidden from slave-catchers, while Tyrion is assigned company bookkeeping.
5. **VERIFIED:** Tyrion tells Jorah that the Second Sons are on the losing side and says their cloaks need turning; the statement is Tyrion's intention, not a witnessed Plumm order (ADWD 66).
6. **VERIFIED:** Barristan tells Meereen's commanders that, if his forces can break the slavers, their sellswords will abandon them; he prepares a beacon signal for the city sortie (ADWD 70). This is a tactical expectation, not evidence that he has negotiated with the Second Sons.

## Competing readings of the Second Sons' incentives

### Reading A — a rapid opportunistic turn

**Support:** Tyrion sees plague, divided Yunkish interests, and dragon risk; Barristan independently expects sellsword desertion under pressure. Tyrion's notes give Plumm a prospective Westerosi claim and Tyrion brings Jorah's sword to the company.

**Ordinary limitation:** Prospective notes are only valuable if Tyrion regains assets and can honor them. A company captain can rationally wait for clearer battlefield information rather than gamble on a city whose queen is absent and whose command has just displaced its king.

**Discriminator:** A shown Plumm order, changed banner, battlefield refusal, or message to Meereen would distinguish intention from completed turncloaking.

### Reading B — Plumm preserves leverage while fighting where paid

**Support:** Tyrion reads Plumm as wary and reactive; Plumm confines the escapees so the company is not exposed to slave-catchers, which protects the company without declaring a new side.

**Counterpressure:** The host's plague, hostile leadership, and the prospect of dragons could make continued Yunkish service unsafe or unrewarding.

**Discriminator:** Continued execution of Yunkish orders after a credible opening to change sides would support this reading; a prearranged nonaggression or city contact would weaken it.

### Reading C — fragmented rather than unitary behavior

**Support:** The company visibly contains Plumm, Kasporio, Inkpots, serjeants, ordinary fighters, and camp followers with different risks. Tyrion's private promises are concentrated in the officer layer, not distributed to all five hundred-plus members.

**Counterpressure:** A free company can still follow a captain in a crisis, and the text has not depicted a mutiny.

**Discriminator:** Any later scene must identify who gives the order and whether the ranks comply; a single officer's bargain cannot automatically speak for every Second Son.

## Information asymmetry that must survive continuation

- **VERIFIED:** Tyrion saw Barristan at Daznak's Pit and deliberately did not reveal himself because he expected hostility tied to the Lannisters and Tywin's death (ADWD 57). That is Tyrion's fear and calculation, not Barristan's declared view.
- **VERIFIED:** Barristan's ADWD 70 council does not name Tyrion or Jorah, and it treats hired companies as general strategic variables.
- **INFERRED:** The first contact is more likely to be operational than personal: a company signal, scout, prisoner, or envoy can precede any direct Tyrion–Barristan conversation. This follows from their separated positions and the absence of a current communication channel.
- **UNKNOWN:** Whether Barristan recognizes Tyrion at once, accepts a Second Sons approach, learns Jorah's identity, or grants either man safe conduct.
- **UNKNOWN:** Whether Penny's apparent exhaustion is illness, grief, both, or neither; the uncertainty is a material restraint on any movement plan.

## Prohibited shortcuts

1. Do not make Tyrion Barristan's adviser merely because both oppose the Yunkish siege.
2. Do not equate Tyrion's signature and promissory notes with Plumm's completed defection.
3. Do not grant Barristan knowledge of Tyrion's Lannister identity, Plumm's notes, Jorah's location, or the company's internal discussions before an actual carrier or encounter supplies it.
4. Do not make Penny a willing soldier, erase her illness risk, or use her as proof that Tyrion controls the company.
5. Do not convert Barristan's prediction about sellswords into proof that the Second Sons, Windblown, or any other specific company will act together.
