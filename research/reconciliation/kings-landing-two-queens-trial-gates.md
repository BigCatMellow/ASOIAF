## Different legal positions at the endpoint

| Person | What is verified | What is not resolved |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cersei | She confesses specified sexual offenses, denies directing the false accusation against Margaery, completes a public walk, and is released to the Red Keep pending trial; Qyburn presents Ser Robert Strong as her Kingsguard champion. | Her legal guilt on all charges, trial date/procedure, champion eligibility/acceptance, political control of Tommen, and what follows Kevan’s murder. |
| Margaery | She is accused and held by the Faith; her cousins are also entangled. Her public support and Tyrell interest are visible. | The truth of adultery/treason allegations, treatment of coerced witnesses, trial form/timing, and whether political settlement changes custody. |
| Faith | It holds prisoners, conducts inquiries, fields armed orders, and asserts jurisdiction no longer controlled by Cersei. | Its final verdicts, internal unanimity, response to the murders, and willingness to accept any proposed champion/process. |
| Tyrell/Lannister court | Kevan tried to manage both cases and a council balance; Mace has direct family interest. | Who takes authority after discovery, whether either family credits the other, and how a southern invasion report changes priorities. |

## Evidence categories that must remain separate

| Evidence / claim | What it supports | Why it is insufficient alone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Blue Bard and Osney statements | Cersei actively manufactured an accusation against Margaery; Osney later accuses Cersei. | Both testimony chains involve pressure/torture and shifting incentives; neither makes every alleged act a verified fact. |
| Pycelle’s moon-tea account | Margaery received a drug that can have multiple explanations. | It does not identify a father, consent, timing, or treason. |
| Faith examinations/interrogations | The Faith has invasive custody and witnesses. | Bodily examinations and coerced accounts do not establish adultery or a fair process. |
| Cersei’s walk | A negotiated release after confession/atonement. | It is not acquittal, repentance, restoration as regent, or a completed trial. |
| Robert Strong’s appearance | Cersei has a large, silent Kingsguard whom Qyburn says is vowed to silence. | His identity, legal acceptance, capability under a particular procedure, and outcome are unshown. |

## Procedure and political sequence that a continuation must supply

1. The murders of Kevan/Pycelle are discovered and a provisional authority secures Tommen and the court.
2. That authority decides whether to prioritize trials, investigate the murders, negotiate with the Faith/Tyrells, or react to military reports.
3. The Faith and affected parties establish or contest a specific procedure, witnesses, custody terms, and champions.
4. Any result produces a new legitimacy problem: acquittal does not erase torture or factional distrust; conviction requires enforcement against a powerful family and public reaction.

## Competing paths that remain book-compatible

| Path | Why it is possible | What would make it invalid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cersei seeks trial by combat with Robert Strong | Her hope and Qyburn’s presentation make the attempt plausible. | Assuming the Faith has approved the procedure/champion or that combat decides every charge. |
| Mace uses military/political pressure for Margaery | His daughter’s custody and Tyrell power give him motive. | Treating pressure as a narrated rescue, acquittal, or Faith collapse. |
| Faith retains leverage through delay or conditions | It has custody, armed authority, and a jurisdiction Cersei helped restore. | Treating it as a monolith whose every member shares one secret plan. |
| Murder crisis alters the trial timetable | Kevan’s stabilizing role is removed before any result is narrated. | Skipping discovery/attribution and assuming Varys’s reader-only explanation guides court behavior. |

## Adaptation boundary

No later television trial, Sept catastrophe, Cersei regime, or city destruction can solve these unresolved book procedures. A comparable book event would first need an actor, decision, security failure, public response, and direct causal route through the live Faith/Tyrell/Lannister conflicts.

Independent challenge remains open.
