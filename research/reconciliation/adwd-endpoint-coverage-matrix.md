# ADWD endpoint handoff coverage matrix

- Record role: `RESEARCH / RECONCILIATION COVERAGE`.
- Source scope: the five published novels, with final state anchored in *A Dance with Dragons* chapters 49–72 and its Epilogue where applicable.
- Status: `WORKING — MAJOR ENDPOINT HANDOFFS MAPPED; INDEPENDENT SOURCE CHALLENGE REMAINS OPEN`.
- Use: a retrieval map, not a plot forecast. Each linked audit owns a particular uncertainty; a checked row does not make its open gate resolved.

## What this matrix tests

The final portions of ADWD cut between theaters rather than completing them. This matrix checks that each consequential closing POV has (a) a source-grounded end position, (b) an information/custody/command boundary, and (c) an identified research owner. It prevents a continuation from treating an earlier or later POV’s information as shared merely because it appears nearby in book order.

## Residual review queue

The table finds no unowned **major** end-of-ADWD handoff among the final POV positions. The remaining work is not to invent another endpoint, but to:

1. independently challenge the source classifications and alternative explanations in the linked owner records;
2. reconcile exact travel and message timing only where a proposed branch needs it;
3. connect a local gate to another theater only after a carrier, recipient, and credible receipt are specified; and
4. move to reversible gardening only after these tests, without importing adaptation outcomes as missing facts.

Independent challenge remains open. “Covered” means a source-grounded uncertainty has a retrieval owner; it does not mean the narrative outcome is known or adopted.
