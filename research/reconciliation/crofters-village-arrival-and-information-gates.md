# Crofter's village — arrival, custody, and information gates

- Record role: `RESEARCH / RECONCILIATION`.
- Source scope: ADWD 44 and 49 (*Jon*), 51 (*Theon*), and 62 (*The Sacrifice*); no sample chapters or adaptation material used.
- Status: `WORKING — NEW WITNESSES HAVE ARRIVED; THEIR REPORTS AND COMMAND CONSEQUENCES ARE UNNARRATED`.

## Narrow purpose

This ledger reconstructs the last demonstrated chain from Winterfell and Castle Black to Stannis's snowbound camp. It distinguishes a person physically delivered to the camp from information Stannis has actually heard, credited, and acted upon. It also keeps an arrival by Tycho Nestoris separate from a completed Iron Bank arrangement.

## Chronology without invented simultaneity

1. **CHARACTER CLAIM:** Alys reports the Karstark–Bolton arrangement to Jon. Jon has reason to regard the warning seriously, but the relevant compact is not shown from Arnolf's or Roose's POV.
2. **VERIFIED:** Clydas dispatches a raven to Deepwood Motte warning Stannis of Arnolf’s treachery (ADWD 49). Jon does not know whether it reaches the king in time; delivery, onward routing from Deepwood if required, and receipt remain unshown amid war and weather.
3. **VERIFIED:** Theon and Jeyne's flight is a violent, improvised extraction. Jeyne is terrified, initially denies having brothers, says the escape may be Ramsay's test, and asks Theon not to leave her (ADWD 51). Those facts matter before treating her as an uncomplicated political witness.
4. **VERIFIED:** At the crofter's village, Stannis's force has remained roughly three days from Winterfell for nineteen days amid hunger, exhausted horses, depleted fishing, and factional hostility (ADWD 62). A speaker's proposed march date is therefore a report or aspiration, not an observed operational order.
5. **VERIFIED:** Asha witnesses the late arrival of Tycho, six surviving Ironborn riders, Theon, and Jeyne. Tycho says Mors Umber handed the two fugitives to him after the party found Mors beneath Winterfell.
6. **UNKNOWN:** The precise route and timing between Winterfell, Mors's position, and Stannis's encampment; whether any Bolton party observed it; and which people other than the arrival party recognize Jeyne's name or status.

## Immediate decision gates

1. **Secure-custody gate:** Stannis must protect or confine Theon and Jeyne, allocate guards and medical attention, and prevent hostile factions from treating either as disposable before useful testimony can be obtained.
2. **Identity gate:** A named person must ask and record whether the girl is Jeyne Poole rather than Arya Stark. Theon's recognition is strong direct evidence, but public acceptance and Jeyne's own account are separate acts.
3. **Debrief/corroboration gate:** The camp must decide what to ask, how to weigh trauma and self-interest, and which claims can be tested against Tycho, Mors, seized correspondence, local scouts, or later witnesses.
4. **Karstark-security gate:** Stannis must decide whether the timing, troop dispositions, ravens, and supplies justify monitoring, disarming, separating, or trusting Arnolf's contingent. A reader cannot replace that decision with an assumed coup or counter-coup.
5. **Operational gate:** Hunger, remaining horses, weather, and divided officers constrain any march, retreat, siege, or battle. The proposed tactics voiced in Asha's chapter are argument, not a settled plan.
6. **Message/receipt gate:** Findings affect Jon, the Boltons, White Harbor, or Braavos only after a feasible messenger, recipient, and surviving transmission are identified.

## Rival readings that remain open

### Arnolf's arrival is already a confirmed betrayal in motion

**Support:** Alys gives a specific accusation; Arnolf joins Stannis with ravens, a substantial force, and only self-sufficient provisions; his public urgings to march could serve an outside design.

**Counterweight:** The compact itself is reported, not directly witnessed, and the published ending supplies no intercepted order, attack, or admission. Arriving with usable military resources also has an ordinary military explanation.

**Future test:** A source-authenticated message, a witnessed act coordinated with Bolton forces, or a command decision responding to corroborated evidence.

### The fugitives instantly resolve Stannis's northern legitimacy problem

**Support:** Exposing the Bolton bride as Jeyne could weaken a marriage-based claim and Theon knows the imposture.

**Counterweight:** A political audience must first identify, protect, hear, believe, and decide how to use two traumatized survivors; the real Arya remains elsewhere and the text has not staged any disclosure.

**Future test:** An on-page interview followed by a named recipient and a specific public or private use of the evidence.

### Tycho's arrival means the Iron Bank has chosen Stannis

**Support:** Tycho has travelled at risk to propose terms, and Jon has separately contracted with him for Watch needs.

**Counterweight:** The banker says his business with Stannis is too delicate for letters; negotiation, credit, collateral, and later performance remain unshown. Jon's Watch agreement is not automatically Stannis's royal financing.

**Future test:** A depicted audience, signed terms, disbursement, or an authenticated Bank instruction.

## Continuity prohibitions

- Do not give Stannis Jon's Arnolf warning, the Pink Letter, or an assumed Night Lamp plan without a delivery and receipt event.
- Do not make Jeyne's identity common camp knowledge or a freely usable claim before she is safely heard and a recipient chooses to act.
- Do not equate Theon's arrival with instant trust, full recollection, absolution, or a settled custody outcome.
- Do not treat Tycho's presence as an already-completed loan or as proof that Braavos knows the camp's later choices.
- Do not convert hunger, fishing holes, or a soldier's rumor into a completed battle design.
