# High-impact theory discrimination matrix

- Record role: `RESEARCH / THEORY RECONCILIATION`.
- Scope: theories capable of changing immediate continuation decisions after ADWD.
- Rule: popularity and thematic fit do not upgrade a theory to fact.

| Question | Verified anchor | Serious alternative / counterevidence | What later observation would discriminate | Planning disposition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Who wrote the Pink Letter? | Jon receives a pink-sealed letter in Ramsay’s name and changes course (ADWD 69). | Ramsay’s style/knowledge supports him, but signature/wax do not prove authorship; capture or third-party knowledge can explain Mance details. | A reliable witness, authenticated writing/route, or a source-side admission corroborated by independent facts. | Keep authorship open; use Jon’s reaction as the certain consequence. |
| Is Aegon a Blackfyre? | Connington believes him Rhaegar’s son; Varys promotes him; Golden Company has Blackfyre history. | No birth record/confession; company motives include land and homecoming; prophecy labels are ambiguous. | Independent childhood/pedigree evidence, informed confession, or evidence contradicting the substitute-child account. | Do not let biology dictate alliances before characters assess a claim. |
| Does Dragonbinder bind dragons for Victarion/Euron? | Horn exists; a prior blower dies; Moqorro gives a blood-master reading (AFFC 19; ADWD 63). | Euron’s provenance and Moqorro’s translation are interested claims; no dragon obeys on-page. | A witnessed sounding, identified master, and observable dragon behavior with alternatives excluded. | Treat as a lethal uncertain object, not a command button. |
| Is Robert Strong Gregor Clegane reanimated? | Qyburn presents an immense silent armored Kingsguard; Gregor’s alleged skull is delivered to Dorne. | Identity is never narrated; the skull’s identity and Strong’s body are not independently verified. | A reliable unmasking, medical evidence, or corroborated witness account. | Preserve as a strong inference, not a courtroom fact. |
| Will the valonqar be Jaime? | Maggy’s prophecy uses “little brother”; Cersei reads it as Tyrion. | Prophecy is ambiguous; Jaime’s candidacy is plausible but not compelled, and fulfillment may not be literal. | Actual future event, or stronger contextual clarification; none exists in five books. | Use prophecy as fear/pressure, never a fixed endpoint. |
| Is the Night Lamp plan already in motion? | Stannis is snowbound near lakes; terrain and enemy approach create a tactical possibility. | No narrated Stannis plan or received Jon warning; starvation and divided command can defeat any elegant design. | An observed Stannis order, terrain preparation, or witness account of the operation. | Keep as a branch, not the starting fact of a battle scene. |
| Are Manderly/Frey tensions a completed grand northern conspiracy? | Manderly has conditional Stark motives; Frey/Manderly violence and mutual suspicion are shown. | Separate factions can have compatible short-term reasons without a unified secret command. | Direct coordination, message, order, or corroborated action beyond local self-interest. | Preserve multiple northern subplots and incentives. |

## Application rule

For every proposed *Winds* scene, declare which row is merely a reader hypothesis and which facts the POV actually knows. A scene may test a theory, but may not begin by assuming its answer. Independent challenge remains open.
