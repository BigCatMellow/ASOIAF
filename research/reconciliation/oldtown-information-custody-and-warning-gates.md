# Oldtown — information custody and warning gates

- Record role: `RESEARCH / RECONCILIATION`.
- Status: `WORKING — TESTIMONY RECEIVED LOCALLY; INSTITUTIONAL ACTION UNPUBLISHED`.
- Primary text: AFFC 45, “Samwell”; related [Oldtown contact gates](oldtown-ironborn-contact-gates.md) and [coastal-force ledger](oldtown-coastal-force-ledger.md).

## The central distinction

**VERIFIED:** Sam tells Alleras, Marwyn, and the pasty-faced novice in Marwyn’s room about major Wall events, Aemon’s interpretation, dragons, and his journey. Marwyn has a physically burning glass candle and says he will sail for Slaver’s Bay. Sam is offered a cell near Walgrave’s ravenry.

**NOT VERIFIED:** The official Seneschal hears Sam’s account or Jon’s letters; any archmaester adopts a policy; a raven is sent to the Wall; Marwyn departs or arrives; the man calling himself Pate has access to the ravens/keys; or a glass-candle message is successfully sent or received.

## Who holds which information

| Holder / channel | What the books show it holds | Constraint before it can change another theater |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sam | Aemon’s death, Jon’s letters, reports of Others/wights, and secrets he deliberately withholds (Bran and the infant exchange). | He must choose a recipient and survive Oldtown’s institutional/social pressures; the reader cannot make his withheld facts public. |
| Alleras | Sam’s broad testimony and a direct link to Marwyn. | His identity, motives, and any subsequent message are not established; he cannot stand for “the Citadel.” |
| Marwyn | Sam’s testimony, a glass candle, and his own claims about prophecy/Citadel hostility; he announces a voyage to Dany. | His claims are not institutional proof, and his travel/message success is unshown. |

| Ravenry / Walgrave | Ravens, including white and black birds, and a senile archmaester’s chambers are physically present. | Access is not permission; a bird’s existence is not a dispatched message or a received answer. |
| “Pate” | He offers Sam lodging near the ravenry and shares the name of the prologue victim. | Replacement identity is a strong inference, but purpose, patron, key/raven access, and action remain unknown. |
| Oldtown defenders | They visibly/credibly face Ironborn coastal danger and inspect ships. | They are not shown receiving Sam’s Wall intelligence or possessing an operational magical warning system. |

## Immediate choices that keep the plot honest

1. **Sam’s formal warning:** He can follow Marwyn’s advice to conceal dragons/prophecy, attempt official testimony, confide selectively, or prioritize Gilly and the children. Each route creates different records and risks.
2. **Raven custody:** Before a Wall message exists, someone needs authority, a text, a bird, a destination, dispatch, and eventual receipt. Walgrave’s compromised capacity and Pate’s proximity make this a security question, not a convenient telephone.
3. **Marwyn’s departure:** A declared intention to take the Cinnamon Wind is not yet a voyage. Crew consent, port security, Ironborn danger, destination, and Dany’s eventual willingness to receive him all remain gates.
4. **Citadel response:** Skepticism, investigation, compartmentalization, cooperation, and hostility are distinct possible responses. Marwyn’s accusation that orthodox maesters would poison Sam is a `CHARACTER CLAIM`, not a verified institutional plan.
5. **Gilly and the infants:** Their location and safety constrain Sam’s freedom. Their history is not an all-purpose reason to assume Sam abandons study, the Watch, or them.

| Pate’s presence means immediate sabotage | The original Pate apparently dies after selling a key, and the new man uses his name near ravens. | No action, access, or identity is confessed on-page. | A witnessed key/raven/text use, an identified patron, or a linked intrusion. |
| Marwyn can rapidly change Dany’s trajectory | He means to sail toward her and carries unusual knowledge. | Dany is lost in the Grass Sea and Marwyn has no demonstrated contact route. | Departure, survival at sea, arrival, and an actual audience. |

## Consequence for the broader convergence map

Oldtown can become relevant to the Wall, Daenerys, or the Ironborn only through separate, vulnerable routes. It is therefore plausible for its knowledge to be important without being timely, unanimous, or trusted. Any continuation that makes Sam’s testimony instantly solve the Others, dragon, or Citadel plot bypasses every source-grounded custody gate above.

Independent challenge remains open.
