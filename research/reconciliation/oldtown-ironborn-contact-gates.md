2. **“Citadel suppresses Sam”:** require a recipient, institutional response, or recorded obstruction after Sam’s warning.
3. **“Glass candle solves distance”:** require a depicted user, target, message, and interpretable result.
4. **“Pate works for Euron”:** require an actual chain of communication or a reliable identification; shared magical interest is insufficient.
5. **“Victarion’s horn affects Oldtown”:** require a mechanism across separate theaters, not shared readership.
