# Riverlands — Jaime, Brienne, and Stoneheart endpoint handoff

- Record role: `RESEARCH / RECONCILIATION`.
- Status: `WORKING — LURE SPOKEN; ACCEPTANCE, CUSTODY, AND DESTINATION UNPUBLISHED`.
- Primary text: AFFC 42, “Brienne”; ADWD 48, “Jaime.”
- Detailed evidence: [Jaime’s incomplete Stoneheart intelligence](../synthesis/jaime-stoneheart-rumor-to-brienne-pennytree-information-gates.md).

## Endpoint in one sentence

Brienne, alive and visibly injured after Stoneheart ordered her, Podrick, and Hyle hanged, tells Jaime that she found “the girl” and that the Hound will kill her unless Jaime rides alone; the five-book narrative stops before Jaime answers, leaves, or meets anyone.

## Separate what each principal knows

| Actor | Verified knowledge at the endpoint | Critical ignorance / constraint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Jaime | Brienne has returned wounded with Oathkeeper; she claims a girl is a day away and invokes the Hound. He has heard reports of dangerous outlaws, including a figure called Stoneheart. | He has not seen Sansa, a Hound, Stoneheart, Podrick, or Hyle; does not know Stoneheart is Catelyn; has not agreed to travel alone. |

| Stoneheart / Brotherhood | They possess their own grievance evidence, Oathkeeper, and Tommen’s safe-conduct; Stoneheart gave a sword-or-noose demand in Brienne’s hearing. | No on-page order after Brienne’s scream, no shown current cave/camp location, and no narrated receipt of Jaime’s response. |
| Jaime’s Pennytree party | Brienne is brought in injured; Jaime has men, pages, and Hoster Blackwood in his charge. | They are not shown hearing a full explanation, receiving an order, guarding a departure, or learning about Stoneheart. |

## Necessary links before a proposed confrontation

1. **Jaime’s decision:** He must accept, challenge, modify, or refuse Brienne’s lone-rider demand. None is narrated.
2. **Departure and command:** If he goes, the party needs a custody/command arrangement for Hoster Blackwood and its own route; “alone” cannot erase their physical presence without a choice.
3. **Destination:** Brienne must lead him to a real location; no text identifies where Stoneheart or the alleged girl is at the ADWD endpoint.
4. **Hostage leverage:** A continuation must establish whether Podrick/Hyle are alive, where they are, and how any threat reaches Brienne/Jaime. Their prior survival is not proof of current safety.
5. **Hound referent:** Lem’s helmet is evidence for a deliberately misleading identity; it does not establish Sandor, Lem, or anyone else currently holds Sansa.
6. **Stoneheart’s hearing:** Any judgment requires her followers, evidence, and priorities to be shown; she cannot be assumed to know Jaime’s private intentions or the reader’s evidence.

## Theory safeguards

- **Not verified:** Jaime is captured, executed, tried, exchanged, or used in a Red Wedding 2.0 plan.
- **Not verified:** Brienne freely chooses betrayal or has found Sansa.
- **Not verified:** Sandor Clegane is the person threatening the girl.
- **Not verified:** Stoneheart has a specific future wedding operation, knows Robb’s will, or has received Tom o’ Sevens’s Riverrun intelligence.
- **Inference with support:** Brienne’s line is likely a coercive lure, because it directly parallels Stoneheart’s earlier demand and uses an identity Brienne knows is misleading. This remains an inference because the terms after her scream are withheld.

## Consequence for Winds trajectory work

This strand should initially be treated as a **private, asymmetric-information crisis**, not an instant Riverlands revolution. Its most plausible first narrative effect is to test whether Jaime and Brienne can act under incompatible oaths and hostage pressure; only after a shown meeting can it alter Riverrun, the Freys, Sansa, or wider succession politics.

Independent challenge remains open.
