## Continuity prohibitions and viable tests

1. Do not let Stannis use Jon’s Karstark warning as received intelligence until a delivery path is supplied.
2. Do not let Castle Black know that Theon and Jeyne reached Stannis without a later messenger.
3. Do not treat Jeyne’s identity as a free strategic resource: she is a survivor whose consent and testimony are unresolved.
4. Do not have the Pink Letter settle either Stannis’s fate or its own authorship.
5. Before any North/Wall convergence, test travel, weather, ravens, and who would risk carrying news through hostile territory.

## High-value next evidence

- A source-grounded chronology of Mors Umber, Tycho, and ravens around the crofter’s village.
- A person-by-person ledger of witnesses who can identify Jeyne as not Arya, separated from those merely repeating the public story.
- A Watch succession analysis that separates formal office, armed control, wildling loyalty, and Melisandre’s influence.
