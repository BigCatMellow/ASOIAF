## What the books actually show about Shireen and fire

- **VERIFIED:** Shireen bears healed greyscale scars; she is alive and physically present at Castle Black (ADWD 44, 69).
- **VERIFIED:** Selyse has religious commitments and speaks harshly of abandoning Hardhome refugees; Melisandre supports that view in ADWD 69.
- **CHARACTER CLAIM:** Val calls Shireen’s condition “grey death” and warns it can return (ADWD 53). This contradicts/competes with the maesterly understanding and is not medical proof of imminent danger.
- **VERIFIED:** Stannis has previously authorized burning/sacrifice in his religious-political conflict, which makes ethical escalation a meaningful possibility.
- **NOT VERIFIED:** Stannis orders Shireen burned, Shireen is moved toward Stannis, a fire is prepared for her, or any specific magical result requires her death.

## Why the show route cannot be silently imported

Public showrunner reporting makes Stannis–Shireen sacrifice a high-priority **possibility**, but the published books place the two characters in different endangered institutions. A credible book path must answer all of these questions:

1. Who has custody of Shireen when a decision is proposed?
2. What does Stannis himself know, and by what carrier?
3. Is he physically present, issuing a verifiable order, or absent while others claim his authority?
4. What concrete danger or belief makes an adult consider the child’s life instrumental?
5. Who can object, protect her, or authenticate the order amid Castle Black’s command crisis?
6. What mechanism/result is expected, and what ordinary alternative has been considered?

Without those answers, an analogous event is not a forecast; it is an adaptation-shaped blank.

## Provisional conclusion

The books make sacrifice a serious ethical pressure point, not an imminent verified event. The most responsible forecast is that any Shireen crisis must first be a **custody-and-command problem across distance**. The future text must supply a message, movement, delegated authority, or local decision before a sacrifice can be treated as a viable plotted event. Independent challenge remains open.
