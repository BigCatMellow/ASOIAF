- **VERIFIED:** Brienne later appears wounded with Oathkeeper and tells Jaime that she found “the girl,” who is allegedly a day away; she says he must come alone because “the Hound” will kill her.
- **UNKNOWN:** the word she screamed, any ensuing bargain, Podrick’s and Hyle’s post-gallows status, a current Stoneheart location, the alleged girl’s identity/location, Jaime’s response, and any departure.

No material source-classification correction was required in those Riverlands records. Their stated coercive-lure interpretation remains an **INFERENCE**, not an adopted outcome.

## Reusable test

For each subsequent endpoint audit, test these separately:

1. Was the cited event directly depicted, or only reported/claimed?
2. If a message exists, was it dispatched, delivered, received, and acted upon?
3. If an actor is injured, accused, or afraid, does the text establish the diagnosis, guilt, or cause?
4. Does the text actually depict the proposed response, departure, contact, or result?
