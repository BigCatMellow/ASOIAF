# Aegon invasion — report and recipient ledger

- Record role: `RESEARCH / RECONCILIATION`.
- Primary text: ADWD 61, “The Griffin Reborn”; ADWD Epilogue, “Kevan.”
- Scope: messages and reports from the Cape Wrath landing through the five-book endpoint.
- Status: `WORKING — SOURCE-BASED ROUTES; INDEPENDENT CHALLENGE OPEN`.

## Why this ledger is necessary

Connington's opening strategy combines visible seizures with deliberately confused attribution. The reader can see the claimant, Golden Company, and later Varys's explanation, while Dorne and the capital receive only the messages and reports that can actually survive their routes. The books therefore establish neither one shared news state nor a reliable timetable for a unified response.

## Chronological transmission ledger

## Continuation constraints and tests

1. A later Dorne scene needs a carrier, route, recipient, authentication judgment, and a decision to share or withhold Aegon or Quentyn news.
2. A royal response must begin with the reports actually available and can reasonably be delayed, divided, or misdirected; it cannot begin with Varys's private explanation as public fact.
3. A claimed Storm's End victory requires a depicted assault or a later attributable report. The council's report that Connington is *moving on* the castle is not a result.
4. Confusion is purposeful but not limitless: the Epilogue demonstrates that the capital has enough intelligence to recognize Connington as a live concern. Treating the invasion as wholly invisible contradicts that council scene.
