# King’s Landing after Kevan and Pycelle — decision gates

- Record role: `RESEARCH / RECONCILIATION`.
- Source scope: AFFC 17, 28, 39, 43; ADWD 54–55, 65, Epilogue.
- Status: `WORKING — MURDER DISCOVERED ONLY AFTER ENDPOINT`.

## Starting condition

The reader sees Varys kill Pycelle and mortally wound Kevan. The court is not shown discovering either body, agreeing on a culprit, appointing a replacement, resolving Cersei or Margaery’s cases, or receiving a definitive report of Aegon’s campaign. Any opening must begin with discovery and contested interpretation, not a fully informed regime response.

| Decision gate | Actors with immediate stake | Verified constraint | Branches that must remain open |
|---|---|---|---|
| Discover and frame the murders | Cersei, Mace, Faith, remaining council, guards | Varys’s role is reader-only; children and crossbow create possible misdirection. | Blame can fall on competing factions, opportunists, or unseen enemies; no public culprit is established. |
| Govern for Tommen | Cersei, Mace, Faith, royal household | Kevan had been limiting Cersei; Tommen remains king. | Cersei may seek restoration, Mace may seek dominance, Faith may claim moral necessity, or a compromise may emerge. |
| Resolve the trials | Cersei, Margaery, High Sparrow, Tyrells | Cersei’s walk is release, not acquittal; Margaery’s guilt is not proved by coerced testimony. | Trial timing, champions, procedure, and political settlement are unshown. |
| Use Robert Strong | Cersei, Qyburn, Faith, court observers | Strong is publicly a silent Kingsguard; his identity is not textually proved. | Acceptance, investigation, or tactical use cannot be assumed. |
| Answer Aegon news | Mace, council, Dorne-facing actors, Varys covertly | Reports are incomplete; Connington’s force/Storm’s End result are not shown. | Overreaction, dismissal, divided response, or deliberate misinformation are all viable. |

## Character-led tests

- A Cersei branch must ask whether she learns vulnerability, doubles down on coercion, or attempts compromise; it cannot begin with her automatically restored as a competent ruler.
- A Tyrell branch must distinguish Mace’s family interest from Margaery’s legal position and from the Reach’s military capacity.
- A Faith branch must reckon with both real popular legitimacy and coercive armed power, rather than function as a single villainous bloc.
- A Varys branch must show how destabilization advances Aegon without granting him omniscience or instantaneous public control.

## Adaptation guardrail

The television destruction of the Sept, wildfire catastrophe, and later regime sequence are adaptation outcomes, not a book starting fact. They can be tested only after this court has generated a book-native chain of discovery, trial, command, and military response.

Independent challenge remains open.
