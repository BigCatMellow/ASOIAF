## Planning rule

Adaptation-reported “destination” material raises a question: *what choice, cost, and mechanism could make an analogous book event grow organically?* If no book-native bridge exists, retain the possibility as a failed garden test rather than forcing it into an outline.

## Immediate research result

The strongest useful future pressure points are ethical rather than predictive: Bran’s continuing violation of Hodor, Stannis’s starvation/religious command crisis, and Victarion’s willingness to sacrifice another person for Dragonbinder. None determines an ending by itself.

Independent challenge remains open.
