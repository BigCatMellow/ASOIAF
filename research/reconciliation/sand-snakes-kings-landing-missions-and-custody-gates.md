# Sand Snakes — King's Landing missions and custody gates

- Record role: `RESEARCH / RECONCILIATION`.
- Scope: Nymeria Sand's Myrcella/council mission and Tyene Sand's Faith mission as established in **ADWD 38 (The Watcher)** and carried to the five-novel endpoint in **ADWD 72 (Epilogue)**. The narrow Alleras/Sarella boundary comes from **AFFC 45 (Samwell)**.
- Status: `WORKING — NYMERIA'S JOURNEY IS CONFIRMED; ARRIVAL, OFFICE, AND TYENE'S APPROACH ARE NOT SHOWN`.
- Boundary: This is a specialized counterpart to [Dorne — outbound missions and receipt gates](dorne-outbound-missions-and-receipt-gates.md). It does not duplicate the Balon/Obara/Darkstar chain, use *The Winds of Winter* material or adaptation events, or identify Alleras as Sarella as a fact.

## Starting positions and instructions

**VERIFIED:** In ADWD 38, Doran has released Obara, Nymeria, and Tyene from detention and makes their new assignments only after requiring each to swear obedience on Oberyn's grave. The oath is an on-page constraint and a reason to expect an attempt; it is not proof of internal assent, safe travel, or successful execution.

| Mission | Doran's stated purpose | What the text directly establishes by ADWD's end | Still unshown |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nymeria | Return Myrcella to her mother, occupy Dorne's vacant small-council place, and gain a voice in council and an ear at court (**ADWD 38**). | Kevan calls her the Dornishwoman "now escorting Myrcella home" and expects her as the council's seventh voice (**ADWD 72**). | Her arrival, Myrcella's delivery, formal appointment or seating, private access, information acquired, and any report to Dorne. |
| Tyene | Go to King's Landing and seek closeness to the newly powerful High Septon through her maternal/performative religious connection (**ADWD 38**). | The order and Tyene's oath. | Departure, route, identity/presentation used, admission to the Faith, audience, trust, leverage, intelligence, or a message home. |

The missions are related only in intended destination and Doran's strategy. They have different hosts, access rules, information pools, and dangers. Nymeria's prospective institutional entry is royal government; Tyene's is an autonomous religious power. They should not be treated as a single already-coordinated King's Landing cell.

## Nymeria, Myrcella, and court custody

### What has changed by the endpoint

1. **VERIFIED — Doran's instruction is a managed retreat as well as an insertion.** He says open defiance of the Iron Throne is not yet possible, so Myrcella must be returned, and gives Nymeria that charge. This is not simply a voluntary royal invitation: Myrcella's physical custody, dynastic betrothal, and Dorne's exposure to retaliation all make the return consequential (**ADWD 38**).
2. **VERIFIED — the crown anticipates a Dornish council claimant.** In the epilogue, Kevan privately describes Lady Nym as escorting Myrcella home and as the seventh expected council voice, replacing the seat briefly held by Oberyn. This confirms movement after Doran's instruction and that at least Kevan expects a political claim (**ADWD 72**).
3. **VERIFIED — entry is not equivalent to influence.** Kevan has deliberately withheld Nymeria's coming from Mace Tyrell and expects Mace to dislike it. The projected council balance also depends on Paxter Redwyne returning. A named seat, a useful vote, confidential access, and the ability to obtain reliable intelligence are separate gates (**ADWD 72**).
4. **VERIFIED — Myrcella's reception cannot be presumed calm.** Kevan's council is preparing for the princess while treating Dorne as a necessary but risky ally amid the Connington/Aegon threat. Mace voices a wish for another match; Kevan fears Doran could align with Connington. These are present incentives to accommodate Myrcella and Nymeria, not a guarantee of goodwill or security (**ADWD 72**).
5. **VERIFIED — the apparent receiving authority is disrupted before any arrival is shown.** Varys kills Kevan and Pycelle in the same epilogue. Neither Nymeria nor Myrcella is shown learning this, arriving in the city, or receiving altered instructions. Any subsequent reception must therefore establish who controls the court and who has custody of the princess, rather than carry forward Kevan's expectation automatically (**ADWD 72**).

### Causal gates

1. **Journey/custody gate:** Nymeria and Myrcella must complete the return journey. The epilogue's present-tense description supports that it is under way; it does not locate them, prove a route or establish arrival.
2. **Reception gate:** A royal officer must receive Myrcella and decide where she lives, who guards her, and whether Nymeria remains with her. Doran's appointment language and Kevan's expectation do not themselves confer physical access to the princess after handover.
3. **Office gate:** Nymeria needs recognition as Dorne's councillor and an actual place in deliberations. The vacancy since Oberyn and Kevan's calculation support the possibility; they do not show a patent, oath, or first meeting.
4. **Credibility gate:** Court intelligence can be factionalized, staged, or incomplete. A council seat gives exposure to policy disputes, not omniscience or a shared view with Tyene.
5. **Transmission/receipt gate:** A letter, raven, trusted carrier, or personal return must reach an identified Dornish recipient before Nymeria's purported findings can alter Doran's or Arianne's choices. A message received by Nymeria is not automatically received in Sunspear.

## Tyene and the Faith

**VERIFIED:** Doran asks Tyene to reach the High Septon because her mother was a septa who read the *Seven-Pointed Star* to her as a child. He identifies the restored Swords and Stars and the new High Septon's independence from prior royal manipulation as the reason this channel matters (**ADWD 38**). The scene establishes a plausible cover or social bridge; it does not show Tyene using one, nor prove the High Septon will regard a noblewoman's piety as genuine.

The target institution has real coercive and political capacity at the endpoint. Kevan believes defying the High Septon would produce urban violence and bolster competing claimants; the Faith controls the timing and form of Margaery's trial, and places a septa and rotating novices around Cersei (**ADWD 72**). These are **VERIFIED** signs of independent leverage, not evidence that Tyene has joined or influenced it.

Tyene's particular gates are therefore:

1. **Movement and presentation:** She must arrive and choose a presentation that secures an introduction without surrendering her own agency. No departure or arrival is narrated.
2. **Institutional admission:** The High Septon, his household, or a useful subordinate must permit access. Religious ancestry is not membership, office, or trust.
3. **Identity and suspicion:** Tyene's cultivated innocence and known Sand Snake identity can pull in opposite directions. The prose supplies no scene testing which identity is visible to a Faith contact.
4. **Operational boundary:** An audience, devotional role, or apparent confidence would still not demonstrate control of the Faith Militant, knowledge of every Faith decision, or power to direct its armed men.
5. **Reporting boundary:** Any intelligence needs a credible carrier and recipient. It must remain distinct from Nymeria's court channel until a coordination or receipt scene connects them.

## Alleras and the Sarella question: an evidence boundary

**VERIFIED:** Sam meets Alleras, an acolyte called Sphinx, at Oldtown's Citadel. Sam observes a slight youth with light-brown skin, black curls, a soft Dornish drawl, and Alleras says he is half Dornish. Alleras listens to Sam's Wall/dragon account, takes him to Marwyn, and says Marwyn sent him to intercept Sam (**AFFC 45**).

**THEORY / INFERENCE:** A reader may connect "Alleras" and "Sarella" by reversible-looking names, the Dornish/Summer Isles-associated description, and the separate published-book identification of Sarella as Oberyn's daughter. Those details make the identity hypothesis worth retaining, but the five novels never call Alleras "Sarella," show a transformation or disguise, or have a character recognize the person. Alleras's work is at Oldtown, not an on-page King's Landing mission; even a correct identity inference would not make Alleras a courier, agent, or recipient for Nymeria and Tyene.

**Competing ordinary explanation:** Alleras can be a distinct half-Dornish Citadel acolyte. The visual and linguistic details establish background but do not compel parentage. The immediately relevant on-page function is Marwyn's interception of Sam, not a revealed Dornish operation.

## Future verification tests

1. Locate Nymeria and Myrcella's arrival and identify their ship/road party, guards, first royal receiver, and Myrcella's ensuing residence before asserting custody or political access.
2. Separate a formal council appointment from the first attended meeting, meaningful vote, private informant, and message successfully received in Dorne.
3. Test whether the capital's post-Kevan command arrangement honors, delays, exploits, or rejects the planned reception; do not make the dead Hand's plan self-executing.
4. Require an on-page Faith contact before describing Tyene as infiltrated, trusted, informed, or influential.
5. Retain Sarella = Alleras only as a theory until direct identification or unambiguous independent corroboration appears; keep Oldtown and King's Landing routes separate even if the identity is later established.

## Non-adoption rule

No continuation may begin with a seated, informed, coordinated Nymeria–Tyene apparatus in King's Landing. The source supports a journey in progress for Nymeria and a stated assignment for Tyene; every material form of capital access, custody, intelligence, coordination, and Dornish receipt remains an open narrative gate. Independent challenge remains open.
