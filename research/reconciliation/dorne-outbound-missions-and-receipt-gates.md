# Dorne — outbound missions and receipt gates

- Record role: `RESEARCH / RECONCILIATION`.
- Scope: Doran Martell's instructions to Obara, Nymeria, and Tyene in **ADWD 38 (The Watcher)**, and what must happen before any of those instructions can affect another front.
- Status: `WORKING — NO MISSION COMPLETION OR RECEIPT SHOWN`.
- Boundary: This record does not use *The Winds of Winter* sample chapters, adaptation events, or reader knowledge of Aegon's and Quentyn's separate news channels as if any Dornish recipient possesses them.

## The orders are real; their execution is not yet narrated

After requiring Obara, Nymeria, and Tyene to swear obedience on Oberyn's grave, Doran tells each a distinct assignment. His language immediately before the assignments is conditional: if unspecified developments occur, he will send word. The scene nevertheless establishes the intended roles below; it does **not** establish a departure date, route, escort, successful arrival, access, or report back.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Obara Sand | Lead Ser Balon Swann to High Hermitage to confront/hunt Gerold Dayne, whom Doran's public account blames for Arys Oakheart's death and Myrcella's injury. | Balon must hear Myrcella's managed story, accept it sufficiently to pursue Darkstar, and depart with Obara. | Obara has sworn obedience in Sunspear. | That Balon believes the account, that he leaves, that Obara travels with him, reaches High Hermitage, finds Darkstar, or sends intelligence to Doran. |

| Tyene Sand | Go to King's Landing and seek proximity to the newly empowered High Septon, using a pious presentation as access. | Travel to the capital and successful admission into the Faith's orbit; the High Septon or his household must accept her proximity. | Tyene has sworn obedience in Sunspear. | A departure, arrival, disguise/admission, audience, influence over the Faith, intelligence collection, or a return channel. |

**VERIFIED:** The assignments follow an oath extracted after Doran says he has learned he cannot yet trust the three women. An oath and stated intention therefore strengthen the likelihood of a mission attempt, but they do not erase agency, logistical obstacles, or Doran's conditional reservation of further word.

## Mission-specific causal gates

### 1. Obara, Balon, and Darkstar

The proposed Darkstar pursuit is not a simple Dornish arrest order. It rests on a public explanatory story: Arianne tells the Sand Snakes that Gerold Dayne killed Arys and tried to kill Myrcella, while the reader has seen Areo Hotah kill Arys in **AFFC 21**. Doran intends Myrcella to tell this account to Balon at the Water Gardens, then expects Balon—if he is the honorable man Doran judges him to be—to take up the hunt with Obara.

1. **Receipt gate:** Balon must personally receive Myrcella's account; the chapter has not yet shown that meeting.
2. **Credibility gate:** Balon may doubt the story, investigate it, or accept it provisionally. His unease and Doran's interpretation of it are not a confession of complicity in the alleged Trystane plot.
3. **Authority/choice gate:** As a Kingsguard, Balon still has duties to Myrcella and the crown. A decision to hunt Darkstar, and the terms under which Obara accompanies him, require depiction.
4. **Travel/contact gate:** High Hermitage, Gerold's presence there, and a confrontation are all future contingencies at the five-book endpoint.
5. **Report gate:** Even a successful encounter becomes Doran's knowledge only through a named survivor/messenger and a credible route back.

This mission cannot by itself validate Doran's account of Arys's death, prove Gerold's intent toward Myrcella, or reveal who informed Doran of Arianne's earlier plot.

### 2. Nymeria, Myrcella, and the royal court

Doran says Dorne must return Myrcella to her mother because open defiance is not yet viable, while making Nymeria the intended Dornish escort and political observer. His phrase about needing a voice in council and an ear at court expresses a strategic objective, not a granted office. The crown's earlier invitation is also politically unstable: Doran believes, based on unnamed court friends and Balon's reaction, that Cersei arranged a fatal ambush for Trystane, but this remains a **CHARACTER CLAIM / INFERENCE**, not an event depicted in the text.

1. **Movement gate:** Myrcella's actual departure, health, escort, route, and arrival are not shown after ADWD 38.
2. **Reception gate:** Nymeria must be admitted into the royal political environment; Doran's assertion that the Lannisters "dare not refuse" is a forecast, not a verified appointment.
3. **Access gate:** A council seat, informal information source, and ability to distinguish rumor from policy are separate achievements.
4. **Transmission gate:** Any court finding needs a carrier, cipher/letter, raven, or trusted personal return before Doran or Arianne may act on it.

The endpoint must also account for the capital's own unresolved disruption: Kevan and Pycelle are murdered in the Epilogue, but no Dornish traveler is shown receiving that fact. Nymeria cannot react to the reader's knowledge until she first reaches the capital and then receives credible information there.

### 3. Tyene and the Faith

Tyene's maternal history as a septa and her cultivated appearance give Doran a plausible access strategy, but no actual infiltration has occurred on page. The High Septon is newly significant because the Faith Militant has been restored, yet his personal disposition, admissions process, staff, and reaction to a Dornish noblewoman remain open. "Get close" does not establish consent, trust, leverage, or operational control.

1. **Travel gate:** Tyene must leave Dorne and reach the capital; neither step is narrated.
2. **Institutional gate:** She must obtain access to the High Septon or persons who actually carry useful Faith information.
3. **Interpretation gate:** Information she obtains may be incomplete, staged, or wrong; access is not omniscience.
4. **Return gate:** Doran, Arianne, or Nymeria need a demonstrated receipt before any purported Tyene intelligence changes their decisions.

This is a separate intelligence channel from Nymeria's proposed court presence. Treating the two cousins as a single fully coordinated King's Landing apparatus would erase both the need for receipt and possible divergent loyalties, risks, or access.

## Decision consequences without outcome assumptions

- Doran's three deployments are an attempt to create **future** options: a managed response to the Myrcella crisis, an institutional observer at court, an institutional observer in the Faith, and an outward pursuit of Darkstar. They are not evidence that Dorne has selected a claimant or gone to war.
- A first successful receipt can change only the recipient's information set. Doran privately receiving a report does not automatically mean Arianne, the Sand Snakes, Dornish lords, or a foreign ally know it.
- The channels can arrive in any order. A court report, Faith report, Aegon letter, Quentyn news, and a Darkstar outcome should each retain a carrier, recipient, credibility test, and interval before a downstream decision is attributed to Dorne.
- Doran's stated caution and illness constrain speed and personal mobility, but do not prove that he will reject risk. Conversely, the Sand Snakes' oath does not prove wholehearted obedience or success.

## Non-adoption rule

No theory or projected continuation may treat the three missions as completed, coordinated, or informed by Aegon's claim, Quentyn's death, or the capital's Epilogue crisis until the missing movements and receipts are narratively supplied. Independent challenge remains open.
