## Verified sequence

1. **AFFC 4:** Ser Shadrich tells Brienne he seeks Sansa Stark, describes her appearance and Dontos connection, and says Varys offered a reward. This establishes Shadrich’s stated search and informed lead; the bounty’s status/amount and his ultimate motive remain his claim.
2. **AFFC 4:** Brienne recognizes him as a practical rival searcher and avoids treating him as an ally. No Sansa sighting, partnership, or seizure occurs.
3. **AFFC 23:** Sansa’s Alayne disguise survives Bronze Yohn’s momentary uncertainty; her darkened hair, false biography, and Petyr’s protection remain active rather than foolproof.
4. **AFFC 41:** Petyr introduces Ser Shadrich, Byron, and Morgarth at the Gates of the Moon as hired knights. Sansa is presented as Alayne, Petyr’s natural daughter. No recognition, private exchange, threat, or report from Shadrich is narrated.

## Interaction with Petyr’s Harry plan

Petyr’s intended Sansa reveal is conditional on Robert’s death, Sansa’s legal/marital position, Harry’s acceptance, and Vale support. Shadrich’s possible knowledge could threaten, delay, redirect, or be absorbed into that plan, but it cannot be used to make the reveal automatic. Sansa’s care for Robert and her limited independent security remain live ethical constraints.

## Provisional conclusion

Shadrich’s arrival makes Sansa’s concealment a **sharper local security question**, not a completed capture plot. The first responsible continuation scene must establish who recognizes whom and who physically controls Sansa’s movement before it can use the bounty or Harry plan to move the Vale. Independent challenge remains open.
