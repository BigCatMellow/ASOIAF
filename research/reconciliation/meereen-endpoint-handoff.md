## Claims that must stay disaggregated

1. **Poisoned locusts:** poison is reported/detected, but intended victim and perpetrator remain unresolved. Barristan’s suspicion and Hizdahr’s accusation of Quentyn are rival character interpretations.
2. **Sons of the Harpy:** violence is verified; a single command structure, specific leadership, and every noble’s complicity are not.
3. **Dragonbinding:** the horn’s fatal prior sounder is witnessed/reported in part, but Euron’s provenance claim and Moqorro’s blood-master translation have not produced a dragon response.
4. **Dany’s political turn:** her revulsion at compromise and “fire and blood” resolve are real interior material, not proof of a future massacre, immediate return, or abandonment of Meereen.
5. **Mercenary allegiance:** prior turncloaking and signed incentives are evidence of conditional behavior, not reliable proof of the next battlefield choice.

## Synchronization tests before a proposed convergence

- A Dany return needs a Dothraki, Drogon, and travel mechanism rather than reader knowledge of the siege.
- A Tyrion–Barristan meeting requires crossing battle lines or a communicated defection.
- Victarion’s fleet needs a naval encounter or surviving messenger to alter Meereen’s land command.
- Any Dorne reaction to Quentyn requires a named survivor, ship, and elapsed travel time.
- A decision about Hizdahr must distinguish security necessity from retrospective proof of guilt.
