## Cross-theater constraints

1. A POV may not react to a development until a plausible dispatch, witness, or travel route supplies it.
2. “Reported dead” must remain a character claim without body, reliable witness, or later verification.
3. Dany’s absence is the shared constraint in Meereen, but it does not automatically authorize Barristan, Hizdahr, or any sellsword faction’s preferred policy.
4. The Pink Letter, Dragonbinder translations, Varys’s Aegon narrative, and religious/prophetic readings must stay separately labeled claims.
5. A planned convergence must pass both geography and institutional legitimacy checks; proximity is not communication.

## Priority reconciliation queue

- North: construct a timestamped dispatch/receipt matrix from Winterfell, the crofter’s village, Castle Black, and Braavos.
- Meereen: reconcile the opening battle order, captives, ships, dragons, and who knows Dany is missing versus dead.
- Southern war: connect Aegon’s landing to King’s Landing’s last verified council information and Dorne’s unanswered letter.
- Court: distinguish Cersei’s release from acquittal and determine the consequences of Kevan/Pycelle’s removal without assuming the murderer is publicly known.
- Theory audit: carry all unresolved identity and prophecy questions as rival explanations, not plot commitments.
