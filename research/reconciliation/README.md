# Endpoint reconciliation — research router

- Record role: `RESEARCH / RECONCILIATION INDEX`.
- Scope: the five published novels, unless a linked record explicitly says otherwise.
- Status: `WORKING — SOURCE-BASED HANDOFFS, NOT ADOPTED CONTINUATION`.

## Purpose

The close reading is complete, but the end of *A Dance with Dragons* is not a single synchronized present. These records identify what has actually been seen, who has received which claim, and the missing movement, custody, command, or verification steps before a later event may be treated as causally available. They are guardrails for theory review and eventual gardening, not chapter outlines or canon predictions.

Use the narrowest record that owns a question. A link establishes a research dependency, not that every linked fact has reached the same character.

## Application rules

1. Start a proposed scene with the POV’s received information, not the reader’s cross-theater map.
2. Treat a letter, prophecy, report, or character’s recollection as its correct evidence class until independent support appears.
3. For every proposed convergence, state a carrier, route, time interval, recipient, and reason the recipient credits the message.
4. For custody or succession, distinguish a person’s survival from their safety, consent, legal authority, and political acceptance.
5. Consult [the theory discrimination matrix](high-impact-theory-discrimination-matrix.md) before adopting an identity, authorship, prophecy, or battle-plan theory as a premise.

Independent challenge remains open. A record’s open gate is a prompt for source testing, not a license to fill it with an attractive outcome.
