This is a **geographical and informational split**, not merely a difference of intention. Victarion's ban on Maester Kerwin's ravens (ADWD 56) eliminates the ordinary ongoing report channel to Euron; no later scene supplies a replacement message.

## First-contact gates

### 1. Euron's western force and Oldtown

Before “Euron attacks Oldtown” can be used as a plot event, require: (a) a named sighting, message, or order locating him; (b) actual contact with an Oldtown patrol, harbor boom, defenders, or shore; (c) a means by which local actors learn that the force is his rather than generic ironborn; and (d) a tactical objective—raid, blockade, harbor breach, landing, or negotiation. AFFC 45 verifies threat and regional violence, but not those connections. Rumors of Leyton/Malora's spells and Euron's sorcery cannot replace boats, people, signals, and decisions.

### 2. Victarion and Meereen

Before “Victarion delivers Euron's dragon weapon to Daenerys” can occur, require: (a) survival through the imminent naval conflict; (b) a first receiving actor with authority or access—Barristan, a city faction, Daenerys, or another specified party; (c) a credible account of his hostile/ambiguous fleet and prisoners; (d) knowledge of Daenerys's absence or return; and (e) a choice to display, hide, surrender, use, or destroy the horn. Victarion's private betrayal makes Euron's intended delivery a rival outcome, not a default.

### 3. Dragonbinder and a dragon

Before “Dragonbinder works,” show: a proposed blower; the actual sounding; whether the blower survives; which actor is the horn's master under its claimed rule; a dragon's observable response; and ordinary alternatives to that response. The three dragons are not co-located in a single known accessible place at ADWD's end: Drogon carries Daenerys into the Dothraki sea, while Rhaegal and Viserion are loose around Meereen. This makes a one-step seizure claim especially unsafe.

### 4. Euron learning Meereen's result—or Victarion learning Euron's western result

The brothers cannot react to each other merely because readers see both POVs. Their next reciprocal contact needs a surviving carrier, ship, raven, claimed glass-candle message, or later face-to-face encounter; then receipt, credibility, and a new decision. Victarion's suppressed maester-raven channel is affirmative evidence of delay, not evidence that magical communication has already solved it.

## Competing interpretations requiring distinct tests

| Interpretation | Support | Serious alternative | Discriminator |
|---|---|---|---|
| Euron is personally directing an imminent Oldtown assault. | His western strategy and Euron-bannered wreck link his faction to the threat. | Captains can act on prior orders while Euron is elsewhere; local damage does not locate him. | Named contemporaneous sighting, order, or battle POV. |
| Aeron is Euron's captive. | He disappears after openly opposing Euron, and the regime has punished dissent. | He may be travelling, concealed, killed, or held by another actor; no endpoint scene resolves it. | Direct custody/location evidence or a reliable named witness. |
| Victarion will faithfully fulfill Euron's commission. | He accepted it and carries the horn. | His internal purpose is to claim Daenerys for himself, and he has severed routine reports. | His first post-contact decision in Meereen, not his earlier oath. |
| Dragonbinder makes Euron the eventual dragon master at a distance. | Euron's pitch and Moqorro's blood-rule claim invite it. | Neither asserted mechanism shows remote control, and both men may misunderstand or lie. | A depicted sounder, response, and durable command relation. |

## Forecasting constraint

The book-supported forecast is a sequence of **separate first contacts**: western defenders must identify a real ironborn threat; Victarion must survive and negotiate an entry into Meereen's crisis; the horn requires a demonstrated test; and any news crossing between these theaters requires an actual channel. Aeron's absence is a live unknown, not permission to assign him to an Euron ritual, an Oldtown scene, or a Meereen message chain.

Independent challenge remains open.
