## Why it exists

Maintain a **living, auditable library** of the strongest, most interesting and best-researched *A Song of Ice and Fire* fan theories. Include annual r/asoiaf award nominees, historically influential analyses, and serious rebuttals. The owner cannot always access Reddit, so **every detailed case must stand alone**: summarize the claim, evidence, objections, alternate explanations, and consequences without requiring the reader to open the external source.

**Start:** [sortable-by-reading-priority catalog](CATALOG.md) · [awards archive](awards/README.md) · [case template and standards](METHOD.md).

## Admission and status

The catalog may contain a theory before its full case file is written. Every candidate states `QUEUED`, `SOURCE AUDIT`, `REVIEWED`, `CHALLENGED` or `ADOPTED WORKING BRANCH`. A completed file requires the criteria in [METHOD](METHOD.md). Preserve `UNKNOWN`; don't make up quotations, source authors, publication years or author intent.

**No exhaustive-completion claim:** The archive is designed for systematic expansion across decades of discussion. Award lists provide *discovery*, not quality assurance.
