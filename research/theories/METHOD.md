# Evidence standard for fan-theory case files

- Record role: `PROCEDURE`
- Primary information class: `SKILL / PROCEDURE`
- Status: `ACTIVE / VERSION 1`
- Lifecycle: `ACTIVE`
- Parent: [Theory library](README.md)
- Source authority: [SOURCES.md](../../SOURCES.md)

## Intake

1. **Identity:** stable slug, descriptive title, originating author if verified, original URL, publication year if known, awards/nominations (category + year + result), other substantial versions and rebuttals. Mark `AUTHOR UNKNOWN` rather than guessing.
2. **Claim:** one testable sentence; distinguish narrow textual claim from proposed entire endgame.
3. **Mechanism:** a causal explanation; what choices/events would have to occur?
4. **Evidence ledger:** minimum two precise five-book locators when available, including a concise primary-language excerpt only if directly checked, paraphrase and a label. If a theory relies on *World of Ice & Fire*, *Fire & Blood* or unreleased chapters, label `SUPPLEMENTARY — NOT YET AUDITED` until checked.
5. **Counterevidence:** the strongest realistic alternative; avoid a straw man. Note uncorroborated character claims, unreliable narrators and chronology conflicts.
6. **Disposition:** `STRONG INFERENCE`, `PLAUSIBLE`, `OPEN / UNDERDETERMINED`, `WEAK`, or `REJECTED`. Confidence is about *claim support*, not worth as fiction.
7. **Story relevance:** concrete decision consequences for our continuation, with no forced ending.
8. **Review:** date, evidence gaps, next smallest audit and independent challenge status.

## Quotation and public repository limits

- User-supplied EPUBs are available as source material. Record **short, selective excerpt fragments** directly verified in the EPUB, normally a few words, with the book acronym and this bundle's chapter number. **No large passages, extracted scenes, scraped essays, or full chapters** in this public GitHub repository. The EPUB itself remains separately in `Source/` at the owner's request.
- A quote is not evidence of the theory's *inference* unless its causal link is explained. Reproducing an isolated poetic phrase can mislead.
- Prefer paraphrases of a scene plus exact locator for additional evidence. For prior critic/forum text, summarize in independent language and link the original.
- An award nomination is proof only of nomination, not a scholarly review or official endorsement by Martin.

## Scoring without false precision

- `TEXT`: close textual support and clear source reliability.
- `CAUSAL`: does the mechanism plausibly follow from actual character knowledge and incentives?
- `REBUTTAL`: major contrary evidence confronted, not ignored.
- `CONTINUITY`: time, setting, magic rules, geography.
- `PAYOFF`: usefulness for an organic final-two-books story.
- `NOVELTY`: meaningful explanatory power rather than hidden-identity accumulation.

Do **not** collapse these into a single unvalidated numerical confidence. Record qualitative assessment and why. Keep status and source certainty separately.

## Case template

```md
# THEORY-ID — descriptive claim
- Origin, URL, award status, source-review state
## Claim in one sentence
## Mechanism and strongest interpretation
## Evidence ledger
| Passage locator | Short verified excerpt or paraphrase | What it supports | Limitation |
## Serious challenges / alternative readings
## Story consequence if true
## Story consequence if false
## Disposition; reversal trigger; next source pass
```

## THINK → PLAN → CHECK

THINK: decompose claim, assumptions, best counterexample, relevant context. PLAN: shortest material source check, not exhaustive search by default. CHECK: update theory and any affected character file; do not automatically revise an ending to preserve a favored idea. Independent literary review is a separate future gate.
