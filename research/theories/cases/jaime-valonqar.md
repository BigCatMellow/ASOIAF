# Jaime, not Tyrion, may be the valonqar

- Origin: established prophecy theory, early authorship `UNKNOWN`.
- Status: `SOURCE AUDITED / PLAUSIBLE, NOT DETERMINED`
- Checked: AFFC 3, 36, 39; AFFC Jaime chapters and ASOS Jaime arc
- Related: [Jaime/Brienne/Stoneheart](../../characters/jaime-brienne-stoneheart.md)

## Claim

Maggy the Frog's prophecy says the *valonqar*, 'little brother,' will kill Cersei. Cersei assumes Tyrion. **Jaime was born after Cersei and could technically be her younger brother**, making him another candidate; their growing estrangement gives the prophecy emotional force.

## Passage ledger

- **AFFC 36 (Cersei):** “the valonqar shall wrap his hands about your pale white throat”. The actual prophecy does **not** name Tyrion or Jaime.
- **AFFC 39 (Cersei):** Cersei explicitly says “Tyrion is the valonqar”; this is **Cersei's interpretation**, not an independent confirmation.
- **AFFC 3 (Cersei):** Cersei visualizes Tyrion murdering her, showing that her fear shapes how she reads the future.

## Challenges

`Valonqar` might name another literal younger brother; the grammar may admit other familial relations. Jaime's missing sword hand complicates, but does not logically prohibit, the prophesied strangling. More importantly, **a prophetic image does not guarantee literal fulfillment**, and Cersei might create the very estrangement she fears.

## Gardening payoff

The best story is not 'Jaime kills Cersei on schedule'. It is a self-fulfilling conflict between Cersei's fear, Jaime's promises and his moral limits. Keep alternative readings in play.

**Disposition:** `PLAUSIBLE / NO DETERMINISTIC PROPHECY OUTCOME`.
