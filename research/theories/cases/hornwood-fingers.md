# Ramsay may have flayed Lady Hornwood's fingers

- Nomination: [2025 Old Nan, u/ducknerd2002](https://www.reddit.com/r/asoiaf/comments/1pgfm2s/)
- Status: `SOURCE AUDITED / PLAUSIBLE SMALL-SCALE REVISION`
- Checked: ACOK 35; ADWD 12, 19, 29, 41

## Claim

The familiar account that Lady Hornwood chewed off her fingers due solely to starvation may be incomplete. Ramsay uses torture of the fingers to induce his victims to beg for mutilation. He may have similarly flayed Hornwood's fingers before she bit them off.

## Passage comparisons

| Locator | Short excerpt | Evidential weight |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **ADWD 29 (Davos)** | “It is said she ate her own fingers” | This is an **in-story rumor**, not direct observation of the cause. |
| **ADWD 41 (Theon)** | “He will flay me inch by inch.” | Theon knows Ramsay's method and fears further removal of fingers. |
| **ADWD 19 (Davos)** | Ramsay “made her eat her fingers.” | A northern speaker attributes purposeful cruelty to Ramsay, not just neglect. |

## Objections

Hunger and captivity alone might cause desperate self-injury. The precise details of Lady Hornwood's confinement were not shown in her own POV. Ramsay's later behavior makes the method credible but does not prove the exact event.

## Fictional significance

Ramsay's crimes are often visible only in damaged survivors and secondhand testimony. Rather than invent a new secret, this theory points to an existing **reliability problem of historical reporting**.

**Disposition:** `PLAUSIBLE, NOT VERIFIED`; no direct endgame consequence necessary.
