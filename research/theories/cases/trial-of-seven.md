# Cersei's trial could become a Trial of Seven

- Nomination: [2025 Beric Dondarrion, u/InGenNateKenny](https://www.reddit.com/r/asoiaf/comments/1n5p51f/)
- Status: `FIVE-BOOK ANCHORS AUDITED / KEY PRECEDENT OUTSIDE SCOPE`
- Needed supplementary source: *The Hedge Knight* (not yet provided/independently checked for this project)
- Checked: AFFC Cersei/Faith developments; ADWD 65 and Epilogue

## Claim

Instead of one champion versus another, the Faith may require a **seven-on-seven trial by combat**. Robert Strong would remain formidable but would no longer guarantee Cersei's victory if her teammates were overmatched or absent.

## Evidence and limitation

- **AFFC Cersei chapters / ADWD 65 (Cersei):** Cersei's position deteriorates under the Faith's authority and the High Sparrow's religious demands. This supports institutional conflict, **not** a specific judicial format.
- **ADWD Epilogue (Kevan):** Kevan and Mace Tyrell discuss the critical importance of Ser Robert Strong prevailing; trial outcome affects the legitimacy of Cersei's children.
- **Historical procedure:** Trial of Seven is established in *The Hedge Knight*, **but that text is not part of the current five-novel bundle**. This is a flagged external-source dependency; no forged book-five passage is offered for it.

## Strong challenges

The Faith has not announced a Trial of Seven in the five-book ending. The High Sparrow could accept an ordinary combat. Cersei might secure other champions. Even the exact operational rules and number of accusers require verification.

## Consequence

This may become a powerful institutional obstacle for a queen who believes resurrected Gregor solves every problem. Keep the exact trial conditional on the Faith leadership's reasons and legal precedent.
