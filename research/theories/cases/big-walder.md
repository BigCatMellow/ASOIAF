# Did Big Walder kill Little Walder Frey?

- 2024 work: [u/InGenNateKenny, Big Walder / Trojan Horse at Winterfell](https://www.reddit.com/r/asoiaf/comments/1ibazan/)
- Status: `SOURCE AUDITED / PLAUSIBLE, NOT SOLVED`
- Main source: ACOK 4; ADWD 32, 51

## Claim

Little Walder's corpse may be the work of his cousin Big Walder, rather than Manderly's men or Abel's disguised spearwives. The younger Frey's changing relationship with Ramsay and inheritance politics give Big Walder a conceivable motive, and his bloody appearance is potentially suspicious.

## Verified anchors

- **ACOK 4 (Bran):** The cousins' nicknames invert their actual sizes, foreshadowing mistaken expectations of the pair.
- **ADWD 32 (Reek):** “Little Walder had become Lord Ramsay's best boy” while Big Walder kept more distance from cruelty.
- **ADWD 51 (Theon):** Big Walder enters “spattered with blood” and says he found Little Walder beneath a ruined keep; he claims he warned his cousin against going alone.

## What must not be inferred

Blood may come from carrying the victim rather than striking the fatal blow. Big Walder's warning is his account, not an independently proved timeline. Theon doesn't see the murder. Hosteen accuses Wyman, but **that accusation is not a witness statement to the killing**.

## Narrative consequence

The death *already* sparks Frey–Manderly violence and Roose's outward sortie. Its murderer need not be revealed immediately. A credible investigation could implicate several internal factions and show that the Bolton alliance is destroying itself.
