## Three separate propositions

1. **Verified historical planning:** Doran and Oberyn backed a secret Viserys–Arianne betrothal and later dispatched Quentyn to solicit Daenerys. Doran avows revenge for Elia and her children. His activities are not simply passivity.
2. **Political inference:** Doran simultaneously manages tensions with House Yronwood, attempts to prevent a disastrous war with the Iron Throne and shields his plans from politically active Arianne. His secrecy creates consequences.
3. **Grand conspiracy:** Quentyn was *intentionally sent to fail*, hidden networks coordinated his downfall, or Doran has a flawless scheme for Aegon/Daenerys/dragons. **No such proof in the five novels.**

## Strongest counterargument to the grand version

If Doran truly meant Quentyn to fail, the theorist must explain why he chose his legitimate son, why his private instructions and Quentyn's POV consistently frame genuine marriage success as the mission, and how he could anticipate Daenerys's unpredictable circumstances. The **under-resourced expedition** can be explained by secrecy, slaver-era travel risk, and Doran's limited reach.

Arianne also has a real grievance: she remained ignorant long enough to initiate a reckless coup that mutilates Myrcella. Doran's caution is not harmless, even if it prevents immediate war.

## If true / if false

- **Grand plan true:** Doran may be hiding a larger alignment with Aegon or other claimants, but the mechanism must be found in actual communications and witnesses. Avoid secret agents invented solely to validate the theory.
- **Grand plan false:** Dorne still faces an organic conflict between Arianne's autonomy, Quentyn's fate, Yronwood's standing, Ellaria's anti-vengeance counsel and the new Aegon opportunity.

## Gardening disposition

`ADOPT` only the limited, text-supported premise that Doran's **multiple contingent schemes have become mutually destabilizing**. `KEEP OPEN` hidden additional plans. `DO NOT ADOPT` “Quentyn sent to die” or predicted Dornish civil war without further evidence.
