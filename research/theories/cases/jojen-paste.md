# Jojen Paste — did Bran unknowingly consume Jojen?

- Origin: longstanding disturbing theory; earliest proposer `UNVERIFIED`
- Status: `SOURCE AUDITED / WEAK-TO-OPEN`
- Checked: ACOK 28, 35; ADWD 13, 34

## Claim

Jojen's expected death and depressed demeanor, combined with the mysterious reddish weirwood paste Bran consumes under Bloodraven's instruction, may imply that Jojen was sacrificed and incorporated into the substance.

## Book evidence

- **ADWD 34 (Bran):** “The red veins were only weirwood sap, he supposed” — Bran observes reddish veins that remind him of blood, but supplies a natural in-world explanation.
- **ADWD 34 (Bran):** Jojen is described as sadder and haunted. The chapter includes his belief he will not leave the cave.
- **ACOK 35 (Bran):** Jojen's green dreams produce frightening predictions, but are not mechanically perfect explanations of how events happen.

## Weak links

**No direct scene shows Jojen killed, absent because of sacrifice, or his remains incorporated into food.** A character's grim knowledge of his destiny doesn't specify *this* fate. Pasting together 'red' and 'sad' is vulnerable to evocative association instead of causality.

## Story relevance

The valuable conflict is Bran's increasing dependence on powers he can't audit, and the ethical limits of using others' lives. A hidden cannibal sacrifice should not be added merely to make the cave shocking.

**Disposition:** `SPECULATIVE; DO NOT ADOPT WITHOUT MORE EVIDENCE`.
