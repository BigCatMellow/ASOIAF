# Is the Oldtown alchemist Jaqen H'ghar?

- Origin: widely established textual recognition theory; first proposer unknown
- Status: `SOURCE AUDITED (PARTIAL) / STRONG IDENTITY POSSIBILITY`
- Sources: ACOK 47–64 (Arya, Jaqen's changing appearance); AFFC Prologue, AFFC 45

## Claim

The mysterious alchemist who pays Pate with a gold dragon for the Citadel's master key, seemingly kills him, and disappears may be the Faceless Man formerly calling himself Jaqen H'ghar. Later a person called Pate appears alive and potentially replaced by someone else.

## Primary evidence

- **AFFC Prologue (Pate):** The stranger declares “I can change iron into gold.” Pate receives the coin and later collapses after handing over the key.
- **ACOK 47–64 (Arya):** Jaqen reveals a capacity to change appearance, and Arya watches the face-changing transformation before he leaves her.
- **AFFC 45 (Samwell):** Pate the Citadel acolyte is present again despite the earlier apparent death. His precise identity is not explained.

## Serious counterargument

The alchemist's resemblance to Jaqen's final appearance is a clue, not direct confirmation; a second Faceless Man could use similar disguises. Pate could survive an uncertain death scene, or a third party could impersonate him. We have not fully audited all facial description variants.

## Continuation use

A possible spy and potential threat to forbidden knowledge in Oldtown; whether the Faceless Men oppose dragons or serve a particular client is **unproven**.

**Disposition:** `STRONG POSSIBILITY / OPERATOR AND MISSION UNKNOWN`.
