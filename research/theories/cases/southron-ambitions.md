# Rickard Stark's Southron Ambitions

- Historical analysis: [Tower of the Hand, Southron Ambitions](https://towerofthehand.com/blog/2012/01/05-southron-ambitions/)
- Originator: `UNVERIFIED`; multiple prior variations
- Status: `SOURCE AUDITED / PLAUSIBLE HISTORICAL POLITICS`
- Checked: ADWD 41; AGOT early Eddard/Catelyn memories, with specific primary anchor below

## Claim

Before Robert's Rebellion, great houses formed politically unusual cross-regional matches, friendships and fosterages. Rickard Stark, Jon Arryn, Hoster Tully and the Baratheons may have built a counterweight to Aerys II's crown—possibly with no single agreed goal about replacing him.

## Verified passage

**ADWD 41 (The Turncloak / Theon):** Lady Barbrey Dustin recalls “Southron ambitions that would not be served by having his heir marry” a local northern lady. She attributes Rickard's Brandon–Catelyn match to politics beyond the North.

**What the quote proves:** Lady Dustin believes Rickard deliberately preferred a southern alliance. **What it doesn't prove:** a secret signed conspiracy, agreed deposition of Aerys, or Rhaegar's participation. Lady Dustin resents the match and has her own motives for retelling the past.

## Support in established alliances

Brandon–Catelyn, Ned–Robert's fostering with Jon Arryn, and Lyanna–Robert's betrothal create a recognizable web of major houses. They can be modeled as marriage diplomacy and security coordination *without* assuming all parties knew of one eventual rebellion.

## Rival readings

- Normal elite family alliances may be enough to explain the patterns.
- Rickard may have sought influence at court, not regime change.
- Lady Dustin has biased personal knowledge; she is still useful as a witness, not an objective historian.

## Continuation use

Treat past 'southron ambitions' as a **history of alliances misunderstood by descendants**. Useful for Sansa, northern succession, Dorne and the contrast between political contingency and heroic origin myths.
