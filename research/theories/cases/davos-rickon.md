# Davos might find Rickon but refuse to deliver him

- Nominee: [2025 Beric Dondarrion, u/CautionersTale](https://www.reddit.com/r/asoiaf/comments/1je75hr/)
- Status: `SOURCE AUDITED / STRONG MORAL QUESTION, PREDICTION OPEN`
- Checked: ASOS 63 (Davos), ADWD 29 (Davos)
- Character sheet: [Davos](../../characters/davos-seaworth.md)

## Claim

A father who once defied a king to save Edric Storm might decide it is wrong to deliver traumatized Rickon Stark to a political faction whose plan requires his name and body.

## Evidence

- **ASOS 63 (Davos):** Davos engineers Edric Storm's escape to prevent sacrificing an innocent boy.
- **ADWD 29 (Davos):** Wyman Manderly asks Davos to find Rickon as a condition of supporting Stannis; Davos is suitable because he's a smuggler.
- **ADWD 29:** The destination comes from Wex's account; **no direct scene establishes Rickon's treatment, consent, or conditions on Skagos**.

## Serious challenge

Edric's threatened ritual murder and Rickon's potential lawful restoration are *not the same moral situation*. Returning Rickon might actually save his life and reunite his remaining family. Davos's convictions do not mechanically compel him to lie to Manderly.

## Good continuation use

Make Davos encounter the child, guardian and actual risks **before** choosing his course. His options might include safe return, conditional protection, a temporary delay, or refusal.

**Disposition:** `PLAUSIBLE DECISION CONFLICT; FATE OPEN`.
