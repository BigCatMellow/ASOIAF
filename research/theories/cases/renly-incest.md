# Renly already knew Cersei's children were not Robert's

- Source theory: [u/Augustus_Chevismo](https://www.reddit.com/r/asoiaf/comments/1prb0vw/)
- Recognition: **2025 Beric Dondarrion Award winner** (revived older theory)
- Status: `SOURCE AUDITED / PLAUSIBLE POLITICAL INFERENCE`
- Checked: ACOK 31; ASOS 6; AFFC 24

## Main argument

Renly's pre-death plan to marry Margaery Tyrell to Robert is politically more coherent **if he already suspects the children are illegitimate**. Simply replacing Cersei leaves Joffrey and Tommen ahead of any potential Tyrell child. Knowledge of Jaime's incest may therefore have shaped his earlier ambitions.

## Short primary anchors

| Locator | Verified excerpt / observation | Implication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **ACOK 31 (Catelyn)** | Renly: “A year ago I was scheming to make the girl Robert's queen.” | The pre-rebellion marriage plan is expressly admitted by Renly. |
| **ASOS 6 (Sansa)** | Olenna notes Robert had two sons and Renly had an older brother. | Tyrells knew Renly's claims were not legally straightforward. |
| **AFFC 24 (Cersei)** | Cersei remembers “Renly's mockery.” | A hostile relationship is observed but is not proof he knew about incest. |

## Counterevidence

Renly could have planned to have Robert set Cersei aside, disinherit her children, or simply marry for Tyrell influence, without ever knowing the secret. Nor does the text show Renly explicitly stating the incest before Robert's death. If he *did* know, his refusal to promote Stannis's succession after Robert's death is understandable as self-interest but not proven motivation.

## Continuation value

A useful precedent for actors keeping accurate information secret because revealing it would benefit a rival. Compare Aegon's contested claim and Manderly's hidden Rickon intelligence.

**Disposition:** `PLAUSIBLE; KNOWLEDGE UNVERIFIED`.
