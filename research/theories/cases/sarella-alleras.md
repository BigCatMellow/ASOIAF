# Is the Citadel's Alleras really Sarella Sand?

- Origin: broadly established reader inference; first author unverified.
- Status: `SOURCE AUDITED / STRONG INFERENCE`
- Checked: AFFC Prologue, 2, 21 and 45
- Related: [Oldtown and Dorne future-source work](../../../ROADMAP.md)

## Claim

Alleras the Sphinx, a quick-learning acolyte at Oldtown, is likely Sarella Sand, Oberyn Martell's inquisitive daughter, living under a reversed name and a male presentation.

## Evidence ledger

| Chapter | Short exact excerpt | Inference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **AFFC Prologue** | “his father was a Dornishman, his mother a black-skinned Summer Islander.” | Fits the known parentage of Sarella; an identity match requires more than ancestry. |
| **AFFC 2 (The Captain of Guards)** | “Leave her to her … game.” | Doran indicates that Sarella is pursuing something unusual rather than imprisoned with her sisters. |
| **AFFC 21 (The Queenmaker)** | “wanted to know everything there was to know” | Arianne recalls Sarella's curiosity about ancient ruins, fitting Citadel study. |
| **AFFC 45 (Samwell)** | “The sphinx is the riddle, not the riddler.” | Aemon's message lends possible significance to Alleras but its referent is not guaranteed. |

## Why it is persuasive

`Alleras` is `Sarella` reversed; family, complexion, youth, intellectual interests and Doran's evasive 'game' converge. Different books supply independent-looking details. No witness openly unmasks her, however.

## Counterevidence / open question

A different Dornish/Summer Islander child could share traits; the reverse name is suggestive, not an in-story test. More important to the future is *why* Sarella is there—her information mission and loyalties are not specified.

## Gardening relevance

Sam's discovery of an unexpected Dornish connection in Oldtown may tie Dorne, the Citadel and ancient history without a contrived spy-master reveal.

**Disposition:** `STRONG INFERENCE / PURPOSE UNKNOWN`.
