# Bloodraven and the Three-Eyed Crow could be different entities

- Contemporary development: [2026 discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/asoiaf/comments/1rnddsa/)
- Origin: older theory; first proposer not identified
- Status: `SOURCE AUDITED / OPEN`
- Checked: ACOK 16; ASOS 56; ADWD 13, 34

## Claim

Bran's dream-guide appears as a three-eyed crow, while Brynden Rivers / Bloodraven is linked more strongly to weirwood imagery. **Bran's identification** of the cave greenseer as the crow might be mistaken; another influence may guide or manipulate Bran.

## Source anchors

| Locator | Short excerpt | Supports / fails to support |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **ACOK 16 (Bran)** | “Fly or die!” | A crow visibly commands Bran in a dream; its ontological nature isn't explained. |
| **ASOS 56 (Bran)** | “Are you the three-eyed crow?” | Bran previously asks another guide for identification; he can misinterpret images. |
| **ADWD 13 (Bran)** | “A three-eyed crow should have three eyes.” | Bran questions whether the one-eyed cave figure fits the crow dream. |
| **ADWD 34 (Bran)** | Weirwood and greenseer memories occupy Bloodraven's cave instruction. | Different imagery need not denote different people. |

## Counterevidence

Dream entities may use forms not resembling their bodies. Bloodraven's discomfort or confusion about the label could be unfamiliarity with Bran's imagery, not evidence of a rival supernatural actor. 'Crow' already evokes Night's Watch identity.

## Continuation relevance

Keep alive the risk of Bran trusting an interpretation supplied by dreams, but **do not invent an additional omnipotent puppeteer** merely to make a twist. The strongest conflict is Bran's consent, agency and use of Hodor.

**Disposition:** `UNDERDETERMINED; NO NEW ENTITY ADOPTED`.
