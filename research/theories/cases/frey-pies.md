# Frey Pies — Wyman Manderly's covert revenge

- Origin: long-running community reading of Winterfell's wedding feast; first author `UNKNOWN`
- Status: `SOURCE AUDITED / STRONG SUSPICION, NO DIRECT RECIPE PROOF`
- Evidence: ADWD 29, 37, 41, 46

## Hypothesis

Wyman Manderly killed three Freys who left White Harbor under the protection of guest right and used their remains to prepare pies eaten at Ramsay's wedding. The theory interprets his strange hospitality and the Rat Cook song as deliberate retaliation for the Red Wedding.

## Verified textual anchors

- **ADWD 29 (Davos):** Wyman speaks of the three Freys and notes the departure custom of guest gifts. His earlier apparent collaboration is political cover.
- **ADWD 37 (The Prince of Winterfell):** The wedding feast includes three large meat pies provided by Wyman; he eats six portions. The Rat Cook story is invoked, making food and violated hospitality unusually conspicuous.
- **ADWD 46 (The Ghost in Winterfell):** Wyman openly taunts Freys about the missing/late visitors and Wendel's death, intensifying the feud.

## Alternative readings

The men might have been killed elsewhere by unrelated raiders or Bolton enemies; pie ingredients are never tested or identified by a witness. Wyman's overindulgence might be theatrical provocation rather than confession. Later violence and his loyalty to the Starks do not prove the exact recipe.

## Story implications

This is not merely a revenge twist: it shows that northern resistance can be as cruel and dishonorable as its enemies, potentially undermining a neat division into 'good' northerners and 'evil' Freys.
