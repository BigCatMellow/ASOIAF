# The Grand Northern Conspiracy — one movement or competing conspiracies?

- Historical intake: [early collective discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/asoiaf/comments/1iwfc1/), [compiled historical reading](https://gaelicstark.wordpress.com/2018/07/20/the-grand-northern-conspiracy/)
- Recognition: widely discussed community theory; not proof of a unified secret organization
- Status: `SOURCE AUDITED / STRONG NARROW CORE; GRAND VERSION UNPROVEN`
- Checked: ADWD 29, 41, 46, 51, 62; ASOS 20

## Claim and useful decomposition

**Strong narrow claim:** Many northern houses privately resist Bolton rule and prefer Stark restoration. **Much stronger 'grand' claim:** They coordinate as one hidden alliance—with Manderly, the mountain clans, Umbers, Mormonts, Glover, perhaps Robb's will and Jon's claim—all sharing plans in real time. The second does **not** follow automatically from the first.

## Passage evidence

| Source | Short verified excerpt | Textual significance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **ASOS 20 (Catelyn)** | “The north remembers.” | Robb explicitly recognizes durable family memory and feudal retaliation. |
| **ADWD 29 (Davos)** | “The north remembers” | Wyman's private explanation to Davos ties his public loyalty to hostage leverage and a plan to recover Rickon. |
| **ADWD 46 (The Ghost in Winterfell)** | “The north remembers, Frey.” | Lady Dustin invokes the same idiom, but her purposes are not identical to Wyman's. |
| **ADWD 51 (Theon)** | Frey/Manderly infighting leads Roose to order separate sorties. | Hatred is demonstrated; actual coordination with Stannis isn't. |

## Counterevidence

Wyman's alliance with Stannis is **conditional** on Davos returning a Stark boy (ADWD 29). Manderly's troops may not know Wyman's full plan, and Arnolf Karstark demonstrates that some northern lords actively betray the anti-Bolton cause (ADWD 44, 62). Hostage constraints differ by house. Not every anti-Frey action serves the same prospective heir.

## Paths for a character-led continuation

A Stark restoration can emerge from **multiple partially coordinated plots**, whose leaders disagree about Rickon, Jon, Sansa or northern independence. That is richer—and more faithful to the novels' limited knowledge—than an invisible committee controlling every murder and battle.

**Disposition:** `ADOPT NARROW FACT OF RESISTANCE; KEEP GRAND COORDINATION OPEN`.
