# Sandor Clegane may be the Quiet Isle gravedigger

- Origin: long-standing fan identification; first proposer unverified.
- Status: `SOURCE AUDITED / STRONG INFERENCE`
- Checked: ASOS 74; AFFC 31
- Also relevant: [Jaime/Brienne/Stoneheart](../../characters/jaime-brienne-stoneheart.md)

## Claim

The enormous limping gravedigger doing menial work on the Quiet Isle is Sandor Clegane, physically alive but renouncing the violent public identity of 'the Hound.'

## Passage ledger

| Locator | Short excerpt | Interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **ASOS 74 (Arya)** | Arya leaves Sandor grievously wounded and refuses to kill him. | His death is not narrated on-page; survival is feasible though uncertain. |
| **AFFC 31 (Brienne)** | “a brother bigger than Brienne was struggling to dig a grave.” | Stature fits Sandor, though not exclusive. |
| **AFFC 31 (Brienne)** | “it was plain to see that he was lame.” | Fits Sandor's injured leg. |
| **AFFC 31 (Brienne)** | Elder Brother distinguishes 'the Hound' as dead from the possible fate of Sandor the man. | Allows symbolic death rather than proof of a corpse. |

## Serious alternative

Sandor could truly be dead and the gravedigger simply a similarly built crippled man. The Quiet Isle brotherhood includes other wounded war refugees. Size and lameness are not identifying fingerprints.

## Consequence for the continuation

The theory is stronger as a **moral identity arc** than a setup for a spectacular 'Cleganebowl.' Sandor's choice to work and bury the dead might matter more than another duel. The Quiet Isle remains vulnerable if vengeance-driven actors arrive.

**Disposition:** `STRONG INFERENCE / FUTURE ROLE OPEN`.
