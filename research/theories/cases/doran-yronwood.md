# Doran's real achievement may be preventing Dornish civil war

- Influential 2025 analysis: [u/YezenIRL, Doran Is Actually Smart and Quentyn Is Important](https://www.reddit.com/r/asoiaf/comments/1m88m49/)
- Status: `SOURCE AUDITED / STRONG POLITICAL MOTIVE, FUTURE WAR UNPROVEN`
- Main sources: AFFC 13, 21, 40; ADWD 60 (Quentyn's future; not every Quentyn chapter reviewed)

## Core theory

Doran's decision to foster Quentyn with House Yronwood is not simply education or weak indecision: it settles a dangerous feud after Oberyn killed Edgar Yronwood. The 'blood debt' makes Quentyn a political bond between powerful houses. Doran's overseas marriage plans may simultaneously strengthen Dorne and unsettle his carefully maintained local balance.

## Verified book passages

- **AFFC 21 (The Queenmaker, Arianne):** “there is a blood debt, and Quentyn is the only coin Lord Ormond” — Arianne recalls Doran's explicit reasoning for sending Quentyn away.
- **AFFC 13 (The Soiled Knight, Arys):** Quentyn was fostered, trained and knighted by the Yronwoods, not simply kept there briefly.
- **AFFC 40 (The Princess in the Tower, Arianne):** Anders Yronwood is regarded as among the most powerful Dornish lords; Arianne doubts she can enlist him as an ally.

## Likely stakes, unproven next steps

Arianne's importance and Quentyn's death can destabilize the agreement, especially if Yronwood's family expected a future Dorne under Quentyn. That supports **risk of civil disagreement**. It doesn't show an agreed coup, an armed succession war, or Darkstar declaring a new kingdom.

## Objections

The Yronwoods might accept Arianne despite past resentments; Doran might have provided assurances not narrated; Quentyn's fosterage may have made durable kinship, not merely hostage coercion. Doran's intended Targaryen marriage could also make Arianne queen, not automatically leave Dorne for Quentyn.

## Value to our two-book gardening

Dorne should not march as a unit simply because Doran secretly 'planned everything.' Its houses can have loyal but divergent incentives, especially after the failure of overseas marriages.

**Disposition:** `STRONG INFERENCE: fostering served blood-debt stability; OPEN: Dornish civil war`.
