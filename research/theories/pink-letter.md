## What is directly shown

**ADWD 69 (Jon):** Clydas brings Jon a message, addressed with an insult and sealed with pink wax, signed Ramsay Bolton. The text alleges Stannis is dead and Ramsay has his magic sword; alleges Mance is captive and six spearwives were flayed; demands Ramsay's bride, Reek, Selyse, Shireen, Melisandre, Val and Mance's supposed son. Jon reacts, discusses its possible falsehood with Tormund, announces he intends to ride south with volunteers, and is stabbed by Watchmen.

**VERIFIED:** Jon receives and reacts to a letter in Ramsay's name. **NOT VERIFIED:** the author is Ramsay or that any report in the letter is true.

## Why authorship remains unresolved

- **In favor of Ramsay:** Voice, cruelty, threats and knowledge of missing bride/Reek align with him. Some infiltration information could have been uncovered through surviving witnesses.
- **Against automatic certainty:** Pink wax and a signature can be imitated; some claims are not independently verifiable; the letter has a strong effect on Jon and the Watch.
- **Other possible source routes (hypotheses only):** Someone in Winterfell with partial knowledge; someone who obtained Mance's secrets through capture; a third party who wants Jon to march; deliberate misinformation from Ramsay himself.
- **Weak evidence to avoid overstating:** A comparison with a wet message Roose receives in ADWD 51 does **not** prove the Pink Letter was dry or originated at Castle Black. An omitted description cannot localize a raven.

## The consequential truth

Even if every military claim in the message is false, the **fallout at Castle Black is real**. Jon changes his plan, addresses the wildlings, explicitly says the Watch will go to Hardhome and he will ride south, observes Marsh and Yarwyck leave the meeting, and is attacked amid Wun Wun's violence (ADWD 69).

The story can move forward from **Jon's decision and its consequences** while leaving authorship unknown.

## THINK counterexample

A theorist may claim "Ramsay wouldn't know about Mance, so the letter must be fake." Yet Ramsay has already hosted the disguised Mance and could discover his identity through a captured participant. Conversely, "Only Ramsay would say Reek" is not conclusive: the name is known in Winterfell. Neither claim settles provenance without a chain of knowledge.

## PLAN: discriminating evidence

1. Track who encounters Abel/Mance and his companions in ADWD 37/46/51, and who survives the escape.
2. Establish the time interval required for any source to learn of Jeyne/Theon's location and send a raven.
3. Compare the letter to established Ramsay communications in all five novels, separating style inference from witness evidence.
4. Assess what Watch officers knew about Jon's secret mission and what motive they had **before** the letter arrived.

**Disposition:** `KEEP OPEN`. Do not select a letter author merely to enable a desired resurrection or Bolton battle outcome.
