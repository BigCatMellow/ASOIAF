## Priority rule

1. Record chronological cause/effect and major scene action before promoting any hidden-clue theory.
2. Prioritize a clue when it materially changes a character's plausible choices or can disconfirm multiple competing hypotheses.
3. Retain disputed details and discarded hypotheses with specific locators; avoid endless symbolism without a causal test.
4. When confirmed by a later chapter, update the canonical [character sheet](characters/README.md) or [theory case](theories/README.md), then update this index's reading priority.

This queue is **not** a ranked list of true fan theories or a completed series-wide clue census. It currently draws on the twenty-nine first-pass chapters listed in the coverage tracker.
