## 1. A five-way case study in how to force somebody to 'choose'

| POV | What they demand | Leverage | Status at chapter end |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Jaime / Edmure** | Edmure yield Riverrun without general assault | Legitimate Tully authority, comfort and Roslin access **combined with an explicit threat of sacking the castle and hurling newborn child from trebuchet** | Threat directly made; Edmure's compliance **not yet shown** in Jaime POV |
| **Cersei / Wat and Osney** | Testimony supporting Margaery's treason | **On-page torture, eye and nipple mutilation, promised Watch reprieve and royal/sexual patronage** | Blue Bard has been coerced into a tailored confession; Osney still has **not** confessed to High Septon in source |
| **Doran / Arianne** | Arianne stop plan against crown ward, join concealed strategy | Isolation, silent servants, protection or exile of accomplices, promise of inheritance and revelation of pact | Arianne learns of **Viserys marriage pact** and Quentyn's mission, no mission success |
| **Petyr / Sansa (Alayne)** | Alayne wed Harrold and ultimately reveal Stark identity | Waynwood debt purchases, rich dower, Sansa's fear, emotionally inappropriate kisses and envisioned Vale restoration | **Contingent betrothal** requiring Harry's consent, Sansa's widowhood and Robert Arryn's death |
| **Stoneheart / Brienne** | Brienne murder Jaime to prove loyalty | Oathkeeper, royal warrant, threatened execution of Brienne, Hyle and **Podrick** | Brienne refuses proposed binary and is hanged; **final shouted word unrevealed** |

**Causal contrast:** Jaime says he seeks to avoid mass death but constructs a terror threat. Cersei claims to discover truth yet chooses confession details according to military expediency. Doran's secrecy protects external diplomacy at the expense of family trust. Petyr treats marriage and mortality as moveable counters. Stoneheart's historical injury makes guilt-by-association overriding evidence. None of these mechanisms should be treated as morally equivalent in severity simply because they appear in a table.

## 2. The common vocabulary of vows, oaths, masks and nooses

- **Jaime** refuses a duel with Brynden while bound by Catelyn's oath, then bypasses the defender through Edmure and threatens atrocities, wondering how Genna's assertion that Tyrion is Tywin's true son looks now. Distinguish `not bearing arms personally` from broader complicity in force.
- **Cersei** uses deliberate sexual/treason perjury: Blue Bard's torture yields politically selected names, and Osney is told to make a knowingly false confession to the High Septon. An oath's legitimacy requires more than having a witness say the prescribed words.
- **Arianne** discovers Doran's secret promise to Viserys; Arys Oakheart's violated Kingsguard vow and death leave a second fresh wound in her conscience.
- **Alayne/Sansa** is legally still **Tyrion's wife**, even as Petyr promises her to Harry. A rehearsed false identity does not by itself erase a binding marriage.
- **Brienne** holds oaths to both Catelyn and Jaime. Stoneheart defines obedience solely as killing Jaime and converts refusing a false binary into capital guilt. The chapter ends with Brienne struggling to save Pod, not a demonstrated renunciation of her moral obligations.

## 4. Objects that change the interpretation of people

**The bronze circlet rimmed by iron swords:** Ryman Frey's woman wears the crown during Jaime's gallows intervention in **AFFC 38**; **Stoneheart is handling a very similar bronze-and-sword crown** in AFFC 42. This is more than generalized thematic resemblance; it is an unusual repeated object. Nevertheless neither the intermediate transfer nor Ryman's fate is witnessed in the batch. Priority: locate subsequent custody and all accounts of Ryman's travel.

**Sandor's hound helmet:** Formerly Sandor's; Elder Brother AFFC 31 says it marked the grave; AFFC 37 Brienne killed Rorge while he wore it; **AFFC 42 Lem wears it**. Brienne directly states Lem took it from Rorge's body; he admits taking possession without a protest from the dead. Thoros objects. An observer's identification of 'the Hound' can therefore correspond to **three distinct men** in the evidence chain. This makes physical artifacts and masks poor stand-ins for identity, and continues to leave Sandor's body and Quiet Isle gravedigger theory unresolved.

**Oathkeeper + Tommen warrant:** Both are authentic things made for an effort to help Sansa; both become incriminating *evidence* before Stoneheart, who imputes Jaime's Red Wedding complicity and Brienne's betrayal. The existence of a Lannister-made sword is not proof of intent to harm a Stark child.

**Arianne's onyx dragon and Petyr's contracts:** Doran's carved dragon signals a Targaryen-oriented justice mission; Petyr's Waynwood debt and Harry betrothal make a prospective Stark restoration an instrument of his control. Both are strategic *plans* contingent on other humans' choices, not guaranteed outcomes.

## 5. Major theory audits with source-grading

### Doran and the Targaryens
**HIGH confidence:** the secret groom who died from a pot of molten gold is **Viserys**, already killed in AGOT. Doran admits an actual concealed marriage scheme and that Quentyn travels abroad with five named/status-defined companions in the pursuit of Dornish justice. **INFERRED:** the immediate destination/counterparty is Daenerys, given the final dragon language and Viserys link. **NOT PROVED:** signed pact terms, dragon queen acceptance, success, or that Doran's precise scheme matches another character's prophecies. Connect to Aemon's [AFFC 35](../Source/Main-Series/04-A-Feast-for-Crows/chapters/35-samwell.md) appeal for help to Dany without claiming the two networks coordinated.

### Harry the Heir, Robert Arryn, and sweetsleep
**HIGH confidence:** Harrold Hardyng's maternal descent from **Alys Arryn's youngest surviving daughter** places him in the succession if Sweetrobin dies. Petyr calls it *when*, not *if*, and has acquired Lady Waynwood's debts as marital leverage. **CONFIRMED danger:** Colemon says sweetsleep remains in Robert's body and too-frequent dosing can kill; Alayne orders more doses after one three days previously. **UNKNOWN:** whether two new cups were administered, the actual total dose, whether any harm follows, and whether Petyr intends murder. Track objective symptoms such as nosebleeds and fits against the drug schedule, not mere suspicion.

### The 'unkiss' and altered memory
In AFFC 41 Sansa remembers Sandor as taking a kiss and leaving a bloody cloak on Blackwater night. This conflicts with prior textual reporting of the Blackwater encounter (the commonly researched **un-kiss** phenomenon). Require direct ACOK chapter quotation/line audit before deciding whether Sansa's memory is false, retroactively compressed, or the source supports a literal kiss. Do not invent that Sandor was physically present in the Vale.

### Stoneheart and lost Brotherhood ethics
**HIGH confidence:** Brienne directly sees Catelyn's mutilated face; Thoros says Beric transferred life into Catelyn's three-day-old corpse. ASOS epilogue independently features the Stoneheart persona directing killings, but **the mechanism and moral-personality continuity remain speculative**. A charitable network shelters crossroads orphans, and Long Jeyne treats Brienne's wound, even as Lem and Stoneheart now execute alleged collaborators with no fair hearing. Thoros mourns the deterioration: a deliberate institutional contradiction.

### The crown and the killings
**MEDIUM-HIGH connection:** crown recurrence in AFFC 38/42 is specific, but exact physical identity should be checked with description and intervening ownership. The salted hanging victims near crossroads vs Jaime's crabapple-filled corpses may be linked to the Brotherhood's evolving punitive practices, but no direct witness assigns every corpse and particular method to Stoneheart.

### Cersei's manufactured sexual case
**CONFIRMED conspiracy:** Cersei commissions an interrogation that produces false, tortured 'evidence', edits inconvenient names away, instructs Osney to perjure himself to the High Septon and recruits Taena to pressure Alla. **NO proof:** Margaery actually has lovers, took moon tea herself, or is guilty of incest with Loras. Pycelle's pressured disclosure about moon tea is a source lead but not proof of target recipient or purpose. Follow what the High Septon actually does independently of Cersei's expectations.
