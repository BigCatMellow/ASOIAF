## Executive conclusion — there is no recovered Duskendale letter

**[ACOK 64 (Arya)](../../Source/Main-Series/02-A-Clash-of-Kings/text/065-arya.md)** definitively shows **Roose Bolton personally ordering a communication to Ser Helman Tallhart**: Tallhart should execute the already surrendered Darry Lannister garrison, burn the castle, then join **Robett Glover** and advance **east toward Duskendale**. **Qyburn explicitly says he will prepare a message for Roose's seal**. Arya hears the dictation; **Steelshanks Walton is also in the room**.

The published text **does not separately show** (i) Qyburn putting pen to parchment, (ii) the finished written text, (iii) Roose sealing or signing it, (iv) a named courier leaving with it, (v) Tallhart physically receiving/read­ing it, (vi) a retained original or copy, or (vii) any later Northern/royal tribunal examining it. Troops do move toward Duskendale and suffer an independently reported disaster, so execution or transmission of **some** instructions is a **reasonable inference**, but the exact documentary chain is **not verified**.

**Other original evidence makes this missing message important without proving a secret tip-off:**
- **[ACOK 39 (Catelyn)](../../Source/Main-Series/02-A-Clash-of-Kings/text/040-catelyn.md):** **Edmure tells Catelyn he has *already sent word*** for Tallhart to bring the Twins garrison to **Roose at the ruby ford/crossroads**. That is a **different, earlier order**—join Roose, *not* attack Duskendale. Edmure's claim of dispatch has no acknowledged recipient in that passage. It is an antecedent to Tallhart's position, **not evidence Edmure devised Roose's later eastern attack**.
- **[ASOS 4 (Tyrion)](../../Source/Main-Series/03-A-Storm-of-Swords/text/005-tyrion.md):** **Tywin explicitly tells Tyrion** that he knows **Tallhart and Glover** are advancing toward Duskendale and **has sent Randyll Tarly against them**, with **Gregor Clegane cutting their retreat**. `DIRECT ENEMY COMMAND CLAIM`. This is real evidence that the enemy leadership knew the route *before* the defeat, but **no report identifies who told Tywin**. It could be scouts, local reports, prisoners, a loyal household, a raven, or some other route; **Qyburn as informant is only a theory**.
- **[ASOS 19 (Tyrion)](../../Source/Main-Series/03-A-Storm-of-Swords/text/020-tyrion.md):** **Varys announces** to the small council that Tarly crushed Glover's force, that **Helman Tallhart is reported killed**, and that Glover's survivors are withdrawing toward Gregor. `CHARACTER REPORT` of outcome, not a captured Northern order or its author.
- **[ASOS 35 (Catelyn)](../../Source/Main-Series/03-A-Storm-of-Swords/text/036-catelyn.md):** **Robb is shocked** to hear his foot went to Duskendale, has no explanation for the eastern objective, and blames himself for losing approximately **a third of his foot**. He seeks to exchange for **Robett Glover**, later captured near the kingsroad. `POV-OBSERVED REACTION`, not proof Robb had never authorized any other raid.
- **[ASOS 49 (Catelyn)](../../Source/Main-Series/03-A-Storm-of-Swords/text/050-catelyn.md):** **Roose** endorses Robb's condemnation of Glover and attributes the blunder to Glover's grief over Deepwood, **without mentioning Roose's own ACOK 64 order**. The juxtaposition is direct source-based evidence of **misattributed responsibility**, not proof Roose predicted Tarly's exact maneuver.

**Recommended evidence threshold:** `AUTHORSHIP SPOKEN / PREPARATION PROMISED / LATER BATTLE VERIFIED / ENEMY ROUTE KNOWLEDGE VERIFIED / TEXT OF LETTER UNKNOWN / TALLHART RECEIPT UNKNOWN / TIPOFF UNKNOWN`. Do **not** upgrade it to `WRITTEN WAR ORDER RECOVERED`, `QYBURN SENDS TYWIN A COPY`, or `ROBB SECRETLY AUTHORIZED THE PLAN`.

### The draft's two legally distinct orders

Roose gives **one message with two operative components**: (a) kill surrendered garrison and burn Darry; (b) reunite Tallhart with Glover, then raid toward Duskendale. The first is closely qualified by *the king's command*. The second is framed as revenge for Glover's lost castle and Tallhart's dead son. Treating both components as **certified Robb orders** is unsupported. Conversely, declaring Roose **knowingly forged Robb's name** is also unsupported without showing he lacked authorization. At minimum, **the person instructing Qyburn in that room is Roose**, not Robb or Glover.

### Focused follow-up: the eastward raid was already ordinary war news

The [Tywin intelligence provenance and reconnaissance audit](duskendale-tywin-field-intelligence-reconnaissance-provenance-audit.md) adds **AGOT 62 (Tyrion)** as a direct control: Tywin explicitly cites **Ser Addam Marbrand's outriders** to locate the advancing Stark army before the Green Fork. Crucially, **ASOS 4 (Tyrion)** has **Bronn** relay news of northerners burning toward Duskendale *before* Tyrion hears Tywin's more precise commander and counterdeployment account; the public description was therefore **not exclusively confined to a private enemy war letter** by the time Tyrion learned it. Bronn's **upstream source remains unknown**, so this is not proof of two independent military intelligence networks. **ASOS 19 (Tyrion)** has Varys report Tarly's victory from `yesterday at dawn`, again without naming his courier. **ACOK 64 (Arya)** expressly dates **Tothmure's birds to the night Harrenhal fell, preceding Roose's later Duskendale dictation in that chapter**—these particular ravens cannot already have carried the not-yet-spoken order. Finally, **ASOS 53 (Tyrion)** later establishes Tywin's active clandestine **Frey/Red Wedding** arrangement and compartmentalization; **this is real secret diplomacy but a distinct, later confirmed scheme**, not authenticated documentary evidence of an earlier Duskendale leak. The relative probabilities remain unquantified.

## 2. The enemy's timeline is a real source problem

**Tywin receives intelligence earlier than Robb's retrospective realization.** ASOS 4 has Tywin identify **both intended Northern field leaders** and the **Duskendale vector**, and send two wings to catch them; this predates Varys's report of the defeat in ASOS 19 and Robb's surprise in ASOS 35.

| Theory of how Tywin learned | Minimum evidence consistent with it | Source-based objection or unobserved step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Ordinary reconnaissance / local observers** | Two large Northern columns marching east into Lannister–Tyrell-controlled or contested Riverlands; an enemy commander with scouts has obvious ways to notice a march. | No particular scout, intercepted report or date of intelligence receipt is narrated. |
| **Cooperating locals or captives** | Tywin has previously used garrisons and his own informants in the Riverlands; his detailed ASOS 4 briefing could reflect conventional military reports. | Specific informant, location, and knowledge of a Northern sealed command are unshown. |
| **An intercepted Tallhart letter** | ACOK 64's incoming rider and planned outgoing order generate plausible documents or messenger routes. | No interception, retained letter, Lannister reader, or named courier is shown. |
| **Roose/Qyburn deliberately informs Tywin** | Roose ordered the destination, later omits his role, and Qyburn has experience with court intelligence. | No Roose–Tywin contact or Qyburn dispatch to the enemy is narrated. **Qyburn's observed move to King's Landing occurs later, with Jaime in ASOS 44**, so his later *physical presence at court* cannot explain Tywin already knowing in ASOS 4. A hypothetical earlier raven requires new evidence. |
| **Tywin deduces it without inside help** | From the physical direction of the marching army and commander names, he can position Tarly/Gregor without obtaining a private message. | Whether his ASOS 4 specificity exceeds plausible scouting is subjective; no proof of the timing of first sightings. |

**Another caution:** **Varys** relays the *battle outcome* in ASOS 19. This does **not** retroactively prove he personally supplied Tywin's *earlier* intelligence, nor can his later speech serve as independent confirmation of a letter from Roose.

**Structural significance:** the reader hears **Roose's actual directive**, **Tywin's target knowledge**, **Robb's bewilderment** and **Roose's subsequent blame shift** through separate POVs. They constitute an unusually effective *distributed-evidence puzzle* even without a single secret correspondence file. The missing recipient and intelligence source are worthwhile mysteries; neither can be filled by the existence of Qyburn.

## 3. Qyburn's later correspondence: direct proof he *can* get a letter delivered, not that he did earlier

### Actual movement between governments

- **[ASOS 44 (Jaime)](../../Source/Main-Series/03-A-Storm-of-Swords/text/045-jaime.md):** **Roose sends Qyburn with Jaime** to treat his damaged hand en route to **King's Landing**. Roose frames the appointment partly around Qyburn's hope to regain his Citadel chain via Lannister gratitude. **Steelshanks Walton** commands Jaime's escort. `OBSERVED APPOINTMENT AND JOURNEY`; not an observed spying pact.
- **[AFFC 3 (Cersei)](../../Source/Main-Series/04-A-Feast-for-Crows/text/004-cersei.md):** Qyburn is present at court and Cersei recognizes him as Jaime's healer. `POV-OBSERVED ARRIVAL`.
- **[AFFC 7 (Cersei)](../../Source/Main-Series/04-A-Feast-for-Crows/text/008-cersei.md):** Qyburn reports inquiries about the vanished gaoler Rugen, having inspected a hiding place and produced an unusual coin which Cersei interprets politically. `DIRECT REPORT` about discovered evidence, **not objective proof the coin's owner, provenance or implied Tyrell conspiracy**. Qyburn's material report directly influences Cersei's theories of treachery.
- **[AFFC 17 (Cersei)](../../Source/Main-Series/04-A-Feast-for-Crows/text/018-cersei.md):** Qyburn states he maintains **informants in several Westerosi ports and Free Cities**. Cersei uses him as an intelligence officer. `CHARACTER CLAIM / OBSERVED SERVICE`—the quality of every informer remains unverified.
- **[AFFC 24 (Cersei)](../../Source/Main-Series/04-A-Feast-for-Crows/text/025-cersei.md):** Qyburn **personally briefs** Cersei in her solar on foreign and Dornish reports. `POV-OBSERVED BRIEFING`. A **different later council scene in the same chapter** has **Grand Maester Pycelle** report Wyman Manderly's purported execution of Davos; **Cersei directs a raven promising Wylis Manderly's return**. **Do not falsely credit that report or the order's execution to Qyburn merely because he appears earlier in the same chapter.** [Wylis/Maidenpool custody](maidenpool-prisoner-handoffs-tarly-mooton-connington.md) owns the actual Wylis release endpoints.
- **[AFFC 43 (Cersei)](../../Source/Main-Series/04-A-Feast-for-Crows/text/044-cersei.md):** While confined by the Faith, Cersei **specifically asks Qyburn** to send her pleading message to Jaime at Riverrun, by **raven if possible, otherwise rider**; he acknowledges the wording and agrees to act. `DIRECT ORDER / PROMISE`.
- **[AFFC 44 (Jaime)](../../Source/Main-Series/04-A-Feast-for-Crows/text/045-jaime.md):** **Maester Vyman personally hands Jaime a raven-carried letter from King's Landing**. Jaime observes Qyburn's terse formulation and Cersei's emotional appeal; after reading, **orders Peck to burn it without replying**. `INDEPENDENTLY VERIFIED ARRIVAL / RECEIPT`; the *burn order is observed*, not the actual flame consuming it. This is stronger proof of **one successful Qyburn-mediated correspondence chain** than any documentary trace of the Duskendale directive.
- **[ADWD 54 (Cersei)](../../Source/Main-Series/05-A-Dance-with-Dragons/text/055-cersei.md):** **Cersei fears** Qyburn never got the letter away and might be dead or held by her captors. `CHARACTER IGNORANCE`. **The reader already saw the letter arrive at Riverrun**, proving that the message-sender's knowledge can lag actual receipt. Do not infer Jaime answered or that Cersei learned his decision.
- **[ADWD 65 (Cersei)](../../Source/Main-Series/05-A-Dance-with-Dragons/text/066-cersei.md)** and **[ADWD Epilogue (Kevan)](../../Source/Main-Series/05-A-Dance-with-Dragons/text/073-epilogue.md):** Qyburn remains active at the capital and is associated with the court's information about Aegon/Golden Company and Robert Strong. `OBSERVED / REPORTED CONTINUED INFLUENCE`; not an admission of Duskendale letter retention.

**Comparator:** For Cersei's appeal we possess (a) oral assignment, (b) Qyburn's assent, (c) **independent receiving POV**, (d) **no response and a destruction order**. For Roose's Tallhart directive we only possess (a) oral assignment, (b) Qyburn's assent, (c) **independently reported eastern campaign**, *without a witnessed receiving POV*. The comparator supports **plausibility of competence**, **not successful delivery in every case**.

## 4. Credibility and incentives if Qyburn is ever questioned

- **What he can most plausibly know firsthand:** Roose's words, the requested destination and reprisals, and any document **he actually drafted**. The first two are already corroborated from Arya's POV; the last is `UNKNOWN` until Qyburn says so or an actual text surfaces.
- **What he cannot certify merely from that room:** Robb's earlier genuine consent or ignorance, why Tywin learned the route, Tallhart's actual receipt, whether Roose intended Tarly's precise outcome, and details of the Trident ferry allocations.
- **His current incentives differ from an impartial chronicler's:** his benefactor Roose protected him at Harrenhal and sent him to Jaime, while the Lannister court now gives him status and access. He has served Vargo Hoat's company and practices widely condemned experimentation. These facts supply reasons **either** to conceal compromises or to use information opportunistically. No actual disclosure, blackmail attempt, retained copy or secret pact is narrated.
- **If he purports to produce a letter**, require proof of handwriting, seal, provenance, destination, storage and timeline. A letter Qyburn *could have written* is more readily manufactured than the **observed speech in Arya's chapter**. His court role and his discovery of the *Rugen* coin in AFFC 7 show he can supply politically influential “evidence,” but **do not prove the coin was planted**.
- **Independent backup:** **Arya** is the direct original eyewitness; **Walton** was present, alive later at Winterfell (ADWD 46/51), but neither is currently a proven Northern recipient of a public accusation. **Robett Glover** can recount orders his force got and the battle, but no five-book conversation names the text of Qyburn's specific letter. **Varys** is the battlefield-news reporter, not independently a Duskendale writ custodian.

### What would falsify a fashionable total-conspiracy version?

A straightforward source-grounded reading needs no raven to Tywin: Roose planned an eastward raid, Tallhart/Glover followed some instructions, Tywin's scouts spotted the army, Tarly defeated it, and Roose later blamed the survivors. Another path would find an **intercepted order** or **Qyburn-sent intelligence**, upgrading deliberate exposure/treachery. Both are possible; only the first has fewer unshown intermediaries. The **blame displacement remains evident** whichever intelligence explanation wins.

## 5. Conditional uses in an original continuation (not a fixed future)

**C1 — Qyburn withholds the history:** He remains embedded at King's Landing, is never challenged about Duskendale, and any physical copy never surfaces. The North acts from **Glover's wartime recollection or other surviving testimony**, not magically informed by this dossier. `LOWEST ASSUMPTION`.

**C2 — someone asks the right question:** A survivor of Glover/Tallhart's force identifies the anomalous order and sends a trusted question toward a relevant source; Qyburn may be reached indirectly, perhaps unwillingly. Requires **named messenger, incentive, access to royal offices and independent corroboration**. A blind claim that Manderly or Stannis “knows Qyburn ordered Duskendale” is invalid.

**C3 — the document is recovered or disputed:** A private copy exists in Harrenhal or a surviving dispatch bag; another actor discovers it and authenticates its chain. Requires showing **where the paper stayed through Tywin/Lorch/Hoat/Bolton/Gregor/Hasty** or **where Qyburn personally carried it**; castle room ownership alone cannot fill that gap. A forged copy could support a false accusation, so its chain of custody matters.

**C4 — opponent's intelligence supplies an independent lead:** Lannister/Tarly officers or Varys recall *how* Tywin learned Tallhart/Glover's approach. A named scout or intercept could close the old gap without asking Qyburn to confess; but Tywin's exact source is absent and some participants have died. Needs a **credible surviving officer and actual reported message**.

**Critical tests to run if fresh canon appears:**
1. Has anyone explicitly read or quoted the Tallhart order's **written** form (not merely Roose's oral instruction)?
2. Can anyone date a **dispatch/receipt** separate from the incoming Tallhart rider?
3. Does a Lannister or Tyrell participant identify the **source of Tywin's advance intelligence**?
4. Does Qyburn ever admit handling this specific **old wartime** letter, rather than one of his many later royal reports?
5. Has an authenticated statement reached **a claimant or court with power and motive to act**, as opposed to merely existing with a witness?

**Disposition:** `ROOSE ORDER OBSERVED`; `QYBURN PROMISES DRAFT`; `ACTUAL DRAFT/SEAL/COURIER/RECEIPT UNKNOWN`; `EDMURE PREVIOUSLY SENT JOIN-ROOSE REQUEST, DISTINCT FROM DUSKENDALE`; `TYWIN KNOWS FORCE AND ROUTE, TWO-ARM INTERCEPTION REPORTED`; `VARYS SUBSEQUENTLY REPORTS DEFEAT`; `ROBB SURPRISED`; `ROOSE BLAMES GLOVER`; `QYBURN LATER DELIVERS ANOTHER LETTER TO JAIME VIA KING'S LANDING→RIVERRUN`; `COURT DISCLOSURE OF DUSKENDALE UNKNOWN`. Independent critical challenge remains OPEN.
