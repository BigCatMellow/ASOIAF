## Distinctions enforced here

`OBSERVED` = a POV directly sees an event; `REPORTED` = a character asserts or hears it; `DISPATCHED` = a message/order is sent but not proved received; `INTENDED` = planned action not executed on-page; `INFERRED` = our explanation; `SPECULATIVE` = optional continuation. A POV can be mistaken even when sincerely recounting what they saw.

The **five published novels** are the source boundary. `The Forsaken` and other *Winds* sample chapters are not five-book facts. No fixed two-book outcome, solved magic system, supposed author intention, or TV-derived ending is adopted.

**Maintenance:** Add new cross-plot facts here only when they require at least two otherwise independently maintained plot strands. Leave individual chapter facts to the sequential agent; leave faction-local mail/travel chronology to continuity files. Update a handoff when newly verified evidence changes a gate.
