## Why this matters more than a single timeline

Most 'convergences' are **asymmetric receipt situations**:
- Reader knows Quentyn died; Doran does not.
- Reader knows false Arya is Jeyne; the northern political world might not.
- Reader knows Bran lives; most Stark heirs and northern lords cannot confirm.
- Reader knows the Brienne threat comes in the wake of Stoneheart's ultimatum; Jaime hears only her story.
- Reader knows Sam saw a burning candle and described Others to an interested archmaester; the entire Citadel does not necessarily know.
- Reader knows Euron claimed a horn would bind dragons; neither Euron nor Victarion has observed it compel one on-page.

**Research test:** For every proposed new scene, ask “What precisely did this character hear; from whom; how long ago; why trust it; and who else can verify it?” If any answer is missing, use the uncertainty as conflict rather than giving them the reader's information.

2. `#4 + #5`: Can Doran receive Aegon's claim **before** news of Quentyn's fate? The two arrival orders imply different political motivations and scenes.
3. `#7 + #8`: Does Sam deliver his warning beyond Marwyn, and can Marwyn still travel if the Reach coast deteriorates?
4. `#9 + #10`: Does Victarion's battle/horn intervention precede Dany's political return, or does she encounter its consequences later?
5. `#11 + #13`: If Stoneheart hears of Robb's will, how much could she explain to Jaime/Brienne and why would they trust it? The books show no such conversation.

**Stop rule:** Do not write messenger receipt into canon merely to make a preferred outline possible. If a receipt is unpublished, branch into “arrives,” “delayed” and “misread” rather than selecting one.
