## Why this matters more than a single timeline

Most 'convergences' are **asymmetric receipt situations**:
- Reader knows Quentyn died; Doran does not.
- Reader knows false Arya is Jeyne; the northern political world might not.
- Reader knows Bran lives; most Stark heirs and northern lords cannot confirm.
- Reader knows the Brienne threat comes in the wake of Stoneheart's ultimatum; Jaime hears only her story.
- Reader knows Sam saw a burning candle and described Others to an interested archmaester; the entire Citadel does not necessarily know.
- Reader knows Euron claimed a horn would bind dragons; neither Euron nor Victarion has observed it compel one on-page.

**Research test:** For every proposed new scene, ask “What precisely did this character hear; from whom; how long ago; why trust it; and who else can verify it?” If any answer is missing, use the uncertainty as conflict rather than giving them the reader's information.

**Stop rule:** Do not write messenger receipt into canon merely to make a preferred outline possible. If a receipt is unpublished, branch into “arrives,” “delayed” and “misread” rather than selecting one.

## Castle Black emergency news and hostage-custody transmission (2026-10-15)

The [first-night command/custody audit](castle-black-command-custody-first-night.md) adds two important post-ADWD communication gates, **without promoting any hypothetical dispatch to fact**:

1. **Castle Black → Eastwatch and Shadow Tower:** `HOSTAGES TRANSFERRED BEFORE ATTACK / JON'S FATE NOT REPORTED`. **ADWD 58 (Jon)** records sixty of Tormund's one hundred hostage boys **sent onward to these two garrisons**; the split and subsequent guards are unshown. **ADWD 49 (Jon)** identifies **Ser Glendon Hewett** as the commander at Eastwatch while Cotter Pyke sails to Hardhome; **ASOS 78 (Samwell)** establishes Denys Mallister as an independently respected Watch commander. **ADWD 69** ends before either receives news of Jon's stabbing. Distinguish a pre-existing hostage transfer from a post-attack messenger, and neither officer's potential response from a decision already made.
2. **Jon's infant exchange → royal household / other Free Folk:** `READER VERIFIED / ROYAL RECIPIENT UNKNOWN`. **ADWD 7 (Jon)** establishes Jon's deliberate exchange of Mance/Dalla's and Gilly's infants; **AFFC 45 (Samwell)** shows Gilly reaching Oldtown with **Mance's real son**. The child left at Castle Black is Gilly's. **ADWD 49 (Jon)** places wet nurses supplied by Old Flint and the Norrey at the Wall for that infant, not proof they know its biological identity. **ADWD 69** records the Pink Letter's demand for the **wildling babe**, which is a sender's claim/request, not evidence of correct identification. There is no shown transmission of the exchange to Selyse, Marsh, the infant's nurses, Stannis or Ramsay.

A **third unsent message**, Jon's injury or death from **Castle Black → Stannis or the northern clans**, would materially affect Alys/Sigorn's marriage support, Cregan's custody and the standing of Jon's prior warning about Arnolf. Neither a messenger nor a receipt occurs in ADWD 69. **Do not assume the same courier carries earlier wedding testimony, the infant identity and the assassination news.**

## Command recognition and cross-garrison ballots — unsent Castle Black news

The [Castle Black command/custody dossier](castle-black-command-custody-first-night.md) now separately establishes a **historic ballot mechanism** that should not be confused with a **newly sent communication**:

- **ASOS 75 (Samwell), `VERIFIED HISTORIC PROCEDURE`:** voters on duty could use a friend's token; **Denys Mallister and Cotter Pyke cast ballots on behalf of garrisons they had left behind**. Any future 999th Lord Commander choosing must account for the whole Watch's elective body and the two-thirds threshold. A post-stabbing ballot or new proxies have **NOT** been observed.
- **ASOS 78 (Samwell), `VERIFIED OLD ATTITUDES / NOT PRESENT RESPONSE`:** both Pyke and Mallister call **Bowen Marsh unsuited to overall command** despite agreeing with neither each other nor all of Jon's later decisions. Sam also privately **lies to each that Stannis means to appoint the other** to induce a third-candidate agreement. The Watch's previous successful election used these distorted private beliefs; **Sam is now in Oldtown, Aemon is dead** (AFFC 45).
- **ADWD 49 (Jon), `ASSIGNED AND REPORTED`:** Pyke leaves **Ser Glendon Hewett** commanding Eastwatch during the rescue mission. **ADWD 58 (Jon)** reports Pyke's shipboard disaster at Hardhome and the relocation of **sixty Free Folk boy hostages** to Eastwatch/Shadow Tower. These are **separate local custody and command chains**, with no evidenced response to Jon's stabbing in **ADWD 69**.
- **`UNSENT/UNKNOWN`:** a new Castle Black message must specify **author, exact allegation(s), addressed commander(s), dispatch, receipt, and any proxy-vote arrangements**. The moment Hewett learns a report is not necessarily the moment Pyke at Hardhome learns it; Mallister's response cannot be inferred from either man's preferences.

**Information-failure test:** A dispatch saying only “Jon is dead; Marsh commands” could spur different reactions than one truthfully describing the attackers, Patrek's death, surviving hostages and the lack of any election. The *exact letter text* and proof of receipt are prerequisites to selecting a reunion, reprisal or choosing scene. Both possible messages are **invented continuation branches**, not canonical letters.

## Free Folk fathers and the missing hostage-location message

**ADWD 58 (Jon), `VERIFIED / INITIAL-LOCATION PARTIALLY UNKNOWN`:** Tormund's hundred boys include sons of named, separately acting captains: **Soren Shieldbreaker, Morna White Mask, Brogg, Howd Wanderer, Gerrick Kingsblood, Devyn Sealskinner, and the Great Walrus** among others. A Watch steward records names at the gate; sixty are later sent toward Eastwatch and the Shadow Tower, but **no named son's individual destination is verified**. This is not a single “Tormund's hostages” political constituency. Full chief-by-chief evidence: [Free Folk coalition audit](free-folk-alliance-hostage-and-command-gates.md).

**After ADWD 69, `UNSENT / NO RECEPTION`:** Any captain learning that Jon was stabbed still needs separate proof of **the boy's identity, actual place of custody, current safety and which Watch officer can guarantee him** before threats, negotiations or rescue can be attributed to that captain. **Gerrick** is also Selyse's chosen prospective royal client while a father of one hostage; this creates competing incentives, **not** a verified Gerrick defection or a proved execution risk. Boys from separate families could produce different and even contradictory reactions to the **same** future Castle Black report.

## Hostage registry is not custody confirmation — two missing transmissions

**Source audit:** **ADWD 53/58 (Jon)** establish a Watch-created entry roster (two stewards wrote the hundred children’s names), Jon's orders to Marsh to process toll valuables and to Yarwyck to prepare settlement wagons, and Marsh's later report that **sixty boys were sent toward Eastwatch and Shadow Tower**. The source does **not** state the roster records **paternity or later assignment**; it does not say the boys have arrived, that all parents know their child's destination, or that a receiving officer has confirmed safe custody. [Child custody / wardship analysis](free-folk-alliance-hostage-and-command-gates.md).

A proposed protective exchange after **ADWD 69**, `NOT SENT / NO REPLY`, needs **two distinct checks**: (1) the stewards' **Castle Black list** reconciled to currently observed children and departures; (2) independent **Eastwatch and Shadow Tower** replies confirming each named child and warder. No message, receipt or fresh list is published. **Do not invent a clean administrative record simply because a counting scene occurred.**

The coercive logic is itself asymmetric: **ADWD 58** introduces putative sons of the *already killed* Alfyn Crowkiller and a boy called Varamyr Sixskins’ son. **ADWD Prologue (Varamyr)** confirms Varamyr's human death and subsequent wolf second life, a **reader-only fact**. A deceased human father cannot be coerced by threatening a child. Separately, Jon requests **two male replacements for two disguised girls** and intends to transfer the girls to Long Barrow; actual replacement/delivery is **not shown**. These facts require careful identity verification before a future faction announces a “one hundred boys, each with a living chief father” ultimatum.
