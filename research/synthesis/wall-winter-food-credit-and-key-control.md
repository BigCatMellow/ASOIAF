## Executive finding — a mutineer was previously the storeroom keyholder

**VERIFIED, ADWD 17 (Jon):** Bowen Marsh leads Jon through Castle Black's underground storerooms and compares current stocks with records from **three turns earlier**. **Wick Whittlestick** is personally holding a ring of keys around his neck, opening large **padlocked** storeroom doors and chalk-marking barrels, sacks and casks as the count proceeds. These are the actual granaries, root cellars and preserved meat stores, connected by Castle Black's **wormways**. Marsh recommends guarding the supplies when winter arrives.

**VERIFIED, ADWD 69 (Jon):** Wick Whittlestick is near Marsh in Shieldhall and is the **first attacker identified by the narration** when he slashes at Jon with a knife; Bowen Marsh subsequently stabs Jon as well.

**High-value continuity implication / INFERRED:** The conspiracy involves the man **previously demonstrated to operate the locks on the food stores**. This makes the keyholder's loyalty and the physical disposition of the keys as important to a first-night continuation as any claim to lawful Watch command. **It does NOT establish that Wick retained those keys on the night of the assassination, brought them to Hardin's Tower, alone controls every food vault, ordered the rationing system or presently prevents anyone else from retrieving supplies.** An order from a newly acclaimed commander cannot simply replace a shown guard, a padlock, a key, and the stewards who move provisions.

## 2. The sequence matters: this is not an undifferentiated “winter stores” problem

1. **ADWD 3 (Jon) — royal provisioning already strains stores.** Jon regards royal lodging, clothing and food as materially costly before the Tormund bargain.
2. **ADWD 17 (Jon) — Marsh and Wick audit locked stores.** Comparison records exist; Wick wears storehouse keys. Marsh recommends a reduction; Jon agrees to a **quarter off men's portions**. Marsh describes shortages from royal men and refugees *already present*, and proposes imports by sea.
3. **ADWD 21 (Jon) — Mole's Town distribution produces competition over food.** Individual ration allotments prompt jostling and crowd conflict; Jon offers food to those who will help fight. **VERIFIED: insufficient distribution can be a security incident before Tormund's mass entry.**
4. **ADWD 44 (Jon) — Jon signs a bank credit/shipping agreement.** The copy in his solar is **verified on that day**; an enduring loan asset exists in a stronger sense than “Jon hopes to borrow”.
5. **ADWD 49 (Jon) — hard-use of the vessels.** Pyke's letter announces ships **at sea for Hardhome**, with two Lyseni described as barely seaworthy, and Glendon Hewett administering Eastwatch.
6. **ADWD 53 (Jon) — new multi-fort ration burden committed.** Jon says he'll source sea imports via Eastwatch; he does **not** tell the skeptical northmen or Marsh that Iron Bank funds are the planned supplement. Marsh is told to send collected treasure to Eastwatch.
7. **ADWD 58 (Jon) — Free Folk actually admitted.** **3,119** pass the gate that day; the toll is collected and sixty hostages are sent toward Eastwatch/Shadow Tower. Later, Pyke's message from Hardhome confirms a disastrous rescue situation; no completed bulk-food import is attested.
8. **ADWD 69 (Jon) — rival uses for diminishing resources.** Selyse refuses Hardhome support; Marsh prefers letting the trapped people die rather than increasing the Watch's mouths to feed. Jon plans a land rescue, then pivots to the separate Ramsay/Winterfell response. **Wick and Marsh then stab him.**

**Timing qualification:** These chapters supply this partial order, **not an exact number of elapsed days or a complete ledger of all supplies added/consumed** between every chapter.

## 3. The keyholder, the ledger, and the old wormways — three different instruments

**People with demonstrated past actions**:

- **Wick**: ADWD 17 actual **keys to padlocked underground provisions** and marks containers; ADWD 69 **knife attack**. `VERIFIED PRIOR ACCESS + LATER PARTICIPATION IN MUTINY`. His current pockets, whereabouts immediately *after* the stabbing, and whether keys changed hands are `UNKNOWN`.
- **Bowen Marsh**: ADWD 17 **compares count sheets, plans winter rations and advocates buying food by sea**; ADWD 53 assigned responsibility for Free Folk toll valuables; ADWD 58 supervises stewards and tracks arriving people, transfers of hostages. ADWD 69 personally stabs Jon. `VERIFIED ACCESS TO ADMINISTRATION + MURDER PARTICIPATION`, **not** proved total custody of food or bank instruments.
- **Dolorous Edd**: ADWD 17 accompanies Jon through supply spaces; later **stationed with Iron Emmett at Long Barrow**, and transports wagons of women (ADWD 58). `VERIFIED KNOWLEDGE / OTHERWISE LOCALLY SEPARATE`. Nothing proves he knows the Wick-Marsh plan or has a current set of Castle Black store keys.
- **Jon**: retains signed credit agreement copy **in his solar** in ADWD 44 and issues admission, transfer and relief orders. By ADWD 69 his access cannot be assumed after the attack. **Where the paperwork is now is unknown.**
- **Three-Finger Hobb and other stewards**: kitchen and distribution capacity exists, but the novel does not show which steward would release a night's emergency food or endorse which leadership faction.

**Material access puzzle:** Wormways permit winter passage **through Castle Black** (ADWD 17), but no book evidence supports a previously built secret raid path to Eastwatch, an unguarded bank-vault entrance, or a guaranteed covert rescue of wards. This is a **site for a scene about guarding, reopening and accounting**, not a license to invent a tunnel exploit.

**Revision test:** If a caretaker announces emergency rations, require `(1) identified signer/orderer; (2) keeper of keys and accessible room; (3) current count; (4) distributor; (5) receiver group; (6) human/armed enforcement`. If any link fails, the “agreement” is still just a declaration.

## 4. Five rational but incompatible demands on the same scarce resources

| Claimant or responsible party | Evidence-backed priority | What cannot be assumed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Marsh + stores stewards** | Preserve the Watch's own winter survival; propose ration cuts, restrict admissions and avoid fatal expeditions (**ADWD 17/53/69**). | Marsh would automatically deny food to *all* previously admitted noncombatants, or has complete power over the stockpile with Wick alone. |
| **Tormund / other Free Folk chiefs** | Food, shelter and safety of families and captive sons were central to agreement; Tormund surrendered inherited gold and led his people through (**ADWD 53/58**). | All chiefs will accept a smaller ration, all will attack stores, or Tormund physically commands separate garrisons. |
| **Selyse / remaining royal household** | Claim security and supplies; refuse Hardhome rescue, maintain heavily guarded entourage (**ADWD 69**). | Selyse can legally revoke the Night's Watch's own agreements or automatically redirect the signed Iron Bank credit. |
| **Eastwatch under Glendon Hewett** | Uncertain hostage custody, port/coastal provisions and potentially receiving Free Folk toll treasures; separate local command while Pyke is away (**ADWD 49/53/58**). | Ship availability, new coin, food deliveries, or Eastwatch allegiance to Marsh, especially during storm and sea losses. |
| **Tormund's proposed Hardhome party / trapped refugees** | Relief is vital but would need food, carts, draft animals and armed guards; naval effort went badly (**ADWD 58/69**). | That the Watch could sustain both the existing population and a large rescue, or that a newly named leader has financed a fresh expedition. |

**The genuine contradiction:** Jon's treaty is both a humanitarian/military strategy **and a costly transfer of food and risk to the Watch**, which Marsh identified *before* agreeing to help implement admission logistics. A compromise successor cannot preserve every aspect of Jon's program cheaply; equally, political disapproval cannot make the living newcomers and their dependents disappear.

## 5. A short, reversible continuation test — no outcome adopted

**Immediate first-night question:** Wick has stabbed Jon near Hardin's Tower; where are the **locked provisions and any keys now**, who controls the stewards, and can existing guards distribute an ordinary meal to both sworn brothers and admitted refugees without sparking a confrontation? A neutral logistical act might reveal genuine support or resistance more reliably than a declaration of who “commands.”

**Within the next day:** A credible temporary governing group could confirm counts, establish guarded access to food, ensure a fixed minimum ration for resident children and ill people and transmit requests to Eastwatch and Shadow Tower. **All of these are newly imagined steps**. Neither the nightly count nor any messages have been verified after the knives.

**Within following days, if warranted:** Locate **Jon's copy of the Iron Bank agreement** and ask whether its signatories/terms still permit provision orders under the Watch's next administrator. Separately ascertain **if any financing actually arrived**, where the Free Folk valuables went, and what ships could safely carry food once their rescue assignments are considered. **No amount or delivery schedule should be invented.**

**A disconfirming branch:** The same locked stores, mass influx, uneasy queen and shortage could force harsh rationing and breakdown **even if the Watch's officers attempt to protect all children**. If so, factional violence would derive from a real capacity shortfall, **not proof that Marsh planned an extermination**. Conversely a temporary food arrangement does **not** prove he has been accepted as a lawful Lord Commander.

**Key-control distinction (2026-10-15):** [The operational command-and-control audit](castle-black-operational-control-and-factional-obedience.md) sources **Mully's separate role as holder of the three Wall tunnel gate keys in ADWD 39 (Jon)**, compared with **Wick's food-vault keys in ADWD 17 (Jon)**. These were **different moments and different kinds of locks**; neither man's key custody at the moment of Jon's stabbing is shown. The political faction holding access to a meal does **not** thereby hold the tunnel, sentries or external Watch outposts.
