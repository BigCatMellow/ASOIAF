## Executive conclusion — Strongboar's orders chase a face, not a verified person

In **[AFFC 30 (Jaime), at Darry](../../Source/Main-Series/04-A-Feast-for-Crows/text/031-jaime.md)**, survivors' reports about the slaughter at **Saltpans** are treated as evidence of the Hound because witnesses saw his **distinctive snarling helmet**. **Ser Arwood Frey** visited Saltpans **after** receiving reports at the Twins, with Harys and Donnel Haigh and armed men; **Arwood was not portrayed seeing the original attacker personally**. Jaime questions the identity because the atrocities sound more like **Gregor** than **Sandor**. Ser **Lyle Crakehall (“Strongboar”)** nevertheless pledges **Lady Mariya and Lady Amerei** that, once Riverrun falls, he will **hunt and kill the Hound**. `DIRECT PLEDGE / ASSAILANT IDENTITY VIA HELM AND SECONDHAND WITNESSES`.

**[AFFC 31 (Brienne), Quiet Isle](../../Source/Main-Series/04-A-Feast-for-Crows/text/032-brienne.md)**: the **Elder Brother reports** personally tending the wounded man he calls the Hound, marking a grave with **Sandor's helmet**; an **unknown traveler took the helmet**. He says the man who committed the Saltpans atrocities **was not Sandor**. His account is an on-page `EYEWITNESS-CLAIM BY ELDER BROTHER` to Sandor's care and helmet's initial location; it is not an on-page independent witness to the theft or an objective resolution of the [Quiet Isle gravedigger hypothesis](../theories/cases/gravedigger-hound.md). The difference between **Sandor the man** and **the Hound identity** matters.

**[AFFC 37 (Brienne), crossroads inn](../../Source/Main-Series/04-A-Feast-for-Crows/text/038-brienne.md)**: Brienne confronts former Brave Companion **Rorge** wearing the **Hound's helmet**. She **kills Rorge with Oathkeeper** while defending the inn's children. **Gendry** subsequently kills **Biter**, who has severely injured Brienne. `ATTACKER AND DEATH POV-OBSERVED`, with neither Brienne nor Gendry seeing Rorge loot the original grave.

**[AFFC 42 (Brienne), Stoneheart's cave](../../Source/Main-Series/04-A-Feast-for-Crows/text/043-brienne.md)**: Brienne **recognizes Lem Lemoncloak** as the new bearer. Brienne explicitly explains that **Rorge took the helmet from Clegane's grave and Lem stole it from Rorge's corpse**; **Lem answers without disputing he took it**, and **Thoros condemns his decision to display such a violent public face**. Lem states that the sight will frighten opponents. Later he puts the same helm back on while proceeding with Brienne's hanging. `BRIENNE CHARACTER ACCOUNT + LEM RESPONSE + DIRECT LEM WEARER CONFIRMED`.

**[AFFC 44 (Jaime), after the Riverrun surrender](../../Source/Main-Series/04-A-Feast-for-Crows/text/045-jaime.md)**: Strongboar **actually departs** to return toward **Darry** and hunt the **Hound and Beric Dondarrion**. His companions include **his squire, unspecified men-at-arms, and Beardless Jon Bettley**. Jaime authorizes taking **the Hound's head**, but explicitly **orders Beric brought back *alive* to King's Landing** for a public execution. Strongboar agrees reluctantly. `DEPARTURE AND ORDERS POV-OBSERVED`; **arrival at Darry, discovery of Lem, capture of anyone, later route, and a return report all `UNKNOWN`**.

**[ADWD 48 (Jaime), Pennytree](../../Source/Main-Series/05-A-Dance-with-Dragons/text/049-jaime.md)**: Brienne, who knows Lem's helmet, tells Jaime `the Hound` will kill `the girl` unless Jaime **comes alone** to a place allegedly a day's ride away. **The reader can suspect Lem is her intended referent**; the sentence itself identifies **neither a person beneath the helm nor an actually observed endangered Sansa**, and the book closes before Jaime accepts, departs, or meets a captor.

**Result:** Lyle has **orders and a small active party**. Lem has **the famous visual marker and an outlaw group**. The Hound's original identity has become dangerously portable. Yet **no five-book scene puts Lyle, Lem, Brienne and Jaime together or gives a confirmed path of pursuit between them**.

## 1. The observable custody chain — three “Hounds,” not one verified man

| Stage | Who/where | Directly supported information | Do not silently assert |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Original owner before Saltpans** | **Sandor Clegane** with helmet and horse **Stranger**; last wounded with Arya, [ASOS 74 (Arya)](../../Source/Main-Series/03-A-Storm-of-Swords/text/075-arya.md). | Arya **leaves him grievously wounded** without killing him; later witness Elder Brother says he tended him. | Arya observed death; Sandor certainly survived into a public future; Sandor personally sacked Saltpans. |
| **Grave marker** | Quiet Isle Elder Brother, **AFFC 31**. | Brother says he **marked what he called the Hound's burial with the helmet** and later discovered it missing; saved Stranger. | A reader saw a body positively identified as Sandor under cairn; the theft's date, witness or raider is observed. |
| **Saltpans attacker** | **Rorge**, helmet; Darry hears only **helmet/atrocity** reports, **AFFC 30**. | **AFFC 37** confirms Rorge wore helm; **AFFC 42** Brienne expressly attributes theft and earlier identity to him. | Darry knights already know the perpetrator is Rorge; helmet is forensic proof of Sandor's face. |
| **Rorge killed** | Brienne's crossroads-inn defense, **AFFC 37**. | Brienne kills the wearer with Oathkeeper; Gendry kills Biter, not Rorge. | The Hound's iron mask was destroyed; this terminates use of its fame. |
| **New Hound** | **Lem Lemoncloak**, Stoneheart follower, **AFFC 42**. | Brienne recognizes **Lem wearing Rorge's stolen helm**; Lem chooses its intimidation value, Thoros objects. | Lem committed Rorge's Saltpans massacre or is Sandor resurrected; every Brotherhood member approves his usage. |
| **Name deployed as threat** | Brienne → Jaime at Pennytree, **ADWD 48**. | Brienne invokes **the Hound** as reason Jaime must come alone; Jaime hears a known outlaw label. | Jaime knows Lem has the helm; Sansa is physically there; the threat's specific words were dictated by Stoneheart; Jaime has complied. |

**Identity trap:** The argument `helmet seen at Saltpans → Sandor performed massacre` fails. A new argument `helmet seen with Lem → Lem performed massacre` would fail for the **same reason**: the helmet can change hands while its public association survives. Lem's own real misconduct may make him a viable target, but **a different charge must be substantiated rather than inheriting Rorge's crimes with the steel**.

### The search infrastructure and a failed Brotherhood alert are now separately audited

The [Darry/Hardstone/Strongboar intelligence-contest investigation](stoneheart-brotherhood-strongboar-hardstone-darry-crossroads-intelligence-contest.md) corrects any implication that Lyle can simply inherit a known trail to Stoneheart. In **AFFC 30 (Jaime)** Darry commander **Ser Harwyn Plumm (“Hardstone”)** is **already absent** pursuing **a new report beyond the Trident with five knights and twenty archers**; this is **not Strongboar's later armed departure from Riverrun (AFFC 44)**. Mariya's **Lord Vypren/Fairmarket** and **Black Walder/Hag's Mire→Neck** accounts are **older lost tracks** of separately dispersed Oldstones outlaws, not a map to Stoneheart's current shelter. **AFFC 37/42 (Brienne)** supplies a public **crossroads inn** protected by Gendry and children, **Lem's assigned concealed watch with an intended chimney-smoke distress signal**, and **Thoros's account of a misleading “Mad Dog” report** drawing Lem north while Rorge's group used a stream to double back; a Frey knight column delayed him further. **The original rumor's sender, actual smoke-signal transmission, and any hunter receipt remain unshown**. Lyle's suggestion to mutilate villagers contrasts with Jaime's Arthur Dayne example of earning cooperation.

## 2. The actual anti-outlaw search: men, command limits and target mismatch

### At Darry before the siege — AFFC 30

Strongboar is already with **Jaime's sizable crown field force** at Darry. They discuss Oldstones, the **hooded woman** whom peasants allegedly saw leading outlaws, and **Lord Vypren/Black Walder** pursuing separate trails. The Frey account gives reports of damage at Saltpans and several surviving witnesses, but **the distinctive helmet** functions as the crucial suspect identification; nobody presents Rorge's name or actual face as proof. Jaime doubts the suspect identity even before Brienne's separate discoveries.

Strongboar **promises** the women he will return when Riverrun is taken. He also speaks harshly of smallfolk who conceal outlaws, advocating severe punishment; **Jaime immediately counters** that winning cooperation worked better when **Arthur Dayne** sought the Kingswood Brotherhood by paying villagers, taking grievances seriously and protecting local interests. `POV-OBSERVED DISAGREEMENT IN HOW TO GATHER INFORMATION`.

**Practical implication:** If Strongboar treats peasants as enemies for refusing to identify a hooded outlaw, he could destroy the human information channels he needs. This is an **inference** from his words and Jaime's rebuttal, **not proof he subsequently tortured informants or that a future chase fails**.

**[AFFC 25 (Brienne)](../../Source/Main-Series/04-A-Feast-for-Crows/text/026-brienne.md)** supplies another distortion: **Hyle Hunt reports** Randyll Tarly intentionally attributes Saltpans to Beric's Brotherhood to turn the smallfolk against it. This is **Hyle's reported characterization**, not a narrated Tarly private order and not evidence Strongboar heard Hyle explain it. It shows how anti-outlaw campaigns can circulate an **organizational label** as inaccurately as the helmet circulates a personal identity.

### After Riverrun falls — AFFC 44

Strongboar complains he has not had the fight he wanted. He asks to pursue **both the Hound and `the marcher lord` (Beric)** and intends to return toward Darry in accordance with the earlier promise. Jaime **authorizes lethal violence against the supposed Hound**, but insists Beric be **captured alive** to prove publicly that he can die.

**Double irony:** [AFFC 42 (Brienne)](../../Source/Main-Series/04-A-Feast-for-Crows/text/043-brienne.md) **Thoros** has already told Brienne **Beric gave his life to revive Catelyn**. Jaime and Strongboar **do not know this**; the target `Beric brought alive` is therefore impossible **if Thoros's firsthand account is accurate**, and the person likely to be mistaken for `Sandor the Hound` has become Lem. *Both named targets as Strongboar understands them are outdated.*

The observed outgoing party consists of **Strongboar, his squire, men-at-arms (unnumbered), and Beardless Jon Bettley**. It is **not** Jaime's entire army. The chapter does not show the pursuers enter Darry, follow a particular trail or cross the Brotherhood's perimeter. The phrase `return to Darry` is **intended destination**, not certified subsequent arrival.

### Material capacity

Strongboar is physically powerful and eager for direct combat; **AFFC 38 (Jaime)** shows him at Riverrun ready to assault the walls or challenge the Blackfish, but Jaime uses negotiations instead. This establishes **aggression and willingness to fight**, not a tested duel against Lem, a named scout network, or a documented mastery of small-unit tracking. He has men-at-arms, so an encounter need **not** become a fair one-on-one duel unless circumstances force one.

A Brotherhood ambush has its own demonstrated precedent: [ASOS Epilogue (Merrett)](../../Source/Main-Series/03-A-Storm-of-Swords/text/082-epilogue.md) uses a false ransom, hidden support and prepared execution site. Yet **that does not certify where Lyle will be ambushed or that Stoneheart has heard about him**. Each group needs actionable intelligence, proximity and a tactical choice.

### Hunting feasibility and missing fresh sightings

[Strongboar tracking and Brotherhood counterintelligence audit](strongboar-darry-informants-hag-mire-outlaw-tracking-counterintelligence.md) distinguishes **AFFC 30** Black Walder's *reported earlier* hound search through Hag's Mire toward the Neck from **AFFC 44** Strongboar's *later actual departure* for Darry. Peasants both conceal fugitives and supply descriptions when questioned; Lyle urges mutilation, Jaime points to Arthur Dayne's policy of earning help. **ASOS Epilogue** the Brotherhood itself tracks Sandor using ferrymen and a robbed traveler; **AFFC 25** Meribald knows unmarked trails. A meeting with Lem still requires a **new helmet sighting, actual witness/courier, geographical overlap and timing**; no such lead appears.

**Location and timing gate:** Darry, Saltpans, the crossroads inn, Riverrun and Pennytree are distinct places, and AFFC/ADWD regional chapters overlap. The novel does not provide a dated calendar of Strongboar's actual chase or Lem's patrol after AFFC 42. Do **not** infer that the two parties must meet merely because both operate in the Riverlands, nor invent a travel time/route from a chapter sequence.

**Knowledge gate:** Jaime hears of `Stoneheart` separately at Riverrun and Raventree [see Jaime knowledge owner](jaime-stoneheart-rumor-to-brienne-pennytree-information-gates.md), but is **not shown knowing the scarred woman is Catelyn or Lem now wears the helmet**. Strongboar **does not know** the helmet moved from Sandor to Rorge to Lem; Brienne **does** know the latter chain. A plausible warning chain would need Brienne → Jaime → Strongboar, yet they are **not shown having that conversation** and Lyle has already left the main army by ADWD 48.

**Moral mirror:** Jaime rejects harsh coercion of smallfolk as an anti-outlaw strategy at Darry, but **continues to threaten noble hostages at Riverrun/Raventree**. Lyle loudly champions brutal vengeance for victimized women, but knows too little to identify the culprit accurately. Lem claims the helmet inspires fear and acts violently toward captives despite Thoros's objections. None is a simple unqualified “good knight,” and any confrontation should test **which acts** are justified, not make a helmet a substitute for guilt.

### Ranked evidentiary strength, *not probability of a future scene*

1. **Certain primary-text observations:** Lyle's promise at Darry, later actual departure with armed men; distinctive helm misused by Rorge; Rorge killed by Brienne; Lem visible in the helm; Jaime/Brienne have separate knowledge.
2. **Strong cross-POV inference:** Lyle will likely identify a helmet wearer as `the Hound` if he relies on **the same Darry report criteria** rather than new facial identification. A rational hunter could question the wearer, therefore mistaken attack is **not certain**.
3. **Plausible future collision:** hunters and a wanted portable helmet in roughly the same broader region. **No routes, distances, current sightings or actual meetings** verified.
4. **Highly conditional Jaime rescue or second-wedding disruption:** requires **Jaime to travel, be captured, Lyle to learn relevant coordinates, and Lyle to arrive in a narrow window**, plus a separate unshown wedding plot. **Interesting but assumption-heavy**.
5. **Fixed Sandor/gravedigger intervention:** requires a live Sandor identification, new route to Quiet Isle and consent/escape from a healing sanctuary. Existing [gravedigger theory](../theories/cases/gravedigger-hound.md) is separate and does not support assigning this rescue.
