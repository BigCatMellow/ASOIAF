# The Winds of Winter — first-pass branch continuity tests

- Record role: `RESEARCH / GARDENING SYNTHESIS`.
- Parent: [first-pass trajectory branches](winds-trajectory-branches-first-pass.md).
- Status: `COMPETING TESTS / NOT ADOPTED`.
- Scope: test the three initial branches against the end-of-five-books handoffs. This is not a chapter order, calendar, or prediction of any unshown result.

## Test method

A branch survives only if each proposed consequence has a **carrier**, a **recipient**, a plausible interval or encounter, and a character choice after receipt. Reader knowledge and an adaptation outcome cannot supply a missing link. `PASS` means the branch can be written without contradicting the present record; it does not mean it is likely.

| Constraint | Source audit | Minimum continuity requirement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| North/Wall | [North and Wall endpoint handoff](../reconciliation/north-wall-endpoint-handoff.md) | Separate the Castle Black crisis, Stannis's camp, and Winterfell news; preserve Jeyne's consent and Theon's traumatic reliability limits. |
| Meereen | [Meereen endpoint handoff](../reconciliation/meereen-endpoint-handoff.md) and [battle-start order](../reconciliation/meereen-battle-start-order.md) | A land or naval actor needs contact before reacting to another command; Dany learns nothing merely because the reader did. |
| Dorne | [two-news arrival gate](../reconciliation/dorne-two-news-arrival-gate.md) | Aegon's claim and Quentyn's death need independent dispatch, reception, authentication, and sharing paths. |
| King's Landing | [post-Kevan decision gates](../reconciliation/kings-landing-post-kevan-decision-gates.md) | Somebody must discover the murders and frame them before a regime response can be treated as public policy. |
| Magic and adaptation | [supernatural endpoint gates](../reconciliation/supernatural-endpoint-gates.md) and [adaptation evidence protocol](../adaptation/README.md) | Possession, sacrifice, horn, vision, or television endpoint needs a book-native actor, mechanism, cost, and witness. |

## Branch A — local survival before grand convergence

| Proposed pressure | Continuity result | What would fail it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stannis questions/protects Theon and Jeyne before treating their escape as usable intelligence. | `PASS`: Asha has seen their arrival, but an on-page interrogation, corroboration, and Jeyne's willing participation are still required. | Letting Jon's undelivered Arnolf warning or the Pink Letter settle Stannis's planning. |
| Barristan limits hostage deaths while acting under siege. | `PASS`: he already holds Hizdahr and faces practical military pressure; this does not establish Hizdahr's guilt. | Calling a restraint policy Dany's order, or assuming Tyrion/Victarion know it without a crossing or messenger. |
| Aegon gains territory before distant courts formulate a response. | `PASS, conditional`: local speed is compatible with delayed letters and rumor. | Treating Storm's End as already fallen, Dorne as allied, or King's Landing as holding authenticated campaign intelligence. |
| Cersei's court fragments after the murders. | `PASS, conditional`: discovery and competing attribution can create the fragment. | Starting from public knowledge that Varys did it, or from a completed trial/adaptation catastrophe. |

**Net assessment:** `SURVIVES`. Its vulnerability is not plausibility but inertia: a continuation must force a costly decision rather than allow winter delay to replace plot.

## Branch B — legitimacy contests create unstable coalitions

| Proposed pressure | Continuity result | What would fail it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Theon/Jeyne's testimony destabilizes Bolton legitimacy. | `PASS, narrow`: their firsthand knowledge can matter after protection, verification, and a recipient decides to use it. | Publicly naming Jeyne as Arya or spending her testimony as currency without her consent and a surviving witness chain. |
| Doran assesses Aegon before, after, or apart from Quentyn news. | `PASS`: the divergent receipt order is exactly the open gate. | Giving Doran/Arianne either report at endpoint, or making receipt prove Aegon's parentage or force a marriage. |
| The capital receives alarming southern reports. | `PASS, conditional`: rumors/letters can arrive in stages after discovery of the murders. | Treating an unshown report as reliable military fact or assuming a unified Cersei–Tyrell–Faith response. |
| Sansa's local identity and Sweetrobin problem becomes political. | `PASS, local only`: Petyr's plan and Sweetrobin's vulnerability make a Vale decision plausible. | Marching the Vale north, marrying Harry, or revealing Sansa publicly without an intervening choice and consent. |

**Net assessment:** `SURVIVES`. It should be downgraded if proposed coalitions become stable merely because a reader recognizes the true claimant; the books require people, force, food, and incentives, not correct genealogy alone.

## Branch C — power escalation exposes the cost of instrumental people

| Proposed pressure | Continuity result | What would fail it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stannis, Victarion, or another leader uses a sacrificial/coercive tool. | `PASS only as a decision test`: prior authorization of burning and present captives create pressure, not a verified future victim or outcome. | Selecting Shireen's fate, a horn result, or a military victory in advance. |
| Dragonbinder changes Meereen's balance. | `OPEN`: Victarion carries the horn and Moqorro gives an interpretation, but no dragon response is shown. | Treating Euron's provenance claim, Moqorro's translation, or a television-style dragon outcome as proof of control. |
| Dany returns to consequences of decisions made in her absence. | `PASS, conditional`: others are acting without her, while her location/return path remain unresolved. | Giving her Barristan's battle information, a timely return, or a predetermined punitive response. |
| Court/Oldtown violence has political and civilian cost. | `PASS as a guardrail`: several actors already instrumentalize people and institutions. | Using spectacle as causation; a book-specific perpetrator, target, aftermath, and information flow must be named. |

**Net assessment:** `PARTIALLY SURVIVES`. It is the most thematically productive branch and the easiest to overfit to the television ending. It may be used to test consequences, never to smuggle in outcomes.

## Cross-branch collision tests

| Collision | Required sequence | Premature version to reject |
| --- | --- | --- |
| North victory/defeat changes Wall authority | Camp event → named messenger or traveler → Castle Black recipient → factional response. | A Watch faction instantly knows Stannis's situation or resolves Jon's office through battlefield news it has not received. |
| Meereen battle changes Dany or Dorne's choices | Battle/contact → surviving informed carrier → travel → credible recipient. | Dany returns already briefed, or Doran learns Quentyn's death at the same narrative instant as Meereen. |
| Aegon's success reshapes King's Landing | Local campaign result → report reaches a court already processing Kevan/Pycelle → actors contest its meaning. | The capital acts on Varys's private purpose or on settled facts about Storm's End. |
| Magical act forces political alignment | Demonstrated effect → witnesses/rumor → actor weighs cost and credibility. | Magic supplies universally accepted proof, transport, or obedience. |

## Evidence that would discriminate, rather than merely decorate, the branches

1. The first credible account of Jon's stabbing and the first person who controls Castle Black's food, gates, and prisoners would distinguish containment from immediate factional escalation.
2. Stannis's first private treatment of Theon and Jeyne would distinguish protected testimony from tactical exploitation.
3. The first Meereen cross-line contact would distinguish a negotiated survival branch from unilateral escalation.
4. The order and authentication of the Aegon/Quentyn messages would distinguish exploratory Dornish policy from grief-driven commitment.
5. The court's first public explanation for Kevan and Pycelle would distinguish a temporary compact from renewed Cersei/Faith/Tyrell warfare.
6. An observed Dragonbinder effect, if any, would test whether the horn is an operational weapon, a lethal fraud, or something whose cost defeats its apparent benefit.

## Provisional conclusion

All three branches remain available as **tools**, with Branches A and B presently cleaner under the handoff constraints. Branch C remains conditional because its key mechanisms are the least demonstrated. None should become a story forecast until the continuation supplies the missing deliveries, consent, encounters, and consequences. Independent challenge remains open.
