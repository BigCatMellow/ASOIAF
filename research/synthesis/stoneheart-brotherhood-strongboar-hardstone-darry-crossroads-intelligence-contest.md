## Executive verdict

**It is source-plausible that Strongboar could *find an exposed Brotherhood cell*, but not source-proven that he can *find Stoneheart or Lem*.** The novels give both sides identifiable intelligence infrastructure, a failed deception, and demonstrated political liabilities:

1. **[AFFC 30 (Jaime), Darry](../../Source/Main-Series/04-A-Feast-for-Crows/text/031-jaime.md):** Jaime asks after `Hardstone`. **Maester Ottomore says Ser Harwyn has already led five knights and twenty archers beyond the Trident on a new report of outlaws**. Later in the same chapter Jaime's thoughts identify **Harwyn Plumm** and connect him to Hardstone. `REPORT OF LIVE FIELD DETACHMENT`, **not the later Strongboar mission**. The contact, report sender, exact location, success, and return date are unshown.
2. **AFFC 30:** Lady Mariya says **Lord Vypren** previously followed **one** Oldstones band to **Fairmarket**, and **Black Walder** pursued **another** with hounds north from **Hag's Mire** before losing the trail at the **Neck**. `MARIYA SECONDHAND REPORT OF OLDER TRACKS`. These may reflect *different fleeing groups*, and **neither puts Stoneheart/Lem at a current campsite**. She relays descriptions of a scarred hooded woman, one-eyed man, yellow cloak; the villagers initially denied seeing them and gave a story after sharp questioning. Strongboar **cannot simply follow Black Walder's already-lost trail straight to Stoneheart**.
3. **AFFC 30:** Mariya believes some **riverlords** cooperate with the band; Amerei says **smallfolk feed and hide the outlaws**, attributing complaints to **Ser Harwyn**. `SPEAKER CLAIM ABOUT LOCAL SUPPORT`. This is **indirect intelligence**, not a complete roster of sympathizers or evidence all peasants approve. Strongboar suggests **mutilating lying peasants**, but Jaime points to **Arthur Dayne's Kingswood Brotherhood campaign**: pay for provisions, address grievances, grant hunting/grazing benefits, and change whose interests the villagers trust. Their disagreement supplies a concrete counterinsurgency weakness.
4. **[AFFC 37 (Brienne), crossroads inn](../../Source/Main-Series/04-A-Feast-for-Crows/text/038-brienne.md):** **Gendry**, **Willow**, and orphaned children occupy the old Heddle inn near a road lined with hanged broken men. Children have loaded crossbows; **Gendry expects friendly riders** and the inn has a real covert relationship with Brotherhood members, although the incoming riders are Rorge and Biter's hostile group. `SMALL, ACCESSIBLE SUPPORT NODE POV-SEEN`, with vulnerable noncombatants; **not proof that Strongboar has located it or that the entire local population serves Stoneheart**.
5. **[AFFC 42 (Brienne), Thoros testimony](../../Source/Main-Series/04-A-Feast-for-Crows/text/043-brienne.md):** **Lem had been ordered to stay hidden near the inn and respond if smoke rose from the chimney**. A report that the **“Mad Dog of Saltpans” was traveling north along the Green Fork** lured him away. Thoros says the mummers **used a stream to disguise their return trail and doubled back**, and Lem further delayed his return by going around **a column of Frey knights**. This is **the most specific observed/report-backed failure of Brotherhood operational intelligence**: a false or misleading enemy-location report disrupted their local protective cover. **No narrated sender establishes who invented or planted the report**, and no scene confirms that Willow or Gendry actually emitted the smoke alarm.
6. **[ASOS Epilogue (Merrett)](../../Source/Main-Series/03-A-Storm-of-Swords/text/082-epilogue.md):** **Tom/Stoneheart's band themselves find ferrymen who carried Sandor Clegane across the Trident and a traveler Sandor robbed on the kingsroad**, obtaining his movements while searching for him and a child. `ACTUAL TRACKING VIA NAMED KINDS OF CIVILIAN WITNESSES`, demonstrating that the Brotherhood can trace *other people's* road movements as well. No automatic location of Strongboar follows.
7. **[AFFC 44 (Jaime), after Riverrun](../../Source/Main-Series/04-A-Feast-for-Crows/text/045-jaime.md):** **Lyle “Strongboar” Crakehall departs toward Darry** with **his squire, some men-at-arms and Beardless Jon Bettley** to hunt **the Hound** and **Beric**. Jaime permits the Hound killed, orders Beric brought to King's Landing **alive**. `DEPARTURE POV-SEEN`, **actual arrival and route unknown**, **no merger with Harwyn Plumm's earlier twenty-five-man detachment shown**.
8. **[AFFC 42 (Brienne)](../../Source/Main-Series/04-A-Feast-for-Crows/text/043-brienne.md)** gives the relevant hidden complication: **Lem now wears the Hound helmet**, while Thoros reports **Beric gave his life to resurrect Catelyn**. Strongboar's *named target list* is therefore compromised by **false/obsolete identity information**. **[ADWD 48 (Jaime)](../../Source/Main-Series/05-A-Dance-with-Dragons/text/049-jaime.md)** still stops at **Brienne asking Jaime to travel alone**; Lyle's later activity and Jaime's decision are not narrated.

**Conclusion at the five-book baseline:** `HARDSTONE SEARCH EXISTS`; `STRONGBOAR DEPARTS SEPARATELY`; `DAR RY RUMOR NETWORK EXISTS`; `CROSSROADS INN IS A REACHABLE BROTHERHOOD SUPPORT NODE`; `CHIMNEY SMOKE ALARM SPECIFIED / EMISSION UNKNOWN`; `LEM'S MISLED PATROL/DELAY REPORTED BY THOROS`; `CURRENT STONEHEART CAVE LOCATION / STRONGBOAR TRACE UNKNOWN`.

## 1. Keep the opposing search units separate

| **Brotherhood inquiry into Sandor** | **Tom**, others, and presumed Brotherhood contacts at Oldstones (ASOS Epilogue). | Find a dangerous man and a possible **child**. | Identifies **ferrymen and robbed traveler**; questions Merrett, who denies seeing Sandor at feast. | Exact source of every other Brotherhood report, a reliable Stoneheart HQ map, or interception of Strongboar. |

**Critical anti-merge:** Several armed parties in the Riverlands are **not one unified royal manhunt**. No one tells Strongboar what Hardstone's five knights and twenty archers found, and no text places Strongboar under Harwyn's command. Nor does Mariya's older Black Walder/Neck story count as a fresh sighting of Lem near Darry.

### Darry's political authority is itself unsettled

**[AFFC 30 (Jaime)](../../Source/Main-Series/04-A-Feast-for-Crows/text/031-jaime.md)** has **Lancel** tell Jaime he intends **the next day** to relinquish his marriage to Amerei and his Darry lordship and join the revived **Warrior's Sons**; he says Hardstone can have the wife and lordship if he wants. `LANCEL ANNOUNCED INTENTION` in that scene, not an investiture of Harwyn or automatic castle transfer. Jaime's thoughts also suggest Amerei admires Harwyn, **not proof of an affair or succession agreement**. The uncertainty matters if Lyle expects a stable local government to supply scouts/provisions; the **Darry maester, Mariya/Amerei, Frey household and garrison captain** have different interests. The chapter **does not identify the actual commander issuing orders to Lyle once he returns**.

## 2. Local knowledge is obtainable, but unreliable under force

The strongest clear example of civilian testimony is **Mariya's AFFC 30 report**: local people at first **denied** seeing the Oldstones pursuers' targets, then after aggressive questioning gave visual descriptions of **the hooded woman, one-eyed man and yellow-cloaked companion**. That is positive evidence that some people observed or heard about the outlaws **and did not immediately report them to the nobility**. Whether all descriptions are accurate and whether denial arose from loyalty, fear of reprisals, poor memory, dislike of Freys or simple ignorance **cannot be disentangled**.

Amerei attributes food and shelter to the smallfolk on Harwyn's authority; Mariya claims **some riverlords** are secretly in league with Beric. These are interested `CHARACTER ASSERTIONS`, not signed support agreements. They nonetheless align with actual observed **crossroads shelter**.

**The contrast in anti-outlaw doctrine is explicit in AFFC 30:**
- `LYLE` suggests **mutilating deceptive peasants**. He is motivated by protecting local ladies and eager for an honorable fight, but his proposed method risks suppressing answers as well as inducing terror.
- `JAIME` invokes the **Arthur Dayne / Kingswood Brotherhood precedent**: food paid for, grievances conveyed, rights and grazing improved so the people stopped shielding outlaw leaders. Jaime is not generally a pacifist—he still uses severe threats on noble hostages in later chapters—so this is a **specific operational criticism**, not proof of universal mercy.
- `UNKNOWN`: no next chapter confirms Lyle subsequently used torture, changed his policy, won over an informant, or found a guide. His *remark* cannot substitute for actual patrol conduct.

**The controlled source distinction:** `PEASANTS KNOW SOMETHING` is not `LYLE CAN COMPEL EXACT CAVE ADDRESS`; `HOUND HELM SEEN` is not `SANDOR LOCATED`; `RAVEN/SCOUT RECEIPT` must name sender and actual message before it is elevated into a Stoneheart raid order.

## 3. A protectable node, not a fortress: the crossroads inn

**[AFFC 37 (Brienne)](../../Source/Main-Series/04-A-Feast-for-Crows/text/038-brienne.md)** provides real location and resource constraints:
- The **old Heddle inn at the crossroads** is a public, recognizable site. The road to it is lined with hanged victims marked with **salt**, an intimidating but very visible signal of the outlaws' control. **No claim that the inn itself is an undiscoverable secret hideout** is justified.
- **Willow** organizes orphaned children, some armed with loaded crossbows; **Gendry** works at the forge, has been knighted by Beric in [ASOS 39 (Arya)](../../Source/Main-Series/03-A-Storm-of-Swords/text/040-arya.md), and treats the arrival of friendly riders as possible. The children are **dependents and witnesses, not an army**.
- Brienne, Podrick, Septon Meribald and Ser Hyle obtain entry and food on the road; outsiders can approach the inn **without first penetrating Stoneheart's actual refuge**.
- A band in the **Hound helmet** can arrive under cover of bad weather. Brienne recognizes the wearer's danger and defends the children; **Rorge is killed by Brienne, Biter by Gendry**, and Brienne is badly hurt.

**[AFFC 42 (Brienne)](../../Source/Main-Series/04-A-Feast-for-Crows/text/043-brienne.md)** gives more granular operational information *from Thoros*, not from a generic narrator:
- **Lem was told to remain hidden within response distance**, with **smoke from the chimney** as the recognizable alert condition.
- A **misleading report** of the `Mad Dog of Saltpans` going **north along Green Fork** redirected Lem. Thoros believes the fleeing mummers **used a stream to mask their tracks and doubled back**. It took Lem **half a day to realize the false path**, then more time to avoid a column of **Frey knights**.
- Thoros directly acknowledges that, without Brienne, the children might have been murdered before Lem returned. This is **an actual protective failure**, not a future prophecy. But **the original informant behind the reported sighting is never named**; saying **Rorge definitely planted the story** exceeds evidence. The **smoke alarm was a standing contingency**, not a proved signal raised during the fight.
- **Gendry returned to his forge and Willow's children after the battle** according to Jeyne Heddle. A support node with a stationary blacksmith can be physically pressured, and protecting it could divert followers. No future renewed attack by Lyle is narrated.

### The final refuge is a different information problem

Brienne is **bound and feverish** in AFFC 42. She recalls or dreams of being carried, possibly on a boat in fog, and later **wakes in a cave**. [Thoros](../../Source/Main-Series/04-A-Feast-for-Crows/text/043-brienne.md) confirms the band shelters in caves and runs to their holes to hide from pursuers. **These fever scenes do not produce an authenticated river crossing, named landing, campsite coordinate, trip length, safehouse address or usable route map.**

A pursuer could find **the public crossroads inn** and still have no actionable intelligence about **which cave Stoneheart occupies or whether she stayed there**. Conversely, Lyle could follow a specific rider or messenger **from the inn** if a future source supplies a sighting. The novel has **not** supplied such a witness, trail, or message.

## 4. A symmetric information contest — rather than inevitable Hound duel

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **New sighting of helmet bearer Lem at a road or inn** | Lyle/Harwyn/Frey scouts | Identifies **a conspicuous target**, may provoke an attempt to kill or capture the *wrong “Hound”*. | `NOT RECEIVED`. Lyle has no known fresh Lem sighting. |
| **Reliable witness to an identifiable shelter or children's inn** | Any hunter from Darry | Could expose the **crossroads support node**, create risk for Willow/Gendry. | `PUBLIC SITE POV-SEEN`, **no documented Lyle report linking it to Stoneheart**. |

| **A paid/voluntary civilian informant** | Lyle or Jaime's forces | Could supply roads, disguised identities, timings and a track. | Jaime urges **earning** such cooperation; Lyle suggests mutilation. **No proven new witness**. |
| **False Mad Dog sighting on Green Fork** | Rorge's fleeing group, whether planted or opportunistic | Diverts Lem and leaves orphans exposed; exposes a **counterintelligence weakness in the Brotherhood**. | **Thoros reports the diversion succeeded**; source of report unverified. |
| **Actual crossroads chimney smoke** | Lem's concealed patrol | Requests immediate intervention if noticed and understood. | Signal instruction **CONFIRMED**; any emission/receipt `UNKNOWN`. |
| **A sighting of Brienne or Jaime together near Pennytree** | Lyle or Brotherhood scouts | Could make two apparently unrelated hunts converge, perhaps place Jaime in danger or produce rescue. | `NO LYLE SIGHTING`; Jaime not shown accepting the lone journey in [ADWD 48](../../Source/Main-Series/05-A-Dance-with-Dragons/text/049-jaime.md). |
| **Thoros reveals Beric's death to an enemy hunter** | Lyle/Jaime if actually told | Would eliminate a central obsolete target and redirect search toward Stoneheart. | Thoros **tells Brienne**, **not Lyle or Jaime**. |
| **Leaked captive or cave route from Jeyne, Gendry or a ferry operator** | Lyle/Harwyn/other enemy | Could bypass the support node and expose Stoneheart. | Potential witnesses exist but **no recorded leak or usable travel direction**. |

**THINK / opponent's best countercase:** Skilled hunters have hounds and archers, local nobles have horses/castles, and the Brotherhood's **own** men have been deceived before. It would be wrong to insist that Stoneheart is *unfindable*. But the supportive smallfolk, fog, roads, river crossings and decoy sightings generate enough uncertainty that **an unearned precisely timed Strongboar rescue at Stoneheart's cave would also be poor causal writing**. The minimum plausible encounter is **Lyle finds a recognizable *public node or an exposed Lem patrol***, not Lyle instantly identifies the resurrected Catelyn and knows her hiding place.

## 5. Differentiated conditional continuations, none selected

**H1 — Harwyn produces a credible local witness (`POSSIBLE`):** His twenty-five-armed detachment returns to Darry with a traceable report, and Lyle can exploit it. Must show **result, runner, elapsed time, named location and why informer spoke**. Harwyn may be chasing an unrelated outlaw band.

**H2 — Lyle follows the Hound helm and collides with Lem (`SOURCE-ANCHORED THEORY`):** A witness sees Lem in the steel dog mask after AFFC 42 and credibly directs Lyle to him. Lyle must then **confirm or fail to confirm the face**, and his Hound kill order conflicts with the fact **Rorge, the Saltpans culprit, is already dead**. Such a fight needs no Sandor return or future wedding.

**H3 — Lyle threatens the orphans (`POSSIBLE / ETHICAL PRESSURE`):** Pursuing support informants brings Lyle to Willow and Gendry. He may seek testimony by threat; Jaime's counterstrategy of paid cooperation could become a real choice. **No five-book source shows Lyle at the inn or harming the children**. Assumption that all orphaned children deliberately help Stoneheart plan murders is unjustified.

**H4 — Brotherhood plants a false trail to divert Lyle (`POSSIBLE`):** Lem was deceived by a misleading report; similar methods could target a pursuer, but **no shown Stoneheart false report directed at Strongboar**. Requires a sender trusted by Lyle and a concrete target path. A misdirection is not automatically high-quality intelligence.

**H5 — Hunter accidentally exposes a Brotherhood faction split (`POSSIBLE`):** Thoros's dismay and Lem's brutality suggest internal distrust; the protective inn cell has noncombatant priorities. But no observed factional refusal or decision to inform on Stoneheart. A leak via conscience needs an actual refusal, motive and transmitted information, not the mere existence of sympathetic characters.

**H6 — Lyle intervenes during Jaime's capture or a hypothetical “Red Wedding 2.0” (`HIGH-ASSUMPTION`):** Requires Jaime leave Pennytree alone, Stoneheart take custody rather than execute, Lyle receive a timely signal, and an operation involving Daven's proposed Frey wedding which itself has **no date, venue or confirmed attack plan**. Too many missing causal gates to select as a predicted outcome.

## 6. Research progression and reversal triggers

**Next highest-value source test:** Are there further **first-person scenes** of **Harwyn after AFFC 30**, Lyle after AFFC 44, or Lem reporting actual patrol routes after the AFFC 42 cave hearing? In five printed books **none is shown in the passages traced here**, so do not invent a successful pursuit.

To upgrade any collision require a definite **sighting** (who saw Lem/helmet and where); a **communication handoff** to a named hunter; a **real hunt response and travel path**; and a **scene establishing contact** rather than assuming all Riverlands are adjacent. To upgrade a Stoneheart HQ capture require a second chain linking **shelter → moving/captured person → cave**, plus sufficient force to restrain the band.

The most valuable thematic tension is **information quality**. The Lannister-allied searchers publicly treat the Hound helmet as proof of a specific criminal; the Brotherhood itself nearly loses children because Lem treats the latest `Mad Dog` sighting as actionable. Both sides can kill wrongly when a famous name or apparent track substitutes for a validated person.

**Disposition:** `AFFC 30 HARWYN PLUMM/HARDSTONE LIVE DETACHMENT FIVE KNIGHTS + TWENTY ARCHERS`; `OLDSTONES PURSUITS TO FAIRMARKET AND NECK ARE EARLIER/SEPARATE REPORTED TRACKS`; `SMALLFOLK REFUSE/LATER DISCLOSE UNDER PRESSURE, INTENT UNKNOWN`; `LYLE MUTILATION SUGGESTION / JAIME DAYNE COOPERATION PRECEDENT`; `AFFC 37 HEDDLE INN SUPPORT NODE, ORPHANS AND GENDRY`; `AFFC 42 LEM SMOKE-CHIMNEY WATCH ORDER / FALSE MAD DOG REPORT / STREAM DOUBLING / FREY KNIGHTS DELAY`; `SMOKE ACTUALLY SENT UNKNOWN`; `BRIENNE FEVER-BOAT IS NOT A MAP`; `AFFC 44 STRONGBOAR DEPARTS WITH OWN FORCE, NO INTEROPERABILITY SHOWN`; `LEM REUSES HOUND HELM, BERIC DEAD PER THOROS`; `LYLE→LEM, LYLE→STONEHEART AND LYLE→JAIME ACTUAL CONTACT UNCONFIRMED`.
