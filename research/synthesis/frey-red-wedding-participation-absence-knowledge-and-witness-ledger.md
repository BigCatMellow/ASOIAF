## Central result: Merrett supplies a named plan and task allocation, not merely an all-Frey conspiracy

**[ASOS Epilogue (Merrett)](../../Source/Main-Series/03-A-Storm-of-Swords/text/082-epilogue.md)** is unusually direct about operational responsibility:

- **Merrett's participant understanding** identifies **Lord Walder Frey** as ordering the murders and **Lame Lothar Frey and Roose Bolton** as planning how the massacre would work, right down to the songs. `POV-INTERNAL PARTICIPANT ACCOUNT` about others' planning. It is **stronger than circumstantial reconstruction**, but the actual negotiation between Lothar and Roose is **not itself staged on-page**.
- Merrett separately **remembers Lothar assigning *him personally*** one specific job: get **Greatjon Umber** too drunk to fight. `POV-MEMORY / DIRECT PERSONAL TASK`. [ASOS 51 (Catelyn)](../../Source/Main-Series/03-A-Storm-of-Swords/text/052-catelyn.md) independently shows him drinking with the Greatjon. Merrett's later claim that he merely drank is **strategic minimization**: he knowingly participated in the incapacitation plan, even if he was not a crossbowman.
- When threatened with execution by the Brotherhood, Merrett **names Lothar** as responsible for rigging tents and placing crossbowmen with the musicians, and **Bastard Walder Rivers** as commanding the attack on the exterior camps. `PERPETRATOR'S CHARACTER ACCOUNT`, plus physical corroboration that attackers fired from the gallery and tents/camps were assaulted. His plea shifts blame to others, so the allocation should be **attributed to Merrett**, not treated as first-person observation of every placement.
- Merrett remembers **Raymund Frey** cutting Catelyn's throat. He also recounts Frey losses: for example **Jinglebell** killed by Catelyn after she took him hostage, plus Frey soldiers lost in the camps. These do not excuse the betrayal or independently establish the number of every casualty. `POV-MEMORY`.

**The practical finding:** Walder's authority, Lothar/Roose operational design, Bastard Walder's reported camp command, Merrett's individualized incapacitation assignment, and specific hall violence by Hosteen, Edwyn, Black Walder, Ryman and Benfrey establish **differentiated roles**. Do not describe all Freys who took part as equally knowledgeable planners or every absent Frey as a conscientious objector.

This is evidence for individual loyalty to Robb, albeit reported by Robb. | In **ASOS 51**, **Robb asks Ryman** why Olyvar is not at either feast and says he had hoped to reappoint him squire. **Ryman answers that Olyvar is away on some “duty”**, no details. Catelyn recognizes his absence at the first signs of massacre. | `ABSENT / PRIOR ROBB LOYALTY SUPPORTED`. **No sighting of Olyvar receiving an advance warning, refusing Walder's command, being detained, personally planning a rescue, or knowing what was to occur.** Deliberate protective or preventative removal is **plausible but unverified**. She groups Alesander with Olyvar and Perwyn as absentees when the killing starts. | `REPORTED ABSENCE / NO MOTIVE OR LOCATION`. A professional singer's absence is **notable** because the planned musical performance masks weaponry, but does **not** prove he refused to play, was protected, or had specific knowledge of the replacement musicians. |

**Crucial family grouping:** **Olyvar, Perwyn, Roslin and Benfrey** are **full siblings** born to Walder's sixth wife **Bethany Rosby** (the [ASOS appendix](../../Source/Main-Series/03-A-Storm-of-Swords/text/999-back-matter.md) also lists their brother Maester Willamen, serving away in the Vale). Alesander is a different branch of the very large family. However **Benfrey** is **present at the wedding** and becomes involved in the violence, rather than being absent with Perwyn and Olyvar. Thus a claim that the Rosby-blood branch **as a whole** was spared the murder or protested it is false. Likewise, Roslin's distress is not evidence that her three brothers shared the same choices.

## 2. Roslin Frey — visible terror versus later testimony

**[ASOS 49 (Catelyn)](../../Source/Main-Series/03-A-Storm-of-Swords/text/050-catelyn.md):** Edmure greets Roslin, finds her attractive; Roslin cries and *tells Edmure* it is joy. Walder interrupts her, directing Benfrey to escort her away. Catelyn interprets the tears as bridal emotion and worrying about childbirth, **not yet understanding the impending massacre**.

**[ASOS 51 (Catelyn)](../../Source/Main-Series/03-A-Storm-of-Swords/text/052-catelyn.md):** Roslin's smile looks fixed, she pales during the bedding preparations and cries while carried away by the Greatjon. Catelyn initially attributes this to the customary bedding and her youth; once Edwyn's **concealed mail under silk** reveals the ambush, Catelyn retrospectively links Roslin's tears with the three missing Freys.

**[AFFC 38 (Jaime)](../../Source/Main-Series/04-A-Feast-for-Crows/text/039-jaime.md):** **Edmure**, now captive and speaking directly to Jaime, says **he did not know of the murders**, but that **Roslin had been made to distract him** and **did not want to cooperate**. He recalls her weeping; his account is `FIRSTHAND SPOUSAL TESTIMONY ABOUT HER ACTIONS AND EMOTION / HIS INFERENCE ABOUT COERCION`, **not an on-page Roslin confession identifying who threatened her or exactly when she learned**.

**[AFFC 33 (Jaime)](../../Source/Main-Series/04-A-Feast-for-Crows/text/034-jaime.md):** Daven Lannister says **Roslin is pregnant**, apparently fond of Edmure, and **Perwyn has reported that she prays for a daughter**. Daven and Jaime understand the political danger: a **son** could sustain a Tully heir and give Walder/the Lannisters incentive to dispose of Edmure. `REPORT THROUGH DAVEN VIA PERWYN / CHARACTER FEAR-INTERPRETATION`. It may reflect **Roslin's fear for Edmure**, but **no Roslin POV** states her reason or foresees a confirmed infanticide/execution order.

**Interpretation:** Roslin appears **aware of danger and actively distressed**, and Edmure's testimony strongly favors **coerced involvement as a pretext to remove him from the hall**, rather than cheerful co-authorship. **Neither the exact threat nor the extent of her advance knowledge is known**. Importantly, **her pregnancy now makes her a political figure with interests contrary to the men who arranged the wedding**, and her continued contact with Perwyn may provide an imperfect human messenger route.

The **outdoor guests and Stark camp** also die in great numbers. That is a separate combat space from the musicians' gallery and the bridal bedding chamber. ### What Merrett actually told the surviving victim — and did not tell her

The [Stoneheart/Jaime evidence and hearing audit](stoneheart-jaime-red-wedding-attribution-evidence-and-hearing-audit.md) gives the **ASOS Epilogue** an important epistemic distinction. Merrett **privately thinks** that **Walder ordered** the slaughter and **Lame Lothar planned it with Roose**, including music, and recalls Lothar **personally assigning him** the Greatjon drinking task. But when the Brotherhood threatens him **in Stoneheart's presence**, Merrett **speaks** a narrower distribution of blame—**Walder, Ryman, Roose, Lothar, Bastard Walder**—claiming his own job was only drinking. **He does not name Jaime as a conspirator**, but neither formally clears him. Stoneheart **nods only when asked whether Merrett himself participated**. Her direct earlier **ASOS 51 Catelyn** memory of Robb's murderer invoking Jaime does not transform the killer's taunt into an authenticated secret order, and Merrett's thoughts cannot be treated as Stoneheart's received testimony. The reader separately sees Jaime's innocent farewell (**ASOS 44**) and shared Brienne/Jaime receipt of the news at Brindlewood (**ASOS 62**).

## 4. Outside corroboration and reliability

### Merrett's confession is unusually detailed, but he tries to save himself

Merrett's **private thoughts** name Lothar, Walder, and Roose and describe the intentional intoxication assignment; this has strong **participant proximity**. Under threat of hanging he also names Lothar's gallery and tent preparation, Bastard Walder's camp leadership, and Raymund's assault. His last plea is to separate himself from them. **He has incentives to understate his culpability**, but he would need an implausibly specific alternative fabrication to explain the correct scene details and his acknowledged role. Still, no scene independently observes Lothar personally rigging the tents; use the epistemic labels.

### The Brotherhood knows about the crime, but not necessarily its internal plan

In the **ASOS Epilogue**, the Brotherhood lures Merrett with **Petyr Pimple's ransom** and executes both. **Lady Stoneheart/Catelyn** is present, giving direct victim memory of the hall. However her throat is damaged and she does not speak. The Brotherhood's immediate questioning of Merrett concerns **Sandor Clegane and a young companion**, not a forensic examination of his detailed planning claims. Merrett **does state some of the operational names in their hearing**, but no later POV shows those particular claims **independently checked or delivered to a court**.

**Useful distinction:** Stoneheart knows who struck **inside her field of view** and saw evidence of Edwyn's concealed mail. She **did not survive as a speaking observer of the outside camp attack** and cannot verify Lothar's private negotiations simply by remembering the massacre. Her revenge group is pursuing Freys, but that does not constitute a credible universal dossier identifying each cousin's degree of knowledge.

### House Frey fights itself after the murders

- **[AFFC 33 (Jaime)](../../Source/Main-Series/04-A-Feast-for-Crows/text/034-jaime.md):** **Ser Daven Lannister** distinguishes Perwyn as relatively decent while speaking with hostility of **Ryman, Edwyn, Emmon and Walder Rivers**. This is **Daven's personal opinion**, not an innocence judgment. He also relays Perwyn's communication about Roslin hoping for a girl.
- **[AFFC 44 (Jaime)](../../Source/Main-Series/04-A-Feast-for-Crows/text/045-jaime.md):** **Edwyn** blames **Black Walder** for leaking Ser Ryman's small-escort route to the outlaws after Ryman is hanged. **Walder Rivers** points out the absence of proof and that Black Walder was in **Seagard**. This shows a real intra-Frey succession rivalry and **an unconfirmed espionage allegation**, not that Black Walder ordered Ryman's death.
- **[ASOS Epilogue (Merrett)](../../Source/Main-Series/03-A-Storm-of-Swords/text/082-epilogue.md):** Merrett thinks the Frey family will fracture over inheritance once Walder dies, recalls hostility between Edwyn and Black Walder, and distrusts Lothar. The danger within the victorious family predates Stoneheart's later hangings.

**Important genealogical caution:** Walder's many sons, half siblings, cousins, and great-grandsons each have different inheritances, obligations, and alliances. The blanket expression “the Freys were traitors” describes the House's leadership and coordinated massacre but **cannot replace a person-by-person account** of who was absent, coerced, unwarned, resistant, or an active killer.

### Separate family succession owner — Ryman, Edwyn and the northern Walders

The [Frey succession, outlaw killings and Winterfell cousin audit](frey-twins-succession-edwyn-black-walder-brotherhood-north-fracture.md) owns **ASOS Epilogue Merrett's senior-line ranking**, **AFFC 44 Edwyn's uncorroborated accusation against Black Walder after Ryman's hanging**, and the family-tree consequences of **ACOK 4 Big/Little Walder's cousin rivalry → ADWD 51 Little Walder's murder and Hosteen's Manderly accusation**. Merrett/Petyr are actually killed at Oldstones under Stoneheart, but a link from that to the separate ambush of Ryman remains **unproven**, just as Big Walder's bloody clothes do not prove he killed Little. Keep those uncertain individual murders distinct from the **source-established Red Wedding perpetrators** in the present participation ledger.

## 5. Conditional future testimony / political consequences

| Path | Necessary evidence and steps | Reason it could matter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Olyvar returns to a Stark claimant** | Establish his post-ASOS 14 whereabouts, ability to travel, whether he knew anything beyond his own removal by Ryman, and recipient trust. | A Frey whose **personal desire to remain with Robb** is reported could challenge hereditary blame—but **he cannot testify to planning he never observed**. |

| **Alesander explains his absence** | Locate the singer after ASOS 51, explain whether moved by decree or personal decision, and specify what songs/performers he knew. | His absence during a **musically cued murder** is striking, but currently just an unexplained vacancy. |
| **Roslin chooses to challenge Walder** | Separate Edmure's corroborated memory of her weeping from her own eventual speech; show the immediate risk to her child and family. | Her coerced participation and pregnancy could create a conflict between Frey blood and Tully household allegiance, especially if she fears for Edmure's survival. |
| **Lothar's actual planning exposed by witnesses** | Merrett's own knowledge is lost with his death; need a surviving second perpetrator, physical planning order, or credible firsthand participant (e.g., Lothar himself, Raymund, Bastard Walder). | Converts a `POV-MERRETT REPORT` of planning into independently established operational responsibility while allowing characters to disagree about motives. |
| **Stoneheart mistakenly targets a less-culpable Frey** | A named absent member is encountered, her group knows only surname, and some trustworthy evidence of the person's actual conduct becomes available. | A credible moral pressure test for Lady Stoneheart's revenge versus distinguishing guilty participants from uninvolved relatives. **No such later incident asserted as already occurred.** |

**No fixed path:** Merrett's account points to a highly organized murder, not a hive mind of all Freys; Olyvar's genuine attachment to Robb does not make him the future savior; Roslin's weeping does not conclusively establish what she knew before leaving the dais; Alesander's absence is not proof of principled refusal; Black Walder's intra-family ambition is not proof he killed Ryman.

**Disposition:** `WALDER ORDERS MURDER (MERRETT INSIDER REPORT)`; `LOTHAR + ROOSE DETAILED PLANNING (MERRETT INSIDER REPORT)`; `MERRETT TASKED WITH INTOXICATING GREATJON (FIRSTHAND MEMORY, CATELYN CORROBORATES DRINKING)`; `BASTARD WALDER CAMP COMMAND, LOTHAR TENTS/GALLERY (MERRETT ACCOUNT)`; `RAYMUND ASSAULT ON CATELYN (MERRETT MEMORY)`; `OLYVAR PERWYN ALESANDER ABSENT (SOURCE SHOWN/REPORTED)`; `ROSLIN DISTRESS OBSERVED / COERCION REPORTED BY EDMURE`; `PERWYN PROBABLE ROSLIN CONTACT THROUGH DAVEN (REPORT)`; `FULL ROSBY SIBLING BRANCH NOT INNOCENT: BENFREY IN HALL`; `EDWYN-BLACK WALDER RIVALRY AND RYMAN ALLEGATIONS UNPROVEN`.
