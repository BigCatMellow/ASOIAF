## Executive findings: two different kinds of useful survivors

**1. An exceptionally durable local household:** in **ACOK 47 (Arya)**, **Gendry** talks about an elderly armorer **Ben Blackthumb** who had worked for the **Lothstons** before serving successive generations of **Whents** and now **Tywin**. In **ACOK 64 (Arya)**, Ben holds the armory's blades under **Roose**. In **AFFC 27 (Jaime)**, Ben survives as one of **only three of Lady Whent's people** Jaime finds under the Mountain's garrison. This establishes genuine cross-regime survival and workplace access, *not* an interview in which Ben speaks about prisoners.

**2. A cook who outlives opposing powers and participates in a breach:** **ACOK 47 (Arya)** depicts a **chief cook** running the kitchens when Arya, Jaqen, Rorge and Biter exploit a broth delivery to free northern prisoners. **ACOK 64 (Arya)** says *the cook* was **spared** during Roose's retaliatory executions; **some claimed** it was because he had prepared the infamous soup. **ASOS 62 (Jaime)** has **Tywin report** that `some of Lady Whent's old people` opened a postern to **Gregor Clegane**, replacing Vargo Hoat. **AFFC 27 (Jaime)** subsequently **specifically identifies the cook** as someone who opened that gate. These are consistent identifications of a household cook; the actual opening is **reported in later POV accounts**, *not* narrated during a cook POV or witnessed directly by Arya/Jaime. **The cook is not the sole identified gate opener**: Tywin's earlier report is expressly plural.

**3. Roose's purge destroyed the older information system:** **ACOK 64** records execution of **Maester Tothmure** for sending birds to **Casterly Rock and King's Landing** when Harrenhal changed hands; **Lucan** (Gendry's employer), **Goodwife Harra**, and the **steward** are also executed for various kinds of service to Tywin. **ACOK 30 (Arya)** earlier depicts Tothmure's serving girl's *third-party report* of a message questioning Joffrey's parentage, which she says Tywin ordered burned and suppressed. These examples show messages were **received, suppressed, or sent** at Tywin's Harrenhal and that sending them could carry fatal retaliation. Neither identifies a confirmed recipient for Tothmure's final ravens nor proves **Maester Gulian**, present much later under Jaime, possesses his predecessor's records.

**4. The stronger witnesses to *Roose's Duskendale order* are alive outside Harrenhal:** in **ACOK 64 (Arya)**, after the Freys depart Roose's room, **Qyburn, Steelshanks Walton and Arya remain**. Roose instructs Qyburn to prepare a message directing Tallhart to join Glover for a march **east to Duskendale**; **Qyburn explicitly promises to draft the document for Roose's seal**. **Steelshanks is last stated in the room and no exit is narrated before that exchange**, making him a *present potential earwitness*, not someone independently shown reading the document. Qyburn **later reaches King's Landing**, shown with Cersei and advising her (**AFFC 3, 24 Cersei**). Walton **later serves Roose at Winterfell**, observed by Theon (**ADWD 46, 51**). Both have their own interests and neither testifies publicly to the Duskendale directive. This directly sharpens the [existing Duskendale responsibility audit](roose-bolton-rival-banner-management-duskendale-winterfell.md): unlike local artisans, these two were **in the command room**.

**Central check:** `WITNESS TO CASTLE HANDOVER ≠ WITNESS TO RIVER FORDS`; `WITNESS TO ROOSE'S DUSKENDALE ORDER ≠ PROOF OF HIS TRIDENT INTENT`. The unanswered question of the **unnamed highborn Northern captives still alive in AFFC 27** remains separate.

## 3. The “weasel soup” error to prevent

**ACOK 47 (Arya)** depicts the **chief cook preparing ordinary food for Hoat's arrivals**, a broth kettle being ready, and Arya claiming an order for it; **Hot Pie** is stirring the broth when **Rorge, Jaqen and Biter** arrive. They use broth as part of the prison-break operation. The chief cook **complains** about serving Hoat; **he is not shown being initiated into Jaqen's plan**.

**ACOK 64 (Arya)** mentions *some said* the cook escaped execution because **he made the weasel soup**. This is an **in-universe rumor assigning credit**, not proof the cook invented the scheme, directed Rorge, or chose Glover as the person to free. The same expression becomes a nickname for Arya's action: Gendry explicitly blames **her** and her soup for helping put the Mummers and Bolton in power. Neither Gendry's anger nor the castle rumor licenses promotion of the chief cook into a third conspirator.

This has a practical consequence for later claims of loyalty. **Being spared by Bolton does not prove Bolton was his preferred ruler.** The **later postern collaboration with Gregor** might reflect opportunism, fear, retaliation, outside coordination, or the desire to unseat Hoat; we have the physical-act attribution, not an explanation of motive.

### Do not identify a specific postern without direct evidence

In **ACOK 64**, Arya plans to meet Gendry at an **east-wall postern behind the Tower of Ghosts** during her escape. **ASOS 62 (Jaime)** reports Whent servants admitted Gregor through *a postern*, without naming that gate. **AFFC 27** identifies the cook but not which postern. The shared term is **not evidence** Gregor entered by Arya's later escape route.

## 4. The maester replacement and the cost of communicating

**Earlier system, ACOK 30/64:** Tothmure has an active rookery and messages; an earlier sensitive communication concerning Joffrey is **reported secondhand** to Arya, and his later actual **bird dispatch to two Lannister centers is reported** by Arya's narrative as the reason he is executed. The evidence is **outbound birds**, not **their confirmed destination arrival** or written contents.

**Later system, AFFC 27:** Jaime rides with **Maester Gulian and four cages of ravens**, then Hasty expects Gulian to **remain at Harrenhal with the birds**. He is **not Tothmure returned under another name**, and the books do not state he inherited any letters, correspondence rolls, hostage lists or cipher keys from Tothmure.

**Mechanism:** a household can hold crucial secret knowledge but die, flee or be forced into silence when occupiers punish communication with enemies. Tothmure is a concrete observed example of this pressure; Cersei's prior unanswered ravens under Gregor are a *different* later symptom. Nothing proves the cook or Ben ever handled outgoing ravens, but both lived alongside changing communicators.

**Critical negative:** the **AFFC 27 Trident prisoners** are held in the same castle as Ben and the cook. The mere coexistence of kitchens, smithy and prisoners does not establish either man's access to their quarters or permission to release them. Both could potentially testify to castle power transitions; neither is a certified witness of Roose's Trident ferry orders.

## 5. Qyburn and Walton: the more actionable Duskendale witness split

### Roose's actual message instruction

In [ACOK 64 (Arya)](../../Source/Main-Series/02-A-Clash-of-Kings/text/065-arya.md) the last names present after the Frey commanders leave Roose's room are **Qyburn, Steelshanks Walton and Arya**. Roose directs that the victorious **Helman Tallhart** punish Darry's surrendered captives and burn the castle, and **then join Glover** and turn **toward Duskendale**. Qyburn commits to preparing the message for Roose's seal. The first phrasing, *by command of the king*, is at least applied to the reprisal; avoid presuming Roose claimed **every strategic detail** was Robb's personal command.

**Qyburn** was the **addressed writer**. As a result, he is a far stronger potential source for the **existence and wording of the intended outgoing order** than the elderly armorer or cook. His presence is verified; that he actually **wrote, sent, saved, or later reproduced** it is not. The **actual sent copy and Tallhart's receipt are unobserved**.

**Steelshanks Walton** is identified as **remaining in the room** with them. Unlike Qyburn, **he gives no acknowledgment of that specific writing instruction**, and the narration does not isolate his attention. He is a strong *possible earwitness* and an undoubtedly surviving **Bolton-side conduit**, not a proven dissenting witness.

**Arya** is the direct POV eyewitness and understands the destination, but the narrative places her in different later theaters without a delivered message of this event.

### Later positions, incentives and test

| **Walton** | Roose's Winterfell inner security circle by **ADWD 46 and 51 (Theon)**. | Roose's spoken order (if he heard it), practices of the purge, Walton's own actions. | Direct continued service and loyalty to Roose; unsafe to presume he will testify against his commander. |
| **Arya** | Beyond Harrenhal; last relevant firsthand custody knowledge belongs to her ACOK chapters. | Duskendale destination, named attendees, punishment of Harrenhal staff. | Information has not travelled to anyone who can act on it; child in disguise, later separated by great distance. |

**Counterargument:** Roose might defend the Duskendale command as a legitimate raid that became disastrous after unexpected military developments. The reliable evidentiary criticism is narrower: **he gave the destination in ACOK 64 yet subsequently blamed Glover's supposedly emotional decision (ASOS 49)**. Qyburn and Walton would not *necessarily* prove advance plans for a massacre, which require additional intent and foreknowledge evidence. Both may be truthful about hearing an order yet disagree on interpretation.

### Special continuity point: Qyburn knows more than a local worker, but not everything

Qyburn **was in Harrenhal under Roose** and later **in the capital under Cersei**. That creates a genuine observed *human movement of information-holding individuals across theaters*. Yet neither the actual draft nor the recipient of its strategic meaning is shown in the second theater. Likewise Walton transports Jaime and later returns to Roose, but no evidence says he delivered an account of the order to a rival.

These are three different evidentiary problems; an apparent witness to one cannot resolve the other two.
