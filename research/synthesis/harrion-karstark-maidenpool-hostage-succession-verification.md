## Result: Harrion's reported custody is important *because it is old*, not proof he is dead

**ADWD 44 (Jon): Alys Karstark personally says** her brother Harrion was a **prisoner at Maidenpool when the family last heard**, but the news was **nearly a year old**, and she fears he may already be dead. She identifies **Harrion as the rightful Lord of Karhold while alive** and **herself as his successor** ahead of their great-uncle, the castellan Arnolf.

**ADWD 49 (Jon): Cregan asserts** that Harrion has died, but **offers no dated death report or witness**. Jon privately considers the possibility Harrion *will* die soon. Neither the assertion nor Jon's expectation verifies death.

**ADWD 44: Alys accuses Arnolf** of two converging schemes: his public support for Stannis would provoke Lannister retaliation against the captive Harrion, and his secret arrangement with Bolton involved money, pardon and Harrion's head. Her report is independently supported **only as to Arnolf's apparent Bolton-side duplicity** by **ADWD 32 (Reek)**, where Roose and Ramsay discuss Arnolf as a participant in their planned trap for Stannis. **That Bolton conversation never names a bargain for Harrion's death.** Do not promote Alys's full alleged terms into an observed Bolton–Lannister execution agreement.

The question is therefore not simply **"Where is Harry?"**. It is **"Who can authenticate that the rightful lord is alive or dead, who controls his person or news, and who benefits from an unreliable death claim?"**

## 2. How Harrion's death could serve Arnolf's plan, without assuming it happens

**Alys's version, ADWD 44:** Arnolf, Rickard's uncle and **castellan** rather than hereditary lord while Harrion lives, proposes that Alys marry his son **Cregan**. Alys reasons that killing Harrion would place **her** ahead of Arnolf in succession, then her forced marriage and a child by Cregan could secure Arnolf's line. She fears the family would have little further use for her once that child exists.

**Her charges are specific**: Arnolf's declared allegiance to Stannis might turn his Lannister-held great-nephew into a politically dangerous hostage, and a secret Bolton pact allegedly includes Harrion's death. **Neither effect follows automatically.** It requires Lannister decision-makers to know or hear of Arnolf's actions, decide to retaliate *against Harrion*, still possess him, and successfully carry out the act; the publication contains **no authenticated instruction to do so**.

**Independent partial corroboration, ADWD 32 (Reek):** Roose speaks privately about Arnolf trying to bait Stannis toward a trap. That verifies collusion-related *discussion* between Bolton commanders, **not the full consideration Alys alleges** (gold, pardon, Harry's head). There is no shown direct Bolton-to-Maidenpool dispatch ordering harm to the hostage.

**Contradicting assertion, ADWD 49:** Cregan tells Jon Harrion already died. Its apparent purpose is to claim his father is now lord, but the assertion conflicts with Alys's last-status uncertainty and offers no verification. It might be knowingly false, based on delayed rumor, or true but unsupported to the reader. Jon's internal anticipation of a future death is a concern, **not a vision or confirmation**.

**Separate prospective reward, ADWD 17 (Jon):** Stannis contemplates granting **Winterfell** to Arnolf as a seemingly reliable Northern follower. Stannis's remark about Arnolf's heirs is not a ruling that Arnolf lawfully owns **Karhold**. Nor does a proposed gift of Winterfell confer control of the prisoner or title at Maidenpool.

## 3. Maidenpool custody — an available location, not a confirmed current jail

- **ASOS 35 (Catelyn):** Randyll Tarly defeats Glover and Tallhart at Duskendale. **ASOS 60 (Tyrion)** reports Tarly holds Maidenpool, Duskendale and the kingsroad as the Lannister–Tyrell order is re-established. Connection of military victory, territorial authority and possible prisoner transfer is a **plausible route** for Harrion, not witnessed transportation.
- **AFFC 14 (Brienne)** and neighboring Brienne/Jaime chapters independently depict Tarly maintaining authority in **Maidenpool**. **No examined POV personally visits Harrion there** and none describes the cell, guard, payment or prisoner register.
- **AFFC 43 (Cersei):** Cersei is **told** Tarly is heading south from Maidenpool toward the capital. **ADWD Epilogue (Kevan)** shows Tarly in King's Landing commenting on misbehavior by Ser Ronnet's escort when those men were in Maidenpool. This demonstrates **an officer with direct knowledge of Maidenpool's garrison and occupation** who can in principle answer *custody* questions. It does **not** show he knows Harrion's current condition, personally held him or ordered his death.
- **No Lannister-side custody receipt:** Cersei, Jaime and Kevan chapters checked provide no letter or conversation proving Harrion's release, death, transfer, exchange conditions or execution decision. Tywin's old ransom discussions are **not** proof of present custody after his death.
- **Appendix reliability test:** despite the AFFC/ADWD status list naming Maidenpool, the same late appendix leaves Wylis's known change of status uncorrected; treat the labels as useful navigation cues but **not up-to-date prisoner manifests**.

**Military counterpart:** Robett Glover survived Duskendale and is shown later supporting Wyman Manderly. He may be a natural witness to Harrion's field service but **has no on-page testimony confirming Harrion's capture or a shared Maidenpool custody route**. See [White Harbor/Northern faction motives](northern-coalition-manderly-umber-clans-arya-revelation-gates.md).

## 5. Alternative models — evidence, costs, and what would falsify them

| Model | Why plausible | Counterargument and decisive missing proof |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **M1 — Harrion remains alive under a southern jailer** | Alys's last affirmative report put him captive at Maidenpool; no narrated death. A valuable heir can be retained as leverage. | Nearly a year passes without confirmation. Need a *dated* live witness, letter or custody action. |
| **M2 — Harrion is dead, but Alys has not received the news** | Cregan flatly says he died; wartime communications fail. | Cregan is interested and uncorroborated; Jon does not accept the statement as proved. Need body, recognized witness or authenticated death message. |
| **M3 — Arnolf seeks his death by manipulating royal allegiances** | Alys gives explicit political mechanism; Roose privately acknowledges Arnolf's trap role. | **Lannister execution order is missing**, Arnolf's precise promised terms are only Alys's report, and successful provocation is not observed. |
| **M4 — Tarly/Lannisters retain Harrion but ignore or resist Arnolf's scheme** | A living highborn hostage is useful for negotiation; killing him may squander leverage with Karhold's legitimate branch. | We have no current captor statement or confirmed incentive. A tactical argument is not evidence a specific jailer chose restraint. |
| **M5 — Harrion reappears and contests everyone else's presumption** | He is the reported senior legitimate heir; Brynden and Robb explicitly disagree over whether he must hate Robb; nobody has heard his opinion. | Needs proven survival, a credible release/travel route, and Harrion's own freely chosen political response; the narrative has none of these yet. |

**Revision gate:** news of survival would immediately reopen succession and Alys's role as protective claimant, while news of confirmed death would open her senior inheriting claim—**not** prove that Cregan's proposed marriage was valid or that Sigorn would be accepted. Either outcome affects Stannis's tentative Northern grants, but only after knowledge reaches his court.

## 6. Consequence chains for a character-led continuation

1. **Southern hostage still alive:** dated Maidenpool source → authenticated survival delivered to Alys or relevant northern leadership → factions must distinguish Harrion's **lordship** from Arnolf's temporary **castellanship** → Harrion chooses whether to contest Arnolf or accept another settlement. Alys's marriage remains a real personal choice and defensive alliance, but it does not automatically install her over a living brother.
2. **Confirmed death reaches the North:** identify corpse, witness or document → authenticate when/how death happened → distinguish **ordinary war death** from Arnolf's alleged engineered execution → legal claim passes to Alys *under her asserted custom* → Karhold garrison, king and local households must decide whether to accept Sigorn and any Thenn settlement.
3. **Uncertainty persists:** Cregan's unfounded death assertion competes with Alys's out-of-date captivity report. Arnolf or Bolton can benefit from **administrative control and ambiguity**, without anyone being required to have assassinated Harrion. Outside arbiters such as Stannis may first need urgent battlefield decisions rather than a completed family-court hearing.

**The strongest character-level contrast:** **ASOS 20** places the possibility of Harrion's **independent judgment** opposite Robb's certainty that a son must hate his father's executioner. A later living Harrion might expose that certainty as an understandable but incorrect projection—or confirm it. That decision must come from Harrion's own behavior, not be supplied by a family name or lordly lineage.

## 7. Bounded follow-up evidence requests

1. Find a **named Maidenpool prison official, letter, release, transport or execution** concerning Harrion. The five published novels in this pass supply none; do not turn appendix listing into a fresh sighting.
2. Trace **Robett Glover's last directly observed exchange/release** separately and test whether he ever discusses Harrion. No generic prisoner swap can be assumed for both.
3. Identify the **recipient and date** of Arnolf's public Stannis declaration on the Lannister side, if any, before alleging it caused or was designed to cause an actual order.
4. Recheck whether Tarly's movement from Maidenpool to King's Landing creates a **real information route** and whether any protagonist has both motive and access to ask him about the Karstark heir. His presence alone is not an inquiry.
5. Assess the **risk of a false death certificate** versus a true one as separate pressure tests. An interested statement by Cregan cannot defeat an authenticated survivor.
6. If any continuation depicts **Stannis learning Alys's marriage and Harrion's custody**, name the messenger, the actual message content, receipt, and the political decision. [Karstark communications audit](karstark-message-coalition-gates.md) owns those later deliveries.

**Disposition:** CONFIRMED = Harrion was Green Fork captive; Roose later *says* he sent him with Glover; Duskendale Northern force was defeated; Alys provides an old Maidenpool-captive report; Cregan disputes his status. UNCONFIRMED = direct Duskendale capture, current Maidenpool incarceration, execution, Lannister death order, Roose–Arnolf deal terms concerning the hostage, and contemporary wider Northern knowledge. **Harrion alive/dead: UNKNOWN in current narrated five-book evidence.**
