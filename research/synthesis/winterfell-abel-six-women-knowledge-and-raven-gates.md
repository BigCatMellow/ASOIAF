## Executive finding: the rescue failed at several separable points, but the text doesn't complete the capture story

**ADWD 37 (Theon):** A singer styling himself **Abel** enters Winterfell with **six supposed female relatives**, accepted in part because other musicians are present but the castle lacks a singer. Their claimed relationships do not resemble their appearance; several sing or play music, and they present themselves as washerwomen. With **ADWD 31 (Melisandre)** establishing Mance alive and disguised, **ADWD 35 (Jon)** confirming the Mance-plus-six rescue dispatched, and later women's coordinated actions, `ABEL = MANCE` is a **very strong cross-POV identity inference**, not a verbal revelation to the Winterfell court. Theon does not independently unmask the bard as the former King-Beyond-the-Wall.

**ADWD 51 (Theon):** The rescue party's plan is to remove the woman they believe to be **Arya**, using a servant-clothes exchange with **Squirrel** and Theon's practical access to Ramsay's rooms. **Theon internally says Abel is pursuing the wrong girl, nearly reveals the imposture and remains silent**. As Jeyne falteringly begins to say her name to the women, **Theon stops her before she finishes**. These are unusually strong **negative disclosure constraints**: neither scene contains the party learning the real bride is **Jeyne Poole**. Theon's assessment that Abel is fooled is a **POV inference**; a secret independent discovery by Abel is **not shown** and cannot be ruled out by an omniscient claim.

**ADWD 51:** The six women separate at the decisive moment. **Holly is struck by two crossbow bolts and falls bleeding on the battlements; Frenya is last heard fighting pursuing guards after remaining behind on the drawbridge**. **Rowan, Willow and Myrtle** return toward the Great Hall for Abel; **Squirrel** remains behind in the bride's bed; **Theon and Jeyne** make their escape by a desperate jump. **No direct scene thereafter accounts for Abel's capture, Rowan/Willow/Myrtle/Squirrel's capture, Frenya's death, or the retrieval/flaying of Holly's body.** Thus a letter that claims **all six** are skinned may convey intelligence obtained after this scene, exaggerate, or invent it; the published POV does not decide.

**ADWD 62 (Asha):** Tycho Nestoris and companions reach Stannis's crofter-village force from the direction of Winterfell and deliver **Theon and the girl** who had been collected by **Mors Umber**. **Asha recognizes Theon**. This confirms survival of Theon and Jeyne beyond their jump and a potential human knowledge channel into Stannis's camp. It does **not show** an outgoing letter from there to Castle Black, or that all present know the bride's true identity.

## 3. The false Arya problem is an intelligence trap

**Theon is the key informed insider:** He has known the real Stark children, is forced to endorse the substitution at the wedding (**ADWD 37**), and in **ADWD 51** knows the Bolton bride is Jeyne. His thought that the singer intends to rescue Ned's daughter and would be risking his life for a false target is explicit. He is tempted to reveal the deception at Abel's ruined-tower meeting, but keeps quiet.

**Rowan's language matters:** In the rescue bedroom she **calls the girl Arya** and tells her they will take her to her **half-brother, Jon Snow**. When the girl begins to recover her true name, **Theon physically and verbally stops her from saying it**; he refers to speaking later, when the immediate danger has passed. This is **strong positive evidence** that the group *acts* as if she is Arya and **no direct proof** that the real identity reaches them.

**Nuance:** Theon does say aloud that he knows her name, and she begins responding before he silences her. This *might* make a perceptive listener question the imposed identity; no woman is shown identifying her as Jeyne from that exchange. **Do not state a metaphysical impossibility for anyone to suspect the fraud**. Conversely, there is no book-evidenced route by which the Mance group would learn the precise true name from Theon's withheld thought.

**Why this affects authorship testing:** A candidate **Mance** who is exposed as Abel might know his own false identity, the six women's names, the rescue, the false “Arya” designation, the fact that **Theon/Reek** is implicated, and the lord's public battle preparations. These **specifics overlap the Pink Letter**; nevertheless Mance's **post-escape freedom, knowledge of Jeyne's true name, possession of Stannis's sword, a seven-day battle, or access to official Bolton correspondence are NOT shown**. A candidate **Ramsay/inner Bolton household** has obvious practical access to his **own bride, Reek's former service and local guards**, and might discover details of captured women, but neither capture nor particular authorial participation in a Pink Letter is narrated. **Neither model can be closed.**

**Alternative:** A sophisticated liar could have **partial** information and bluff the rest. The letter's unusually specific demands are not an all-or-nothing proof of literal access to each person and military trophy.

## 4. Stannis's forces, Roose's bird, and the difference between an open channel and a delivered letter

1. **ADWD 51 (Theon):** **Maester Rhodry holds a raven and Roose Bolton holds a parchment** in the hall. The bird is wet, suggesting a recent flight through foul weather. **POV INFERENCE:** Theon supposes the paper is wet too. **VERIFIED:** Roose says Stannis is snowbound, approximately three days' ride, and orders Frey and Manderly formations out to engage him. **NOT SHOWN:** sender, full wording, destination of any *outbound* bird, an authentication mark, any report that Stannis has already been slain or evidence the note is the threatening Pink Letter.
2. **ADWD 51:** Abel is called to perform as the military decision is announced. He is **in the same hall**, but there is **no demonstration he handled Roose's note, raven, rookery or the sorcerous report**. Proximity **is not** access to the letter writer's desk.
3. **ADWD 51:** Theon and Jeyne escape. **ADWD 62 (Asha):** **Tycho Nestoris** arrives at Stannis's camp with an entourage including Asha's companions; **Mors Umber** had held Theon and the girl after their jump and passes them into that party. Asha recognizes her brother. This is a **verified human information handoff** from Bolton territory toward Stannis's camp. It does not identify an outward raven carrying any subsequent report.
4. **ADWD 69 (Jon):** the letter signed as Ramsay claims a **seven-day** destruction of Stannis's host, capture of Mance and the flaying of the six women. No five-book POV independently shows that alleged seven-day battle, Mance's captivity or the entire skinning sequence. **Earlier living Stannis at his camp does NOT logically disprove a later loss**, because the cross-POV chronology is not precisely closed; the testimony is still `LETTER CLAIM / UNKNOWN`.
5. **Timing issue:** Winterfell's **incoming raven and sorties** precede the **observed escape** in Theon's local chronology. A letter reporting that *specific* later disappearance would need the writer to learn of the subsequent event; the **raven seen before the escape cannot itself convey the already-unhappened escape**. Do **not** reverse a scene's chronology to make a preferred author or courier route work. A **later unshown outgoing dispatch remains possible**, but unverified.

**Particularly weak shortcut:** Do not infer from Theon's observation of a **wet incoming raven** that Jon's later Pink Letter must have traveled on the same bird, originated at Winterfell, or **was physically dry**. The later text does not offer a matched weather/handwriting comparison. Theon infers wet parchment in Roose's hands but does not inspect it; this is not a forensic disproof of any provenance.

## 6. Competing hypotheses and disconfirming observations

- **H1. Ramsay writes using interrogations after the escape.** Could explain specific identification of a bard/party and the bride/Reek demand if any accomplice is caught. **Critical missing premise:** **none of the six women's captures nor Mance's capture is shown**, and Stannis's defeat remains a letter assertion. `TEST:` name the prisoner, witness, interrogation, date and outgoing raven.
- **H2. Mance writes to bring Jon out of Castle Black.** His own infiltration makes him an informed candidate about mission participants. **Counterevidence:** Theon repeatedly believes Abel is deceived about the false Arya; the rescue falls apart; Abel's capture/survival and rookery access are unshown. The message *might* prompt many responses other than the one Jon chooses. `TEST:` physical letter and a demonstrable exit/dispatch point, a writer's revealed intention.
- **H3. A different actor uses captured or public intelligence to mimic Ramsay.** Pink wax is familiar from **ADWD 28 (Jon)**; wedding, bride and Reek facts are not unshareable state secrets. **Critical missing premise:** no demonstrated collection of the nonpublic mission details or falsified-seal/letter transmission. `TEST:` authentic source handoff and named actor.
- **H4. The letter is largely truthful and locally written.** Bolton has guards/courtiers, and one could learn that the rescue failed. **Critical missing premise:** no verified complete capture or battle and no executed later bird route. `TEST:` independent corpse/trophy inspections and military survivor accounts, rather than relying on the threat itself.
- **H5. Mance's entire six-woman band died before the escape completed.** **Directly inconsistent with last scenes** if phrased as six simultaneously dying at the Battlements Gate: **Rowan, Myrtle, Willow turn back; Squirrel is hidden in a bed; only Holly and Frenya accompany Theon/Jeyne toward the gate**. A *later* capture of all remains possible but unshown. `TEST:` the specific separation and each named woman's subsequent whereabouts.
