## Executive result — ordinary operational intelligence needs fewer unshown steps than a Qyburn leak

**[ASOS 4 (Tyrion)](../../Source/Main-Series/03-A-Storm-of-Swords/text/005-tyrion.md)** is stronger than a single vague Tywin claim: **Bronn**, discussing current war news with Tyrion *before Tyrion meets Tywin in the same chapter*, already says Northern forces are burning toward **Duskendale**, and Tywin is sending **Randyll Tarly** against them. **Tywin** subsequently identifies **Tallhart and Robett Glover**, says his force is moving toward Duskendale, and explains he sent **Tarly** to meet it while **Gregor** marches up the kingsroad to block retreat. These are **two directly observed speeches** in two different conversations, not proof of two *independent primary informants*. Bronn could repeat ordinary soldier gossip or widely circulated court war news; he does not name his source. Nevertheless, **the march's general direction and the Tarly response were *not restricted to a secret letter read by Tywin*** by the time Tyrion heard them.

**[AGOT 62 (Tyrion)](../../Source/Main-Series/01-A-Game-of-Thrones/text/063-tyrion.md)** already shows **Tywin citing Addam Marbrand's mounted outriders** when estimating the Northern army's approach from the Twins before the Green Fork. This is an **explicit working intelligence channel** in a comparable riverlands military campaign. It does not prove these *same* scouts supplied the Duskendale report one book later.

**[ASOS 19 (Tyrion)](../../Source/Main-Series/03-A-Storm-of-Swords/text/020-tyrion.md)** independently presents **Varys briefing the royal council** on Tarly's victory, with the battle described as having happened **yesterday at dawn**, Tallhart reported dead and Glover's surviving men falling back toward Gregor. The page does **not say how** Varys heard so quickly, identify the informant or display a battle-message raven. He also provides other espionage intelligence at that meeting. This is `RECEIVED BATTLE NEWS AT COURT / MEDIUM UNKNOWN`, **not** proof Varys personally tipped off Tywin *before* battle.

**[ASOS 53 (Tyrion)](../../Source/Main-Series/03-A-Storm-of-Swords/text/054-tyrion.md)** supplies a crucial but **later, separate** control case: in private Tywin confirms Tyrion was excluded from the planning of the Frey-engineered **Red Wedding**, defends strict compartmentalization, and accepts the Lannister benefit and promised protection. Earlier in **ASOS 4** and **ASOS 19**, he talks about winning battles with written letters and ravens. Thus **secret Tywin diplomacy is not imaginary**—it is source-confirmed in **the Red Wedding case**. But this does **not** identify a Duskendale intercept or prove Roose had already agreed to a military betrayal when the Tallhart order was spoken. Treat `TYWIN CAPABLE OF SECRET DEALS` separately from `TYWIN PROVED TO HAVE HAD THIS PARTICULAR SECRET SOURCE`.

**The best-supported narrower model:** A large Northern force was moving visibly east and raiding; conventional mounted intelligence and wartime information circulation could identify it, allowing Tywin to organize a pincer. Roose had given that destination and later obscured his responsibility. Whether Roose **wanted the loss** and whether **he or Qyburn leaked precise orders** remain unproven; neither follows from Tywin learning an enemy army's location.

### Critical temporal correction: Tothmure cannot automatically be the leaked Duskendale letter

An appealing theory is that Maester Tothmure's **Harrenhal → King's Landing/Casterly Rock** birds betrayed the operation. But **ACOK 64 states his ravens were sent on the *night the castle changed hands***, while Roose's **Duskendale instructions come later**, in the very same Arya chapter. As that particular dispatch predates the instruction, it **cannot carry that not-yet-issued order** unless the text supplies an independent earlier plan, which it does not. It could warn that **Roose had taken Harrenhal** or describe enemy dispositions available then; contents and receipt are not shown. Tothmure is dead by the time Arya hears Roose dictate the march.

The **rookery remains operational** under Bolton (Arya observes a raven descending and wonders about its message), but no specific **post-dictation raven to Tywin** is attributed to Qyburn, Roose, or another keeper. **Raven capability** is different from **proof that it carried a battle plan**.

### Tywin's ASOS 4 statement that Roose holds Harrenhal “for Robb Stark”

Tywin also characterizes Baelish's grant of Harrenhal as an **empty title so long as Roose controls the castle for Robb** (ASOS 4). This is a contemporaneous **statement of public alignments** and cuts against assuming the novel has already explicitly established a Tywin–Roose pact at that moment. But Tywin can conceal information in meetings, so the statement **does not prove innocence** either. It is a countertest to an overconfident timeline: **a later demonstrated secret conspiracy with Freys cannot be silently backdated to every earlier Bolton decision**.

### Collusion date is separate from tactical reconnaissance

The [Bolton/Frey/Tywin first-contact source chronology](bolton-frey-tywin-collusion-first-contact-chronology.md) establishes **ACOK 64 (Arya)** narrates Frey defeatism, **Roose's Duskendale dictation**, and *only later that same day* receipt among the Freys of the broken-marriage raven; **ASOS 4** Tywin has **important letters** underway but neither writer nor Tyrion specifies their addressees; **ASOS 19** Tyrion suspects a hidden diplomatic option; **ASOS 37/44** Roose's release of Jaime is a concrete possible outreach but has an explicit self-protection explanation. **ASOS 51/53** confirm later Frey–Bolton murder and Tywin's reward bargain, yet do **not** timestamp an earlier source to Tywin about Duskendale. Therefore `CONSPIRACY PROVED LATER` is compatible with `TACTICAL SCOUTS SUFFICE EARLIER`; neither model can be converted to a fixed historical start date without a named communication.

## 2. Bronn's information is a useful “how secret was the march?” control

Bronn is **not** in Tywin's inner war conference in ASOS 4; he visits Tyrion's recovering sickroom and talks casually about a known northern advance and Tarly's prospective opportunity for plunder. This is `PERSON-REPEATS-CURRENT-WAR-NEWS`, not a second authenticated scout report. Its **independent scene** shows the information circulating at least to Tyrion's household, perhaps via serving men, soldiers, merchants, lordly retinues or official Lannister announcements. **None of those upstream relays is identified**.

Two separable kinds of intelligence:
1. **Observable:** a sizable force under known banners marching/burning toward Duskendale, suitable for reconnaissance or ordinary wartime gossip. **Bronn's report corroborates wide awareness at this level.**
2. **Private:** Roose authored the order, described its pretext as revenge, and directed Qyburn to draft it for his seal. **No Lannister speech says it received the dictated letter or knew Roose's private motive**. Even Tywin might infer Stark incompetence from the march without knowing the origin of the order.

**Discriminator:** an enemy's knowledge of an army's movement and the enemy's successful counterdeployment is **not itself** evidence that the enemy obtained its commander's sealed orders. To claim the latter, demand an informant's identity, an intercepted document, the contents of a known outgoing message, or a named participant saying he communicated the plan.

## 3. Varys, Tywin and the limits of secret-war correspondence

**Varys's direct performance:**
- In **ASOS 19**, Tywin opens the council's war business by prompting Varys, who describes the previous day's battle, its casualties and possible retreat path. This confirms that the **royal court has timely tactical battlefield information** after the fact.
- Later in the **same ASOS 19 council**, Varys produces a **parchment** when Tywin asks whether he has other reports, and discusses matters as disparate as the Fingers, Stepstones, Free Cities and Qarth. This confirms a **multi-area reporting role**. The parchment in this subsequent exchange is **not identified as the Duskendale intelligence or a dispatch from Roose**.
- The text supplies **no named pre-battle courier or source to Varys** in the Duskendale episode, nor proof he used the very same informant who briefed Tywin earlier.

**Tywin's plausible rival mechanisms:**
- **Mounted scouts:** a directly shown Lannister precedent (AGOT 62) and a highly visible raiding column; fewest specific unshown conspirators. `SUPPORTED CAPABILITY / ACTUAL DUSKENDALE SCOUT UNNAMED`.
- **Garrison/local or allied Tyrell intelligence:** territory and roads contested; enemies could report a march as it approaches. `PLAUSIBLE / NO PARTICULAR REPORT IDENTIFIED`.
- **Varys's informants:** court's multi-location reports and accurate post-battle briefing. `CAPABILITY VERIFIED / PREBATTLE SOURCE NOT LINKED`.
- **An intercepted Roose/Tallhart dispatch:** an intended sealed message exists in instructions; riders or ravens could be intercepted. `NO INTERCEPTION, LETTER OR RECIPIENT SHOWN`.
- **Deliberate Roose/Qyburn cooperation with Tywin:** Roose author of the hazardous assignment, later shifts blame, and Tywin later proves capable of covert agreements (ASOS 53). `SERIOUS THEORY / MISSING DIRECT TRANSMISSION`. **Qyburn's later physical move to King's Landing with Jaime happens *after* Tywin's ASOS 4 briefing**, so that journey **cannot explain prior intelligence**. An earlier message would need its own evidence.
- **Tywin strategic guess from broad northward/eastward maneuvers:** no need to read a particular spy note if his scouts have tracked the column; `POSSIBLE`, cannot say he purely guessed because he speaks confidently of the leaders and road.

**The Red Wedding control case matters in both directions.** In **ASOS 53** Tywin demonstrates operational secrecy and true conspiracy with Frey; it is wrong to call every suspected Tywin backchannel impossible. However, the very same passage emphasizes **compartmentalization**. The absence of direct clues to an earlier, different pact does not authorize treating Qyburn, Varys, Roose and Tywin as a single coordinated information network for Duskendale. **Capability is not a named link.**

## 4. Message transport, time and geography

The narrative supports no complete time-and-distance ledger for any of these Duskendale reports. Do not infer actual travel days from chapter numbers alone. Three communications use **different receipt conditions**:

| Information | Sender and target | What is observed | Do not infer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Tallhart falls upon Darry** (ACOK 64) | **Tallhart's rider → Roose/Harrenhal** | `RIDER ARRIVAL` reported by Arya; Roose now knows the surrender happened. | Rider was also commissioned to carry Roose's **later** sealed response; same person or route unshown. |
| **Duskendale order** (ACOK 64) | **Roose/Qyburn → Tallhart** | `ROOSE SPEECH + QYBURN PROMISE`; later army goes east. | Physical draft, dispatch, receipt, private duplicate, or extra copy to King's Landing. |
| **Enemy identifies eastward march** (ASOS 4) | **Unknown observer → Tywin and wider KL war information** | Bronn knows general news; Tywin knows commanders and deploys intercepting forces. | Secret leak rather than scouts, a Varys source, or an exact time the report arrived. |
| **Varys's victory report** (ASOS 19) | **Unknown battlefield witness → Varys/KL council** | Varys relates yesterday's battle. | Message medium is a raven; a single sender supplied every detail; Tywin was earlier told by same network. |
| **Robb learns defeat** (ASOS 35) | **Unknown messenger → Robb** | Robb reacts to report and loss estimate. | Robb received Duskendale order in time to stop it, or no one tried to warn him. |
| **Tywin–Frey secret arrangement** (ASOS 53) | **Actual plotters and intermediaries → outcome already occurred** | Tywin privately acknowledges conspiracy and information compartmentalization. | Precise starting date of conspiracy, content of every exchange, a proven Duskendale component. |

**Communication asymmetry matters:** [ACOK 39 (Catelyn)](../../Source/Main-Series/02-A-Clash-of-Kings/text/040-catelyn.md) explicitly says a raven cannot find **Robb's moving field army**, whereas known castles can receive birds and a rider can locate a known garrison or route. This provides a nonconspiratorial mechanism by which **the capital knew the eastward column earlier than Robb learned the disaster**, without proving that Robb's people were deliberately cut off. The actual messages, dates and routes still need proving.

## 5. Hypothesis challenge with reversal conditions

| Explanation | Strength in this audit | Distinguishing new evidence | Serious objection |

| **H1 — ordinary reconnaissance and publicly spreading war news** | `STRONG PLAUSIBILITY / DIRECT SCOUT PRECEDENT + BRONN`; **NOT** proven specific source. | A report naming a particular scout or local informant with dated observation of Tallhart/Glover. | Tywin's precise two-wing intercept and named commanders may imply more detailed military reports than a casual gossip source, but scouting could supply them. |
| **H2 — Varys's pre-battle network** | `POSSIBLE / NETWORK DEMONSTRATED, SOURCE NOT`. | Varys or a courier identifies a pre-battle Duskendale observation and its arrival at Tywin. | Varys's recorded speech is *after* the battle; later accurate reports do not prove earlier source. |
| **H3 — Tothmure warning from fall of Harrenhal** | `POSSIBLE FOR OLD CASTLE INTELLIGENCE ONLY`. | A recovered letter carrying prior troop dispositions or evidence that Duskendale plans were already formulated. | **Ravens sent before the depicted destination order**; cannot simply be “Qyburn's Duskendale plan.” |
| **H4 — intercepted intended Tallhart order** | `UNPROVEN`. | Order text, seal, courier capture, recipient report; time allowing Tywin to react. | No paper, handoff or interception in five books; incoming Tallhart rider is distinct. |
| **H5 — Roose or Qyburn intentionally warned Tywin** | `POSSIBLE CONSPIRACY / NO DIRECT LINK`. | Roose/Tywin exchange, Qyburn confession, second signed dispatch or a named cooperator with chronology. | Bronn and scout evidence support an alternative, and later Red Wedding conspiracy cannot be backdated by itself. |
| **H6 — tywin/allies inferred eastward objective from roads alone** | `POSSIBLE`. | A planning discussion based on visible enemy movement without any private papers. | No first-person account of Tywin's intelligence intake or reconnaissance patrol for Duskendale. |

### What this adds to Roose accountability

A **wrongful-blame assessment** and a **fully deliberate sacrifice plot** require different proof. The former is supported by **ACOK 64** (Roose's spoken choice), **ASOS 35** (disaster and Robb's surprise) and **ASOS 49** (Roose's later public attribution to Glover). The latter requires evidence Roose anticipated enemy interception, intended to lose particular men, possibly informed an enemy, or chose an obviously impossible route over safe alternatives with conscious knowledge. The result does not logically prove the intent.

The **first source-worthy addition** to pursue is a named **scout, Varys intermediary, Tyrell officer, captured messenger or surviving Tallhart man** who can say whether Tywin learned *by observation*, *by enemy report*, or *from the actual sealed Roose directive*. Until one appears, mark the source `UNKNOWN` and retain competing mechanisms.

**Disposition:** `SCOUTING CHANNEL EXPLICIT AGOT 62`; `GENERAL DUSKENDALE MARCH NEWS PRESENT IN BRONN'S ASOS 4 SPEECH`; `TYWIN'S NAMED-LEADERS/TARLY-GREGOR INTERCEPT ASOS 4`; `VARYS TIMELY VICTORY REPORT ASOS 19`; `TOTHMURE'S BIRDS PREDATE ROOSE'S ORDER`; `EDMURE'S EARLIER ORDER TO JOIN ROOSE NOT DUSKENDALE`; `TYWIN'S SECRET FREY DEAL LATER ADMITTED, DIFFERENT EVENT`; `NO PROOF OF ROOSE–TYWIN DUSKENDALE TRANSMISSION`; `NO PRESERVED QYBURN LETTER`; `ROOSE'S BLAME SHIFT STILL SUPPORTED`.
