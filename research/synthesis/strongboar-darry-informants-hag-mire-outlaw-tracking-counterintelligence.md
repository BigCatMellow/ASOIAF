## Executive conclusion

**Strongboar's search is causally plausible, but no trail yet connects his hunting party to Lem.** [AFFC 30 (Jaime)](../../Source/Main-Series/04-A-Feast-for-Crows/text/031-jaime.md) contains evidence that an earlier Frey operation physically tracked Brotherhood outlaws, but that **earlier party was commanded by Black Walder, not Strongboar**; and its last reported direction was **toward the Neck**, not a confirmed later Stoneheart headquarters. [ASOS Epilogue (Merrett)](../../Source/Main-Series/03-A-Storm-of-Swords/text/082-epilogue.md) proves the outlaws themselves trace travelers using ferrymen, highway witnesses and controlled rendezvous. [AFFC 25 (Brienne)](../../Source/Main-Series/04-A-Feast-for-Crows/text/026-brienne.md) shows both a crown commander's confessed difficulty finding the leaders and **Septon Meribald's precise familiarity with unmarked local routes**. These observations establish an **asymmetric, contested information terrain**, not invisibility or certainty of capture.

## 2. What the Brotherhood demonstrably knows and can do

**[ASOS Epilogue (Merrett)](../../Source/Main-Series/03-A-Storm-of-Swords/text/082-epilogue.md)**: Tom o' Sevens and associates lure **Merrett** to Oldstones using a purported ransom for Petyr Pimple. They have an armed party and a prepared hanging site. **Tom asks Merrett about Sandor and the Stark child**, reporting their own informants tracked **ferrymen carrying Sandor across the Trident** and a **robbed kingsroad traveler**. `POV-SEEN INTERROGATION AND ACCOUNT OF SOURCES`, **not proof Sandor was at the Red Wedding**. The group controls when and where the captive goes, sees and speaks. Their knowledge is **localized and assembled**, not supernatural.

**[ASOS 34 (Arya)](../../Source/Main-Series/03-A-Storm-of-Swords/text/035-arya.md)** shows Beric's company using the concealed **hollow hill** as a refuge, with scouts, guards and civilians; the party can bring armed captives through woodland before exposing the hideout. **[ASOS 39 (Arya)](../../Source/Main-Series/03-A-Storm-of-Swords/text/040-arya.md)** depicts small-unit reconnaissance and an attack on **Brave Companion** outposts near a burned septry, including guards surprised in the dark; they can also assist villagers and move through destroyed settlements. `BERIC-ERA OBSERVED`, not a guarantee that the same cave/number/command continues under Stoneheart.

**[AFFC 25 (Brienne)](../../Source/Main-Series/04-A-Feast-for-Crows/text/026-brienne.md)** gives a second, distinct information source: **Septon Meribald** says he knows the local country intimately, including **unnamed paths, woodland tracks and caves that do not appear on formal maps**. Brienne recognizes these as roads outlaws can use. `LOCAL TRAVEL KNOWLEDGE DIRECTLY CLAIMED`; Meribald has not been established as a Stoneheart conspirator, nor can **his personal map knowledge** be attributed to every outlaw.

**[AFFC 37 (Brienne)](../../Source/Main-Series/04-A-Feast-for-Crows/text/038-brienne.md)** and **[AFFC 42 (Brienne)](../../Source/Main-Series/04-A-Feast-for-Crows/text/043-brienne.md)** show **Gendry at the crossroads inn** and then **Lem and fellow Brotherhood men** rescuing/controlling surviving participants, but Lem is too late to prevent Brienne's injuries and child's peril, as Thoros points out. Their informal civilian base **does not give perfect warning of attackers**, making the group vulnerable to discovery and failed timing. [Tom's institutional access at Riverrun](stoneheart-brotherhood-riverrun-tom-jaime-hostage-red-wedding-two-audit.md) is a separate operational cell, not a verified direct relay to the current cave.

### Trust and mistaken identities as sources of tactical failure

Strongboar's punitive proposal at Darry gives an intelligible route to **destroying human cooperation**, while Jaime uses **Arthur Dayne's older, successful remedy** as a serious countercase. Treat **Strongboar mismanages peasants and is misled** as `THEORY`: no five-book chapter follows his actual interrogation. Likewise **the Brotherhood's control of old forest routes** does not mean they know Strongboar left Riverrun; no watcher or courier reports his departure to Lem. Both can be surprised.

The **portable Hound helm** adds a distinct identity error. Strongboar's suspect is `Sandor` on the basis of survivors recognizing helmet armor; **AFFC 31/37/42** give the reader a chain **grave marker → Rorge → Lem**. Even if Strongboar reaches a man wearing the helm, **finding helmet ≠ arresting Saltpans murderer**. Rorge is dead; Lem wears his equipment. The [helmet audit](strongboar-hound-helm-lem-stoneheart-jaime-pursuit-audit.md) owns this sequence and its separate influence on Jaime/Brienne.

## 4. Does the observed evidence put hunter and prey within reach?

**Plausibility**, not predicted event:
- Strongboar is **actually in the Riverlands** and motivated to seek an infamous armored man and Beric (AFFC 44).
- Lem belongs to **Stoneheart's locally operating band** and wears the helmet (AFFC 42).
- Reports of outlaws and a hooded leader **circulate in regional households**, and their bands are traceable at least episodically (AFFC 30).
- The Brotherhood has **local knowledge and social access**, so Lyle may not find it readily (ASOS Epilogue, AFFC 25).

**Decisive missing links:**
1. `STRONGBOAR ARRIVES AT DARRY` — intended, **not depicted**.
2. `STRONGBOAR RECEIVES A POST-AFFC 42 LEM SIGHTING` — **not depicted**.
3. `LEM REMAINS HELMETED AT AN INTERCEPTABLE PATROL LOCATION` — **not depicted**.
4. `A SCOUT OR VILLAGER ACTUALLY DELIVERS THE LEAD` — **not depicted**.
5. `STRONGBOAR AND LEM ARE PHYSICALLY AT SAME PLACE/TIME` — **not depicted**.
6. `JAIME CONSENTS TO BRIENNE'S LONE TRIP AND IS TAKEN BY STONEHEART` — **not depicted**, so a **rescue timed to that moment** requires another full causal branch.

**Best economical continuation model:** A party could plausibly obtain **a fresh village sighting**, then follow Lem's distinct recognizable helm, encounter the Brotherhood, and learn that the rumored `Hound` is a different man. It is a short causal path **if** the new sighting exists. **This is only a conditional scenario**, not a primary-text fact. A competing model has villagers shelter the outlaws, supplies dry up, and Lyle returns empty-handed; equally consistent.

## 5. What would upgrade the theory

- A **source-named scout / hunter / ferryman** telling Strongboar where a man in Sandor's helmet was seen after **Rorge's death**.
- A **fresh pursuit reported from Darry or Saltpans**, not Mariya's weeks-old hunt.
- An **outlaw-side lookout** warning Lem that a boar-banner party has left Riverrun.
- The Brotherhood's **current camp location and composition** (the older hollow hill is an example, not verified fixed headquarters).
- A **confirmed prisoner or actual weapon collision** between Lyle's men and Lem's group.
- Independent confirmation that **Jaime leaves Pennytree** and is captured before hypothesizing a rescue.
