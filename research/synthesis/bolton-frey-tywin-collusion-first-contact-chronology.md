## Executive conclusion — the pivotal earlier evidence is within *one Arya chapter*

**ACOK 64 (Arya) places Roose's Duskendale instruction *before* the Frey household's receipt of the broken-betrothal news.** This is stronger than comparing chapter numbers across books:

1. `EARLIER SAME DAY` Arya hears **Aenys, Hosteen and other Freys** arguing that Robb's army cannot defeat the Lannister–Tyrell alliance and should seek peace. **Roose replies that Robb has never lost a battle** and dismisses them. The meeting proves Frey pessimism; it **does not prove Roose agreed to betray Robb or that the Frey commanders knew of a Lannister pact**.
2. Immediately after the Freys exit, **Walda Frey's personal letter** is read aloud by **Qyburn**. Arya says **Walda writes from the Twins almost daily** and the usual letters express attachment to Roose and hope of children. `REGULAR BOLTON–TWINS PRIVATE COMMUNICATION CAPACITY`; these letters' **shown content is domestic**, not a secretly disclosed change of allegiance.
3. **Roose then personally tells Qyburn** to compose the **Tallhart → Glover → east to Duskendale** instruction, following Tallhart's reported capture of Darry. Qyburn promises to **prepare** it for Roose's seal. `ROOSE FIELD ORDER OBSERVED / DOCUMENT DISPATCH UNKNOWN`.
4. Arya finishes duties; **later the same day**, Roose returns from hunting wolves, then tells her he will leave Harrenhal to Vargo Hoat when he goes north. At night, she hears **the Freys arguing in the Wailing Tower**. Elmar tells her **a bird from the Twins** has brought news of the dishonored marriage understanding: he must wed someone other than the princess once promised him. `FREY GRIEVANCE MESSAGE RECEIVED AFTER DICTATION IN THE LOCAL SCENE`.

**Interpretive discipline:** this is a **local within-chapter order of events**, not a precise kingdom-wide date. Frey leaders or Roose **could** have learned of Robb's marriage independently/earlier; nothing here proves no private communication predated the raven. However, it is unsound to explain Roose's **already-spoken** Duskendale instruction as **necessarily caused by the fresh Wailing Tower raven and Frey reaction that occur afterward**. Frey dissatisfaction had other, explicitly stated reasons before this incoming news.

A parallel subtle image has Roose kill wolves including pups during the intervening hunt; it can be interpreted as narrative foreshadowing, but **is not factual evidence that a Frey–Bolton–Tywin murder agreement then existed**.

### Earliest **verified** contact versus earliest **verified pact**

- **Roose↔Frey household connection** predates ACOK 64: Roose is married to Fat Walda; her **repeated letters** to Harrenhal are a specific **actual communication channel**. Yet family marriage and affection are not a documented secret war plan.
- **Frey leadership's open disillusionment** exists **before the broken-match raven** in ACOK 64. The deterioration did **not begin exclusively** with the marriage insult; political defeatism already supplies a motive for peace or desertion.
- **Roose↔Lannister contact opportunity:** `ASOS 37/44` Roose deliberately arranges Jaime's release to his father **to obtain exculpation and avoid Tywin's revenge**. This occurs **before the massacre**, but the scene doesn't contain a Lannister counterparty accepting any secret terms. His willingness to release Jaime can be explained as self-preservation **without** a preexisting Red Wedding pact.
- **Tywin↔unknown correspondents:** `ASOS 4` shows writing **unspecified letters**, later recalled suspiciously in `ASOS 19`. It is plausible they concern disloyal Northern houses, but **the correspondence itself never names Roose or Walder** and does not demonstrate when any prospective deal became firm.
- **Roose+Frey violent cooperation:** `ASOS 51` massacre is `DIRECT` on-page, but the preparation is unshown; clearly **planning predates execution**, its start unknown.
- **Tywin→Frey/Bolton bargain:** `ASOS 53` Tywin's account and the agreed compensation retrospectively confirm the **three-sided arrangement by the massacre**. They do **not** supply a reliable earliest agreement date.

## 2. Jaime's “regards” and an intentional literary re-use

**ASOS 44 (Jaime):** Bolton asks Jaime to give his warm regards to Tywin; Jaime, insisting on reciprocal civilities, asks Bolton to give **his regards to Robb Stark**. Bolton agrees. At the Red Wedding in **ASOS 51 (Catelyn)**, the pink-cloaked attacker of Robb utters the message attributing regards to **Jaime Lannister**, immediately before striking.

This is a **distinctive cross-POV verbal echo**, not an independent source proving Jaime was aware of the planned murder. The actual earlier line was uttered in an apparently routine farewell by a **prisoner hoping to get home** who had threatened his captors earlier and offered Roose an exculpatory message for Tywin. Roose later **weaponizes** the line in a horrifying new context. A continuation can use this as evidence of Roose's **deliberately cruel theater and personal memory**, without indicting Jaime as co-planner on the basis of the quotation.

**Countertest:** If Roose was *already* collaborating with Tywin when Jaime said it, the double meaning is striking. If Roose only later committed to the plot, the same phrase would still be available as vicious irony. Its capacity for foreshadowing **cannot timestamp a bargain**.

## 3. Was Duskendale already part of the plan?

### The strongest “yes” case, classified `THEORY`

- Roose (ACOK 64) directly assigns Glover/Tallhart and a large foot component to Duskendale.
- Tywin knows the named column and deploys Tarly plus Gregor against it (ASOS 4).
- Roose later lets Robb blame Glover (ASOS 49), obscuring his own initiative.
- By ASOS 51/53 Roose, Frey and Tywin have acted together on murder and reward.
- **A coherent proposed mechanism:** Roose intentionally exposes rival-banner Northern infantry and later profits from diminished opposition to his own authority. It requires a hypothesis about Roose's foresight **and** an agreement/leak that is not shown.

### The strongest “not yet proven” countercase

- **Local ordering:** the Duskendale directive in ACOK 64 is **prior to the incoming Frey raven** of broken marriage. The **Frey officers present at its dictation have already left**, so they are not direct witnesses of its exact destination in that scene.
- Roose offers an intelligible **stated rationale**—rich lands comparatively unharmed by war, revenge for Glover and Tallhart. It could be disastrous judgment or opportunistic risk-shifting without a finished murder pact.
- **Conventional enemy reconnaissance** can explain Tywin's knowledge of a visible advance. [Tywin's field-intelligence audit](duskendale-tywin-field-intelligence-reconnaissance-provenance-audit.md) establishes a named Lannister scouting precedent and Bronn casually knowing the raiders' direction.
- **Tywin's ASOS 4 letters are unspecified**; the **ASOS 19** retrospective suspicion is foreshadowing rather than a certified letter recipient. **Tywin's ASOS 53 acknowledgement** does not describe **Duskendale** as a component of the conspiracy.
- Roose's **ASOS 37/44** freeing Jaime has an explicitly given self-preservation rationale (avoid Tywin blaming him for the mutilation). A favorable gift to an enemy could be *pre-pact outreach*, *part of an already-arranged bargain*, or simple insurance.
- No source confirms **Tywin ordered or approved** Duskendale, knew **Roose** originated the move, or that Roose knew Tarly/Gregor's dispositions in advance.

## 6. Next high-value source and story tests

1. Find a **dated, addressed letter** between **Tywin, Walder or Roose** from *before* ASOS 51 that contains unambiguous terms of changing allegiance, hostage release, murder or guarantees.
2. Check whether **Jaime ever reports back to Tywin** on Roose's treatment of him and whether his report precedes a specific Tywin instruction; the five-book source scenes do not display this proof.
3. Distinguish **Tywin's secret Red Wedding pact** from separate Westerling maternal collusion. [AFFC 44 (Jaime)](../../Source/Main-Series/04-A-Feast-for-Crows/text/045-jaime.md) shows Sybell claiming a contraceptive assignment **but no timestamp** for the bargain or proof Jeyne herself cooperated.
4. Re-examine **Tywin's undisclosed ASOS 4 letters** and **ASOS 19 better-option comment** only as **potential foreshadowing** unless future source corroborates recipients and substance.
5. Seek the **first independent Frey or Bolton participant** who gives an actual planning date. Even a first-person confession must be separated from strategic boasting, selective memory and later justifications.
6. If reconstructing future accountability, ask whether a **Roose subordinate (Qyburn, Walton, Glover)** has firsthand evidence of *which* wrongdoing: a Duskendale order, a Trident crossing decision, or the Red Wedding plan. One does not establish the others.
