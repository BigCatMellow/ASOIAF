## Verified vs. claimed vs. unknown

**VERIFIED:** Davos defies Melisandre's proposed sacrifice and risks his own position to save Edric (ASOS 63); Manderly says he wants Ned Stark's boy returned (ADWD 29); Wex marks the apparent destination on a map. Davos ends his printed arc with an assignment, **not a completed voyage**.

**CHARACTER CLAIM:** Wex's illustrated account, Manderly's account of Ramsay at Winterfell and northern political support, assumptions about the Skagosi. Some claims are credible but not independently shown in Davos's POV.

**INFERRED:** Davos is susceptible to promises of loyal service but has a demonstrated limit: he will oppose Stannis when the king's command might endanger a child.

**UNKNOWN:** Whether he safely reaches Skagos; Rickon's condition and wishes; Osha's plans; how Shaggydog behaves; whether the boy can be returned; whether Manderly would genuinely support Stannis after delivery.

## Decision knot: rescue or political extraction?

Do not write the answer first. Establish Skagos's actual situation, the safe routes, the child's circumstances, and Davos's present political information.

- **Route A — Bring Rickon to White Harbor:** Strengthens a Stark-centered northern coalition, but could turn Rickon into a hostage or figurehead.
- **Route B — Conceal/leave Rickon:** Follows the logic of protecting Edric, but might deprive Rickon of a family, allies, and a safer future. Davos would risk his mission and Stannis's cause.
- **Route C — Conditional return:** Negotiate safety, guardianship, consent or terms with Osha and the boy before moving him; may prove impossible or provoke competing claims.

These are options, not completed plot facts.

## Counterexample / challenge

**Tempting shorthand:** "Davos saved Edric, so he'll definitely abandon his mission when he finds Rickon."

**Counterevidence:** Edric was being considered for ritual killing; Manderly's stated goal is to restore a Stark heir against the Boltons. Those are different harms. The analogy produces a moral question, **not** a conclusion. No source chapter establishes Rickon's happiness or safety on Skagos.

**Other challenge:** Stannis is not merely a selfish claimant in Davos's experience. At the close of ASOS 63, Davos convinces him to respond to the threat from the Wall. The crown and defense of the living may conflict in complex ways.

## Dependencies before drafting

- Theon's survival and the separate false-Arya plot (Jeyne Poole).
- Manderly's position vis-à-vis Freys, Bolton and Stannis after leaving White Harbor.
- The Wolf's Den/Skagos chronology compared with Battle of Ice.
- Rickon's age, capacity and the role of Osha/Shaggydog as stakeholders.
