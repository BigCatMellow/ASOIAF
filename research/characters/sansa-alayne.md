## Central question

**Can Sansa turn the capacity to observe and influence people into ethically independent power, rather than become Littlefinger's collaborator or victim?** The last published scene places her within a proposed inheritance marriage that depends on a vulnerable boy's death, while she has genuine responsibilities to that boy.

## Competing motives and opportunities

**Sansa's needs:** safety, belonging, truthful identity and protection of young Robert; potentially a route home. Her courtesy and memory are survival tools as well as emerging political skills. She has repeatedly been used in marriage arrangements without full consent; avoid describing the Harry plan as her own desire.

**Littlefinger's motives:** command a fractious Vale without broad hereditary legitimacy; control an apparent daughter who is in reality valuable Stark marriage property; anticipate Robert's death and install a more popular heir. His forecast of Robert dying is **ominous but not proof he has decided to poison him**.

**Sweetrobin / Robert:** An anxious, sickly child with real emotional dependence on Sansa. His medical risk is both political vulnerability and moral responsibility. He is **alive** in the last five-book chapter.

**Myranda and the Royces:** Myranda plays the vivacious gossip but may have recognized a discrepancy in Alayne's story; Bronze Yohn is an independent armed opponent of Littlefinger; Nestor is indebted to Petyr and Myranda may have different knowledge from her father.

**Harry Hardyng:** Exists as Robert's heir under the described genealogy, has been knighted and fathered a child out of wedlock. Sansa knows little about his actual character and has **not met or committed to him in the five-book ending**.

## Explicit corrections to earlier outline assumptions

- **No Vale food strategy yet:** AFFC 10, 23 and 41 do *not* show Sansa coordinating grain exports or relief for the North. Future stewardship or food leverage must be *earned* from control over household finances, roads and political patrons.
- **No Littlefinger overthrow underway:** Sansa sees Lyn's staged hostility but does not yet have a coalition capable of displacing Petyr.
- **No Robert death or marriage:** Petyr's confidence is **his prediction**, not a narrated death or assured match.
- **No proved deliberate sweetsleep homicide:** Colemon identifies a cumulative medical risk. Sansa's immediate desire is to avert shaking on a dangerous descent. Do not flatten that into willful poisoning without subsequent conscious decisions.

## THINK — adjacent decisions

1. **Challenge the medicine:** Sansa may learn the risk from Colemon and insist on safer care, potentially endangering the descent/public ceremony and challenging Petyr. Or she may comply out of fear that a fit could kill Robert immediately.
2. **Manage Myranda's questions:** Sansa might befriend her cautiously, become dependent on her local political intelligence, or withdraw from a possible identity trap. Each path creates different trust costs.
3. **Choose how to handle the Harry plan:** Petyr expects Sansa to win Harry. She could comply temporarily, demand conditions, or seek independent allies. Acting openly as Sansa is dangerous until someone guarantees protection.

**Counterexample to 'Sansa is Littlefinger 2.0':** Her concern for Robert, guilt about lying, and continuing longing for family challenge the claim that she seeks power for its own sake. These concerns can also be manipulated by an unscrupulous guardian.
