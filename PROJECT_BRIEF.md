## Evidence and quality

1. Cite book + EPUB chapter number + POV/title, e.g. `ADWD 69 (Jon)`.
2. Distinguish **VERIFIED (in text)**, **CHARACTER CLAIM**, **INFERRED**, **THEORY**, **UNKNOWN**. Only the first is authoritative for narrated events; narrators themselves may misread events.
3. Give each consequential theory disconfirming evidence and at least one feasible alternative.
4. Preserve contradictory motives, incomplete knowledge and factional incentives.
5. Choose the smallest documentation change that aids retrieval. One owner per evolving fact/decision, link elsewhere.
6. Reconcile continuity before writing encounters involving previously separated POVs.
