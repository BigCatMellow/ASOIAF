## Outcome

A sourced, contradiction-aware, character-led basis for two unofficial further novels. No fixed ending receives authority just because it appeared in an earlier conversation.

## Working method: THINK → PLAN → DO → CHECK

**THINK (question framing):** For each character or plot knot, identify the real decision, separate narrated facts from interpretations, decompose conflicting loyalties, map assumptions, seek one counterexample, and retain two or three viable next paths. Borrow from [Pilot THINK](https://github.com/BigCatMellow/Pilot_Projects/blob/main/complete-ai-work-system/roadmaps/03-THINK-CREATIVITY-AND-IDEA-ECOLOGY.md), *not* its unvalidated search machinery.

**PLAN (smallest bounded next step):** Choose the research needed to discriminate paths, specify chapter locators, predicted implications and reversal triggers. Later waves are directional. Borrow from [Pilot PLAN](https://github.com/BigCatMellow/Pilot_Projects/blob/main/complete-ai-work-system/roadmaps/05-PLAN-ORCHESTRATION-AND-TASK-COMPILATION.md).

**DO:** Inspect original chapters; record supported facts, character beliefs, omissions and consequences in one canonical sheet per active POV/problem. **CHECK:** Compare with the source, challenge assumptions, update dependent sheets; if the story premise fails, reconsider it instead of rationalizing it.

## Triggers for revision

- A chapter contradicts the accepted state or relationship → fix source sheet and all affected downstream assumptions.
- Theory depends on unreliable testimony → change `VERIFIED` to `CHARACTER CLAIM`.
- Temporal overlap/travel times don't permit a proposed encounter → re-plan rather than hand-wave.
- A discovery changes a major ending premise → return to THINK and reopen alternatives.
- Artistic choice cannot be resolved from text → present distinct supported branches for human choice.
