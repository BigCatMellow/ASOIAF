## Goal

Research the five published *A Song of Ice and Fire* novels; evaluate competing theories against the text; develop a **provisional**, character-driven continuation for *The Winds of Winter* and *A Dream of Spring*. Avoid imitating George R. R. Martin's distinctive prose. Favor consequences and evolving character decisions over forcing a television-derived ending.
